# Approve, Approved

man *approved* of God among you by miracles, [[Acts 2.22|Acts 2:22]].

*approvest* the things that are more excellent, [[Romans 2.18|Romans 2:18]].

acceptable to God, and *approved* of men, [[Romans 14.18|Romans 14:18]].

*approving* ourselves as the ministers of God, [[2 Corinthians 6.4|2 Corinthians 6:4]].

ye may *approve* things that are excellent, [[Philippians 1.10|Philippians 1:10]].

Study to shew thyself *approved* unto God, [[2 Timothy 2.15|2 Timothy 2:15]].

learns not his duty and shows himself not *approved*, [[D&C 107.100|D&C 107:100]].

fill all these offices and *approve* of those names, [[D&C 124.144|D&C 124:144]].
