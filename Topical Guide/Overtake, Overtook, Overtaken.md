he will cause that it shall soon *overtake* you, [[3 Nephi 29.4|3 Nephi 29:4]].

judgments of God will *overtake* the wicked, [[Mormon 4.5|Mormon 4:5]].

being *overtaken* in a fault, [[D&C 20.80|D&C 20:80]].

hearken unto my voice, lest death shall *overtake* you, [[D&C 45.2|D&C 45:2]].
