# Unfruitful

choke the word, and he becometh *unfruitful*, [[Matthew 13.22|Matthew 13:22]] ([[Mark 4.19|Mark 4:19]]).

my understanding is *unfruitful*, [[1 Corinthians 14.14|1 Corinthians 14:14]].

have no fellowship with the *unfruitful* works of darkness, [[Ephesians 5.11|Ephesians 5:11]].

maintain good works … that they be not *unfruitful*, [[Titus 3.14|Titus 3:14]].

neither be barren nor *unfruitful* in the knowledge, [[2 Peter 1.8|2 Peter 1:8]] ([[D&C 107.31|D&C 107:31]]).

land shall be barren and *unfruitful*, [[Moses 7.7|Moses 7:7]].
