# Trade

dwell and *trade* ye therein, [[Genesis 34.10|Genesis 34:10]].

Judah, and the land of Israel … *traded* in thy market, [[Ezekiel 27.17|Ezekiel 27:17]].

he that had received the five talents went and *traded*, [[Matthew 25.16|Matthew 25:16]].

might know how much every man had gained by *trading*, [[Luke 19.15|Luke 19:15]].

as many as *trade* by sea, stood afar off, [[Revelation 18.17|Revelation 18:17]].

began to *trade* one with another, [[Mosiah 24.7|Mosiah 24:7]].
