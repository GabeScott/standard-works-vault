# Creature


bring forth abundantly the moving *creature*, [[Genesis 1.20|Genesis 1:20]].

preach the gospel to every *creature*, [[Mark 16.15|Mark 16:15]].

served the *creature* more than the Creator, [[Romans 1.25|Romans 1:25]].

the *creature* itself also shall be delivered, [[Romans 8.21|Romans 8:21]].

if any man be in Christ, he is a new *creature*, [[2 Corinthians 5.17|2 Corinthians 5:17]].

nor uncircumcision, but a new *creature*, [[Galatians 6.15|Galatians 6:15]].

preached to every *creature* which is under heaven, [[Colossians 1.23|Colossians 1:23]].

every *creature* of God is good, [[1 Timothy 4.4|1 Timothy 4:4]].

every *creature* which is in heaven, [[Revelation 5.13|Revelation 5:13]].
