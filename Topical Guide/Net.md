hunt every man his brother with a *net*, [[Micah 7.2|Micah 7:2]].

kingdom of heaven is like unto a *net*, [[Matthew 13.47|Matthew 13:47]].

Master … at thy word I will let down the *net*, [[Luke 5.5|Luke 5:5]].
