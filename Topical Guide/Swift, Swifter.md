# Swift, Swifter


feet that be *swift* in running to mischief, [[Proverbs 6.18|Proverbs 6:18]].

race is not to the *swift*, [[Ecclesiastes 9.11|Ecclesiastes 9:11]].

horses are *swifter* than eagles, [[Jeremiah 4.13|Jeremiah 4:13]].

flight shall perish from the *swift*, [[Amos 2.14|Amos 2:14]].

Their feet are *swift* to shed blood, [[Romans 3.15|Romans 3:15]].

let every man be *swift* to hear, [[James 1.19|James 1:19]].

Ye are *swift* to do iniquity, [[1 Nephi 17.45|1 Nephi 17:45]].

not needful for this whole company ... to be moving *swiftly* upon the waters, [[D&C 61.3|D&C 61:3]].
