# Proceed

man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that *proceedeth* out of the mouth of the Lord, [[Deuteronomy 8.3|Deuteronomy 8:3]] ([[Matthew 4.4|Matthew 4:4]]; [[D&C 84.44|D&C 84:44]]; [[D&C 98.11|D&C 98:11]]).

out of the heart *proceed* evil thoughts, [[Matthew 15.19|Matthew 15:19]] ([[Mark 7.21|Mark 7:21]]).

Spirit of truth, which *proceedeth* from the Father, [[John 15.26|John 15:26]].

Out of the same mouth *proceedeth* blessing and cursing, [[James 3.10|James 3:10]].

book *proceeded* forth from the mouth of a Jew, [[1 Nephi 13.24|1 Nephi 13:24]] ([[1 Nephi 14.23|1 Nephi 14:23]]).

God will *proceed* to do a marvelous work among the Gentiles, [[1 Nephi 22.8|1 Nephi 22:8]] ([[2 Nephi 25.17|2 Nephi 25:17]]; [[2 Nephi 27.26|2 Nephi 27:26]]; [[2 Nephi 29.1|2 Nephi 29:1]]).

out of small things *proceedeth* that which is great, [[D&C 64.33|D&C 64:33]].

light *proceedeth* forth from the presence of God, [[D&C 88.12|D&C 88:12]].
