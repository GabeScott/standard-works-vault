# Lend, Lent


*lent* unto them such things as they required, [[Exodus 12.36|Exodus 12:36]].

*lend* money to any of my people that is poor, [[Exodus 22.25|Exodus 22:25]].

thou shalt *lend* unto many nations, [[Deuteronomy 15.6|Deuteronomy 15:6]].

*lend* him sufficient for his need, [[Deuteronomy 15.8|Deuteronomy 15:8]].

unto thy brother thou shalt not *lend* upon usury, [[Deuteronomy 23.20|Deuteronomy 23:20]].

When thou dost *lend* thy brother any thing, [[Deuteronomy 24.10|Deuteronomy 24:10]].

shall *lend* to thee, and thou shalt not *lend* to him, [[Deuteronomy 28.44|Deuteronomy 28:44]].

hath pity upon the poor *lendeth* unto the Lord, [[Proverbs 19.17|Proverbs 19:17]].

neither *lent* on usury, nor men have *lent* to me, [[Jeremiah 15.10|Jeremiah 15:10]].

if ye *lend* to them of whom ye hope to receive, [[Luke 6.34|Luke 6:34]].

say unto him, Friend, *lend* me three loaves, [[Luke 11.5|Luke 11:5]].

blessed are those who ... *lend* an ear unto my counsel, [[2 Nephi 28.30|2 Nephi 28:30]].

serve him who has created you from the beginning ... by *lending* you breath, [[Mosiah 2.21|Mosiah 2:21]].

every man ... would not borrow neither would he *lend*, [[Ether 14.2|Ether 14:2]].
