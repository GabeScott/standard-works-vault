# Salvation of Little Children


*See also* [[Children.md|Children]]; [[Conceived in Sin.md|Conceived in Sin]]

Except ye ... become as little *children*, ye shall not enter into the kingdom, [[Matthew 18.3|Matthew 18:3]].

little *children* ... Christ atoneth for their sins, [[Mosiah 3.16|Mosiah 3:16]].

little *children* also have eternal life, [[Mosiah 15.25|Mosiah 15:25]].

all little *children* are alive in Christ, [[Moroni 8.22|Moroni 8:22]].

unless he has arrived unto the years of *accountability*, [[D&C 20.71|D&C 20:71]].

*children* are redeemed ... through mine Only Begotten, [[D&C 29.46|D&C 29:46]].

*children* shall grow up without sin unto salvation, [[D&C 45.58|D&C 45:58]].

*children* shall be baptized ... when eight years old, [[D&C 68.27|D&C 68:27]] ([[D&C 68.25|68:25-28]][[D&C 68.26|]][[D&C 68.27|]][[D&C 68.28|]]).

*children* are holy, being sanctified through the atonement, [[D&C 74.7|D&C 74:7]].

men became again, in their *infant* state, innocent, [[D&C 93.38|D&C 93:38]].

all *children* who die before ... accountability are saved in the celestial kingdom, [[D&C 137.10|D&C 137:10]].

*children* ... are whole from the foundation of the world, [[Moses 6.54|Moses 6:54]].

*See also* [[Matthew 19.14|Matthew 19:14]]; [[Mark 9.37|Mark 9:37]]; [[Mark 10.16|Mark 10:16]].
