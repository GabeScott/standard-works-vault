# Author

God is not the *author* of confusion, [[1 Corinthians 14.33|1 Corinthians 14:33]].

became the *author* of eternal salvation, [[Hebrews 5.9|Hebrews 5:9]].

unto Jesus the *author* and finisher of our faith, [[Hebrews 12.2|Hebrews 12:2]] ([[Moroni 6.4|Moroni 6:4]]).

it is he who is the *author* of all sin, [[Helaman 6.30|Helaman 6:30]].
