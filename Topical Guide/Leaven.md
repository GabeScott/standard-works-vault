# Leaven


*See also* [[Example, Ensample.md|Example]]; [[Jesus Christ, Exemplar.md|Jesus Christ, Exemplar]]; [BD Leaven](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/leaven?lang=eng)

Seven days shall ye eat *unleavened* bread, [[Exodus 12.15|Exodus 12:15]].

neither shall there be *leaven* seen with thee, [[Exodus 13.7|Exodus 13:7]].

not offer the blood of my sacrifice with *leaven*, [[Exodus 34.25|Exodus 34:25]] ([[Leviticus 2.11|Leviticus 2:11]]).

It shall not be baken with *leaven*, [[Leviticus 6.17|Leviticus 6:17]] ([[Leviticus 23.17|Leviticus 23:17]]).

Thou shalt eat no *leavened* bread with it, [[Deuteronomy 16.3|Deuteronomy 16:3]].

offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving with *leaven*, [[Amos 4.5|Amos 4:5]].

kingdom of heaven is like unto *leaven*, [[Matthew 13.33|Matthew 13:33]] ([[Luke 13.21|Luke 13:21]]).

Take heed and beware of the *leaven* of the Pharisees, [[Matthew 16.6|Matthew 16:6]] ([[Mark 8.15|Mark 8:15]]; [[Luke 12.1|Luke 12:1]]).

a little *leaven* leaveneth the whole lump, [[1 Corinthians 5.6|1 Corinthians 5:6]] ([[Galatians 5.9|Galatians 5:9]]).

let us keep the feast, not with old *leaven*, [[1 Corinthians 5.8|1 Corinthians 5:8]].
