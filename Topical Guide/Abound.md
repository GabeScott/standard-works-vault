# Abound


because iniquity shall *abound*, the love of many shall wax cold, [[Matthew 24.12|Matthew 24:12]] ([[D&C 45.27|D&C 45:27]]; [[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.30|Joseph Smith Matthew 1:30]]).

where sin *abounded*, grace did much more *abound*, [[Romans 5.20|Romans 5:20]].

Shall we continue in sin, that grace may *abound*, [[Romans 6.1|Romans 6:1]].

peace in believing, that ye may *abound* in hope, [[Romans 15.13|Romans 15:13]].

always *abounding* in the work of the Lord, [[1 Corinthians 15.58|1 Corinthians 15:58]].

sufferings of Christ *abound* in us, so our consolation also *aboundeth*, [[2 Corinthians 1.5|2 Corinthians 1:5]].

as ye *abound* in every thing, ... see that ye *abound* in this grace, [[2 Corinthians 8.7|2 Corinthians 8:7]].

God is able to make all grace *abound* toward you, [[2 Corinthians 9.8|2 Corinthians 9:8]].

*abounded* toward us in all wisdom and prudence, [[Ephesians 1.8|Ephesians 1:8]].

in the faith, ... *abounding* therein with thanksgiving, [[Colossians 2.7|Colossians 2:7]].

*abound* in love one toward another, [[1 Thessalonians 3.12|1 Thessalonians 3:12]].

if these things be in you, and *abound*, [[2 Peter 1.8|2 Peter 1:8]] ([[D&C 107.31|D&C 107:31]]).

always *abound* in good works, [[Alma 7.24|Alma 7:24]] ([[Mosiah 5.15|Mosiah 5:15]]; [[Ether 12.4|Ether 12:4]]).
