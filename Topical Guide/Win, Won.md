# Win, Won


he that *winneth* souls is wise, [[Proverbs 11.30|Proverbs 11:30]].

count them but dung, that I may *win* Christ, [[Philippians 3.8|Philippians 3:8]].

*won* by the conversation of the wives, [[1 Peter 3.1|1 Peter 3:1]].

be diligent, that thereby he might *win* the prize, [[Mosiah 4.27|Mosiah 4:27]].

show forth my power ... that I may *win* the hearts, [[Alma 17.29|Alma 17:29]].
