# Sluggard

*See also* [[Slothful, Slothfulness.md|Slothful, Slothfulness]]

Go to the ant, thou *sluggard*; consider her ways, [[Proverbs 6.6|Proverbs 6:6]].

as smoke to the eyes, so is the *sluggard*, [[Proverbs 10.26|Proverbs 10:26]].

soul of the *sluggard* desireth, and hath nothing, [[Proverbs 13.4|Proverbs 13:4]].

*sluggard* will not plow by reason of the cold, [[Proverbs 20.4|Proverbs 20:4]].

*sluggard* is wiser in his own conceit than seven, [[Proverbs 26.16|Proverbs 26:16]].
