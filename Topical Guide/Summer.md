# Summer


While the earth remaineth ... *summer* and winter ... shall not cease, [[Genesis 8.22|Genesis 8:22]].

thou hast made *summer* and winter, [[Psalms 74.17|Psalms 74:17]].

Provideth her meat in the *summer*, [[Proverbs 6.8|Proverbs 6:8]].

He that gathereth in *summer* is a wise son, [[Proverbs 10.5|Proverbs 10:5]].

*summer* is ended, and we are not saved, [[Jeremiah 8.20|Jeremiah 8:20]] ([[D&C 56.16|D&C 56:16]]).

know that *summer* is nigh, [[Matthew 24.32|Matthew 24:32]] ([[Mark 13.28|Mark 13:28]]; [[Luke 21.30|Luke 21:30]]; [[D&C 35.16|D&C 35:16]]; [[D&C 45.37|D&C 45:37]]; [[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.38|Joseph Smith Matthew 1:38]]).

I am calm as a *summer's* morning, [[D&C 135.4|D&C 135:4]].
