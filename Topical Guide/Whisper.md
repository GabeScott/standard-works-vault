# Whisper

*whisperer* separateth chief friends, [[Proverbs 16.28|Proverbs 16:28]].

thy speech shall *whisper* out of the dust, [[Isaiah 29.4|Isaiah 29:4]] ([[2 Nephi 26.16|2 Nephi 26:16]]).

Being filled with all unrighteousness … *whisperers*, [[Romans 1.29|Romans 1:29]].

thus he *whispereth* in their ears, [[2 Nephi 28.22|2 Nephi 28:22]].

I do this for a wise purpose; for thus it *whispereth* me, [[Words of Mormon 1.7|Words of Mormon 1:7]].

still voice … as if it had been a *whisper*, [[Helaman 5.30|Helaman 5:30]] ([[Helaman 5.46|Helaman 5:46]]).

still small voice, which *whispereth* through and pierceth all things, [[D&C 85.6|D&C 85:6]].
