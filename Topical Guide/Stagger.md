# Stagger


They ... *stagger* like a drunken man, [[Psalms 107.27|Psalms 107:27]].

as a drunken man *staggereth* in his vomit, [[Isaiah 19.14|Isaiah 19:14]].

they *stagger*, but not with strong drink, [[Isaiah 29.9|Isaiah 29:9]] ([[2 Nephi 27.4|2 Nephi 27:4]]).

He *staggered* not at the promise of God, [[Romans 4.20|Romans 4:20]].
