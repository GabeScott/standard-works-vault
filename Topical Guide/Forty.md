# Forty


I will cause it to rain upon the earth *forty* days and *forty* nights, [[Genesis 7.4|Genesis 7:4]].

children of Israel did eat manna *forty* years, [[Exodus 16.35|Exodus 16:35]].

in the mount *forty* days and *forty* nights, [[Exodus 24.18|Exodus 24:18]] ([[Deuteronomy 9.9|Deuteronomy 9:9]]).

shall wander in the wilderness *forty* years, [[Numbers 14.33|Numbers 14:33]].

*Forty* stripes he may give him, and not exceed, [[Deuteronomy 25.3|Deuteronomy 25:3]].

*forty* days, and Nineveh shall be overthrown, [[Jonah 3.4|Jonah 3:4]].

fasted *forty* days and *forty* nights, [[Matthew 4.2|Matthew 4:2]].

Being *forty* days tempted of the devil, [[Luke 4.2|Luke 4:2]].

being seen of them *forty* days, [[Acts 1.3|Acts 1:3]].

five times received I *forty* stripes, [[2 Corinthians 11.24|2 Corinthians 11:24]].
