# Priest, Melchizedek Priesthood


*See also* [[High Priest, Melchizedek Priesthood.md|High Priest, Melchizedek Priesthood]]; [[Priest, Aaronic Priesthood.md|Priest, Aaronic Priesthood]]; [[Priesthood, Melchizedek.md|Priesthood, Melchizedek]]

Melchizedek ... *priest* of the most high God, [[Genesis 14.18|Genesis 14:18]] ([[Hebrews 7.1|Hebrews 7:1]]).

ye shall be unto me a kingdom of *priests*, [[Exodus 19.6|Exodus 19:6]].

I will raise me up a faithful *priest*, [[1 Samuel 2.35|1 Samuel 2:35]].

Thou art a *priest* for ever after the order of Melchizedek, [[Psalms 110.4|Psalms 110:4]] ([[Hebrews 5.6|Hebrews 5:6]]; [[Hebrews 7.17|Hebrews 7:17]], [[Hebrews 7.21|Hebrews 7:21]]).

he shall be a *priest* upon his throne, [[Zechariah 6.13|Zechariah 6:13]].

if ... he should not be a *priest*, [[Hebrews 8.4|Hebrews 8:4]].

made us kings and *priests* unto God and his Father, [[Revelation 1.6|Revelation 1:6]] ([[Revelation 5.10|Revelation 5:10]]).

they shall be *priests* of God and of Christ, [[Revelation 20.6|Revelation 20:6]].

Jacob and Joseph ... *priests* and teachers over the land of my people, [[2 Nephi 5.26|2 Nephi 5:26]] ([[Jacob 1.18|Jacob 1:18]]; [[Jarom 1.11|Jarom 1:11]]; [[Mosiah 6.3|Mosiah 6:3]]; [[Mosiah 23.17|Mosiah 23:17]]; [[Alma 15.13|Alma 15:13]]; [[Alma 45.22|Alma 45:22]]; [[Moroni 3.1|Moroni 3:1]], [[Moroni 3.4|Moroni 3:4]]).

Alma ... ordained *priests*, [[Mosiah 18.18|Mosiah 18:18]].

*priests* whom he had ordained should labor, [[Mosiah 18.24|Mosiah 18:24]].

*priests* were not to depend upon the people for their support, [[Mosiah 18.26|Mosiah 18:26]].

impart of their substance ... to those *priests* that stood in need, [[Mosiah 18.28|Mosiah 18:28]].

he consecrated all their *priests*, [[Mosiah 23.17|Mosiah 23:17]].

every church having their *priests* ... and every *priest* preaching the word, [[Mosiah 25.21|Mosiah 25:21]].

remember that the Lord God ordained *priests*, after his holy order, [[Alma 13.1|Alma 13:1]].
