# Devilish

they are carnal and *devilish*, [[Mosiah 16.3|Mosiah 16:3]].

restoration is to bring back … *devilish* for *devilish*, [[Alma 41.13|Alma 41:13]] ([[Alma 42.10|Alma 42:10]]).

how evil, and *devilish* … are the children of men, [[Helaman 12.4|Helaman 12:4]].

by the transgression of these holy laws man became sensual and *devilish*, [[D&C 20.20|D&C 20:20]].

men began … to be carnal, sensual, and *devilish*, [[Moses 5.13|Moses 5:13]].
