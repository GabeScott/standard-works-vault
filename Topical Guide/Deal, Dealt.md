# Deal, Dealt


let us *deal* wisely with them, [[Exodus 1.10|Exodus 1:10]].

*deal* kindly and truly with thee, [[Joshua 2.14|Joshua 2:14]].

treacherous dealer *dealeth* treacherously, [[Isaiah 21.2|Isaiah 21:2]] ([[Isaiah 24.16|Isaiah 24:16]]).

Is it not to *deal* thy bread to the hungry, [[Isaiah 58.7|Isaiah 58:7]].

from the prophet even unto the priest every one *dealeth* falsely, [[Jeremiah 6.13|Jeremiah 6:13]] ([[Jeremiah 8.10|Jeremiah 8:10]]).

hath *dealt* to every man the measure of faith, [[Romans 12.3|Romans 12:3]].
