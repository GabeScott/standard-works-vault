consulter with *familiar* spirits, [[Deuteronomy 18.11|Deuteronomy 18:11]].

put away those that had *familiar* spirits, [[1 Samuel 28.3|1 Samuel 28:3]].

Seek me a woman that hath a *familiar* spirit, [[1 Samuel 28.7|1 Samuel 28:7]] ([[1 Chronicles 10.13|1 Chronicles 10:13]]).

dealt with *familiar* spirits, [[2 Kings 21.6|2 Kings 21:6]] ([[2 Chronicles 33.6|2 Chronicles 33:6]]).

be *familiar* with all, [[Jacob 2.17|Jacob 2:17]].
