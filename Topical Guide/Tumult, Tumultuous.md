# Tumult, Tumultuous

What meaneth the noise of this *tumult*, [[1 Samuel 4.14|1 Samuel 4:14]].

Pilate saw … that rather a *tumult* was made, [[Matthew 27.24|Matthew 27:24]].

in imprisonments, in *tumults*, in labours, [[2 Corinthians 6.5|2 Corinthians 6:5]].

lest there be … swellings, *tumults*, [[2 Corinthians 12.20|2 Corinthians 12:20]].

I heard … all manner of *tumultuous* noises, [[1 Nephi 12.4|1 Nephi 12:4]].

neither was it a voice of a great *tumultuous* noise, [[Helaman 5.30|Helaman 5:30]].

*tumultuous* noises did pass away, [[3 Nephi 10.9|3 Nephi 10:9]].

no envyings, nor strifes, nor *tumults*, [[4 Nephi 1.16|4 Nephi 1:16]].
