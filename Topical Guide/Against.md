# Against

He that is not with me is *against* me, [[Matthew 12.30|Matthew 12:30]] ([[Luke 11.23|Luke 11:23]]).

that is not *against* us is on our part, [[Mark 9.40|Mark 9:40]] ([[Luke 9.50|Luke 9:50]]).

we know that every where it is spoken *against*, [[Acts 28.22|Acts 28:22]].

If God be for us, who can be *against* us, [[Romans 8.31|Romans 8:31]].

they who are not for me are *against* me, [[2 Nephi 10.16|2 Nephi 10:16]].
