# Blow

Thou didst *blow* with thy wind, [[Exodus 15.10|Exodus 15:10]].

priests that *blew* with the trumpets, [[Joshua 6.9|Joshua 6:9]].

great trumpet shall be *blown*, [[Isaiah 27.13|Isaiah 27:13]].

spirit of the Lord *bloweth* upon it, [[Isaiah 40.7|Isaiah 40:7]].

wind *bloweth* where it listeth, [[John 3.8|John 3:8]].

wind should not *blow* on the earth, [[Revelation 7.1|Revelation 7:1]].
