# Faint

Esau came from the field, and he was *faint*, [[Genesis 25.29|Genesis 25:29]].

If thou *faint* in the day of adversity, [[Proverbs 24.10|Proverbs 24:10]].

whole heart *faint*, [[Isaiah 1.5|Isaiah 1:5]].

walk, and not *faint*, [[Isaiah 40.31|Isaiah 40:31]] ([[D&C 89.20|D&C 89:20]]).

fair virgins and young men *faint* for thirst, [[Amos 8.13|Amos 8:13]].

compassion on them, because they *fainted*, [[Matthew 9.36|Matthew 9:36]].

not send them away fasting, lest they *faint*, [[Matthew 15.32|Matthew 15:32]] ([[Mark 8.3|Mark 8:3]]).

ought always to pray, and not to *faint*, [[Luke 18.1|Luke 18:1]] ([[2 Nephi 32.9|2 Nephi 32:9]]; [[D&C 75.11|D&C 75:11]]; [[D&C 88.126|D&C 88:126]]).

we shall reap, if we *faint* not, [[Galatians 6.9|Galatians 6:9]].

that ye *faint* not at my tribulations, [[Ephesians 3.13|Ephesians 3:13]].

lest ye be wearied and *faint* in your minds, [[Hebrews 12.3|Hebrews 12:3]].

nor *faint* when thou art rebuked, [[Hebrews 12.5|Hebrews 12:5]].
