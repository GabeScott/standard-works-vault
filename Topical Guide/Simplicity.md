# Simplicity


How long, ye simple ones, will ye love *simplicity*, [[Proverbs 1.22|Proverbs 1:22]].

he that giveth, let him do it with *simplicity*, [[Romans 12.8|Romans 12:8]].

testimony of our conscience, that in *simplicity* and godly sincerity, [[2 Corinthians 1.12|2 Corinthians 1:12]].

be corrupted from the *simplicity* that is in Christ, [[2 Corinthians 11.3|2 Corinthians 11:3]].

reasoning in plainness and *simplicity*, [[D&C 133.57|D&C 133:57]].
