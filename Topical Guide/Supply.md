# Supply


that which was lacking on your part they have *supplied*, [[1 Corinthians 16.17|1 Corinthians 16:17]].

your abundance may be a *supply*, [[2 Corinthians 8.14|2 Corinthians 8:14]].

this service not only *supplieth* the want of the saints, [[2 Corinthians 9.12|2 Corinthians 9:12]].

the brethren which came from Macedonia *supplied*, [[2 Corinthians 11.9|2 Corinthians 11:9]].

to *supply* your lack of service toward me, [[Philippians 2.30|Philippians 2:30]].

my God shall *supply* all your need, [[Philippians 4.19|Philippians 4:19]].

who has need may be amply *supplied*, [[D&C 42.33|D&C 42:33]].
