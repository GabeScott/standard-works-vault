power of God, by the *armour* of righteousness, [[2 Corinthians 6.7|2 Corinthians 6:7]].

Put on the whole *armour* of God, [[Ephesians 6.11|Ephesians 6:11]].

put on the *armor* of righteousness, [[2 Nephi 1.23|2 Nephi 1:23]].

take upon you my whole *armor*, [[D&C 27.15|D&C 27:15]].
