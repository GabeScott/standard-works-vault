# Follower


be ye *followers* of me, [[1 Corinthians 4.16|1 Corinthians 4:16]] ([[1 Corinthians 11.1|1 Corinthians 11:1]]; [[Philippians 3.17|Philippians 3:17]]).

Be ye therefore *followers* of God, as dear children, [[Ephesians 5.1|Ephesians 5:1]].

ye became *followers* of us, and of the Lord, [[1 Thessalonians 1.6|1 Thessalonians 1:6]].

*followers* of them who ... inherit the promises, [[Hebrews 6.12|Hebrews 6:12]].

if ye be *followers* of that which is good, [[1 Peter 3.13|1 Peter 3:13]].

true *followers* of his Son ... shall be like him, [[Moroni 7.48|Moroni 7:48]].

having been myself a *follower* of righteousness, [[Abraham 1.2|Abraham 1:2]].
