# Travel


days of Jael ... *travellers* walked through byways, [[Judges 5.6|Judges 5:6]].

kingdom of heaven is as a man *travelling* into a far country, [[Matthew 25.14|Matthew 25:14]].

*traveling* bishops ... may have the privilege of ordaining, [[D&C 20.66|D&C 20:66]].

Son of Man cometh not in the form of ... a man *traveling* on the earth, [[D&C 49.22|D&C 49:22]].

high priests should *travel*, [[D&C 84.111|D&C 84:111]].

Twelve are a *Traveling* Presiding High Council, [[D&C 107.33|D&C 107:33]] ([[D&C 124.127|D&C 124:127]]).

presidency ... designed for those who do not *travel* into all the world, [[D&C 107.90|D&C 107:90]].

seventy are to be *traveling* ministers, [[D&C 107.97|D&C 107:97]].

other officers ... are not under the responsibility to *travel* among all nations, [[D&C 107.98|D&C 107:98]].

difference between this quorum and the quorum of elders is that one is to *travel*, [[D&C 124.140|D&C 124:140]].
