# Beware


*Beware* of him, and obey his voice, [[Exodus 23.21|Exodus 23:21]].

*beware* lest thou forget the Lord, [[Deuteronomy 6.12|Deuteronomy 6:12]].

*Beware* of false prophets, [[Matthew 7.15|Matthew 7:15]] ([[3 Nephi 14.15|3 Nephi 14:15]]).

*beware* of men: for they will deliver you up, [[Matthew 10.17|Matthew 10:17]].

*beware* of the leaven, [[Matthew 16.6|Matthew 16:6]] ([[Mark 8.15|Mark 8:15]]; [[Luke 12.1|Luke 12:1]]).

*Beware* of the scribes, [[Mark 12.38|Mark 12:38]].

*beware* of covetousness, [[Luke 12.15|Luke 12:15]].

*Beware* of dogs, *beware* of evil workers, [[Philippians 3.2|Philippians 3:2]].

*Beware* lest any man spoil you through philosophy, [[Colossians 2.8|Colossians 2:8]].

*beware* of pride, [[D&C 23.1|D&C 23:1]] ([[D&C 38.39|D&C 38:39]]).

*beware* lest ye are deceived, [[D&C 46.8|D&C 46:8]].

let all men *beware* how they take my name, [[D&C 63.61|D&C 63:61]] ([[D&C 63.61|63:61-64]][[D&C 63.62|]][[D&C 63.63|]][[D&C 63.64|]]).
