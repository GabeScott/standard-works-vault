# Bite

Dan shall be a serpent … that *biteth* the horse heels, [[Genesis 49.17|Genesis 49:17]].

fiery serpents … *bit* the people, [[Numbers 21.6|Numbers 21:6]].

At the last it *biteth* like a serpent, [[Proverbs 23.32|Proverbs 23:32]].

send serpents … and they shall *bite* you, [[Jeremiah 8.17|Jeremiah 8:17]].

hand on the wall, and a serpent *bit* him, [[Amos 5.19|Amos 5:19]] ([[Amos 9.3|Amos 9:3]]).

prophets … that *bite* with their teeth, [[Micah 3.5|Micah 3:5]].

if ye *bite* and devour one another, [[Galatians 5.15|Galatians 5:15]].
