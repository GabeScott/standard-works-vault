# Mission of Early Saints


*See also* [[Israel, Mission of.md|Israel, Mission of]]

in thee shall all families of the earth be *blessed*, [[Genesis 12.3|Genesis 12:3]] ([[Galatians 3.8|Galatians 3:8]]; [[Abraham 2.11|Abraham 2:11]]).

in thy seed shall all the nations of the earth be *blessed*, [[Genesis 22.18|Genesis 22:18]] ([[D&C 110.12|D&C 110:12]]).

Ye are the *salt* of the earth, [[Matthew 5.13|Matthew 5:13]] ([[3 Nephi 12.13|3 Nephi 12:13]]).

Ye are the *light* of the world, [[Matthew 5.14|Matthew 5:14]].

Be ye therefore *perfect*, [[Matthew 5.48|Matthew 5:48]] ([[3 Nephi 12.48|3 Nephi 12:48]]).

seek ye first the *kingdom* of God, [[Matthew 6.33|Matthew 6:33]] ([[3 Nephi 13.33|3 Nephi 13:33]]).

Go ye therefore, and *teach* all nations, baptizing, [[Matthew 28.19|Matthew 28:19]].

repentance ... should be *preached* in his name among all nations, [[Luke 24.47|Luke 24:47]].

go and bring forth *fruit*, [[John 15.16|John 15:16]].

Ye are the children ... of the *covenant* which God made with our fathers, [[Acts 3.25|Acts 3:25]] ([[3 Nephi 20.25|3 Nephi 20:25]]).

called to be *saints*, [[1 Corinthians 1.2|1 Corinthians 1:2]].

we ... as Isaac was, are the children of *promise*, [[Galatians 4.28|Galatians 4:28]].

put on the *new* man, [[Ephesians 4.24|Ephesians 4:24]].

perverse nation, among whom ye *shine* as lights, [[Philippians 2.15|Philippians 2:15]].

royal *priesthood*, an holy nation, a peculiar people, [[1 Peter 2.9|1 Peter 2:9]].

*persuade* our children, and also our brethren, to believe in Christ, [[2 Nephi 25.23|2 Nephi 25:23]].

*teach* them the word of God, [[Jacob 1.19|Jacob 1:19]] ([[Alma 38.10|Alma 38:10]]).

*Preach* unto them repentance, and faith, [[Alma 37.33|Alma 37:33]].

Go ye into all the world, and *preach* the gospel, [[Mormon 9.22|Mormon 9:22]].
