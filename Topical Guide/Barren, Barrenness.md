# Barren, Barrenness


Sarai was *barren*, [[Genesis 11.30|Genesis 11:30]].

Isaac entreated the Lord for his wife, because she was *barren*, [[Genesis 25.21|Genesis 25:21]].

Rachel was *barren*, [[Genesis 29.31|Genesis 29:31]].

There shall nothing ... be *barren*, in thy land, [[Exodus 23.26|Exodus 23:26]].

not be male or female *barren*, [[Deuteronomy 7.14|Deuteronomy 7:14]].

thou art *barren*, [[Judges 13.3|Judges 13:3]].

Hannah had *no children*, [[1 Samuel 1.2|1 Samuel 1:2]].

the *barren* hath born seven, [[1 Samuel 2.5|1 Samuel 2:5]].

there shall not be ... *barren* land, [[2 Kings 2.21|2 Kings 2:21]].

*barren* woman ... to be a joyful mother, [[Psalms 113.9|Psalms 113:9]].

Sing, O *barren*, thou that didst not bear, [[Isaiah 54.1|Isaiah 54:1]].

they had no child, because that Elisabeth was *barren*, [[Luke 1.7|Luke 1:7]].

Blessed are the *barren*, [[Luke 23.29|Luke 23:29]].

Rejoice, thou *barren* that bearest not, [[Galatians 4.27|Galatians 4:27]].

neither be *barren* ... in the knowledge of our Lord, [[2 Peter 1.8|2 Peter 1:8]].

because your ground is *barren* ... ye cannot have the fruit thereof, [[Alma 32.39|Alma 32:39]].

in the *barren* deserts there shall come forth pools, [[D&C 133.29|D&C 133:29]].

*barrenness* thereof shall go forth, [[Moses 7.8|Moses 7:8]].

*See also* [[Genesis 30.23|Genesis 30:23]]; [[Luke 1.25|Luke 1:25]].
