# Defraud


Thou shalt not *defraud* thy neighbour, [[Leviticus 19.13|Leviticus 19:13]].

whom have I *defrauded*, [[1 Samuel 12.3|1 Samuel 12:3]].

Do not bear false witness, *Defraud* not, [[Mark 10.19|Mark 10:19]].

rather suffer yourselves to be *defrauded*, [[1 Corinthians 6.7|1 Corinthians 6:7]].

*Defraud* ye not one the other, [[1 Corinthians 7.5|1 Corinthians 7:5]].
