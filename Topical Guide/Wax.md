# Wax [noun]

my heart is like *wax*, [[Psalms 22.14|Psalms 22:14]].

as *wax* melteth before the fire, so let the wicked perish, [[Psalms 68.2|Psalms 68:2]].

hills melted like *wax* at the presence of the Lord, [[Psalms 97.5|Psalms 97:5]].

valleys shall be cleft, as *wax* before the fire, [[Micah 1.4|Micah 1:4]].
