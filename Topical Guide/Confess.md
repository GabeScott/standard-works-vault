# Confess

*See also* [[Acknowledge.md|Acknowledge]]; [[Confession.md|Confession]]

Whosoever therefore shall *confess* me before men, him will I *confess*, [[Matthew 10.32|Matthew 10:32]] ([[Luke 12.8|Luke 12:8]]).

he *confessed* … I am not the Christ, [[John 1.20|John 1:20]].

if any man did *confess* that he was Christ, [[John 9.22|John 9:22]].

because of the Pharisees they did not *confess*, [[John 12.42|John 12:42]].

many that believed came, and *confessed*, [[Acts 19.18|Acts 19:18]].

*confess* with thy mouth the Lord … thou shalt be saved, [[Romans 10.9|Romans 10:9]].

every knee shall bow to me, and every tongue shall *confess* to God, [[Romans 14.11|Romans 14:11]] ([[Isaiah 45.23|Isaiah 45:23]]; [[Mosiah 27.31|Mosiah 27:31]]; [[D&C 76.110|D&C 76:110]]; [[D&C 88.104|D&C 88:104]]).

I will *confess* to thee among the Gentiles, [[Romans 15.9|Romans 15:9]].

Every spirit that *confesseth* that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is of God, [[1 John 4.2|1 John 4:2]].

*confess* that Jesus is the Son of God, [[1 John 4.15|1 John 4:15]].

deceivers … who *confess* not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh, [[2 John 1.7|2 John 1:7]].

I will *confess* his name before my Father, [[Revelation 3.5|Revelation 3:5]].

call on his name and *confess* their sins, [[Alma 17.4|Alma 17:4]].

*confesses* by the power of the Holy Ghost that Jesus is the Christ, [[Moroni 7.44|Moroni 7:44]].

offend God … those who *confess* not his hand, [[D&C 59.21|D&C 59:21]].
