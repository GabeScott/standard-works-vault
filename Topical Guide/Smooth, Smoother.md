he ... chose him five *smooth* stones, [[1 Samuel 17.40|1 Samuel 17:40]].

words of his mouth were *smoother* than butter, [[Psalms 55.21|Psalms 55:21]].

her mouth is *smoother* than oil, [[Proverbs 5.3|Proverbs 5:3]].

speak unto us *smooth* things, [[Isaiah 30.10|Isaiah 30:10]].

rough ways shall be made *smooth*, [[Luke 3.5|Luke 3:5]] ([[1 Nephi 17.46|1 Nephi 17:46]]; [[D&C 49.23|D&C 49:23]]; [[D&C 109.74|D&C 109:74]]).

if ye shall say unto this mountain ... become *smooth*, [[Helaman 10.9|Helaman 10:9]].

many *smooth* places became rough, [[3 Nephi 8.13|3 Nephi 8:13]] ([[Helaman 12.10|Helaman 12:10]]).
