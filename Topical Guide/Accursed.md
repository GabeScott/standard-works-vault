wish that myself were *accursed* from Christ for my brethren, [[Romans 9.3|Romans 9:3]].

no man speaking by the Spirit ... calleth Jesus *accursed*, [[1 Corinthians 12.3|1 Corinthians 12:3]].

preach any other gospel ... let him be *accursed*, [[Galatians 1.8|Galatians 1:8]].

treasure ... *accursed*, because of the iniquity of him who hath hid it, [[Helaman 12.18|Helaman 12:18]].

contend against the word of the Lord, let him be *accursed*, [[Ether 4.8|Ether 4:8]].
