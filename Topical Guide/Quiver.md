# Quiver

take … thy *quiver* and thy bow, [[Genesis 27.3|Genesis 27:3]].

Happy is the man that hath his *quiver* full, [[Psalms 127.5|Psalms 127:5]].

in his *quiver* hath he hid me, [[Isaiah 49.2|Isaiah 49:2]].

Their *quiver* is as an open sepulchre, [[Jeremiah 5.16|Jeremiah 5:16]].

my lips *quivered* at the voice, [[Habakkuk 3.16|Habakkuk 3:16]].

weapons of war—yea, the sharp pointed arrow, and the *quiver*, [[Jarom 1.8|Jarom 1:8]].
