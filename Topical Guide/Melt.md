# Melt

inhabitants of Canaan shall *melt* away, [[Exodus 15.15|Exodus 15:15]].

made the heart of the people *melt*, [[Joshua 14.8|Joshua 14:8]].

mountains *melted* from before the Lord, [[Judges 5.5|Judges 5:5]].

hills *melted* like wax at the presence of the Lord, [[Psalms 97.5|Psalms 97:5]] ([[Amos 9.13|Amos 9:13]]).

every man’s heart shall *melt*, [[Isaiah 13.7|Isaiah 13:7]] ([[2 Nephi 23.7|2 Nephi 23:7]]).

I will *melt* them, and try them, [[Jeremiah 9.7|Jeremiah 9:7]].

God … toucheth the land, and it shall *melt*, [[Amos 9.5|Amos 9:5]].

mountains quake at him, and the hills *melt*, [[Nahum 1.5|Nahum 1:5]].

elements shall *melt* with fervent heat, [[2 Peter 3.10|2 Peter 3:10]] ([[3 Nephi 26.3|3 Nephi 26:3]]; [[D&C 101.25|D&C 101:25]]).

when the Lord shall come … the elements shall *melt*, [[Mormon 9.2|Mormon 9:2]].

their prospects shall *melt* away, [[D&C 121.11|D&C 121:11]].

presence of the Lord shall be as the *melting* fire, [[D&C 133.41|D&C 133:41]].
