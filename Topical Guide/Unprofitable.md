# Unprofitable

cast ye the *unprofitable* servant into outer darkness, [[Matthew 25.30|Matthew 25:30]].

when ye shall have done all … say, We are *unprofitable* servants, [[Luke 17.10|Luke 17:10]].

they are together become *unprofitable*, [[Romans 3.12|Romans 3:12]].

avoid foolish questions … they are *unprofitable*, [[Titus 3.9|Titus 3:9]].

yet ye would be *unprofitable* servants, [[Mosiah 2.21|Mosiah 2:21]].
