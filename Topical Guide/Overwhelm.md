# Overwhelm

I complained, and my spirit was *overwhelmed*, [[Psalms 77.3|Psalms 77:3]].

sea *overwhelmed* their enemies, [[Psalms 78.53|Psalms 78:53]].

my spirit was *overwhelmed* within me, [[Psalms 142.3|Psalms 142:3]].

sleep had fled from my eyes, and I lay *overwhelmed*, [[Joseph Smith History 1.46|Joseph Smith History 1:46]].
