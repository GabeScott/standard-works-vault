# Sport


Isaac was *sporting* with Rebekah, [[Genesis 26.8|Genesis 26:8]].

Call for Samson, that he may make us *sport*, [[Judges 16.25|Judges 16:25]].

It is as *sport* to a fool to do mischief, [[Proverbs 10.23|Proverbs 10:23]].

*sporting* themselves with their own deceivings, [[2 Peter 2.13|2 Peter 2:13]].
