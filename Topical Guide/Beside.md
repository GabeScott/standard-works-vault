# Beside

*beside* me there is no saviour, [[Isaiah 43.11|Isaiah 43:11]] ([[Hosea 13.4|Hosea 13:4]]; [[D&C 76.1|D&C 76:1]]).

He is *beside* himself, [[Mark 3.21|Mark 3:21]].

Festus said … Paul, thou art *beside* thyself, [[Acts 26.24|Acts 26:24]].

whether we be *beside* ourselves, it is to God, [[2 Corinthians 5.13|2 Corinthians 5:13]].

there is no God *beside* me, [[Moses 1.6|Moses 1:6]].
