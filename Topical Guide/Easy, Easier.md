# Easy, Easier


knowledge is *easy* unto him, [[Proverbs 14.6|Proverbs 14:6]].

whether is *easier*, to say, [[Matthew 9.5|Matthew 9:5]] ([[Mark 2.9|Mark 2:9]]; [[Luke 5.23|Luke 5:23]]).

my yoke is *easy*, [[Matthew 11.30|Matthew 11:30]].

*easier* for a camel to go through the eye of a needle, [[Matthew 19.24|Matthew 19:24]] ([[Mark 10.25|Mark 10:25]]; [[Luke 18.25|Luke 18:25]]).

*easier* for heaven and earth to pass, [[Luke 16.17|Luke 16:17]].

wisdom that is from above is ... *easy* to be entreated, [[James 3.17|James 3:17]].

things which were written were ... *easy* to the understanding, [[1 Nephi 14.23|1 Nephi 14:23]].

ye should be ... *easy* to be entreated, [[Alma 7.23|Alma 7:23]].

*easy* to give heed to the word of Christ, [[Alma 37.44|Alma 37:44]].

not *easy* for him to obtain a forgiveness, [[Alma 39.6|Alma 39:6]].
