If any man among you *seem* to be religious, [[James 1.26|James 1:26]].

in the mouth of as many witnesses as *seemeth* him good, [[2 Nephi 27.14|2 Nephi 27:14]].

they shall be cut off ... as *seemeth* me good, [[D&C 52.6|D&C 52:6]].

Lord, command and revoke, as it *seemeth* me good, [[D&C 56.4|D&C 56:4]].
