# Ministry, Unpaid


*See also* [[Priestcraft.md|Priestcraft]]

freely ye have received, *freely* give, [[Matthew 10.8|Matthew 10:8]].

these hands have ministered unto my *necessities*, [[Acts 20.34|Acts 20:34]].

*working* with our own hands, [[1 Corinthians 4.12|1 Corinthians 4:12]].

I preach ... the gospel of Christ without *charge*, [[1 Corinthians 9.18|1 Corinthians 9:18]].

we ... wrought with labour ... not be *chargeable* to any of you, [[2 Thessalonians 3.8|2 Thessalonians 3:8]].

Feed the flock ... not for filthy *lucre*, [[1 Peter 5.2|1 Peter 5:2]].

commandeth that there shall be no *priestcrafts* ... may get gain, [[2 Nephi 26.29|2 Nephi 26:29]].

if they labor for *money* they shall perish, [[2 Nephi 26.31|2 Nephi 26:31]].

priests were not to *depend* upon the people for their support, [[Mosiah 18.26|Mosiah 18:26]].

when the priest had imparted unto them the word of God they all *returned* ... unto their labors, [[Alma 1.26|Alma 1:26]].
