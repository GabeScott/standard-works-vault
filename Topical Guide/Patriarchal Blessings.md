# Patriarchal Blessings


*See also* [[Blessing.md|Blessing]]; [[Patriarch.md|Patriarch]]

Isaac had made an end of *blessing* Jacob, [[Genesis 27.30|Genesis 27:30]].

Isaac called Jacob, and *blessed* him, [[Genesis 28.1|Genesis 28:1]].

Israel stretched out his right hand, and *laid* it upon Ephraim's head, [[Genesis 48.14|Genesis 48:14]].

he *blessed* Joseph, [[Genesis 48.15|Genesis 48:15]].

twelve tribes of Israel ... their father ... *blessed* them, [[Genesis 49.28|Genesis 49:28]].

Hyrum may take the office of Priesthood and Patriarch, which was appointed unto him by his father, by *blessing*, [[D&C 124.91|D&C 124:91]].

he shall hold the keys of the *patriarchal blessings*, [[D&C 124.92|D&C 124:92]].

Hyrum Smith to be a patriarch unto you, to hold the sealing *blessings* of my church, [[D&C 124.124|D&C 124:124]].
