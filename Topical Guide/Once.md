# Once

*once* enlightened by the Spirit of God, [[Alma 24.30|Alma 24:30]].

suppose that ye must not worship God only *once* in a week, [[Alma 32.11|Alma 32:11]].

a Nephite … who had *once* belonged to the church of God, [[Helaman 5.35|Helaman 5:35]].
