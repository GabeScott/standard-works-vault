# Thresh


Gideon *threshed* wheat by the winepress, [[Judges 6.11|Judges 6:11]].

thou shalt *thresh* the mountains, [[Isaiah 41.15|Isaiah 41:15]].

*threshed* Gilead with threshing instruments of iron, [[Amos 1.3|Amos 1:3]].

thou didst *thresh* the heathen in anger, [[Habakkuk 3.12|Habakkuk 3:12]].

he that *thresheth* in hope should be partaker of his hope, [[1 Corinthians 9.10|1 Corinthians 9:10]].

tools to till the earth ... and also to *thrash*, [[Ether 10.25|Ether 10:25]].

I call upon the weak things of the world ... to *thresh* the nations, [[D&C 35.13|D&C 35:13]] ([[D&C 133.59|D&C 133:59]]).
