# Stripe


Forty *stripes* he may give him, [[Deuteronomy 25.3|Deuteronomy 25:3]].

reproof entereth more into a wise man than an hundred *stripes* into a fool, [[Proverbs 17.10|Proverbs 17:10]].

*stripes* for the back of fools, [[Proverbs 19.29|Proverbs 19:29]].

cleanseth away evil: so do *stripes* the inward parts, [[Proverbs 20.30|Proverbs 20:30]].

with his *stripes* we are healed, [[Isaiah 53.5|Isaiah 53:5]] ([[1 Peter 2.24|1 Peter 2:24]]; [[Mosiah 14.5|Mosiah 14:5]]).

prepared not himself ... shall be beaten with many *stripes*, [[Luke 12.47|Luke 12:47]].

when they had laid many *stripes* upon them, [[Acts 16.23|Acts 16:23]].

I am more ... in *stripes* above measure, [[2 Corinthians 11.23|2 Corinthians 11:23]].

God will beat us with a few *stripes*, [[2 Nephi 28.8|2 Nephi 28:8]].
