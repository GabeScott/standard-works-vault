# Breathe

*breathed* into his nostrils the breath of life, [[Genesis 2.7|Genesis 2:7]] ([[Moses 3.19|Moses 3:19]]; [[Abraham 5.7|Abraham 5:7]]).

false witnesses … such as *breathe* out cruelty, [[Psalms 27.12|Psalms 27:12]].

*breathe* upon these slain, that they may live, [[Ezekiel 37.9|Ezekiel 37:9]].

*breathed* on them … Receive ye the Holy Ghost, [[John 20.22|John 20:22]].

Saul, yet *breathing* out threatenings and slaughter, [[Acts 9.1|Acts 9:1]].
