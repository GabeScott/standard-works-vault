# Plow, Plough

Thou shalt not *plow* with an ox and an ass, [[Deuteronomy 22.10|Deuteronomy 22:10]].

Elisha … was *plowing* with twelve yoke of oxen, [[1 Kings 19.19|1 Kings 19:19]].

sluggard will not *plow* by reason of the cold, [[Proverbs 20.4|Proverbs 20:4]].

Doth the plowman *plow* all day to sow, [[Isaiah 28.24|Isaiah 28:24]].

Zion shall be *plowed* like a field, [[Jeremiah 26.18|Jeremiah 26:18]] ([[Micah 3.12|Micah 3:12]]).

Ye have *plowed* wickedness, [[Hosea 10.13|Hosea 10:13]].

put his hand to the *plough*, and looking back, [[Luke 9.62|Luke 9:62]].

he that *ploweth* should *plow* in hope, [[1 Corinthians 9.10|1 Corinthians 9:10]].

tools … both to *plow* and to sow, [[Ether 10.25|Ether 10:25]].
