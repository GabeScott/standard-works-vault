# Wot, Wist


*See also* [[Know, Knew, Known.md|Know]]

It is manna: for they *wist* not what it was, [[Exodus 16.15|Exodus 16:15]].

Moses *wist* not that the skin of his face shone, [[Exodus 34.29|Exodus 34:29]].

*wist* ye not that I must be about my Father's business, [[Luke 2.49|Luke 2:49]].

*Wot* ye not what the scripture saith of Elias, [[Romans 11.2|Romans 11:2]].

what I shall choose I *wot* not, [[Philippians 1.22|Philippians 1:22]].
