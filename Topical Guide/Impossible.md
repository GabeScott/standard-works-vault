# Impossible


nothing shall be *impossible* unto you, [[Matthew 17.20|Matthew 17:20]].

With men this is *impossible*, [[Matthew 19.26|Matthew 19:26]] ([[Mark 10.27|Mark 10:27]]; [[Luke 18.27|Luke 18:27]]).

with God nothing shall be *impossible*, [[Luke 1.37|Luke 1:37]].

*impossible* for those who were once enlightened, [[Hebrews 6.4|Hebrews 6:4]].

*impossible* for God to lie, [[Hebrews 6.18|Hebrews 6:18]].

without faith it is *impossible* to please him, [[Hebrews 11.6|Hebrews 11:6]].

*impossible* that man should find out all his ways, [[Jacob 4.8|Jacob 4:8]].

*impossible* for the tongue to describe, [[Mormon 4.11|Mormon 4:11]].

*impossible* for a man to be saved in ignorance, [[D&C 131.6|D&C 131:6]].
