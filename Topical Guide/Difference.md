# Difference

Lord doth put a *difference* between the Egyptians and Israel, [[Exodus 11.7|Exodus 11:7]].

ye may put *difference* between holy and unholy, [[Leviticus 10.10|Leviticus 10:10]].

put *difference* between clean beasts and unclean, [[Leviticus 20.25|Leviticus 20:25]].

have put no *difference* between the holy and profane, [[Ezekiel 22.26|Ezekiel 22:26]].

put no *difference* between us and them, [[Acts 15.9|Acts 15:9]].

no *difference* between the Jew and the Greek, [[Romans 10.12|Romans 10:12]].

*differences* of administrations, but the same Lord, [[1 Corinthians 12.5|1 Corinthians 12:5]] ([[D&C 46.15|D&C 46:15]]).
