vile person shall be no more called *liberal*, [[Isaiah 32.5|Isaiah 32:5]].

deviseth *liberal* things; and by *liberal* things shall he stand, [[Isaiah 32.8|Isaiah 32:8]].

I send to bring your *liberality* unto Jerusalem, [[1 Corinthians 16.3|1 Corinthians 16:3]].

abounded unto the riches of their *liberality*, [[2 Corinthians 8.2|2 Corinthians 8:2]].

they were *liberal* to all, [[Alma 1.30|Alma 1:30]] ([[Alma 6.5|Alma 6:5]]).
