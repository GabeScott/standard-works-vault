# Millstone


No man shall take the nether or the upper *millstone*, [[Deuteronomy 24.6|Deuteronomy 24:6]].

cast a piece of a *millstone* upon Abimelech's head, [[Judges 9.53|Judges 9:53]].

better ... a *millstone* were hanged about his neck, [[Matthew 18.6|Matthew 18:6]] ([[D&C 121.22|D&C 121:22]]).

sound of a *millstone* shall be heard no more, [[Revelation 18.22|Revelation 18:22]].
