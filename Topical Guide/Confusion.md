# Confusion


let them cover themselves with their own *confusion*, [[Psalms 109.29|Psalms 109:29]].

city of *confusion* is broken down, [[Isaiah 24.10|Isaiah 24:10]].

our *confusion* covereth us, [[Jeremiah 3.25|Jeremiah 3:25]].

their everlasting *confusion* shall never be forgotten, [[Jeremiah 20.11|Jeremiah 20:11]].

God is not the author of *confusion*, [[1 Corinthians 14.33|1 Corinthians 14:33]].

where envying and strife is, there is *confusion*, [[James 3.16|James 3:16]].
