# Corruptible


an image made like to *corruptible* man, [[Romans 1.23|Romans 1:23]].

to obtain a *corruptible* crown, [[1 Corinthians 9.25|1 Corinthians 9:25]].

this *corruptible* must put on incorruption, [[1 Corinthians 15.53|1 Corinthians 15:53]].

not redeemed with *corruptible* things, [[1 Peter 1.18|1 Peter 1:18]].

every *corruptible* thing ... shall be consumed, [[D&C 101.24|D&C 101:24]].
