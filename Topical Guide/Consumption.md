# Consumption

appoint over you terror, *consumption*, [[Leviticus 26.16|Leviticus 26:16]].

Lord shall smite thee with a *consumption*, [[Deuteronomy 28.22|Deuteronomy 28:22]].

*consumption* decreed shall overflow with righteousness, [[Isaiah 10.22|Isaiah 10:22]] ([[2 Nephi 20.22|2 Nephi 20:22]]).

a *consumption*, even determined upon the whole earth, [[Isaiah 28.22|Isaiah 28:22]].

until the *consumption* decreed hath made a full end of all nations, [[D&C 87.6|D&C 87:6]].
