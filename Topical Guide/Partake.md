# Partake


*See also* [[Partaker.md|Partaker]]

I did go forth and *partake*, [[1 Nephi 8.11|1 Nephi 8:11]].

Adam and Eve had *partaken* of the forbidden fruit, [[2 Nephi 2.19|2 Nephi 2:19]].

they shall not *partake* of his salvation, [[2 Nephi 26.24|2 Nephi 26:24]].

*partake* of the goodness of God, [[Jacob 1.7|Jacob 1:7]].

*partake* of his salvation, [[Omni 1.26|Omni 1:26]].

*partake* of the waters of life, [[Alma 42.27|Alma 42:27]] ([[D&C 10.66|D&C 10:66]]).

not suffer any one knowingly to *partake* of my flesh and blood unworthily, [[3 Nephi 18.28|3 Nephi 18:28]].

see that ye *partake* not of the sacrament of Christ unworthily, [[Mormon 9.29|Mormon 9:29]].

meet together oft to *partake* of bread and wine, [[Moroni 6.6|Moroni 6:6]] ([[D&C 20.75|D&C 20:75]]).

mattereth not what ye shall eat or what ye shall drink when ye *partake* of the sacrament, [[D&C 27.2|D&C 27:2]].

let him not *partake* until he makes reconciliation, [[D&C 46.4|D&C 46:4]].

lest he put forth his hand and *partake* also of the tree of life, [[Moses 4.28|Moses 4:28]].
