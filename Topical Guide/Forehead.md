# Forehead


David ... smote the Philistine in his *forehead*, [[1 Samuel 17.49|1 Samuel 17:49]].

set a mark upon the *foreheads* of the men, [[Ezekiel 9.4|Ezekiel 9:4]].

sealed the servants of our God in their *foreheads*, [[Revelation 7.3|Revelation 7:3]] ([[D&C 77.9|D&C 77:9]]).

which have not the seal of God in their *foreheads*, [[Revelation 9.4|Revelation 9:4]].

mark in their right hand, or in their *foreheads*, [[Revelation 13.16|Revelation 13:16]].

having his Father's name written in their *foreheads*, [[Revelation 14.1|Revelation 14:1]] ([[D&C 133.18|D&C 133:18]]).

receive his mark in his *forehead*, [[Revelation 14.9|Revelation 14:9]].

neither had received his mark upon their *foreheads*, [[Revelation 20.4|Revelation 20:4]].

his name shall be in their *foreheads*, [[Revelation 22.4|Revelation 22:4]].

Amlicites ... had marked themselves with red in their *foreheads*, [[Alma 3.4|Alma 3:4]].
