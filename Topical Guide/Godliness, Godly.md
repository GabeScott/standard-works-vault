# Godliness, Godly


Lord hath set apart him that is *godly*, [[Psalms 4.3|Psalms 4:3]].

every one that is *godly* pray unto thee, [[Psalms 32.6|Psalms 32:6]].

seek a *godly* seed. Therefore take heed to your spirit, [[Malachi 2.15|Malachi 2:15]].

*godly* sorrow worketh repentance to salvation, [[2 Corinthians 7.10|2 Corinthians 7:10]].

which becometh women professing *godliness*, [[1 Timothy 2.10|1 Timothy 2:10]].

*godliness* is profitable unto all things, [[1 Timothy 4.8|1 Timothy 4:8]].

*godliness* with contentment is great gain, [[1 Timothy 6.6|1 Timothy 6:6]].

increase unto more *ungodliness*, [[2 Timothy 2.16|2 Timothy 2:16]].

Having a form of *godliness*, but denying the power, [[2 Timothy 3.5|2 Timothy 3:5]] ([[Joseph Smith History 1.19|Joseph Smith History 1:19]]).

all that will live *godly* in Christ Jesus shall suffer, [[2 Timothy 3.12|2 Timothy 3:12]].

denying *ungodliness* and worldly lusts, we should live ... *godly*, [[Titus 2.12|Titus 2:12]].

righteous scarcely be saved, where shall the *ungodly* ... appear, [[1 Peter 4.18|1 Peter 4:18]].

all things that pertain unto life and *godliness*, [[2 Peter 1.3|2 Peter 1:3]].

Lord knoweth how to deliver the *godly*, [[2 Peter 2.9|2 Peter 2:9]].

what manner of persons ought ye to be in all holy conversation and *godliness*, [[2 Peter 3.11|2 Peter 3:11]].

*ungodly* men, turning the grace of our God into lasciviousness, [[Jude 1.4|Jude 1:4]].

showing themselves ... in every form of *godliness*, [[Moroni 7.30|Moroni 7:30]].

Remember faith ... *godliness*, [[D&C 4.6|D&C 4:6]].

members shall manifest ... by a *godly* walk, [[D&C 20.69|D&C 20:69]].

in the ordinances ... power of *godliness* is manifest, [[D&C 84.20|D&C 84:20]].

decisions of these quorums ... are to be made in all ... *godliness*, [[D&C 107.30|D&C 107:30]].
