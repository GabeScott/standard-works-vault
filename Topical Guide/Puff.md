some are *puffed* up, as though I would not come, [[1 Corinthians 4.18|1 Corinthians 4:18]].

charity ... is not *puffed* up, [[1 Corinthians 13.4|1 Corinthians 13:4]] ([[Moroni 7.45|Moroni 7:45]]).

vainly *puffed* up by his fleshly mind, [[Colossians 2.18|Colossians 2:18]].

*puffed* up because of their learning, [[2 Nephi 9.42|2 Nephi 9:42]].

many ... shall be *puffed* up in their hearts, [[2 Nephi 28.9|2 Nephi 28:9]] ([[Alma 5.37|Alma 5:37]]).

Satan had great power ... to the *puffing* them up with pride, [[3 Nephi 6.15|3 Nephi 6:15]].
