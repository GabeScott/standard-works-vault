# Beguile

serpent *beguiled* me, and I did eat, [[Genesis 3.13|Genesis 3:13]] ([[Moses 4.6|Moses 4:6]]; [[Moses 4.19|Moses 4:19]]).

Wherefore have ye *beguiled* us, [[Joshua 9.22|Joshua 9:22]].

serpent *beguiled* Eve through his subtilty, [[2 Corinthians 11.3|2 Corinthians 11:3]].

lest any man should *beguile* you with enticing words, [[Colossians 2.4|Colossians 2:4]].

Let no man *beguile* you of your reward, [[Colossians 2.18|Colossians 2:18]].

Having eyes full of adultery … *beguiling* unstable souls, [[2 Peter 2.14|2 Peter 2:14]].

that being who *beguiled* our first parents, [[2 Nephi 9.9|2 Nephi 9:9]] ([[Mosiah 16.3|Mosiah 16:3]]; [[Ether 8.25|Ether 8:25]]).
