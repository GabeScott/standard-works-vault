# Keeper


Abel was a *keeper* of sheep, [[Genesis 4.2|Genesis 4:2]] ([[Moses 5.17|Moses 5:17]]).

Am I my brother's *keeper*, [[Genesis 4.9|Genesis 4:9]] ([[Moses 5.34|Moses 5:34]]).

Lord is thy *keeper*, [[Psalms 121.5|Psalms 121:5]].

for fear of him the *keepers* did shake, [[Matthew 28.4|Matthew 28:4]].

*keepers* standing without before the doors, [[Acts 5.23|Acts 5:23]].

*keeper* of the prison awaking out of his sleep, [[Acts 16.27|Acts 16:27]].

To be discreet, chaste, *keepers* at home, [[Titus 2.5|Titus 2:5]].

*keeper* of the gate is the Holy One of Israel, [[2 Nephi 9.41|2 Nephi 9:41]].
