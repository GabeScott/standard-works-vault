God having *provided* some better thing for us, [[Hebrews 11.40|Hebrews 11:40]].

God has *provided* a means that man, through faith, might work mighty miracles, [[Mosiah 8.18|Mosiah 8:18]].

I will *provide* means whereby thou mayest accomplish, [[D&C 5.34|D&C 5:34]].

every man who is obliged to *provide* for his own family, [[D&C 75.28|D&C 75:28]].
