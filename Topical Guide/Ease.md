displeased with the heathen that are at *ease*, [[Zechariah 1.15|Zechariah 1:15]].

take thine *ease*, eat, drink, and be merry, [[Luke 12.19|Luke 12:19]].

wo be unto him that is at *ease* in Zion, [[2 Nephi 28.24|2 Nephi 28:24]].

do not let us be slothful because of the *easiness* of the way, [[Alma 37.46|Alma 37:46]].
