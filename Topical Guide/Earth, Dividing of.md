# Earth, Dividing of


Let the *waters* ... be gathered together unto one place, [[Genesis 1.9|Genesis 1:9]] ([[Moses 2.9|Moses 2:9]]).

in his days was the *earth divided*, [[Genesis 10.25|Genesis 10:25]] ([[1 Chronicles 1.19|1 Chronicles 1:19]]).

waters had receded ... it became a choice *land*, [[Ether 13.2|Ether 13:2]].

*earth* shall be like as it was ... before it was *divided*, [[D&C 133.24|D&C 133:24]].

many lands; and each land was called *earth*, [[Moses 1.29|Moses 1:29]].
