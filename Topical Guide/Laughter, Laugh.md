# Laughter, Laugh

*See also* [[Happiness.md|Happiness]]; [[Levity.md|Levity]]; [[Scorn.md|Scorn]]

Abraham fell upon his face, and *laughed*, [[Genesis 17.17|Genesis 17:17]].

Lord said unto Abraham, Wherefore did Sarah *laugh*, [[Genesis 18.13|Genesis 18:13]].

Sarah said, God hath made me to *laugh*, [[Genesis 21.6|Genesis 21:6]].

daughter of Zion hath … *laughed* thee to scorn, [[2 Kings 19.21|2 Kings 19:21]].

they *laughed* us to scorn, and despised us, [[Nehemiah 2.19|Nehemiah 2:19]] ([[Alma 26.23|Alma 26:23]]).

He that sitteth in the heavens shall *laugh*, [[Psalms 2.4|Psalms 2:4]].

Lord shall *laugh* at him, [[Psalms 37.13|Psalms 37:13]].

righteous … shall *laugh* at him, [[Psalms 52.6|Psalms 52:6]].

Even in *laughter* the heart is sorrowful, [[Proverbs 14.13|Proverbs 14:13]].

I said of *laughter*, It is mad, [[Ecclesiastes 2.2|Ecclesiastes 2:2]].

time to weep, and a time to *laugh*, [[Ecclesiastes 3.4|Ecclesiastes 3:4]].

so is the *laughter* of the fool … vanity, [[Ecclesiastes 7.6|Ecclesiastes 7:6]].

maid is not dead … they *laughed* him to scorn, [[Matthew 9.24|Matthew 9:24]] ([[Mark 5.40|Mark 5:40]]; [[Luke 8.53|Luke 8:53]]).

Blessed are ye that weep now: for ye shall *laugh*, [[Luke 6.21|Luke 6:21]].

let your *laughter* be turned to mourning, [[James 4.9|James 4:9]].

devil *laugheth*, and his angels rejoice, [[3 Nephi 9.2|3 Nephi 9:2]] ([[Moses 7.26|Moses 7:26]]).

they that have *laughed* shall see their folly, [[D&C 45.49|D&C 45:49]].

not with much *laughter*, for this is sin, [[D&C 59.15|D&C 59:15]].

cast away … your excess of *laughter* far from you, [[D&C 88.69|D&C 88:69]].
