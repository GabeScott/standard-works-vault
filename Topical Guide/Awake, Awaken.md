# Awake, Awaken

Lord *awaked* as one out of sleep, [[Psalms 78.65|Psalms 78:65]].

*Awake*, *awake*, put on strength, [[Isaiah 51.9|Isaiah 51:9]] ([[Isaiah 52.1|Isaiah 52:1]]).

I go, that I may *awake* him, [[John 11.11|John 11:11]].

now it is high time to *awake* out of sleep, [[Romans 13.11|Romans 13:11]].

*Awake* to righteousness, and sin not, [[1 Corinthians 15.34|1 Corinthians 15:34]].

*Awake* thou that sleepest, [[Ephesians 5.14|Ephesians 5:14]].

has *awakened* you to a sense of your nothingness, [[Mosiah 4.5|Mosiah 4:5]].

*awake*, and arise from the dust, O Jerusalem, [[Moroni 10.31|Moroni 10:31]].

then shall all the dead *awake*, [[D&C 29.26|D&C 29:26]].
