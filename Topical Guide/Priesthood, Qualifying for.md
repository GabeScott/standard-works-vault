# Priesthood, Qualifying for

*See also* [[Worthiness, Worthy.md|Worthiness, Worthy]]

It *appertaineth* not unto thee, Uzziah, to burn incense, [[2 Chronicles 26.18|2 Chronicles 26:18]].

genealogy … not found: therefore were they, as polluted, put from the *priesthood*, [[Ezra 2.62|Ezra 2:62]] ([[Nehemiah 7.64|Nehemiah 7:64]]; [[D&C 85.12|D&C 85:12]]).

Ezra had *prepared* his heart to seek the law, [[Ezra 7.10|Ezra 7:10]].

be ye *clean*, that bear the vessels of the Lord, [[Isaiah 52.11|Isaiah 52:11]] ([[3 Nephi 20.41|3 Nephi 20:41]]).

look ye out among you seven men of *honest* report, [[Acts 6.3|Acts 6:3]].

*renounced* … dishonesty, not walking in craftiness, [[2 Corinthians 4.2|2 Corinthians 4:2]].

bishop then must be *blameless*, [[1 Timothy 3.2|1 Timothy 3:2]].

be thou an *example* of the believers, [[1 Timothy 4.12|1 Timothy 4:12]].

If a man therefore *purge* himself, [[2 Timothy 2.21|2 Timothy 2:21]].

*called* of God, as was Aaron, [[Hebrews 5.4|Hebrews 5:4]].

exercising exceedingly great *faith*, are called, [[Alma 13.3|Alma 13:3]].

faith, hope, charity and love, with an eye single to the glory of God, *qualify* him, [[D&C 4.5|D&C 4:5]].

*repenting*, and humbling himself sincerely, [[D&C 20.6|D&C 20:6]].

*embrace* it with singleness of heart, [[D&C 36.7|D&C 36:7]].

except he be *ordained* by some one who has authority, [[D&C 42.11|D&C 42:11]].

having been myself a follower of *righteousness*, [[Abraham 1.2|Abraham 1:2]].

We believe that a man must be *called* of God, [[Articles of Faith 1.5|Articles of Faith 1:5]].
