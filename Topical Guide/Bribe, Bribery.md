# Bribe, Bribery


thou shalt take no *gift*, [[Exodus 23.8|Exodus 23:8]].

have I received any *bribe* to blind mine, [[1 Samuel 12.3|1 Samuel 12:3]].

fire shall consume the tabernacles of *bribery*, [[Job 15.34|Job 15:34]].

their right hand is full of *bribes*, [[Psalms 26.10|Psalms 26:10]].

wicked man taketh a *gift* out of the bosom, [[Proverbs 17.23|Proverbs 17:23]].

every one loveth *gifts*, and followeth after rewards, [[Isaiah 1.23|Isaiah 1:23]].

shaketh his hands from holding of *bribes*, [[Isaiah 33.15|Isaiah 33:15]].

they afflict the just, they take a *bribe*, [[Amos 5.12|Amos 5:12]].

thought that the gift of God may be *purchased*, [[Acts 8.20|Acts 8:20]].

justify the wicked for *reward*, [[2 Nephi 15.23|2 Nephi 15:23]].

lucre which doth *corrupt* the soul, [[Mosiah 29.40|Mosiah 29:40]].

these will I *give* thee if thou wilt deny, [[Alma 11.22|Alma 11:22]].

touch not the evil *gift*, nor the unclean thing, [[Moroni 10.30|Moroni 10:30]].
