# Foolishness

O God, thou knowest my *foolishness*, [[Psalms 69.5|Psalms 69:5]].

heart of fools proclaimeth *foolishness*, [[Proverbs 12.23|Proverbs 12:23]].

*foolishness* of fools is folly, [[Proverbs 14.24|Proverbs 14:24]].

mouth of fools poureth out *foolishness*, [[Proverbs 15.2|Proverbs 15:2]].

mouth of fools feedeth on *foolishness*, [[Proverbs 15.14|Proverbs 15:14]].

*foolishness* of man perverteth his way, [[Proverbs 19.3|Proverbs 19:3]].

thought of *foolishness* is sin, [[Proverbs 24.9|Proverbs 24:9]].

*foolishness* of God is wiser than men, [[1 Corinthians 1.25|1 Corinthians 1:25]].

natural man receiveth not the things of the Spirit … *foolishness* unto him, [[1 Corinthians 2.14|1 Corinthians 2:14]].

wisdom of this world is *foolishness* with God, [[1 Corinthians 3.19|1 Corinthians 3:19]].

*foolishness* of men! When they are learned they think they are wise, [[2 Nephi 9.28|2 Nephi 9:28]].

reward of … *foolishness* they shall reap destruction, [[2 Nephi 26.10|2 Nephi 26:10]].

suppose that this is *foolishness* in me, [[Alma 37.6|Alma 37:6]].
