# Talebearer

*See also* [[Gossip.md|Gossip]]

Thou shalt not go up and down as a *talebearer*, [[Leviticus 19.16|Leviticus 19:16]].

*talebearer* revealeth secrets, [[Proverbs 11.13|Proverbs 11:13]] ([[Proverbs 20.19|Proverbs 20:19]]).

words of a *talebearer* are as wounds, [[Proverbs 18.8|Proverbs 18:8]].

where there is no *talebearer*, the strife ceaseth, [[Proverbs 26.20|Proverbs 26:20]].
