# Ignorance, Ignorant


*See also* [[Accountability.md|Accountability]]; [[Education.md|Education]]; [[Knowledge.md|Knowledge]]

If a soul shall sin through *ignorance*, [[Leviticus 4.2|Leviticus 4:2]].

art our father, though Abraham be *ignorant* of us, [[Isaiah 63.16|Isaiah 63:16]].

Whom therefore ye *ignorantly* worship, him declare I, [[Acts 17.23|Acts 17:23]].

I would not have you *ignorant*, [[Romans 1.13|Romans 1:13]] ([[1 Corinthians 10.1|1 Corinthians 10:1]]; [[1 Corinthians 12.1|1 Corinthians 12:1]]; [[2 Corinthians 1.8|2 Corinthians 1:8]]; [[1 Thessalonians 4.13|1 Thessalonians 4:13]]).

being *ignorant* of God's righteousness, [[Romans 10.3|Romans 10:3]].

Satan ... we are not *ignorant* of his devices, [[2 Corinthians 2.11|2 Corinthians 2:11]].

alienated from the life of God through the *ignorance*, [[Ephesians 4.18|Ephesians 4:18]].

I obtained mercy, because I did it *ignorantly*, [[1 Timothy 1.13|1 Timothy 1:13]].

Who can have compassion on the *ignorant*, [[Hebrews 5.2|Hebrews 5:2]].

according to the former lusts in your *ignorance*, [[1 Peter 1.14|1 Peter 1:14]].

put to silence the *ignorance* of foolish men, [[1 Peter 2.15|1 Peter 2:15]].

For this they willingly are *ignorant*, [[2 Peter 3.5|2 Peter 3:5]].

we must have suffered in *ignorance*, [[Mosiah 1.3|Mosiah 1:3]].

blood atoneth for ... who have *ignorantly* sinned, [[Mosiah 3.11|Mosiah 3:11]].

died before Christ came, in their *ignorance*, [[Mosiah 15.24|Mosiah 15:24]].

some were *ignorant* because of their poverty, [[3 Nephi 6.12|3 Nephi 6:12]].

they did not sin *ignorantly*, for they knew the will, [[3 Nephi 6.18|3 Nephi 6:18]].

they that *knew* no law shall have part in the first resurrection, [[D&C 45.54|D&C 45:54]].

impossible for a man to be saved in *ignorance*, [[D&C 131.6|D&C 131:6]].

Let him that is *ignorant* learn wisdom, [[D&C 136.32|D&C 136:32]].
