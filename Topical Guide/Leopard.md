# Leopard

*leopard* shall lie down with the kid, [[Isaiah 11.6|Isaiah 11:6]] ([[2 Nephi 21.6|2 Nephi 21:6]]; [[2 Nephi 30.12|2 Nephi 30:12]]).

Ethiopian change his skin, or the *leopard* his spots, [[Jeremiah 13.23|Jeremiah 13:23]].

I beheld, and lo another, like a *leopard*, [[Daniel 7.6|Daniel 7:6]].

horses also are swifter than the *leopards*, [[Habakkuk 1.8|Habakkuk 1:8]].

beast which I saw was like unto a *leopard*, [[Revelation 13.2|Revelation 13:2]].
