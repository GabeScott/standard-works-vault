# January 11–17. “We Have Found the Messiah”: John 1

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/03?lang=eng)

Have you ever wondered whether you would have recognized Jesus of Nazareth as the Son of God if you had been alive during His mortal ministry? For years, faithful Israelites—people like Andrew, Peter, Philip, and Nathanael—had waited and prayed for the coming of the promised Messiah. When they met Him, how did they know He was the One they had been seeking? The same way all of us come to know the Savior—by accepting the invitation to “come and see” for ourselves ([[John 1.39|John 1:39]]). We hear His doctrine. We learn from His witnesses. We observe His way of living. We feel His Spirit. Along the way, we discover, as Nathanael did, that the Savior knows us and loves us and wants to prepare us to receive “greater things” ([[John 1.50|John 1:50]]).

For more about John, see Guide to the Scriptures, “[[GS John, Son of Zebedee|John, Son of Zebedee]],” Gospel Library.

### Jesus Christ is the Light.

Another word John used often to describe the Savior is *light*. Look for this word in [[John 1.1|John 1:1–14]][[John 1.2|]][[John 1.3|]][[John 1.4|]][[John 1.5|]][[John 1.6|]][[John 1.7|]][[John 1.8|]][[John 1.9|]][[John 1.10|]][[John 1.11|]][[John 1.12|]][[John 1.13|]][[John 1.14|]].

- Why is *light* a good word to describe Jesus Christ? Why do we need light—both physical and spiritual?

- How has the Savior provided spiritual light in your life?

As you read [[John 1.5|verse 5]], consider what it might mean to not “comprehend” the Savior and His light. Has this ever happened to you? What helps you comprehend Him better?

See also “[Lead, Kindly Light](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/hymns/lead-kindly-light?lang=eng),” *Hymns*, no. 97.

[[John 1.11|John 1:11–13]][[John 1.12|]][[John 1.13|]]
### Jesus Christ gives me “power to become” a daughter or son of God.

We are all spirit children of God, even when we sin. However, our sins separate us from our Father in Heaven. Jesus Christ offers us a way back through His atoning sacrifice so we can become like our Heavenly Father.

- What does it mean to you to have “power to become” a daughter or son of God? (see [[Romans 8.14|Romans 8:14–18]][[Romans 8.15|]][[Romans 8.16|]][[Romans 8.17|]][[Romans 8.18|]]; [[Mosiah 5.7|Mosiah 5:7–9]][[Mosiah 5.8|]][[Mosiah 5.9|]]).

## Ideas for Teaching Children

[[John 1.1|John 1:1–5]][[John 1.2|]][[John 1.3|]][[John 1.4|]][[John 1.5|]]
### Jesus Christ lived with Heavenly Father before He was born.

- You and your children could look at a picture of Jesus as a baby (see, for example, last week’s outline). What did Jesus do *before* He was born? Look for answers in [[John 1.1|John 1:1–5]][[John 1.2|]][[John 1.3|]][[John 1.4|]][[John 1.5|]] and [[Moses 4.2|Moses 4:2]].

- You might also remind your children that they were once babies—show pictures, if you have them. Then you could use “[I Lived in Heaven](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/childrens-songbook/i-lived-in-heaven?lang=eng)” (*Children’s Songbook*, 4) to teach them what happened when they lived with Heavenly Father before they were born.

[[John 1.3|John 1:3]]
### All things were made by Jesus Christ.

- To teach about Jesus Christ and His creations, you and your children might start by taking a walk outside or looking out a window to see things He has created. Then you could read [[John 1.3|John 1:3]] together and look at the picture at the end of this outline. It might also be fun to create something together and talk about your gratitude for the Lord’s creations.

[[John 1.4|John 1:4–9]][[John 1.5|]][[John 1.6|]][[John 1.7|]][[John 1.8|]][[John 1.9|]]
### Jesus Christ is my light.

- You could talk with your children about why we are thankful for light. Then help them find the word *light* in [[Psalms 27.1|Psalm 27:1]]; [[John 1.4|John 1:4–9]][[John 1.5|]][[John 1.6|]][[John 1.7|]][[John 1.8|]][[John 1.9|]]; [[Mosiah 16.9|Mosiah 16:9]]; and [[D&C 39.1|Doctrine and Covenants 39:1–2]][[D&C 39.2|]]. How is Jesus Christ like a light?

- Consider showing a picture of the Savior and some objects that give light, such as a flashlight. Then your children could sing “[The Lord Is My Light](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/hymns/the-lord-is-my-light?lang=eng)” (*Hymns*, no. 89) and point at the picture of Jesus whenever they sing “light.”

> **Share object lessons.** It’s often easier to understand gospel principles when we can relate them to experiences or objects we’re familiar with. As you study or teach, look for objects you can use to visualize principles found in the scriptures you are reading. For instance, a candle or flashlight shining in a dark room could represent Jesus Christ, “the Light” ([[John 1.7|John 1:7]]).

### I can invite others to come to Jesus Christ and learn of Him.

- Read with your children about how Andrew, Simon, Philip, and Nathanael met the Savior in [[John 1.35|John 1:35–51]][[John 1.36|]][[John 1.37|]][[John 1.38|]][[John 1.39|]][[John 1.40|]][[John 1.41|]][[John 1.42|]][[John 1.43|]][[John 1.44|]][[John 1.45|]][[John 1.46|]][[John 1.47|]][[John 1.48|]][[John 1.49|]][[John 1.50|]][[John 1.51|]] (see also “Come and See,” in *New Testament Stories*). What helped these people learn about Jesus? You could also tell your children how you learned about the Savior and His Church. Who invited you to “come and see”?> 1:44


- Here’s a simple game you could try: Hide a picture of Jesus in a box or under a blanket. Invite one child to “come and see” it and to tell the other children about it. The child could then invite another child to “come and see.” Why is an invitation to “come and see” a good way to share the gospel? Consider sharing some of the examples in Elder Gary E. Stevenson’s message “[[2022 April - Love, Share, Invite.md|Love, Share, Invite]]” (*Liahona*, May 2022, 84–87).

- Maybe your children could tell about a time when they shared something, such as a toy or gift, with someone. Help the children see how sharing the gospel can be like sharing other things we love (for an example, see the video “Good Things to Share” \[Gospel Library\]). Help your children practice how they might invite a friend to learn about Jesus.> 2:21
