# October 18–24. “Be Thou an Example of the Believers”: 1 and 2 Timothy; Titus; Philemon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/43?lang=eng)

In the epistles Paul wrote to Timothy, Titus, and Philemon, we get a glimpse into the heart of a servant of the Lord. These letters aren’t general counsel to entire congregations. They are Paul’s intimate messages to close, trusted friends—people he called “mine own son after the common faith” ([[Titus 1.4|Titus 1:4]]) and “dearly beloved, and fellowlabourer” ([[Philemon 1.1|Philemon 1:1]]). Paul might never have expected that so many people around the world would one day read these personal letters. But we’re thankful we can, because it almost feels like Paul could have been writing to us—calling us fellow laborers and giving us loving counsel and encouragement in the cause of Christ.

For more about these epistles, see Guide to the Scriptures, “[[GS Timothy|Timothy]],” “[[GS Timothy, Epistles to|Timothy, Epistles to]],” “[[GS Titus|Titus]],” “[[GS Titus, Epistle to|Titus, Epistle to]],” “[[GS Philemon|Philemon]],” and “[[GS Philemon, Epistle to|Philemon, Epistle to]],” Gospel Library.

### “Be thou an example of the believers.”

Do your friends and acquaintances know that you believe in Jesus Christ? What counsel do you find in [[1 Timothy 4.10|1 Timothy 4:10–16]][[1 Timothy 4.11|]][[1 Timothy 4.12|]][[1 Timothy 4.13|]][[1 Timothy 4.14|]][[1 Timothy 4.15|]][[1 Timothy 4.16|]]; [[Titus 2.7|Titus 2:7–8]][[Titus 2.8|]] that inspires you to share your belief in Christ?

Paul gave Timothy a list of ways to be “an example of the believers” in Jesus Christ ([[1 Timothy 4.12|1 Timothy 4:12]]). For each one, you might ask yourself, “What does it look like to be an example of the believers in this way? Who has been an example in this way to me?”

### “Neglect not the gift that is in thee.”

Reading [[1 Timothy 4.14|1 Timothy 4:14]] might inspire you to think about “the gift that is in \[you\].” What gifts have you been given through “the laying on of the hands,” such as through receiving priesthood blessings or being set apart for a calling? What gifts have you noticed in others?

- What does it mean to you to “neglect not” those gifts?

- How can you “stir up” those gifts? ([[2 Timothy 1.6|2 Timothy 1:6]]).

### Disciples of Jesus Christ treat one another like brothers and sisters.

Philemon had a slave named Onesimus. Onesimus escaped, met Paul, and converted to the gospel. In a letter, Paul encouraged Philemon to receive Onesimus “not now as a servant, but above a servant, a brother beloved,” a fellow Christian ([[Philemon 1.16|Philemon 1:16]]).

As you read this letter, imagine you were Onesimus. Then imagine you were Philemon. You might even imagine that Paul’s words to Philemon are the Savior’s words to you. How do these perspectives affect the way you read the letter?

You could also consider:

- [[Philemon 1.16|Verses 1–7]]: What do words like “fellowlabourer” and “fellowsoldier” suggest to you about relationships among followers of Christ? When have you felt “refreshed by” a brother or sister in Christ?

- [[Philemon 1.16|Verse 16]]: What does it mean to be “a brother \[or sister\] beloved … in the Lord”? Do you know someone you need to receive in this way?

- [[Philemon 1.17|Verses 17–21]][[Philemon 1.18|]][[Philemon 1.19|]][[Philemon 1.20|]][[Philemon 1.21|]]: What was Paul willing to do for Onesimus? How is this similar to what the Savior did for us? (see also [[1 Timothy 2.5|1 Timothy 2:5–6]][[1 Timothy 2.6|]]; [[D&C 45.3|Doctrine and Covenants 45:3–5]][[D&C 45.4|]][[D&C 45.5|]]).

## Ideas for Teaching Children

[[1 Timothy 4.12|1 Timothy 4:12]]
### I can be “an example of the believers.”

- To teach your children what it means to be an example, you could play a game: One child could do an action, and the other children could imitate it. Then you could read [[1 Timothy 4.12|1 Timothy 4:12]] together and talk about the actions of a believer in Christ. You might tell each other about someone who has been “an example of the believers” in your lives. Consider telling your children how they have been Christlike examples to you.

- You could also sing together “[This Little Light of Mine](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/music/hymns-for-home-and-church/this-little-light-of-mine?lang=eng)” (*Hymns—For Home and Church*, Gospel Library). How is being a good example like shining a light for others? Help your children think of ways they can be an example of the believers to their family and friends.

[[2 Timothy 1.7|2 Timothy 1:7]]
### God gives me power and love, not fear.

- To give your children some context for [[2 Timothy 1.7|2 Timothy 1:7]], you can use “[Letters to Timothy](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/new-testament-scripture-stories/67-letters-to-timothy?lang=eng)” (in *New Testament Stories*). You might ask your children why Timothy might have felt afraid and what Paul said to help him have courage. You might share with each other how the Lord’s power and love have helped you when you felt afraid. You could also find the three principles Elder David A. Bednar shared for overcoming fear in “[[2015 April - Therefore They Hushed Their Fears.md|Therefore They Hushed Their Fears]]” (*Ensign* or *Liahona*, May 2015, 47–48).> 2:14


[[2 Timothy 3.14|2 Timothy 3:14–17]][[2 Timothy 3.15|]][[2 Timothy 3.16|]][[2 Timothy 3.17|]]
### Heavenly Father gave me scriptures to help me learn truth.

- While you and your children read [[2 Timothy 3.14|2 Timothy 3:14–17]][[2 Timothy 3.15|]][[2 Timothy 3.16|]][[2 Timothy 3.17|]] together, you could let them hold copies of the scriptures. Help them become familiar with each of the standard works by showing them the title pages. You might also show them verses that are especially meaningful to you. Tell your children why you love the scriptures. Share how they have helped you in the ways Paul describes. Then you could invite your children to share scripture verses they love.

- Another way to help your children love the scriptures is to gather pictures of favorite scripture stories. Maybe your children could pick a picture and tell the story. (You can find pictures in the [Gospel Art Book](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/media/collection/new-testament-gospel-art-book-images) and in *Come, Follow Me* materials.) How do these stories help us have faith in Jesus Christ?
