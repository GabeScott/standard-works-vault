# April 19–25. “I Am the Good Shepherd”: John 7–10

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/17?lang=eng)

Jesus Christ came to bring “peace \[and\] good will toward men” ([[Luke 2.14|Luke 2:14]]). Even so, there was “a division among the people because of him” ([[John 7.43|John 7:43]]). People who witnessed the same events came to different conclusions about who Jesus was. Some declared, “He is a good man,” while others said, “He deceiveth the people” ([[John 7.12|John 7:12]]). When Jesus healed a blind man on the Sabbath, some insisted, “This man is not of God, because he keepeth not the sabbath day,” while others asked, “How can a man that is a sinner do such miracles?” ([[John 9.16|John 9:16]]). Yet despite all the confusion, those who searched for truth recognized the power in His words, for “never man spake like this man” ([[John 7.46|John 7:46]]). When the Jews asked Jesus to “tell \[them\] plainly” whether He was the Christ, He revealed a principle that can help us distinguish truth from error: “My sheep hear my voice,” He said, “and I know them, and they follow me” ([[John 10.24|John 10:24, 27]][[John 10.27|]]).

### As I live the Savior’s teachings, I will come to know they are true.

The Jews marveled that Jesus knew so much, since He was not educated in traditional ways (see [[John 7.15|John 7:15]]). But Jesus taught a different way of knowing truth.

- What do you learn from [[John 7.14|John 7:14–17]][[John 7.15|]][[John 7.16|]][[John 7.17|]] about the Savior’s way of learning doctrine?

- How has living a gospel principle helped you know it is true?

[[John 8.1|John 8:1–11]][[John 8.2|]][[John 8.3|]][[John 8.4|]][[John 8.5|]][[John 8.6|]][[John 8.7|]][[John 8.8|]][[John 8.9|]][[John 8.10|]][[John 8.11|]]
### The Savior’s mercy is available to me.

As you read [[John 8.1|John 8:1–11]][[John 8.2|]][[John 8.3|]][[John 8.4|]][[John 8.5|]][[John 8.6|]][[John 8.7|]][[John 8.8|]][[John 8.9|]][[John 8.10|]][[John 8.11|]], consider what the woman, Christ, and the accusers may have been thinking or feeling.

- What do you learn about the Savior from how He handled this situation?

We don’t know much about the woman in this account. However, as Sister Amy A. Wright taught, we do know “that Jesus Christ was the answer then, and He is the answer now. He knows our complete story and exactly what we suffer, as well as our capabilities and vulnerabilities” (“[[2022 April - Christ Heals That Which Is Broken.md|Christ Heals That Which Is Broken]],” *Liahona*, May 2022, 82).

- How has Jesus Christ been your answer? How has He shown that He understands your capabilities and vulnerabilities?

- Consider how the Savior encouraged the woman to change (note that the Joseph Smith Translation of [[John 8.11|John 8:11]] includes these words: “And the woman glorified God from that hour, and believed on his name” \[in [[John 8.11|John 8:11]], footnote *c*\]). How can you follow His example?

See also “Go and Sin No More” (video), Gospel Library; “People Want to Punish a Woman Who Sinned,” in *New Testament Stories.*

[[John 8.31|John 8:31–36]][[John 8.32|]][[John 8.33|]][[John 8.34|]][[John 8.35|]][[John 8.36|]]
### The truth can make me free.

Think of a task you can complete only if you follow instructions carefully—like baking or cooking something you’ve never tried before. What happens if you skip a step or an ingredient? Think about how this relates to Jesus’s words in [[John 8.31|John 8:31–36]][[John 8.32|]][[John 8.33|]][[John 8.34|]][[John 8.35|]][[John 8.36|]].

How does truth make you free? To help answer this question, you might read “Truth Will Make You Free,” in *For the Strength of Youth: A Guide for Making Choices* (58–61). Then you could see how many ways you can complete this sentence: “Because I know , I am free from (or free to) .”

> **Object lessons enhance learning.** If you are teaching, consider how you might include an object lesson to teach a gospel principle. For example, when teaching about [[John 8.31|John 8:31–36]][[John 8.32|]][[John 8.33|]][[John 8.34|]][[John 8.35|]][[John 8.36|]], you might give learners a task that requires them to follow instructions—but without giving them the instructions. Then you could discuss how the instructions are like gospel truths. How do they make us free?

### Acting in faith can help me see the Savior more in my life.

When the Savior healed a man who was born blind, He could have given him sight immediately. Instead, He gave the man something to do (see [[John 9.5|John 9:5–7]][[John 9.6|]][[John 9.7|]]). Why do you think He did this? When have you had to act in faith like this man did?

As you read the rest of [[John 9.1|John 9]][[John 9.2|]][[John 9.3|]][[John 9.4|]][[John 9.5|]][[John 9.6|]][[John 9.7|]][[John 9.8|]][[John 9.9|]][[John 9.10|]][[John 9.11|]][[John 9.12|]][[John 9.13|]][[John 9.14|]][[John 9.15|]][[John 9.16|]][[John 9.17|]][[John 9.18|]][[John 9.19|]][[John 9.20|]][[John 9.21|]][[John 9.22|]][[John 9.23|]][[John 9.24|]][[John 9.25|]][[John 9.26|]][[John 9.27|]][[John 9.28|]][[John 9.29|]][[John 9.30|]][[John 9.31|]][[John 9.32|]][[John 9.33|]][[John 9.34|]][[John 9.35|]][[John 9.36|]][[John 9.37|]][[John 9.38|]][[John 9.39|]][[John 9.40|]][[John 9.41|]], ponder questions like these:

- What evidence do you find of the man’s faith?

- What do you find that indicates he gained spiritual sight as well as physical sight?

- What do you learn about the Savior from [[John 9.35|verses 35–38]][[John 9.36|]][[John 9.37|]][[John 9.38|]]?

## Ideas for Teaching Children

[[John 7.14|John 7:14–17]][[John 7.15|]][[John 7.16|]][[John 7.17|]]
### Keeping the commandments will help me know they are true.

- You could read [[John 7.17|John 7:17]] together and share with each other how obeying a commandment helped you know it came from God. You might sing “[Keep the Commandments](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/childrens-songbook/keep-the-commandments?lang=eng)” together (*Children’s Songbook*, 146–47); your children could stand when they sing about a blessing we receive by obeying the commandments.

[[John 8.29|John 8:29]]
### I can obey Heavenly Father like Jesus did.

- After reading [[John 8.29|John 8:29]] together, you and your children could talk about how Jesus pleased Heavenly Father, or made Him happy. To give them ideas, you could show your children pictures from the [Gospel Art Book](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-art-book/new-testament?lang=eng). You might also invite them to draw pictures of themselves doing things that would make Heavenly Father happy. You could share with each other times when you knew that Heavenly Father had “not left \[you\] alone.”
