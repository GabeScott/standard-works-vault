# March: Heavenly Father’s Prophets Write His Words in Scripture

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/54-appendix-b/003-prophets-scriptures?lang=eng)

Many children love the stories in the scriptures—an ark full of animals, a den of lions, stones that shine in the darkness. You can help them understand *why* we love the scriptures. It’s not just because the stories are interesting or exciting. It’s because they are messages from God, given to us through His servants, the prophets. Children who learn to love the scriptures and words of prophets while they’re young will have a way to feel close to Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ. Tell the children how the Lord has spoken to you through the scriptures and the words of His prophets. Then help them have their own experiences with His holy word.
