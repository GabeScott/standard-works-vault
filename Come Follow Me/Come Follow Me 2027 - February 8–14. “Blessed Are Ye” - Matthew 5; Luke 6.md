# February 8–14. “Blessed Are Ye”: Matthew 5; Luke 6

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/07?lang=eng)

By this point in His ministry, it was clear that Jesus’s teachings would be unlike what the people of His time were used to hearing. The poor will receive the kingdom of God? The meek will inherit the earth? Blessed are the persecuted? The scribes and Pharisees were not teaching such things. And yet those who truly understood God’s law recognized truth in the Savior’s words. “An eye for an eye” and “hate thine enemy” were lesser laws ([[Matthew 5.38|Matthew 5:38, 43]][[Matthew 5.43|]]). Jesus Christ had come to teach a higher law (see [[3 Nephi 15.2|3 Nephi 15:2–10]][[3 Nephi 15.3|]][[3 Nephi 15.4|]][[3 Nephi 15.5|]][[3 Nephi 15.6|]][[3 Nephi 15.7|]][[3 Nephi 15.8|]][[3 Nephi 15.9|]][[3 Nephi 15.10|]]), designed to help us one day become “perfect, even as \[our\] Father which is in heaven is perfect” ([[Matthew 5.48|Matthew 5:48]]).

### Disciples of Jesus Christ are “the salt of the earth” and the “light of the world.”

Why do you suppose Jesus wants you to be like salt? As you ponder this symbol in [[Matthew 5.13|Matthew 5:13]], it might help you to know that salt had religious meaning for the Israelites. It was associated with the ancient practice of animal sacrifice under the law of Moses (see [[Leviticus 2.13|Leviticus 2:13]]; [[Numbers 18.19|Numbers 18:19]]). What else about salt makes it a good symbol for a disciple of Jesus Christ? (For some ideas, see José A. Teixeira, “[[2024 October - Bonded to Jesus Christ - Becoming the Salt of the Earth.md|Bonded to Jesus Christ: Becoming the Salt of the Earth]],” *Liahona*, Nov. 2024, 30–31.)

Salt loses its savor when mixed with other elements, and it becomes “good for nothing” ([[Matthew 5.13|Matthew 5:13]]). How will you keep your savor as a disciple of Jesus Christ? What does being “the salt of the earth” look like to you?

Jesus also wants you to be “the light of the world” ([[Matthew 5.14|verse 14]]).

- What does this phrase mean to you?

- What could it mean to hide your light “under a bushel”? ([[Matthew 5.15|verse 15]]). What do you think the candlestick might represent?

- Who has been like a light for you? How can you be a light to others?

See also [[3 Nephi 18.16|3 Nephi 18:16, 24–25]][[3 Nephi 18.24|]][[3 Nephi 18.25|]].

### Living Jesus Christ’s higher law helps me become more like my Heavenly Father.

Have you ever climbed a hill or mountain or looked at your town from the top of a building? How does being on higher ground change the way you see things? The Savior’s higher law can help us see life differently and prepare to become more like Heavenly Father. Below are some aspects of His higher law. Ponder how each one lifts you higher and brings you closer to God:

- Dealing with anger and conflict—[[Matthew 5.21|Matthew 5:21–25, 38–47]][[Matthew 5.22|]][[Matthew 5.23|]][[Matthew 5.24|]][[Matthew 5.25|]][[Matthew 5.38|]][[Matthew 5.39|]][[Matthew 5.40|]][[Matthew 5.41|]][[Matthew 5.42|]][[Matthew 5.43|]][[Matthew 5.44|]][[Matthew 5.45|]][[Matthew 5.46|]][[Matthew 5.47|]] (note that the phrase “without a cause” in [[Matthew 5.22|verse 22]] does not appear in the Joseph Smith Translation, in footnote *b*)

- Moral cleanliness—[[Matthew 5.27|Matthew 5:27–28]][[Matthew 5.28|]]

- Integrity—[[Matthew 5.33|Matthew 5:33–37]][[Matthew 5.34|]][[Matthew 5.35|]][[Matthew 5.36|]][[Matthew 5.37|]]

## Ideas for Teaching Children

[[Matthew 5.3|Matthew 5:3–12]][[Matthew 5.4|]][[Matthew 5.5|]][[Matthew 5.6|]][[Matthew 5.7|]][[Matthew 5.8|]][[Matthew 5.9|]][[Matthew 5.10|]][[Matthew 5.11|]][[Matthew 5.12|]]
### Jesus taught me what it means to be “blessed.”

- As you read [[Matthew 5.3|Matthew 5:3–12]][[Matthew 5.4|]][[Matthew 5.5|]][[Matthew 5.6|]][[Matthew 5.7|]][[Matthew 5.8|]][[Matthew 5.9|]][[Matthew 5.10|]][[Matthew 5.11|]][[Matthew 5.12|]] together (see also “Jesus Teaches the Sermon on the Mount,” in *New Testament Stories*), your children could act out some of the Christlike attributes described in these verses. Help them also notice the blessings Jesus promises. How has He fulfilled these promises to you?> 2:37


- Your children might also enjoy a matching game: They could match the qualities in [[Matthew 5.3|Matthew 5:3–12]][[Matthew 5.4|]][[Matthew 5.5|]][[Matthew 5.6|]][[Matthew 5.7|]][[Matthew 5.8|]][[Matthew 5.9|]][[Matthew 5.10|]][[Matthew 5.11|]][[Matthew 5.12|]] (such as “meek” and “merciful”) with the promised blessings (such as “inherit the earth” and “obtain mercy”). Maybe you could write these phrases on cards for your children to match.

[[Matthew 5.9|Matthew 5:9, 44–45]][[Matthew 5.44|]][[Matthew 5.45|]]
### I can be a peacemaker, even to people who aren’t kind to me.

- Perhaps you can think of common situations that require a peacemaker. Write a few on strips of paper, and invite each child to pick one and share how they could be a peacemaker in that situation. You could also read together the story at the beginning of President Russell M. Nelson’s message “[[2023 April - Peacemakers Needed.md|Peacemakers Needed]]” (*Liahona*, May 2023, 98).

- You could read together [[Matthew 5.44|Matthew 5:44–45]][[Matthew 5.45|]] and discuss what some of the words and phrases mean. Share with each other how you’ve tried to show love for someone even though it was hard. Then you could sing a song such as “[Love One Another](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/childrens-songbook/love-one-another?lang=eng)” (*Children’s Songbook*, 136–37). What do we learn from this song about loving others?

[[Matthew 5.13|Matthew 5:13–16]][[Matthew 5.14|]][[Matthew 5.15|]][[Matthew 5.16|]]
### My example can light the way for others to follow Jesus.

- To discuss [[Matthew 5.13|Matthew 5:13]], maybe you and your children could eat some food seasoned with salt and the same food without salt. Why does Jesus want us to be “the salt of the earth”?

- To help your family understand Jesus’s invitation in [[Matthew 5.14|Matthew 5:14–16]][[Matthew 5.15|]][[Matthew 5.16|]], you could turn off the lights, shine a flashlight around the room, and invite your children to follow the light with their eyes. Then you could use the light to lead their eyes to a picture of the Savior. How can we shine our light to help others find the Savior? Consider singing together “[This Little Light of Mine](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/music/hymns-for-home-and-church/this-little-light-of-mine?lang=eng)” (*Hymns—For Home and Church*, Gospel Library).
