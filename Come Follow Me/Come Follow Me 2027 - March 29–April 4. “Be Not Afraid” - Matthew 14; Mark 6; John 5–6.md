# March 29–April 4. “Be Not Afraid”: Matthew 14; Mark 6; John 5–6

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/13?lang=eng)

What could have inspired Peter to leave the safety of his boat in the middle of the Sea of Galilee during a storm? What led him to believe that if Jesus could walk on water, he could too? We can’t know for certain, but perhaps Peter understood that the Son of God didn’t come just to do wonderful things for us; He also came to empower us to do wonderful things. Jesus’s invitation, after all, was “Come, follow me” ([[Luke 18.22|Luke 18:22]]). Peter had accepted this invitation once, and he was willing to accept it again, even if it meant facing his fears and doing something that seemed impossible. Perhaps the Lord will not ask us to step out of a boat in the middle of a storm or contribute our meager supply of bread when thousands need to eat, but He may ask us to accept counsel we don’t fully understand. His invitations to us may seem surprising or even frightening. But miracles can happen as we, like Peter, set aside our fears, our doubts, and our limited understanding and follow Him in faith.

### Jesus Christ can make me whole.

Have you ever felt incomplete or broken emotionally, spiritually, or physically, and it seemed like relief was always just out of reach? Perhaps you’ve been treated unfairly—instead of helping you, people have blocked your way. Feelings like those might help you relate with the man described in [[John 5.1|John 5:1–16]][[John 5.2|]][[John 5.3|]][[John 5.4|]][[John 5.5|]][[John 5.6|]][[John 5.7|]][[John 5.8|]][[John 5.9|]][[John 5.10|]][[John 5.11|]][[John 5.12|]][[John 5.13|]][[John 5.14|]][[John 5.15|]][[John 5.16|]]. Ponder in particular the Savior’s interaction with him.

- The question “Wilt thou be made whole?” seems rather obvious. Why do you suppose Jesus asked it? What kinds of “wholeness” does the Savior offer you? How can you show your willingness to be made whole?

- It seems that the help the man had in mind was different from what Jesus gave him. Has that ever happened to you? Ponder how the Savior might be helping you in ways you weren’t expecting. How is He inviting you to “rise”?

### The Savior can magnify my humble offerings to accomplish His purposes.

Have you ever felt inadequate to meet all the needs you see around you—in your home, in your relationships, in your calling, or in society? Jesus’s disciples must have felt that way when He asked them to feed over 5,000 hungry people when all they had was five loaves of bread and two fish. As you read [[John 6.5|John 6:5–14]][[John 6.6|]][[John 6.7|]][[John 6.8|]][[John 6.9|]][[John 6.10|]][[John 6.11|]][[John 6.12|]][[John 6.13|]][[John 6.14|]], ponder how God might use your humble offerings to bless those around you.

### When I focus on Jesus Christ, I can set aside my fears and doubts and exercise faith in Him.

Picture in your mind the scene described in [[Matthew 14.22|Matthew 14:22–33]][[Matthew 14.23|]][[Matthew 14.24|]][[Matthew 14.25|]][[Matthew 14.26|]][[Matthew 14.27|]][[Matthew 14.28|]][[Matthew 14.29|]][[Matthew 14.30|]][[Matthew 14.31|]][[Matthew 14.32|]][[Matthew 14.33|]]. Imagine how Peter and the other disciples may have felt. Have you ever felt like Peter when he cried out, “Lord, save me”? ([[Matthew 14.30|verse 30]]). Read [[Matthew 14.26|Matthew 14:26–33]][[Matthew 14.27|]][[Matthew 14.28|]][[Matthew 14.29|]][[Matthew 14.30|]][[Matthew 14.31|]][[Matthew 14.32|]][[Matthew 14.33|]], looking for truths about the Savior that can help you turn to Him when you are afraid or feel like you’re sinking.

- What do you think would have given Peter the faith and courage to leave the boat? What does the Savior invite you to do that might be like stepping out of a boat? What gives you the faith to do it?

- Why do you think Peter started sinking? What do you learn from this?

- In what ways has Jesus Christ come to your rescue in your moments of fear or doubt?

## Ideas for Teaching Children

[[John 5.1|John 5:1–9]][[John 5.2|]][[John 5.3|]][[John 5.4|]][[John 5.5|]][[John 5.6|]][[John 5.7|]][[John 5.8|]][[John 5.9|]]
### Jesus Christ knows what I need and can help me.

- As you read [[John 5.1|John 5:1–9]][[John 5.2|]][[John 5.3|]][[John 5.4|]][[John 5.5|]][[John 5.6|]][[John 5.7|]][[John 5.8|]][[John 5.9|]] with your children, ask them to listen for how Jesus showed love (see also “Jesus at the Pool of Bethesda,” in *New Testament Stories*). They could also name some things that are hard for them or that make them sad. How does Jesus show us His love during those times? Maybe you and your children could share an experience when the Savior helped you during a difficult trial.> 2:52


- A song like “[He Cares for Me](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/music/hymns-for-home-and-church/he-cares-for-me?lang=eng)” (*Hymns—For Home and Church*, Gospel Library) can help you testify that Jesus knows about all our problems and wants to help us.

[[Matthew 14.15|Matthew 14:15–21]][[Matthew 14.16|]][[Matthew 14.17|]][[Matthew 14.18|]][[Matthew 14.19|]][[Matthew 14.20|]][[Matthew 14.21|]]; [[John 6.5|John 6:5–13]][[John 6.6|]][[John 6.7|]][[John 6.8|]][[John 6.9|]][[John 6.10|]][[John 6.11|]][[John 6.12|]][[John 6.13|]]
### Jesus can do great things with my small efforts to serve others.

- As you read together [[John 6.5|John 6:5–13]][[John 6.6|]][[John 6.7|]][[John 6.8|]][[John 6.9|]][[John 6.10|]][[John 6.11|]][[John 6.12|]][[John 6.13|]] (see also “Jesus Feeds Thousands of People,” in *New Testament Stories*), help your children visualize how big a crowd of five thousand people is. How much bread and fish would be needed to feed them? Maybe your children could cut out five paper loaves of bread and two paper fish and talk about how they would feel if they were the child in [[John 6.9|verse 9]]. What did Jesus do with the child’s small offering? On each paper loaf or fish, your children could write or draw something small and simple they can do to serve others. Share your testimony that Jesus can bless many people with even our small acts of service.> 3:3

### Faith in Jesus Christ can help me not be afraid.

- Your children might enjoy pretending to be Peter as you read [[Matthew 14.22|Matthew 14:22–33]][[Matthew 14.23|]][[Matthew 14.24|]][[Matthew 14.25|]][[Matthew 14.26|]][[Matthew 14.27|]][[Matthew 14.28|]][[Matthew 14.29|]][[Matthew 14.30|]][[Matthew 14.31|]][[Matthew 14.32|]][[Matthew 14.33|]] (see also “Walking on the Water,” in *New Testament Stories*). Ask them to notice signs of faith and signs of fear in the story. What did Jesus do to help when people were afraid? You could also talk about why Peter started to sink and what he did about it. What do we learn from Peter’s example? (See Elder Alvin F. Meredith III’s comments on this story in “[[2021 October - Look down the Road.md|Look down the Road]]” \[*Liahona*, Nov. 2021, 114–16\].) You might tell your children about a time when you felt afraid and share how the Savior helped you.> 2:10


- To help your children imagine the miracle of walking on water, you could let them pretend to “walk” their fingers across the surface of water in a small bowl. Consider using the painting or activity page in this outline to help your children tell the story in their own words.
