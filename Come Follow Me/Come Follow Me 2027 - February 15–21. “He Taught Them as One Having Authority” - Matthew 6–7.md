# February 15–21. “He Taught Them as One Having Authority”: Matthew 6–7

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/08?lang=eng)

The Sermon on the Mount is one of the best-known discourses in Christianity. The Savior taught with rich images, such as lilies in a field, wolves disguised as sheep, and a house in a storm. But the Sermon on the Mount is far more than a beautiful speech. The power of the Savior’s teachings is their power to change our lives. Of course, this happens only when we both hear them and live by them. Then His words become more than words; they become a sure foundation that, like the wise man’s house, can withstand life’s winds and floods (see [[Matthew 7.24|Matthew 7:24–25]][[Matthew 7.25|]]).

### The Savior taught me how to pray.

Studying Jesus’s example of prayer in [[Matthew 6.9|Matthew 6:9–13]][[Matthew 6.10|]][[Matthew 6.11|]][[Matthew 6.12|]][[Matthew 6.13|]] (or [[Luke 11.1|Luke 11:1–4]][[Luke 11.2|]][[Luke 11.3|]][[Luke 11.4|]]) might help you know how to improve your prayers. Consider noting words that stand out to you. If the Savior were reading these verses with you, what do you think He would want you to notice?

See also “Sermon on the Mount: The Lord’s Prayer” (video), Gospel Library.

[[Matthew 7.1|Matthew 7:1–5]][[Matthew 7.2|]][[Matthew 7.3|]][[Matthew 7.4|]][[Matthew 7.5|]]
### I can judge righteously.

In [[Matthew 7.1|Matthew 7:1]], Jesus seems to be saying we should never make any judgments, but in other scriptures (including other verses in this chapter), He gives us instructions about how to judge. If that seems puzzling, the Joseph Smith Translation of this verse might help: “Judge not unrighteously, that ye be not judged; but judge righteous judgment” (in [[JST Matthew 7.1|Matthew 7:1]], footnote *a*).

- What do you find in [[Matthew 7.1|Matthew 7:1–5]][[Matthew 7.2|]][[Matthew 7.3|]][[Matthew 7.4|]][[Matthew 7.5|]], along with the rest of the chapter, that helps you know how to “judge righteous judgment”?

See also “Looking through Windows” (video), Gospel Library; Topics and Questions, “[Judgment](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/judging-others-study-guide?lang=eng),” Gospel Library.

### Heavenly Father answers prayers.

In [[Matthew 7.7|Matthew 7:7]], Jesus gives three invitations and promises three blessings. How have you acted on the invitations, and how has the Lord kept the promises? Can you think of examples in the scriptures where God has answered humble prayers? What do you learn from these examples? (See, for instance, [[1 Nephi 11.1|1 Nephi 11:1]]; [[Ether 2.18|Ether 2:18–25]][[Ether 2.19|]][[Ether 2.20|]][[Ether 2.21|]][[Ether 2.22|]][[Ether 2.23|]][[Ether 2.24|]][[Ether 2.25|]]; [[Ether 3.1|3:1–6]][[Ether 3.2|]][[Ether 3.3|]][[Ether 3.4|]][[Ether 3.5|]][[Ether 3.6|]]; [[Joseph Smith History 1.11|Joseph Smith—History 1:11–17]][[Joseph Smith History 1.12|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.13|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.14|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.15|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.16|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.17|]].)

See also Guide to the Scriptures, “[[GS Prayer|Prayer]],” Gospel Library; Taylor G. Godoy, “[[2024 April - Call, Don’t Fall.md|Call, Don’t Fall]],” *Liahona*, May 2024, 101–3.

[[Matthew 7.15|Matthew 7:15–20]][[Matthew 7.16|]][[Matthew 7.17|]][[Matthew 7.18|]][[Matthew 7.19|]][[Matthew 7.20|]]
### I can identify true and false prophets by their fruits.

You may have come across ideas and philosophies—on the internet or elsewhere—that seem contrary to the Savior’s gospel. You may have also heard people criticize the Lord’s servants. How can you know what’s true and what isn’t? In [[Matthew 7.15|Matthew 7:15–20]][[Matthew 7.16|]][[Matthew 7.17|]][[Matthew 7.18|]][[Matthew 7.19|]][[Matthew 7.20|]], the Savior teaches principles that can help. What “fruits” have you experienced from following the counsel of living prophets?

[[Matthew 7.21|Matthew 7:21–23]][[Matthew 7.22|]][[Matthew 7.23|]]
### I come to know Jesus Christ by doing His will.

The phrase “I never knew you” in [[Matthew 7.23|Matthew 7:23]] was changed in the Joseph Smith Translation to “Ye never knew me” (in [[JST Matthew 7.23|Matthew 7:23]], footnote *a*).

- How does this change help you better understand what the Lord taught in [[Matthew 7.21|verses 21–22]][[Matthew 7.22|]][[Matthew 7.23|]]?

- How well do you feel you know the Lord? What can you do to know Him better?

[[Matthew 7.24|Matthew 7:24–27]][[Matthew 7.25|]][[Matthew 7.26|]][[Matthew 7.27|]]
### Obeying the Savior’s teachings creates a firm foundation for my life.

Jesus concluded His sermon with a parable that might help you understand the importance of living by His teachings (see [[Matthew 7.24|Matthew 7:24–27]][[Matthew 7.25|]][[Matthew 7.26|]][[Matthew 7.27|]]). What insights do you gain about this parable from the painting at the end of this outline? What do you learn about building a firm foundation for your life from pages 4–5 of *For the Strength of Youth: A Guide for Making Choices*?

Of course, following Christ doesn’t remove adversity from our lives—the storm hit both houses in the parable. But one of the houses withstood it. How has living the Savior’s teachings created a solid foundation for you?

### Heavenly Father hears and answers me when I pray.

- Consider using principles found in [[Matthew 6.5|Matthew 6:5–13]][[Matthew 6.6|]][[Matthew 6.7|]][[Matthew 6.8|]][[Matthew 6.9|]][[Matthew 6.10|]][[Matthew 6.11|]][[Matthew 6.12|]][[Matthew 6.13|]] and the song “[Anytime, Anywhere](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/music/hymns-for-home-and-church/anytime-anywhere?lang=eng)” (*Hymns—For Home and Church*, Gospel Library) to help your children find truths about prayer.

- Your children might enjoy memorizing [[Matthew 7.7|Matthew 7:7]] by playing a game like the following: One child recites the first word or phrase from the verse and then tosses a ball to another child, who then recites the next word or phrase. Your children could also act out [[Matthew 7.9|Matthew 7:9–10]][[Matthew 7.10|]] using simple props. What does Jesus want us to know about prayer?

- You could show these phrases to your children: *pray to know*, *pray to grow*, and *pray to show*. Then you and your children could read or listen to portions of Susan H. Porter’s message “[[2024 April - Pray, He Is There.md|Pray, He Is There]]” (*Liahona*, May 2024, 77–79). When President Porter says one of the phrases, your children could share how prayer helps us know, grow, and show.

[[Matthew 6.19|Matthew 6:19–21]][[Matthew 6.20|]][[Matthew 6.21|]]
### I can seek eternal treasures instead of earthly treasures.

- Consider filling a “treasure” box with objects that represent worldly things, like money or toys. As you read [[Matthew 6.19|Matthew 6:19–21]][[Matthew 6.20|]][[Matthew 6.21|]] together, your children could share or draw heavenly treasures that could replace the worldly items in the box.

[[Matthew 7.12|Matthew 7:12]]
### Jesus asks me to treat others the way I want to be treated.

- Jesus’s teaching in [[Matthew 7.12|Matthew 7:12]]—also known as the Golden Rule—provides a simple guide for how to treat others. After reading this verse with your children, help them think of several ways to complete sentences like these: “I like it when others  for me. So I should  for others.”

[[Matthew 7.24|Matthew 7:24–27]][[Matthew 7.25|]][[Matthew 7.26|]][[Matthew 7.27|]]
### I can build on a strong foundation by following Jesus.

- To help your children understand [[Matthew 7.24|Matthew 7:24–27]][[Matthew 7.25|]][[Matthew 7.26|]][[Matthew 7.27|]], it might be fun to build small houses with blocks or other objects. Maybe they could build (and knock down) their houses while singing “[The Wise Man and the Foolish Man](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/childrens-songbook/the-wise-man-and-the-foolish-man?lang=eng)” (*Children’s Songbook*, 281). What could the rain and floods represent in our lives? How can we be like the wise man?
