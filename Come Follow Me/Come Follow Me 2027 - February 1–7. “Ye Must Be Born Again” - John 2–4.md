# February 1–7. “Ye Must Be Born Again”: John 2–4

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/06?lang=eng)

At a marriage feast in Cana, Jesus changed water into wine. John called it the “beginning of miracles” ([[John 2.11|John 2:11]]). That’s true in more than one sense. This first recorded public miracle can symbolize another miraculous beginning—the process of our hearts being transformed by the Savior and His gospel. It’s a lifelong miracle, and it begins with the decision to follow Jesus Christ, to change, and to live a better life through Him. This miracle can be so life-changing that being “born again” is one of the best ways to describe it ([[John 3.7|John 3:7]]). But rebirth is just the beginning of the path of discipleship. Christ’s words to the Samaritan woman at the well remind us that as we continue on this path, eventually the gospel will become “a well of water” inside us, “springing up into everlasting life” ([[John 4.14|John 4:14]]).

### Jesus Christ is a God of miracles.

As you read about Jesus changing water into wine in [[John 2.1|John 2:1–11]][[John 2.2|]][[John 2.3|]][[John 2.4|]][[John 2.5|]][[John 2.6|]][[John 2.7|]][[John 2.8|]][[John 2.9|]][[John 2.10|]][[John 2.11|]], consider the perspectives of people who were there, including Mary and others.

- If you had witnessed this miracle, how would you have felt about Jesus?

- What does this miracle teach you about Him?

### The house of the Lord is a sacred place.

- As you study [[John 2.13|John 2:13–22]][[John 2.14|]][[John 2.15|]][[John 2.16|]][[John 2.17|]][[John 2.18|]][[John 2.19|]][[John 2.20|]][[John 2.21|]][[John 2.22|]], what impresses you about Jesus’s attitude toward the temple?

### I must be “born again” to enter the kingdom of God.

To teach Nicodemus about the kingdom of God, Jesus used the phrase “born again.” As you read [[John 3.1|John 3:1–21]][[John 3.2|]][[John 3.3|]][[John 3.4|]][[John 3.5|]][[John 3.6|]][[John 3.7|]][[John 3.8|]][[John 3.9|]][[John 3.10|]][[John 3.11|]][[John 3.12|]][[John 3.13|]][[John 3.14|]][[John 3.15|]][[John 3.16|]][[John 3.17|]][[John 3.18|]][[John 3.19|]][[John 3.20|]][[John 3.21|]], consider:

- What do you know about birth? What does it suggest to you about the change we need to experience to enter God’s kingdom? (see also [[Moses 6.59|Moses 6:59–60]][[Moses 6.60|]]).

- How has the Savior changed you?

To help you ponder this, you could make a list of attributes or attitudes that make it hard to “see” or “enter into the kingdom of God” ([[John 3.3|John 3:3, 5]][[John 3.5|]]). What attitudes or attributes make it easier?

Nicodemus was confused about how being born again is even possible. How would you explain it? You may want to refer to Guide to the Scriptures, “[[GS Born Again, Born of God|Born Again, Born of God]]” (Gospel Library), or Elder David A. Bednar’s message “[[2016 April - Always Retain a Remission of Your Sins.md|Always Retain a Remission of Your Sins]]” (*Ensign* or *Liahona*, May 2016, 59–62).

See also “Jesus Teaches of Being Born Again” (video), Gospel Library.

> **Look for spontaneous learning moments.** The Savior took advantage of common, everyday experiences to teach spiritual truths—eating, filling a waterpot, feeling the wind blow. What spiritual lessons can you draw from the simple moments in your life? (see “[Learning at Home Can Be Planned but Also Spontaneous](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/teaching-in-the-saviors-way-2022/12-part-3/13-suggestions-for-a-variety-of-teaching?lang=eng&id=title5-p7#title5),” *Teaching in the Savior’s Way*, [31](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/teaching-in-the-saviors-way-2022/12-part-3/13-suggestions-for-a-variety-of-teaching?lang=eng&id=p6#p6)).

### The temple is a sacred place.

- The video “Jesus Cleanses the Temple” (Gospel Library) can help your children visualize [[John 2.13|John 2:13–17]][[John 2.14|]][[John 2.15|]][[John 2.16|]][[John 2.17|]]. Why was the temple sacred or important to Jesus? What things are sacred to us? How can we show that they are sacred?> 1:34

### Being baptized and confirmed is like being born again.

- Jesus compared baptism to being “born again.” Pictures of a newborn baby, a baptism, and a confirmation could help illustrate this (see *Gospel Art Book*, nos. [104](https://assets.churchofjesuschrist.org/86/0d/860d7750d5212692c97c6f6bdbb9db3f7fdc3a09/baptism_baptismal_font_girl_man.jpeg), [105](https://assets.churchofjesuschrist.org/8e/69/8e69acda5ab2b8d3a856e4397c574500984fd277/girl_being_confirmed.jpeg)). How are we like a newborn baby after we are baptized and confirmed? (see [[John 3.3|John 3:3–5]][[John 3.4|]][[John 3.5|]]).

- If you have children who have been baptized, they could share memories of their baptisms, or you could share your memories. What do we promise when we are baptized? (see [[Mosiah 18.10|Mosiah 18:10, 13]][[Mosiah 18.13|]]; [[D&C 20.37|Doctrine and Covenants 20:37]]). How does keeping this covenant help us enter the kingdom of God? (see [[John 3.5|John 3:5]]).

[[John 3.16|John 3:16–17]][[John 3.17|]]
### Heavenly Father loves me, so He gave me a Savior.

- To begin a discussion about [[John 3.16|John 3:16]], you and your children could draw a picture of a favorite gift you’ve received. Then you could read [[John 3.16|John 3:16]] to learn about the gift Heavenly Father gave us. How does this gift show His love? Perhaps your children could then draw a picture of the gift Heavenly Father gave us—His Son, Jesus Christ. They could also sing “[He Sent His Son](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/childrens-songbook/he-sent-his-son?lang=eng)” (*Children’s Songbook*, 34–35) and hold up their picture every time they sing a word like “Son,” “Jesus,” or “Savior.”

- How would you and your children complete a sentence like this one: “Because God loves me, He …”? As you read [[John 3.16|John 3:16]] together, replace the words “the world” with your own names. Share with each other how you have felt God’s love for you (for an example, see the story about Paul in Elder Michael T. Ringwood’s message “[[2022 April - For God So Loved Us.md|For God So Loved Us]]” \[*Liahona*, May 2022, 88–90\]).

- Looking at, drinking, or feeling some running water might help your children ponder the Savior’s teachings about “living water” in [[John 4.5|John 4:5–15]][[John 4.6|]][[John 4.7|]][[John 4.8|]][[John 4.9|]][[John 4.10|]][[John 4.11|]][[John 4.12|]][[John 4.13|]][[John 4.14|]][[John 4.15|]].
