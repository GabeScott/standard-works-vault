# July 12–18. “The Word of God Grew and Multiplied”: Acts 10–15

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/29?lang=eng)

Even though the Savior was no longer personally on the earth, He continued to lead His Church through revelation to His Apostles. This was especially clear when Peter learned, by revelation, of a new direction for the Lord’s Church: the gospel could be preached to everyone, not just the Jews (see [[Acts 10.34|Acts 10:34–35]][[Acts 10.35|]]). That seems obvious to us today, but it wasn’t at the time. For generations, the Jews had believed that because they were chosen by God, they should not “keep company” with Gentiles ([[Acts 10.28|Acts 10:28]]). So while some “glorified God” when they learned that the gospel would be shared with the Gentiles, others were angry (see [[Acts 11.18|Acts 11:18]]; [[Acts 13.50|13:50]]; [[Acts 14.4|14:4]]). However, despite resistance, the Apostles continued to preach, and “the word of God grew and multiplied” ([[Acts 12.24|Acts 12:24]]; see also [[Acts 10.34|Acts 10:34–43]][[Acts 10.35|]][[Acts 10.36|]][[Acts 10.37|]][[Acts 10.38|]][[Acts 10.39|]][[Acts 10.40|]][[Acts 10.41|]][[Acts 10.42|]][[Acts 10.43|]]; [[Acts 15.7|15:7–9]][[Acts 15.8|]][[Acts 15.9|]]). Today, the true and living Church continues to grow, because it is led the same way it was in Peter’s day—by Jesus Christ Himself, through revelation to His Apostles.

But those revelations didn’t give them all the answers they needed right away. As you read this chapter, consider how Cornelius and Peter continued to receive further light and truth. Questions like these might help:

- What kind of man was Cornelius? What prepared him to receive revelation? (see [[Acts 10.2|verses 2, 4, 22, 30–31]][[Acts 10.4|]][[Acts 10.22|]][[Acts 10.30|]][[Acts 10.31|]]).

- Why do you think the Lord may have wanted Cornelius and Peter to have their own spiritual experiences?

- How did Cornelius and Peter both act in faith as they gradually learned the Lord’s will? (see [[Acts 10.7|verses 7–8, 17–23, 29, 33]][[Acts 10.8|]][[Acts 10.17|]][[Acts 10.18|]][[Acts 10.19|]][[Acts 10.20|]][[Acts 10.21|]][[Acts 10.22|]][[Acts 10.23|]][[Acts 10.29|]][[Acts 10.33|]]).

### As a Christian, I believe in and follow Jesus Christ.

What does it mean to you to be a Christian? (see [[Acts 11.26|Acts 11:26]]). Consider why names are important. What things do you put your name on? [[Acts 10.38|Acts 10:38]]
### Jesus “went about doing good.”

- In [[Acts 10.38|Acts 10:38]], Peter remembered how the Savior “went about doing good.” Share with each other what this phrase means to you, and tell your children how you’ve seen them following Jesus’s example. You might also invite your children to pick a friend or family member they can do kind things for this week like Jesus would. Afterward, ask your children how it felt to serve that person.

[[Acts 11.26|Acts 11:26]]
### I am a Christian because I believe in and follow Jesus Christ.

- Perhaps you could write the word *Christian* and the word *Christ* and invite your children to find similarities between the two words.
