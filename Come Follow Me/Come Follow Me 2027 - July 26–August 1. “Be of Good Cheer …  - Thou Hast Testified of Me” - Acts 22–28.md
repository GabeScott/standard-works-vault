# July 26–August 1. “Be of Good Cheer … : Thou Hast Testified of Me”: Acts 22–28

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/31?lang=eng)

Because of Paul’s testimony, “all Jerusalem was in an uproar” ([[Acts 21.31|Acts 21:31]]). People were screaming and tearing their clothes—and about ready to do the same to Paul—when a Roman guard intervened. He locked Paul in the soldiers’ barracks, as much for Paul’s own safety as anything. That night, Jesus Christ came to Paul, “stood by him, and said, Be of good cheer, Paul.” And what did Paul have to be cheerful about? “As thou hast testified of me in Jerusalem,” the Savior continued, “so must thou bear witness also at Rome” ([[Acts 23.11|Acts 23:11]]).

Maybe that doesn’t sound like a cheerful assignment. But Paul understood that the message of the gospel of Jesus Christ is a joyful one, even if not everyone receives it with joy. And the call to share that message is also joyful, because no matter what happens, the Lord stands by His servants, just as He stood by Paul.

The people he spoke to had the power to condemn him to death. Yet he chose to boldly bear witness of Jesus Christ and “the heavenly vision” ([[Acts 26.19|Acts 26:19]]) he had received. What inspires you about his words?

Consider the opportunities you have to share your testimony of Jesus Christ. For example, do your friends know how you feel about Him? Does your family know how you gained your testimony? When was the last time you told someone about a spiritual experience you had?

When young Joseph Smith was mocked for telling about his First Vision, he was inspired by the way Paul testified of his vision (see [[Joseph Smith History 1.24|Joseph Smith—History 1:24–25]][[Joseph Smith History 1.25|]]).

- How would you summarize what Joseph Smith learned from Paul?

- What do you learn from these two witnesses of Jesus Christ?

Like the Church in Paul’s day (called a “sect” in [[Acts 28.22|Acts 28:22]]), the Church today is often “spoken against.”

- When people spoke against the Savior and His Church, how did Paul respond?

- What do you learn from Paul’s experience?

### Because of Jesus Christ, I can repent and have a clear conscience.

Paul told his accusers that he had “always a conscience void of offence toward God, and toward men” ([[Acts 24.16|Acts 24:16]]; see also [[Acts 23.1|23:1]]). What do you learn about Jesus Christ and repentance from these scriptures and Paul’s words?

## Ideas for Teaching Children

[[Acts 23.11|Acts 23:11]]
### Jesus Christ cares about me and will help me during hard times.

- The Savior’s words to Paul in [[Acts 23.11|Acts 23:11]] could help your children “be of good cheer” when they have hard times. Maybe you could work together to make a small poster displaying these words. As you do, you could talk about things that are hard for them and ways the Savior and His gospel help them be of good cheer.

- You and your children might also think of someone you can invite to be of good cheer—perhaps someone who is sad or worried. How can you help that person find joy in Jesus Christ?
