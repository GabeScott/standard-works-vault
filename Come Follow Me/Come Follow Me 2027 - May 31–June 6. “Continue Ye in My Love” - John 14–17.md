# May 31–June 6. “Continue Ye in My Love”: John 14–17

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/23?lang=eng)

Today we call it the “Last Supper,” but we don’t know if Jesus’s disciples understood that this would be their last meal with their Master before His death. Jesus, however, “knew that his hour was come” ([[John 13.1|John 13:1]]). He would soon face the suffering of Gethsemane, the betrayal and denial of His closest friends, and an agonizing death on the cross. Yet even with all of this looming before Him, Jesus’s focus was not on Himself but on His disciples. What would they need to know in the days and years ahead? What would comfort them and prepare them? Jesus’s tender teachings in [[John 14.1|John 14–17]][[John 14.2|]][[John 14.3|]][[John 14.4|]][[John 14.5|]][[John 14.6|]][[John 14.7|]][[John 14.8|]][[John 14.9|]][[John 14.10|]][[John 14.11|]][[John 14.12|]][[John 14.13|]][[John 14.14|]][[John 14.15|]][[John 14.16|]][[John 14.17|]][[John 14.18|]][[John 14.19|]][[John 14.20|]][[John 14.21|]][[John 14.22|]][[John 14.23|]][[John 14.24|]][[John 14.25|]][[John 14.26|]][[John 14.27|]][[John 14.28|]][[John 14.29|]][[John 14.30|]][[John 14.31|]] reveal how He feels about His disciples, then and now. Among the many comforting truths He shared was the reassurance that, in one sense, He will never leave us. “If ye keep my commandments,” He promised, “ye shall abide in my love” ([[John 15.10|John 15:10]]).

### I show my love for Jesus Christ by keeping His commandments.

As you read [[John 14.1|John 14–15]][[John 14.2|]][[John 14.3|]][[John 14.4|]][[John 14.5|]][[John 14.6|]][[John 14.7|]][[John 14.8|]][[John 14.9|]][[John 14.10|]][[John 14.11|]][[John 14.12|]][[John 14.13|]][[John 14.14|]][[John 14.15|]][[John 14.16|]][[John 14.17|]][[John 14.18|]][[John 14.19|]][[John 14.20|]][[John 14.21|]][[John 14.22|]][[John 14.23|]][[John 14.24|]][[John 14.25|]][[John 14.26|]][[John 14.27|]][[John 14.28|]][[John 14.29|]][[John 14.30|]][[John 14.31|]], you might note or mark each use of the word *love*. - How are love and commandments connected? What difference does it make to you to think of God’s commandments—and your obedience—as expressions of love?

Pages 29–30 of *For the Strength of Youth: A Guide for Making Choices* can help you think of ways you can show your love for God by keeping His commandments.

### The Holy Ghost helps me be a true disciple of Jesus Christ.

[[John 14.1|John 14:1–6]][[John 14.2|]][[John 14.3|]][[John 14.4|]][[John 14.5|]][[John 14.6|]]
### Jesus Christ is “the way, the truth, and the life.”

Take time to ponder the titles Jesus uses for Himself in [[John 14.6|John 14:6]].

- In what sense is He “the way” for you? The way to what?

- How is being “the truth” different from just teaching the truth?

- In your opinion, why is “the life” a good title for Jesus?

[[John 14.27|John 14:27]]; [[John 15.10|15:10–11]][[John 15.11|]]; [[John 16.20|16:20–24, 33]][[John 16.21|]][[John 16.22|]][[John 16.23|]][[John 16.24|]][[John 16.33|]]
### Jesus Christ offers me peace and joy.

Jesus knew the difficulties that were coming for His disciples, so He must have known that they needed to feel peace and joy. He also knows about your difficulties and that you need to feel peace and joy. Look for His messages to you in [[John 14.27|John 14:27]]; [[John 15.10|15:10–11]][[John 15.11|]]; and [[John 16.20|16:20–24, 33]][[John 16.21|]][[John 16.22|]][[John 16.23|]][[John 16.24|]][[John 16.33|]].

- How are the peace and joy He offers different from what the world offers?

Consider including a hymn like “[Where Can I Turn for Peace?](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/hymns/where-can-i-turn-for-peace?lang=eng)” as part of your study (*Hymns*, no. 129). How do you turn to the Savior for peace?

### As I abide in Christ, He will help me bring forth good fruit.

As you read [[John 15.1|John 15:1–12]][[John 15.2|]][[John 15.3|]][[John 15.4|]][[John 15.5|]][[John 15.6|]][[John 15.7|]][[John 15.8|]][[John 15.9|]][[John 15.10|]][[John 15.11|]][[John 15.12|]], ponder what it might mean to “abide in” Christ. What helps you feel connected to the Savior? What is the “fruit” you bring forth when you are connected to Him?

See also David A. Bednar, “[[2023 April - “Abide in Me, and I in You; Therefore Walk with Me”.md|Abide in Me, and I in You; Therefore Walk with Me]],” *Liahona*, May 2023, 123–26.

[[John 17.3|John 17:3]]
### Eternal life is to know Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.

What do you think it means to “know … God, and Jesus Christ”? How is that different from knowing about Them?

If you were to introduce Jesus Christ to someone, what would you say to help them know Him?

[[John 17.11|John 17:11, 21–23]][[John 17.21|]][[John 17.22|]][[John 17.23|]]
### Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ are perfectly united.

Note that the Savior prayed that His disciples may be one “even as”—or in the same way that—He and His Father are one ([[John 17.22|John 17:22]]). What does that mean for you? Think about your relationships—for example, with your spouse or other family members, with ward members, and with fellow Christians. How can you work toward the kind of unity that Jesus has with the Father?

See also D. Todd Christofferson, “[[2023 April - One in Christ.md|One in Christ]],” *Liahona*, May 2023, 77–80.

## Ideas for Teaching Children

[[John 14.6|John 14:6]]
### Jesus Christ is “the way.”

- Your children might enjoy taking turns leading you on a walk along a path as you read [[John 14.6|John 14:6]] together. How is Jesus like a “way” or a path? Maybe your children could suggest ways to complete this sentence: Jesus is “the way” to . Where does He lead us?

[[John 14.15|John 14:15]]
### I show my love for Jesus Christ by keeping His commandments.

- After reading [[John 14.15|John 14:15]], your children could take turns drawing pictures of themselves keeping a commandment while the rest of the children guess the commandment. Then you could talk about how keeping that commandment shows the Savior that we love Him.

- You could also help your children memorize [[John 14.15|John 14:15]]. For example, you could ask them to say “If ye love me” when you hold up a paper heart and “Keep my commandments” when you hold up the scriptures.

[[John 14.26|John 14:26–27]][[John 14.27|]]; [[John 15.26|15:26]]; [[John 16.13|16:13–14]][[John 16.14|]]
### The Holy Ghost guides, comforts, and testifies of truth.

- Perhaps your children could make a list of the things the Holy Ghost does for us. They could look in [[John 14.26|John 14:26–27]][[John 14.27|]]; [[John 15.26|15:26]]; [[John 16.13|16:13–14]][[John 16.14|]]; “[The Holy Ghost](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/childrens-songbook/the-holy-ghost?lang=eng)” (*Children’s Songbook*, 105); and Elder Gary E. Stevenson’s message “[[2017 April - How Does the Holy Ghost Help You.md|How Does the Holy Ghost Help You?]]” (*Ensign* or *Liahona*, May 2017, 117–20). Then you could share experiences when the Holy Ghost helped you in these ways.

[[John 15.1|John 15:1–9]][[John 15.2|]][[John 15.3|]][[John 15.4|]][[John 15.5|]][[John 15.6|]][[John 15.7|]][[John 15.8|]][[John 15.9|]]; [[John 17.3|17:3]]
### Heavenly Father and Jesus want me to know Them and stay close to Them.

- It might be fun to read [[John 15.1|John 15:1–9]][[John 15.2|]][[John 15.3|]][[John 15.4|]][[John 15.5|]][[John 15.6|]][[John 15.7|]][[John 15.8|]][[John 15.9|]] next to a vine, a tree, or another plant (see also the picture at the end of this outline). What happens to a branch when it is removed from the plant? You could talk about how we are like branches and what we can do to “abide in” (or stay close to) Jesus.

- After reading [[John 17.3|John 17:3]] together, you and your children could talk about things you do to get to know a friend better. How do we get to know Heavenly Father and Jesus better?

[[John 17.11|John 17:11, 21–23]][[John 17.21|]][[John 17.22|]][[John 17.23|]]
### Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ are perfectly united.

- What would help your children understand what Jesus taught about being “one”? Maybe you could talk about a favorite sports team and how they work together toward a common goal. Or you could listen to a choir or orchestra and discuss how the musicians unite to create beautiful music. Then you could read [[John 17.11|John 17:11, 21–23]][[John 17.21|]][[John 17.22|]][[John 17.23|]] and talk about how you can be more united, as Heavenly Father and Jesus are. Your children could take turns leading the actions in the song “[Do as I’m Doing](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/childrens-songbook/do-as-im-doing?lang=eng)” (*Children’s Songbook*, 276) to illustrate how we can be united with Heavenly Father and Jesus by following Them.
