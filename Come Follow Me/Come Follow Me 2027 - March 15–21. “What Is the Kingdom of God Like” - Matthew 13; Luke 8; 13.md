# March 15–21. “What Is the Kingdom of God Like?”: Matthew 13; Luke 8; 13

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/12?lang=eng)

The kingdom of God is not like the kingdoms we know in this world. So to help us understand, Jesus used parables—simple stories comparing God’s kingdom to things we are used to. “Unto what is the kingdom of God like?” He wondered aloud. “And \[to what\] shall I \[compare\] it?” ([[Luke 13.18|Luke 13:18]]). Taken together, these parables provide a rich understanding of God’s kingdom, with multiple valuable messages. Interestingly, many of Jesus’s parables compare the kingdom of God not to something grand but to something small and simple. A seed. Some leaven. A pearl. Does this mean the kingdom will be small—or, as one listener wondered, “Are there few that be saved?” ([[Luke 13.23|Luke 13:23]]). Yes, the kingdom of God may start small. But it is more precious than any earthly possession. It has an “uplifting” influence far beyond its size. And it grows—slowly, perhaps, but as surely as a seed planted in good soil.

### My heart must be prepared to receive the word of God.

Why is it that sometimes our hearts are receptive to truth, while at other times we’re tempted to resist it? Reading the parable of the sower is a good opportunity to think about how well you receive truth from the Lord.

- What can you do to make your heart like the “good ground” to receive God’s word? (see [[Matthew 13.8|Matthew 13:8, 23]][[Matthew 13.23|]]; [[Luke 8.8|Luke 8:8, 15]][[Luke 8.15|]]).

- What do you feel the roots represent in [[Matthew 13.6|Matthew 13:6, 21]][[Matthew 13.21|]]? (see also [[Luke 8.6|Luke 8:6, 13]][[Luke 8.13|]]).

- What might be some “thorns” that keep you from hearing and following His word? (see [[Matthew 13.7|Matthew 13:7, 22]][[Matthew 13.22|]]; [[Luke 8.7|Luke 8:7, 14]][[Luke 8.14|]]).

Pondering this parable could also influence how you read the parable in [[Luke 13.6|Luke 13:6–9]][[Luke 13.7|]][[Luke 13.8|]][[Luke 13.9|]].

- What is the “fruit” that the Lord seeks from you?

- Is there anything “cumbering” your ground that could be replaced with something more likely to “bear fruit”?

### I need to prepare my heart to receive the teachings of Jesus.

- Perhaps you and your children could go for a walk and examine different kinds of soil as you read [[Matthew 13.3|Matthew 13:3–8]][[Matthew 13.4|]][[Matthew 13.5|]][[Matthew 13.6|]][[Matthew 13.7|]][[Matthew 13.8|]] together. Notice where things grow and where they do not. Then you could talk about how our testimonies are like seeds. How do we help our testimony “seeds” grow? President Dallin H. Oaks’s message “[[2015 April - The Parable of the Sower.md|The Parable of the Sower]]” could help your children understand this parable (*Ensign* or *Liahona*, May 2015, 32–35).

- As you read [[Matthew 13.9|Matthew 13:9, 15]][[Matthew 13.15|]], your children could point to parts of their body when they hear them mentioned. How can we use these body parts to learn the teachings of Jesus?

[[Matthew 13.44|Matthew 13:44–46]][[Matthew 13.45|]][[Matthew 13.46|]]
### The gospel of Jesus Christ is a treasure.

- What could help your children visualize the parables in [[Matthew 13.44|Matthew 13:44–46]][[Matthew 13.45|]][[Matthew 13.46|]]? Perhaps you could hide pictures of a treasure chest and a pearl in the room and let the children find them. Why would someone give up everything they have for a beautiful pearl or a treasure in a field? You and your children could talk about why the gospel is valuable to you and what you would be willing to give up for the Savior.

- Your children could write, on small stones or paper “coins,” things they love about the gospel of Jesus Christ. Or they could find objects or pictures that represent “treasures” we have because of the gospel. They could put their “coins” or “treasures” in a box or chest and take turns taking one out and talking about why it is precious to them. A song like “[The Church of Jesus Christ](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/childrens-songbook/the-church-of-jesus-christ?lang=eng)” (*Children’s Songbook*, 77) could help them talk about reasons they’re thankful for the Savior’s Church.
