# June 21–27. “He Is Risen”: Matthew 28; Mark 16; Luke 24; John 20–21

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/26?lang=eng)

To many observers, the death of Jesus of Nazareth may have seemed like an ironic end to a remarkable life. Wasn’t this the man who raised Lazarus from the dead? Hadn’t He withstood murderous threats from the Pharisees time after time? He had power to heal blindness, leprosy, and all kinds of sickness. The winds and the seas obeyed Him. And yet here He was, hanging from a cross, declaring, “It is finished” ([[John 19.30|John 19:30]]). There may have been some sincere surprise in the mocking words “He saved others; himself he cannot save” ([[Matthew 27.42|Matthew 27:42]]). But we know that Jesus’s death was not the end of the story. The silence of the tomb was temporary; Christ’s saving work was just beginning. “He is risen” ([[Mark 16.6|Mark 16:6]]), and His teachings have not been silenced, for His disciples preach the gospel in “all nations,” trusting His promise that He is “with \[us always\], even unto the end of the world” ([[Matthew 28.19|Matthew 28:19–20]][[Matthew 28.20|]]).

### Because Jesus Christ was resurrected, everyone will live again.

These passages describe one of the most important events in the history of humankind: the Resurrection of Jesus Christ. As you read, put yourself in the place of the people who witnessed this event. If you had the chance to talk to these witnesses, what would you ask them? What do you imagine they would say to you about the Savior?

Consider how the Savior’s Resurrection has affected you—your outlook on life, your relationships, and your faith in Christ and His gospel.

Before Jesus’s Resurrection, no one had ever seen a resurrected being. They may not have fully understood what resurrection even means. What truths do you find in [[Luke 24.36|Luke 24:36–43]][[Luke 24.37|]][[Luke 24.38|]][[Luke 24.39|]][[Luke 24.40|]][[Luke 24.41|]][[Luke 24.42|]][[Luke 24.43|]] and [[John 20.1|John 20]][[John 20.2|]][[John 20.3|]][[John 20.4|]][[John 20.5|]][[John 20.6|]][[John 20.7|]][[John 20.8|]][[John 20.9|]][[John 20.10|]][[John 20.11|]][[John 20.12|]][[John 20.13|]][[John 20.14|]][[John 20.15|]][[John 20.16|]][[John 20.17|]][[John 20.18|]][[John 20.19|]][[John 20.20|]][[John 20.21|]][[John 20.22|]][[John 20.23|]][[John 20.24|]][[John 20.25|]][[John 20.26|]][[John 20.27|]][[John 20.28|]][[John 20.29|]][[John 20.30|]][[John 20.31|]] about resurrection? ### I can have faith in Jesus Christ, even though I can’t see Him.

- You might show your children the painting above while you read [[John 20.24|John 20:24–29]][[John 20.25|]][[John 20.26|]][[John 20.27|]][[John 20.28|]][[John 20.29|]].
