# June 28–July 4. “Ye Shall Be Witnesses unto Me”: Acts 1–5

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/27?lang=eng)

The Apostles had just watched the resurrected Savior ascend into the sky and disappear into a cloud. The Church He had founded was now in their care. The mission to “teach all nations” now rested on them ([[Matthew 28.19|Matthew 28:19]]). It’s easy to understand why “they looked steadfastly toward heaven” as their Master left ([[Acts 1.10|Acts 1:10]]). Then two angels appeared. “Ye men of Galilee,” they said, “why stand ye gazing up into heaven?” ([[Acts 1.11|Acts 1:11]]). Implied in the angels’ question was an invitation not to look back but to look forward. “This same Jesus,” they continued, “shall so come in like manner as ye have seen him go into heaven” ([[Acts 1.11|Acts 1:11]]). In other words, Jesus will return, so what are we standing around for? There’s work to do!

And that’s what the Apostles did. Empowered by the Holy Ghost and guided by revelation, they bore fearless testimony, brought thousands to repentance and baptism, and spread Christ’s Church. As they did, it became clear that even though the Church had been entrusted to earthly servants, it continued to be the work of God, and Jesus Christ continued to lead it.

For more about the book of Acts, see Guide to the Scriptures, “[[GS Acts of the Apostles|Acts of the Apostles]],” Gospel Library.

### The Apostles of Jesus Christ are called by God through revelation.

Think about groups or organizations you’ve been a part of outside the Church. How do they choose their leaders? What qualifications do they look for? With those thoughts in mind, read about how the Apostle Matthias was called in [[Acts 1.15|Acts 1:15–26]][[Acts 1.16|]][[Acts 1.17|]][[Acts 1.18|]][[Acts 1.19|]][[Acts 1.20|]][[Acts 1.21|]][[Acts 1.22|]][[Acts 1.23|]][[Acts 1.24|]][[Acts 1.25|]][[Acts 1.26|]]. What do you notice about the Lord’s way of choosing leaders?

President Gordon B. Hinckley said this about calling a new Apostle: “The procedure is peculiar to the Lord’s church. There is no seeking for office, no jockeying for position, no campaigning to promote one’s virtues. Contrast the Lord’s way with the way of the world. The Lord’s way is quiet, it is a way of peace, it is without fanfare or monetary costs. It is without egotism or vanity or ambition. Under the Lord’s plan, those who have responsibility to select officers are governed by one overriding question: ‘Whom would the Lord have?’ There is quiet and thoughtful deliberation. And there is much of prayer to receive the confirmation of the Holy Spirit that the choice is correct” (“[[1994 April - God Is at the Helm.md|God Is at the Helm]],” *Ensign*, May 1994, 53).

- How have you gained a witness that today’s Apostles are called by God?

### Jesus Christ can bless others through me.

- Perhaps your children would enjoy doing actions to go with the story in [[Acts 3.1|Acts 3:1–11]][[Acts 3.2|]][[Acts 3.3|]][[Acts 3.4|]][[Acts 3.5|]][[Acts 3.6|]][[Acts 3.7|]][[Acts 3.8|]][[Acts 3.9|]][[Acts 3.10|]][[Acts 3.11|]], such as putting out their hands for money and leaping for joy. Then you could talk about how Jesus blessed someone through Peter and John, even though they didn’t have money. How can He use us to bless others? Singing “[Have I Done Any Good?](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/hymns/have-i-done-any-good?lang=eng)” (*Hymns*, no. 223) might spark some ideas.

[[Acts 4.32|Acts 4:32]]
### Followers of Jesus are “of one heart and of one soul.”

- After reading [[Acts 4.32|Acts 4:32]] together, you and your children could talk about what it means to be “of one heart and of one soul.” Maybe you could draw a large heart and write inside it the names of people who feel your love. Then you might think of people who need to feel your love. How can we reach out to them?
