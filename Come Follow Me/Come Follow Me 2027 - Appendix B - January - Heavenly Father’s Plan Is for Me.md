# January: Heavenly Father’s Plan Is for Me

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/54-appendix-b/001-plan-of-happiness?lang=eng)

Many people wonder about the purpose of their lives, their place in the universe, and their relationship with God. Sadly, too many of God’s children face such questions and find no answers. But that won’t be true of the children in your class. They will likely look back on sacred moments with you, with their parents, and with others when they learned eternal truths like these: They are each a child of God. He sent them here to learn and grow. And because of Jesus Christ, they can live with Him again someday. These truths are part of what we call Heavenly Father’s plan of salvation. And His Son, Jesus Christ, makes it all possible.

See also Topics and Questions, “[Plan of Salvation](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/plan-of-salvation-study-guide?lang=eng),” Gospel Library.

## Heavenly Father sent me here to learn, grow, and prepare to return to Him.

It might be fun to create a simple path in the classroom, with stops along the way that represent our pre-earth life, our life on earth, and our return to Heavenly Father. You can use papers or pictures on the floor to make your path, with chairs at each stop. Then you could lead children from stop to stop and learn together about parts of God’s plan for us. For example:

1. “I lived with Heavenly Father.” Consider singing together a song like “[I Lived in Heaven](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/childrens-songbook/i-lived-in-heaven?lang=eng)” (*Children’s Songbook*, 4) or “[O My Father](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/hymns/o-my-father?lang=eng)” (*Hymns*, no. 292). As you sing, help the children notice phrases that teach us about our life with Heavenly Father before we were born (like “Heav’nly Father presented a beautiful plan” or “Was I nurtured near thy side?”). Emphasize God’s love, His plan, and His choice to send Jesus Christ to be our Savior.

2. “Heavenly Father sent me here.” Below are some questions, scriptures, and pictures you could discuss with the children.

3. “I can return to Heavenly Father.” After reading together [[Enos 1.27|Enos 1:27]] or [[Revelation 21.3|Revelation 21:3–4]][[Revelation 21.4|]], you and the children could share with each other what it might be like to be with Heavenly Father and Jesus again. How do you think you will feel? What will you say to Them?

## Jesus followed Heavenly Father’s plan, and so can I.

- Read [[John 6.38|John 6:38]] and [[Moses 4.2|Moses 4:2]] together, and help the children find out what Jesus Christ came to earth to do. Then you could invite the children to list as many examples as they can of Jesus doing what Heavenly Father wanted Him to do. If they need help, they can look at pictures in *Come, Follow Me* or the *Gospel Art Book* ([nos. 28–62](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-art-book/new-testament?lang=eng&id=p1-figure35_p1#p1)). This could lead to a conversation about ways we can follow Heavenly Father’s plan like Jesus did.
