# December 20–26. “Good Tidings of Great Joy”: Christmas

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/52?lang=eng)

Why does the birth of a baby bring such great joy? Perhaps because a new baby can symbolize hope. There’s something about a brand-new life, full of possibilities, that invites us to ponder what life might hold for that child and what wonderful things he or she will accomplish. Never has this been truer than at the birth of the Son of God, Jesus Christ. Never has there been more hope placed in a child, and never has there been one born with so much promise.

Mary, Jesus’s mother, was perhaps the greatest witness of this sacred event. After the stress of the crowded inn, the miracle of the holy birth, and the visit of the shepherds, who told of angels and light from heaven, “Mary kept all these things, and pondered them in her heart” ([[Luke 2.19|Luke 2:19]]). Perhaps it would be good to follow Mary’s example this Christmas: to ponder in your heart the things you have learned about the Savior this year. How did He fulfill His redemptive mission in the accounts you have read? And more importantly, how has He done so in your life?

> **A daily study plan.** The Christmas season can be a wonderful but busy time. The following study plan includes daily devotionals that can help you take a moment each day to remember and think about Jesus Christ and why we celebrate Christmas. You can use these suggestions to guide your study individually or as a family.


## Suggested Daily Study

### Monday: Prophets foretold the coming of Jesus Christ and testified of His mission.

As you look forward to Christmas Day, you might start by reading the words of prophets who also looked forward to His coming:

- [[Isaiah 7.14|Isaiah 7:14]]; [[Isaiah 9.6|9:6–7]][[Isaiah 9.7|]]. Isaiah’s prophecies came during a difficult time for God’s people. What do you find in his words that offers hope?

- [[Micah 5.2|Micah 5:2]]. What does this prophecy teach you about the Savior, His mission, and His character?

- [[Zechariah 9.9|Zechariah 9:9]]; [[Zechariah 12.10|12:10]]. Why do you think God revealed these details about the Savior’s life so many years beforehand?

- [[Mosiah 3.5|Mosiah 3:5–10]][[Mosiah 3.6|]][[Mosiah 3.7|]][[Mosiah 3.8|]][[Mosiah 3.9|]][[Mosiah 3.10|]]. How would you summarize the angel’s message to King Benjamin? What do you feel is the message to you in these verses?

These prophecies might lead you to ponder the blessing of continuing revelation in our day. What have our latter-day prophets and apostles taught us to help “earth receive her King”? (“[Joy to the World](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/hymns/joy-to-the-world?lang=eng),” *Hymns*, no. 201). What have you learned from them about Jesus Christ?

### Wednesday: God trusted Mary and Joseph with the responsibility to raise His Son.

- What do Mary’s words in [[Luke 1.46|Luke 1:46–55]][[Luke 1.47|]][[Luke 1.48|]][[Luke 1.49|]][[Luke 1.50|]][[Luke 1.51|]][[Luke 1.52|]][[Luke 1.53|]][[Luke 1.54|]][[Luke 1.55|]] reveal to you about her faith? What important truths about Jesus Christ did she understand?

- When Joseph learned that Mary was pregnant, he could have chosen to break his engagement with her publicly, but he didn’t (see [[Matthew 1.19|Matthew 1:19]]). What does this tell you about Joseph’s character? What else do you learn about him from [[Matthew 1.20|Matthew 1:20–25]][[Matthew 1.21|]][[Matthew 1.22|]][[Matthew 1.23|]][[Matthew 1.24|]][[Matthew 1.25|]]?

### Friday: I can seek Jesus Christ and bear witness of Him.

- What impresses you about their words or actions after they found the Savior?
