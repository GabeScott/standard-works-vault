# For Parents—Preparing Your Children for a Lifetime of Discipleship

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/53-appendix-a?lang=eng)

Because He loves you, trusts you, and knows your potential, Heavenly Father has given you the opportunity to help your children enter and progress along His covenant path (see [[D&C 68.25|Doctrine and Covenants 68:25–28]][[D&C 68.26|]][[D&C 68.27|]][[D&C 68.28|]]). This includes helping them prepare to make and keep sacred covenants, such as the covenant of baptism and the covenants made in the temple. Through these covenants, your children will strengthen their relationship with Heavenly Father and the Savior, Jesus Christ.

There are many ways to help your children along the covenant path, and Heavenly Father will help you discover the best ways for you and your children. As you seek inspiration, keep in mind that not all learning happens during scheduled lessons. In fact, part of what makes learning at home so powerful is the opportunity to learn by example and through small, simple moments—the kind that occur naturally in the flow of daily living. Just as following the covenant path is a consistent, lifelong process, so is learning about the covenant path. (See “[Home and Family](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/teaching-in-the-saviors-way-2022/12-part-3/13-suggestions-for-a-variety-of-teaching?lang=eng&id=title2-figure1_p1#title2),” in *Teaching in the Savior’s Way* \[2024\], 30–31.)

Below are some ideas that could lead to further inspiration.

## Baptism and Confirmation

Nephi taught that “the gate by which \[we\] enter” the covenant path “is repentance and baptism by water” ([[2 Nephi 31.17|2 Nephi 31:17]]). When we are baptized, we covenant to follow the Savior. Your efforts to help your children prepare for baptism and confirmation can put them firmly on that path. These efforts begin with teaching about faith in Jesus Christ and repentance. You can also teach about how we continue to covenant each week to follow Jesus Christ by partaking of the sacrament.

Here are some resources that can help you: [[2 Nephi 31.1|2 Nephi 31]][[2 Nephi 31.2|]][[2 Nephi 31.3|]][[2 Nephi 31.4|]][[2 Nephi 31.5|]][[2 Nephi 31.6|]][[2 Nephi 31.7|]][[2 Nephi 31.8|]][[2 Nephi 31.9|]][[2 Nephi 31.10|]][[2 Nephi 31.11|]][[2 Nephi 31.12|]][[2 Nephi 31.13|]][[2 Nephi 31.14|]][[2 Nephi 31.15|]][[2 Nephi 31.16|]][[2 Nephi 31.17|]][[2 Nephi 31.18|]][[2 Nephi 31.19|]][[2 Nephi 31.20|]][[2 Nephi 31.21|]]; “[Baptism and Confirmation](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/friend/2023/08-se?lang=eng),” *Friend*, Aug. 2023 (special issue); Topics and Questions, “[Baptism](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/baptism-study-guide?lang=eng),” Gospel Library.

- Whenever you have an experience that strengthens your faith in Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ, share it with your child. Doing this can help them see that faith can grow stronger throughout our lives. What are some things your child can do to develop faith in Christ before they are baptized? How can they keep their faith growing afterward?

- When your child makes a wrong choice, talk about the joy of repentance and the Atonement of Jesus Christ. When your child seeks forgiveness, forgive freely. And when you make a wrong choice, you might tell your child about the joy you feel when you repent. Find opportunities to testify that because Jesus Christ suffered and died for our sins, we can change and become more like Him.

- You could tell your child about your baptism. Perhaps you could show pictures and share memories. Describe your feelings when you were baptized, and talk about how your baptismal covenant has helped you come to know Jesus Christ. How does this covenant continue to bless your life?

- When there is a baptism in your family or your ward, you could take your child to see it. Afterward, you and your child could talk about what you both saw and felt. Talk about the symbolism of wearing white clothing and being fully immersed in the water. If possible, talk to the person who was baptized and ask questions like “How did you make this decision? How did you prepare?”

- Whenever you notice your child doing something they promised to do, give sincere praise. Point out that keeping commitments prepares us to keep the covenant we make at baptism. What do we promise God when we are baptized? What does He promise us? (See [[Mosiah 18.8|Mosiah 18:8–10, 13]][[Mosiah 18.9|]][[Mosiah 18.10|]][[Mosiah 18.13|]].)

- When you and your child have a sacred experience together (such as at church, while reading the scriptures, or while serving someone), you might tell each other about your spiritual feelings or impressions. Doing this often can help your child discover that the Spirit speaks to us in many ways, and it can help them recognize how He speaks to them.

- Perhaps you could tell your child how becoming a member of the Church of Jesus Christ has blessed you. How has it brought you closer to Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ? This could lead to a conversation about how your child can help with the Lord’s work as a member of His Church.

- You and your child might think of ways to make the sacrament a sacred and joyful event in your family. How can your family focus on Jesus Christ during the sacrament and show love for Him?

- The [Friend](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/magazines/friend?lang=eng) magazine often includes articles, stories, and activities to help children prepare for baptism and confirmation. Your child could choose some and then read them with you.

## Priesthood Power, Authority, and Keys

The priesthood is the authority and power of God, by which He blesses His children. “All Church members who keep their covenants … are blessed with God’s priesthood power” (*General Handbook: Serving in The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints*, [3.6](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/3-priesthood-principles?lang=eng&id=title_number19-p85#title_number19), Gospel Library). This power strengthens us and assists us in accomplishing God’s work of salvation and exaltation.

When men and women serve in Church callings, they do so with priesthood authority, under the direction of people who hold priesthood keys. Heavenly Father’s children—His sons and His daughters—are blessed as they understand the priesthood.

We receive ordinances by the authority of the priesthood. Worthy male Church members are ordained to priesthood offices. When authorized by someone who holds priesthood keys, they can perform priesthood ordinances.

To learn more about the priesthood, see Russell M. Nelson, “[[2024 April - Rejoice in the Gift of Priesthood Keys.md|Rejoice in the Gift of Priesthood Keys]],” *Liahona*, May 2024, 119–23; Russell M. Nelson, “[[2019 October - Spiritual Treasures.md|Spiritual Treasures]],” *Ensign* or *Liahona*, Nov. 2019, 76–79; Dallin H. Oaks, “[[2020 April - The Melchizedek Priesthood and the Keys.md|The Melchizedek Priesthood and the Keys]],” *Ensign* or *Liahona*, May 2020, 69–72; “[Priesthood Principles](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/3-priesthood-principles?lang=eng),” chapter 3 in *General Handbook*.

- How can you make priesthood ordinances a consistent part of your family life? For example, you could help your child spiritually prepare for the sacrament. You could encourage them to seek priesthood blessings when they are sick or need comfort or direction. Make it a habit to point out how the Lord blesses your family through priesthood power.

- As you read the scriptures together, watch for opportunities to discuss how God blesses people through His power. When has God blessed you through His priesthood? Consider sharing your experiences with your child.

- You could review together President Russell M. Nelson’s message “[[2019 October - Spiritual Treasures.md|Spiritual Treasures]]” (*Ensign* or *Liahona*, Nov. 2019, 76–79). Talk about how receiving priesthood ordinances and keeping covenants has brought God’s power into your life. For a list of some ways we are blessed by priesthood power, see *General Handbook*, [3.5](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/3-priesthood-principles?lang=eng&id=p_tggs1-p_rnciQ#p_tggs1).

- What is a servant of the Lord like? You and your child could discuss this question and look for answers in [[D&C 121.36|Doctrine and Covenants 121:36–42]][[D&C 121.37|]][[D&C 121.38|]][[D&C 121.39|]][[D&C 121.40|]][[D&C 121.41|]][[D&C 121.42|]]. Then, whenever you notice your child (or anyone else) living one of the principles or attributes in these verses, you could point it out. Share how that trait blesses your family or others.

- When using keys to unlock a door or start a car, you might take a moment to compare your keys to the keys that priesthood leaders hold. (For a definition of priesthood keys, see *General Handbook*, [3.4.1](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/3-priesthood-principles?lang=eng&id=title_number8-p42#title_number8).) What do priesthood keys “unlock” or “start” for us? See also Gary E. Stevenson, “[[2016 April - Where Are the Keys and Authority of the Priesthood.md|Where Are the Keys and Authority of the Priesthood?]],” *Ensign* or *Liahona*, May 2016, 29–32; “Where Are the Keys?” (video), Gospel Library.> 2:51


- When you are set apart for a calling, invite your child to watch, if possible. Let your child see you fulfill your calling, and look for appropriate ways they can help you. You could also talk with your child about how you feel the Lord’s power as you serve.

## Being Baptized and Confirmed for Ancestors

Through the covenants and ordinances of the temple, all of God’s children can return to live with Him after this life. At the beginning of the year your child turns 12, they are old enough to baptized and confirmed in the temple for ancestors. Your child’s participation in these ordinances can help them feel the Spirit and strengthen their relationship with God and Jesus Christ.

- Go to the house of the Lord as often as you can. When you do, you could talk to your child about why you are going and how the temple helps you feel closer to Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ.

- You and your child could review and discuss the temple recommend questions together. You can find them on pages 69–70 of *For the Strength of Youth: A Guide for Making Choices* (2026). Talk to your child about what happens in a temple recommend interview, and share why having a current temple recommend is important to you. (See also Ronald A. Rasband, “[[2020 October - Recommended to the Lord.md|Recommended to the Lord]],” *Ensign* or *Liahona*, Nov. 2020, 22–25.)

- Perhaps you could read [[Malachi 4.6|Malachi 4:6]] with your child and discuss how your hearts could turn to your ancestors. Then you might learn more about your ancestors together by exploring your family history on [FamilySearch.org](https://www.familysearch.org) (a ward temple and family history consultant can help). If any ancestors need to be baptized and confirmed, you and your child could plan how to complete those ordinances. (See also David A. Bednar, “[[2020 April - “Let This House Be Built unto My Name”.md|Let This House Be Built unto My Name]],” *Ensign* or *Liahona*, May 2020, 84–87.)

## Receiving a Patriarchal Blessing

A patriarchal blessing can be a source of guidance, comfort, and inspiration. It contains personal counsel from Heavenly Father to us and helps us understand our eternal identity and purpose. You can help your child prepare to receive a patriarchal blessing by teaching them about the significance and sacred nature of patriarchal blessings.

To learn more, see Topics and Questions, “[Patriarchal Blessings](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/patriarchal-blessings-study-guide?lang=eng),” Gospel Library.

- If you have received a patriarchal blessing, share with your child how you prepared to receive it, how it has helped you come closer to God, and how you use the blessing in your life. You could also invite your child to talk to other family members who have received their patriarchal blessings.

- You might review together Elder Randall K. Bennett’s message “[[2023 April - Your Patriarchal Blessing—Inspired Direction from Heavenly Father.md|Your Patriarchal Blessing—Inspired Direction from Heavenly Father]]” and Elder Kazuhiko Yamashita’s message “[[2023 April - When to Receive Your Patriarchal Blessing.md|When to Receive Your Patriarchal Blessing]]” (*Liahona*, May 2023, 42–43, 88–90). Share with each other what you learn from these messages about why Heavenly Father wants us to receive a patriarchal blessing. To learn about the process of receiving a patriarchal blessing, see *General Handbook*, [18.17](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/18-priesthood-ordinances-and-blessings?lang=eng&id=title_number54-p240#title_number54).

- If you have ancestors who received patriarchal blessings, it might be inspiring to read some of them with your child. You can request the blessings of ancestors who have died by going to [pb.ChurchofJesusChrist.org/pbrequest/home/family](https://pb.churchofjesuschrist.org/pbrequest/home/personal).

- After your child has received a patriarchal blessing, consider inviting any family members who were present to record their feelings and to share them with your child.

## Receiving the Endowment

God wants to endow, or bless, all His children with “power from on high” ([[D&C 95.8|Doctrine and Covenants 95:8]]). We go to the temple to receive our own endowment only once, but the covenants we make with God and the spiritual power He gives us as part of the endowment can bless us every day of our lives.

- Consider displaying a picture of the temple in your home. This could give you opportunities to talk often with your child about your love for the Lord, His house, and the covenants you have made there.

- You and your child could explore [temples.ChurchofJesusChrist.org](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/temples) and read articles like “[About the Temple Endowment](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/temples/what-is-temple-endowment)” and “[Prepare for the House of the Lord](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/temples/prepare-for-your-temple-visit)” together. Invite your child to ask any questions they have about the temple. For guidance on appropriately talking about the temple experience outside of the temple, see Elder David A. Bednar’s message “[[2019 April - Prepared to Obtain Every Needful Thing.md|Prepared to Obtain Every Needful Thing]]” (*Ensign* or *Liahona*, May 2019, 101–4; see especially the section titled “[[2019 April - Prepared to Obtain Every Needful Thing.md|Home-Centered and Church-Supported Learning and Temple Preparation]]”).

- As you and your child participate in or witness ordinances (such as the sacrament or a blessing of healing), you might take a moment to discuss the symbolism involved in the ordinance. What do the symbols represent? How do they testify of Jesus Christ? This can help your child prepare to ponder the symbolic meaning of temple ordinances and how they testify of Jesus Christ.

- One of the best ways to prepare for temple covenants is to keep the baptismal covenant (see [[Mosiah 18.8|Mosiah 18:8–10, 13]][[Mosiah 18.9|]][[Mosiah 18.10|]][[Mosiah 18.13|]]). Help your child see how they are keeping their covenant to serve God and keep His commandments—and how the Lord is blessing them. This can build your child’s confidence that they can keep other covenants with God.

- To help your child gain a testimony of covenants, talk openly and frequently about how your temple covenants guide your choices and help you grow closer to Jesus Christ. The covenants of the temple endowment are described in *General Handbook*, [27.2](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/27-temple-ordinances-for-the-living?lang=eng&id=title_number11-p69#title_number11).

- If you have received the endowment, tell your child about how the temple garment helps you remember your covenants with Jesus Christ (see “Sacred Temple Clothing” \[video\], Gospel Library; J. Anette Dennis, “[[2024 April - Put Ye On the Lord Jesus Christ.md|Put Ye On the Lord Jesus Christ]],” *Liahona*, May 2024, 10–13).

## Serving a Mission

Elder David A. Bednar taught: “The single most important thing you can do to prepare for a call to serve is to *become* a missionary long before you *go* on a mission. … The issue is not going on a mission; rather, the issue is becoming a missionary and serving throughout our entire life with all of our heart, might, mind, and strength. … You are preparing for a lifetime of missionary work” (“[[2005 October - Becoming a Missionary.md|Becoming a Missionary]],” *Ensign* or *Liahona*, Nov. 2005, 45–46). The experiences your child has becoming a missionary will bless them eternally, not just for the time they might serve as a missionary.

To learn more, see Russell M. Nelson, “[[2022 April - Preaching the Gospel of Peace.md|Preaching the Gospel of Peace]],” *Liahona*, May 2022, 6–7; M. Russell Ballard, “[[2022 April - Missionary Service Blessed My Life Forever.md|Missionary Service Blessed My Life Forever]],” *Liahona*, May 2022, 8–10; *Preach My Gospel: A Guide to Sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ* (2023).

- One way your child can learn about the importance of missionary work is from your own example. Always be alert to opportunities to show love for others, to share with them your feelings about the Savior and His gospel, and to invite them to “come and see” ([[John 1.46|John 1:46]]). Consider how you might include your child in your efforts. (See Gary E. Stevenson, “[[2022 April - Love, Share, Invite.md|Love, Share, Invite]],” *Liahona*, May 2022, 84–87.) Help your child look for natural opportunities to share the gospel. They could talk about positive Church experiences with friends or think of someone to invite to a Church or family activity. As your child practices sharing the gospel, you might discuss together what they have learned about being a missionary.

- Seek opportunities for your family to interact with missionaries. For example, you could invite the missionaries to teach in your home. You could also ask the missionaries about what they did (or wish they had done) to prepare to serve. How does missionary service help them draw closer to Jesus Christ?

- If you served a mission, talk regularly with your child about your experiences. Or you might invite friends or family members who served missions to talk about theirs. You could also talk about ways you’ve shared the gospel throughout your life.

- Your child might enjoy opportunities to teach a gospel principle in your home. Or your child could try answering questions that a missionary might be asked, such as “What is the Book of Mormon?” or “What do you believe about Jesus Christ?” (see Ahmad S. Corbitt, “[[2023 April - Do You Know Why I as a Christian Believe in Christ.md|Do You Know Why I as a Christian Believe in Christ?]],” *Liahona*, May 2023, 119–21).

- Consider how you could help your child become comfortable talking to people. What are some good ways to start a conversation? Help your child learn how to listen to others and respond in loving ways like Jesus would.

- You could prepare your child to be a missionary by teaching them about other cultures and faiths. This might include helping your child recognize, respect, and celebrate the good and true principles in others’ beliefs.

## Receiving the Sealing Ordinance

In the temple, a husband and wife can be united for eternity through the sealing ordinance. Even though this ordinance might seem far away for your son or daughter, there are small, simple, and consistent things you can do together to help them prepare for this wonderful blessing.

To learn more, see Topics and Questions, “[Sealing](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/sealing?lang=eng),” Gospel Library.

- You and your child could read together “[The Family: A Proclamation to the World](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/the-family-a-proclamation-to-the-world/the-family-a-proclamation-to-the-world?lang=eng)” (Gospel Library). What does the proclamation teach about happiness in family life and successful marriages? Perhaps you could choose together one of the principles listed in the proclamation and learn more about it (the [Guide to the Scriptures](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs?lang=eng) can help). How could your family practice applying that principle more fully? How could it bless your lives?

- In marriage, husbands and wives work together as equal partners. You could illustrate this principle by teaching your child what a partnership is and then completing a task together. You might also read with your child Elder Ulisses Soares’s message “[[2022 October - In Partnership with the Lord.md|In Partnership with the Lord]]” (*Liahona*, Nov. 2022, 42–45). Why is it important that husbands and wives treat each other as equal partners? What can we do to practice being good partners now?

- If you are married, you could talk with your child about the things you feel you’re doing well as a couple, the things you’re learning, and the ways you’re trying to improve. You might also find ways to show your child by your example that your spouse and your marriage are important to you.

- You might review with your child President Henry B. Eyring’s message “[[2024 April - All Will Be Well Because of Temple Covenants.md|All Will Be Well Because of Temple Covenants]]” (*Liahona*, May 2024, 24–27). Then you could discuss how temple covenants can bless a marriage. If you and your spouse have been sealed in the temple, you could also testify to your child of how your covenants have strengthened your relationship with each other and with the Lord. How do you strive to honor your covenants? How do they bring you power, protection, and peace?

- When family decisions need to be made, you could hold family councils and discussions. In these councils, invite your child to participate fully, and ensure that all family members’ opinions are heard and valued. These discussions can model healthy communication and kindness in family relationships, even when not everyone sees things the same way.

- Your child will learn much about handling conflict through your example. Perhaps you could ponder how you might demonstrate patience and compassion when your family has disagreements or conflict. You might also discuss with your child how handling conflict in Christlike ways can lay a foundation for a happy marriage.

- You and your child could read together [[D&C 121.41|Doctrine and Covenants 121:41–42]][[D&C 121.42|]]. How could the principles in these verses apply to marriage? You could ask your child which principle they would like to focus on and discuss how improving ourselves can prepare us for all kinds of relationships.
