# Using Come, Follow Me—For Home and Church

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/00b?lang=eng)

## Who Is This Resource For?

*Come, Follow Me* is for anyone who wants to learn from the scriptures—individually, as a family, and in Church classes. If you haven’t studied the scriptures regularly in the past, this resource can help you get started. If you already have a good scripture study habit, this resource can help you have more meaningful experiences. *Come, Follow Me* is available in both print and digital formats. The digital version has updated resources to help your learning and teaching.

## Individuals and Families at Home

The home is the ideal place to learn the gospel. Your teachers at church can support you, and other ward members can encourage you. But to survive spiritually, you and your family need daily nourishment from “the good word of God” ([[Moroni 6.4|Moroni 6:4]]; see also Russell M. Nelson, “[Opening Remarks](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/ensign/2018/11/saturday-morning-session/opening-remarks?lang=eng),” *Ensign* or *Liahona*, Nov. 2018, 6–8).

Use this resource in any way that helps you. The study guides highlight some eternal truths found in the New Testament. They also suggest ideas and activities to help you study the scriptures individually, with your family, or with friends. As you study, follow the Spirit’s guidance to find eternal truths that are meaningful to you. Look for God’s messages for you, and follow the impressions you receive.

## Teachers and Learners at Church

*Come, Follow Me—For Home and Church* is the curriculum for Sunday classes in Primary, youth and adult Sunday School, Young Women classes, and Aaronic Priesthood quorum meetings. If you teach in any of these settings, use *Come, Follow Me* for your personal study and as you prepare to teach. Start by having your own experiences in the scriptures. Your most important preparation will occur as you search the scriptures and seek the Holy Ghost’s inspiration. Look for eternal truths that help you become more like Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ. *Come, Follow Me* can help you identify some of these truths and understand the context of the scriptures.

Keep in mind that learning the gospel, at its best, is home centered and Church supported. In other words, your main responsibility is to support the people you teach in their efforts to learn and live the gospel at home. Give them opportunities to share their experiences, thoughts, and questions about the scripture passages they’ve read at home. Invite them to share the eternal truths they have found. This is more important than covering a certain amount of material.

### Youth and Adult Sunday School Classes

We gather in Sunday School classes to support and encourage each other as we strive to follow Jesus Christ. A simple way to support learners is to ask a question like “What has the Holy Ghost taught you this week as you studied the scriptures?” Answers could lead to meaningful discussions that build faith in Jesus Christ and His gospel.

Then you could invite discussion based on the study suggestions in *Come, Follow Me*. For instance, a study idea might suggest searching [[Luke 4.16|Luke 4:16–32]][[Luke 4.17|]][[Luke 4.18|]][[Luke 4.19|]][[Luke 4.20|]][[Luke 4.21|]][[Luke 4.22|]][[Luke 4.23|]][[Luke 4.24|]][[Luke 4.25|]][[Luke 4.26|]][[Luke 4.27|]][[Luke 4.28|]][[Luke 4.29|]][[Luke 4.30|]][[Luke 4.31|]][[Luke 4.32|]], looking for words that describe the Savior’s mission. You might ask class members to share any thoughts or feelings that these words inspired for them. Or you could spend some time looking for these words as a class.

### Primary

Your preparation to teach Primary begins as you study the scriptures personally and with your family. As you do, be open to spiritual impressions and insights from the Holy Ghost about the children in your Primary class. Be prayerful, and the Spirit can inspire you with ideas to help them learn the gospel of Jesus Christ.

As you prepare to teach, you might gain additional inspiration by exploring the teaching ideas in *Come, Follow Me*. Each study guide in this resource has a section titled “Ideas for Teaching Children.” Think of these ideas as suggestions to spark your inspiration. You know the children in your Primary class—and you will get to know them even better as you interact with them in class. God knows them too, and He will inspire you with the best ways to teach and bless them.

Some children in your class might have already done some *Come, Follow Me* activities with their families. That’s OK. Repetition is good. Consider inviting the children to share with each other what they learned at home—though you should also plan ways for children to participate even if they aren’t learning at home. Children learn gospel truths more effectively when they are taught these truths repeatedly through a variety of activities. If you find that a learning activity is effective for the children, consider repeating it, especially if you are teaching younger children. You might also review an activity from a previous lesson.
