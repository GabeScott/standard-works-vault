# November: I Am Blessed by Priesthood Power

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/54-appendix-b/011-priesthood?lang=eng)

The priesthood is God’s authority and power. He uses it to bless His children—old and young, male and female. Through this power, God gives us guidance for our lives. He gives us help and strength to follow Jesus Christ. Angels from heaven came to restore priesthood authority to Joseph Smith, and because of that, we can receive ordinances, make covenants, and access God’s power. Without priesthood authority, the Church would be a helpful organization that tries to do good in this world, but it would be powerless to prepare us for eternal life. How will you help your children see how God’s priesthood power blesses them, personally and daily?

## God blesses His children with priesthood power.

- “All Church members who keep their covenants—women, men, and children—are blessed with God’s priesthood power” (*General Handbook: Serving in The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints*, 3.6, Gospel Library). One way we receive this blessing is through priesthood ordinances, such as baptism (see [[D&C 84.20|Doctrine and Covenants 84:20]]). To help the children discover this, you could show them the pictures at the end of this lesson (see also the video “God’s Priesthood Power” in Gospel Library). They could take turns pointing to one of the pictures and talking about what is happening. Help them understand how God is blessing His children with His priesthood power in the picture. You could also tell them how Heavenly Father has blessed you through His priesthood power.> 5:26


## God’s work is accomplished by priesthood authority.

- The children could share with each other some experiences when they have seen someone be ordained to a priesthood office or get set apart for a calling (or you could tell about experiences you have had; see *General Handbook*, 3.4.3.1). Help them notice how those experiences are similar to what the Savior did with His Apostles (see “Jesus Chooses His Apostles,” in *New Testament Stories*). You might tell the children how you have seen people act with God’s authority as they serve in His Church—including how you’ve experienced His power in your service.> 2:43


## God’s work is directed by priesthood keys.

- Priesthood keys are needed to organize Jesus Christ’s Church. The Savior holds all priesthood keys. He gives all the keys to the prophet, and the prophet shares those keys with certain Church leaders, such as stake presidents, bishops, and quorum presidents. That means they can direct how the priesthood is used to bless others in the Church. To help the children learn about priesthood keys, you could show them a set of keys and ask them to name some things we need a key for. What would happen if we lost our keys? (see the video “Where Are the Keys?” in Gospel Library). Then you could talk about what blessings are unlocked by priesthood keys (for example, the gift of the Holy Ghost or being sealed as a family).> 2:51
