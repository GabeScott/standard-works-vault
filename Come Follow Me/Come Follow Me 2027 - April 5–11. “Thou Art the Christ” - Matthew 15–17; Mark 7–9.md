# April 5–11. “Thou Art the Christ”: Matthew 15–17; Mark 7–9

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/15?lang=eng)

It’s a bit surprising that the Pharisees and Sadducees would demand that Jesus show them “a sign from heaven.” His many miracles were well known, not to mention His powerful teachings or the many ways He had fulfilled ancient prophecies. No, the problem wasn’t a lack of signs—it was a lack of willingness to “discern the signs” and accept them. (See [[Matthew 16.1|Matthew 16:1–4]][[Matthew 16.2|]][[Matthew 16.3|]][[Matthew 16.4|]].)

Peter, like the Pharisees and Sadducees, witnessed the Savior’s miracles and heard His teachings. But Peter’s testimony that Jesus is “the Christ, the Son of the living God” did not come from seeing signs. It was revealed to him by our “Father which is in heaven.” Revelation is the rock upon which the Savior built His Church then and now—revelation from heaven to His servants, including Apostles like Peter. And this is the rock upon which we can build our discipleship—revelation that Jesus is the Christ and that His servants hold “the keys of the kingdom.” When we are built upon this foundation, “the gates of hell shall not prevail against \[us\].” (See [[Matthew 16.15|Matthew 16:15–19]][[Matthew 16.16|]][[Matthew 16.17|]][[Matthew 16.18|]][[Matthew 16.19|]].)

### I honor God with a pure heart.

In these verses, the Savior compare the lips or mouth to the heart. He does this to teach spiritual truths.

- What is the difference between honoring God with your lips and honoring Him with your heart?

- Think about the care you take not to put anything dangerous into your body. How can you show even more care about what you put into your heart?

Note the questions Jesus asked His disciples in [[Matthew 16.13|Matthew 16:13–17]][[Matthew 16.14|]][[Matthew 16.15|]][[Matthew 16.16|]][[Matthew 16.17|]]. How would you answer these questions?

The “keys of the kingdom of heaven” that the Savior promised to give Peter are priesthood keys ([[Matthew 16.19|Matthew 16:19]]). But what are priesthood keys? You can find a simple definition in Guide to the Scriptures, “[[GS Keys of the Priesthood|Keys of the Priesthood]]” (Gospel Library).

Why do we need priesthood keys? Here are some resources that help answer this question: [[D&C 65.2|Doctrine and Covenants 65:2]]; [[D&C 107.18|107:18–20]][[D&C 107.19|]][[D&C 107.20|]]; [[D&C 110.11|110:11–16]][[D&C 110.12|]][[D&C 110.13|]][[D&C 110.14|]][[D&C 110.15|]][[D&C 110.16|]]; [[D&C 128.9|128:9–11]][[D&C 128.10|]][[D&C 128.11|]]; President Russell M. ### When seeking greater faith, I can start with the faith I have.

President Jeffrey R. Holland used the account in [[Mark 9.14|Mark 9:14–29]][[Mark 9.15|]][[Mark 9.16|]][[Mark 9.17|]][[Mark 9.18|]][[Mark 9.19|]][[Mark 9.20|]][[Mark 9.21|]][[Mark 9.22|]][[Mark 9.23|]][[Mark 9.24|]][[Mark 9.25|]][[Mark 9.26|]][[Mark 9.27|]][[Mark 9.28|]][[Mark 9.29|]] to teach about what to do when our faith is challenged (see “[[2013 April - “Lord, I Believe”.md|Lord, I Believe]],” *Ensign* or *Liahona*, May 2013, 93–95). Consider reading these verses from that perspective. Questions like these can help:

- What does the Spirit teach you about faith as you read about this miracle?

- How has Heavenly Father helped you build the faith you already have?

- What can you do to build upon that faith?

President Holland made three observations about this story. How might his counsel apply to you?

“Observation number one regarding this account is that when facing the challenge of faith, the father asserts his strength first and only then acknowledges his limitation. His initial declaration is affirmative and without hesitation: ‘Lord, I believe.’ I would say to all who wish for more faith, remember this man! In moments of fear or doubt or troubling times, hold the ground you have already won, even if that ground is limited. …

“The second observation is a variation of the first. When problems come and questions arise, do not start your quest for faith by saying how much you do *not* have, leading as it were with your ‘unbelief.’ … I am not asking you to pretend to faith you do not have. I *am* asking you to be true to the faith you *do* have. …

“Last observation: When doubt or difficulty come, do not be afraid to ask for help. If we want it as humbly and honestly as this father did, we can get it” (“[[2013 April - “Lord, I Believe”.md|Lord, I Believe]],” 93–94).

## Ideas for Teaching Children

[[Matthew 16.13|Matthew 16:13–17]][[Matthew 16.14|]][[Matthew 16.15|]][[Matthew 16.16|]][[Matthew 16.17|]]
### I can know that Jesus Christ is the Son of God.

- Peter’s testimony in [[Matthew 16.15|Matthew 16:15–16]][[Matthew 16.16|]] and Jesus’s response in [[Matthew 16.17|verse 17]] can help your children understand what a testimony is and inspire them to seek their own. For example, you could help your children find Peter’s testimony of Jesus Christ in these verses. Then they could find who helped Peter gain that testimony.

- You could ask your children what they would say if someone asked them, “Who is Jesus?” Then they could read [[Matthew 16.13|Matthew 16:13–17]][[Matthew 16.14|]][[Matthew 16.15|]][[Matthew 16.16|]][[Matthew 16.17|]] to find out how Peter answered that question. Share with each other how you gained your testimonies of Jesus (see “[Read the Book of Mormon and Pray](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/music/hymns-for-home-and-church/read-the-book-of-mormon-and-pray?lang=eng),” *Hymns—For Home and Church*, Gospel Library).

[[Matthew 16.15|Matthew 16:15–19]][[Matthew 16.16|]][[Matthew 16.17|]][[Matthew 16.18|]][[Matthew 16.19|]]
### Jesus Christ leads His Church through revelation.

- To help your children understand that the Church is led by revelation, you could show them a rock as you read [[Matthew 16.18|Matthew 16:18]] together. Repeat with them the phrase “Upon this rock I will build my church,” adding actions to go with the words. Then you could talk about how the Savior leads His Church by instructing His prophets and apostles. Maybe you could share examples of how you have been blessed through the inspired words of prophets and apostles.

You could also show your children some keys and ask them what keys are used for. You might even let them try unlocking things with the keys. What blessings do priesthood keys unlock for us? [[Matthew 17.19|Matthew 17:19–20]][[Matthew 17.20|]]
### Jesus Christ can work miracles as I exercise faith.

- To learn the principle of faith found in [[Matthew 17.19|Matthew 17:19–20]][[Matthew 17.20|]], your children could read with you what President Russell M. Nelson said about mustard seeds, faith, and mountains in “[[2021 April - Christ Is Risen; Faith in Him Will Move Mountains.md|Christ Is Risen; Faith in Him Will Move Mountains]]” (*Liahona*, May 2021, 102–3). Then they could draw a picture of a mountain and write on it something that is hard for them. Then they could name some things they can do to gain more faith in Jesus Christ (President Nelson’s message has [[2021 April - Christ Is Risen; Faith in Him Will Move Mountains.md|five suggestions]]). Each time they name something, they can move the mountain to another place in the room.
