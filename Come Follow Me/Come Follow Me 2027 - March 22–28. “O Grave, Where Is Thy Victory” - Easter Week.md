# March 22–28. “O Grave, Where Is Thy Victory?”: Easter Week

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/14?lang=eng)

During the last week of the Savior’s life, many around Him were participating in the traditions of Passover. They prepared meals, sang songs, and gathered to remember the deliverance of the house of Israel from slavery in Egypt. Families listened to the story of the destroying angel passing over the doors marked with lambs’ blood. Amid all these celebrations so rich with the symbolism of deliverance, most people had no idea that Jesus Christ, the Lamb of God, was about to deliver them from a different kind of slavery. Through His sacrifice and Resurrection, He would deliver them from the slavery of sin and death. There were some, however, who did recognize Jesus as their eternal Deliverer. Ever since, disciples of Jesus Christ have borne witness to all the world that “he is risen”! ([[Matthew 28.6|Matthew 28:6]]).

## Suggested Daily Study

> **A daily study plan.** Some people like to spend the week of Easter studying the last week of the Savior’s life. Here is a simple day-by-day study schedule you might follow. We don’t know exactly which day of the week some of these events happened, and there are many other important things that happened during that sacred week. But this suggested schedule can give you something meaningful to ponder each day as you prepare to celebrate the Savior’s Resurrection on Easter Sunday. You can use this to guide your study individually or as a family.
