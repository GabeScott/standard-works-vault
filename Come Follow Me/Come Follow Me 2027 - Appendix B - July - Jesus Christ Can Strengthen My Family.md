# July: Jesus Christ Can Strengthen My Family

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/54-appendix-b/007-family?lang=eng)

Take a moment to think about the families of the children you teach. You’ll probably notice that each family is different, with their own strengths and challenges. But they all have one thing in common: Each is precious to Heavenly Father. The fact that He has made families “central to \[His\] plan for the eternal destiny of His children” is a sign of love. He certainly knows the challenges and flaws of earthly families, but He has a plan for that too—the same plan He prepared to address our personal imperfections. He sent His Son, Jesus Christ. Jesus gave us the teachings and example upon which “happiness in family life is most likely to be achieved” (“[The Family: A Proclamation to the World](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/the-family-a-proclamation-to-the-world/the-family-a-proclamation-to-the-world?lang=eng),” Gospel Library). And when we make mistakes, the Savior has the power to heal, strengthen, and reunite us. So, as you teach the children about families, teach them also about Jesus Christ.

See also Topics and Questions, “[Family](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/family-study-guide?lang=eng),” Gospel Library.

## Families are important in Heavenly Father’s plan.

- To help explain why Heavenly Father wants us to be born into strong families, you could show a picture of a baby. Ask the children to help you make a list of what this baby needs in order to have a life of health and happiness. Then you could talk about how our families help provide those things. The song “Fathers and Mothers” could help (*Hymns for Home and Church*, Gospel Library). You might also show a picture of your family members and talk about how Heavenly Father has blessed you through them. Give the children a chance to talk about their families too.

## Jesus honored His parents, and so can I.

- To help your children see how Jesus honored His parents, you could read together [[Luke 2.42|Luke 2:42–51]][[Luke 2.43|]][[Luke 2.44|]][[Luke 2.45|]][[Luke 2.46|]][[Luke 2.47|]][[Luke 2.48|]][[Luke 2.49|]][[Luke 2.50|]][[Luke 2.51|]] or “The Boy Jesus” (in *New Testament Stories*). You could ask the children to stand up every time they hear an example of Jesus honoring His Heavenly Father, Mary, or Joseph (see especially verses [[Luke 2.49|49]] and [[Luke 2.51|51]]).> 1:50


- You could also read together [[Ephesians 6.1|Ephesians 6:1–2]][[Ephesians 6.2|]] and invite the children to take turns acting out something they can do to honor their parents. Let the other children guess what they are doing.

## Jesus taught me how to have a happy family.

- “Happiness in family life is … founded upon the teachings of the Lord Jesus Christ” (“[The Family: A Proclamation to the World](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/the-family-a-proclamation-to-the-world/the-family-a-proclamation-to-the-world?lang=eng)”). Help the children identify some of those teachings that help us build happy families. Here are some scriptures you could search together: [[Matthew 25.35|Matthew 25:35–36]][[Matthew 25.36|]]; [[John 13.34|John 13:34–35]][[John 13.35|]]; [[3 Nephi 18.21|3 Nephi 18:21]]; [[D&C 64.10|Doctrine and Covenants 64:10]]. Maybe you could build a small home out of blocks together, adding a block after reading each verse.

- To help the children think of how they can strengthen their family, you could give them each a paper house (see this week’s activity page). On one side of the house, they could draw a picture of their family and show it to the class. On the other side, they could write or draw one thing they can do to show love to their family. Invite them to show the paper house to their family.
