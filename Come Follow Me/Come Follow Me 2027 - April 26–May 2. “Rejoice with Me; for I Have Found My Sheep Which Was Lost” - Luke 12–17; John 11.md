# April 26–May 2. “Rejoice with Me; for I Have Found My Sheep Which Was Lost”: Luke 12–17; John 11

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/18?lang=eng)

In most situations, 99 out of 100 would be considered excellent—but not when these numbers refer to beloved children of God. In that case, even one lost soul merits a thorough, diligent search “until \[we\] find it” ([[Luke 15.4|Luke 15:4]]). Then the rejoicing can begin, for “joy shall be in heaven over one sinner that repenteth, more than over ninety and nine just persons, which need no repentance” ([[Luke 15.7|Luke 15:7]]). In truth, there are none who “need no repentance.” We all need rescuing. And we all can participate in the rescue, rejoicing together over every soul who is saved (see [[D&C 18.15|Doctrine and Covenants 18:15–16]][[D&C 18.16|]]).

### Heavenly Father rejoices when the lost are found.

How should we feel toward someone who has sinned or is otherwise “lost”? Consider how the Pharisees and scribes in [[Luke 15.1|Luke 15:1–2]][[Luke 15.2|]] might have answered this question. Jesus’s response can be found in three parables in [[Luke 15.3|Luke 15:3–7]][[Luke 15.4|]][[Luke 15.5|]][[Luke 15.6|]][[Luke 15.7|]], [[Luke 15.8|8–10]][[Luke 15.9|]][[Luke 15.10|]], [[Luke 15.11|11–32]][[Luke 15.12|]][[Luke 15.13|]][[Luke 15.14|]][[Luke 15.15|]][[Luke 15.16|]][[Luke 15.17|]][[Luke 15.18|]][[Luke 15.19|]][[Luke 15.20|]][[Luke 15.21|]][[Luke 15.22|]][[Luke 15.23|]][[Luke 15.24|]][[Luke 15.25|]][[Luke 15.26|]][[Luke 15.27|]][[Luke 15.28|]][[Luke 15.29|]][[Luke 15.30|]][[Luke 15.31|]][[Luke 15.32|]]. As you read, think about what Jesus was trying to teach the scribes and Pharisees.

- What do you learn about Heavenly Father from these parables?

It’s probably important that Jesus used multiple parables to make this point—perhaps because each one teaches something unique. It might be helpful, then, to compare the three parables. For example, you could identify what was lost in each parable, why it was lost, how it was found, and how people reacted when it was found.

- What messages does Jesus have for those who are “lost”?

- What messages does He have for people who seek those who are lost?

### Gratitude brings me closer to God.

Why do you think nine of the lepers didn’t return to thank the Savior? What additional blessings did the leper who returned receive because he gave thanks?

You might also ponder the Savior’s words “Thy faith hath made thee whole” ([[Luke 17.19|Luke 17:19]]). In your opinion, how are gratitude and faith related? How do both help us become whole? The video “President Russell M. Nelson on the Healing Power of Gratitude” (Gospel Library) can help you ponder these questions.
