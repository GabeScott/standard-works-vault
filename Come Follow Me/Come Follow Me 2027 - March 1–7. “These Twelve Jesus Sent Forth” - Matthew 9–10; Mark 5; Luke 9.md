# March 1–7. “These Twelve Jesus Sent Forth”: Matthew 9–10; Mark 5; Luke 9

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/10?lang=eng)

Word of Jesus’s healing miracles was spreading quickly. Multitudes followed Him, hoping for relief from their sicknesses. But when the Savior looked at the multitudes, He saw more than their physical pain. Filled with compassion, He saw “sheep having no shepherd” ([[Matthew 9.36|Matthew 9:36]]). “The harvest truly is plenteous,” He observed, “but the labourers are few” ([[Matthew 9.37|Matthew 9:37]]). So He called twelve Apostles, “gave them power,” and sent them to teach and minister “to the lost sheep of the house of Israel” ([[Matthew 10.1|Matthew 10:1, 6]][[Matthew 10.6|]]). Today the need for more laborers to serve Heavenly Father’s children is just as great. There are still twelve Apostles, but there are more disciples of Jesus Christ than ever before—people who can declare to all the world, “The kingdom of heaven is at hand” ([[Matthew 10.7|Matthew 10:7]]).

## Ideas for Learning at Home and at Church

### I can exercise faith in Jesus Christ in times of uncertainty and fear.

What impresses you about Jairus’s expression of faith in [[Mark 5.22|Mark 5:22–24]][[Mark 5.23|]][[Mark 5.24|]]? How do you think Jairus might have felt as he watched Jesus talk with the woman in [[Mark 5.22|verses 25–34]][[Mark 5.23|]][[Mark 5.24|]] and then as he received the news in [[Mark 5.35|verse 35]]? How do you think the Savior’s words in [[Mark 5.36|verse 36]] would have helped Jairus in this moment?

As you ponder this story, you might think about things you are afraid of.

- What do you know about Jesus Christ that helps you believe in Him, even in times of fear?

- How can you follow the Savior’s invitation to “be not afraid, only believe”?

As you read the rest of the story in [[Mark 5.37|Mark 5:37–43]][[Mark 5.38|]][[Mark 5.39|]][[Mark 5.40|]][[Mark 5.41|]][[Mark 5.42|]][[Mark 5.43|]], ponder how you can maintain your faith in Christ in seemingly hopeless situations.

### “Spiritual stretching” is required to access the Savior’s power.

Her physical stretching was symbolic of her spiritual stretching. … When the Savior knows you truly want to reach up to Him—when He can feel that the greatest desire of your heart is to draw His power into your life—you will be led by the Holy Ghost to know exactly what you should do” (“[[2017 April - Drawing the Power of Jesus Christ into Our Lives.md|Drawing the Power of Jesus Christ into Our Lives]],” *Ensign* or *Liahona*, May 2017, 41–42).

- What obstacles do you need to overcome as you reach out to the Savior in faith?

- What do you learn about Jesus Christ from the way He responded to this woman’s faith?

## Ideas for Teaching Children

[[Matthew 9.18|Matthew 9:18–30]][[Matthew 9.19|]][[Matthew 9.20|]][[Matthew 9.21|]][[Matthew 9.22|]][[Matthew 9.23|]][[Matthew 9.24|]][[Matthew 9.25|]][[Matthew 9.26|]][[Matthew 9.27|]][[Matthew 9.28|]][[Matthew 9.29|]][[Matthew 9.30|]]; [[Mark 5.22|Mark 5:22–43]][[Mark 5.23|]][[Mark 5.24|]][[Mark 5.25|]][[Mark 5.26|]][[Mark 5.27|]][[Mark 5.28|]][[Mark 5.29|]][[Mark 5.30|]][[Mark 5.31|]][[Mark 5.32|]][[Mark 5.33|]][[Mark 5.34|]][[Mark 5.35|]][[Mark 5.36|]][[Mark 5.37|]][[Mark 5.38|]][[Mark 5.39|]][[Mark 5.40|]][[Mark 5.41|]][[Mark 5.42|]][[Mark 5.43|]]
### Jesus Christ has the power to heal me.

- As you and your children read [[Matthew 9.18|Matthew 9:18–30]][[Matthew 9.19|]][[Matthew 9.20|]][[Matthew 9.21|]][[Matthew 9.22|]][[Matthew 9.23|]][[Matthew 9.24|]][[Matthew 9.25|]][[Matthew 9.26|]][[Matthew 9.27|]][[Matthew 9.28|]][[Matthew 9.29|]][[Matthew 9.30|]] or [[Mark 5.22|Mark 5:22–43]][[Mark 5.23|]][[Mark 5.24|]][[Mark 5.25|]][[Mark 5.26|]][[Mark 5.27|]][[Mark 5.28|]][[Mark 5.29|]][[Mark 5.30|]][[Mark 5.31|]][[Mark 5.32|]][[Mark 5.33|]][[Mark 5.34|]][[Mark 5.35|]][[Mark 5.36|]][[Mark 5.37|]][[Mark 5.38|]][[Mark 5.39|]][[Mark 5.40|]][[Mark 5.41|]][[Mark 5.42|]][[Mark 5.43|]], ask them to find things Jesus said. Maybe you can help them memorize these phrases. You could also help them think of situations in which Jesus might say similar things to us. What do we learn about the power of Jesus Christ from these stories?

- Consider using “Jesus Heals a Woman and Raises Jairus’s Daughter” in *New Testament Stories* to help your children learn the stories in [[Mark 5.22|Mark 5:22–43]][[Mark 5.23|]][[Mark 5.24|]][[Mark 5.25|]][[Mark 5.26|]][[Mark 5.27|]][[Mark 5.28|]][[Mark 5.29|]][[Mark 5.30|]][[Mark 5.31|]][[Mark 5.32|]][[Mark 5.33|]][[Mark 5.34|]][[Mark 5.35|]][[Mark 5.36|]][[Mark 5.37|]][[Mark 5.38|]][[Mark 5.39|]][[Mark 5.40|]][[Mark 5.41|]][[Mark 5.42|]][[Mark 5.43|]]. Try asking questions that will help your children put themselves in the stories. For example, at certain points, you could ask how they would feel if they were Jairus, the woman, or someone watching what was happening. You could also let your children repeat the stories back to you in their own words. Maybe you and your children could share experiences when someone you know received a priesthood blessing of healing.> 3:33


[[Matthew 10.1|Matthew 10:1–10]][[Matthew 10.2|]][[Matthew 10.3|]][[Matthew 10.4|]][[Matthew 10.5|]][[Matthew 10.6|]][[Matthew 10.7|]][[Matthew 10.8|]][[Matthew 10.9|]][[Matthew 10.10|]]
### Jesus called twelve Apostles and gave them power to do His work.

- To help your children understand [[Matthew 10.1|Matthew 10:1–10]][[Matthew 10.2|]][[Matthew 10.3|]][[Matthew 10.4|]][[Matthew 10.5|]][[Matthew 10.6|]][[Matthew 10.7|]][[Matthew 10.8|]][[Matthew 10.9|]][[Matthew 10.10|]], you could share with them “Jesus Chooses His Apostles” in *New Testament Stories*. As you do, invite your children to look for what the Apostles do to help Jesus Christ.> 2:43


- Are your children familiar with the twelve Apostles we have today? You can find pictures of them on ChurchofJesusChrist.org or in a  issue of the [Liahona](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/magazines/liahona?lang=eng). Perhaps you could help your children match their names with their pictures. Or you could share brief messages these Apostles have given about Jesus Christ (see, for example, the video collection “[Special Witnesses of Christ](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/media/collection/special-witnesses-of-christ-video-collection)” in the Gospel Library). You could also share how the Lord’s Apostles have helped you have stronger faith in Jesus Christ.

[[Matthew 10.29|Matthew 10:29–31]][[Matthew 10.30|]][[Matthew 10.31|]]
### Heavenly Father notices and cares for me.

- When children feel small and unimportant, Jesus’s words in [[Matthew 10.29|Matthew 10:29–31]][[Matthew 10.30|]][[Matthew 10.31|]] can give them reassurance. Perhaps you could read these verses to them while they look at a picture of a sparrow (like the one below). You might tell them about a time when you felt small, like this sparrow, but you knew Heavenly Father noticed and cared about you as His child. Singing or listening to “[His Eye Is on the Sparrow](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/music/hymns-for-home-and-church/his-eye-is-on-the-sparrow?lang=eng)” (*Hymns—For Home and Church*, Gospel Library) could reinforce this message.

[[Luke 9.23|Luke 9:23–25]][[Luke 9.24|]][[Luke 9.25|]]
### Following Jesus Christ requires sacrifice.

- To introduce [[Luke 9.23|Luke 9:23–25]][[Luke 9.24|]][[Luke 9.25|]], you could share with your children a time when you gave up something to get something better. After reading the verses together, you could take turns naming some things in our lives that Jesus asks us to give up to follow Him. Why are we willing to make these sacrifices?

- You could also help your children think of other examples in the scriptures where someone gave up something in order to follow Jesus Christ. Help them think of a sacrifice they could make to show their love for Him.

> **Emphasize the example of Jesus Christ.** “We can place Jesus Christ at the center of teaching and learning by recognizing and emphasizing that He is the perfect example of all gospel principles. As disciples, we don’t just follow principles—we follow Jesus Christ. As we focus on the Savior’s perfect example, the Holy Ghost will testify of Him and inspire us to follow Him” (*Teaching in the Savior’s Way*, [6](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/teaching-in-the-saviors-way-2022/04-part-1/05-teach-about-jesus-christ?lang=eng&id=p4#p4)).
