# October 4–10. “I Can Do All Things Through Christ Which Strengtheneth Me”: Philippians; Colossians

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/41?lang=eng)

Paul wrote his epistles to the Philippians and Colossians while he was a prisoner in Rome. But these letters don’t have the tone you might expect from someone in prison. Paul spoke more about joy, rejoicing, and thanksgiving than he did about afflictions and trials: “Christ is preached,” he said, “and I therein do rejoice, yea, and will rejoice” ([[Philippians 1.18|Philippians 1:18]]). And “though I be absent in the flesh, yet am I with you in the spirit, joying and beholding … the steadfastness of your faith in Christ” ([[Colossians 2.5|Colossians 2:5]]). Certainly, “the peace of God” that Paul experienced in his difficult circumstances “passeth all understanding” ([[Philippians 4.7|Philippians 4:7]]), but it was nonetheless real. In our own trials, we can feel this same peace and “rejoice in the Lord alway” ([[Philippians 4.4|Philippians 4:4]]). We can, like Paul, rely upon Jesus Christ, “in whom we have redemption” ([[Colossians 1.14|Colossians 1:14]]). And we can say, as Paul did, “I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me” ([[Philippians 4.13|Philippians 4:13]]; see also [[Colossians 1.11|Colossians 1:11]]).

For more about these epistles, see Guide to the Scriptures, “[[GS Philippians, Epistle to|Philippians, Epistle to]]” and “[[GS Colossians, Epistle to|Colossians, Epistle to]],” Gospel Library.

## Ideas for Learning at Home and at Church

[[Philippians 1.6|Philippians 1:6]]; [[Philippians 2.12|2:12–13]][[Philippians 2.13|]]
### God is working “a good work” in me.

Paul wrote to the Philippian Saints of the “good work” that God had “begun” in them ([[Philippians 1.6|Philippians 1:6]]). He also told them, “Work out your own salvation” ([[Philippians 2.12|Philippians 2:12]]). These verses might inspire you to ponder how your work to receive salvation aligns with God’s work to give you salvation. What does [[Philippians 2.13|Philippians 2:13]] add to your understanding? How is “God \[working\] in you”?

See also [[Ephesians 2.8|Ephesians 2:8]]; [[2 Nephi 25.23|2 Nephi 25:23]].

[[Philippians 3.4|Philippians 3:4–14]][[Philippians 3.5|]][[Philippians 3.6|]][[Philippians 3.7|]][[Philippians 3.8|]][[Philippians 3.9|]][[Philippians 3.10|]][[Philippians 3.11|]][[Philippians 3.12|]][[Philippians 3.13|]][[Philippians 3.14|]]
### The gospel of Jesus Christ is worth every sacrifice.

Paul gave up much when he converted to the gospel of Jesus Christ, including the influential role he held in Jewish society as a Pharisee. In [[Philippians 3.4|Philippians 3:4–14]][[Philippians 3.5|]][[Philippians 3.6|]][[Philippians 3.7|]][[Philippians 3.8|]][[Philippians 3.9|]][[Philippians 3.10|]][[Philippians 3.11|]][[Philippians 3.12|]][[Philippians 3.13|]][[Philippians 3.14|]], look for what Paul gained because of his sacrifices.

- How did Paul feel about his sacrifices? (see [[Philippians 3.8|verses 8, 13–14]][[Philippians 3.13|]][[Philippians 3.14|]]).

- What have you sacrificed for the gospel of Jesus Christ? What have you received?

- Are there any sacrifices you feel inspired to make to become a more dedicated disciple of the Savior?

See also [[3 Nephi 9.19|3 Nephi 9:19–20]][[3 Nephi 9.20|]]; [[D&C 58.2|Doctrine and Covenants 58:2–5]][[D&C 58.3|]][[D&C 58.4|]][[D&C 58.5|]]; Topics and Questions, “[Sacrifice](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/sacrifice-study-guide?lang=eng),” Gospel Library.

### I can find joy in my circumstances because of Jesus Christ.

Paul experienced severe trials, yet he continually rejoiced because of Jesus Christ. Review some of Paul’s trials (see, for example, [[2 Corinthians 11.23|2 Corinthians 11:23–28]][[2 Corinthians 11.24|]][[2 Corinthians 11.25|]][[2 Corinthians 11.26|]][[2 Corinthians 11.27|]][[2 Corinthians 11.28|]]). Then, as you read [[Philippians 4.1|Philippians 4:1–13]][[Philippians 4.2|]][[Philippians 4.3|]][[Philippians 4.4|]][[Philippians 4.5|]][[Philippians 4.6|]][[Philippians 4.7|]][[Philippians 4.8|]][[Philippians 4.9|]][[Philippians 4.10|]][[Philippians 4.11|]][[Philippians 4.12|]][[Philippians 4.13|]], look for his counsel that can help you rejoice and “stand fast in the Lord” in your challenges ([[Philippians 4.1|Philippians 4:1]]). What do you find in Paul’s words that gives you courage?

Elder Dieter F. Uchtdorf taught, “If we seek joy—if we look for reasons to rejoice and to happily follow the Savior, we will find them” (“[[2024 April - A Higher Joy.md|A Higher Joy]],” *Liahona*, May 2024, 68). What reasons do you have to rejoice because of Jesus Christ? Record any impressions you receive.

See also “God Delivers Us in Our Trials” (video), Gospel Library; “[Rejoice, the Lord Is King!](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/hymns/rejoice-the-lord-is-king?lang=eng),” *Hymns*, no. 66.

### I can be “rooted” in Jesus Christ through my covenants.

Elder David A. Bednar taught that becoming rooted in Christ happens as we “faithfully receive, review, remember, and renew sacred covenants” (“[[2024 April - “Be Still, and Know That I Am God”.md|Be Still, and Know That I Am God]],” *Liahona*, May 2024, 30). While reading [[Colossians 1.12|Colossians 1:12–23]][[Colossians 1.13|]][[Colossians 1.14|]][[Colossians 1.15|]][[Colossians 1.16|]][[Colossians 1.17|]][[Colossians 1.18|]][[Colossians 1.19|]][[Colossians 1.20|]][[Colossians 1.21|]][[Colossians 1.22|]][[Colossians 1.23|]]; [[Colossians 2.2|2:2–8]][[Colossians 2.3|]][[Colossians 2.4|]][[Colossians 2.5|]][[Colossians 2.6|]][[Colossians 2.7|]][[Colossians 2.8|]], reflect on how your covenants help you become “rooted” in the Savior.

- How do your covenants bring you closer to Jesus Christ? What do you know about Him because of those covenants?

- How does being “rooted and built up” in Jesus Christ strengthen you against adversity? (see [[Colossians 2.7|Colossians 2:7–8]][[Colossians 2.8|]]; see also [[Helaman 5.12|Helaman 5:12]]; [[Ether 12.4|Ether 12:4]]).

- How can you review, remember, or renew your covenants more often?

See also Neil L. Andersen, “[[2022 October - Drawing Closer to the Savior.md|Drawing Closer to the Savior]],” *Liahona*, Nov. 2022, 73–76.

### I can become “new” by living the gospel of Jesus Christ.

Paul encouraged the Colossian Saints to set their “affection on things above … the earth” and to become “new” by doing away with sin ([[Colossians 3.2|Colossians 3:2, 10]][[Colossians 3.10|]]). As you read [[Colossians 3.1|Colossians 3:1–17]][[Colossians 3.2|]][[Colossians 3.3|]][[Colossians 3.4|]][[Colossians 3.5|]][[Colossians 3.6|]][[Colossians 3.7|]][[Colossians 3.8|]][[Colossians 3.9|]][[Colossians 3.10|]][[Colossians 3.11|]][[Colossians 3.12|]][[Colossians 3.13|]][[Colossians 3.14|]][[Colossians 3.15|]][[Colossians 3.16|]][[Colossians 3.17|]], ponder how you can follow this counsel. You might list some ways you can improve your discipleship. For example, how can you “seek those things which are above”? How do the Savior and His gospel renew you? What else do these verses inspire you to do?

## Ideas for Teaching Children

[[Philippians 4.4|Philippians 4:4–13]][[Philippians 4.5|]][[Philippians 4.6|]][[Philippians 4.7|]][[Philippians 4.8|]][[Philippians 4.9|]][[Philippians 4.10|]][[Philippians 4.11|]][[Philippians 4.12|]][[Philippians 4.13|]]
### With faith in Jesus Christ, I can be happy even during hard times.

- As you read [[Philippians 4.4|Philippians 4:4–13]][[Philippians 4.5|]][[Philippians 4.6|]][[Philippians 4.7|]][[Philippians 4.8|]][[Philippians 4.9|]][[Philippians 4.10|]][[Philippians 4.11|]][[Philippians 4.12|]][[Philippians 4.13|]] with your children, you could ask them to look for words like “rejoice,” “peace,” and “content.” Then ask your children to find the words “Lord,” “God,” and “Christ” in these verses. Help them understand that Paul’s faith in Jesus Christ made it possible for him to rejoice. How can our faith in Jesus Christ help us with something hard in our lives right now? For some ideas, see Elder Dieter F. Uchtdorf’s message “[[2024 April - A Higher Joy.md|A Higher Joy]]” (*Liahona*, May 2024, 66–69).

- Your children might enjoy looking at objects or pictures that represent things that help you “rejoice in the Lord” ([[Philippians 4.4|Philippians 4:4]]), such as His creations, a temple, His Resurrection, or a family. Tell your children why those things make you happy. Then invite them to share things that help them rejoice in the Lord. You could also sing “[Beautiful Savior](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/childrens-songbook/beautiful-savior-crusaders-hymn?lang=eng)” (*Children’s Songbook*, 62–63).

[[Philippians 4.8|Philippians 4:8]]
### I can “think on” things that are true, honest, and pure.

- You could ask one child to read [[Philippians 4.8|Philippians 4:8]] aloud while others look for words in [[Articles of Faith 1.13|Articles of Faith 1:13]] that are the same or similar. Invite someone to write down these words. Then ask your children what the words mean, and explain the words they don’t know. Why should we “think on” these things? How can we “seek after” them?

> **Children need variety.** They learn better and remember longer when you use activities that help them move about, use all their senses, explore, and try new things (see *Teaching in the Savior’s Way*, [31–32](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/teaching-in-the-saviors-way-2022/12-part-3/13-suggestions-for-a-variety-of-teaching?lang=eng&id=title9-p14#title9)). For example, the ideas for teaching children in this outline suggest at least eight different ways of learning.

[[Colossians 1.23|Colossians 1:23]]; [[Colossians 2.6|2:6–7]][[Colossians 2.7|]]
### I can be “rooted” in Jesus Christ through my covenants.

- Perhaps you could read [[Colossians 1.23|Colossians 1:23]]; [[Colossians 2.6|2:6–7]][[Colossians 2.7|]] to your children while sitting around a tree or looking at a picture of one. You could talk about what it means to be “rooted” in Jesus Christ. How are our covenants with God like the roots of a tree? How does keeping our covenants help us withstand spiritual storms, such as temptations and hard times? The video “Spiritual Whirlwinds” (Gospel Library) can help your children visualize this principle.> 2:25


- After reading [[Colossians 1.23|Colossians 1:23]]; [[Colossians 2.6|2:6–7]][[Colossians 2.7|]] together, your children could draw a picture of a tree and name things they can do to be “rooted” in Jesus Christ. Each time they name something, invite them to add a root to their drawing. Share with each other how being rooted in the Savior has helped you be strong.
