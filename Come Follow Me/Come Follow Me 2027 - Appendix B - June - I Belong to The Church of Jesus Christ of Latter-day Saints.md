# June: I Belong to The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/54-appendix-b/006-church?lang=eng)

Do the children you teach know that when they are baptized and confirmed, they not only make a covenant with the Lord but also become members of His Church? Do they know why that matters? Maybe you could tell them why it matters to you to belong to The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. Help them see how they can have that same sense of belonging—that they belong to the Lord’s Church! They can participate. They can serve and bless others. Even the smallest member of “the body of Christ” is needed ([[1 Corinthians 12.27|1 Corinthians 12:27]]).

## When I am confirmed, I become a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

- “[The Church of Jesus Christ Is Organized](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/doctrine-and-covenants-stories-2025/10-the-church-is-organized?lang=eng)” (in *Doctrine and Covenants Stories*, 40–43) tells the story of the first meeting of the restored Church. Maybe you and the children could review it with this question in mind: Why did Jesus give us a church? Then you could share with each other possible answers.> 2:37


- Consider writing on the board *I am happy to belong to the Church because …* Help the children make a list of ways they would complete that sentence. You could help them find ideas in President Dallin H. Oaks’s message “[[2021 October - The Need for a Church.md|The Need for a Church]]” (*Liahona*, Nov. 2021, 24–26). For example, in the Church we receive ordinances like baptism and the sacrament. We receive guidance from inspired leaders. And we receive—and give—support and love from other members. (See “[Children Sharing the Gospel](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/media/video/2019-08-0040-children-sharing-the-gospel)” \[video\], Gospel Library.)> 15:54
> 3:33


## I can participate in the Church of Jesus Christ.

- One of the most important reasons we go to church is to partake of the sacrament. To help the children understand this, you could read together [[3 Nephi 18.1|3 Nephi 18:1–11]][[3 Nephi 18.2|]][[3 Nephi 18.3|]][[3 Nephi 18.4|]][[3 Nephi 18.5|]][[3 Nephi 18.6|]][[3 Nephi 18.7|]][[3 Nephi 18.8|]][[3 Nephi 18.9|]][[3 Nephi 18.10|]][[3 Nephi 18.11|]] (see also “[Jesus Shares the Sacrament](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/book-of-mormon-stories-2024/50-jesus-shares-the-sacrament?lang=eng),” in *Book of Mormon Stories*, 222–25). Help the children find phrases that explain why we take the sacrament and how the Lord blesses us when we do. If possible, you might have this conversation in the chapel, near the sacrament table.> 2:8


- To learn about other reasons we come to church, you could read together [[Moroni 6.4|Moroni 6:4–6, 9]][[Moroni 6.5|]][[Moroni 6.6|]][[Moroni 6.9|]]. Maybe the children would enjoy drawing pictures of themselves doing these things (such as praying, teaching, singing, and taking the sacrament; see this week’s activity page). Talk to the children about other things they can do to help in the Lord’s Church, like giving talks and prayers, helping family and others, inviting friends, or finding ancestors for temple ordinances.

## This is the Church of Jesus Christ.

- To help your children learn the name of the Church and understand why it is important, consider asking if any of them can say the Church’s full name. You could then help them find the name in [[D&C 115.4|Doctrine and Covenants 115:4]] and ask them to repeat it with you. You could also sing together “[The Church of Jesus Christ](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/childrens-songbook/the-church-of-jesus-christ?lang=eng)” (*Children’s Songbook*, 77).
