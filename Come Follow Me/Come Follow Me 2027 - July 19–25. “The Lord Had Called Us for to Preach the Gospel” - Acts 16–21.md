# July 19–25. “The Lord Had Called Us for to Preach the Gospel”: Acts 16–21

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-new-testament-2027/30?lang=eng)

Among the Lord’s final words to His Apostles was the commandment “Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost: teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you” ([[Matthew 28.19|Matthew 28:19–20]][[Matthew 28.20|]]). While the Apostles didn’t quite make it to all nations, [[Acts 16.1|Acts 16–21]][[Acts 16.2|]][[Acts 16.3|]][[Acts 16.4|]][[Acts 16.5|]][[Acts 16.6|]][[Acts 16.7|]][[Acts 16.8|]][[Acts 16.9|]][[Acts 16.10|]][[Acts 16.11|]][[Acts 16.12|]][[Acts 16.13|]][[Acts 16.14|]][[Acts 16.15|]][[Acts 16.16|]][[Acts 16.17|]][[Acts 16.18|]][[Acts 16.19|]][[Acts 16.20|]][[Acts 16.21|]][[Acts 16.22|]][[Acts 16.23|]][[Acts 16.24|]][[Acts 16.25|]][[Acts 16.26|]][[Acts 16.27|]][[Acts 16.28|]][[Acts 16.29|]][[Acts 16.30|]][[Acts 16.31|]][[Acts 16.32|]][[Acts 16.33|]][[Acts 16.34|]][[Acts 16.35|]][[Acts 16.36|]][[Acts 16.37|]][[Acts 16.38|]][[Acts 16.39|]][[Acts 16.40|]] shows that they did make amazing progress. They taught, baptized, gave the gift of the Holy Ghost, and established the Church widely. And this wasn’t just the work of Apostles. The Church grew with the help of devoted disciples like Lydia, Damaris and Dionysius, and Priscilla and Aquila (see [[Acts 16.14|Acts 16:14–15, 40]][[Acts 16.15|]][[Acts 16.40|]]; [[Acts 17.34|17:34]]; [[Acts 18.2|18:2, 26]][[Acts 18.26|]]).

The work they started continues today. Modern Apostles, along with devoted disciples like you, are helping fulfill the Savior’s commission in ways Paul and his friends never could have imagined. Perhaps you are aware of people who do not know their Heavenly Father or His gospel. Perhaps you have felt that your “spirit was stirred” to share with them what you know about Him ([[Acts 17.16|Acts 17:16]]). With the Spirit guiding you as He guided those early disciples, you may find someone “whose heart the Lord \[has\] opened” ([[Acts 16.14|Acts 16:14]]).

### God opens the hearts of His children to receive the gospel.

The Prophet Joseph Smith taught that there are many people “who are only kept from the truth because they know not where to find it” ([[D&C 123.12|Doctrine and Covenants 123:12]]). And the Savior said, “My Spirit is sent forth into the world to enlighten the humble and contrite” ([[D&C 136.33|Doctrine and Covenants 136:33]]). Paul and Silas’s experiences in [[Acts 16.1|Acts 16]][[Acts 16.2|]][[Acts 16.3|]][[Acts 16.4|]][[Acts 16.5|]][[Acts 16.6|]][[Acts 16.7|]][[Acts 16.8|]][[Acts 16.9|]][[Acts 16.10|]][[Acts 16.11|]][[Acts 16.12|]][[Acts 16.13|]][[Acts 16.14|]][[Acts 16.15|]][[Acts 16.16|]][[Acts 16.17|]][[Acts 16.18|]][[Acts 16.19|]][[Acts 16.20|]][[Acts 16.21|]][[Acts 16.22|]][[Acts 16.23|]][[Acts 16.24|]][[Acts 16.25|]][[Acts 16.26|]][[Acts 16.27|]][[Acts 16.28|]][[Acts 16.29|]][[Acts 16.30|]][[Acts 16.31|]][[Acts 16.32|]][[Acts 16.33|]][[Acts 16.34|]][[Acts 16.35|]][[Acts 16.36|]][[Acts 16.37|]][[Acts 16.38|]][[Acts 16.39|]][[Acts 16.40|]] illustrate what can happen when the efforts of God’s servants to share the gospel align with God’s work to prepare people to receive it.

### I can declare the gospel in all circumstances.

Being thrown in prison for preaching the gospel might seem like a good reason to stop preaching. But to Paul and Silas, it became an opportunity to share the gospel with a jailer (see [[Acts 16.16|Acts 16:16–34]][[Acts 16.17|]][[Acts 16.18|]][[Acts 16.19|]][[Acts 16.20|]][[Acts 16.21|]][[Acts 16.22|]][[Acts 16.23|]][[Acts 16.24|]][[Acts 16.25|]][[Acts 16.26|]][[Acts 16.27|]][[Acts 16.28|]][[Acts 16.29|]][[Acts 16.30|]][[Acts 16.31|]][[Acts 16.32|]][[Acts 16.33|]][[Acts 16.34|]]). ### “We are the offspring of God.”

In Athens, Paul found people with differing opinions and religious views. They were always seeking “to hear some new thing,” and what Paul taught was definitely new to them (see [[Acts 17.19|Acts 17:19–21]][[Acts 17.20|]][[Acts 17.21|]]). They worshipped many gods, including one they called “the unknown God” ([[Acts 17.23|Acts 17:23]]). Ponder what Paul said to help them come to know their Heavenly Father.

- What does it mean to you to be the “offspring of God”? ([[Acts 17.29|Acts 17:29]]).

- How does this truth influence how you see yourself and others?

See also “[I Am a Child of God](https://churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/hymns/i-am-a-child-of-god?lang=eng),” *Hymns*, no. 301; “We Are the Offspring of God” (video), Gospel Library.

### I believe in Jesus Christ and His gospel, even when it’s not popular.

Damaris and Dionysius lived in a society where many people dismissed the message of Jesus Christ. But despite being surrounded by different cultural and religious influences, they saw the truth in Paul’s words and chose to believe, even when others mocked his teachings. As you read [[Acts 17.32|Acts 17:32–34]][[Acts 17.33|]][[Acts 17.34|]], ponder times when you have been in a situation like what Damaris and Dionysius faced. What helped you keep your faith strong?

### The gospel of Jesus Christ is more important than earthly things.

As you read [[Acts 19.18|Acts 19:18–20]][[Acts 19.19|]][[Acts 19.20|]], notice what the people gave up to embrace the gospel (see [[Acts 19.19|verse 19]]). Are there worldly possessions or activities you need to give up to receive heavenly blessings? You may also want to consider what Paul taught in [[Acts 20.32|Acts 20:32–36]][[Acts 20.33|]][[Acts 20.34|]][[Acts 20.35|]][[Acts 20.36|]]. What do you learn about the blessings of sacrificing worldly things?
