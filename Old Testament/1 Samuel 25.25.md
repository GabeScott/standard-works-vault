# 1 Samuel 25:25

> Let not my lord, I pray thee, regard this man of Belial, <i>even</i> <u>Nabal</u>[^a]: for as his name <i>is,</i> so <i>is</i> he; Nabal <i>is</i> his name, and folly <i>is</i> with him: but I thine handmaid saw not the young men of my lord, whom thou didst send.

[1 Samuel 25:25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/1-sam/25.25?lang=eng#p25)


[^a]: IE Fool (insensitive, churlish person). Such a name must have been given him by those who knew him.
