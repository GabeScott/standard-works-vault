# Leviticus 11:42

> Whatsoever goeth upon the belly, and whatsoever goeth upon <i>all</i> four, or <u>whatsoever</u>[^a] hath more feet among all creeping things that creep upon the earth, them ye shall not eat; for they <i>are</i> an abomination.

[Leviticus 11:42](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/lev/11.42?lang=eng#p42)


[^a]: OR every millipede (or that which has numerous legs).
