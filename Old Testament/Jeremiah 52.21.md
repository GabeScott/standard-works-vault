# Jeremiah 52:21

> And <i>concerning</i> the pillars, the height of one pillar <i>was</i> eighteen cubits; and <u>a fillet</u>[^a] of twelve <u>cubits</u>[^b] did compass it; and the thickness thereof <i>was</i> four fingers: <i>it was</i> hollow.
