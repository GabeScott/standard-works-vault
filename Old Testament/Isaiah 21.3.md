# Isaiah 21:3

> Therefore are my loins filled with pain: pangs have taken hold upon me, as the pangs of a woman that travaileth: I was bowed down at the hearing <i>of it;</i> I was <u>dismayed</u>[^a] at the seeing <i>of it.</i>

[Isaiah 21:3](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/21.3?lang=eng#p3)


[^a]: IE Isaiah was astonished at the cataclysmic scene he saw in vision, even though an enemy was the nation destroyed.
