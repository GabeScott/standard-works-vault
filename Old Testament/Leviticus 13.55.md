# Leviticus 13:55

> And the priest shall look on the plague, after that it is washed: and, behold, <i>if</i> the plague have not changed his colour, and the plague be not spread; it <i>is</i> unclean; thou shalt burn it in the fire; <u>it</u>[^a] <i>is</i> fret inward, <i>whether</i> it <i>be</i> bare within or without.
