# Isaiah 10:15

> <u>Shall</u>[^a] the axe <u>boast</u>[^b] itself against him that heweth therewith? <i>or</i> shall the saw magnify itself against him that <u>shaketh</u>[^c] it? as if the rod should shake <i>itself</i> against them that lift it up, <i>or</i> as if the staff should lift up <i>itself, as if it were</i> no wood.

[Isaiah 10:15](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/isa/10.15?lang=eng#p15)


[^a]: All the metaphors in this verse ask the same question: Can man (e.g., the Assyrian king) prosper against God?  
[^b]: [[Boast, Boasting|TG Boast]].  
[^c]: HEB wields, moves.
