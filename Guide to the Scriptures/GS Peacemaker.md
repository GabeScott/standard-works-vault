# Peacemaker

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/peacemaker?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Peace|Peace]]

A person who brings about or promotes peace ([[Matthew 5.9|Matt. 5:9]]; [[3 Nephi 12.9|3 Ne. 12:9]]). A peacemaker may also be someone who proclaims the gospel ([[Mosiah 15.11|Mosiah 15:11–18]][[Mosiah 15.12|]][[Mosiah 15.13|]][[Mosiah 15.14|]][[Mosiah 15.15|]][[Mosiah 15.16|]][[Mosiah 15.17|]][[Mosiah 15.18|]]).
