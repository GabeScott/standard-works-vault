# Sexual Immorality

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/sexual-immorality?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adultery|Adultery]]; [[GS Fornication|Fornication]]; [[GS Sensual, Sensuality|Sensual, Sensuality]]

Willful participation in adultery, fornication, homosexual and lesbian behavior, incest, or any other unholy, unnatural, or impure sexual activity.

- Let us make our father drink wine, and we will lie with him, [[Genesis 19.30|Gen. 19:30–36]][[Genesis 19.31|]][[Genesis 19.32|]][[Genesis 19.33|]][[Genesis 19.34|]][[Genesis 19.35|]][[Genesis 19.36|]].
- Reuben went and lay with Bilhah his father’s concubine, [[Genesis 35.22|Gen. 35:22]] ([[Genesis 49.4|Gen. 49:4]]; [[1 Chronicles 5.1|1 Chr. 5:1]]).
- Homosexual behavior and other sexual perversions are an abomination, [[Leviticus 18.22|Lev. 18:22–23]][[Leviticus 18.23|]].
- If a man forces a woman to lie with him, only the man is guilty of sin, [[Deuteronomy 22.25|Deut. 22:25–27]][[Deuteronomy 22.26|]][[Deuteronomy 22.27|]].

- Whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery with her already in his heart, [[Matthew 5.28|Matt. 5:28]] ([[3 Nephi 12.28|3 Ne. 12:28]]).
- Fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection are idolatry, [[Colossians 3.5|Col. 3:5]].
- In the last days men shall be without natural affection, [[2 Timothy 3.1|2 Tim. 3:1–3]][[2 Timothy 3.2|]][[2 Timothy 3.3|]].

- Sexual sin is an abomination, [[Alma 39.3|Alma 39:3–5]][[Alma 39.4|]][[Alma 39.5|]].
