# Rome

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/rome?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Roman Empire|Roman Empire]]

In the New Testament, the capital city of the Roman Empire, located on the Tiber River in Italy ([[Acts 18.2|Acts 18:2]]; [[Acts 19.21|19:21]]; [[Acts 23.11|23:11]]). Paul taught the gospel in Rome when he was a prisoner of the Roman government ([[Acts 28.14|Acts 28:14–31]][[Acts 28.15|]][[Acts 28.16|]][[Acts 28.17|]][[Acts 28.18|]][[Acts 28.19|]][[Acts 28.20|]][[Acts 28.21|]][[Acts 28.22|]][[Acts 28.23|]][[Acts 28.24|]][[Acts 28.25|]][[Acts 28.26|]][[Acts 28.27|]][[Acts 28.28|]][[Acts 28.29|]][[Acts 28.30|]][[Acts 28.31|]]; [[Romans 1.7|Rom. 1:7, 15–16]][[Romans 1.15|]][[Romans 1.16|]]).
