# Unbelief

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/unbelief?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Belief, Believe|Belief, Believe]]

Lack of faith in God and His gospel.

- He did not many mighty works there because of their unbelief, [[Matthew 13.58|Matt. 13:58]].
- Because of their unbelief Jesus’ disciples could not cast out a devil, [[Matthew 17.14|Matt. 17:14–21]][[Matthew 17.15|]][[Matthew 17.16|]][[Matthew 17.17|]][[Matthew 17.18|]][[Matthew 17.19|]][[Matthew 17.20|]][[Matthew 17.21|]].
- Help thou mine unbelief, [[Mark 9.23|Mark 9:23–24]][[Mark 9.24|]].
- Jesus upbraided his Apostles with their unbelief and hardness of heart, [[Mark 16.14|Mark 16:14]].
- Shall unbelief make the faith of God without effect, [[Romans 3.3|Rom. 3:3]].

- It is better that one man should perish than that a nation should dwindle and perish in unbelief, [[1 Nephi 4.13|1 Ne. 4:13]].
- When the time cometh that they dwindle in unbelief, he will cause them to be scattered and smitten, [[2 Nephi 1.10|2 Ne. 1:10–11]][[2 Nephi 1.11|]] ([[D&C 3.18|D&C 3:18]]).
- Because of their unbelief they could not understand the word of God, [[Mosiah 26.1|Mosiah 26:1–5]][[Mosiah 26.2|]][[Mosiah 26.3|]][[Mosiah 26.4|]][[Mosiah 26.5|]].
- I could not show unto the Jews so great miracles, because of their unbelief, [[3 Nephi 19.35|3 Ne. 19:35]].

- Your minds in times past have been darkened because of unbelief, [[D&C 84.54|D&C 84:54–58]][[D&C 84.55|]][[D&C 84.56|]][[D&C 84.57|]][[D&C 84.58|]].
