# Priestcraft

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/priestcraft?lang=eng)

Men preaching and setting themselves up for a light to the world that they may get gain and praise of the world; they do not seek the welfare of Zion ([[2 Nephi 26.29|2 Ne. 26:29]]).

- Feed the flock of God, not for filthy lucre, [[1 Peter 5.2|1 Pet. 5:2]].

- Churches which are built up to get gain must be brought low, [[1 Nephi 22.23|1 Ne. 22:23]] ([[Mormon 8.32|Morm. 8:32–41]][[Mormon 8.33|]][[Mormon 8.34|]][[Mormon 8.35|]][[Mormon 8.36|]][[Mormon 8.37|]][[Mormon 8.38|]][[Mormon 8.39|]][[Mormon 8.40|]][[Mormon 8.41|]]).
- Because of priestcrafts and iniquities, Jesus will be crucified, [[2 Nephi 10.5|2 Ne. 10:5]].
- Were priestcraft to be enforced among this people it would prove their entire destruction, [[Alma 1.12|Alma 1:12]].
- The Gentiles shall be filled with all manner of priestcrafts, [[3 Nephi 16.10|3 Ne. 16:10]].
