# Man, Men

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/man-men?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Brethren, Brother|Brethren, Brother]]; [[GS God, Godhead|God, Godhead]]; [[GS Sister|Sister]]; [[GS Sons and Daughters of God|Sons and Daughters of God]]; [[GS Spirit|Spirit]]; [[GS Spirit Creation|Spirit Creation]]; [[GS Woman, Women|Woman, Women]]

Refers to all mankind, both male and female. All men and women are the literal spirit offspring of a Heavenly Father. When they are born into mortality, they receive physical, mortal bodies. These bodies were created in God’s image ([[Genesis 1.26|Gen. 1:26–27]][[Genesis 1.27|]]). Men and women who are faithful in receiving the necessary ordinances, keeping their covenants, and obeying God’s commands will enter into their exaltation and become as God.

- God created man in his own image, [[Genesis 1.27|Gen. 1:27]] ([[Mosiah 7.27|Mosiah 7:27]]; [[D&C 20.17|D&C 20:17–18]][[D&C 20.18|]]).
- What is man, that thou art mindful of him, [[Psalms 8.4|Ps. 8:4–5]][[Psalms 8.5|]].
- Cursed is the man that trusteth in man and maketh flesh his arm, [[Jeremiah 17.5|Jer. 17:5]] ([[2 Nephi 4.34|2 Ne. 4:34]]; [[2 Nephi 28.26|28:26, 31]][[2 Nephi 28.31|]]).

- When I became a man, I put away childish things, [[1 Corinthians 13.11|1 Cor. 13:11]].

- Men are that they might have joy, [[2 Nephi 2.25|2 Ne. 2:25]].
- The natural man is an enemy to God, [[Mosiah 3.19|Mosiah 3:19]].
- What manner of men ought ye to be, [[3 Nephi 27.27|3 Ne. 27:27]].

- The works of man are frustrated, not the works of God, [[D&C 3.3|D&C 3:3]].
- You should not fear man more than God, [[D&C 3.7|D&C 3:7]] ([[D&C 30.11|D&C 30:11]]; [[D&C 122.9|122:9]]).
- All things are made for the benefit and the use of man, [[D&C 59.18|D&C 59:18]].

- I know that man is nothing, [[Moses 1.10|Moses 1:10]].
- God’s work and glory is to bring to pass the immortality and eternal life of man, [[Moses 1.39|Moses 1:39]].

- Ye are the children of the Lord your God, [[Deuteronomy 14.1|Deut. 14:1]].
- Ye are gods and all of you are children of the Most High, [[Psalms 82.6|Ps. 82:6]].
- Ye are the sons of the living God, [[Hosea 1.10|Hosea 1:10]].
- Have we not all one Father? Hath not one God created us, [[Malachi 2.10|Mal. 2:10]].

- We are the offspring of God, [[Acts 17.29|Acts 17:29]].
- The Spirit itself beareth witness that we are the children of God, [[Romans 8.16|Rom. 8:16]].
- Be in subjection unto the Father of spirits, [[Hebrews 12.9|Heb. 12:9]].

- The spirits of all men are taken home to that God who gave them life, [[Alma 40.11|Alma 40:11]].

- The inhabitants of the worlds are begotten sons and daughters of God, [[D&C 76.24|D&C 76:24]].
- Man was in the beginning with the Father, [[D&C 93.23|D&C 93:23, 29]][[D&C 93.29|]].

- I am God; I made the world, and men before they were in the flesh, [[Moses 6.51|Moses 6:51]].
