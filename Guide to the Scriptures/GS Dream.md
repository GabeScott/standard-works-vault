# Dream

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/dream?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Revelation|Revelation]]

One way that God reveals His will to men and women on earth. Not all dreams are revelations, however. Inspired dreams are the fruit of faith.

- He dreamed, and behold a ladder reached to heaven, [[Genesis 28.12|Gen. 28:12]].
- Joseph dreamed a dream, [[Genesis 37.5|Gen. 37:5]].
- The Lord will speak to him in a dream, [[Numbers 12.6|Num. 12:6]].
- Nebuchadnezzar dreamed dreams, [[Daniel 2.1|Dan. 2:1–3]][[Daniel 2.2|]][[Daniel 2.3|]].
- Old men shall dream dreams, [[Joel 2.28|Joel 2:28]] ([[Acts 2.17|Acts 2:17]]).

- The angel of the Lord appeared to him in a dream, [[Matthew 1.20|Matt. 1:20]] ([[Matthew 2.19|Matt. 2:19]]).

- Lehi wrote many things he had seen in dreams, [[1 Nephi 1.16|1 Ne. 1:16]].
