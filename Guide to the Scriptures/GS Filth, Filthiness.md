# Filth, Filthiness

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/filth-filthiness?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Clean and Unclean|Clean and Unclean]]; [[GS Sin|Sin]]; [[GS Ungodly|Ungodly]]; [[GS Unrighteous, Unrighteousness|Unrighteous, Unrighteousness]]; [[GS Wicked, Wickedness|Wicked, Wickedness]]

Spiritual uncleanness because of willful disobedience to God.

- The Lord shall wash away the filth of the daughters of Zion, [[Isaiah 4.4|Isa. 4:4]] ([[2 Nephi 14.4|2 Ne. 14:4]]).

- The kingdom of God is not filthy, [[1 Nephi 15.34|1 Ne. 15:34]] ([[Alma 7.21|Alma 7:21]]).
- They who are filthy shall be filthy still, [[2 Nephi 9.16|2 Ne. 9:16]] ([[Mormon 9.14|Morm. 9:14]]).
- How will you feel, if ye shall stand before the bar of God, having your garments stained with filthiness, [[Alma 5.22|Alma 5:22]].

- They must remain filthy still, [[D&C 88.35|D&C 88:35]].

- When shall I rest, and be cleansed from filthiness, [[Moses 7.48|Moses 7:48]].
