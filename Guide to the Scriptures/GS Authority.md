# Authority

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/authority?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Call, Called of God, Calling|Call, Called of God, Calling]]; [[GS Keys of the Priesthood|Keys of the Priesthood]]; [[GS Ordain, Ordination|Ordain, Ordination]]; [[GS Power|Power]]; [[GS Priesthood|Priesthood]]

The permission granted to men on earth called or ordained to act for and in behalf of God the Father or Jesus Christ in doing God’s work.

- I have sent thee, [[Exodus 3.12|Ex. 3:12–15]][[Exodus 3.13|]][[Exodus 3.14|]][[Exodus 3.15|]].
- Speak all that I command thee, [[Exodus 7.2|Ex. 7:2]].

- He gave the twelve disciples power, [[Matthew 10.1|Matt. 10:1]].
- Ye have not chosen me, but I have chosen you, and ordained you, [[John 15.16|John 15:16]].

- Nephi and Lehi preached with great authority, [[Helaman 5.18|Hel. 5:18]].
- Nephi, the son of Helaman, was a man of God, having great power and authority from God, [[Helaman 11.18|Hel. 11:18]] ([[3 Nephi 7.17|3 Ne. 7:17]]).
- Jesus gave power and authority to twelve Nephites, [[3 Nephi 12.1|3 Ne. 12:1–2]][[3 Nephi 12.2|]].

- Joseph Smith was called of God and ordained, [[D&C 20.2|D&C 20:2]].
- No one shall preach my gospel or build up my Church unless he is ordained and it is known to the Church that he has authority, [[D&C 42.11|D&C 42:11]].
- Elders are to preach the gospel, acting in authority, [[D&C 68.8|D&C 68:8]].
- The Melchizedek Priesthood has authority to administer in spiritual things, [[D&C 107.8|D&C 107:8, 18–19]][[D&C 107.18|]][[D&C 107.19|]].
- What is done by divine authority becomes law, [[D&C 128.9|D&C 128:9]].

- Any who preach or administer for God must be called of God by those in authority, [[Articles of Faith 1.5|A of F 1:5]].
