# Guile

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/guile?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Deceit, Deceive, Deception|Deceit, Deceive, Deception]]

In the scriptures, guile is deceitful cunning.

- Blessed is the man in whose spirit there is no guile, [[Psalms 32.2|Ps. 32:2]] ([[Psalms 34.13|Ps. 34:13]]; [[1 Peter 2.1|1 Pet. 2:1]]).

- Nathanael was an Israelite in whom there was no guile, [[John 1.47|John 1:47]] ([[D&C 41.9|D&C 41:9–11]][[D&C 41.10|]][[D&C 41.11|]]).

- Pure knowledge enlarges the soul without guile, [[D&C 121.42|D&C 121:42]].
