# Scriptures

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/scriptures?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Bible|Bible]]; [[GS Book of Mormon|Book of Mormon]]; [[GS Canon|Canon]]; [[GS Doctrine and Covenants|Doctrine and Covenants]]; [[GS Pearl of Great Price|Pearl of Great Price]]; [[GS Word of God|Word of God]]

Words, both written and spoken, by holy men of God when moved upon by the Holy Ghost. The official canonized scriptures of the Church today consist of the Bible, Book of Mormon, Doctrine and Covenants, and Pearl of Great Price. Jesus and the writers of the New Testament regarded the books of the Old Testament as scripture ([[Matthew 22.29|Matt. 22:29]]; [[John 5.39|John 5:39]]; [[2 Timothy 3.15|2 Tim. 3:15]]; [[2 Peter 1.20|2 Pet. 1:20–21]][[2 Peter 1.21|]]). See also [Chronology](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bible-chron/introduction?lang=eng) in the Bible appendix.

## Lost scriptures

There are many sacred writings mentioned in the scriptures that we do not have today, among which are these books and writers: the covenant ([[Exodus 24.7|Ex. 24:7]]), the wars of the Lord ([[Numbers 21.14|Num. 21:14]]), Jasher ([[Joshua 10.13|Josh. 10:13]]; [[2 Samuel 1.18|2 Sam. 1:18]]), the acts of Solomon ([[1 Kings 11.41|1 Kgs. 11:41]]), Samuel the seer ([[1 Chronicles 29.29|1 Chr. 29:29]]), Nathan the prophet ([[2 Chronicles 9.29|2 Chr. 9:29]]), Shemaiah the prophet ([[2 Chronicles 12.15|2 Chr. 12:15]]), Iddo the prophet ([[2 Chronicles 13.22|2 Chr. 13:22]]), Jehu ([[2 Chronicles 20.34|2 Chr. 20:34]]), the sayings of the seers ([[2 Chronicles 33.19|2 Chr. 33:19]]), Enoch ([[Jude 1.14|Jude 1:14]]), and the words of Zenock, Neum, and Zenos ([[1 Nephi 19.10|1 Ne. 19:10]]), Zenos ([[Jacob 5.1|Jacob 5:1]]), Zenock and Ezias ([[Helaman 8.20|Hel. 8:20]]), and a book of remembrance ([[Moses 6.5|Moses 6:5]]); and epistles to the Corinthians ([[1 Corinthians 5.9|1 Cor. 5:9]]), to the Ephesians ([[Ephesians 3.3|Eph. 3:3]]), from Laodicea ([[Colossians 4.16|Col. 4:16]]), and from Jude ([[Jude 1.3|Jude 1:3]]).
