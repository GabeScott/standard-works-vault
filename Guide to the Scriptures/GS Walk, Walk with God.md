# Walk, Walk with God

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/walk-walk-with-god?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Obedience, Obedient, Obey|Obedience, Obedient, Obey]]; [[GS Righteous, Righteousness|Righteous, Righteousness]]; [[GS Way|Way]]

To be in harmony with God’s teachings and to live as God would have His people live; to be receptive and obedient to the promptings of the Holy Ghost.

- I may prove them, whether they will walk in my law, or not, [[Exodus 16.4|Ex. 16:4]].
- No good thing will be withheld from them that walk uprightly, [[Psalms 84.11|Ps. 84:11]].
- Those who walk in my statutes and keep my ordinances shall be my people, [[Ezekiel 11.20|Ezek. 11:20–21]][[Ezekiel 11.21|]] ([[Deuteronomy 8.6|Deut. 8:6]]).
- What does the Lord require of you but that you walk humbly with God, [[Micah 6.8|Micah 6:8]] ([[D&C 11.12|D&C 11:12]]).

- Walk in the light, as God is in the light, [[1 John 1.7|1 Jn. 1:7]] ([[2 John 1.6|2 Jn. 1:6]]; [[3 John 1.4|3 Jn. 1:4]]; [[4 Nephi 1.12|4 Ne. 1:12]]).

- Walk in the strait path which leads to life, [[2 Nephi 33.9|2 Ne. 33:9]].
- King Benjamin walked with a clear conscience before God, [[Mosiah 2.27|Mosiah 2:27]].
- Your duty is to walk blameless after the holy order of God, [[Alma 7.22|Alma 7:22]].

- Members of the Church shall manifest a godly walk and conversation, [[D&C 20.69|D&C 20:69]].
- Give heed to the prophet and walk in all holiness before the Lord, [[D&C 21.4|D&C 21:4]].
- Teach children to pray and to walk uprightly before the Lord, [[D&C 68.28|D&C 68:28]].

- Abide in me, and I in you; therefore walk with me, [[Moses 6.34|Moses 6:34]].
