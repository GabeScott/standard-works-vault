# Nineveh

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/nineveh?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Assyria|Assyria]]; [[GS Jonah|Jonah]]

In the Old Testament, the capital of Assyria and for over two hundred years a great commercial center on the eastern bank of the Tigris river. It fell at the downfall of the Assyrian empire, 606 

- Sennacherib, the king of Assyria, lived in Nineveh, [[2 Kings 19.36|2 Kgs. 19:36]].
- Jonah was sent to call the city to repentance, [[Jonah 1.1|Jonah 1:1–2]][[Jonah 1.2|]] ([[Jonah 3.1|Jonah 3:1–4]][[Jonah 3.2|]][[Jonah 3.3|]][[Jonah 3.4|]]).
- The people of Nineveh repented, [[Jonah 3.5|Jonah 3:5–10]][[Jonah 3.6|]][[Jonah 3.7|]][[Jonah 3.8|]][[Jonah 3.9|]][[Jonah 3.10|]].

- Christ used Nineveh as an example of repentance before the Jews, [[Matthew 12.41|Matt. 12:41]].
