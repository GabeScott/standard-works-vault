# Jealous, Jealousy

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/jealous-jealousy?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Envy|Envy]]

As used in the scriptures, the word *jealous* has two meanings: (1) to be fervent and to have sensitive and deep feelings about someone or something, and (2) to be envious of someone or suspicious that another will gain some advantage.

## To have fervent feelings

- I the Lord am a jealous God, [[Exodus 20.5|Ex. 20:5]] ([[Deuteronomy 5.9|Deut. 5:9]]; [[Deuteronomy 6.15|6:15]]; [[Mosiah 11.22|Mosiah 11:22]]).
- I will be jealous for my holy name, [[Ezekiel 39.25|Ezek. 39:25]].
- I am jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion, [[Zechariah 1.14|Zech. 1:14]].

## To be envious or suspicious

- Jealousy is the rage of a man, [[Proverbs 6.32|Prov. 6:32–35]][[Proverbs 6.33|]][[Proverbs 6.34|]][[Proverbs 6.35|]].

- Akish began to be jealous of his son, [[Ether 9.7|Ether 9:7]].

- Strip yourselves from jealousies and fears, [[D&C 67.10|D&C 67:10]].
