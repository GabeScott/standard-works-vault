# Philistines

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/philistines?lang=eng)

In the Old Testament, a tribe that originally came from Caphtor ([[Amos 9.7|Amos 9:7]]) and occupied the rich lowland on the Mediterranean coast from Joppa to the Egyptian desert before the days of Abraham ([[Genesis 21.32|Gen. 21:32]]). For many years there was a military struggle between the Philistines and the Israelites. Eventually Palestine, the name of the Philistine territory, became a familiar title for all of the Holy Land.
