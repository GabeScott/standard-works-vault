# Children of Christ

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/children-of-christ?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Begotten|Begotten]]; [[GS Born Again, Born of God|Born Again, Born of God]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Sons and Daughters of God|Sons and Daughters of God]]

Those who have accepted the gospel of Jesus Christ.

- Whosoever shall humble himself as this little child is greatest, [[Matthew 18.1|Matt. 18:1–4]][[Matthew 18.2|]][[Matthew 18.3|]][[Matthew 18.4|]].
- Believe in the light that ye may be the children of light, [[John 12.36|John 12:36]].

- Put off the natural man and become as a child, [[Mosiah 3.19|Mosiah 3:19]]; [[Mosiah 27.25|27:25–26]][[Mosiah 27.26|]].
- Because of the covenant ye shall be called the children of Christ, [[Mosiah 5.7|Mosiah 5:7]].
- If ye will lay hold upon every good thing, ye certainly will be a child of Christ, [[Moroni 7.19|Moro. 7:19]].

- As many as received me, gave I power to become my sons, [[D&C 39.4|D&C 39:4]].
- Fear not, little children, for you are mine, [[D&C 50.40|D&C 50:40–41]][[D&C 50.41|]].

- Thou art one in me, a son of God, [[Moses 6.68|Moses 6:68]].
