# Grave

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/grave?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Resurrection|Resurrection]]

The burial place of the mortal body. Because of the Atonement, everyone will be resurrected from the grave.

- After Christ’s resurrection, the graves were opened and many bodies arose, [[Matthew 27.52|Matt. 27:52–53]][[Matthew 27.53|]] ([[3 Nephi 23.9|3 Ne. 23:9–13]][[3 Nephi 23.10|]][[3 Nephi 23.11|]][[3 Nephi 23.12|]][[3 Nephi 23.13|]]).
- O grave, where is thy victory, [[1 Corinthians 15.55|1 Cor. 15:55]].

- The grave must deliver up its dead, [[2 Nephi 9.11|2 Ne. 9:11–13]][[2 Nephi 9.12|]][[2 Nephi 9.13|]].

- They who have slept in the grave shall come forth, [[D&C 88.97|D&C 88:97–98]][[D&C 88.98|]].
- A baptismal font is a symbol of the grave, [[D&C 128.12|D&C 128:12–13]][[D&C 128.13|]].
