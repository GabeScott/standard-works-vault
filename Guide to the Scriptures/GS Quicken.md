# Quicken

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/quicken?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Resurrection|Resurrection]]

To make alive, to resurrect, or to change a person so that he can be in the presence of God.

- God hath quickened us together with Christ, [[Ephesians 2.4|Eph. 2:4–5]][[Ephesians 2.5|]] ([[Colossians 2.6|Col. 2:6, 12–13]][[Colossians 2.12|]][[Colossians 2.13|]]).
- Christ was put to death in the flesh, but quickened by the Spirit, [[1 Peter 3.18|1 Pet. 3:18]] ([[D&C 138.7|D&C 138:7]]).

- No man has seen God, except quickened by the Spirit, [[D&C 67.11|D&C 67:11]].
- Redemption is through him that quickeneth all things, [[D&C 88.16|D&C 88:16–17]][[D&C 88.17|]].
- The Saints will be quickened and caught up to meet Christ, [[D&C 88.96|D&C 88:96]].

- Adam became quickened in the inner man, [[Moses 6.65|Moses 6:65]].
