# Eve

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/eve?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adam|Adam]]; [[GS Eden|Eden]]; [[GS Fall of Adam and Eve|Fall of Adam and Eve]]

The first woman to live on this earth ([[Genesis 2.21|Gen. 2:21–25]][[Genesis 2.22|]][[Genesis 2.23|]][[Genesis 2.24|]][[Genesis 2.25|]]; [[Genesis 3.20|3:20]]). She was Adam’s wife. In Hebrew the name means “life.” She was so named because she was the “mother of all living” ([[Moses 4.26|Moses 4:26]]). She and Adam, the first man, will share eternal glory for their role in making possible the eternal progress of all mankind.
