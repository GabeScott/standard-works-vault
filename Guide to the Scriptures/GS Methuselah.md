# Methuselah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/methuselah?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Enoch|Enoch]]

A son of Enoch in the Old Testament. Methuselah lived 969 years ([[Genesis 5.21|Gen. 5:21–27]][[Genesis 5.22|]][[Genesis 5.23|]][[Genesis 5.24|]][[Genesis 5.25|]][[Genesis 5.26|]][[Genesis 5.27|]]; [[Luke 3.37|Luke 3:37]]; [[Moses 8.7|Moses 8:7]]). He was a righteous prophet who was left on the earth when the city of Enoch was taken to heaven. He remained on earth to provide a posterity through which Noah would come ([[Moses 8.3|Moses 8:3–4]][[Moses 8.4|]]).
