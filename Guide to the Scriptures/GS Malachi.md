## The book of Malachi

The book or prophecy of Malachi is the last book in the Old Testament. It seems to follow four major themes: (1) the sins of Israel—[[Malachi 1.1|Malachi 1:6–2:17]][[Malachi 1.2|]][[Malachi 1.3|]][[Malachi 1.4|]][[Malachi 1.5|]][[Malachi 1.6|]][[Malachi 1.7|]][[Malachi 1.8|]][[Malachi 1.9|]][[Malachi 1.10|]][[Malachi 1.11|]][[Malachi 1.12|]][[Malachi 1.13|]][[Malachi 1.14|]]; [[Malachi 3.8|3:8–9]][[Malachi 3.9|]]; (2) the judgments to come upon Israel because of their disobedience—[[Malachi 1.14|Malachi 1:14]]; [[Malachi 2.2|2:2–3, 12]][[Malachi 2.3|]][[Malachi 2.12|]]; [[Malachi 3.5|3:5]]; (3) the promises for obedience—[[Malachi 3.10|Malachi 3:10–12, 16–18]][[Malachi 3.11|]][[Malachi 3.12|]][[Malachi 3.16|]][[Malachi 3.17|]][[Malachi 3.18|]]; [[Malachi 4.2|4:2–3]][[Malachi 4.3|]]; and (4) prophecies pertaining to Israel—[[Malachi 3.1|Malachi 3:1–5]][[Malachi 3.2|]][[Malachi 3.3|]][[Malachi 3.4|]][[Malachi 3.5|]]; [[Malachi 4.1|4:1, 5–6]][[Malachi 4.5|]][[Malachi 4.6|]] ([[D&C 2.1|D&C 2]][[D&C 2.2|]][[D&C 2.3|]]; [[D&C 128.17|128:17]]; [[Joseph Smith History 1.37|JS—H 1:37–39]][[Joseph Smith History 1.38|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.39|]]).
