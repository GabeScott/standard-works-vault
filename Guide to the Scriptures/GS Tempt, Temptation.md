# Tempt, Temptation

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/tempt-temptation?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Agency|Agency]]; [[GS Devil|Devil]]; [[GS Endure|Endure]]

A test of a person’s ability to choose good instead of evil; an enticement to sin and follow Satan instead of God.

- Lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil, [[Matthew 6.13|Matt. 6:13]] ([[3 Nephi 13.12|3 Ne. 13:12]]).
- God will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able to bear, [[1 Corinthians 10.13|1 Cor. 10:13]].
- Christ was tempted like as we are, [[Hebrews 4.14|Heb. 4:14–15]][[Hebrews 4.15|]].
- Blessed is the man that endureth temptation, [[James 1.12|James 1:12–14]][[James 1.13|]][[James 1.14|]].

- The temptations of the adversary cannot overpower those who hearken unto the word of God, [[1 Nephi 15.24|1 Ne. 15:24]] ([[Helaman 5.12|Hel. 5:12]]).
- Man could not act for himself save he was enticed by the one or the other, [[2 Nephi 2.11|2 Ne. 2:11–16]][[2 Nephi 2.12|]][[2 Nephi 2.13|]][[2 Nephi 2.14|]][[2 Nephi 2.15|]][[2 Nephi 2.16|]].
- Watch and pray continually, that ye may not be tempted above that which ye can bear, [[Alma 13.28|Alma 13:28]].
- Teach them to withstand every temptation of the devil, with their faith on the Lord Jesus Christ, [[Alma 37.33|Alma 37:33]].
- Pray always lest ye enter into temptation, [[3 Nephi 18.15|3 Ne. 18:15, 18]][[3 Nephi 18.18|]] ([[D&C 20.33|D&C 20:33]]; [[D&C 31.12|31:12]]; [[D&C 61.39|61:39]]).

- Beware of pride, lest thou shouldst enter into temptation, [[D&C 23.1|D&C 23:1]].
- Adam became subject to the will of the devil because he yielded to temptation, [[D&C 29.39|D&C 29:39–40]][[D&C 29.40|]].

- I was left to all kinds of temptations, [[Joseph Smith History 1.28|JS—H 1:28]].
