# Divorce

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/divorce?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Marriage, Marry|Marriage, Marry]]

The termination of a marriage through the power of civil or ecclesiastical law. According to the New Testament, God permitted divorce under some conditions because of the hardness of the people’s hearts; however, as Jesus explained, “from the beginning it was not so” ([[Matthew 19.3|Matt. 19:3–12]][[Matthew 19.4|]][[Matthew 19.5|]][[Matthew 19.6|]][[Matthew 19.7|]][[Matthew 19.8|]][[Matthew 19.9|]][[Matthew 19.10|]][[Matthew 19.11|]][[Matthew 19.12|]]). The scriptures counsel against divorce generally and advise husbands and wives to love each other in righteousness ([[1 Corinthians 7.10|1 Cor. 7:10–12]][[1 Corinthians 7.11|]][[1 Corinthians 7.12|]]; [[D&C 42.22|D&C 42:22]]).
