# Millennium

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/millennium?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Hell|Hell]]; [[GS Second Coming of Jesus Christ|Second Coming of Jesus Christ]]

The thousand-year period of peace that will begin when Christ returns to reign personally on the earth ([[Articles of Faith 1.10|A of F 1:10]]).

- People will not lift up sword, neither learn war any more, [[Isaiah 2.4|Isa. 2:4]] ([[Micah 4.3|Micah 4:3]]; [[2 Nephi 12.4|2 Ne. 12:4]]).
- Land that was desolate is become like the garden of Eden, [[Ezekiel 36.35|Ezek. 36:35]].

- They lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years, [[Revelation 20.4|Rev. 20:4]].

- Because of righteousness, Satan has no power, [[1 Nephi 22.26|1 Ne. 22:26]].

- I will dwell in righteousness on earth a thousand years, [[D&C 29.11|D&C 29:11]].
- When the thousand years are ended, I will spare the earth for a little season, [[D&C 29.22|D&C 29:22]].
- The great Millennium shall come, [[D&C 43.30|D&C 43:30]].
- Children of the righteous will grow up without sin, [[D&C 45.58|D&C 45:58]].
- Children shall grow up until they become old; men shall be changed in the twinkling of an eye, [[D&C 63.51|D&C 63:51]].
- In the beginning of the seventh thousand years, the Lord will sanctify the earth, [[D&C 77.12|D&C 77:12]].
- They live not again until the thousand years are ended, [[D&C 88.101|D&C 88:101]].
- Satan shall be bound for a thousand years, [[D&C 88.110|D&C 88:110]].
- The Millennium is described, [[D&C 101.23|D&C 101:23–34]][[D&C 101.24|]][[D&C 101.25|]][[D&C 101.26|]][[D&C 101.27|]][[D&C 101.28|]][[D&C 101.29|]][[D&C 101.30|]][[D&C 101.31|]][[D&C 101.32|]][[D&C 101.33|]][[D&C 101.34|]].

- For a thousand years the earth shall rest, [[Moses 7.64|Moses 7:64]].
