# Preach

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/preach?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Gospel|Gospel]]; [[GS Missionary Work|Missionary Work]]

To give a message that provides a better understanding of a gospel principle or doctrine.

- The Lord hath anointed me to preach good tidings unto the meek, [[Isaiah 61.1|Isa. 61:1]] ([[Luke 4.16|Luke 4:16–21]][[Luke 4.17|]][[Luke 4.18|]][[Luke 4.19|]][[Luke 4.20|]][[Luke 4.21|]]).
- Arise, go unto Nineveh, and preach unto it, [[Jonah 3.2|Jonah 3:2–10]][[Jonah 3.3|]][[Jonah 3.4|]][[Jonah 3.5|]][[Jonah 3.6|]][[Jonah 3.7|]][[Jonah 3.8|]][[Jonah 3.9|]][[Jonah 3.10|]].

- From that time Jesus began to preach, [[Matthew 4.17|Matt. 4:17]].
- Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature, [[Mark 16.15|Mark 16:15]].
- We preach Christ crucified, [[1 Corinthians 1.22|1 Cor. 1:22–24]][[1 Corinthians 1.23|]][[1 Corinthians 1.24|]].
- He went and preached unto the spirits in prison, [[1 Peter 3.19|1 Pet. 3:19]].

- There was nothing save it were preaching and stirring them up continually to keep them in fear of the Lord, [[Enos 1.23|Enos 1:23]].
- He commanded them that they should preach nothing save it were repentance and faith on the Lord, [[Mosiah 18.20|Mosiah 18:20]].
- The preaching of the word had a great tendency to lead the people to do that which is just, [[Alma 31.5|Alma 31:5]].

- You need not suppose that you are called to preach until you are called, [[D&C 11.15|D&C 11:15]].
- It shall not be given to any one to go forth to preach except he be ordained, [[D&C 42.11|D&C 42:11]].
- This gospel shall be preached to every nation, [[D&C 133.37|D&C 133:37]].

- The gospel began to be preached from the beginning, [[Moses 5.58|Moses 5:58]].
