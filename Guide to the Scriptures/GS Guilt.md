# Guilt

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/guilt?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Repent, Repentance|Repent, Repentance]]

The condition of having done wrong, or the feelings of regret and sorrow that should accompany sin.

- He hath sinned and is guilty, [[Leviticus 6.1|Lev. 6:1–6]][[Leviticus 6.2|]][[Leviticus 6.3|]][[Leviticus 6.4|]][[Leviticus 6.5|]][[Leviticus 6.6|]].

- Whosoever shall partake of the sacrament unworthily is guilty of the body and blood of Jesus, [[1 Corinthians 11.27|1 Cor. 11:27]].

- The guilty take the truth to be hard, [[1 Nephi 16.2|1 Ne. 16:2]].
- We shall have a perfect knowledge of all our guilt, [[2 Nephi 9.14|2 Ne. 9:14]].
- My guilt was swept away, [[Enos 1.6|Enos 1:6]].
- There was a punishment affixed, which brought remorse of conscience, [[Alma 42.18|Alma 42:18]].
- Let your sins trouble you, with that trouble which shall bring you down unto repentance, [[Alma 42.29|Alma 42:29]].

- Some of you are guilty before me, but I will be merciful, [[D&C 38.14|D&C 38:14]].

- The Son of God has atoned for original guilt, [[Moses 6.54|Moses 6:54]].
