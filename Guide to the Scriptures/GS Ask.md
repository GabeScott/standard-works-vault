# Ask

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/ask?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Prayer|Prayer]]

To inquire, question, or petition God for a special favor.

- Ask, and it shall be given you, [[Matthew 7.7|Matt. 7:7]].
- If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, [[James 1.5|James 1:5]] ([[Joseph Smith History 1.7|JS—H 1:7–20]][[Joseph Smith History 1.8|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.9|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.10|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.11|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.12|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.13|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.14|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.15|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.16|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.17|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.18|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.19|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.20|]]).

- Ask me in faith, [[1 Nephi 15.11|1 Ne. 15:11]].
- If ye cannot understand these words, it will be because ye ask not, [[2 Nephi 32.4|2 Ne. 32:4]].
- Ask in sincerity of heart, [[Mosiah 4.10|Mosiah 4:10]].
- God doth grant unto you whatsoever ye ask that is right, in faith, [[Mosiah 4.21|Mosiah 4:21]].
- Ask God if these things are not true, [[Moroni 10.4|Moro. 10:4]].

- They love darkness rather than light; therefore, they will not ask of me, [[D&C 10.21|D&C 10:21]].
- Ye are commanded in all things to ask of God, [[D&C 46.7|D&C 46:7]].
