# Martyr, Martyrdom

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/martyr-martyrdom?lang=eng)

A person who gives his life rather than forsake Christ, the gospel, or his righteous beliefs or principles.

- All the righteous blood from Abel to Zacharias will testify against the wicked, [[Matthew 23.35|Matt. 23:35]] ([[Luke 11.50|Luke 11:50]]).
- Whosoever shall lose his life for Christ’s sake and the gospel’s shall save it, [[Mark 8.35|Mark 8:35]] ([[D&C 98.13|D&C 98:13]]).
- And they stoned Stephen, [[Acts 7.59|Acts 7:59]] ([[Acts 22.20|Acts 22:20]]).
- Where a testament is there must be the death of the testator, [[Hebrews 9.16|Heb. 9:16–17]][[Hebrews 9.17|]].

- Abinadi fell, having suffered death by fire, [[Mosiah 17.20|Mosiah 17:20]].
- Converted Ammonihahites were thrown into the fire, [[Alma 14.8|Alma 14:8–11]][[Alma 14.9|]][[Alma 14.10|]][[Alma 14.11|]].
- Many were slain because they testified of these things, [[3 Nephi 10.15|3 Ne. 10:15]].

- Whoso layeth down his life in my cause shall find life eternal, [[D&C 98.13|D&C 98:13–14]][[D&C 98.14|]].
- Joseph Smith and Hyrum Smith were martyrs to the restoration of the gospel, [[D&C 135.1|D&C 135]][[D&C 135.2|]][[D&C 135.3|]][[D&C 135.4|]][[D&C 135.5|]][[D&C 135.6|]][[D&C 135.7|]].
- Joseph Smith sealed his testimony with his blood, [[D&C 136.39|D&C 136:39]].
