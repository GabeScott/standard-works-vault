# Heaven

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/heaven?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Celestial Glory|Celestial Glory]]; [[GS Kingdom of God or Kingdom of Heaven|Kingdom of God or Kingdom of Heaven]]; [[GS Paradise|Paradise]]

The term *heaven* has two basic meanings in the scriptures. (1) It is the place where God lives and the future home of the Saints ([[Genesis 28.12|Gen. 28:12]]; [[Psalms 11.4|Ps. 11:4]]; [[Matthew 6.9|Matt. 6:9]]). (2) It is the expanse around the earth ([[Genesis 1.1|Gen. 1:1, 17]][[Genesis 1.17|]]; [[Exodus 24.10|Ex. 24:10]]). Heaven is clearly not *paradise,* which is the temporary place for the faithful spirits of those who have lived and died on this earth. Jesus visited paradise after His death on the cross, but on the third day, He informed Mary that He had not yet been to the Father ([[Luke 23.39|Luke 23:39–44]][[Luke 23.40|]][[Luke 23.41|]][[Luke 23.42|]][[Luke 23.43|]][[Luke 23.44|]]; [[John 20.17|John 20:17]]; [[D&C 138.11|D&C 138:11–37]][[D&C 138.12|]][[D&C 138.13|]][[D&C 138.14|]][[D&C 138.15|]][[D&C 138.16|]][[D&C 138.17|]][[D&C 138.18|]][[D&C 138.19|]][[D&C 138.20|]][[D&C 138.21|]][[D&C 138.22|]][[D&C 138.23|]][[D&C 138.24|]][[D&C 138.25|]][[D&C 138.26|]][[D&C 138.27|]][[D&C 138.28|]][[D&C 138.29|]][[D&C 138.30|]][[D&C 138.31|]][[D&C 138.32|]][[D&C 138.33|]][[D&C 138.34|]][[D&C 138.35|]][[D&C 138.36|]][[D&C 138.37|]]).
