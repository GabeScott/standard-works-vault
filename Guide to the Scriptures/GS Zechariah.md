# Zechariah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/zechariah?lang=eng)

An Old Testament prophet who prophesied around 520  He lived at the same time as the prophet Haggai ([[Ezra 5.1|Ezra 5:1]]; [[Ezra 6.14|6:14]]).

## The book of Zechariah

The book is noted for its prophecies of Christ’s mortal ministry and His Second Coming ([[Zechariah 9.9|Zech. 9:9]]; [[Zechariah 11.12|11:12–13]][[Zechariah 11.13|]]; [[Zechariah 12.10|12:10]]; [[Zechariah 13.6|13:6]]).
