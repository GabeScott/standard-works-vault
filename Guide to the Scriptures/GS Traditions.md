# Traditions

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/traditions?lang=eng)

Beliefs and practices that are passed on from one generation to another ([[2 Thessalonians 2.15|2 Thes. 2:15]]). In the scriptures, the Lord consistently warns the righteous to avoid the evil traditions of men ([[Leviticus 18.30|Lev. 18:30]]; [[Mark 7.6|Mark 7:6–8]][[Mark 7.7|]][[Mark 7.8|]]; [[Mosiah 1.5|Mosiah 1:5]]; [[D&C 93.39|D&C 93:39–40]][[D&C 93.40|]]).
