# Mortal, Mortality

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/mortal-mortality?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Body|Body]]; [[GS Death, Physical|Death, Physical]]; [[GS Fall of Adam and Eve|Fall of Adam and Eve]]; [[GS World|World]]

The time from birth until physical death. This is sometimes called the second estate.

- In the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die, [[Genesis 2.16|Gen. 2:16–17]][[Genesis 2.17|]] ([[Moses 3.16|Moses 3:16–17]][[Moses 3.17|]]).
- At death the spirit shall return to God and the body to the dust of the earth, [[Ecclesiastes 12.7|Eccl. 12:7]] ([[Genesis 3.19|Gen. 3:19]]; [[Moses 4.25|Moses 4:25]]).

- Do not let sin reign in your mortal body, [[Romans 6.12|Rom. 6:12]].
- This mortal body must put on immortality, [[1 Corinthians 15.53|1 Cor. 15:53]] ([[Enos 1.27|Enos 1:27]]; [[Mosiah 16.10|Mosiah 16:10]]; [[Mormon 6.21|Morm. 6:21]]).

- Man’s state became a state of probation, [[2 Nephi 2.21|2 Ne. 2:21]] ([[Alma 12.24|Alma 12:24]]; [[Alma 42.10|42:10]]).
- Adam fell that men might be, [[2 Nephi 2.25|2 Ne. 2:25]].
- Do you look forward and view this mortal body raised in immortality, [[Alma 5.15|Alma 5:15]].
- This life is the time to prepare to meet God, [[Alma 34.32|Alma 34:32]].

- Do not fear death, for your joy is not full in this world, [[D&C 101.36|D&C 101:36]].

- Those who keep their second estate shall have glory, [[Abraham 3.26|Abr. 3:26]].
