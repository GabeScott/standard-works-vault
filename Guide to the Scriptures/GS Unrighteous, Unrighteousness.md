# Unrighteous, Unrighteousness

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/unrighteous-unrighteousness?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Filth, Filthiness|Filth, Filthiness]]; [[GS Righteous, Righteousness|Righteous, Righteousness]]; [[GS Sin|Sin]]; [[GS Ungodly|Ungodly]]; [[GS Wicked, Wickedness|Wicked, Wickedness]]

Wicked, unjust; people who do not love God or the things of God and who do not support His cause.

- The unrighteous shall not inherit the kingdom of God, [[1 Corinthians 6.9|1 Cor. 6:9–10]][[1 Corinthians 6.10|]].
- Those who have pleasure in unrighteousness are damned, [[2 Thessalonians 2.12|2 Thes. 2:12]].
- Jesus Christ can cleanse us from all unrighteousness, [[1 John 1.9|1 Jn. 1:9]].

- An unrighteous king doth pervert the ways of all righteousness, [[Mosiah 29.23|Mosiah 29:23]].
- The foundation of destruction is laid by the unrighteousness of lawyers and judges, [[Alma 10.27|Alma 10:27]].

- I send you out to reprove the world of unrighteous deeds, [[D&C 84.87|D&C 84:87]].
- The soul must be sanctified from all unrighteousness, [[D&C 88.17|D&C 88:17–18]][[D&C 88.18|]].
- The disposition of almost all men is to begin to exercise unrighteous dominion, [[D&C 121.39|D&C 121:39]].
