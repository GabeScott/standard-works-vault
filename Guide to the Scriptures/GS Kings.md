# Kings

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/kings?lang=eng)

Two books in the Old Testament. These books narrate the history of Israel from the rebellion of Adonijah, the fourth son of King David (about 1015 ), to the final captivity of Judah (about 586 ). They include the whole history of the northern kingdom (the ten tribes of Israel) from the separation until the Assyrians took them captive into the north countries. See also [Chronology](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bible-chron/old-testament?lang=eng&id=p47-p270#p47) in the Bible appendix.
