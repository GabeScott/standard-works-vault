# Bridegroom

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/bridegroom?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

Jesus Christ is symbolized in the scriptures as the Bridegroom. The Church is His symbolic bride.

- Ten virgins went forth to meet the Bridegroom, [[Matthew 25.1|Matt. 25:1–13]][[Matthew 25.2|]][[Matthew 25.3|]][[Matthew 25.4|]][[Matthew 25.5|]][[Matthew 25.6|]][[Matthew 25.7|]][[Matthew 25.8|]][[Matthew 25.9|]][[Matthew 25.10|]][[Matthew 25.11|]][[Matthew 25.12|]][[Matthew 25.13|]].
- He that has the bride is the Bridegroom, [[John 3.27|John 3:27–30]][[John 3.28|]][[John 3.29|]][[John 3.30|]].
- Blessed are they who are called unto the marriage supper of the Lamb, [[Revelation 19.5|Rev. 19:5–10]][[Revelation 19.6|]][[Revelation 19.7|]][[Revelation 19.8|]][[Revelation 19.9|]][[Revelation 19.10|]].

- Be ready at the coming of the Bridegroom, [[D&C 33.17|D&C 33:17]].
- Make ready for the Bridegroom, [[D&C 65.3|D&C 65:3]].
