# Vengeance

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/vengeance?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Enmity|Enmity]]

Revenge or retaliation for an injury or offense.

- Your God will come with vengeance, [[Isaiah 35.4|Isa. 35:4]].

- Vengeance is mine; I will repay, [[Romans 12.19|Rom. 12:19]] ([[Mormon 3.15|Morm. 3:15]]; [[Mormon 8.20|8:20]]).

- The sword of vengeance hangeth over you, [[Mormon 8.40|Morm. 8:40–41]][[Mormon 8.41|]].

- I will take vengeance upon the wicked, for they will not repent, [[D&C 29.17|D&C 29:17]].

- The Savior came in the days of wickedness and vengeance, [[Moses 7.45|Moses 7:45–46]][[Moses 7.46|]].
