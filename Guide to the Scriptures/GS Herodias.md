# Herodias

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/herodias?lang=eng)

Sister of Herod Agrippa in the New Testament. She was married to her uncle, Herod Philip, by whom she had a daughter, Salome. She and Salome conspired to have John the Baptist beheaded ([[Matthew 14.3|Matt. 14:3–11]][[Matthew 14.4|]][[Matthew 14.5|]][[Matthew 14.6|]][[Matthew 14.7|]][[Matthew 14.8|]][[Matthew 14.9|]][[Matthew 14.10|]][[Matthew 14.11|]]).
