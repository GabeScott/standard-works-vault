# Presiding Bishop

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/presiding-bishop?lang=eng)

A General Authority in the Church. He has general responsibility over the temporal welfare of the Church ([[D&C 107.68|D&C 107:68]]). The Presiding Bishop and his counselors, who are also General Authorities, preside over the Aaronic Priesthood of the Church ([[D&C 68.16|D&C 68:16–17]][[D&C 68.17|]]; [[D&C 107.76|107:76, 87–88]][[D&C 107.87|]][[D&C 107.88|]]).

- Edward Partridge to be ordained a bishop, [[D&C 41.9|D&C 41:9]].
- Bishops are to be called and set apart by the First Presidency, [[D&C 68.14|D&C 68:14–15]][[D&C 68.15|]].
- Literal descendants of Aaron, who are firstborn, have the right to preside if called, set apart, and ordained by the First Presidency, [[D&C 68.16|D&C 68:16, 18–20]][[D&C 68.18|]][[D&C 68.19|]][[D&C 68.20|]].
- May be tried only before the First Presidency, [[D&C 68.22|D&C 68:22–24]][[D&C 68.23|]][[D&C 68.24|]] ([[D&C 107.82|D&C 107:82]]).
