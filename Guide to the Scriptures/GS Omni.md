# Omni

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/omni?lang=eng)

A Nephite record keeper in the Book of Mormon who wrote in about 361  ([[Jarom 1.15|Jarom 1:15]]; [[Omni 1.1|Omni 1:1–3]][[Omni 1.2|]][[Omni 1.3|]]).

## The book of Omni

A book translated from the small plates of Nephi in the Book of Mormon. The book has only a single chapter, which contains an account of the wars among the Nephites and Lamanites. Omni wrote only the [[Omni 1.1|first three verses]][[Omni 1.2|]][[Omni 1.3|]] of the book. The plates were then passed in turn to Amaron, Chemish, Abinadom, and finally Amaleki. Amaleki delivered the plates to King Benjamin, king of Zarahemla.
