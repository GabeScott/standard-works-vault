# Virtue

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/virtue?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Chastity|Chastity]]; [[GS Integrity|Integrity]]; [[GS Power|Power]]

Integrity and moral excellence, power and strength ([[Luke 8.46|Luke 8:46]]), or sexual purity ([[Moroni 9.9|Moro. 9:9]]).

- Thou art a virtuous woman, [[Ruth 3.11|Ruth 3:11]].
- He that has clean hands and a pure heart shall stand in the Lord’s holy place, [[Psalms 24.3|Ps. 24:3–4]][[Psalms 24.4|]].
- A virtuous woman is a crown to her husband, [[Proverbs 12.4|Prov. 12:4]].
- The price of a virtuous woman is far above rubies, [[Proverbs 31.10|Prov. 31:10–31]][[Proverbs 31.11|]][[Proverbs 31.12|]][[Proverbs 31.13|]][[Proverbs 31.14|]][[Proverbs 31.15|]][[Proverbs 31.16|]][[Proverbs 31.17|]][[Proverbs 31.18|]][[Proverbs 31.19|]][[Proverbs 31.20|]][[Proverbs 31.21|]][[Proverbs 31.22|]][[Proverbs 31.23|]][[Proverbs 31.24|]][[Proverbs 31.25|]][[Proverbs 31.26|]][[Proverbs 31.27|]][[Proverbs 31.28|]][[Proverbs 31.29|]][[Proverbs 31.30|]][[Proverbs 31.31|]].

- Add to your faith virtue, [[2 Peter 1.5|2 Pet. 1:5]] ([[D&C 4.6|D&C 4:6]]).

- Try the virtue of the word of God, [[Alma 31.5|Alma 31:5]].

- Let virtue garnish thy thoughts unceasingly, [[D&C 121.45|D&C 121:45]].

- We believe in being virtuous, [[Articles of Faith 1.13|A of F 1:13]] ([[Philippians 4.8|Philip. 4:8]]).
