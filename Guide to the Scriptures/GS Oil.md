# Oil

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/oil?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Administration to the Sick|Administration to the Sick]]; [[GS Anoint|Anoint]]; [[GS Olive Tree|Olive Tree]]

Usually olive oil is meant when oil is mentioned in the scriptures. From Old Testament times, olive oil has been used for temple and tabernacle rites, for anointings, for burning in lamps, and for food. Olive oil is sometimes a symbol for purity and for the Holy Spirit and its influence ([[1 Samuel 10.1|1 Sam. 10:1, 6]][[1 Samuel 10.6|]]; [[1 Samuel 16.13|16:13]]; [[Isaiah 61.1|Isa. 61:1–3]][[Isaiah 61.2|]][[Isaiah 61.3|]]).

- The priest shall put oil upon the tip of the right ear, [[Leviticus 14.28|Lev. 14:28–29]][[Leviticus 14.29|]].
- The Lord sent me to anoint thee to be king over his people, [[1 Samuel 15.1|1 Sam. 15:1]].
- The widow’s container of oil did not become empty, [[1 Kings 17.10|1 Kgs. 17:10–16]][[1 Kings 17.11|]][[1 Kings 17.12|]][[1 Kings 17.13|]][[1 Kings 17.14|]][[1 Kings 17.15|]][[1 Kings 17.16|]].
- The Lord anoints my head with oil, [[Psalms 23.5|Ps. 23:5]].

- They anointed with oil many that were sick, [[Mark 6.13|Mark 6:13]].
- Let the elders pray over him, anointing him with oil, [[James 5.13|James 5:13–15]][[James 5.14|]][[James 5.15|]].

- Have your lamps trimmed and burning and oil with you, [[D&C 33.17|D&C 33:17]] ([[Matthew 25.1|Matt. 25:1–13]][[Matthew 25.2|]][[Matthew 25.3|]][[Matthew 25.4|]][[Matthew 25.5|]][[Matthew 25.6|]][[Matthew 25.7|]][[Matthew 25.8|]][[Matthew 25.9|]][[Matthew 25.10|]][[Matthew 25.11|]][[Matthew 25.12|]][[Matthew 25.13|]]).
