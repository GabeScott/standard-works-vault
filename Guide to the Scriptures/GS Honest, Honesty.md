# Honest, Honesty

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/honest-honesty?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Integrity|Integrity]]

To be sincere, truthful, and without deceit.

- They that deal truly are the Lord’s delight, [[Proverbs 12.22|Prov. 12:22]].
- Pay that which thou hast vowed, [[Ecclesiastes 5.4|Eccl. 5:4–5]][[Ecclesiastes 5.5|]].

- We have renounced the hidden things of dishonesty, [[2 Corinthians 4.1|2 Cor. 4:1–2]][[2 Corinthians 4.2|]].
- Have your conversation honest, [[1 Peter 2.12|1 Pet. 2:12]].

- Wo unto the liar, for he shall be thrust down to hell, [[2 Nephi 9.34|2 Ne. 9:34]].
- The Spirit speaketh the truth and lieth not, [[Jacob 4.13|Jacob 4:13]].
- Whosoever borroweth of his neighbor should return the thing that he borroweth, [[Mosiah 4.28|Mosiah 4:28]] ([[D&C 136.25|D&C 136:25]]).
- See that you deal justly, judge righteously, and do good, [[Alma 41.14|Alma 41:14]].

- Let every man deal honestly, [[D&C 51.9|D&C 51:9]].
- All who know their hearts are honest are accepted of me, [[D&C 97.8|D&C 97:8]].
- Honest, wise, and good men should be sought for political offices, [[D&C 98.4|D&C 98:4–10]][[D&C 98.5|]][[D&C 98.6|]][[D&C 98.7|]][[D&C 98.8|]][[D&C 98.9|]][[D&C 98.10|]].
- Diligently search to return that which thy neighbor has lost, [[D&C 136.26|D&C 136:26]].

- We believe in being honest, [[Articles of Faith 1.13|A of F 1:13]].
