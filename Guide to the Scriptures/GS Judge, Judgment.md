# Judge, Judgment

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/judge-judgment?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Condemn, Condemnation|Condemn, Condemnation]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ—Judge]]; [[GS Judgment, the Last|Judgment, the Last]]

To evaluate behavior in relation to the principles of the gospel; to decide; to discern good from evil.

- Moses sat to judge the people, [[Exodus 18.13|Ex. 18:13]].
- In righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbor, [[Leviticus 19.15|Lev. 19:15]].

- Judge not, that ye be not judged, [[Matthew 7.1|Matt. 7:1]] ([[JST Matthew 7.1|JST, Matt. 7:1–2]][[JST Matthew 7.2|]]; [[Luke 6.37|Luke 6:37]]; [[3 Nephi 14.1|3 Ne. 14:1]]).
- As many as have sinned in the law shall be judged by the law, [[Romans 2.12|Rom. 2:12]].
- The Saints shall judge the world, [[1 Corinthians 6.2|1 Cor. 6:2–3]][[1 Corinthians 6.3|]].

- The Son of the everlasting God was judged of the world, [[1 Nephi 11.32|1 Ne. 11:32]].
- The Twelve Apostles of the Lamb shall judge the twelve tribes of Israel, [[1 Nephi 12.9|1 Ne. 12:9]] ([[D&C 29.12|D&C 29:12]]).
- Death, hell, the devil, and all that have been seized therewith must be judged, [[2 Nephi 28.23|2 Ne. 28:23]] ([[1 Nephi 15.33|1 Ne. 15:33]]).
- If ye judge the man who putteth up his petition to you for substance, how much more just will be your condemnation for withholding your substance, [[Mosiah 4.22|Mosiah 4:22]].
- Men shall be judged according to their works, [[Alma 41.3|Alma 41:3]].
- Judge righteously, and you shall have justice restored unto you again, [[Alma 41.14|Alma 41:14]].
- Out of the books which shall be written shall the world be judged, [[3 Nephi 27.23|3 Ne. 27:23–26]][[3 Nephi 27.24|]][[3 Nephi 27.25|]][[3 Nephi 27.26|]] ([[Revelation 20.12|Rev. 20:12]]).
- The remnant of this people shall be judged by the twelve whom Jesus chose in this land, [[Mormon 3.18|Morm. 3:18–20]][[Mormon 3.19|]][[Mormon 3.20|]].
- Mormon explained the way to judge good from evil, [[Moroni 7.14|Moro. 7:14–18]][[Moroni 7.15|]][[Moroni 7.16|]][[Moroni 7.17|]][[Moroni 7.18|]].

- Put your trust in that Spirit which leadeth to judge righteously, [[D&C 11.12|D&C 11:12]].
- Ye ought to say in your hearts—let God judge between me and thee, [[D&C 64.11|D&C 64:11]].
- The Lord’s Church will judge the nations, [[D&C 64.37|D&C 64:37–38]][[D&C 64.38|]].
- The Son visited the spirits in prison that they might be judged according to men in the flesh, [[D&C 76.73|D&C 76:73]] ([[1 Peter 4.6|1 Pet. 4:6]]).
- A bishop shall be a common judge, [[D&C 107.72|D&C 107:72–74]][[D&C 107.73|]][[D&C 107.74|]].
- The Lord will judge all men according to their works, according to the desire of their hearts, [[D&C 137.9|D&C 137:9]].
