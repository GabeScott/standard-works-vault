# Whitmer, John

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/whitmer-john?lang=eng)

An early leader in the restored Church and one of the Eight Witnesses to the Book of Mormon. See “[The Testimony of Eight Witnesses](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/eight?lang=eng)” in the introductory pages of the Book of Mormon. He was also called to preach the gospel ([[D&C 30.9|D&C 30:9–11]][[D&C 30.10|]][[D&C 30.11|]]).
