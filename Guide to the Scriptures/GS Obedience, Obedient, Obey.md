# Obedience, Obedient, Obey

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/obedience-obedient-obey?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Bless, Blessed, Blessing|Bless, Blessed, Blessing]]; [[GS Commandments of God|Commandments of God]]; [[GS Hearken|Hearken]]; [[GS Joy|Joy]]; [[GS Law|Law]]; [[GS Walk, Walk with God|Walk, Walk with God]]

In a spiritual sense, obedience is doing God’s will.

- Noah did according to all that God commanded, [[Genesis 6.22|Gen. 6:22]].
- Abraham obeyed the Lord, [[Genesis 22.15|Gen. 22:15–18]][[Genesis 22.16|]][[Genesis 22.17|]][[Genesis 22.18|]].
- All that the Lord hath said will we do, [[Exodus 24.7|Ex. 24:7]].
- Hear therefore, O Israel, and observe to do it, [[Deuteronomy 6.1|Deut. 6:1–3]][[Deuteronomy 6.2|]][[Deuteronomy 6.3|]].
- Love the Lord and obey his voice, [[Deuteronomy 30.20|Deut. 30:20]].
- To obey is better than sacrifice, [[1 Samuel 15.22|1 Sam. 15:22]].
- Fear God, and keep his commandments, [[Ecclesiastes 12.13|Eccl. 12:13–14]][[Ecclesiastes 12.14|]].

- Not every one shall enter the kingdom of heaven, but he who does the will of the Father, [[Matthew 7.21|Matt. 7:21]] ([[3 Nephi 14.21|3 Ne. 14:21]]).
- If any man will do his will, he shall know if the doctrine is of God, [[John 7.17|John 7:17]].
- We ought to obey God rather than men, [[Acts 5.29|Acts 5:29]].
- Children, obey your parents, [[Ephesians 6.1|Eph. 6:1]] ([[Colossians 3.20|Col. 3:20]]).

- I will go and do the things which the Lord hath commanded, [[1 Nephi 3.7|1 Ne. 3:7]].
- I did obey the voice of the Spirit, [[1 Nephi 4.6|1 Ne. 4:6–18]][[1 Nephi 4.7|]][[1 Nephi 4.8|]][[1 Nephi 4.9|]][[1 Nephi 4.10|]][[1 Nephi 4.11|]][[1 Nephi 4.12|]][[1 Nephi 4.13|]][[1 Nephi 4.14|]][[1 Nephi 4.15|]][[1 Nephi 4.16|]][[1 Nephi 4.17|]][[1 Nephi 4.18|]].
- If the children of men keep the commandments of God he doth nourish them, [[1 Nephi 17.3|1 Ne. 17:3]].
- Beware lest ye obey the evil spirit, [[Mosiah 2.32|Mosiah 2:32–33, 37]][[Mosiah 2.33|]][[Mosiah 2.37|]] ([[D&C 29.45|D&C 29:45]]).
- Men reap their reward according to the spirit they obey, [[Alma 3.26|Alma 3:26–27]][[Alma 3.27|]].

- Men should do many things of their own free will, [[D&C 58.26|D&C 58:26–29]][[D&C 58.27|]][[D&C 58.28|]][[D&C 58.29|]].
- In nothing does man offend God, save those who confess not his hand and obey not his commandments, [[D&C 59.21|D&C 59:21]].
- I the Lord am bound when you do what I say, [[D&C 82.10|D&C 82:10]].
- Every soul who obeys my voice shall see my face and know that I am, [[D&C 93.1|D&C 93:1]].
- People must be chastened until they learn obedience, [[D&C 105.6|D&C 105:6]].
- When we obtain any blessing from God, it is by obedience to that law upon which it is predicated, [[D&C 130.21|D&C 130:21]].

- Adam was obedient, [[Moses 5.5|Moses 5:5]].
- We will prove them to see if they will do all things whatsoever the Lord shall command, [[Abraham 3.25|Abr. 3:25]].
