# Courage, Courageous

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/courage-courageous?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Faith|Faith]]; [[GS Fear|Fear]]

To not be afraid, especially of doing what is right.

- Be strong and of a good courage, [[Deuteronomy 31.6|Deut. 31:6]] ([[Joshua 1.6|Josh. 1:6–7]][[Joshua 1.7|]]).
- Be very courageous to keep and to do all that is written, [[Joshua 23.6|Josh. 23:6]].

- God has not given us the spirit of fear, [[2 Timothy 1.7|2 Tim. 1:7]].

- His heart took courage when he heard, [[Alma 15.4|Alma 15:4]] ([[Alma 62.1|Alma 62:1]]).
- The sons of Helaman were exceedingly valiant for courage, [[Alma 53.20|Alma 53:20–21]][[Alma 53.21|]].
- Never had I seen such great courage, [[Alma 56.45|Alma 56:45]].

- Courage, brethren, and on, on to the victory, [[D&C 128.22|D&C 128:22]].
