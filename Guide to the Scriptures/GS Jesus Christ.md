*Christ* (a Greek word) and *Messiah* (a Hebrew word) mean “the anointed.” Jesus Christ is the Firstborn of the Father in the spirit ([[Hebrews 1.6|Heb. 1:6]]; [[D&C 93.21|D&C 93:21]]). He is the Only Begotten of the Father in the flesh ([[John 1.14|John 1:14]]; [[John 3.16|3:16]]). He is Jehovah ([[D&C 110.3|D&C 110:3–4]][[D&C 110.4|]]) and was foreordained to His great calling before the creation of the world. Under the direction of the Father, Jesus created the earth and everything on it ([[John 1.3|John 1:3, 14]][[John 1.14|]]; [[Moses 1.31|Moses 1:31–33]][[Moses 1.32|]][[Moses 1.33|]]). He was born to Mary at Bethlehem, lived a sinless life, and made a perfect atonement for the sins of all mankind by the shedding of His blood and giving His life on the cross ([[Matthew 2.1|Matt. 2:1]]; [[1 Nephi 11.13|1 Ne. 11:13–33]][[1 Nephi 11.14|]][[1 Nephi 11.15|]][[1 Nephi 11.16|]][[1 Nephi 11.17|]][[1 Nephi 11.18|]][[1 Nephi 11.19|]][[1 Nephi 11.20|]][[1 Nephi 11.21|]][[1 Nephi 11.22|]][[1 Nephi 11.23|]][[1 Nephi 11.24|]][[1 Nephi 11.25|]][[1 Nephi 11.26|]][[1 Nephi 11.27|]][[1 Nephi 11.28|]][[1 Nephi 11.29|]][[1 Nephi 11.30|]][[1 Nephi 11.31|]][[1 Nephi 11.32|]][[1 Nephi 11.33|]]; [[3 Nephi 27.13|3 Ne. 27:13–16]][[3 Nephi 27.14|]][[3 Nephi 27.15|]][[3 Nephi 27.16|]]; [[D&C 76.40|D&C 76:40–42]][[D&C 76.41|]][[D&C 76.42|]]). He rose from the dead, thus assuring the eventual resurrection of all mankind. Through Jesus’ Atonement and Resurrection, those who repent of their sins and obey God’s commandments can live eternally with Jesus and the Father ([[2 Nephi 9.10|2 Ne. 9:10–12]][[2 Nephi 9.11|]][[2 Nephi 9.12|]]; [[2 Nephi 21.1|21–22]][[2 Nephi 21.2|]][[2 Nephi 21.3|]][[2 Nephi 21.4|]][[2 Nephi 21.5|]][[2 Nephi 21.6|]][[2 Nephi 21.7|]][[2 Nephi 21.8|]][[2 Nephi 21.9|]][[2 Nephi 21.10|]][[2 Nephi 21.11|]][[2 Nephi 21.12|]][[2 Nephi 21.13|]][[2 Nephi 21.14|]][[2 Nephi 21.15|]][[2 Nephi 21.16|]]; [[D&C 76.50|D&C 76:50–53, 62]][[D&C 76.51|]][[D&C 76.52|]][[D&C 76.53|]][[D&C 76.62|]]).

Jesus Christ is the greatest being to be born on this earth. His life is the perfect example of how all mankind should live. All prayers, blessings, and priesthood ordinances should be done in His name. He is the Lord of lords, King of kings, the Creator, the Savior, and the God of the whole earth.

Jesus Christ will come again in power and glory to reign on the earth during the Millennium. At the last day, He will judge all mankind ([[Alma 11.40|Alma 11:40–41]][[Alma 11.41|]]; [[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.1|JS—M 1]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.2|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.3|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.4|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.5|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.6|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.7|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.8|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.9|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.10|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.11|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.12|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.13|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.14|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.15|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.16|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.17|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.18|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.19|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.20|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.21|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.22|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.23|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.24|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.25|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.26|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.27|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.28|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.29|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.30|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.31|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.32|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.33|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.34|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.35|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.36|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.37|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.38|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.39|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.40|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.41|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.42|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.43|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.44|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.45|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.46|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.47|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.48|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.49|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.50|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.51|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.52|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.53|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.54|]][[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.55|]]).
