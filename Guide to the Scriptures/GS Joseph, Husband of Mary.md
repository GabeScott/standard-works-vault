# Joseph, Husband of Mary

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/joseph-husband-of-mary?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Mary, Mother of Jesus|Mary, Mother of Jesus]]

Husband of Mary, mother of Jesus. Joseph was a descendant of David ([[Matthew 1.1|Matt. 1:1–16]][[Matthew 1.2|]][[Matthew 1.3|]][[Matthew 1.4|]][[Matthew 1.5|]][[Matthew 1.6|]][[Matthew 1.7|]][[Matthew 1.8|]][[Matthew 1.9|]][[Matthew 1.10|]][[Matthew 1.11|]][[Matthew 1.12|]][[Matthew 1.13|]][[Matthew 1.14|]][[Matthew 1.15|]][[Matthew 1.16|]]; [[Luke 3.23|Luke 3:23–38]][[Luke 3.24|]][[Luke 3.25|]][[Luke 3.26|]][[Luke 3.27|]][[Luke 3.28|]][[Luke 3.29|]][[Luke 3.30|]][[Luke 3.31|]][[Luke 3.32|]][[Luke 3.33|]][[Luke 3.34|]][[Luke 3.35|]][[Luke 3.36|]][[Luke 3.37|]][[Luke 3.38|]]) and lived in Nazareth. He was espoused to Mary. Just before their marriage, Mary received a visit from the angel Gabriel, who announced that Mary had been chosen to be the mother of the Savior ([[Luke 1.26|Luke 1:26–35]][[Luke 1.27|]][[Luke 1.28|]][[Luke 1.29|]][[Luke 1.30|]][[Luke 1.31|]][[Luke 1.32|]][[Luke 1.33|]][[Luke 1.34|]][[Luke 1.35|]]). Joseph also received revelation of this divine birth ([[Matthew 1.20|Matt. 1:20–25]][[Matthew 1.21|]][[Matthew 1.22|]][[Matthew 1.23|]][[Matthew 1.24|]][[Matthew 1.25|]]).

Mary was Jesus’ only earthly parent because God the Father was Jesus’ father. But the Jews thought of Joseph as Jesus’ father, and Jesus treated him as such ([[Luke 2.48|Luke 2:48, 51]][[Luke 2.51|]]). Warned by heavenly dreams, Joseph preserved the infant Jesus’ life by fleeing to Egypt ([[Matthew 2.13|Matt. 2:13–14]][[Matthew 2.14|]]). After Herod died, an angel instructed Joseph to take the Christ child back to Israel ([[Matthew 2.19|Matt. 2:19–23]][[Matthew 2.20|]][[Matthew 2.21|]][[Matthew 2.22|]][[Matthew 2.23|]]).
