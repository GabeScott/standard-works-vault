# Smith, Lucy Mack

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/smith-lucy-mack?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Smith, Joseph, Jr.|Smith, Joseph, Jr.]]; [[GS Smith, Joseph, Sr.|Smith, Joseph, Sr.]]

The mother of the Prophet Joseph Smith and wife of Joseph Smith Sr. ([[Joseph Smith History 1.4|JS—H 1:4, 7, 20]][[Joseph Smith History 1.7|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.20|]]). She was born July 8, 1776, and died May 5, 1856.

- Joseph saw his mother in a vision of the celestial kingdom, [[D&C 137.5|D&C 137:5]].
