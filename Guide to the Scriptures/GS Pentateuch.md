# Pentateuch

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/pentateuch?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Deuteronomy|Deuteronomy]]; [[GS Exodus|Exodus]]; [[GS Genesis|Genesis]]; [[GS Leviticus|Leviticus]]; [[GS Moses|Moses]]; [[GS Numbers|Numbers]]; [[GS Old Testament|Old Testament]]

A name given to the first five books of the Old Testament—Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy. The Jews refer to these books as the Torah or law of Israel. They were written by Moses ([[1 Nephi 5.10|1 Ne. 5:10–11]][[1 Nephi 5.11|]]).
