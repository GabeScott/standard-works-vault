# Flood at Noah’s Time

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/flood-at-noahs-time?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Ark|Ark]]; [[GS Noah, Bible Patriarch|Noah, Bible Patriarch]]; [[GS Rainbow|Rainbow]]

During Noah’s time the earth was completely covered with water. This was the baptism of the earth and symbolized a cleansing ([[1 Peter 3.20|1 Pet. 3:20–21]][[1 Peter 3.21|]]).

- God will bring a flood of waters upon the earth to destroy all flesh, [[Genesis 6.17|Gen. 6:17]] ([[Moses 7.34|Moses 7:34, 43, 50–52]][[Moses 7.43|]][[Moses 7.50|]][[Moses 7.51|]][[Moses 7.52|]]; [[Moses 8.17|8:17, 30]][[Moses 8.30|]]).
- The waters of the flood were upon the earth, [[Genesis 7.10|Gen. 7:10]].
- God set a bow in the cloud as a token of the covenant, [[Genesis 9.9|Gen. 9:9–17]][[Genesis 9.10|]][[Genesis 9.11|]][[Genesis 9.12|]][[Genesis 9.13|]][[Genesis 9.14|]][[Genesis 9.15|]][[Genesis 9.16|]][[Genesis 9.17|]].

- After the waters had receded, the land of America became a choice land, [[Ether 13.2|Ether 13:2]].

- The wicked shall perish in the flood, [[Moses 7.38|Moses 7:38]]; [[Moses 8.24|8:24]].
