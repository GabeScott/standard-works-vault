# Translated Beings

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/translated-beings?lang=eng)

Persons who are changed so that they do not experience pain or death until their resurrection to immortality.

- Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him, [[Genesis 5.24|Gen. 5:24]] ([[Hebrews 11.5|Heb. 11:5]]; [[D&C 107.48|D&C 107:48–49]][[D&C 107.49|]]).
- No man knoweth of Moses’ sepulchre unto this day, [[Deuteronomy 34.5|Deut. 34:5–6]][[Deuteronomy 34.6|]] ([[Alma 45.19|Alma 45:19]]).
- Elijah went up by a whirlwind into heaven, [[2 Kings 2.11|2 Kgs. 2:11]].

- If I will that he tarry till I come, what is that to thee, [[John 21.22|John 21:22–23]][[John 21.23|]] ([[D&C 7.1|D&C 7:1–3]][[D&C 7.2|]][[D&C 7.3|]]).

- You shall never taste of death, [[3 Nephi 28.7|3 Ne. 28:7]].
- That they might not taste of death there was a change wrought upon their bodies, [[3 Nephi 28.38|3 Ne. 28:38]] ([[4 Nephi 1.14|4 Ne. 1:14]]; [[Mormon 8.10|Morm. 8:10–11]][[Mormon 8.11|]]).

- John the Beloved will live until the Lord comes, [[D&C 7.1|D&C 7]][[D&C 7.2|]][[D&C 7.3|]][[D&C 7.4|]][[D&C 7.5|]][[D&C 7.6|]][[D&C 7.7|]][[D&C 7.8|]].
- I have taken the Zion of Enoch into mine own bosom, [[D&C 38.4|D&C 38:4]] ([[Moses 7.21|Moses 7:21, 31, 69]][[Moses 7.31|]][[Moses 7.69|]]).
- Enoch and his brethren are a city reserved until a day of righteousness, [[D&C 45.11|D&C 45:11–12]][[D&C 45.12|]].
- Elijah was taken to heaven without tasting death, [[D&C 110.13|D&C 110:13]].

- The Holy Ghost fell on many, and they were caught up into Zion, [[Moses 7.27|Moses 7:27]].
