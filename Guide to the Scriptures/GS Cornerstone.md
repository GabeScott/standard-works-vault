# Cornerstone

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/cornerstone?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

The main stone forming the corner of the foundation of a building. Jesus Christ is called the chief cornerstone ([[Ephesians 2.20|Eph. 2:20]]).

- The stone that the builders refused has become the head stone of the corner, [[Psalms 118.22|Ps. 118:22]] ([[Matthew 21.42|Matt. 21:42–44]][[Matthew 21.43|]][[Matthew 21.44|]]; [[Mark 12.10|Mark 12:10]]; [[Luke 20.17|Luke 20:17]]; [[Acts 4.10|Acts 4:10–12]][[Acts 4.11|]][[Acts 4.12|]]).

- The Jews rejected the cornerstone, [[Jacob 4.15|Jacob 4:15–17]][[Jacob 4.16|]][[Jacob 4.17|]].
