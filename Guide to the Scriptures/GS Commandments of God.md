# Commandments of God

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/commandments-of-god?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Commandments, the Ten|Commandments, the Ten]]; [[GS Law|Law]]; [[GS Obedience, Obedient, Obey|Obedience, Obedient, Obey]]; [[GS Sin|Sin]]; [[GS Word of God|Word of God]]

The laws and requirements that God gives to mankind, either individually or collectively. Keeping the commandments will bring blessings of the Lord to the obedient ([[D&C 130.21|D&C 130:21]]).

- Noah did according to all that God commanded him, [[Genesis 6.22|Gen. 6:22]].
- Walk in my statutes, and keep my commandments, [[Leviticus 26.3|Lev. 26:3]].
- Keep my commandments, and live, [[Proverbs 4.4|Prov. 4:4]] ([[Proverbs 7.2|Prov. 7:2]]).

- If ye love me, keep my commandments, [[John 14.15|John 14:15]] ([[D&C 42.29|D&C 42:29]]).
- Whatsoever we ask, we receive of him, because we keep his commandments, [[1 John 3.22|1 Jn. 3:22]].
- His commandments are not grievous, [[1 John 5.3|1 Jn. 5:3]].

- Be immovable in keeping the commandments, [[1 Nephi 2.10|1 Ne. 2:10]].
- The Lord gives no commandments save he shall prepare a way, [[1 Nephi 3.7|1 Ne. 3:7]].
- I must do according to the strict commands of God, [[Jacob 2.10|Jacob 2:10]].
- Inasmuch as ye keep my commandments, ye shall prosper in the land, [[Jarom 1.9|Jarom 1:9]] ([[Alma 9.13|Alma 9:13]]; [[Alma 50.20|50:20]]).
- Learn in thy youth to keep the commandments of God, [[Alma 37.35|Alma 37:35]].

- These commandments are of me, [[D&C 1.24|D&C 1:24]].
- Search these commandments, [[D&C 1.37|D&C 1:37]].
- Those who do not keep the commandments cannot be saved, [[D&C 18.46|D&C 18:46]] ([[D&C 25.15|D&C 25:15]]; [[D&C 56.2|56:2]]).
- My commandments are spiritual; they are not natural or temporal, [[D&C 29.35|D&C 29:35]].
- Commandments are given so that we may understand the Lord’s will, [[D&C 82.8|D&C 82:8]].

- I know not, save the Lord commanded me, [[Moses 5.6|Moses 5:6]].
- The Lord will prove men to see if they will do all that he commands them, [[Abraham 3.25|Abr. 3:25]].
