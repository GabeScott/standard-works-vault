# Eye, Eyes

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/eye-eyes?lang=eng)

In the scriptures, the eye is often used as a symbol of a person’s ability to receive the light of God. Symbolically, a person’s eye also shows spiritual condition and an understanding of the things of God.

- The commandment of the Lord is pure, enlightening the eyes, [[Psalms 19.8|Ps. 19:8]].
- The foolish have eyes and see not, [[Jeremiah 5.21|Jer. 5:21]] ([[Mark 8.18|Mark 8:18]]).

- The light of the body is the eye, [[Matthew 6.22|Matt. 6:22]] ([[Luke 11.34|Luke 11:34]]; [[3 Nephi 13.22|3 Ne. 13:22]]; [[D&C 88.67|D&C 88:67]]).
- Blessed are your eyes for they see, [[Matthew 13.16|Matt. 13:16]].
- The eyes of your understanding will be enlightened, [[Ephesians 1.17|Eph. 1:17–18]][[Ephesians 1.18|]].

- Wo unto the wise in their own eyes, [[2 Nephi 15.21|2 Ne. 15:21]] ([[Isaiah 5.21|Isa. 5:21]]).
- They began to fast and to pray that the eyes of the people might be opened, [[Mosiah 27.22|Mosiah 27:22]].
- Satan did blind their eyes, [[3 Nephi 2.2|3 Ne. 2:2]].
- None can have power to bring the Book of Mormon forth except with an eye single to God’s glory, [[Mormon 8.15|Morm. 8:15]].

- By the power of the Spirit our eyes were opened and our understandings enlightened, [[D&C 76.12|D&C 76:12]].
- The light is through him who enlighteneth your eyes, [[D&C 88.11|D&C 88:11]].
- If your eye be single to my glory, your whole bodies shall be filled with light, [[D&C 88.67|D&C 88:67]].
