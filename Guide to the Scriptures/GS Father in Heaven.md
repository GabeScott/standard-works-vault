# Father in Heaven

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/father-in-heaven?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS God, Godhead|God, Godhead]]

The Father of the spirits of all mankind ([[Psalms 82.6|Ps. 82:6]]; [[Matthew 5.48|Matt. 5:48]]; [[John 10.34|John 10:34]]; [[Romans 8.16|Rom. 8:16–17]][[Romans 8.17|]]; [[Galatians 4.7|Gal. 4:7]]; [[1 John 3.2|1 Jn. 3:2]]). Jesus is His Only Begotten Son in the flesh. Man has been commanded to obey and give reverence to the Father and to pray to Him in Jesus’ name.

- If ye forgive men, your Heavenly Father will also forgive you, [[Matthew 6.14|Matt. 6:14]] ([[Matthew 18.35|Matt. 18:35]]; [[3 Nephi 13.14|3 Ne. 13:14]]).
- Your Heavenly Father knoweth that ye have need of all these things, [[Matthew 6.26|Matt. 6:26–33]][[Matthew 6.27|]][[Matthew 6.28|]][[Matthew 6.29|]][[Matthew 6.30|]][[Matthew 6.31|]][[Matthew 6.32|]][[Matthew 6.33|]] ([[3 Nephi 13.26|3 Ne. 13:26–33]][[3 Nephi 13.27|]][[3 Nephi 13.28|]][[3 Nephi 13.29|]][[3 Nephi 13.30|]][[3 Nephi 13.31|]][[3 Nephi 13.32|]][[3 Nephi 13.33|]]).
- How much more shall your Heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to them that ask him, [[Luke 11.11|Luke 11:11–13]][[Luke 11.12|]][[Luke 11.13|]].
- Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, [[Ephesians 1.3|Eph. 1:3]].

- Ye are eternally indebted to your Heavenly Father, [[Mosiah 2.34|Mosiah 2:34]].
- Christ glorified the name of the Father, [[Ether 12.8|Ether 12:8]].

- The Saints should bear witness of their persecutions before the Father comes forth from his hiding place, [[D&C 123.1|D&C 123:1–3, 6]][[D&C 123.2|]][[D&C 123.3|]][[D&C 123.6|]].

- We experienced great and glorious blessings from our Heavenly Father, [[Joseph Smith History 1.73|JS—H 1:73]].
