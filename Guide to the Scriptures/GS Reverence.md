# Reverence

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/reverence?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Fear|Fear]]; [[GS Honor|Honor]]

Deep respect for sacred things; wonder.

- The Lord commanded Moses to remove his shoes, for he was standing on holy ground, [[Exodus 3.4|Ex. 3:4–5]][[Exodus 3.5|]].
- God is to be feared and had in reverence, [[Psalms 89.7|Ps. 89:7]].

- Serve God acceptably with reverence and godly fear, [[Hebrews 12.28|Heb. 12:28]].

- Moroni bowed himself to the earth, and prayed mightily, [[Alma 46.13|Alma 46:13]].
- The multitude fell to the earth and worshiped Christ, [[3 Nephi 11.12|3 Ne. 11:12–19]][[3 Nephi 11.13|]][[3 Nephi 11.14|]][[3 Nephi 11.15|]][[3 Nephi 11.16|]][[3 Nephi 11.17|]][[3 Nephi 11.18|]][[3 Nephi 11.19|]].

- Bow down before me, [[D&C 5.24|D&C 5:24]].
- All things bow in humble reverence before the throne of God, [[D&C 76.93|D&C 76:93]].
- Your minds have been darkened because you have treated lightly the things you have received, [[D&C 84.54|D&C 84:54–57]][[D&C 84.55|]][[D&C 84.56|]][[D&C 84.57|]].
- Every knee shall bow and every tongue shall confess, [[D&C 88.104|D&C 88:104]].
- Out of respect or reverence to the name of the Supreme Being, the Church called that priesthood after Melchizedek, [[D&C 107.4|D&C 107:4]].
- Blessings will be poured out upon those who reverence the Lord in his house, [[D&C 109.21|D&C 109:21]].
