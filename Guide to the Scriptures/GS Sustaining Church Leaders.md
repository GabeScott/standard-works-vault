# Sustaining Church Leaders

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/sustaining-church-leaders?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Common Consent|Common Consent]]

To pledge support to those serving in general and local Church leadership positions.

- Set Joshua before all the congregation, and give him a charge in their sight, [[Numbers 27.18|Num. 27:18–19]][[Numbers 27.19|]].
- All the people shouted, and said, God save the king, [[1 Samuel 10.24|1 Sam. 10:24]].
- Believe his prophets, so shall ye prosper, [[2 Chronicles 20.20|2 Chr. 20:20]].

- Obey them that have the rule over you, [[Hebrews 13.17|Heb. 13:17]].

- Thou shalt be favored of the Lord, because thou hast not murmured, [[1 Nephi 3.6|1 Ne. 3:6]].
- They who received the prophets were spared, [[3 Nephi 10.12|3 Ne. 10:12–13]][[3 Nephi 10.13|]].
- Give heed unto the words of these twelve, [[3 Nephi 12.1|3 Ne. 12:1]].

- By mine own voice or by the voice of my servants, it is the same, [[D&C 1.38|D&C 1:38]].
- His word ye shall receive, as if from mine own mouth, [[D&C 21.5|D&C 21:5]].
- He that receiveth my servants, receiveth me, [[D&C 84.35|D&C 84:35–38]][[D&C 84.36|]][[D&C 84.37|]][[D&C 84.38|]].
- Whoso receiveth me, receiveth those whom I have sent, [[D&C 112.20|D&C 112:20]].
- If my people will not hearken to the voice of these men whom I have appointed, they shall not be blest, [[D&C 124.45|D&C 124:45–46]][[D&C 124.46|]].
