# Righteous, Righteousness

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/righteous-righteousness?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Commandments of God|Commandments of God]]; [[GS Integrity|Integrity]]; [[GS Unrighteous, Unrighteousness|Unrighteous, Unrighteousness]]; [[GS Walk, Walk with God|Walk, Walk with God]]; [[GS Worthy, Worthiness|Worthy, Worthiness]]

Being just, holy, virtuous, upright; acting in obedience to God’s commands; avoiding sin.

- The Lord will bless the righteous, [[Psalms 5.12|Ps. 5:12]].
- The eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous, [[Psalms 34.15|Ps. 34:15, 17]][[Psalms 34.17|]] ([[1 Peter 3.12|1 Pet. 3:12]]).
- When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice, [[Proverbs 29.2|Prov. 29:2]] ([[D&C 98.9|D&C 98:9–10]][[D&C 98.10|]]).

- Blessed are they who hunger and thirst after righteousness, [[Matthew 5.6|Matt. 5:6]] ([[3 Nephi 12.6|3 Ne. 12:6]]).
- Seek first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, [[Matthew 6.33|Matt. 6:33]].
- The righteous go into life eternal, [[Matthew 25.46|Matt. 25:46]].
- The fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much, [[James 5.16|James 5:16]].

- He that is righteous is favored of God, [[1 Nephi 17.35|1 Ne. 17:35]].
- He will preserve the righteous; they need not fear, [[1 Nephi 22.17|1 Ne. 22:17, 22]][[1 Nephi 22.22|]].
- Satan will have no power because of the righteousness of the Lord’s people, [[1 Nephi 22.26|1 Ne. 22:26]].
- If there be no righteousness there be no happiness, [[2 Nephi 2.13|2 Ne. 2:13]].
- The righteous shall inherit the kingdom of God, [[2 Nephi 9.18|2 Ne. 9:18]].
- The righteous fear not the words of truth, [[2 Nephi 9.40|2 Ne. 9:40]].
- All men must be changed to a state of righteousness, [[Mosiah 27.25|Mosiah 27:25–26]][[Mosiah 27.26|]].
- The names of the righteous shall be written in the book of life, [[Alma 5.58|Alma 5:58]].
- Ye have sought happiness in doing iniquity, which is contrary to the nature of righteousness, [[Helaman 13.38|Hel. 13:38]].

- The song of the righteous is a prayer unto me, [[D&C 25.12|D&C 25:12]].
- Stand, having on the breastplate of righteousness, [[D&C 27.16|D&C 27:16]] ([[Ephesians 6.14|Eph. 6:14]]).
- The death of the righteous is sweet unto them, [[D&C 42.46|D&C 42:46]].
- The righteous shall be gathered out from among all nations, [[D&C 45.71|D&C 45:71]].
- Men should bring to pass much righteousness of their own free will, [[D&C 58.27|D&C 58:27]].
- He who does the works of righteousness shall receive peace in this world and eternal life in the world to come, [[D&C 59.23|D&C 59:23]].
- At the Second Coming, there will be an entire separation of the righteous and the wicked, [[D&C 63.54|D&C 63:54]].
- The powers of heaven can be controlled only upon the principles of righteousness, [[D&C 121.36|D&C 121:36]].
- Among the righteous there was peace, [[D&C 138.22|D&C 138:22]].

- The people of Zion dwelt in righteousness, [[Moses 7.18|Moses 7:18]].
- Abraham was a follower of righteousness, [[Abraham 1.2|Abr. 1:2]].
