# Administration to the Sick

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/administration-to-the-sick?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Anoint|Anoint]]; [[GS Hands, Laying on of|Hands, Laying on of]]; [[GS Heal, Healings|Heal, Healings]]; [[GS Oil|Oil]]; [[GS Priesthood|Priesthood]]

A blessing given to the sick by men who hold the Melchizedek Priesthood, involving the use of consecrated oil.

- Lay thy hand upon her, [[Matthew 9.18|Matt. 9:18]].
- Jesus laid his hands upon a few sick folk and healed them, [[Mark 6.5|Mark 6:5]].
- Christ’s Apostles anointed with oil many that were sick and healed them, [[Mark 6.13|Mark 6:13]].
- The elders are to anoint and heal the sick, [[James 5.14|James 5:14–15]][[James 5.15|]].

- Ye shall not heal the sick except it be required of you by them who desire, [[D&C 24.13|D&C 24:13–14]][[D&C 24.14|]].
- Elders shall lay their hands upon the sick, [[D&C 42.44|D&C 42:44]].
- Lay your hands upon the sick, and they shall recover, [[D&C 66.9|D&C 66:9]].
