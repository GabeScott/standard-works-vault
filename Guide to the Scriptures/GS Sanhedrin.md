# Sanhedrin

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/sanhedrin?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jews|Jews]]

The Jewish senate and the highest Jewish court in both civil and ecclesiastical matters. The Sanhedrin consisted of seventy-one members drawn from among the chief priests, scribes, and elders. In the scriptures, it is often called the council ([[Matthew 26.59|Matt. 26:59]]; [[Mark 14.55|Mark 14:55]]; [[Acts 5.34|Acts 5:34]]).
