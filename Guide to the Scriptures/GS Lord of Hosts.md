# Lord of Hosts

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/lord-of-hosts?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

Another name for Jesus Christ. He reigns over the hosts of heaven and earth and leads the righteous against evil ([[D&C 29.9|D&C 29:9]]; [[D&C 121.23|121:23]]).

- The Lord of Hosts is the King of glory, [[Psalms 24.10|Ps. 24:10]].

- The God of Israel is the Lord of Hosts, [[1 Nephi 20.2|1 Ne. 20:2]].

- My Spirit shall not always strive with man, saith the Lord of Hosts, [[D&C 1.33|D&C 1:33]].
