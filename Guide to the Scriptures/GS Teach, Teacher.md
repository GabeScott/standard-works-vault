# Teach, Teacher

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/teach-teacher?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Holy Ghost|Holy Ghost]]

To give knowledge to others, especially about gospel truths, and guide them to righteousness. Those who teach the gospel should be directed by the Spirit. All parents are teachers within their own families. Saints should seek and be willing to accept instructions from the Lord and His leaders.

- Teach them to thy sons, [[Deuteronomy 4.8|Deut. 4:8–9]][[Deuteronomy 4.9|]].
- Thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children, [[Deuteronomy 6.7|Deut. 6:7]] ([[Deuteronomy 11.18|Deut. 11:18–19]][[Deuteronomy 11.19|]]).
- Train up a child in the way he should go, [[Proverbs 22.6|Prov. 22:6]].
- Thy children shall be taught of the Lord, [[Isaiah 54.13|Isa. 54:13]] ([[3 Nephi 22.13|3 Ne. 22:13]]).

- We know thou art a teacher come from God, [[John 3.2|John 3:2]].
- Thou which teachest another, teachest thou not thyself, [[Romans 2.21|Rom. 2:21]].

- I was taught somewhat in all the learning of my father, [[1 Nephi 1.1|1 Ne. 1:1]] ([[Enos 1.1|Enos 1:1]]).
- Priests and teachers must teach diligently or answer the sins of the people upon their own heads, [[Jacob 1.18|Jacob 1:18–19]][[Jacob 1.19|]].
- Hearken unto me, and open your ears, [[Mosiah 2.9|Mosiah 2:9]].
- Ye will teach your children to love and serve one another, [[Mosiah 4.15|Mosiah 4:15]].
- Trust no one to be your teacher except he be a man of God, [[Mosiah 23.14|Mosiah 23:14]].
- The Lord did pour out his Spirit on all the land to prepare their hearts to receive the word, [[Alma 16.16|Alma 16:16]].
- They taught with power and authority of God, [[Alma 17.2|Alma 17:2–3]][[Alma 17.3|]].
- They had been taught by their mothers, [[Alma 56.47|Alma 56:47]] ([[Alma 57.21|Alma 57:21]]).

- Inasmuch as they sought wisdom they might be instructed, [[D&C 1.26|D&C 1:26]].
- Teach one another according to the office wherewith I have appointed you, [[D&C 38.23|D&C 38:23]].
- Teach the principles of my gospel, which are in the Bible and the Book of Mormon, [[D&C 42.12|D&C 42:12]].
- Ye are to be taught from on high, [[D&C 43.15|D&C 43:15–16]][[D&C 43.16|]].
- Parents shall teach their children, [[D&C 68.25|D&C 68:25–28]][[D&C 68.26|]][[D&C 68.27|]][[D&C 68.28|]].
- Teach one another the doctrine of the kingdom, [[D&C 88.77|D&C 88:77–78, 118]][[D&C 88.78|]][[D&C 88.118|]].
- Appoint among yourselves a teacher, [[D&C 88.122|D&C 88:122]].
- You have not taught your children light and truth, and this is the cause of your affliction, [[D&C 93.39|D&C 93:39–42]][[D&C 93.40|]][[D&C 93.41|]][[D&C 93.42|]].

- Teach these things freely unto your children, [[Moses 6.57|Moses 6:57–61]][[Moses 6.58|]][[Moses 6.59|]][[Moses 6.60|]][[Moses 6.61|]].
