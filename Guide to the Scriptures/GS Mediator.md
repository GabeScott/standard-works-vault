# Mediator

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/mediator?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

An intercessor or intermediary. Jesus Christ is the mediator between God and man. His Atonement made possible a way for people to repent of their sins and become reconciled to God.

- No man cometh unto the Father except by Jesus Christ, [[John 14.6|John 14:6]].
- There is one mediator between God and men, [[1 Timothy 2.5|1 Tim. 2:5]].
- Christ is the mediator of a better covenant, [[Hebrews 8.6|Heb. 8:6]] ([[Hebrews 9.15|Heb. 9:15]]; [[Hebrews 12.24|12:24]]; [[D&C 107.19|D&C 107:19]]).

- The Holy Messiah shall make intercession for all the children of men, [[2 Nephi 2.9|2 Ne. 2:9]] ([[Isaiah 53.12|Isa. 53:12]]; [[Mosiah 14.12|Mosiah 14:12]]).
- Ye should look to the great Mediator, [[2 Nephi 2.27|2 Ne. 2:27–28]][[2 Nephi 2.28|]].

- We are made perfect through Jesus the mediator of the new covenant, [[D&C 76.69|D&C 76:69]].
