# Thomas

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/thomas?lang=eng)

In the New Testament, one of the original Twelve Apostles chosen by the Savior during His mortal ministry ([[Matthew 10.2|Matt. 10:2–3]][[Matthew 10.3|]]; [[John 14.5|John 14:5]]). In Greek the name is Didymus ([[John 20.24|John 20:24–29]][[John 20.25|]][[John 20.26|]][[John 20.27|]][[John 20.28|]][[John 20.29|]]; [[John 21.2|21:2]]). Though Thomas doubted Jesus’ Resurrection until he personally saw the Savior, his strength of character made him willing to face persecution and death with his Lord ([[John 11.16|John 11:16]]; [[John 20.19|20:19–25]][[John 20.20|]][[John 20.21|]][[John 20.22|]][[John 20.23|]][[John 20.24|]][[John 20.25|]]).
