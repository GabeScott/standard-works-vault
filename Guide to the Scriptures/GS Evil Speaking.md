# Evil Speaking

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/evil-speaking?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Contention|Contention]]; [[GS Gossip|Gossip]]; [[GS Lying|Lying]]; [[GS Rumor|Rumor]]

Saying things that are wrong, hurtful, and wicked. Often in scripture such speaking is directed at a person with the specific intent to cause pain.

- Keep thy tongue from evil, [[Psalms 34.13|Ps. 34:13]] ([[1 Peter 3.10|1 Pet. 3:10]]).
- An ungodly man diggeth up evil, [[Proverbs 16.27|Prov. 16:27]].

- Blessed are ye, when men shall say all manner of evil against you falsely, [[Matthew 5.11|Matt. 5:11]] ([[3 Nephi 12.11|3 Ne. 12:11]]).
- Out of the heart proceed evil thoughts, [[Matthew 15.19|Matt. 15:19]] ([[Mark 7.21|Mark 7:21]]).
- Thou shalt not speak evil of the ruler, [[Acts 23.5|Acts 23:5]].
- Let all evil speaking be put away from you, [[Ephesians 4.31|Eph. 4:31]].
- Speak not evil one of another, [[James 4.11|James 4:11]].

- See that there is no backbiting, nor evil speaking, [[D&C 20.54|D&C 20:54]].
