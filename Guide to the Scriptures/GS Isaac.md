# Isaac

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/isaac?lang=eng)

An Old Testament patriarch. His birth to Abraham and Sarah in their old age was a miracle ([[Genesis 15.4|Gen. 15:4–6]][[Genesis 15.5|]][[Genesis 15.6|]]; [[Genesis 17.15|17:15–21]][[Genesis 17.16|]][[Genesis 17.17|]][[Genesis 17.18|]][[Genesis 17.19|]][[Genesis 17.20|]][[Genesis 17.21|]]; [[Genesis 21.1|21:1–8]][[Genesis 21.2|]][[Genesis 21.3|]][[Genesis 21.4|]][[Genesis 21.5|]][[Genesis 21.6|]][[Genesis 21.7|]][[Genesis 21.8|]]). Abraham’s willingness to offer Isaac was a similitude of God and His Only Begotten Son ([[Jacob 4.5|Jacob 4:5]]). Isaac was heir to the promises of the Abrahamic covenant ([[Genesis 21.9|Gen. 21:9–12]][[Genesis 21.10|]][[Genesis 21.11|]][[Genesis 21.12|]]; [[1 Nephi 17.40|1 Ne. 17:40]]; [[D&C 27.10|D&C 27:10]]).
