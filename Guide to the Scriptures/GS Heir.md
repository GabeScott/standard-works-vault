# Heir

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/heir?lang=eng)

A person entitled to inherit physical or spiritual gifts. In the scriptures, the righteous are promised that they will become heirs to all that God has.

- Abraham desired an heir, [[Genesis 15.2|Gen. 15:2–5]][[Genesis 15.3|]][[Genesis 15.4|]][[Genesis 15.5|]].

- Abraham became an heir of the world through the righteousness of faith, [[Romans 4.13|Rom. 4:13]].
- We are the children of God, and if children, then heirs, heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ, [[Romans 8.16|Rom. 8:16–17]][[Romans 8.17|]] ([[D&C 84.38|D&C 84:38]]).
- Thou art a son, an heir of God through Christ, [[Galatians 4.7|Gal. 4:7]].
- God hath appointed his Son an heir of all things, [[Hebrews 1.2|Heb. 1:2]].

- Those who look forward to a remission of their sins are heirs of the kingdom of God, [[Mosiah 15.11|Mosiah 15:11]].
- The people became children of Christ and heirs of the kingdom of God, [[4 Nephi 1.17|4 Ne. 1:17]].

- Those who died without a knowledge of the gospel may become heirs of the celestial kingdom, [[D&C 137.7|D&C 137:7–8]][[D&C 137.8|]].
- The dead who repent are heirs of salvation, [[D&C 138.58|D&C 138:58–59]][[D&C 138.59|]].

- Abraham became a rightful heir by his righteousness, [[Abraham 1.2|Abr. 1:2]].
