# Choose, Chose, Chosen (verb)

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/choose-chose-chosen-verb?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Agency|Agency]]; [[GS Call, Called of God, Calling|Call, Called of God, Calling]]; [[GS Free, Freedom|Free, Freedom]]

When the Lord selects, or chooses, an individual or group, He usually also calls them to serve.

- Choose you this day whom ye will serve, [[Joshua 24.15|Josh. 24:15]] ([[Alma 30.8|Alma 30:8]]; [[Moses 6.33|Moses 6:33]]).
- I have chosen thee in the furnace of affliction, [[Isaiah 48.10|Isa. 48:10]] ([[1 Nephi 20.10|1 Ne. 20:10]]).

- Many are called, but few are chosen, [[Matthew 22.14|Matt. 22:14]] ([[Matthew 20.16|Matt. 20:16]]; [[D&C 95.5|D&C 95:5]]; [[D&C 121.34|121:34, 40]][[D&C 121.40|]]).
- Ye have not chosen me, but I have chosen you, [[John 15.16|John 15:16]].
- God has chosen the foolish things of the world to confound the wise, [[1 Corinthians 1.27|1 Cor. 1:27]].
- He has chosen us before the foundation of the world, [[Ephesians 1.4|Eph. 1:4]].

- We are free to choose liberty and eternal life or captivity and death, [[2 Nephi 2.27|2 Ne. 2:27]].

- Noble and great ones were chosen in the beginning, [[D&C 138.55|D&C 138:55–56]][[D&C 138.56|]].

- Israel was chosen by God, [[Moses 1.26|Moses 1:26]].
- Abraham was chosen before he was born, [[Abraham 3.23|Abr. 3:23]].
