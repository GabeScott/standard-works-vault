# Steal, Stealing

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/steal-stealing?lang=eng)

To take something from another dishonestly or unlawfully. The Lord has always commanded His children to not steal ([[Exodus 20.15|Ex. 20:15]]; [[Matthew 19.18|Matt. 19:18]]; [[2 Nephi 26.32|2 Ne. 26:32]]; [[Mosiah 13.22|Mosiah 13:22]]; [[D&C 59.6|D&C 59:6]]).

- Lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where thieves do not break through nor steal, [[Matthew 6.19|Matt. 6:19–21]][[Matthew 6.20|]][[Matthew 6.21|]].

- The Nephites’ defeats came because of pride, riches, plundering, stealing, [[Helaman 4.12|Hel. 4:12]].

- He that stealeth and will not repent shall be cast out, [[D&C 42.20|D&C 42:20]].
- Those who steal shall be delivered up unto the law of the land, [[D&C 42.84|D&C 42:84–85]][[D&C 42.85|]].
