# Thankful, Thanks, Thanksgiving

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/thankful-thanks-thanksgiving?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Bless, Blessed, Blessing|Bless, Blessed, Blessing]]; [[GS Worship|Worship]]

Gratitude for blessings received from God. Expressing gratitude is pleasing to God, and true worship includes thanking Him. We should give thanks to the Lord for all things.

- It is a good thing to give thanks unto the Lord, [[Psalms 92.1|Ps. 92:1]].
- Come before his presence with thanksgiving, [[Psalms 95.1|Ps. 95:1–2]][[Psalms 95.2|]].
- Be thankful unto him, and bless his name, [[Psalms 100.1|Ps. 100]][[Psalms 100.2|]][[Psalms 100.3|]][[Psalms 100.4|]][[Psalms 100.5|]].

- Cease not to give thanks, [[Ephesians 1.15|Eph. 1:15–16]][[Ephesians 1.16|]].
- Be ye thankful, [[Colossians 3.15|Col. 3:15]].
- Blessing, and glory, and thanksgiving, and honor be unto our God, [[Revelation 7.12|Rev. 7:12]].

- O how you ought to thank your heavenly King, [[Mosiah 2.19|Mosiah 2:19–21]][[Mosiah 2.20|]][[Mosiah 2.21|]].
- Live in thanksgiving daily, [[Alma 34.38|Alma 34:38]].
- When thou risest in the morning let thy heart be full of thanks unto God, [[Alma 37.37|Alma 37:37]].

- Ye should do all things with prayer and thanksgiving, [[D&C 46.7|D&C 46:7]].
- Ye must give thanks unto God, [[D&C 46.32|D&C 46:32]].
- Do these things with thanksgiving, [[D&C 59.15|D&C 59:15–21]][[D&C 59.16|]][[D&C 59.17|]][[D&C 59.18|]][[D&C 59.19|]][[D&C 59.20|]][[D&C 59.21|]].
- Receive this blessing from the hand of the Lord, with a thankful heart, [[D&C 62.7|D&C 62:7]].
- He who receives all things with thankfulness shall be made glorious, [[D&C 78.19|D&C 78:19]].
- In everything give thanks, [[D&C 98.1|D&C 98:1]] ([[1 Thessalonians 5.18|1 Thes. 5:18]]).
- Praise the Lord with a prayer of praise and thanksgiving, [[D&C 136.28|D&C 136:28]].
