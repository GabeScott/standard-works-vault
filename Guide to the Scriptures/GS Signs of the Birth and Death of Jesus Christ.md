## Birth

- A virgin shall conceive and bear a son, [[Isaiah 7.14|Isa. 7:14]].
- Out of Bethlehem shall come forth a ruler in Israel, [[Micah 5.2|Micah 5:2]].

- Samuel the Lamanite prophesied a day, a night, and a day of light; a new star; and other signs, [[Helaman 14.2|Hel. 14:2–6]][[Helaman 14.3|]][[Helaman 14.4|]][[Helaman 14.5|]][[Helaman 14.6|]].
