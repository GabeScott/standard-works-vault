# Church of Jesus Christ

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/church-of-jesus-christ?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Church, Name of|Church, Name of]]; [[GS Church, Signs of the True|Church, Signs of the True]]; [[GS Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, The|Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, The]]; [[GS Kingdom of God or Kingdom of Heaven|Kingdom of God or Kingdom of Heaven]]; [[GS Restoration of the Gospel|Restoration of the Gospel]]; [[GS Saint|Saint]]

An organized body of believers who have taken upon themselves the name of Jesus Christ by baptism and confirmation. To be the true Church it must be the Lord’s Church; must have His authority, teachings, laws, ordinances, and name; and must be governed by Him through representatives whom He has appointed.

- The Lord added to the Church daily, [[Acts 2.47|Acts 2:47]].
- We, being many, are one body in Christ, [[Romans 12.5|Rom. 12:5]].
- By one Spirit we are all baptized into one body, [[1 Corinthians 12.13|1 Cor. 12:13]].
- The Church is built upon the foundation of Apostles and prophets, [[Ephesians 2.19|Eph. 2:19–20]][[Ephesians 2.20|]].
- Apostles and prophets are essential to the Church, [[Ephesians 4.11|Eph. 4:11–16]][[Ephesians 4.12|]][[Ephesians 4.13|]][[Ephesians 4.14|]][[Ephesians 4.15|]][[Ephesians 4.16|]].
- Christ is the head of the Church, [[Ephesians 5.23|Eph. 5:23]].

- Notwithstanding there were many churches, they were all one Church, [[Mosiah 25.19|Mosiah 25:19–22]][[Mosiah 25.20|]][[Mosiah 25.21|]][[Mosiah 25.22|]].
- The Church was cleansed and set in order, [[Alma 6.1|Alma 6:1–6]][[Alma 6.2|]][[Alma 6.3|]][[Alma 6.4|]][[Alma 6.5|]][[Alma 6.6|]].
- Christ’s Church must be called in his name, [[3 Nephi 27.8|3 Ne. 27:8]].
- The Church did meet together oft to fast and to pray and to speak, [[Moroni 6.5|Moro. 6:5]].

- This is the only true and living Church, [[D&C 1.30|D&C 1:30]].
- The Church of Christ rose in these last days, [[D&C 20.1|D&C 20:1]].
- The Lord calls his servants to build up his Church, [[D&C 39.13|D&C 39:13]].
- For thus shall my Church be called in the last days, [[D&C 115.4|D&C 115:4]].
