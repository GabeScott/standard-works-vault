# Hyde, Orson

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/hyde-orson?lang=eng)

A member of the first Quorum of the Twelve Apostles called in this dispensation ([[D&C 68.1|D&C 68:1–3]][[D&C 68.2|]][[D&C 68.3|]]; [[D&C 75.13|75:13]]; [[D&C 102.3|102:3]]; [[D&C 124.128|124:128–129]][[D&C 124.129|]]). He performed many missions for the Church, including dedicating the Holy Land in 1841 for the return of the Jewish people.
