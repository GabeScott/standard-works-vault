# Carnal

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/carnal?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Fall of Adam and Eve|Fall of Adam and Eve]]; [[GS Natural Man|Natural Man]]; [[GS Sensual, Sensuality|Sensual, Sensuality]]

Something that is not spiritual; specifically the word may be used to mean either mortal and temporal ([[D&C 67.10|D&C 67:10]]) or worldly, fleshly, and sensual ([[Mosiah 16.10|Mosiah 16:10–12]][[Mosiah 16.11|]][[Mosiah 16.12|]]).

- To be carnally-minded is death, [[2 Nephi 9.39|2 Ne. 9:39]].
- The devil lulls men away into carnal security, [[2 Nephi 28.21|2 Ne. 28:21]].
- They had viewed themselves in their own carnal state, [[Mosiah 4.2|Mosiah 4:2]].
- He that persists in his own carnal nature remains in his fallen state, [[Mosiah 16.5|Mosiah 16:5]].
- All must be born of God, changed from their carnal and fallen state, [[Mosiah 27.25|Mosiah 27:25]].
- Mankind had become carnal, sensual, and devilish, [[Alma 42.10|Alma 42:10]].

- Those who follow after their own will and carnal desires must fall, [[D&C 3.4|D&C 3:4]].
- Man cannot see God with a carnal mind, [[D&C 67.10|D&C 67:10–12]][[D&C 67.11|]][[D&C 67.12|]].

- Men began to be carnal, sensual, and devilish, [[Moses 5.13|Moses 5:13]]; [[Moses 6.49|6:49]].
