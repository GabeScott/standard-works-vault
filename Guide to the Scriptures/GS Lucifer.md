# Lucifer

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/lucifer?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Antichrist|Antichrist]]; [[GS Destroyer|Destroyer]]; [[GS Devil|Devil]]; [[GS Hell|Hell]]; [[GS Sons of Perdition|Sons of Perdition]]

The name means the “Shining One” or “Lightbearer.” He is also known as the Son of the Morning. Lucifer was a spirit son of Heavenly Father and led the rebellion in the premortal life. The name Lucifer appears only once in the Bible ([[Isaiah 14.12|Isa. 14:12]]). Latter-day revelation gives more detail on Lucifer’s fall ([[D&C 76.25|D&C 76:25–29]][[D&C 76.26|]][[D&C 76.27|]][[D&C 76.28|]][[D&C 76.29|]]).

- Lucifer fell in the premortal existence, [[Isaiah 14.12|Isa. 14:12]] ([[Luke 10.18|Luke 10:18]]; [[2 Nephi 24.12|2 Ne. 24:12]]).

- After his fall he became Satan and the devil, [[D&C 76.25|D&C 76:25–29]][[D&C 76.26|]][[D&C 76.27|]][[D&C 76.28|]][[D&C 76.29|]] ([[Moses 4.1|Moses 4:1–4]][[Moses 4.2|]][[Moses 4.3|]][[Moses 4.4|]]).
