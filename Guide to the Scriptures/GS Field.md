# Field

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/field?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Vineyard of the Lord|Vineyard of the Lord]]; [[GS World|World]]

In scripture, an open area of land used for cultivation or pasture. It often symbolizes the world and its peoples.

- The field is the world, [[Matthew 13.38|Matt. 13:38]].
- The kingdom of heaven is like unto treasure hid in a field, [[Matthew 13.44|Matt. 13:44]].

- I beheld a large and spacious field, [[1 Nephi 8.9|1 Ne. 8:9, 20]][[1 Nephi 8.20|]].
- The field was ripe, [[Alma 26.5|Alma 26:5]].

- The field is white already to harvest, [[D&C 4.4|D&C 4:4]] ([[D&C 6.3|D&C 6:3]]; [[D&C 11.3|11:3]]; [[D&C 12.3|12:3]]; [[D&C 14.3|14:3]]; [[D&C 31.4|31:4]]; [[D&C 33.3|33:3, 7]][[D&C 33.7|]]).
- The field was the world, [[D&C 86.1|D&C 86:1–2]][[D&C 86.2|]].
- I will liken these kingdoms unto a man having a field, [[D&C 88.51|D&C 88:51]].
