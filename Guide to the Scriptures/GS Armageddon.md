# Armageddon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/armageddon?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Gog|Gog]]; [[GS Magog|Magog]]; [[GS Second Coming of Jesus Christ|Second Coming of Jesus Christ]]

The name *Armageddon* is derived from the Hebrew *Har Megiddon,* meaning the “mountain of Megiddo.” The valley of Megiddo is in the western portion of the plain of Esdraelon, fifty miles (eighty kilometers) north of Jerusalem, and is the site of several crucial battles in Old Testament times. A great and final conflict that will take place near the time of the Second Coming of the Lord is called the battle of Armageddon because it will begin in the same locale.
