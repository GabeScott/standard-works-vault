# Olive Tree

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/olive-tree?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Israel|Israel]]; [[GS Oil|Oil]]

A tree common to Israel and an important agricultural resource in Bible lands. It is grown for its wood, fruit, and oil. The olive tree is used most frequently in the scriptures to symbolize the house of Israel.

- The house of Israel is compared to an olive tree whose branches are broken and scattered, [[1 Nephi 10.12|1 Ne. 10:12]] ([[1 Nephi 15.12|1 Ne. 15:12]]).
