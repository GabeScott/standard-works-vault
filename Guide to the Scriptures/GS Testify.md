# Testify

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/testify?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Testimony|Testimony]]

To bear witness by the power of the Holy Ghost; to make a solemn declaration of truth based on personal knowledge or belief.

- The Comforter shall testify of me, [[John 15.26|John 15:26]].
- He commanded us to preach and to testify, [[Acts 10.42|Acts 10:42]].

- Three witnesses shall testify to the truth, [[2 Nephi 27.12|2 Ne. 27:12]].
- The power of the Holy Ghost carrieth it unto the hearts of the children of men, [[2 Nephi 33.1|2 Ne. 33:1]].
- The scriptures testify of Christ, [[Jacob 7.10|Jacob 7:10–11]][[Jacob 7.11|]] ([[John 5.39|John 5:39]]).
- I testify unto you that I do know that these things whereof I have spoken are true, [[Alma 5.45|Alma 5:45]] ([[Alma 34.8|Alma 34:8]]).

- Ye shall testify of them by the power of God, [[D&C 17.3|D&C 17:3–5]][[D&C 17.4|]][[D&C 17.5|]].
- That which the Spirit testifies unto you, ye should do, [[D&C 46.7|D&C 46:7]].
- I sent you out to testify and warn, [[D&C 88.81|D&C 88:81]].
