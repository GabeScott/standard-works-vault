# Sick, Sickness

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/sick-sickness?lang=eng)

To have an illness or disease. In the scriptures, physical illness sometimes serves as a symbol for a lack of spiritual well-being ([[Isaiah 1.4|Isa. 1:4–7]][[Isaiah 1.5|]][[Isaiah 1.6|]][[Isaiah 1.7|]]; [[Isaiah 33.24|33:24]]).

- I have heard thy prayer, I have seen thy tears: behold, I will heal thee, [[2 Kings 20.1|2 Kgs. 20:1–5]][[2 Kings 20.2|]][[2 Kings 20.3|]][[2 Kings 20.4|]][[2 Kings 20.5|]] ([[2 Chronicles 32.24|2 Chr. 32:24]]; [[Isaiah 38.1|Isa. 38:1–5]][[Isaiah 38.2|]][[Isaiah 38.3|]][[Isaiah 38.4|]][[Isaiah 38.5|]]).

- Jesus went about healing all manner of sickness and all manner of disease, [[Matthew 4.23|Matt. 4:23–24]][[Matthew 4.24|]] ([[1 Nephi 11.31|1 Ne. 11:31]]; [[Mosiah 3.5|Mosiah 3:5–6]][[Mosiah 3.6|]]).
- They that be whole need not a physician, but they that are sick, [[Matthew 9.10|Matt. 9:10–13]][[Matthew 9.11|]][[Matthew 9.12|]][[Matthew 9.13|]] ([[Mark 2.14|Mark 2:14–17]][[Mark 2.15|]][[Mark 2.16|]][[Mark 2.17|]]; [[Luke 5.27|Luke 5:27–32]][[Luke 5.28|]][[Luke 5.29|]][[Luke 5.30|]][[Luke 5.31|]][[Luke 5.32|]]).
- Is any sick among you? let him call for the elders, [[James 5.14|James 5:14–15]][[James 5.15|]].

- Christ will take upon him the pains and sicknesses of his people, [[Alma 7.10|Alma 7:10–12]][[Alma 7.11|]][[Alma 7.12|]].
- Jesus healed all the sick among the Nephites, [[3 Nephi 26.15|3 Ne. 26:15]].

- Nourish the sick with tenderness, herbs, and mild food, [[D&C 42.43|D&C 42:43]] ([[Alma 46.40|Alma 46:40]]).
- Remember in all things the sick and the afflicted, [[D&C 52.40|D&C 52:40]].
- Lay your hands upon the sick, and they shall recover, [[D&C 66.9|D&C 66:9]].
