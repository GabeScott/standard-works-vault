# Heal, Healings

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/heal-healings?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Administration to the Sick|Administration to the Sick]]; [[GS Anoint|Anoint]]

To make well or healthy again, both physically and spiritually. The scriptures contain many examples of miraculous healings by the Lord and His servants.

- I am the Lord that heals thee, [[Exodus 15.26|Ex. 15:26]].
- Naaman dipped himself in the Jordan River seven times and was healed, [[2 Kings 5.1|2 Kgs. 5:1–14]][[2 Kings 5.2|]][[2 Kings 5.3|]][[2 Kings 5.4|]][[2 Kings 5.5|]][[2 Kings 5.6|]][[2 Kings 5.7|]][[2 Kings 5.8|]][[2 Kings 5.9|]][[2 Kings 5.10|]][[2 Kings 5.11|]][[2 Kings 5.12|]][[2 Kings 5.13|]][[2 Kings 5.14|]].
- With his stripes we are healed, [[Isaiah 53.5|Isa. 53:5]] ([[Mosiah 14.5|Mosiah 14:5]]).
- The Sun of righteousness shall arise with healing in his wings, [[Malachi 4.2|Mal. 4:2]].

- Jesus healed all manner of sickness, [[Matthew 4.23|Matt. 4:23]] ([[Matthew 9.35|Matt. 9:35]]).
- He gave them power to heal all manner of sickness, [[Matthew 10.1|Matt. 10:1]].
- He hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, [[Luke 4.18|Luke 4:18]].

- They were healed by the power of the Lamb of God, [[1 Nephi 11.31|1 Ne. 11:31]].
- If thou believest in the redemption of Christ, thou canst be healed, [[Alma 15.8|Alma 15:8]].
- He did heal them every one, [[3 Nephi 17.9|3 Ne. 17:9]].

- He that hath faith in me to be healed shall be healed, [[D&C 42.48|D&C 42:48]].
- In my name they shall heal the sick, [[D&C 84.68|D&C 84:68]].

- We believe in the gift of healings, [[Articles of Faith 1.7|A of F 1:7]].
