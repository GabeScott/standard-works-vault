# Fornication

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/fornication?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adultery|Adultery]]; [[GS Chastity|Chastity]]; [[GS Sensual, Sensuality|Sensual, Sensuality]]

Unlawful sexual intercourse between two people not married to each other. In the scriptures it is also occasionally used as a symbol for apostasy.

- Abstain from fornication, [[Acts 15.20|Acts 15:20]].
- The body is not for fornication, but for the Lord, [[1 Corinthians 6.13|1 Cor. 6:13–18]][[1 Corinthians 6.14|]][[1 Corinthians 6.15|]][[1 Corinthians 6.16|]][[1 Corinthians 6.17|]][[1 Corinthians 6.18|]].
- To avoid fornication, let every man have his own wife, [[1 Corinthians 7.2|1 Cor. 7:2–3]][[1 Corinthians 7.3|]].
- This is the will of God, that ye should abstain from fornication, [[1 Thessalonians 4.3|1 Thes. 4:3]].

- Jacob warned the people of Nephi against fornication, [[Jacob 3.12|Jacob 3:12]].
- Ye are ripening, because of your murders and fornication, for destruction, [[Helaman 8.26|Hel. 8:26]].

- Fornicators must repent in order to join the Church, [[D&C 42.74|D&C 42:74–78]][[D&C 42.75|]][[D&C 42.76|]][[D&C 42.77|]][[D&C 42.78|]].
