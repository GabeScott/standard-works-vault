# Anger

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/anger?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Hate, Hatred|Hate, Hatred]]; [[GS Love|Love]]

Anger is the display of temper. The Lord cautioned His Saints to control their anger ([[Matthew 5.22|Matt. 5:22]]). Neither parent nor child should abuse others in the family. In the scriptures, anger often has the figurative image of fire ([[2 Nephi 15.25|2 Ne. 15:25]]; [[D&C 1.13|D&C 1:13]]).

- And Cain was very wroth, and his countenance fell, [[Genesis 4.5|Gen. 4:5]].
- The Lord is slow to anger, and of great mercy, [[Psalms 145.8|Ps. 145:8]].
- A soft answer turneth away wrath, [[Proverbs 15.1|Prov. 15:1]].
- A wrathful man stirreth up strife, but he that is slow to anger appeaseth strife, [[Proverbs 15.18|Prov. 15:18]] ([[Proverbs 14.29|Prov. 14:29]]).
- For my name’s sake will I defer mine anger, [[Isaiah 48.9|Isa. 48:9]].
- I have spread out my hands unto a people that provoketh me to anger continually, [[Isaiah 65.2|Isa. 65:2–3]][[Isaiah 65.3|]].

- Whosoever shall smite thee on thy right cheek, turn to him the other also, [[Matthew 5.39|Matt. 5:39]].
- Ye fathers, provoke not your children to wrath, [[Ephesians 6.4|Eph. 6:4]].

- Because I have told you the truth, ye are angry with me, [[Mosiah 13.4|Mosiah 13:4]].
- I will visit this people in mine anger, [[Alma 8.29|Alma 8:29]].

- Against none is his wrath kindled save those who confess not his hand in all things, [[D&C 59.21|D&C 59:21]].
- I, the Lord, am angry with the wicked, [[D&C 63.32|D&C 63:32]].
