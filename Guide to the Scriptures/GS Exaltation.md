# Exaltation

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/exaltation?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Celestial Glory|Celestial Glory]]; [[GS Crown|Crown]]; [[GS Eternal Life|Eternal Life]]; [[GS Man, Men|Man, Men—Man, potential to become like Heavenly Father]]

The highest state of happiness and glory in the celestial kingdom.

- In thy presence is fulness of joy, [[Psalms 16.11|Ps. 16:11]].

- They are gods, even the sons of God—wherefore, all things are theirs, [[D&C 76.58|D&C 76:58–59]][[D&C 76.59|]].
- The Saints shall receive their inheritance and be made equal with him, [[D&C 88.107|D&C 88:107]].
- These angels did not abide my law; therefore, they remain separately and singly, without exaltation, [[D&C 132.17|D&C 132:17]].
- Men and women must marry according to God’s law in order to gain exaltation, [[D&C 132.19|D&C 132:19–20]][[D&C 132.20|]].
- Strait is the gate and narrow the way that leadeth unto the exaltation, [[D&C 132.22|D&C 132:22–23]][[D&C 132.23|]].
- Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob have entered into their exaltation, [[D&C 132.29|D&C 132:29, 37]][[D&C 132.37|]].
- I seal upon you your exaltation, [[D&C 132.49|D&C 132:49]].
