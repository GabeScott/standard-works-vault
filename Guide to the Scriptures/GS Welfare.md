# Welfare

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/welfare?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Alms, Almsgiving|Alms, Almsgiving]]; [[GS Fast, Fasting|Fast, Fasting]]; [[GS Offering|Offering]]; [[GS Poor|Poor]]; [[GS Service|Service]]

The process of and means for taking care of people’s spiritual and temporal needs.

- Open your hand wide to thy brother, to thy poor, and to thy needy in thy land, [[Deuteronomy 15.11|Deut. 15:11]].
- He that giveth to the poor shall not lack, [[Proverbs 28.27|Prov. 28:27]].
- Is this not the fast that I have chosen? to give thy bread to the hungry, to bring the poor into thy house, [[Isaiah 58.6|Isa. 58:6–7]][[Isaiah 58.7|]].

- I was hungry and ye gave me meat; I was a stranger and ye took me in. When you do it to one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it to me, [[Matthew 25.35|Matt. 25:35–40]][[Matthew 25.36|]][[Matthew 25.37|]][[Matthew 25.38|]][[Matthew 25.39|]][[Matthew 25.40|]].

- Administer your substance to him that stands in need, [[Mosiah 4.16|Mosiah 4:16–26]][[Mosiah 4.17|]][[Mosiah 4.18|]][[Mosiah 4.19|]][[Mosiah 4.20|]][[Mosiah 4.21|]][[Mosiah 4.22|]][[Mosiah 4.23|]][[Mosiah 4.24|]][[Mosiah 4.25|]][[Mosiah 4.26|]].
- They gave to one another temporally and spiritually according to their needs and wants, [[Mosiah 18.29|Mosiah 18:29]].
- They were commanded to join in fasting and prayer for the welfare of those who did not know God, [[Alma 6.6|Alma 6:6]].
- Pray for your welfare and for the welfare of those around you, [[Alma 34.27|Alma 34:27–28]][[Alma 34.28|]].
- They had all things common among them, [[4 Nephi 1.3|4 Ne. 1:3]].

- Remember the poor, [[D&C 42.30|D&C 42:30–31]][[D&C 42.31|]].
- Visit the poor and needy, [[D&C 44.6|D&C 44:6]].
- Remember in all things the poor and needy, [[D&C 52.40|D&C 52:40]].
- Wo to you rich who will not give your substance to the poor, and wo to you poor who are not satisfied, who are greedy and will not labor, [[D&C 56.16|D&C 56:16–17]][[D&C 56.17|]].

- In Zion there were no poor among them, [[Moses 7.18|Moses 7:18]].
