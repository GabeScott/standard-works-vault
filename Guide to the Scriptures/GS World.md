# World

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/world?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Babel, Babylon|Babel, Babylon]]; [[GS Earth|Earth]]; [[GS Mortal, Mortality|Mortal, Mortality]]

The earth; a testing place for mortal men. Figuratively, those people who do not obey God’s commandments.

## Mortal existence

- In the world ye shall have tribulation, [[John 16.33|John 16:33]].

- Fear not unto death; for in this world your joy is not full, [[D&C 101.36|D&C 101:36]].

## People who do not obey the commandments

- I will punish the world for their evil, [[Isaiah 13.11|Isa. 13:11]] ([[2 Nephi 23.11|2 Ne. 23:11]]).

- If the world hate you, ye know that it hated me before, [[John 15.18|John 15:18–19]][[John 15.19|]].

- The great and spacious building was the pride of the world, [[1 Nephi 11.36|1 Ne. 11:36]].

- The world is ripening in iniquity, [[D&C 18.6|D&C 18:6]].
- Keep thyself unspotted from the world, [[D&C 59.9|D&C 59:9]].
- He that is faithful and endureth shall overcome the world, [[D&C 63.47|D&C 63:47]].
- Ye shall not live after the manner of the world, [[D&C 95.13|D&C 95:13]].

## End of the world

- I create a new earth: and the former shall not be remembered, [[Isaiah 65.17|Isa. 65:17]] ([[Revelation 21.1|Rev. 21:1]]; [[Articles of Faith 1.10|A of F 1:10]]).

- In the end of this world, the tares will be gathered and burned in the fire, [[Matthew 13.40|Matt. 13:40, 49]][[Matthew 13.49|]] ([[Malachi 4.1|Mal. 4:1]]; [[Jacob 6.3|Jacob 6:3]]).

- My vineyard will I cause to be burned with fire, [[Jacob 5.77|Jacob 5:77]] ([[D&C 64.23|D&C 64:23–24]][[D&C 64.24|]]).

- The Lord will destroy Satan and his works at the end of the world, [[D&C 19.3|D&C 19:3]].
- The earth shall die, but it shall be quickened again, [[D&C 88.25|D&C 88:25–26]][[D&C 88.26|]].

- The Lord showed Enoch the end of the world, [[Moses 7.67|Moses 7:67]].
