# Dove, Sign of the

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/dove-sign-of-the?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Holy Ghost|Holy Ghost]]

A prearranged sign by which John the Baptist would recognize the Messiah ([[John 1.32|John 1:32–34]][[John 1.33|]][[John 1.34|]]). Joseph Smith taught that the sign of the dove was instituted before the creation of the world as a witness for the Holy Ghost; therefore, the devil cannot come in the sign of a dove.

- The Spirit of God descended like a dove, [[Matthew 3.16|Matt. 3:16]].

- After Jesus was baptized, the Holy Ghost came down in the form of a dove, [[1 Nephi 11.27|1 Ne. 11:27]].

- I, John, bear record, and lo, the heavens were opened, and the Holy Ghost descended upon him in the form of a dove, [[D&C 93.15|D&C 93:15]].
