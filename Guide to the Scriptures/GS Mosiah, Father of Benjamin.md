# Mosiah, Father of Benjamin

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/mosiah-father-of-benjamin?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Benjamin, Father of Mosiah|Benjamin, Father of Mosiah]]; [[GS Zarahemla|Zarahemla]]

In the Book of Mormon, a Nephite prophet who was made king over the people in Zarahemla.

- Mosiah was warned to flee out of the land of Nephi, [[Omni 1.12|Omni 1:12]].
- He discovered the people of Zarahemla, [[Omni 1.14|Omni 1:14–15]][[Omni 1.15|]].
- He had the people of Zarahemla taught in his language, [[Omni 1.18|Omni 1:18]].
- He was appointed king of the united peoples, [[Omni 1.19|Omni 1:19]].
- His son, Benjamin, reigned after his death, [[Omni 1.23|Omni 1:23]].
