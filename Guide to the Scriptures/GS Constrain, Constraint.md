# Constrain, Constraint

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/constrain-constraint?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Holy Ghost|Holy Ghost]]

To be urged strongly to do or not to do something, especially by the influence and power of the Holy Ghost.

- The spirit within me constrains me, [[Job 32.18|Job 32:18]].

- The love of Christ constraineth us, [[2 Corinthians 5.14|2 Cor. 5:14]].

- I was constrained by the Spirit that I should kill Laban, [[1 Nephi 4.10|1 Ne. 4:10]].
- The Spirit constrains me, [[Alma 14.11|Alma 14:11]].
- I am constrained according to the covenant, [[Alma 60.34|Alma 60:34]].
- Ammaron, being constrained by the Holy Ghost, did hide up the records, [[4 Nephi 1.48|4 Ne. 1:48]].

- That which comes from above must be spoken by constraint of the Spirit, [[D&C 63.64|D&C 63:64]].
