# Lust

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/lust?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Sensual, Sensuality|Sensual, Sensuality]]

To have an inappropriately strong desire for something.

- Lust not after her beauty in thine heart, [[Proverbs 6.25|Prov. 6:25]].

- Whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery, [[Matthew 5.28|Matt. 5:28]] ([[3 Nephi 12.28|3 Ne. 12:28]]).
- Men burned in their lust one toward another, [[Romans 1.27|Rom. 1:27]].
- After their own lusts they shall heap to themselves teachers, [[2 Timothy 4.3|2 Tim. 4:3–4]][[2 Timothy 4.4|]].

- Laban saw our property and did lust after it, [[1 Nephi 3.25|1 Ne. 3:25]].
- Go no more after the lusts of your eyes, [[Alma 39.3|Alma 39:3–4, 9]][[Alma 39.4|]][[Alma 39.9|]].

- He that looketh upon a woman to lust after her shall deny the faith, [[D&C 42.23|D&C 42:23]].
- Cease from all your lustful desires, [[D&C 88.121|D&C 88:121]].
