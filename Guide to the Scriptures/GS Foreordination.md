# Foreordination

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/foreordination?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Premortal Life|Premortal Life]]

God’s premortal ordination of His valiant spirit children to fulfill certain missions during their mortal lives.

- God set the bounds of the people, [[Deuteronomy 32.8|Deut. 32:8]].
- Before I formed thee in the belly I ordained thee a prophet, [[Jeremiah 1.5|Jer. 1:5]].

- God determined the times before appointed, [[Acts 17.26|Acts 17:26]].
- For whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate, [[Romans 8.28|Rom. 8:28–30]][[Romans 8.29|]][[Romans 8.30|]].
- He has chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, [[Ephesians 1.3|Eph. 1:3–4]][[Ephesians 1.4|]].
- Jesus Christ was foreordained to be the Redeemer, [[1 Peter 1.19|1 Pet. 1:19–20]][[1 Peter 1.20|]] ([[Revelation 13.8|Rev. 13:8]]).

- They were called and prepared from the foundation of the world, [[Alma 13.1|Alma 13:1–9]][[Alma 13.2|]][[Alma 13.3|]][[Alma 13.4|]][[Alma 13.5|]][[Alma 13.6|]][[Alma 13.7|]][[Alma 13.8|]][[Alma 13.9|]].

- I observed the noble and great ones who were chosen in the beginning, [[D&C 138.55|D&C 138:55–56]][[D&C 138.56|]].

- My Beloved Son was chosen from the beginning, [[Moses 4.2|Moses 4:2]].
- Abraham was chosen before he was born, [[Abraham 3.23|Abr. 3:23]].
