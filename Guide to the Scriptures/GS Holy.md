# Holy

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/holy?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Holiness|Holiness]]; [[GS Pure, Purity|Pure, Purity]]; [[GS Sanctification|Sanctification]]

Sacred, having a godly character, or spiritually and morally pure. The opposite of holy is common or profane.

- Ye shall be unto me a kingdom of priests, and an holy nation, [[Exodus 19.5|Ex. 19:5–6]][[Exodus 19.6|]] ([[1 Peter 2.9|1 Pet. 2:9]]).
- The Lord commanded Israel: Be holy, for I am holy, [[Leviticus 11.44|Lev. 11:44–45]][[Leviticus 11.45|]].
- Those with clean hands and a pure heart shall stand in his holy place, [[Psalms 24.3|Ps. 24:3–4]][[Psalms 24.4|]].
- Teach my people the difference between the holy and profane, [[Ezekiel 44.23|Ezek. 44:23]].

- God hath called us with a holy calling, [[2 Timothy 1.8|2 Tim. 1:8–9]][[2 Timothy 1.9|]].
- From a child thou hast known the holy scriptures, [[2 Timothy 3.15|2 Tim. 3:15]].
- Holy men of God spake as moved upon by the Holy Ghost, [[2 Peter 1.21|2 Pet. 1:21]].

- All men are to be judged according to the truth and holiness which is in God, [[2 Nephi 2.10|2 Ne. 2:10]].
- The natural man becomes a Saint through the atonement of Christ, [[Mosiah 3.19|Mosiah 3:19]].
- Walk after the holy order of God, [[Alma 7.22|Alma 7:22]] ([[Alma 13.11|Alma 13:11–12]][[Alma 13.12|]]).
- Three disciples were sanctified in the flesh and were made holy, [[3 Nephi 28.1|3 Ne. 28:1–9, 36–39]][[3 Nephi 28.2|]][[3 Nephi 28.3|]][[3 Nephi 28.4|]][[3 Nephi 28.5|]][[3 Nephi 28.6|]][[3 Nephi 28.7|]][[3 Nephi 28.8|]][[3 Nephi 28.9|]][[3 Nephi 28.36|]][[3 Nephi 28.37|]][[3 Nephi 28.38|]][[3 Nephi 28.39|]].

- Trifle not with sacred things, [[D&C 6.12|D&C 6:12]].
- You cannot write that which is sacred save it be given you from me, [[D&C 9.9|D&C 9:9]].
- Ye shall bind yourselves to act in all holiness, [[D&C 43.9|D&C 43:9]].
- My disciples shall stand in holy places, [[D&C 45.32|D&C 45:32]].
- That which cometh from above is sacred, [[D&C 63.64|D&C 63:64]].
- Little children are holy, [[D&C 74.7|D&C 74:7]].
- Consecrate that spot and it shall be made holy, [[D&C 124.44|D&C 124:44]].

- The Lord will gather his elect unto a holy city, [[Moses 7.62|Moses 7:62]].
