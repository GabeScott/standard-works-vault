# Worldliness

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/worldliness?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Money|Money]]; [[GS Pride|Pride]]; [[GS Riches|Riches]]; [[GS Vain, Vanity|Vain, Vanity]]

Unrighteous desires and efforts for temporal riches and material goods while forsaking spiritual things.

- For what is a man profited if he shall gain the whole world and lose his own soul, [[Matthew 16.26|Matt. 16:26]].

- They set their hearts upon the vain things of the world, [[Alma 4.8|Alma 4:8]] ([[Alma 31.27|Alma 31:27]]).

- Lay aside the things of this world, [[D&C 25.10|D&C 25:10]].
- Men’s hearts are set so much upon the things of this world, [[D&C 121.35|D&C 121:35]].
