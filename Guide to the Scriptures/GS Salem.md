# Salem

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/salem?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jerusalem|Jerusalem]]; [[GS Melchizedek|Melchizedek]]

The city in the Old Testament where Melchizedek ruled. It may have been located at the site of present-day Jerusalem. The name *Salem* is very similar to a Hebrew word meaning “peace.”

- Melchizedek king of Salem brought forth bread and wine, [[Genesis 14.18|Gen. 14:18]].

- Melchisedec, king of Salem, was a priest of the most high God, [[Hebrews 7.1|Heb. 7:1–2]][[Hebrews 7.2|]].

- Melchizedek was a king over the land of Salem, [[Alma 13.17|Alma 13:17–18]][[Alma 13.18|]].
