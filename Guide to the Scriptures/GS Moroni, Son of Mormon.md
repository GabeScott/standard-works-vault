# Moroni, Son of Mormon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/moroni-son-of-mormon?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Book of Mormon|Book of Mormon]]; [[GS Mormon, Nephite Prophet|Mormon, Nephite Prophet]]

The last Nephite prophet in the Book of Mormon (about  421). Just before Mormon’s death, he delivered a historical record called the plates of Mormon to his son Moroni ([[Words of Mormon 1.1|W of M 1:1]]). Moroni finished compiling the plates of Mormon. He added [[Mormon 8.1|chapters 8 and 9]][[Mormon 8.2|]][[Mormon 8.3|]][[Mormon 8.4|]][[Mormon 8.5|]][[Mormon 8.6|]][[Mormon 8.7|]][[Mormon 8.8|]][[Mormon 8.9|]][[Mormon 8.10|]][[Mormon 8.11|]][[Mormon 8.12|]][[Mormon 8.13|]][[Mormon 8.14|]][[Mormon 8.15|]][[Mormon 8.16|]][[Mormon 8.17|]][[Mormon 8.18|]][[Mormon 8.19|]][[Mormon 8.20|]][[Mormon 8.21|]][[Mormon 8.22|]][[Mormon 8.23|]][[Mormon 8.24|]][[Mormon 8.25|]][[Mormon 8.26|]][[Mormon 8.27|]][[Mormon 8.28|]][[Mormon 8.29|]][[Mormon 8.30|]][[Mormon 8.31|]][[Mormon 8.32|]][[Mormon 8.33|]][[Mormon 8.34|]][[Mormon 8.35|]][[Mormon 8.36|]][[Mormon 8.37|]][[Mormon 8.38|]][[Mormon 8.39|]][[Mormon 8.40|]][[Mormon 8.41|]] to the book of Mormon ([[Mormon 8.1|Morm. 8:1]]). He abridged and included the book of Ether ([[Ether 1.1|Ether 1:1–2]][[Ether 1.2|]]) and added his own book called the book of Moroni ([[Moroni 1.1|Moro. 1]][[Moroni 1.2|]][[Moroni 1.3|]][[Moroni 1.4|]]). Moroni sealed up the plates and hid them in the hill Cumorah ([[Mormon 8.14|Morm. 8:14]]; [[Moroni 10.2|Moro. 10:2]]). In 1823 Moroni was sent as a resurrected being to reveal the Book of Mormon to Joseph Smith ([[D&C 27.5|D&C 27:5]]; [[Joseph Smith History 1.30|JS—H 1:30–42, 45]][[Joseph Smith History 1.31|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.32|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.33|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.34|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.35|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.36|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.37|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.38|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.39|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.40|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.41|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.42|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.45|]]). He instructed the young prophet each year from 1823 to 1827 ([[Joseph Smith History 1.54|JS—H 1:54]]) and finally delivered the plates to him in 1827 ([[Joseph Smith History 1.59|JS—H 1:59]]). After completing the translation Joseph Smith returned the plates to Moroni.
