# Gamaliel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/gamaliel?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Pharisees|Pharisees]]

A well-known Pharisee in the New Testament who knew and taught the Jewish law. The Apostle Paul was one of his students ([[Acts 22.3|Acts 22:3]]). He was very influential in the Sanhedrin ([[Acts 5.34|Acts 5:34–40]][[Acts 5.35|]][[Acts 5.36|]][[Acts 5.37|]][[Acts 5.38|]][[Acts 5.39|]][[Acts 5.40|]]).
