# Confess, Confession

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/confess-confession?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Forgive|Forgive]]; [[GS Repent, Repentance|Repent, Repentance]]

The scriptures use *confession* in at least two ways. In one sense, to confess is to state one’s faith in something, such as to confess that Jesus is the Christ ([[Matthew 10.32|Matt. 10:32]]; [[Romans 10.9|Rom. 10:9]]; [[1 John 4.1|1 Jn. 4:1–3]][[1 John 4.2|]][[1 John 4.3|]]; [[D&C 88.104|D&C 88:104]]).

In another sense, to confess is to admit one’s guilt, such as in a confession of sins. It is a duty of all persons to confess all their sins to the Lord and obtain His forgiveness ([[D&C 58.42|D&C 58:42–43]][[D&C 58.43|]]). When necessary, sins should be confessed to the person or persons sinned against. Serious sins should be confessed to a Church official (in most cases the bishop).

- He shall confess that he has sinned, [[Leviticus 5.5|Lev. 5:5]].
- They shall confess their iniquity, [[Leviticus 26.40|Lev. 26:40–42]][[Leviticus 26.41|]][[Leviticus 26.42|]].
- Give glory to the Lord God of Israel, and make confession unto him, [[Joshua 7.19|Josh. 7:19]].

- People were baptized of him in Jordan, confessing their sins, [[Matthew 3.5|Matt. 3:5–6]][[Matthew 3.6|]].

- The transgressor who confesses his sins shall be forgiven, [[Mosiah 26.29|Mosiah 26:29]].

- Confess your sins, lest you suffer punishments, [[D&C 19.20|D&C 19:20]].
- The repentant will confess and forsake their sins, [[D&C 58.43|D&C 58:43]].
- The Lord is merciful to those who confess sins with humble hearts, [[D&C 61.2|D&C 61:2]].
- The Lord forgives the sins of those who confess and ask forgiveness, [[D&C 64.7|D&C 64:7]].
