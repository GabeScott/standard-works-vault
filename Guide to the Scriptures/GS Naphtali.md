# Naphtali

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/naphtali?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Israel|Israel]]; [[GS Jacob, Son of Isaac|Jacob, Son of Isaac]]

The sixth of the twelve sons of Jacob and the second child of Bilhah, Rachel’s handmaiden ([[Genesis 30.7|Gen. 30:7–8]][[Genesis 30.8|]]). Naphtali had four sons ([[1 Chronicles 7.13|1 Chr. 7:13]]).

## The tribe of Naphtali

Jacob’s blessing on Naphtali is recorded in [[Genesis 49.21|Genesis 49:21]]. Moses’ blessing on the tribe is recorded in [[Deuteronomy 33.23|Deuteronomy 33:23]].
