# Fire

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/fire?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Baptism, Baptize|Baptism, Baptize]]; [[GS Earth|Earth—Cleansing of the earth]]; [[GS Hell|Hell]]; [[GS Holy Ghost|Holy Ghost]]

A symbol for cleansing, purifying, or sanctifying. Fire can also serve as a symbol of God’s presence.

- Thy God is a consuming fire, [[Deuteronomy 4.24|Deut. 4:24]].
- The Lord makes his ministers a flaming fire, [[Psalms 104.4|Ps. 104:4]].
- The Lord of hosts will visit them with the flame of devouring fire, [[Isaiah 29.6|Isa. 29:6]] ([[2 Nephi 27.2|2 Ne. 27:2]]).
- The Lord will come with fire, [[Isaiah 66.15|Isa. 66:15]].
- He is like a refiner’s fire, [[Malachi 3.2|Mal. 3:2]] ([[3 Nephi 24.2|3 Ne. 24:2]]; [[D&C 128.24|D&C 128:24]]).

- He shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire, [[Matthew 3.11|Matt. 3:11]] ([[Luke 3.16|Luke 3:16]]).

- The righteous will be preserved by fire, [[1 Nephi 22.17|1 Ne. 22:17]].
- The wicked will be destroyed by fire, [[2 Nephi 30.10|2 Ne. 30:10]].
- Nephi explained how we receive the baptism of fire and the Holy Ghost, [[2 Nephi 31.13|2 Ne. 31:13–14]][[2 Nephi 31.14|]] ([[3 Nephi 9.20|3 Ne. 9:20]]; [[3 Nephi 12.1|12:1]]; [[3 Nephi 19.13|19:13]]; [[Ether 12.14|Ether 12:14]]; [[D&C 33.11|D&C 33:11]]).

- Thou shalt declare remission of sins by baptism, and by fire, [[D&C 19.31|D&C 19:31]].
- The great and abominable church shall be cast down by devouring fire, [[D&C 29.21|D&C 29:21]].
- The earth shall pass away so as by fire, [[D&C 43.32|D&C 43:32]].
- The presence of the Lord shall be as the melting fire, [[D&C 133.41|D&C 133:41]].

- Adam was baptized with fire and the Holy Ghost, [[Moses 6.66|Moses 6:66]].
