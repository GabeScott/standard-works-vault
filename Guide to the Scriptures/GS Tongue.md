# Tongue

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/tongue?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Tongues, Gift of|Tongues, Gift of]]

A symbol for speech. The Saints are to control their tongues, meaning that they are to control their speech. *Tongue* also refers to languages and peoples. Eventually, every knee shall bow and every tongue confess to God ([[Isaiah 45.23|Isa. 45:23]]; [[Romans 14.11|Rom. 14:11]]).

- Keep thy tongue from evil, [[Psalms 34.13|Ps. 34:13]] ([[1 Peter 3.10|1 Pet. 3:10]]).
- Whoso keepeth his mouth and his tongue keepeth his soul from troubles, [[Proverbs 21.23|Prov. 21:23]].

- If any man bridleth not his tongue, this man’s religion is vain, [[James 1.26|James 1:26]].
- If any man offend not in word, the same is a perfect man, [[James 3.1|James 3:1–13]][[James 3.2|]][[James 3.3|]][[James 3.4|]][[James 3.5|]][[James 3.6|]][[James 3.7|]][[James 3.8|]][[James 3.9|]][[James 3.10|]][[James 3.11|]][[James 3.12|]][[James 3.13|]].
- The gospel will be preached to every nation, kindred, tongue, and people, [[Revelation 14.6|Rev. 14:6–7]][[Revelation 14.7|]] ([[2 Nephi 26.13|2 Ne. 26:13]]; [[Mosiah 3.13|Mosiah 3:13, 20]][[Mosiah 3.20|]]; [[D&C 88.103|D&C 88:103]]; [[D&C 112.1|112:1]]).

- The Lord grants unto all nations, of their own nation and tongue, to teach his word, [[Alma 29.8|Alma 29:8]].
- These plates shall go forth unto every nation, kindred, tongue, and people, [[Alma 37.4|Alma 37:4]].

- Obtain my word, and then shall your tongue be loosed, [[D&C 11.21|D&C 11:21]].
- Every man shall hear the fulness of the gospel in his own tongue, [[D&C 90.11|D&C 90:11]].
