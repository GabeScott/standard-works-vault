# Bread of Life

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/bread-of-life?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Sacrament|Sacrament]]

Jesus Christ is the Bread of Life. The bread of the sacrament symbolically represents Christ’s body.

- I am the bread of life, [[John 6.33|John 6:33–58]][[John 6.34|]][[John 6.35|]][[John 6.36|]][[John 6.37|]][[John 6.38|]][[John 6.39|]][[John 6.40|]][[John 6.41|]][[John 6.42|]][[John 6.43|]][[John 6.44|]][[John 6.45|]][[John 6.46|]][[John 6.47|]][[John 6.48|]][[John 6.49|]][[John 6.50|]][[John 6.51|]][[John 6.52|]][[John 6.53|]][[John 6.54|]][[John 6.55|]][[John 6.56|]][[John 6.57|]][[John 6.58|]].

- Eat and drink of the bread and water of life, [[Alma 5.34|Alma 5:34]].
- Bread is in remembrance of Christ’s body, [[3 Nephi 18.5|3 Ne. 18:5–7]][[3 Nephi 18.6|]][[3 Nephi 18.7|]].

- Bread is an emblem of Christ’s flesh, [[D&C 20.40|D&C 20:40, 77]][[D&C 20.77|]] ([[Moroni 4.1|Moro. 4]][[Moroni 4.2|]][[Moroni 4.3|]]).
