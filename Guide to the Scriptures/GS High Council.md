# High Council

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/high-council?lang=eng)

A council of twelve high priests.

In the early days of the restored Church, the term *high council* referred to two different governing bodies: (1) the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles of the Church ([[D&C 107.33|D&C 107:33, 38]][[D&C 107.38|]]) and (2) the high council serving within each of the stakes ([[D&C 102.1|D&C 102]][[D&C 102.2|]][[D&C 102.3|]][[D&C 102.4|]][[D&C 102.5|]][[D&C 102.6|]][[D&C 102.7|]][[D&C 102.8|]][[D&C 102.9|]][[D&C 102.10|]][[D&C 102.11|]][[D&C 102.12|]][[D&C 102.13|]][[D&C 102.14|]][[D&C 102.15|]][[D&C 102.16|]][[D&C 102.17|]][[D&C 102.18|]][[D&C 102.19|]][[D&C 102.20|]][[D&C 102.21|]][[D&C 102.22|]][[D&C 102.23|]][[D&C 102.24|]][[D&C 102.25|]][[D&C 102.26|]][[D&C 102.27|]][[D&C 102.28|]][[D&C 102.29|]][[D&C 102.30|]][[D&C 102.31|]][[D&C 102.32|]][[D&C 102.33|]][[D&C 102.34|]]; [[D&C 107.36|107:36]]).
