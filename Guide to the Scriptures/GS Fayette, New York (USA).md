# Fayette, New York (USA)

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/fayette-new-york-usa?lang=eng)

The site of the farm owned by Peter Whitmer Sr. where many revelations were given to the Prophet Joseph Smith Jr. Here the Church was organized on April 6, 1830, and the voice of the Lord was heard ([[D&C 128.20|D&C 128:20]]).
