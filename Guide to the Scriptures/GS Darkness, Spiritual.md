# Darkness, Spiritual

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/darkness-spiritual?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Wicked, Wickedness|Wicked, Wickedness]]

Wickedness or ignorance of spiritual things.

- Woe unto them who put darkness for light, [[Isaiah 5.20|Isa. 5:20]] ([[2 Nephi 15.20|2 Ne. 15:20]]).
- Darkness shall cover the earth, and gross darkness the people, [[Isaiah 60.2|Isa. 60:2]].

- Jesus will give light to those that sit in darkness, [[Luke 1.79|Luke 1:79]].
- The light shines in darkness, and the darkness comprehends it not, [[John 1.5|John 1:5]] ([[D&C 45.7|D&C 45:7]]).
- Cast off the works of darkness and put on the armor of light, [[Romans 13.12|Rom. 13:12]].
- Have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, [[Ephesians 5.8|Eph. 5:8–11]][[Ephesians 5.9|]][[Ephesians 5.10|]][[Ephesians 5.11|]].

- Because ye ask not, ye are not brought into the light but must perish in the dark, [[2 Nephi 32.4|2 Ne. 32:4]].
- Satan spreads the works of darkness, [[Helaman 6.28|Hel. 6:28–31]][[Helaman 6.29|]][[Helaman 6.30|]][[Helaman 6.31|]].

- The powers of darkness prevail upon the earth, [[D&C 38.8|D&C 38:8, 11–12]][[D&C 38.11|]][[D&C 38.12|]].
- The whole world groans under darkness and sin, [[D&C 84.49|D&C 84:49–54]][[D&C 84.50|]][[D&C 84.51|]][[D&C 84.52|]][[D&C 84.53|]][[D&C 84.54|]].
- If your eye be single to my glory, there shall be no darkness in you, [[D&C 88.67|D&C 88:67]].

- The works of darkness began to prevail among all the sons of men, [[Moses 5.55|Moses 5:55]].
