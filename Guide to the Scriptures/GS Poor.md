# Poor

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/poor?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Alms, Almsgiving|Alms, Almsgiving]]; [[GS Fast, Fasting|Fast, Fasting]]; [[GS Humble, Humility|Humble, Humility]]; [[GS Offering|Offering]]; [[GS Welfare|Welfare]]

In the scriptures, *poor* can refer to (1) people who are without needed material goods, such as food, clothing, and shelter, or (2) people who are humble and without pride.

## Poor in material goods

- Thou shalt not shut thine hand from thy poor brother, [[Deuteronomy 15.7|Deut. 15:7]].
- The wicked in his pride doth persecute the poor, [[Psalms 10.2|Ps. 10:2]].
- He that giveth unto the poor shall not lack, [[Proverbs 28.27|Prov. 28:27]].
- Bring the poor to thy house, [[Isaiah 58.6|Isa. 58:6–7]][[Isaiah 58.7|]].

- If thou wilt be perfect, give to the poor, [[Matthew 19.21|Matt. 19:21]] ([[Mark 10.21|Mark 10:21]]; [[Luke 18.22|Luke 18:22]]).
- Hath not God chosen the poor of this world, [[James 2.5|James 2:5]].

- Because they are rich they despise the poor, [[2 Nephi 9.30|2 Ne. 9:30]].
- To retain a remission of your sins, impart of your substance to the poor, [[Mosiah 4.26|Mosiah 4:26]].
- They did impart of their substance to the poor, [[Alma 1.27|Alma 1:27]].
- If ye turn away the needy, your prayer is vain, [[Alma 34.28|Alma 34:28]].
- The Nephites had all things in common; there were not rich and poor, [[4 Nephi 1.3|4 Ne. 1:3]].

- Thou wilt remember the poor, [[D&C 42.30|D&C 42:30]] ([[D&C 52.40|D&C 52:40]]).
- Wo unto the poor whose hearts are not broken, [[D&C 56.17|D&C 56:17–18]][[D&C 56.18|]].
- The poor will come unto the marriage of the Lamb, [[D&C 58.6|D&C 58:6–11]][[D&C 58.7|]][[D&C 58.8|]][[D&C 58.9|]][[D&C 58.10|]][[D&C 58.11|]].
- The bishop is to search after the poor, [[D&C 84.112|D&C 84:112]].
- Gospel law governs the care of the poor, [[D&C 104.17|D&C 104:17–18]][[D&C 104.18|]].

- There was no poor among them, [[Moses 7.18|Moses 7:18]].

## Poor in spirit

- More blessed are those who humble themselves without being compelled because of poverty, [[Alma 32.4|Alma 32:4–6, 12–16]][[Alma 32.5|]][[Alma 32.6|]][[Alma 32.12|]][[Alma 32.13|]][[Alma 32.14|]][[Alma 32.15|]][[Alma 32.16|]].
- Blessed are the poor in spirit who come unto me, [[3 Nephi 12.3|3 Ne. 12:3]] ([[Matthew 5.3|Matt. 5:3]]).

- The poor and meek shall have the gospel preached unto them, [[D&C 35.15|D&C 35:15]].
