# John the Baptist

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/john-the-baptist?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Aaronic Priesthood|Aaronic Priesthood]]; [[GS Elias|Elias]]

The son of Zacharias and Elisabeth in the New Testament. John was sent to prepare the people to receive the Messiah ([[John 1.19|John 1:19–27]][[John 1.20|]][[John 1.21|]][[John 1.22|]][[John 1.23|]][[John 1.24|]][[John 1.25|]][[John 1.26|]][[John 1.27|]]). He held the keys of the Aaronic Priesthood and baptized Jesus Christ.

- Isaiah and others prophesied of John’s mission, [[Isaiah 40.3|Isa. 40:3]] ([[Malachi 3.1|Mal. 3:1]]; [[1 Nephi 10.7|1 Ne. 10:7–10]][[1 Nephi 10.8|]][[1 Nephi 10.9|]][[1 Nephi 10.10|]]; [[2 Nephi 31.4|2 Ne. 31:4]]).

- Was put in prison and beheaded, [[Matthew 14.3|Matt. 14:3–12]][[Matthew 14.4|]][[Matthew 14.5|]][[Matthew 14.6|]][[Matthew 14.7|]][[Matthew 14.8|]][[Matthew 14.9|]][[Matthew 14.10|]][[Matthew 14.11|]][[Matthew 14.12|]] ([[Mark 6.17|Mark 6:17–29]][[Mark 6.18|]][[Mark 6.19|]][[Mark 6.20|]][[Mark 6.21|]][[Mark 6.22|]][[Mark 6.23|]][[Mark 6.24|]][[Mark 6.25|]][[Mark 6.26|]][[Mark 6.27|]][[Mark 6.28|]][[Mark 6.29|]]).
- Gabriel announced John’s birth and ministry to Zacharias, [[Luke 1.5|Luke 1:5–25]][[Luke 1.6|]][[Luke 1.7|]][[Luke 1.8|]][[Luke 1.9|]][[Luke 1.10|]][[Luke 1.11|]][[Luke 1.12|]][[Luke 1.13|]][[Luke 1.14|]][[Luke 1.15|]][[Luke 1.16|]][[Luke 1.17|]][[Luke 1.18|]][[Luke 1.19|]][[Luke 1.20|]][[Luke 1.21|]][[Luke 1.22|]][[Luke 1.23|]][[Luke 1.24|]][[Luke 1.25|]].
- Jesus taught that John the Baptist was a great prophet, [[Luke 7.24|Luke 7:24–28]][[Luke 7.25|]][[Luke 7.26|]][[Luke 7.27|]][[Luke 7.28|]].
- Recognized Jesus as the Son of God, [[John 1.29|John 1:29–34]][[John 1.30|]][[John 1.31|]][[John 1.32|]][[John 1.33|]][[John 1.34|]].
- John’s disciples became Jesus’ disciples, [[John 1.25|John 1:25–29, 35–42]][[John 1.26|]][[John 1.27|]][[John 1.28|]][[John 1.29|]][[John 1.35|]][[John 1.36|]][[John 1.37|]][[John 1.38|]][[John 1.39|]][[John 1.40|]][[John 1.41|]][[John 1.42|]] ([[Acts 1.21|Acts 1:21–22]][[Acts 1.22|]]).
- Did no miracle, [[John 10.41|John 10:41]].

- As a resurrected being, was sent to ordain Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery to the Aaronic Priesthood, [[D&C 13.1|D&C 13]] ([[D&C 27.7|D&C 27:7–8]][[D&C 27.8|]]; [[Joseph Smith History 1.68|JS—H 1:68–72]][[Joseph Smith History 1.69|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.70|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.71|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.72|]]).
- Was ordained by an angel when he was eight days old, [[D&C 84.28|D&C 84:28]].
