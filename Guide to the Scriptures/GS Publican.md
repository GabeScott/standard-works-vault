# Publican

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/publican?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Roman Empire|Roman Empire]]

In ancient Rome, a collector of taxes for the government. Publicans were generally hated by the Jews. Some publicans readily accepted the gospel ([[Matthew 9.9|Matt. 9:9–10]][[Matthew 9.10|]]; [[Luke 19.2|Luke 19:2–8]][[Luke 19.3|]][[Luke 19.4|]][[Luke 19.5|]][[Luke 19.6|]][[Luke 19.7|]][[Luke 19.8|]]).
