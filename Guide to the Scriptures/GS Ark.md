# Ark

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/ark?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Flood at Noah’s Time|Flood at Noah’s Time]]; [[GS Noah, Bible Patriarch|Noah, Bible Patriarch]]; [[GS Rainbow|Rainbow]]

In the Old Testament, the boat built by Noah to preserve life during the great Flood.

- Make thee an ark of gopher wood, [[Genesis 6.14|Gen. 6:14]].
- The ark rested on the mountains of Ararat, [[Genesis 8.4|Gen. 8:4]].

- The Jaredite barges were tight like the ark of Noah, [[Ether 6.7|Ether 6:7]].
