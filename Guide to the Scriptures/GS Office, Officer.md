# Office, Officer

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/office-officer?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Ordain, Ordination|Ordain, Ordination]]; [[GS Priesthood|Priesthood]]

Position of authority or responsibility in an organization, often used in the scriptures to mean a position of priesthood authority; may also mean the duties assigned to the position or the person who holds the position.

- All members have not the same office, [[Romans 12.4|Rom. 12:4]].

- We did magnify our office unto the Lord, [[Jacob 1.19|Jacob 1:19]].
- Melchizedek received the office of the high priesthood, [[Alma 13.18|Alma 13:18]].
- The office of the angels’ ministry is to call men unto repentance, [[Moroni 7.31|Moro. 7:31]].

- No person is to be ordained to any office in this Church without the vote of that Church, [[D&C 20.65|D&C 20:65]].
- Let every man stand in his own office, [[D&C 84.109|D&C 84:109]].
- There are presidents, or presiding officers, appointed from among those ordained to the offices in these two priesthoods, [[D&C 107.21|D&C 107:21]].
- The duties of those who preside over the offices of the priesthood quorums are described, [[D&C 107.85|D&C 107:85–98]][[D&C 107.86|]][[D&C 107.87|]][[D&C 107.88|]][[D&C 107.89|]][[D&C 107.90|]][[D&C 107.91|]][[D&C 107.92|]][[D&C 107.93|]][[D&C 107.94|]][[D&C 107.95|]][[D&C 107.96|]][[D&C 107.97|]][[D&C 107.98|]].
- Let every man learn his duty, and to act in the office in which he is appointed, [[D&C 107.99|D&C 107:99–100]][[D&C 107.100|]].
- I give unto you the officers belonging to my priesthood, [[D&C 124.123|D&C 124:123]].
