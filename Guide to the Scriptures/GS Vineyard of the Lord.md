# Vineyard of the Lord

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/vineyard-of-the-lord?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Field|Field]]; [[GS Israel|Israel]]

A symbol for a field of spiritual labor. In the scriptures, the vineyard of the Lord usually refers to the house of Israel or the kingdom of God on the earth. It sometimes refers to the peoples of the world in general.

- The vineyard of the Lord of hosts is the house of Israel, [[Isaiah 5.7|Isa. 5:7]] ([[2 Nephi 15.7|2 Ne. 15:7]]).

- Jesus gave the parable of the laborers in the vineyard, [[Matthew 20.1|Matt. 20:1–16]][[Matthew 20.2|]][[Matthew 20.3|]][[Matthew 20.4|]][[Matthew 20.5|]][[Matthew 20.6|]][[Matthew 20.7|]][[Matthew 20.8|]][[Matthew 20.9|]][[Matthew 20.10|]][[Matthew 20.11|]][[Matthew 20.12|]][[Matthew 20.13|]][[Matthew 20.14|]][[Matthew 20.15|]][[Matthew 20.16|]].
