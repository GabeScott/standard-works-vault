# Lord

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/lord?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS God, Godhead|God, Godhead]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

A title of deep respect and honor for God the Father and the Savior Jesus Christ. The title refers to Their positions as supreme, loving masters over Their creations.

- Nothing is too hard for the Lord, [[Genesis 18.14|Gen. 18:14]].
- The Lord spoke to Moses face to face, [[Exodus 33.11|Ex. 33:11]].
- Thou shalt love the Lord thy God, [[Deuteronomy 6.5|Deut. 6:5]] ([[Matthew 22.37|Matt. 22:37]]; [[Mark 12.30|Mark 12:30]]).
- As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord, [[Joshua 24.15|Josh. 24:15]].
- The Lord is my shepherd, [[Psalms 23.1|Ps. 23:1]].
- The Lord is strong and mighty, mighty in battle, [[Psalms 24.8|Ps. 24:8]].
- The Lord Jehovah is my strength, [[Isaiah 12.2|Isa. 12:2]] ([[2 Nephi 22.2|2 Ne. 22:2]]).
- I the Lord am thy Savior and thy Redeemer, [[Isaiah 60.16|Isa. 60:16]].

- Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, [[Matthew 4.10|Matt. 4:10]] ([[Luke 4.8|Luke 4:8]]).
- How great things the Lord hath done, [[Mark 5.19|Mark 5:19]].
- There is one Lord Jesus Christ, [[1 Corinthians 8.6|1 Cor. 8:6]].
- There is one Lord, one faith, one baptism, [[Ephesians 4.5|Eph. 4:5]].
- The Lord himself shall descend from heaven, [[1 Thessalonians 4.16|1 Thes. 4:16]].

- I will go and do the things which the Lord hath commanded, [[1 Nephi 3.7|1 Ne. 3:7]].
- The Lord shall judge the poor with righteousness, [[2 Nephi 30.9|2 Ne. 30:9]].
- The Lord God, the God of Abraham, did deliver the Israelites out of bondage, [[Alma 29.11|Alma 29:11]].
- Nothing can save the people except repentance and faith on the Lord, [[Helaman 13.6|Hel. 13:6]] ([[Mosiah 3.12|Mosiah 3:12]]).

- Listen to the words of Jesus Christ, your Lord, [[D&C 15.1|D&C 15:1]].
- Seek the face of the Lord always, [[D&C 101.38|D&C 101:38]].
- The Lord shall be red in his apparel at the Second Coming, [[D&C 133.48|D&C 133:48]] ([[Isaiah 63.1|Isa. 63:1–4]][[Isaiah 63.2|]][[Isaiah 63.3|]][[Isaiah 63.4|]]).

- Abraham talked with the Lord face to face, [[Abraham 3.11|Abr. 3:11]].
