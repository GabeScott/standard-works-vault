# Telestial Glory

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/telestial-glory?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Degrees of Glory|Degrees of Glory]]

The lowest of the three degrees of glory in which people will dwell after the Final Judgment.

- Paul saw a glory of the stars, [[1 Corinthians 15.40|1 Cor. 15:40–41]][[1 Corinthians 15.41|]].

- Joseph Smith and Sidney Rigdon saw the telestial glory, [[D&C 76.81|D&C 76:81–90]][[D&C 76.82|]][[D&C 76.83|]][[D&C 76.84|]][[D&C 76.85|]][[D&C 76.86|]][[D&C 76.87|]][[D&C 76.88|]][[D&C 76.89|]][[D&C 76.90|]].
- The inhabitants of the telestial world were as innumerable as the stars, [[D&C 76.109|D&C 76:109–112]][[D&C 76.110|]][[D&C 76.111|]][[D&C 76.112|]].
- He who cannot abide the law of a telestial kingdom cannot abide a telestial glory, [[D&C 88.24|D&C 88:24, 31, 38]][[D&C 88.31|]][[D&C 88.38|]].
