# Nazareth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/nazareth?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

A village among the hills west of the Sea of Galilee. Nazareth was the early home of Jesus ([[Matthew 2.23|Matt. 2:23]]). Jesus taught in the synagogue at Nazareth and declared that He fulfilled the prophecy in [[Isaiah 61.1|Isaiah 61:1–2]][[Isaiah 61.2|]] ([[Matthew 13.54|Matt. 13:54–58]][[Matthew 13.55|]][[Matthew 13.56|]][[Matthew 13.57|]][[Matthew 13.58|]]; [[Mark 6.1|Mark 6:1–6]][[Mark 6.2|]][[Mark 6.3|]][[Mark 6.4|]][[Mark 6.5|]][[Mark 6.6|]]; [[Luke 4.16|Luke 4:16–30]][[Luke 4.17|]][[Luke 4.18|]][[Luke 4.19|]][[Luke 4.20|]][[Luke 4.21|]][[Luke 4.22|]][[Luke 4.23|]][[Luke 4.24|]][[Luke 4.25|]][[Luke 4.26|]][[Luke 4.27|]][[Luke 4.28|]][[Luke 4.29|]][[Luke 4.30|]]).
