# Gossip

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/gossip?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Evil Speaking|Evil Speaking]]; [[GS Rumor|Rumor]]

To share personal facts or information about another person without that person’s approval.

- Every idle word that men shall speak, they shall give account thereof, [[Matthew 12.36|Matt. 12:36]].
- Saints are admonished not to be tattlers and busybodies, speaking things which they ought not, [[1 Timothy 5.11|1 Tim. 5:11–14]][[1 Timothy 5.12|]][[1 Timothy 5.13|]][[1 Timothy 5.14|]].

- Thou shalt not speak evil of thy neighbor, [[D&C 42.27|D&C 42:27]].
- Strengthen your brethren in all your conversation, [[D&C 108.7|D&C 108:7]].
