# Resurrection

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/resurrection?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Body|Body]]; [[GS Death, Physical|Death, Physical]]; [[GS Immortal, Immortality|Immortal, Immortality]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Spirit|Spirit]]

The reuniting of the spirit body with the physical body of flesh and bones after death. After resurrection, the spirit and body will never again be separated, and the person will become immortal. Every person born on earth will be resurrected because Jesus Christ overcame death ([[1 Corinthians 15.20|1 Cor. 15:20–22]][[1 Corinthians 15.21|]][[1 Corinthians 15.22|]]).

Jesus Christ was the first person to be resurrected on this earth ([[Acts 26.23|Acts 26:23]]; [[Colossians 1.18|Col. 1:18]]; [[Revelation 1.5|Rev. 1:5]]).
