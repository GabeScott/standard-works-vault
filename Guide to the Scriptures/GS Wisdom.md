# Wisdom

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/wisdom?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Knowledge|Knowledge]]; [[GS Truth|Truth]]; [[GS Understanding|Understanding]]

The ability or gift from God to judge correctly. A person gains wisdom through experience and study and by following God’s counsel. Without God’s help, man does not have true wisdom ([[2 Nephi 9.28|2 Ne. 9:28]]; [[2 Nephi 27.26|27:26]]).

- God gave Solomon wisdom, [[1 Kings 4.29|1 Kgs. 4:29–30]][[1 Kings 4.30|]].
- Wisdom is the principal thing: therefore get wisdom, [[Proverbs 4.7|Prov. 4:7]].
- He that gets wisdom loveth his own soul, [[Proverbs 19.8|Prov. 19:8]].

- Jesus increased in wisdom, [[Luke 2.40|Luke 2:40, 52]][[Luke 2.52|]].
- If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, [[James 1.5|James 1:5]] ([[D&C 42.68|D&C 42:68]]; [[Joseph Smith History 1.11|JS—H 1:11]]).

- I tell you these things that ye may learn wisdom, [[Mosiah 2.17|Mosiah 2:17]].
- Learn wisdom in thy youth, [[Alma 37.35|Alma 37:35]].

- Saints shall find wisdom and great treasures of knowledge, [[D&C 89.19|D&C 89:19]].
- Let him that is ignorant learn wisdom by humbling himself and calling upon the Lord, [[D&C 136.32|D&C 136:32]].
