# Virgin

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/virgin?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Mary, Mother of Jesus|Mary, Mother of Jesus]]

A man or woman of marriageable age who has never had sexual intercourse. In the scriptures, a virgin may represent someone who is morally clean ([[Revelation 14.4|Rev. 14:4]]).

- A virgin shall conceive and bear a son, [[Isaiah 7.14|Isa. 7:14]] ([[Matthew 1.23|Matt. 1:23]]; [[2 Nephi 17.14|2 Ne. 17:14]]).

- The kingdom of heaven is likened unto ten virgins, [[Matthew 25.1|Matt. 25:1–13]][[Matthew 25.2|]][[Matthew 25.3|]][[Matthew 25.4|]][[Matthew 25.5|]][[Matthew 25.6|]][[Matthew 25.7|]][[Matthew 25.8|]][[Matthew 25.9|]][[Matthew 25.10|]][[Matthew 25.11|]][[Matthew 25.12|]][[Matthew 25.13|]].

- In the city of Nazareth I beheld a virgin, who was the mother of the Son of God, [[1 Nephi 11.13|1 Ne. 11:13–18]][[1 Nephi 11.14|]][[1 Nephi 11.15|]][[1 Nephi 11.16|]][[1 Nephi 11.17|]][[1 Nephi 11.18|]].
- Mary was a virgin, a precious and chosen vessel, [[Alma 7.10|Alma 7:10]].
