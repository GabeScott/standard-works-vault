# Ascension

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/ascension?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Second Coming of Jesus Christ|Second Coming of Jesus Christ]]

The formal departure of the Savior from the earth, forty days after His Resurrection. The Ascension took place from a point on the Mount of Olives in the presence of the disciples ([[Mark 16.19|Mark 16:19]]; [[Luke 24.51|Luke 24:51]]). At that time two angels from heaven testified that in the future the Lord would return “in like manner” ([[Acts 1.9|Acts 1:9–12]][[Acts 1.10|]][[Acts 1.11|]][[Acts 1.12|]]).
