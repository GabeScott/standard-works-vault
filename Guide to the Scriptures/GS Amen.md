# Amen

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/amen?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Prayer|Prayer]]

Means “may it be so” or “so it is.” *Amen* is said to show hearty or solemn acceptance and agreement ([[Deuteronomy 27.14|Deut. 27:14–26]][[Deuteronomy 27.15|]][[Deuteronomy 27.16|]][[Deuteronomy 27.17|]][[Deuteronomy 27.18|]][[Deuteronomy 27.19|]][[Deuteronomy 27.20|]][[Deuteronomy 27.21|]][[Deuteronomy 27.22|]][[Deuteronomy 27.23|]][[Deuteronomy 27.24|]][[Deuteronomy 27.25|]][[Deuteronomy 27.26|]]) or truthfulness ([[1 Kings 1.36|1 Kgs. 1:36]]). Today at the end of prayers, testimonies, and talks, those who heard the prayer or message say an audible *amen* to indicate agreement and acceptance.

In Old Testament times, a person was to say *amen* when making an oath ([[1 Chronicles 16.7|1 Chr. 16:7, 35–36]][[1 Chronicles 16.35|]][[1 Chronicles 16.36|]]; [[Nehemiah 5.12|Neh. 5:12–13]][[Nehemiah 5.13|]]; [[Nehemiah 8.2|8:2–6]][[Nehemiah 8.3|]][[Nehemiah 8.4|]][[Nehemiah 8.5|]][[Nehemiah 8.6|]]). Christ is called “the Amen, the faithful and true witness” ([[Revelation 3.14|Rev. 3:14]]). *Amen* also served as the token of a covenant in the School of the Prophets ([[D&C 88.133|D&C 88:133–135]][[D&C 88.134|]][[D&C 88.135|]]).
