# Asa

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/asa?lang=eng)

In the Old Testament, the third king of Judah. The scriptures record that his “heart was perfect with the Lord all his days” ([[1 Kings 15.14|1 Kgs. 15:14]]). During his reign he raised the army to a state of efficiency, threw off the Ethiopian yoke, removed the false idols, and invited the people to make a covenant to seek Jehovah ([[1 Kings 15.1|1 Kgs. 15–16]][[1 Kings 15.2|]][[1 Kings 15.3|]][[1 Kings 15.4|]][[1 Kings 15.5|]][[1 Kings 15.6|]][[1 Kings 15.7|]][[1 Kings 15.8|]][[1 Kings 15.9|]][[1 Kings 15.10|]][[1 Kings 15.11|]][[1 Kings 15.12|]][[1 Kings 15.13|]][[1 Kings 15.14|]][[1 Kings 15.15|]][[1 Kings 15.16|]][[1 Kings 15.17|]][[1 Kings 15.18|]][[1 Kings 15.19|]][[1 Kings 15.20|]][[1 Kings 15.21|]][[1 Kings 15.22|]][[1 Kings 15.23|]][[1 Kings 15.24|]][[1 Kings 15.25|]][[1 Kings 15.26|]][[1 Kings 15.27|]][[1 Kings 15.28|]][[1 Kings 15.29|]][[1 Kings 15.30|]][[1 Kings 15.31|]][[1 Kings 15.32|]][[1 Kings 15.33|]][[1 Kings 15.34|]]; [[2 Chronicles 14.1|2 Chr. 14–16]][[2 Chronicles 14.2|]][[2 Chronicles 14.3|]][[2 Chronicles 14.4|]][[2 Chronicles 14.5|]][[2 Chronicles 14.6|]][[2 Chronicles 14.7|]][[2 Chronicles 14.8|]][[2 Chronicles 14.9|]][[2 Chronicles 14.10|]][[2 Chronicles 14.11|]][[2 Chronicles 14.12|]][[2 Chronicles 14.13|]][[2 Chronicles 14.14|]][[2 Chronicles 14.15|]]). However, when he became diseased in his feet, he did not seek the Lord’s help and died ([[1 Kings 15.23|1 Kgs. 15:23–24]][[1 Kings 15.24|]]; [[2 Chronicles 16.12|2 Chr. 16:12–13]][[2 Chronicles 16.13|]]).
