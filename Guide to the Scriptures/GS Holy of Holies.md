# Holy of Holies

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/holy-of-holies?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Tabernacle|Tabernacle]]; [[GS Temple, House of the Lord|Temple, House of the Lord]]

The most sacred room in the tabernacle of Moses and later in the temple. The Holy of Holies is also called the “most holy place” ([[Exodus 26.33|Ex. 26:33–34]][[Exodus 26.34|]]).
