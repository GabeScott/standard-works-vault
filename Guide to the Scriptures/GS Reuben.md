# Reuben

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/reuben?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Israel|Israel]]; [[GS Jacob, Son of Isaac|Jacob, Son of Isaac]]

In the Old Testament, the eldest son of Jacob and Leah ([[Genesis 29.32|Gen. 29:32]]; [[Genesis 37.21|37:21–22, 29]][[Genesis 37.22|]][[Genesis 37.29|]]; [[Genesis 42.22|42:22, 37]][[Genesis 42.37|]]). Although Reuben was the firstborn, he lost his birthright because of sin ([[Genesis 35.22|Gen. 35:22]]; [[Genesis 49.3|49:3–4]][[Genesis 49.4|]]).

## The tribe of Reuben

Jacob’s blessing to Reuben is found in [[Genesis 49.3|Genesis 49:3]] and [[Deuteronomy 33.6|Deuteronomy 33:6]]. The tribe’s numbers gradually diminished, and though the tribe continued to exist, it became politically less important. Reuben’s birthright went to Joseph and his sons because Joseph was the firstborn son of Jacob’s second wife, Rachel ([[1 Chronicles 5.1|1 Chr. 5:1–2]][[1 Chronicles 5.2|]]).
