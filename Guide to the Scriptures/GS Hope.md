# Hope

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/hope?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Faith|Faith]]

The confident expectation of and longing for the promised blessings of righteousness. The scriptures often speak of hope as anticipation of eternal life through faith in Jesus Christ.

- Blessed is the man whose hope the Lord is, [[Jeremiah 17.7|Jer. 17:7]].
- The Lord will be the hope of his people, [[Joel 3.16|Joel 3:16]].

- We have hope through patience and the scriptures, [[Romans 15.4|Rom. 15:4]].
- God hath begotten us unto a lively hope in the resurrection of Christ, [[1 Peter 1.3|1 Pet. 1:3]].
- Every man that hath this hope purifieth himself, [[1 John 3.2|1 Jn. 3:2–3]][[1 John 3.3|]].

- Ye must press forward, having a perfect brightness of hope, [[2 Nephi 31.20|2 Ne. 31:20]].
- See that ye have faith, hope, and charity, [[Alma 7.24|Alma 7:24]] ([[1 Corinthians 13.13|1 Cor. 13:13]]; [[Moroni 10.20|Moro. 10:20]]).
- I wish that ye would hearken unto my words, having a hope that ye shall receive eternal life, [[Alma 13.27|Alma 13:27–29]][[Alma 13.28|]][[Alma 13.29|]].
- If ye have faith, ye hope for things which are not seen, which are true, [[Alma 32.21|Alma 32:21]] ([[Hebrews 11.1|Heb. 11:1]]).
- Hope comes of faith and maketh an anchor to the soul, [[Ether 12.4|Ether 12:4]] ([[Hebrews 6.17|Heb. 6:17–19]][[Hebrews 6.18|]][[Hebrews 6.19|]]).
- Man must hope or he cannot receive an inheritance, [[Ether 12.32|Ether 12:32]].
- Mormon spoke concerning faith, hope, and charity, [[Moroni 7.1|Moro. 7:1]].
- Ye shall hope through the atonement of Jesus Christ to be raised to eternal life, [[Moroni 7.40|Moro. 7:40–43]][[Moroni 7.41|]][[Moroni 7.42|]][[Moroni 7.43|]].
- The Holy Ghost fills you with hope, [[Moroni 8.26|Moro. 8:26]] ([[Romans 15.13|Rom. 15:13]]).

- They departed mortal life, firm in the hope of a glorious resurrection, [[D&C 138.14|D&C 138:14]].
