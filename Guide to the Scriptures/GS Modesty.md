# Modesty

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/modesty?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Humble, Humility|Humble, Humility]]

Behavior or appearance that is humble, moderate, and decent. A modest person avoids excesses and pretensions.

- God made coats of skins, and clothed Adam and Eve, [[Genesis 3.21|Gen. 3:21]] ([[Moses 4.27|Moses 4:27]]).

- Women adorn themselves in modest apparel, [[1 Timothy 2.9|1 Tim. 2:9]].
- Be discreet, chaste, keepers at home, [[Titus 2.5|Titus 2:5]].

- Many are lifted up in pride because of the costliness of their apparel, [[Jacob 2.13|Jacob 2:13]].

- Let all thy garments be plain, [[D&C 42.40|D&C 42:40]].

- We believe in being chaste and virtuous, [[Articles of Faith 1.13|A of F 1:13]].
