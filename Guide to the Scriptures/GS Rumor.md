# Rumor

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/rumor?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Evil Speaking|Evil Speaking]]; [[GS Gossip|Gossip]]

Satan spreads rumors and contentions—sometimes based partially on truth—to turn people against God and all good ([[Helaman 16.22|Hel. 16:22]]; [[Joseph Smith History 1.1|JS—H 1:1]]). One of the signs of the Second Coming of Jesus Christ is that people will hear of wars and rumors of wars ([[Matthew 24.6|Matt. 24:6]]; [[D&C 45.26|D&C 45:26]]; [[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.23|JS—M 1:23]]).
