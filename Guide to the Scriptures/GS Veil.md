# Veil

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/veil?lang=eng)

A word used in the scriptures to mean (1) a divider separating areas of the tabernacle or temple, (2) a symbol for a separation between God and man, (3) a thin cloth worn by people to cover their face or head, or (4) a God-given forgetfulness that blocks people’s memories of the premortal existence.

- The veil divides the holy of holies from the holy place, [[Exodus 26.33|Ex. 26:33]].

- The veil of the temple was rent in twain at the crucifixion of Christ, [[Matthew 27.51|Matt. 27:51]] ([[Mark 15.38|Mark 15:38]]; [[Luke 23.45|Luke 23:45]]).
- Now we see through a glass, darkly; but then face to face, [[1 Corinthians 13.12|1 Cor. 13:12]].

- The dark veil of unbelief was being cast away from his mind, [[Alma 19.6|Alma 19:6]].
- The brother of Jared could not be kept from beholding within the veil, [[Ether 3.19|Ether 3:19]] ([[Ether 12.19|Ether 12:19]]).

- The veil shall be rent and you shall see me, [[D&C 67.10|D&C 67:10]] ([[D&C 38.8|D&C 38:8]]).
- The veil of the covering of my temple shall be taken off, [[D&C 101.23|D&C 101:23]].
- The veil was taken from our minds, [[D&C 110.1|D&C 110:1]].

- A veil of darkness shall cover the earth, [[Moses 7.61|Moses 7:61]].
