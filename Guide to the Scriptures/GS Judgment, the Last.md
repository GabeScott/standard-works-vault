# Judgment, the Last

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/judgment-the-last?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Condemn, Condemnation|Condemn, Condemnation]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ—Judge]]; [[GS Judge, Judgment|Judge, Judgment]]

The Final Judgment that will occur after the Resurrection. God, through Jesus Christ, will judge each person to determine the eternal glory he will receive. This judgment will be based on each person’s obedience to God’s commands, including his acceptance of the atoning sacrifice of Jesus Christ.

- The Father hath committed all judgment unto the Son, [[John 5.22|John 5:22]].
- We shall all stand before the judgment seat of Christ, [[Romans 14.10|Rom. 14:10]].
- The dead were judged out of those things which were written, [[Revelation 20.12|Rev. 20:12]] ([[D&C 128.6|D&C 128:6–7]][[D&C 128.7|]]).

- For all thy doings thou shalt be brought into judgment, [[1 Nephi 10.20|1 Ne. 10:20]].
- The Twelve Apostles and the twelve Nephite disciples will judge Israel, [[1 Nephi 12.9|1 Ne. 12:9–10]][[1 Nephi 12.10|]] ([[D&C 29.12|D&C 29:12]]).
- All must appear before the judgment seat of the Holy One, [[2 Nephi 9.15|2 Ne. 9:15]].
- Prepare your souls for that glorious day, [[2 Nephi 9.46|2 Ne. 9:46]].
- Can ye imagine yourselves before the tribunal of God, [[Alma 5.17|Alma 5:17–25]][[Alma 5.18|]][[Alma 5.19|]][[Alma 5.20|]][[Alma 5.21|]][[Alma 5.22|]][[Alma 5.23|]][[Alma 5.24|]][[Alma 5.25|]].
- Jesus Christ shall stand to judge the world, [[3 Nephi 27.16|3 Ne. 27:16]].

- The Lord shall come down with a curse to judgment on the ungodly, [[D&C 133.2|D&C 133:2]].
