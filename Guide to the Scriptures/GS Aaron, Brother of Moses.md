# Aaron, Brother of Moses

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/aaron-brother-of-moses?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Aaronic Priesthood|Aaronic Priesthood]]; [[GS Moses|Moses]]

In the Old Testament, a son of Amram and Jochebed, of the tribe of Levi ([[Exodus 6.16|Ex. 6:16–20]][[Exodus 6.17|]][[Exodus 6.18|]][[Exodus 6.19|]][[Exodus 6.20|]]); the elder brother of Moses ([[Exodus 7.7|Ex. 7:7]]).

- Was appointed by the Lord to assist Moses in bringing the children of Israel out of Egypt and to be his spokesman, [[Exodus 4.10|Ex. 4:10–16, 27–31]][[Exodus 4.11|]][[Exodus 4.12|]][[Exodus 4.13|]][[Exodus 4.14|]][[Exodus 4.15|]][[Exodus 4.16|]][[Exodus 4.27|]][[Exodus 4.28|]][[Exodus 4.29|]][[Exodus 4.30|]][[Exodus 4.31|]]; [[Exodus 5.1|5:1–12:51]][[Exodus 5.2|]][[Exodus 5.3|]][[Exodus 5.4|]][[Exodus 5.5|]][[Exodus 5.6|]][[Exodus 5.7|]][[Exodus 5.8|]][[Exodus 5.9|]][[Exodus 5.10|]][[Exodus 5.11|]][[Exodus 5.12|]][[Exodus 5.13|]][[Exodus 5.14|]][[Exodus 5.15|]][[Exodus 5.16|]][[Exodus 5.17|]][[Exodus 5.18|]][[Exodus 5.19|]][[Exodus 5.20|]][[Exodus 5.21|]][[Exodus 5.22|]][[Exodus 5.23|]].
- On Mount Sinai, Moses received directions about the appointment of Aaron and his four sons to the Aaronic Priesthood, [[Exodus 28.1|Ex. 28:1–4]][[Exodus 28.2|]][[Exodus 28.3|]][[Exodus 28.4|]].
- Made a golden calf at the people’s request, [[Exodus 32.1|Ex. 32:1–6, 21, 24, 35]][[Exodus 32.2|]][[Exodus 32.3|]][[Exodus 32.4|]][[Exodus 32.5|]][[Exodus 32.6|]][[Exodus 32.21|]][[Exodus 32.24|]][[Exodus 32.35|]].
- Died on Mount Hor at the age of 123, [[Numbers 20.22|Num. 20:22–29]][[Numbers 20.23|]][[Numbers 20.24|]][[Numbers 20.25|]][[Numbers 20.26|]][[Numbers 20.27|]][[Numbers 20.28|]][[Numbers 20.29|]] ([[Numbers 33.38|Num. 33:38–39]][[Numbers 33.39|]]).

- The Lord confirmed a priesthood also upon Aaron and his seed, [[D&C 84.18|D&C 84:18, 26–27, 30]][[D&C 84.26|]][[D&C 84.27|]][[D&C 84.30|]].
- Those who magnify priesthood callings become the sons of Moses and of Aaron, [[D&C 84.33|D&C 84:33–34]][[D&C 84.34|]].
