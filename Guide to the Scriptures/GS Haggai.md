# Haggai

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/haggai?lang=eng)

An Old Testament prophet who prophesied in approximately 520  in Jerusalem, soon after the Jewish people returned from their exile in Babylonia ([[Ezra 5.1|Ezra 5:1]]; [[Ezra 6.14|6:14]]). He spoke of rebuilding the Lord’s temple in Jerusalem and reproved the people because it had not been finished. He also wrote of the millennial temple and reign of the Savior.
