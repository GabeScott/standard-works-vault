# Shem

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/shem?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Noah, Bible Patriarch|Noah, Bible Patriarch]]

In the Old Testament, a righteous son of Noah and, according to tradition, the forefather of the Shemite, or Semite, peoples, including the Arabs, Hebrews, Babylonians, Syrians, Phoenicians, and Assyrians ([[Genesis 5.29|Gen. 5:29–32]][[Genesis 5.30|]][[Genesis 5.31|]][[Genesis 5.32|]]; [[Genesis 6.10|6:10]]; [[Genesis 7.13|7:13]]; [[Genesis 9.26|9:26]]; [[Genesis 10.21|10:21–32]][[Genesis 10.22|]][[Genesis 10.23|]][[Genesis 10.24|]][[Genesis 10.25|]][[Genesis 10.26|]][[Genesis 10.27|]][[Genesis 10.28|]][[Genesis 10.29|]][[Genesis 10.30|]][[Genesis 10.31|]][[Genesis 10.32|]]; [[Moses 8.12|Moses 8:12]]). In latter-day revelation Shem is referred to as “the great high priest” ([[D&C 138.41|D&C 138:41]]).
