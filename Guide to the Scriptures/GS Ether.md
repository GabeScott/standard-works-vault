## The book of Ether

A book in the Book of Mormon that contains portions of the record of the Jaredites. The Jaredites were a group of people who lived in the western hemisphere many centuries before the people of Lehi. The book of Ether was taken from twenty-four plates found by the people of Limhi ([[Mosiah 8.8|Mosiah 8:8–9]][[Mosiah 8.9|]]).
