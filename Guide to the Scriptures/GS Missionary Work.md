# Missionary Work

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/missionary-work?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Gospel|Gospel]]; [[GS Preach|Preach]]

To share the gospel of Jesus Christ by word and by example.

- How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that publisheth salvation, [[Isaiah 52.7|Isa. 52:7]].
- I will both search my sheep and seek them out, [[Ezekiel 34.11|Ezek. 34:11]].

- Preach the gospel to every creature, [[Mark 16.15|Mark 16:15]] ([[Mormon 9.22|Morm. 9:22]]).
- The fields are white already to harvest, [[John 4.35|John 4:35]].
- And how shall they preach, except they be sent, [[Romans 10.15|Rom. 10:15]].

- Teach them the word of God with all diligence, [[Jacob 1.19|Jacob 1:19]].
- The Lord grants unto all nations to teach his word, [[Alma 29.8|Alma 29:8]].

- The gospel might be proclaimed by the weak and the simple, [[D&C 1.23|D&C 1:23]].
- A marvelous work is about to come forth, [[D&C 4.1|D&C 4:1]].
- If you labor all your days and bring one soul unto me, how great shall be your joy, [[D&C 18.15|D&C 18:15]].
- Mine elect hear my voice and harden not their hearts, [[D&C 29.7|D&C 29:7]].
- Go forth preaching my gospel, two by two, [[D&C 42.6|D&C 42:6]].
- The sound must go forth from this place, [[D&C 58.64|D&C 58:64]].
- Open your mouths in proclaiming my gospel, [[D&C 71.1|D&C 71:1]].
- Proclaim truth according to the revelations and commandments, [[D&C 75.4|D&C 75:4]].
- Every man who has been warned should warn his neighbor, [[D&C 88.81|D&C 88:81]] ([[D&C 38.40|D&C 38:40–41]][[D&C 38.41|]]).
- The Lord will provide for the families of those who preach the gospel, [[D&C 118.3|D&C 118:3]].
- The servants of God shall go forth, [[D&C 133.38|D&C 133:38]].
- Faithful elders, when they depart from mortal life, continue their labors, [[D&C 138.57|D&C 138:57]].
