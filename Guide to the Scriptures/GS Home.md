# Home

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/home?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Family|Family]]

The home should be the center of gospel and family activities.

- A man shall be free at home to cheer up his wife, [[Deuteronomy 24.5|Deut. 24:5]].

- He sent him away to his house, [[Mark 8.26|Mark 8:26]].
- Let children learn to show piety at home, [[1 Timothy 5.4|1 Tim. 5:4]].
- Be discreet, chaste, keepers at home, [[Titus 2.5|Titus 2:5]].

- Go to your homes and ponder the things I have said, [[3 Nephi 17.2|3 Ne. 17:2–3]][[3 Nephi 17.3|]].
- I did urge them to fight for their wives, and their children, and their houses, and their homes, [[Mormon 2.23|Morm. 2:23]].

- Fathers are commanded to be diligent and concerned at home, [[D&C 93.43|D&C 93:43–44, 48–50]][[D&C 93.44|]][[D&C 93.48|]][[D&C 93.49|]][[D&C 93.50|]].
