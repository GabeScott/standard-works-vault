# Woman, Women

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/woman-women?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Brethren, Brother|Brethren, Brother]]; [[GS Man, Men|Man, Men]]; [[GS Sister|Sister]]

An adult female person, a daughter of God. The term *Woman* is sometimes used in scripture as a title of respect ([[John 19.26|John 19:26]]; [[Alma 19.10|Alma 19:10]]).

- God created male and female, [[Genesis 1.27|Gen. 1:27]] ([[Moses 2.27|Moses 2:27]]; [[Moses 6.9|6:9]]; [[Abraham 4.27|Abr. 4:27]]).
- A virtuous woman is priced above rubies, [[Proverbs 31.10|Prov. 31:10–31]][[Proverbs 31.11|]][[Proverbs 31.12|]][[Proverbs 31.13|]][[Proverbs 31.14|]][[Proverbs 31.15|]][[Proverbs 31.16|]][[Proverbs 31.17|]][[Proverbs 31.18|]][[Proverbs 31.19|]][[Proverbs 31.20|]][[Proverbs 31.21|]][[Proverbs 31.22|]][[Proverbs 31.23|]][[Proverbs 31.24|]][[Proverbs 31.25|]][[Proverbs 31.26|]][[Proverbs 31.27|]][[Proverbs 31.28|]][[Proverbs 31.29|]][[Proverbs 31.30|]][[Proverbs 31.31|]].

- Woman is the glory of the man, [[1 Corinthians 11.7|1 Cor. 11:7]].
- Neither is the man without the woman, neither the woman without the man, in the Lord, [[1 Corinthians 11.11|1 Cor. 11:11]].
- Women should adorn themselves in modest apparel, [[1 Timothy 2.9|1 Tim. 2:9–10]][[1 Timothy 2.10|]].

- I, the Lord God, delight in the chastity of women, [[Jacob 2.28|Jacob 2:28]].

- Thy sins are forgiven thee, and thou art an elect lady, [[D&C 25.3|D&C 25:3]].
- Women have claim on their husbands for maintenance, [[D&C 83.2|D&C 83:2]].
