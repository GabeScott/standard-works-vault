# Zedekiah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/zedekiah?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Mulek|Mulek]]

In the Old Testament, the last king of Judah ([[2 Kings 24.17|2 Kgs. 24:17–20]][[2 Kings 24.18|]][[2 Kings 24.19|]][[2 Kings 24.20|]]; [[2 Kings 25.2|25:2–7]][[2 Kings 25.3|]][[2 Kings 25.4|]][[2 Kings 25.5|]][[2 Kings 25.6|]][[2 Kings 25.7|]]). Zedekiah imprisoned the prophet Jeremiah ([[Jeremiah 32.1|Jer. 32:1–5]][[Jeremiah 32.2|]][[Jeremiah 32.3|]][[Jeremiah 32.4|]][[Jeremiah 32.5|]]), and Jeremiah prophesied Zedekiah’s captivity ([[Jeremiah 34.2|Jer. 34:2–8, 21]][[Jeremiah 34.3|]][[Jeremiah 34.4|]][[Jeremiah 34.5|]][[Jeremiah 34.6|]][[Jeremiah 34.7|]][[Jeremiah 34.8|]][[Jeremiah 34.21|]]). Lehi and his family lived in Jerusalem during the first year of the reign of Zedekiah ([[1 Nephi 1.4|1 Ne. 1:4]]). All but one of the sons of Zedekiah were slain; his son Mulek escaped to the Western Hemisphere ([[Jeremiah 52.10|Jer. 52:10]]; [[Omni 1.15|Omni 1:15]]; [[Helaman 8.21|Hel. 8:21]]).
