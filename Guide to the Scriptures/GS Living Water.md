# Living Water

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/living-water?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

A symbol of the Lord Jesus Christ and His teachings. As water is essential to sustain physical life, the Savior and His teachings (living water) are essential for eternal life.

- With joy shall ye draw water out of the wells of salvation, [[Isaiah 12.3|Isa. 12:3]].
- They have forsaken me the fountain of living waters, [[Jeremiah 2.13|Jer. 2:13]].

- Whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst, [[John 4.6|John 4:6–15]][[John 4.7|]][[John 4.8|]][[John 4.9|]][[John 4.10|]][[John 4.11|]][[John 4.12|]][[John 4.13|]][[John 4.14|]][[John 4.15|]].
- If any man thirst, let him come unto me, and drink, [[John 7.37|John 7:37]].

- The rod of iron led to the fountain of living waters, [[1 Nephi 11.25|1 Ne. 11:25]].

- Partake of the waters of life freely, [[D&C 10.66|D&C 10:66]].
- My commandments shall be a well of living water, [[D&C 63.23|D&C 63:23]].
