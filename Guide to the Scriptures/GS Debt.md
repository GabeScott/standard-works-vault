# Debt

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/debt?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Forgive|Forgive]]

As used in the scriptures, money or property owed to another causes the borrower to be in a form of bondage. In another sense, Jesus taught that we should ask the Father to forgive us our debts, or release us from paying the price for our sins—through the Atonement of Jesus Christ—after we have forgiven others for their offences against us ([[Matthew 6.12|Matt. 6:12]]; [[3 Nephi 13.11|3 Ne. 13:11]]).

- The wicked borrow and pay not again, [[Psalms 37.21|Ps. 37:21]].
- The borrower is servant to the lender, [[Proverbs 22.7|Prov. 22:7]].

- I forgave thee all that debt: Shouldest not thou also have had compassion, [[Matthew 18.23|Matt. 18:23–35]][[Matthew 18.24|]][[Matthew 18.25|]][[Matthew 18.26|]][[Matthew 18.27|]][[Matthew 18.28|]][[Matthew 18.29|]][[Matthew 18.30|]][[Matthew 18.31|]][[Matthew 18.32|]][[Matthew 18.33|]][[Matthew 18.34|]][[Matthew 18.35|]].
- Owe no man any thing, but love one another, [[Romans 13.8|Rom. 13:8]].

- Ye are eternally indebted to your Heavenly Father, [[Mosiah 2.21|Mosiah 2:21–24, 34]][[Mosiah 2.22|]][[Mosiah 2.23|]][[Mosiah 2.24|]][[Mosiah 2.34|]].
- Whosoever borrows from his neighbor should return the thing that he borrows, [[Mosiah 4.28|Mosiah 4:28]].

- Pay the debt, and release thyself from bondage, [[D&C 19.35|D&C 19:35]].
- It is forbidden to get in debt to thine enemies, [[D&C 64.27|D&C 64:27]].
- Pay all your debts, [[D&C 104.78|D&C 104:78]].
- Do not get in debt to build the house of the Lord, [[D&C 115.13|D&C 115:13]].
