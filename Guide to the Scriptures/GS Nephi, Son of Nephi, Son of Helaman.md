# Nephi, Son of Nephi, Son of Helaman

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/nephi-son-of-nephi-son-of-helaman?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Disciple|Disciple]]; [[GS Nephi, Son of Helaman|Nephi, Son of Helaman]]

One of the twelve Nephite disciples chosen by the resurrected Jesus Christ in the Book of Mormon ([[3 Nephi 1.2|3 Ne. 1:2–3]][[3 Nephi 1.3|]]; [[3 Nephi 19.4|19:4]]). This prophet prayed mightily to the Lord in behalf of his people. Nephi heard the voice of the Lord ([[3 Nephi 1.11|3 Ne. 1:11–14]][[3 Nephi 1.12|]][[3 Nephi 1.13|]][[3 Nephi 1.14|]]). Nephi was also visited by angels, cast out devils, raised his brother from the dead, and bore a testimony that could not be disbelieved ([[3 Nephi 7.15|3 Ne. 7:15–19]][[3 Nephi 7.16|]][[3 Nephi 7.17|]][[3 Nephi 7.18|]][[3 Nephi 7.19|]]; [[3 Nephi 19.4|19:4]]). Nephi kept the scriptural record ([[3 Nephi 1.2|3 Ne. 1:2–3]][[3 Nephi 1.3|]]).

## The book of 4 Nephi

This book has only forty-nine verses, all in one chapter, yet it covers nearly three hundred years of Nephite history ( 34–321). Several generations of writers, including Nephi, contributed to the record. [[4 Nephi 1.1|Verses 1–19]][[4 Nephi 1.2|]][[4 Nephi 1.3|]][[4 Nephi 1.4|]][[4 Nephi 1.5|]][[4 Nephi 1.6|]][[4 Nephi 1.7|]][[4 Nephi 1.8|]][[4 Nephi 1.9|]][[4 Nephi 1.10|]][[4 Nephi 1.11|]][[4 Nephi 1.12|]][[4 Nephi 1.13|]][[4 Nephi 1.14|]][[4 Nephi 1.15|]][[4 Nephi 1.16|]][[4 Nephi 1.17|]][[4 Nephi 1.18|]][[4 Nephi 1.19|]] tell that after the resurrected Christ’s visit all the Nephites and Lamanites were converted to the gospel. Peace, love, and harmony reigned. The three Nephite disciples, whom Christ had allowed to remain on earth until His Second Coming ([[3 Nephi 28.4|3 Ne. 28:4–9]][[3 Nephi 28.5|]][[3 Nephi 28.6|]][[3 Nephi 28.7|]][[3 Nephi 28.8|]][[3 Nephi 28.9|]]), ministered to the people. Nephi left the record to his son Amos. [[4 Nephi 1.19|Verses 19–47]][[4 Nephi 1.20|]][[4 Nephi 1.21|]][[4 Nephi 1.22|]][[4 Nephi 1.23|]][[4 Nephi 1.24|]][[4 Nephi 1.25|]][[4 Nephi 1.26|]][[4 Nephi 1.27|]][[4 Nephi 1.28|]][[4 Nephi 1.29|]][[4 Nephi 1.30|]][[4 Nephi 1.31|]][[4 Nephi 1.32|]][[4 Nephi 1.33|]][[4 Nephi 1.34|]][[4 Nephi 1.35|]][[4 Nephi 1.36|]][[4 Nephi 1.37|]][[4 Nephi 1.38|]][[4 Nephi 1.39|]][[4 Nephi 1.40|]][[4 Nephi 1.41|]][[4 Nephi 1.42|]][[4 Nephi 1.43|]][[4 Nephi 1.44|]][[4 Nephi 1.45|]][[4 Nephi 1.46|]][[4 Nephi 1.47|]] are the record of Amos’s ministry (84 years) and that of his son Amos (112 years). In  201, pride began to cause problems among the people, who divided themselves into classes and began false churches to get gain ([[4 Nephi 1.24|4 Ne. 1:24–34]][[4 Nephi 1.25|]][[4 Nephi 1.26|]][[4 Nephi 1.27|]][[4 Nephi 1.28|]][[4 Nephi 1.29|]][[4 Nephi 1.30|]][[4 Nephi 1.31|]][[4 Nephi 1.32|]][[4 Nephi 1.33|]][[4 Nephi 1.34|]]).

The final verses of 4 Nephi show that the people had returned again to wickedness ([[4 Nephi 1.35|4 Ne. 1:35–49]][[4 Nephi 1.36|]][[4 Nephi 1.37|]][[4 Nephi 1.38|]][[4 Nephi 1.39|]][[4 Nephi 1.40|]][[4 Nephi 1.41|]][[4 Nephi 1.42|]][[4 Nephi 1.43|]][[4 Nephi 1.44|]][[4 Nephi 1.45|]][[4 Nephi 1.46|]][[4 Nephi 1.47|]][[4 Nephi 1.48|]][[4 Nephi 1.49|]]). In  305, Amos the son of Amos died and his brother Ammaron hid all the sacred records for safekeeping. Ammaron later entrusted the records to Mormon, who recorded many events of his own lifetime and then abridged them ([[Mormon 1.2|Morm. 1:2–4]][[Mormon 1.3|]][[Mormon 1.4|]]).
