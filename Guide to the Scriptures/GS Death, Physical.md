# Death, Physical

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/death-physical?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Fall of Adam and Eve|Fall of Adam and Eve]]; [[GS Mortal, Mortality|Mortal, Mortality]]; [[GS Resurrection|Resurrection]]; [[GS Salvation|Salvation]]

The separation of the body and the spirit. The Fall brought mortality and death to the earth ([[2 Nephi 2.22|2 Ne. 2:22]]; [[Moses 6.48|Moses 6:48]]). The Atonement of Jesus Christ conquered death so that everyone will be resurrected ([[1 Corinthians 15.21|1 Cor. 15:21–23]][[1 Corinthians 15.22|]][[1 Corinthians 15.23|]]). Resurrection is a free gift to all people regardless of whether they have done good or evil in this life ([[Alma 11.42|Alma 11:42–44]][[Alma 11.43|]][[Alma 11.44|]]). Each person suffers only one physical death since once we are resurrected, our bodies can die no more ([[Alma 11.45|Alma 11:45]]).

- All flesh shall perish, and man shall turn again unto dust, [[Job 34.15|Job 34:15]].
- Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his Saints, [[Psalms 116.15|Ps. 116:15]].
- The dust will return to the earth, and the spirit will return to God, [[Ecclesiastes 12.7|Eccl. 12:7]].

- By man came death, [[1 Corinthians 15.21|1 Cor. 15:21]].
- The Savior holds the keys of hell and of death, [[Revelation 1.18|Rev. 1:18]].
- There shall be no more death, neither sorrow, [[Revelation 21.4|Rev. 21:4]].

- Death hath passed upon all men, [[2 Nephi 9.6|2 Ne. 9:6, 11]][[2 Nephi 9.11|]] ([[Alma 12.24|Alma 12:24]]).
- They never did look upon death with terror, [[Alma 27.28|Alma 27:28]].
- Alma explained the state of the soul between death and the resurrection, [[Alma 40.11|Alma 40:11]].

- Those who die in me shall not taste of death, [[D&C 42.46|D&C 42:46]].
- Those who are not appointed unto death shall be healed, [[D&C 42.48|D&C 42:48]].
- I will prove you in all things even unto death, [[D&C 98.14|D&C 98:14]].

- Unto dust shalt thou return, [[Moses 4.25|Moses 4:25]].
- Adam fell, and by his fall came death, [[Moses 6.48|Moses 6:48]].
