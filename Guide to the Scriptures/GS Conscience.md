# Conscience

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/conscience?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Light, Light of Christ|Light, Light of Christ]]

The inner sense of right and wrong, coming from the Light of Christ in all men ([[Moroni 7.16|Moro. 7:16]]). We are born with a natural capacity to distinguish between right and wrong because of the Light of Christ that is given to every person ([[D&C 84.46|D&C 84:46]]). This faculty is called conscience. The possession of it makes us responsible beings. Like other faculties, our consciences may be deadened through sin or misuse.

- The scribes and Pharisees were convicted by their own conscience, [[John 8.9|John 8:9]].
- Their conscience also bears witness, [[Romans 2.14|Rom. 2:14–15]][[Romans 2.15|]].
- Apostates have their conscience seared with a hot iron, [[1 Timothy 4.2|1 Tim. 4:2]].

- Men are instructed sufficiently that they know good from evil, [[2 Nephi 2.5|2 Ne. 2:5]].
- King Benjamin had a clear conscience before God, [[Mosiah 2.15|Mosiah 2:15]].
- The Nephites were filled with joy, having peace of conscience, [[Mosiah 4.3|Mosiah 4:3]].
- Zeezrom was harrowed up under a consciousness of his own guilt, [[Alma 14.6|Alma 14:6]].
- We are given joy or remorse of conscience according to our desires, [[Alma 29.5|Alma 29:5]].
- A punishment and a just law bring remorse of conscience, [[Alma 42.18|Alma 42:18]].
- The Spirit of Christ is given to every man that he may know good from evil, [[Moroni 7.16|Moro. 7:16]].

- Each individual should have the free exercise of conscience, [[D&C 134.2|D&C 134:2]].
- I have a conscience void of offense, [[D&C 135.4|D&C 135:4]].

- We claim the privilege of worshiping God according to the dictates of our own conscience, [[Articles of Faith 1.11|A of F 1:11]].
