# Gift of the Holy Ghost

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/gift-of-the-holy-ghost?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Gift|Gift]]; [[GS Gifts of the Spirit|Gifts of the Spirit]]; [[GS God, Godhead|God, Godhead]]; [[GS Holy Ghost|Holy Ghost]]

It is the right of every worthy baptized member of the Church to have the constant influence of the Holy Ghost. Following a person’s baptism into the true Church of Jesus Christ, he receives the gift of the Holy Ghost by the laying on of hands from one who has the proper authority ([[Acts 8.12|Acts 8:12–25]][[Acts 8.13|]][[Acts 8.14|]][[Acts 8.15|]][[Acts 8.16|]][[Acts 8.17|]][[Acts 8.18|]][[Acts 8.19|]][[Acts 8.20|]][[Acts 8.21|]][[Acts 8.22|]][[Acts 8.23|]][[Acts 8.24|]][[Acts 8.25|]]; [[Moroni 2.1|Moro. 2]][[Moroni 2.2|]][[Moroni 2.3|]]; [[D&C 39.23|D&C 39:23]]). Receiving the gift of the Holy Ghost is often spoken of as a baptism by fire ([[Matthew 3.11|Matt. 3:11]]; [[D&C 19.31|D&C 19:31]]).

- Men are commanded to repent, be baptized, and receive the gift of the Holy Ghost, [[Acts 2.38|Acts 2:38]].
- Peter and John conferred the gift of the Holy Ghost by the laying on of hands, [[Acts 8.14|Acts 8:14–22]][[Acts 8.15|]][[Acts 8.16|]][[Acts 8.17|]][[Acts 8.18|]][[Acts 8.19|]][[Acts 8.20|]][[Acts 8.21|]][[Acts 8.22|]].
- The Holy Ghost is given by the laying on of hands, [[Acts 19.2|Acts 19:2–6]][[Acts 19.3|]][[Acts 19.4|]][[Acts 19.5|]][[Acts 19.6|]].

- The remission of sins comes by fire and by the Holy Ghost, [[2 Nephi 31.17|2 Ne. 31:17]].

- We believe in the laying on of hands for the gift of the Holy Ghost, [[Articles of Faith 1.4|A of F 1:4]].
