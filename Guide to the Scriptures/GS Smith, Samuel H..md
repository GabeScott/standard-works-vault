# Smith, Samuel H.

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/smith-samuel-h?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Smith, Joseph, Jr.|Smith, Joseph, Jr.]]

A younger brother of the Prophet Joseph Smith ([[Joseph Smith History 1.4|JS—H 1:4]]). Samuel was born in 1808 and died in 1844. He was one of the Eight Witnesses of the Book of Mormon and served as one of the first missionaries for the restored Church ([[D&C 23.4|D&C 23:4]]; [[D&C 52.30|52:30]]; [[D&C 61.33|61:33–35]][[D&C 61.34|]][[D&C 61.35|]]; [[D&C 66.7|66:7–8]][[D&C 66.8|]]; [[D&C 75.13|75:13]]).
