# Music

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/music?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Hymn|Hymn]]; [[GS Sing|Sing]]

Melodies and rhythms sung and played since early biblical times to express joy, praise, and worship ([[2 Samuel 6.5|2 Sam. 6:5]]). It can be a form of prayer. The psalms were probably sung to simple melodies and accompanied by instruments.

- Miriam, the sister of Aaron and Moses, took a timbrel and she and the women danced, [[Exodus 15.20|Ex. 15:20]].
- The Levites who were the singers had cymbals and harps and one hundred twenty priests blew their trumpets, [[2 Chronicles 5.12|2 Chr. 5:12]].

- Jesus and the Twelve sang a hymn after the Last Supper, [[Matthew 26.30|Matt. 26:30]].
- Teach and admonish one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, [[Colossians 3.16|Col. 3:16]].

- Have you felt to sing the song of redeeming love, [[Alma 5.26|Alma 5:26]].

- God’s soul delights in the song of the heart; yea, the song of the righteous is a prayer, [[D&C 25.12|D&C 25:12]].
- Praise the Lord with singing, with music, and with dancing, [[D&C 136.28|D&C 136:28]].
