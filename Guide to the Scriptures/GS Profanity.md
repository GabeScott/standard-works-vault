# Profanity

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/profanity?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Blaspheme, Blasphemy|Blaspheme, Blasphemy]]

Disrespect or contempt for sacred things; especially, irreverence for the name of God.

- Thou shalt not take the name of the Lord thy God in vain, [[Exodus 20.7|Ex. 20:7]] ([[2 Nephi 26.32|2 Ne. 26:32]]; [[Mosiah 13.15|Mosiah 13:15]]; [[D&C 136.21|D&C 136:21]]).
- Why do we profane the covenant of our fathers, [[Malachi 2.10|Mal. 2:10]].

- Every idle word that men shall speak, they shall account for in the judgment, [[Matthew 12.34|Matt. 12:34–37]][[Matthew 12.35|]][[Matthew 12.36|]][[Matthew 12.37|]].
- Out of the same mouth proceedeth blessing and cursing, which ought not so to be, [[James 3.10|James 3:10]].

- Our words will condemn us, [[Alma 12.14|Alma 12:14]] ([[Mosiah 4.30|Mosiah 4:30]]).

- Let all men beware how they take my name in their lips, [[D&C 63.61|D&C 63:61–62]][[D&C 63.62|]].
