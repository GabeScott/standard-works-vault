# Come

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/come?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Disciple|Disciple]]; [[GS Obedience, Obedient, Obey|Obedience, Obedient, Obey]]

In the scriptures, often means to draw near to someone by following or obeying, such as in the phrase “come unto Christ, and be perfected in him” ([[Moroni 10.32|Moro. 10:32]]).

- Incline your ear, and come unto me, [[Isaiah 55.3|Isa. 55:3]].

- Come unto me, all ye that labor, [[Matthew 11.28|Matt. 11:28]].
- Suffer little children to come unto me, [[Matthew 19.14|Matt. 19:14]].
- If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, [[Luke 9.23|Luke 9:23]].
- He that cometh to me shall never hunger, [[John 6.35|John 6:35]].

- Christ invites all to come unto him, [[2 Nephi 26.33|2 Ne. 26:33]].
- Come unto me and be saved, [[3 Nephi 12.20|3 Ne. 12:20]].
- Come unto Christ, [[Moroni 10.32|Moro. 10:32]].

- Invite all to come unto Christ, [[D&C 20.59|D&C 20:59]].
- Come unto me, and your souls shall live, [[D&C 45.46|D&C 45:46]].
