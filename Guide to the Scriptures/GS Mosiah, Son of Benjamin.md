# Mosiah, Son of Benjamin

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/mosiah-son-of-benjamin?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Benjamin, Father of Mosiah|Benjamin, Father of Mosiah]]; [[GS Mosiah, Sons of|Mosiah, Sons of]]

A righteous Nephite king and prophet in the Book of Mormon. Mosiah followed the righteous example of his father ([[Mosiah 6.4|Mosiah 6:4–7]][[Mosiah 6.5|]][[Mosiah 6.6|]][[Mosiah 6.7|]]). He translated the twenty-four gold plates containing the record of the Jaredites ([[Mosiah 28.17|Mosiah 28:17]]).
