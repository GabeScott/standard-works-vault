# Bishop

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/bishop?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Aaronic Priesthood|Aaronic Priesthood]]

Means “overseer,” an office or position of responsibility. Bishop is an ordained office in the Aaronic Priesthood ([[D&C 20.67|D&C 20:67]]; [[D&C 107.87|107:87–88]][[D&C 107.88|]]), and a bishop is a common judge in Israel ([[D&C 107.72|D&C 107:72, 74]][[D&C 107.74|]]).

- The Holy Ghost has made you overseers, [[Acts 20.28|Acts 20:28]].
- Qualifications are set forth for bishops, [[1 Timothy 3.1|1 Tim. 3:1–7]][[1 Timothy 3.2|]][[1 Timothy 3.3|]][[1 Timothy 3.4|]][[1 Timothy 3.5|]][[1 Timothy 3.6|]][[1 Timothy 3.7|]] ([[Titus 1.7|Titus 1:7]]).

- A bishop is to be ordained, [[D&C 20.67|D&C 20:67]].
- Edward Partridge was to serve as a bishop unto the Church, [[D&C 41.9|D&C 41:9]].
- A bishop is to discern spiritual gifts, [[D&C 46.27|D&C 46:27, 29]][[D&C 46.29|]].
- A high priest may officiate in the office of bishop, [[D&C 68.14|D&C 68:14, 19]][[D&C 68.19|]] ([[D&C 107.17|D&C 107:17]]).
- A bishop is appointed of the Lord, [[D&C 72.1|D&C 72]][[D&C 72.2|]][[D&C 72.3|]][[D&C 72.4|]][[D&C 72.5|]][[D&C 72.6|]][[D&C 72.7|]][[D&C 72.8|]][[D&C 72.9|]][[D&C 72.10|]][[D&C 72.11|]][[D&C 72.12|]][[D&C 72.13|]][[D&C 72.14|]][[D&C 72.15|]][[D&C 72.16|]][[D&C 72.17|]][[D&C 72.18|]][[D&C 72.19|]][[D&C 72.20|]][[D&C 72.21|]][[D&C 72.22|]][[D&C 72.23|]][[D&C 72.24|]][[D&C 72.25|]][[D&C 72.26|]].
- A bishop is to care for the poor, [[D&C 84.112|D&C 84:112]].
- A bishop is to administer all temporal things, [[D&C 107.68|D&C 107:68]].
- A bishop is president of the Aaronic Priesthood, [[D&C 107.87|D&C 107:87–88]][[D&C 107.88|]].
