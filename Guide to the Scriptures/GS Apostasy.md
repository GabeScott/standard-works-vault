# Apostasy

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/apostasy?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Rebellion|Rebellion]]; [[GS Restoration of the Gospel|Restoration of the Gospel]]

A turning away from the truth by individuals, the Church, or entire nations.

## General apostasy

- Israel had to guard against their hearts turning away from the Lord, [[Deuteronomy 29.18|Deut. 29:18]].
- Where there is no vision, the people perish, [[Proverbs 29.18|Prov. 29:18]].
- They have broken the everlasting covenant, [[Isaiah 24.5|Isa. 24:5]].

- The winds beat upon that house, and it fell, [[Matthew 7.27|Matt. 7:27]].
- I marvel that ye are so soon removed unto another gospel, [[Galatians 1.6|Gal. 1:6]].

- They commenced in the good path but lost their way in the mist, [[1 Nephi 8.23|1 Ne. 8:23]] ([[1 Nephi 12.17|1 Ne. 12:17]]).
- After they had tasted of the fruit, they fell away into forbidden paths, [[1 Nephi 8.28|1 Ne. 8:28]].
- Nephite apostasy caused a stumbling block for nonbelievers, [[Alma 4.6|Alma 4:6–12]][[Alma 4.7|]][[Alma 4.8|]][[Alma 4.9|]][[Alma 4.10|]][[Alma 4.11|]][[Alma 4.12|]].
- Many members of the Church became proud and persecuted other members, [[Helaman 3.33|Hel. 3:33–34]][[Helaman 3.34|]] ([[Helaman 4.11|Hel. 4:11–13]][[Helaman 4.12|]][[Helaman 4.13|]]; [[Helaman 5.2|5:2–3]][[Helaman 5.3|]]).
- When the Lord prospers his people, they sometimes harden their hearts and forget him, [[Helaman 12.2|Hel. 12:2]]; [[Helaman 13.38|13:38]].
- The Nephites hardened their hearts and fell under Satan’s power, [[3 Nephi 2.1|3 Ne. 2:1–3]][[3 Nephi 2.2|]][[3 Nephi 2.3|]].
- Moroni prophesied about apostasy in the last days, [[Mormon 8.28|Morm. 8:28, 31–41]][[Mormon 8.31|]][[Mormon 8.32|]][[Mormon 8.33|]][[Mormon 8.34|]][[Mormon 8.35|]][[Mormon 8.36|]][[Mormon 8.37|]][[Mormon 8.38|]][[Mormon 8.39|]][[Mormon 8.40|]][[Mormon 8.41|]].

- Apostasy will precede the Second Coming, [[D&C 1.13|D&C 1:13–16]][[D&C 1.14|]][[D&C 1.15|]][[D&C 1.16|]].
