# Jehovah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/jehovah?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS I Am|I Am]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

The covenant or proper name of the God of Israel. It denotes “the eternal I Am” ([[Exodus 3.14|Ex. 3:14]]; [[John 8.58|John 8:58]]). Jehovah is the premortal Jesus Christ and came to earth as a son of Mary ([[Mosiah 3.8|Mosiah 3:8]]; [[Mosiah 15.1|15:1]]; [[3 Nephi 15.1|3 Ne. 15:1–5]][[3 Nephi 15.2|]][[3 Nephi 15.3|]][[3 Nephi 15.4|]][[3 Nephi 15.5|]]). Usually, when the word *Lord* appears in the Old Testament, it means “Jehovah.”

## Jehovah is Christ

Jehovah was known to the ancient prophets ([[Exodus 6.3|Ex. 6:3]]; [[Abraham 1.16|Abr. 1:16]]). The Apostle Paul taught that Christ was the Jehovah of the Old Testament ([[Exodus 17.6|Ex. 17:6]]; [[1 Corinthians 10.1|1 Cor. 10:1–4]][[1 Corinthians 10.2|]][[1 Corinthians 10.3|]][[1 Corinthians 10.4|]]). The brother of Jared in the Book of Mormon saw the premortal Christ and worshiped Him ([[Ether 3.13|Ether 3:13–15]][[Ether 3.14|]][[Ether 3.15|]]). Moroni also called Christ “Jehovah” ([[Moroni 10.34|Moro. 10:34]]). At the Kirtland Temple, Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery saw the resurrected Jehovah ([[D&C 110.3|D&C 110:3–4]][[D&C 110.4|]]).
