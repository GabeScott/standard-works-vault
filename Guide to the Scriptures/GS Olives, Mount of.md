# Olives, Mount of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/olives-mount-of?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Gethsemane|Gethsemane]]

A hill east of the Kidron Valley, east of Jerusalem. On its western slopes, near the bottom, is the garden of Gethsemane. Bethphage and Bethany are located on the top and east slope. This mount was the place of many biblical events ([[Matthew 24.3|Matt. 24:3]]) and will also be an important place in events of the latter days ([[Zechariah 14.3|Zech. 14:3–5]][[Zechariah 14.4|]][[Zechariah 14.5|]]; [[D&C 45.48|D&C 45:48–54]][[D&C 45.49|]][[D&C 45.50|]][[D&C 45.51|]][[D&C 45.52|]][[D&C 45.53|]][[D&C 45.54|]]; [[D&C 133.20|133:20]]).
