# Firstborn

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/firstborn?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Birthright|Birthright]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

In the time of the ancient patriarchs, the firstborn son received the birthright ([[Genesis 43.33|Gen. 43:33]]) and thus inherited the leadership of the family upon the death of the father. The firstborn had to be worthy to assume this responsibility ([[1 Chronicles 5.1|1 Chr. 5:1–2]][[1 Chronicles 5.2|]]) and could lose his birthright by unrighteousness.

Under the law of Moses, the firstborn son was regarded as belonging to God. The firstborn received a double portion of his father’s possessions ([[Deuteronomy 21.17|Deut. 21:17]]). After his father’s death, he was responsible for the care of his mother and sisters.

The male firstborn of animals also belonged to God. Clean animals were used for sacrifices, while unclean animals might be redeemed or sold or put to death ([[Exodus 13.2|Ex. 13:2, 11–13]][[Exodus 13.11|]][[Exodus 13.12|]][[Exodus 13.13|]]; [[Exodus 34.19|34:19–20]][[Exodus 34.20|]]; [[Leviticus 27.11|Lev. 27:11–13, 26–27]][[Leviticus 27.12|]][[Leviticus 27.13|]][[Leviticus 27.26|]][[Leviticus 27.27|]]).

The firstborn symbolized Jesus Christ and His earthly ministry, reminding the people that the great Messiah would come ([[Moses 5.4|Moses 5:4–8]][[Moses 5.5|]][[Moses 5.6|]][[Moses 5.7|]][[Moses 5.8|]]; [[Moses 6.63|6:63]]).

Jesus was the firstborn of the spirit children of our Heavenly Father, the Only Begotten of the Father in the flesh, and the first to rise from the dead in the Resurrection ([[Colossians 1.13|Col. 1:13–18]][[Colossians 1.14|]][[Colossians 1.15|]][[Colossians 1.16|]][[Colossians 1.17|]][[Colossians 1.18|]]). Faithful Saints become members of the Church of the Firstborn in eternity ([[D&C 93.21|D&C 93:21–22]][[D&C 93.22|]]).

- The firstborn of thy sons shalt thou give unto me, [[Exodus 22.29|Ex. 22:29]].
- I hallowed unto me all the firstborn in Israel, [[Numbers 3.13|Num. 3:13]].

- He might be the firstborn among many brethren, [[Romans 8.29|Rom. 8:29]].
- He bringeth in the firstbegotten into the world, [[Hebrews 1.6|Heb. 1:6]].

- They are they who are the Church of the Firstborn, [[D&C 76.54|D&C 76:54, 94]][[D&C 76.94|]].

- It was conferred upon me from the fathers, even the right of the firstborn, [[Abraham 1.3|Abr. 1:3]].
