# Church, Signs of the True

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/church-signs-of-the-true?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Church of Jesus Christ|Church of Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, The|Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, The]]; [[GS Sign|Sign]]

Doctrines and works of a church that show it is approved by God and is the means the Lord has established for His children to gain the fulness of His blessings. Some of the signs of the true Church are as follows:

## Correct understanding of the Godhead

- God created man in his own image, [[Genesis 1.26|Gen. 1:26–27]][[Genesis 1.27|]].
- The Lord spoke unto Moses face to face, [[Exodus 33.11|Ex. 33:11]].

- Eternal life is to know God the Father and Jesus Christ, [[John 17.3|John 17:3]].

- The Father and Son have bodies of flesh and bones, [[D&C 130.22|D&C 130:22–23]][[D&C 130.23|]].

- The Father and the Son appeared to Joseph Smith, [[Joseph Smith History 1.15|JS—H 1:15–20]][[Joseph Smith History 1.16|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.17|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.18|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.19|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.20|]].
- We believe in God, the Eternal Father, [[Articles of Faith 1.1|A of F 1:1]].

## First principles and ordinances

- Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, [[John 3.3|John 3:3–5]][[John 3.4|]][[John 3.5|]].
- Repent and be baptized, every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ, [[Acts 2.38|Acts 2:38]].
- Then they laid their hands on them, and they received the Holy Ghost, [[Acts 8.14|Acts 8:14–17]][[Acts 8.15|]][[Acts 8.16|]][[Acts 8.17|]].
- Become children of God by faith in Jesus Christ, [[Galatians 3.26|Gal. 3:26–27]][[Galatians 3.27|]].

- Repent ye, and be baptized in the name of my Beloved Son, [[2 Nephi 31.11|2 Ne. 31:11–21]][[2 Nephi 31.12|]][[2 Nephi 31.13|]][[2 Nephi 31.14|]][[2 Nephi 31.15|]][[2 Nephi 31.16|]][[2 Nephi 31.17|]][[2 Nephi 31.18|]][[2 Nephi 31.19|]][[2 Nephi 31.20|]][[2 Nephi 31.21|]].

- They who believed were baptized and received the Holy Ghost by the laying on of hands, [[D&C 76.50|D&C 76:50–53]][[D&C 76.51|]][[D&C 76.52|]][[D&C 76.53|]].

- Proper priesthood is needed to baptize and to give the gift of the Holy Ghost, [[Joseph Smith History 1.70|JS—H 1:70–72]][[Joseph Smith History 1.71|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.72|]].
- The first principles and ordinances of the gospel are described, [[Articles of Faith 1.4|A of F 1:4]].

## Revelation

- Where there is no vision, the people perish, [[Proverbs 29.18|Prov. 29:18]].
- The Lord reveals his secrets to his prophets, [[Amos 3.7|Amos 3:7]].

- The Church is built upon the rock of revelation, [[Matthew 16.17|Matt. 16:17–18]][[Matthew 16.18|]] ([[D&C 33.13|D&C 33:13]]).

- Woe unto him who shall say the Lord no longer worketh by revelation, [[3 Nephi 29.6|3 Ne. 29:6]].

- Revelations and commandments come only through the one appointed, [[D&C 43.2|D&C 43:2–7]][[D&C 43.3|]][[D&C 43.4|]][[D&C 43.5|]][[D&C 43.6|]][[D&C 43.7|]].

- We believe all that God has revealed, [[Articles of Faith 1.9|A of F 1:9]].

## Prophets

- The Church is built upon the foundation of Apostles and prophets, [[Ephesians 2.19|Eph. 2:19–20]][[Ephesians 2.20|]].
- Apostles and prophets are essential to the Church, [[Ephesians 4.11|Eph. 4:11–16]][[Ephesians 4.12|]][[Ephesians 4.13|]][[Ephesians 4.14|]][[Ephesians 4.15|]][[Ephesians 4.16|]].

- Joseph Smith was called to be a seer, prophet, and Apostle, [[D&C 21.1|D&C 21:1–3]][[D&C 21.2|]][[D&C 21.3|]].

- We believe in prophets, [[Articles of Faith 1.6|A of F 1:6]].

## Authority

- Jesus gave his disciples power and authority, [[Luke 9.1|Luke 9:1–2]][[Luke 9.2|]] ([[John 15.16|John 15:16]]).

- Nephi, the son of Helaman, had great authority from God, [[Helaman 11.18|Hel. 11:18]] ([[3 Nephi 7.17|3 Ne. 7:17]]).

- The prophet is to receive commandments for the Church, [[D&C 21.4|D&C 21:4–5]][[D&C 21.5|]].
- No one may preach the gospel or build up the Church unless he is ordained by someone who has authority, [[D&C 42.11|D&C 42:11]].
- The elders are to preach the gospel, acting in authority, [[D&C 68.8|D&C 68:8]].

- Any who preach or administer for God must be called of God by those in authority, [[Articles of Faith 1.5|A of F 1:5]].

## Additional scriptures to come forth

- The stick of Judah will be joined with the stick of Joseph, [[Ezekiel 37.15|Ezek. 37:15–20]][[Ezekiel 37.16|]][[Ezekiel 37.17|]][[Ezekiel 37.18|]][[Ezekiel 37.19|]][[Ezekiel 37.20|]].

- The coming forth of latter-day scripture was foretold, [[1 Nephi 13.38|1 Ne. 13:38–41]][[1 Nephi 13.39|]][[1 Nephi 13.40|]][[1 Nephi 13.41|]].

- We believe that God will yet reveal many great and important things, [[Articles of Faith 1.9|A of F 1:9]].

## Church organization

- The Church is built upon the foundation of Apostles and prophets, [[Ephesians 2.19|Eph. 2:19–20]][[Ephesians 2.20|]].
- Apostles and prophets are essential to the Church, [[Ephesians 4.11|Eph. 4:11–16]][[Ephesians 4.12|]][[Ephesians 4.13|]][[Ephesians 4.14|]][[Ephesians 4.15|]][[Ephesians 4.16|]].
- Christ is the head of the Church, [[Ephesians 5.23|Eph. 5:23]].

- Christ’s Church must be called in his name, [[3 Nephi 27.8|3 Ne. 27:8]].

- We believe in the same organization that existed in the Primitive Church, [[Articles of Faith 1.6|A of F 1:6]].

## Missionary work

- Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, [[Matthew 28.19|Matt. 28:19–20]][[Matthew 28.20|]].
- Seventy were called to preach the gospel, [[Luke 10.1|Luke 10:1]].

- They were desirous that salvation should be declared to every creature, [[Mosiah 28.3|Mosiah 28:3]].

- Elders are to go forth, preaching my gospel, two by two, [[D&C 42.6|D&C 42:6]].
- The gospel must be preached unto every creature, [[D&C 58.64|D&C 58:64]].

## Temples
