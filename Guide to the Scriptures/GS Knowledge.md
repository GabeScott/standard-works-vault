# Knowledge

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/knowledge?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Truth|Truth]]; [[GS Understanding|Understanding]]; [[GS Wisdom|Wisdom]]

Understanding and comprehension, especially of truth as taught or confirmed by the Spirit.

- The Lord is a God of knowledge, [[1 Samuel 2.3|1 Sam. 2:3]].
- The Lord is perfect in knowledge, [[Job 37.16|Job 37:16]].
- The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge, [[Proverbs 1.7|Prov. 1:7]].
- He that has knowledge restrains his words, [[Proverbs 17.27|Prov. 17:27]].
- The earth shall be full of the knowledge of the Lord, [[Isaiah 11.9|Isa. 11:9]] ([[2 Nephi 21.9|2 Ne. 21:9]]; [[2 Nephi 30.15|30:15]]).

- Ye have taken away the key of knowledge, [[Luke 11.52|Luke 11:52]].
- The love of Christ passes knowledge, [[Ephesians 3.19|Eph. 3:19]].
- Add to your faith virtue, and to virtue knowledge, [[2 Peter 1.5|2 Pet. 1:5]].

- Nephi had a great knowledge of the goodness of God, [[1 Nephi 1.1|1 Ne. 1:1]].
- They shall come to the knowledge of their Redeemer, [[2 Nephi 6.11|2 Ne. 6:11]].
- The righteous shall have a perfect knowledge of their righteousness, [[2 Nephi 9.14|2 Ne. 9:14]].
- The Spirit gives knowledge, [[Alma 18.35|Alma 18:35]].
- Your knowledge is perfect in that thing, [[Alma 32.34|Alma 32:34]].
- The Lamanites shall be brought to the true knowledge of their Redeemer, [[Helaman 15.13|Hel. 15:13]].
- Ye may know with a perfect knowledge it is of God, [[Moroni 7.15|Moro. 7:15–17]][[Moroni 7.16|]][[Moroni 7.17|]].

- The Saints shall find great treasures of knowledge, [[D&C 89.19|D&C 89:19]].
- Pure knowledge greatly enlarges the soul, [[D&C 121.42|D&C 121:42]].
- He who has the keys of the holy priesthood has no difficulty in obtaining a knowledge of facts, [[D&C 128.11|D&C 128:11]].
- If a person gains knowledge in this life, he has an advantage in the world to come, [[D&C 130.19|D&C 130:19]].
- It is impossible to be saved in ignorance, [[D&C 131.6|D&C 131:6]].
