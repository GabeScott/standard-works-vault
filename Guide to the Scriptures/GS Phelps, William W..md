# Phelps, William W.

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/phelps-william-w?lang=eng)

An early member and leader in the Church after it was restored in 1830. The Lord called William Phelps to be a printer for the Church ([[D&C 57.11|D&C 57:11]]; [[D&C 58.40|58:40]]; [[D&C 70.1|70:1]]).
