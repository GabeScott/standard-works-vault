# Seth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/seth?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adam|Adam]]

In the Old Testament, a righteous son of Adam and Eve.

- Seth was a perfect man, and his likeness was the express likeness of his father, [[D&C 107.42|D&C 107:42–43]][[D&C 107.43|]] ([[Genesis 5.3|Gen. 5:3]]).
- Seth was among the mighty ones in the spirit world, [[D&C 138.40|D&C 138:40]].

- God revealed himself unto Seth, [[Moses 6.1|Moses 6:1–3, 8–14]][[Moses 6.2|]][[Moses 6.3|]][[Moses 6.8|]][[Moses 6.9|]][[Moses 6.10|]][[Moses 6.11|]][[Moses 6.12|]][[Moses 6.13|]][[Moses 6.14|]].
