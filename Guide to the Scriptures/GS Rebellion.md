# Rebellion

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/rebellion?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Apostasy|Apostasy]]; [[GS Devil|Devil]]; [[GS Murmur|Murmur]]; [[GS Sin|Sin]]

Defying or opposing the Lord, including refusing to follow His chosen leaders and willfully disobeying His commandments.

- Rebel not ye against the Lord, [[Numbers 14.9|Num. 14:9]].
- An evil man seeketh only rebellion, [[Proverbs 17.11|Prov. 17:11]].
- Woe to the rebellious children, [[Isaiah 30.1|Isa. 30:1]].

- The Lord redeemeth none such that rebel against him and die in their sins, [[Mosiah 15.26|Mosiah 15:26]].
- The Amlicites came out in open rebellion against God, [[Alma 3.18|Alma 3:18–19]][[Alma 3.19|]].

- The rebellious shall be pierced with much sorrow, [[D&C 1.3|D&C 1:3]].
- The Lord’s anger is kindled against the rebellious, [[D&C 56.1|D&C 56:1]] ([[D&C 63.1|D&C 63:1–6]][[D&C 63.2|]][[D&C 63.3|]][[D&C 63.4|]][[D&C 63.5|]][[D&C 63.6|]]).

- Satan rebelled against God, [[Moses 4.3|Moses 4:3]].
