# Gideon (Book of Mormon)

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/gideon-book-of-mormon?lang=eng)

A faithful Nephite leader.

- Was a strong man and an enemy to King Noah, [[Mosiah 19.4|Mosiah 19:4–8]][[Mosiah 19.5|]][[Mosiah 19.6|]][[Mosiah 19.7|]][[Mosiah 19.8|]].
- Counseled with King Limhi, [[Mosiah 20.17|Mosiah 20:17–22]][[Mosiah 20.18|]][[Mosiah 20.19|]][[Mosiah 20.20|]][[Mosiah 20.21|]][[Mosiah 20.22|]].
- Proposed a plan for escaping from Lamanite bondage, [[Mosiah 22.3|Mosiah 22:3–9]][[Mosiah 22.4|]][[Mosiah 22.5|]][[Mosiah 22.6|]][[Mosiah 22.7|]][[Mosiah 22.8|]][[Mosiah 22.9|]].
- Was killed by Nehor, [[Alma 1.8|Alma 1:8–10]][[Alma 1.9|]][[Alma 1.10|]].
