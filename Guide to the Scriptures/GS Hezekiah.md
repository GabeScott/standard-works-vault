# Hezekiah

Isaiah assisted him in reforming both church and state. He suppressed idolatry and reinstated the temple services. Hezekiah’s life was extended fifteen years through prayer and faith ([[2 Kings 20.1|2 Kgs. 20:1–7]][[2 Kings 20.2|]][[2 Kings 20.3|]][[2 Kings 20.4|]][[2 Kings 20.5|]][[2 Kings 20.6|]][[2 Kings 20.7|]]).
