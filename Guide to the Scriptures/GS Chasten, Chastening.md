# Chasten, Chastening

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/chasten-chastening?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adversity|Adversity]]

Correction or discipline given to individuals or groups in order to help them improve or become stronger.

- Despise not the chastening hand of the Almighty, [[Job 5.17|Job 5:17]] ([[Proverbs 3.11|Prov. 3:11]]).
- Blessed is the man whom thou chastenest, O Lord, [[Psalms 94.12|Ps. 94:12]].

- All scripture is given for reproof, for correction, [[2 Timothy 3.16|2 Tim. 3:16]].
- The Lord chastens those whom he loves, [[Hebrews 12.5|Heb. 12:5–11]][[Hebrews 12.6|]][[Hebrews 12.7|]][[Hebrews 12.8|]][[Hebrews 12.9|]][[Hebrews 12.10|]][[Hebrews 12.11|]].

- The Lord sees fit to chasten his people, [[Mosiah 23.21|Mosiah 23:21–22]][[Mosiah 23.22|]].
- Except the Lord chasten his people, they will not remember him, [[Helaman 12.3|Hel. 12:3]].
- The Lord did talk with the brother of Jared, and chastened him, [[Ether 2.14|Ether 2:14]].

- They were chastened that they might repent, [[D&C 1.27|D&C 1:27]].
- Whom I love I also chasten that their sins may be forgiven, [[D&C 95.1|D&C 95:1]].
- All those who will not endure chastening cannot be sanctified, [[D&C 101.2|D&C 101:2–5]][[D&C 101.3|]][[D&C 101.4|]][[D&C 101.5|]].
- My people must needs be chastened until they learn obedience, [[D&C 105.6|D&C 105:6]].
- He that will not bear chastisement is not worthy of my kingdom, [[D&C 136.31|D&C 136:31]].
