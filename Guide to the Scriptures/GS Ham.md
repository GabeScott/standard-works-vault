# Ham

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/ham?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Noah, Bible Patriarch|Noah, Bible Patriarch]]

In the Old Testament, the third son of Noah ([[Genesis 5.32|Gen. 5:32]]; [[Genesis 6.10|6:10]]; [[Moses 8.12|Moses 8:12, 27]][[Moses 8.27|]]).

- Noah, his sons, and their families entered the ark, [[Genesis 7.13|Gen. 7:13]].
- Canaan, Ham’s son, was cursed, [[Genesis 9.18|Gen. 9:18–25]][[Genesis 9.19|]][[Genesis 9.20|]][[Genesis 9.21|]][[Genesis 9.22|]][[Genesis 9.23|]][[Genesis 9.24|]][[Genesis 9.25|]].

- The government of Ham was patriarchal and was blessed as to things of the earth and wisdom but not as to the priesthood, [[Abraham 1.21|Abr. 1:21–27]][[Abraham 1.22|]][[Abraham 1.23|]][[Abraham 1.24|]][[Abraham 1.25|]][[Abraham 1.26|]][[Abraham 1.27|]].
- Ham’s wife, Egyptus, was a descendant of Cain; the sons of their daughter Egyptus settled in Egypt, [[Abraham 1.23|Abr. 1:23, 25]][[Abraham 1.25|]] ([[Psalms 105.23|Ps. 105:23]]; [[Psalms 106.21|106:21–22]][[Psalms 106.22|]]).
