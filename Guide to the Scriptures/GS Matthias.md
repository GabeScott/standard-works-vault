# Matthias

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/matthias?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Apostle|Apostle—Selection of Apostles]]

The person chosen to replace Judas Iscariot as a member of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles ([[Acts 1.15|Acts 1:15–26]][[Acts 1.16|]][[Acts 1.17|]][[Acts 1.18|]][[Acts 1.19|]][[Acts 1.20|]][[Acts 1.21|]][[Acts 1.22|]][[Acts 1.23|]][[Acts 1.24|]][[Acts 1.25|]][[Acts 1.26|]]). He was a disciple throughout the whole of Jesus’ mortal ministry ([[Acts 1.21|Acts 1:21–22]][[Acts 1.22|]]).
