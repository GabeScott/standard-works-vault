# Widow

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/widow?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Welfare|Welfare]]

A woman whose husband has died and who has not remarried.

- The fatherless and the widow shall come and shall eat, [[Deuteronomy 14.29|Deut. 14:29]].

- This poor widow has cast all that she had, [[Mark 12.41|Mark 12:41–44]][[Mark 12.42|]][[Mark 12.43|]][[Mark 12.44|]].
- Visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, [[James 1.27|James 1:27]].

- The Lord will be a swift witness against those that oppress the widow, [[3 Nephi 24.5|3 Ne. 24:5]] ([[Zechariah 7.10|Zech. 7:10]]).

- Widows and orphans shall be provided for, [[D&C 83.6|D&C 83:6]] ([[D&C 136.8|D&C 136:8]]).
