# Wicked, Wickedness

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/wicked-wickedness?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Darkness, Spiritual|Darkness, Spiritual]]; [[GS Filth, Filthiness|Filth, Filthiness]]; [[GS Sin|Sin]]; [[GS Ungodly|Ungodly]]; [[GS Unrighteous, Unrighteousness|Unrighteous, Unrighteousness]]

Evil, iniquity; to be disobedient to God’s commandments.

- How then can I do this great wickedness, and sin against God, [[Genesis 39.7|Gen. 39:7–9]][[Genesis 39.8|]][[Genesis 39.9|]].
- The Lord is far from the wicked: but he heareth the prayer of the righteous, [[Proverbs 15.29|Prov. 15:29]].
- When the wicked rule, the people mourn, [[Proverbs 29.2|Prov. 29:2]] ([[D&C 98.9|D&C 98:9]]).

- Put away from among yourselves that wicked person, [[1 Corinthians 5.13|1 Cor. 5:13]].
- We wrestle against spiritual wickedness in high places, [[Ephesians 6.12|Eph. 6:12]].

- Come ye out from the wicked, and touch not their unclean things, [[Alma 5.56|Alma 5:56–57]][[Alma 5.57|]] ([[D&C 38.42|D&C 38:42]]).
- This is the final state of the wicked, [[Alma 34.35|Alma 34:35]] ([[Alma 40.13|Alma 40:13–14]][[Alma 40.14|]]).
- Wickedness never was happiness, [[Alma 41.10|Alma 41:10]].
- It is by the wicked that the wicked are punished, [[Mormon 4.5|Morm. 4:5]] ([[D&C 63.33|D&C 63:33]]).

- At that hour cometh an entire separation of the righteous and the wicked, [[D&C 63.54|D&C 63:54]].

- Thus cometh the end of the wicked, [[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.55|JS—M 1:55]].
