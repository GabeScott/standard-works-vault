# Levi

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/levi?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Israel|Israel]]; [[GS Jacob, Son of Isaac|Jacob, Son of Isaac]]

In the Old Testament, the third son of Jacob and Leah ([[Genesis 29.34|Gen. 29:34]]; [[Genesis 35.23|35:23]]). Levi became the father of one of the tribes of Israel.

## The tribe of Levi

Jacob blessed Levi and his descendants ([[Genesis 49.5|Gen. 49:5–7, 28]][[Genesis 49.6|]][[Genesis 49.7|]][[Genesis 49.28|]]). Descendants of Levi ministered in the sanctuaries of Israel ([[Numbers 1.47|Num. 1:47–54]][[Numbers 1.48|]][[Numbers 1.49|]][[Numbers 1.50|]][[Numbers 1.51|]][[Numbers 1.52|]][[Numbers 1.53|]][[Numbers 1.54|]]). Aaron was a Levite, and his descendants were priests ([[Exodus 6.16|Ex. 6:16–20]][[Exodus 6.17|]][[Exodus 6.18|]][[Exodus 6.19|]][[Exodus 6.20|]]; [[Exodus 28.1|28:1–4]][[Exodus 28.2|]][[Exodus 28.3|]][[Exodus 28.4|]]; [[Exodus 29.1|29]][[Exodus 29.2|]][[Exodus 29.3|]][[Exodus 29.4|]][[Exodus 29.5|]][[Exodus 29.6|]][[Exodus 29.7|]][[Exodus 29.8|]][[Exodus 29.9|]][[Exodus 29.10|]][[Exodus 29.11|]][[Exodus 29.12|]][[Exodus 29.13|]][[Exodus 29.14|]][[Exodus 29.15|]][[Exodus 29.16|]][[Exodus 29.17|]][[Exodus 29.18|]][[Exodus 29.19|]][[Exodus 29.20|]][[Exodus 29.21|]][[Exodus 29.22|]][[Exodus 29.23|]][[Exodus 29.24|]][[Exodus 29.25|]][[Exodus 29.26|]][[Exodus 29.27|]][[Exodus 29.28|]][[Exodus 29.29|]][[Exodus 29.30|]][[Exodus 29.31|]][[Exodus 29.32|]][[Exodus 29.33|]][[Exodus 29.34|]][[Exodus 29.35|]][[Exodus 29.36|]][[Exodus 29.37|]][[Exodus 29.38|]][[Exodus 29.39|]][[Exodus 29.40|]][[Exodus 29.41|]][[Exodus 29.42|]][[Exodus 29.43|]][[Exodus 29.44|]][[Exodus 29.45|]][[Exodus 29.46|]]). The Levites assisted the priests, the sons of Aaron ([[Numbers 3.5|Num. 3:5–10]][[Numbers 3.6|]][[Numbers 3.7|]][[Numbers 3.8|]][[Numbers 3.9|]][[Numbers 3.10|]]; [[1 Kings 8.4|1 Kgs. 8:4]]). They sometimes acted as musicians ([[1 Chronicles 15.16|1 Chr. 15:16]]; [[Nehemiah 11.22|Neh. 11:22]]); slaughtered sacrifices ([[2 Chronicles 29.34|2 Chr. 29:34]]; [[Ezra 6.20|Ezra 6:20]]); and generally assisted in the temple ([[Nehemiah 11.16|Neh. 11:16]]). The Levites were dedicated to the service of the Lord to execute the ordinances for the children of Israel. The Levites were themselves offered on behalf of the children of Israel ([[Numbers 8.11|Num. 8:11–22]][[Numbers 8.12|]][[Numbers 8.13|]][[Numbers 8.14|]][[Numbers 8.15|]][[Numbers 8.16|]][[Numbers 8.17|]][[Numbers 8.18|]][[Numbers 8.19|]][[Numbers 8.20|]][[Numbers 8.21|]][[Numbers 8.22|]]); they thus became God’s peculiar property, given to Him in place of the firstborn ([[Numbers 8.16|Num. 8:16]]). They were not consecrated but were cleansed for their office ([[Numbers 8.7|Num. 8:7–16]][[Numbers 8.8|]][[Numbers 8.9|]][[Numbers 8.10|]][[Numbers 8.11|]][[Numbers 8.12|]][[Numbers 8.13|]][[Numbers 8.14|]][[Numbers 8.15|]][[Numbers 8.16|]]). They had no land inheritance in Canaan ([[Numbers 18.23|Num. 18:23–24]][[Numbers 18.24|]]), but they received the tithe ([[Numbers 18.21|Num. 18:21]]), forty-eight cities ([[Numbers 35.6|Num. 35:6]]), and a right to receive the alms of the people at feast times ([[Deuteronomy 12.18|Deut. 12:18–19]][[Deuteronomy 12.19|]]; [[Deuteronomy 14.27|14:27–29]][[Deuteronomy 14.28|]][[Deuteronomy 14.29|]]).
