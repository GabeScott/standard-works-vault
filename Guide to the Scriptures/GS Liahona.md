# Liahona

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/liahona?lang=eng)

In the Book of Mormon, a brass ball with two pointers that gave directions—as a compass—and also spiritual instruction to Lehi and his followers when they were righteous. The Lord provided the Liahona and gave instructions through it.

- Lehi found a brass ball with two spindles that pointed the way that Lehi and his family should go, [[1 Nephi 16.10|1 Ne. 16:10]].
- The ball worked according to faith and diligence, [[1 Nephi 16.28|1 Ne. 16:28–29]][[1 Nephi 16.29|]] ([[Alma 37.40|Alma 37:40]]).
- Benjamin gave the ball to Mosiah, [[Mosiah 1.16|Mosiah 1:16]].
- The ball or director was called the Liahona, [[Alma 37.38|Alma 37:38]].
- The Liahona was compared to the word of Christ, [[Alma 37.43|Alma 37:43–45]][[Alma 37.44|]][[Alma 37.45|]].

- The Three Witnesses of the Book of Mormon are to see the director given to Lehi, [[D&C 17.1|D&C 17:1]].
