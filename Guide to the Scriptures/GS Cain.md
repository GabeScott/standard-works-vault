# Cain

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/cain?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Abel|Abel]]; [[GS Adam|Adam]]; [[GS Murder|Murder]]; [[GS Secret Combinations|Secret Combinations]]

A son of Adam and Eve who slew his younger brother Abel ([[Genesis 4.1|Gen. 4:1–16]][[Genesis 4.2|]][[Genesis 4.3|]][[Genesis 4.4|]][[Genesis 4.5|]][[Genesis 4.6|]][[Genesis 4.7|]][[Genesis 4.8|]][[Genesis 4.9|]][[Genesis 4.10|]][[Genesis 4.11|]][[Genesis 4.12|]][[Genesis 4.13|]][[Genesis 4.14|]][[Genesis 4.15|]][[Genesis 4.16|]]).

- His offering was rejected by the Lord, [[Genesis 4.3|Gen. 4:3–7]][[Genesis 4.4|]][[Genesis 4.5|]][[Genesis 4.6|]][[Genesis 4.7|]] ([[Moses 5.5|Moses 5:5–8, 18–26]][[Moses 5.6|]][[Moses 5.7|]][[Moses 5.8|]][[Moses 5.18|]][[Moses 5.19|]][[Moses 5.20|]][[Moses 5.21|]][[Moses 5.22|]][[Moses 5.23|]][[Moses 5.24|]][[Moses 5.25|]][[Moses 5.26|]]).
- Killed his brother Abel, [[Genesis 4.8|Gen. 4:8–14]][[Genesis 4.9|]][[Genesis 4.10|]][[Genesis 4.11|]][[Genesis 4.12|]][[Genesis 4.13|]][[Genesis 4.14|]] ([[Moses 5.32|Moses 5:32–37]][[Moses 5.33|]][[Moses 5.34|]][[Moses 5.35|]][[Moses 5.36|]][[Moses 5.37|]]).
- The Lord set a curse and a mark upon him, [[Genesis 4.15|Gen. 4:15]] ([[Moses 5.37|Moses 5:37–41]][[Moses 5.38|]][[Moses 5.39|]][[Moses 5.40|]][[Moses 5.41|]]).

- Adam and Eve had many sons and daughters before he was born, [[Moses 5.1|Moses 5:1–3, 16–17]][[Moses 5.2|]][[Moses 5.3|]][[Moses 5.16|]][[Moses 5.17|]].
- Loved Satan more than God, [[Moses 5.13|Moses 5:13, 18]][[Moses 5.18|]].
- Entered into an unholy covenant with Satan, [[Moses 5.29|Moses 5:29–31]][[Moses 5.30|]][[Moses 5.31|]].
