# Justice

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/justice?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Merciful, Mercy|Merciful, Mercy]]

The unfailing consequence of blessings for righteous thoughts and acts, and punishment for unrepented sin. Justice is an eternal law that requires a penalty each time a law of God is broken ([[Alma 42.13|Alma 42:13–24]][[Alma 42.14|]][[Alma 42.15|]][[Alma 42.16|]][[Alma 42.17|]][[Alma 42.18|]][[Alma 42.19|]][[Alma 42.20|]][[Alma 42.21|]][[Alma 42.22|]][[Alma 42.23|]][[Alma 42.24|]]). The sinner must pay the penalty if he does not repent ([[Mosiah 2.38|Mosiah 2:38–39]][[Mosiah 2.39|]]; [[D&C 19.17|D&C 19:17]]). If he does repent, the Savior pays the penalty through the Atonement, invoking mercy ([[Alma 34.16|Alma 34:16]]).

- The soul that sinneth shall die, [[Ezekiel 18.4|Ezek. 18:4]].
- What doth the Lord require of thee, but to do justly, [[Micah 6.8|Micah 6:8]].

- Jesus will be just and forgive our sins, [[1 John 1.9|1 Jn. 1:9]].

- The justice of God did divide the wicked from the righteous, [[1 Nephi 15.30|1 Ne. 15:30]].
- The Atonement satisfies the demands of his justice, [[2 Nephi 9.26|2 Ne. 9:26]].
- All mankind is fallen and is in the grasp of justice, [[Alma 42.14|Alma 42:14]].
- The Atonement appeases the demands of justice, [[Alma 42.15|Alma 42:15]].
- Do ye suppose that mercy can rob justice, [[Alma 42.25|Alma 42:25]].
- The justice of God hangs over you except you repent, [[Alma 54.6|Alma 54:6]].

- Justice and judgment are the penalty that is affixed to my law, [[D&C 82.4|D&C 82:4]].
- Justice continues in its course and claims its own, [[D&C 88.40|D&C 88:40]].
- None shall be exempted from the justice and the laws of God, [[D&C 107.84|D&C 107:84]].
