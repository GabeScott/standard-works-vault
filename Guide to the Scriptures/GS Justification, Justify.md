# Justification, Justify

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/justification-justify?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Sanctification|Sanctification]]

To be pardoned from punishment for sin and declared guiltless. A person is justified by the Savior’s grace through faith in Him. This faith is shown by repentance and obedience to the laws and ordinances of the gospel. Jesus Christ’s Atonement enables mankind to repent and be justified or pardoned from punishment they otherwise would receive.

- In the Lord shall all Israel be justified, [[Isaiah 45.25|Isa. 45:25]].

- Not the hearers but the doers of the law shall be justified, [[Romans 2.13|Rom. 2:13]].
- Man is justified through the blood of Christ, [[Romans 5.1|Rom. 5:1–2, 9]][[Romans 5.2|]][[Romans 5.9|]].
- Ye are justified in the name of the Lord Jesus, [[1 Corinthians 6.11|1 Cor. 6:11]].
- Being justified by his grace, we shall be made heirs, [[Titus 3.7|Titus 3:7]].
- Was not Abraham our father justified by works, [[James 2.21|James 2:21]].
- By works a man is justified, and not by faith only, [[James 2.14|James 2:14–26]][[James 2.15|]][[James 2.16|]][[James 2.17|]][[James 2.18|]][[James 2.19|]][[James 2.20|]][[James 2.21|]][[James 2.22|]][[James 2.23|]][[James 2.24|]][[James 2.25|]][[James 2.26|]].

- By the law no flesh is justified, [[2 Nephi 2.5|2 Ne. 2:5]].
- My righteous servant shall justify many, for he shall bear their iniquities, [[Mosiah 14.11|Mosiah 14:11]] ([[Isaiah 53.11|Isa. 53:11]]).
- Could ye say that your garments have been cleansed through Christ, [[Alma 5.27|Alma 5:27]].

- Justification through the grace of Jesus Christ is true, [[D&C 20.30|D&C 20:30–31]][[D&C 20.31|]] ([[D&C 88.39|D&C 88:39]]).

- By the Spirit ye are justified, [[Moses 6.60|Moses 6:60]].
