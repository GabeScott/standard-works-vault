# Contention

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/contention?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Rebellion|Rebellion]]

Strife, arguing, and disputations. Contention, especially between members of the Lord’s Church or between family members, is not pleasing to the Lord.

- Let there be no strife between me and thee, [[Genesis 13.8|Gen. 13:8]].
- Pride causes contention, [[Proverbs 13.10|Prov. 13:10]].

- If any man have a quarrel against any, forgive as Christ forgave, [[Colossians 3.13|Col. 3:13]].
- Avoid foolish questions and contention, [[Titus 3.9|Titus 3:9]].

- The Lord commands men not to contend with one another, [[2 Nephi 26.32|2 Ne. 26:32]].
- Ye will not suffer your children to fight and quarrel with one another, [[Mosiah 4.14|Mosiah 4:14]].
- Alma commanded that there be no contention among Church members, [[Mosiah 18.21|Mosiah 18:21]].
- Satan spreads rumors and contention, [[Helaman 16.22|Hel. 16:22]].
- The devil is the father of contention and stirs up men to contend with one another, [[3 Nephi 11.29|3 Ne. 11:29]] ([[Mosiah 23.15|Mosiah 23:15]]).

- Establish my gospel, that there may not be so much contention, [[D&C 10.62|D&C 10:62–64]][[D&C 10.63|]][[D&C 10.64|]].
- Cease to contend with one another, [[D&C 136.23|D&C 136:23]].
