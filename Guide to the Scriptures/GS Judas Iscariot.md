# Judas Iscariot

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/judas-iscariot?lang=eng)

One of Jesus’ Twelve Apostles in the New Testament ([[Matthew 10.4|Matt. 10:4]]; [[Mark 14.10|Mark 14:10]]; [[John 6.71|John 6:71]]; [[John 12.4|12:4]]). His surname meant “man of Kerioth.” He was from the tribe of Judah and was the only Apostle who was not a Galilean. Judas betrayed the Lord.

- Received thirty pieces of silver for delivering Christ to one of the chief priests, [[Matthew 26.14|Matt. 26:14–16]][[Matthew 26.15|]][[Matthew 26.16|]] ([[Zechariah 11.12|Zech. 11:12–13]][[Zechariah 11.13|]]).
- Betrayed the Lord with a kiss, [[Matthew 26.47|Matt. 26:47–50]][[Matthew 26.48|]][[Matthew 26.49|]][[Matthew 26.50|]] ([[Mark 14.43|Mark 14:43–45]][[Mark 14.44|]][[Mark 14.45|]]; [[Luke 22.47|Luke 22:47–48]][[Luke 22.48|]]; [[John 18.2|John 18:2–5]][[John 18.3|]][[John 18.4|]][[John 18.5|]]).
- Hanged himself, [[Matthew 27.5|Matt. 27:5]].
- Satan entered into Judas, [[Luke 22.3|Luke 22:3]] ([[John 13.2|John 13:2, 26–30]][[John 13.26|]][[John 13.27|]][[John 13.28|]][[John 13.29|]][[John 13.30|]]).
- David spoke of Judas’s betrayal of Jesus, [[Acts 1.16|Acts 1:16]] ([[Psalms 41.9|Ps. 41:9]]).
