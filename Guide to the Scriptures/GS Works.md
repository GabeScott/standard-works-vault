# Works

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/works?lang=eng)

A person’s actions, whether good or bad. Each person will be judged by his own works.

- The Lord will render to every man according to his works, [[Proverbs 24.12|Prov. 24:12]].

- Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, [[Matthew 5.16|Matt. 5:16]] ([[3 Nephi 12.16|3 Ne. 12:16]]).
- He that doeth the will of my Father shall enter into the kingdom of heaven, [[Matthew 7.21|Matt. 7:21]].
- Faith without works is dead, [[James 2.14|James 2:14–26]][[James 2.15|]][[James 2.16|]][[James 2.17|]][[James 2.18|]][[James 2.19|]][[James 2.20|]][[James 2.21|]][[James 2.22|]][[James 2.23|]][[James 2.24|]][[James 2.25|]][[James 2.26|]].

- They must be judged of their works, [[1 Nephi 15.32|1 Ne. 15:32]] ([[Mosiah 3.24|Mosiah 3:24]]).
- We know that it is by grace that we are saved, after all we can do, [[2 Nephi 25.23|2 Ne. 25:23]].
- Teach them to never be weary of good works, [[Alma 37.34|Alma 37:34]].
- It is requisite that men should be judged according to their works, [[Alma 41.3|Alma 41:3]].
- By their works ye shall know them, [[Moroni 7.5|Moro. 7:5]] ([[D&C 18.38|D&C 18:38]]).

- I, the Lord, will judge all men according to their works, [[D&C 137.9|D&C 137:9]].
