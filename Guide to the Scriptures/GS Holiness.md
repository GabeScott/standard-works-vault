# Holiness

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/holiness?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Holy|Holy]]; [[GS Pure, Purity|Pure, Purity]]; [[GS Sanctification|Sanctification]]

Spiritual and moral perfection. Holiness indicates purity of a person’s heart and intent.

- The members are to manifest that they are worthy of the Church by walking in holiness before the Lord, [[D&C 20.69|D&C 20:69]].
- The Lord’s house is a place of holiness, [[D&C 109.13|D&C 109:13]].

- Man of Holiness is one of God’s names, [[Moses 6.57|Moses 6:57]] ([[Moses 7.35|Moses 7:35]]).
