# Lamb of God

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/lamb-of-god?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Passover|Passover]]

A name for the Savior that refers to Jesus as the sacrificial offering given in our behalf.

- He is brought as a lamb to the slaughter, [[Isaiah 53.7|Isa. 53:7]] ([[Mosiah 14.7|Mosiah 14:7]]).

- Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world, [[John 1.29|John 1:29]] ([[Alma 7.14|Alma 7:14]]).
- Ye were redeemed with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish, [[1 Peter 1.18|1 Pet. 1:18–20]][[1 Peter 1.19|]][[1 Peter 1.20|]].
- Worthy is the Lamb that was slain, [[Revelation 5.12|Rev. 5:12]].
- We overcome Satan by the blood of the Lamb, [[Revelation 12.11|Rev. 12:11]].

- These are made white in the blood of the Lamb, because of their faith in him, [[1 Nephi 12.11|1 Ne. 12:11]].
- The Lamb of God is the Son of the Eternal Father, and the Savior of the world, [[1 Nephi 13.40|1 Ne. 13:40]] ([[1 Nephi 11.21|1 Ne. 11:21]]).
- Cry mightily unto the Father in the name of Jesus, that perhaps ye may be cleansed by the blood of the Lamb, [[Mormon 9.6|Morm. 9:6]] ([[Revelation 7.14|Rev. 7:14]]; [[Alma 34.36|Alma 34:36]]).

- The Son of Man is the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world, [[Moses 7.47|Moses 7:47]].
