# Christians

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/christians?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Disciple|Disciple]]; [[GS Saint|Saint]]

A name given to believers in Jesus Christ. Although this term is commonly used throughout the world, the Lord has designated true followers of Christ as Saints ([[Acts 9.13|Acts 9:13, 32, 41]][[Acts 9.32|]][[Acts 9.41|]]; [[1 Corinthians 1.2|1 Cor. 1:2]]; [[D&C 115.4|D&C 115:4]]).

- The disciples were called Christians, [[Acts 11.26|Acts 11:26]].
- If any man suffer as a Christian, let him not be ashamed, [[1 Peter 4.16|1 Pet. 4:16]].

- Because of the covenant ye shall be called the children of Christ, [[Mosiah 5.7|Mosiah 5:7]].
- True believers were called Christians by those who were not members of the Church, [[Alma 46.13|Alma 46:13–16]][[Alma 46.14|]][[Alma 46.15|]][[Alma 46.16|]].
