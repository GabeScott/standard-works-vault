# Alpha and Omega

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/alpha-and-omega?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

Alpha is the first letter in the Greek alphabet; Omega is the last. They are also names given to Jesus Christ and are used as symbols to show that Christ is both the beginning and the end ([[Revelation 1.8|Rev. 1:8]]; [[D&C 19.1|D&C 19:1]]).
