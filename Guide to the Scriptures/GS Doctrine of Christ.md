# Doctrine of Christ

The principles and teachings of the gospel of Jesus Christ.

- My doctrine shall drop as the rain, [[Deuteronomy 32.2|Deut. 32:2]].
- They that murmured shall learn doctrine, [[Isaiah 29.24|Isa. 29:24]].

- The people were astonished at his doctrine, [[Matthew 7.28|Matt. 7:28]].
- My doctrine is not mine, but his that sent me, [[John 7.16|John 7:16]].
- All scripture is profitable for doctrine, [[2 Timothy 3.16|2 Tim. 3:16]].

- This is the doctrine of Christ, and the only and true doctrine of the Father, [[2 Nephi 31.21|2 Ne. 31:21]] ([[2 Nephi 32.6|2 Ne. 32:6]]).
- There shall be no disputations among you concerning the points of my doctrine, [[3 Nephi 11.28|3 Ne. 11:28, 32, 35, 39–40]][[3 Nephi 11.32|]][[3 Nephi 11.35|]][[3 Nephi 11.39|]][[3 Nephi 11.40|]].

- Satan stirs up the hearts of the people to contention concerning the points of my doctrine, [[D&C 10.62|D&C 10:62–63, 67]][[D&C 10.63|]][[D&C 10.67|]].
- Teach children the doctrine of repentance, faith in Christ, baptism, and the gift of the Holy Ghost, [[D&C 68.25|D&C 68:25]].
- Teach one another the doctrine of the kingdom, [[D&C 88.77|D&C 88:77–78]][[D&C 88.78|]].
- The doctrine of the priesthood shall distil upon thy soul, [[D&C 121.45|D&C 121:45]].
