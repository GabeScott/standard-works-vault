# Anti-Nephi-Lehies

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/anti-nephi-lehies?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Ammon, Son of Mosiah|Ammon, Son of Mosiah]]; [[GS Helaman, Sons of|Helaman, Sons of]]; [[GS Mosiah, Sons of|Mosiah, Sons of]]

In the Book of Mormon, a name given to the Lamanites who were converted by the sons of Mosiah. After their conversion, these people, who were also called the people of Ammon, were faithful throughout their lives ([[Alma 23.4|Alma 23:4–7, 16–17]][[Alma 23.5|]][[Alma 23.6|]][[Alma 23.7|]][[Alma 23.16|]][[Alma 23.17|]]; [[Alma 27.20|27:20–27]][[Alma 27.21|]][[Alma 27.22|]][[Alma 27.23|]][[Alma 27.24|]][[Alma 27.25|]][[Alma 27.26|]][[Alma 27.27|]]).

- They took the name of Anti-Nephi-Lehies, [[Alma 23.16|Alma 23:16–17]][[Alma 23.17|]]; [[Alma 24.1|24:1]].
- They refused to shed blood and buried their weapons, [[Alma 24.6|Alma 24:6–19]][[Alma 24.7|]][[Alma 24.8|]][[Alma 24.9|]][[Alma 24.10|]][[Alma 24.11|]][[Alma 24.12|]][[Alma 24.13|]][[Alma 24.14|]][[Alma 24.15|]][[Alma 24.16|]][[Alma 24.17|]][[Alma 24.18|]][[Alma 24.19|]].
