# Counsel (noun)

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/counsel-noun?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Prophet|Prophet]]

Admonitions, warnings, advice, and instruction from the Lord and His ordained leaders.

- I will give thee counsel, [[Exodus 18.19|Ex. 18:19]].
- God will guide me with his counsel, [[Psalms 73.24|Ps. 73:24]].
- When no counsel is given, the people fall, [[Proverbs 11.14|Prov. 11:14]].

- The Pharisees and lawyers rejected the counsel of God, [[Luke 7.30|Luke 7:30]].

- To be learned is good if they hearken unto the counsel of God, [[2 Nephi 9.29|2 Ne. 9:29]].

- Listen to the counsel of him who has ordained you, [[D&C 78.2|D&C 78:2]].
- Receive counsel of him whom I have appointed, [[D&C 108.1|D&C 108:1]].
- He aspired to establish his counsel instead of the counsel which I have ordained, [[D&C 124.84|D&C 124:84]].
- Hearken to the counsel of my servant Joseph, [[D&C 124.89|D&C 124:89]].
- If any man seeks not my counsel, he shall have no power, [[D&C 136.19|D&C 136:19]].
