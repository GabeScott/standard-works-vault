# Cherubim

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/cherubim?lang=eng)

Figures representing heavenly creatures, the exact form being unknown. Cherubim have been called to guard sacred places.

- The Lord placed cherubim to keep the way of the tree of life, [[Genesis 3.24|Gen. 3:24]] ([[Alma 12.21|Alma 12:21–29]][[Alma 12.22|]][[Alma 12.23|]][[Alma 12.24|]][[Alma 12.25|]][[Alma 12.26|]][[Alma 12.27|]][[Alma 12.28|]][[Alma 12.29|]]; [[Alma 42.2|42:2–3]][[Alma 42.3|]]; [[Moses 4.31|Moses 4:31]]).
- Symbolic representations of cherubim were placed on the mercy seat, [[Exodus 25.18|Ex. 25:18, 22]][[Exodus 25.22|]] ([[1 Kings 6.23|1 Kgs. 6:23–28]][[1 Kings 6.24|]][[1 Kings 6.25|]][[1 Kings 6.26|]][[1 Kings 6.27|]][[1 Kings 6.28|]]; [[Hebrews 9.5|Heb. 9:5]]).
- Cherubim are mentioned in the visions of Ezekiel, [[Ezekiel 10.1|Ezek. 10]][[Ezekiel 10.2|]][[Ezekiel 10.3|]][[Ezekiel 10.4|]][[Ezekiel 10.5|]][[Ezekiel 10.6|]][[Ezekiel 10.7|]][[Ezekiel 10.8|]][[Ezekiel 10.9|]][[Ezekiel 10.10|]][[Ezekiel 10.11|]][[Ezekiel 10.12|]][[Ezekiel 10.13|]][[Ezekiel 10.14|]][[Ezekiel 10.15|]][[Ezekiel 10.16|]][[Ezekiel 10.17|]][[Ezekiel 10.18|]][[Ezekiel 10.19|]][[Ezekiel 10.20|]][[Ezekiel 10.21|]][[Ezekiel 10.22|]]; [[Ezekiel 11.22|11:22]].
