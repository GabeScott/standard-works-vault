# Sanctification

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/sanctification?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Justification, Justify|Justification, Justify]]

The process of becoming free from sin, pure, clean, and holy through the Atonement of Jesus Christ ([[Moses 6.59|Moses 6:59–60]][[Moses 6.60|]]).

- God hath chosen you to salvation through sanctification of the Spirit, [[2 Thessalonians 2.13|2 Thes. 2:13]].
- We are sanctified through the offering of the body of Jesus, [[Hebrews 10.10|Heb. 10:10]].
- Jesus suffered that he might sanctify the people with his own blood, [[Hebrews 13.12|Heb. 13:12]].

- High priests were sanctified and their garments were washed white through the blood of the Lamb, [[Alma 13.10|Alma 13:10–12]][[Alma 13.11|]][[Alma 13.12|]].
- Sanctification cometh to those who yield their hearts unto God, [[Helaman 3.33|Hel. 3:33–35]][[Helaman 3.34|]][[Helaman 3.35|]].
- Repent that ye may be sanctified by the reception of the Holy Ghost, [[3 Nephi 27.20|3 Ne. 27:20]].

- Sanctification through the grace of Jesus Christ is just and true, [[D&C 20.31|D&C 20:31]].
- Jesus came to sanctify the world, [[D&C 76.41|D&C 76:41]].
- Sanctify yourselves that your minds become single to God, [[D&C 88.68|D&C 88:68]].
