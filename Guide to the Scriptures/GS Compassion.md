# Compassion

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/compassion?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Charity|Charity]]; [[GS Love|Love]]; [[GS Merciful, Mercy|Merciful, Mercy]]

In the scriptures, compassion means literally “to suffer with.” It also means to show sympathy, pity, and mercy for another.

- The Lord called upon his people to show compassion, [[Zechariah 7.8|Zech. 7:8–10]][[Zechariah 7.9|]][[Zechariah 7.10|]].

- Jesus was moved with compassion, [[Matthew 9.36|Matt. 9:36]] ([[Matthew 20.34|Matt. 20:34]]; [[Mark 1.41|Mark 1:41]]; [[Luke 7.13|Luke 7:13]]).
- A certain Samaritan had compassion on him, [[Luke 10.33|Luke 10:33]].
- Have compassion for one another, [[1 Peter 3.8|1 Pet. 3:8]].

- Christ is filled with compassion toward the children of men, [[Mosiah 15.9|Mosiah 15:9]].
- My bowels are filled with compassion toward you, [[3 Nephi 17.6|3 Ne. 17:6]].

- Joseph Smith prayed for the Lord’s compassion, [[D&C 121.3|D&C 121:3–5]][[D&C 121.4|]][[D&C 121.5|]].
