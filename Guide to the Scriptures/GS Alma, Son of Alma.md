# Alma, Son of Alma

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/alma-son-of-alma?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Alma the Elder|Alma the Elder]]; [[GS Amulek|Amulek]]; [[GS Mosiah, Sons of|Mosiah, Sons of]]

In the Book of Mormon, the first chief judge and prophet in the Nephite nation. In his early years he sought to destroy the Church ([[Mosiah 27.8|Mosiah 27:8–10]][[Mosiah 27.9|]][[Mosiah 27.10|]]). However, an angel appeared to him and he was converted to the gospel ([[Mosiah 27.8|Mosiah 27:8–24]][[Mosiah 27.9|]][[Mosiah 27.10|]][[Mosiah 27.11|]][[Mosiah 27.12|]][[Mosiah 27.13|]][[Mosiah 27.14|]][[Mosiah 27.15|]][[Mosiah 27.16|]][[Mosiah 27.17|]][[Mosiah 27.18|]][[Mosiah 27.19|]][[Mosiah 27.20|]][[Mosiah 27.21|]][[Mosiah 27.22|]][[Mosiah 27.23|]][[Mosiah 27.24|]]; [[Alma 36.6|Alma 36:6–27]][[Alma 36.7|]][[Alma 36.8|]][[Alma 36.9|]][[Alma 36.10|]][[Alma 36.11|]][[Alma 36.12|]][[Alma 36.13|]][[Alma 36.14|]][[Alma 36.15|]][[Alma 36.16|]][[Alma 36.17|]][[Alma 36.18|]][[Alma 36.19|]][[Alma 36.20|]][[Alma 36.21|]][[Alma 36.22|]][[Alma 36.23|]][[Alma 36.24|]][[Alma 36.25|]][[Alma 36.26|]][[Alma 36.27|]]). Later he gave up his position as chief judge in order to teach the people ([[Alma 4.11|Alma 4:11–20]][[Alma 4.12|]][[Alma 4.13|]][[Alma 4.14|]][[Alma 4.15|]][[Alma 4.16|]][[Alma 4.17|]][[Alma 4.18|]][[Alma 4.19|]][[Alma 4.20|]]).

## The book of Alma

A separate book in the Book of Mormon, comprising an abridgment of the records of the prophets Alma, the son of Alma, and his son Helaman. The events depicted in the book take place from approximately 91 to 52  The book contains 63 chapters.
