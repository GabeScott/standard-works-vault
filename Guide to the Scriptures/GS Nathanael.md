# Nathanael

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/nathanael?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Bartholomew|Bartholomew]]

An Apostle of Christ and a friend of Philip in the New Testament ([[John 1.45|John 1:45–51]][[John 1.46|]][[John 1.47|]][[John 1.48|]][[John 1.49|]][[John 1.50|]][[John 1.51|]]). He came from Cana in Galilee ([[John 21.2|John 21:2]]). Christ said that Nathanael was an Israelite without guile ([[John 1.47|John 1:47]]). He and Bartholomew are generally thought to be the same person ([[Matthew 10.3|Matt. 10:3]]; [[Mark 3.18|Mark 3:18]]; [[Luke 6.14|Luke 6:14]]; [[John 1.43|John 1:43–45]][[John 1.44|]][[John 1.45|]]).
