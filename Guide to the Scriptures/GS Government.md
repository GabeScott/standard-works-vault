# Government

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/government?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Constitution|Constitution]]

When Jesus Christ returns, He will establish a government of righteousness.

- The government shall be upon his shoulder, [[Isaiah 9.6|Isa. 9:6]] ([[2 Nephi 19.6|2 Ne. 19:6]]).

- Render to Caesar the things that are Caesar’s, [[Matthew 22.21|Matt. 22:21]] ([[D&C 63.26|D&C 63:26]]).
- Be subject to the higher powers, [[Romans 13.1|Rom. 13:1]].
- Pray for kings and all in authority, [[1 Timothy 2.1|1 Tim. 2:1–2]][[1 Timothy 2.2|]].
- Be subject to principalities and powers and obey magistrates, [[Titus 3.1|Titus 3:1]].
- Submit yourselves to every ordinance of man for the Lord’s sake, [[1 Peter 2.13|1 Pet. 2:13–14]][[1 Peter 2.14|]].
- Jesus Christ shall be the ultimate governor of the earth, [[Revelation 11.15|Rev. 11:15]].

- Have just men to be your kings, [[Mosiah 23.8|Mosiah 23:8]].
- Do your business by the vote of the people, [[Mosiah 29.26|Mosiah 29:26]].

- Christ will be our ruler when he comes, [[D&C 41.4|D&C 41:4]].
- He that keeps God’s laws has no need to break the laws of the land, [[D&C 58.21|D&C 58:21]].
- When the wicked rule, the people mourn, [[D&C 98.9|D&C 98:9–10]][[D&C 98.10|]].
- Governments are instituted of God for the benefit of mankind, [[D&C 134.1|D&C 134:1–5]][[D&C 134.2|]][[D&C 134.3|]][[D&C 134.4|]][[D&C 134.5|]].
- Men are bound to sustain and uphold governments, [[D&C 134.5|D&C 134:5]].

- We believe in being subject to kings, presidents, rulers, and magistrates, [[Articles of Faith 1.12|A of F 1:12]].
