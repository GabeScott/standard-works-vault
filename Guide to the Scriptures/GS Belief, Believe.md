# Belief, Believe

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/belief-believe?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Faith|Faith]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Trust|Trust]]; [[GS Unbelief|Unbelief]]

To have faith in someone or to accept something as true. A person must repent and believe in Jesus Christ in order to be saved in the kingdom of God ([[D&C 20.29|D&C 20:29]]).

- Believe in the Lord your God; believe his prophets, [[2 Chronicles 20.20|2 Chr. 20:20]].
- Daniel was not hurt in the lions’ den because he believed in God, [[Daniel 6.23|Dan. 6:23]].

- As thou hast believed, so be it done unto thee, [[Matthew 8.13|Matt. 8:13]].
- Whatsoever ye shall ask in prayer, believing, ye shall receive, [[Matthew 21.22|Matt. 21:22]].
- Be not afraid, only believe, [[Mark 5.36|Mark 5:36]].
- All things are possible to him that believes, [[Mark 9.23|Mark 9:23–24]][[Mark 9.24|]].
- He that believes and is baptized shall be saved, [[Mark 16.16|Mark 16:16]] ([[2 Nephi 2.9|2 Ne. 2:9]]; [[3 Nephi 11.33|3 Ne. 11:33–35]][[3 Nephi 11.34|]][[3 Nephi 11.35|]]).
- He that believes in the Son has everlasting life, [[John 3.16|John 3:16, 18, 36]][[John 3.18|]][[John 3.36|]] ([[John 5.24|John 5:24]]; [[D&C 10.50|D&C 10:50]]).
- We believe and are sure that thou art the Christ, [[John 6.69|John 6:69]].
- He that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live, [[John 11.25|John 11:25–26]][[John 11.26|]].
- We who have believed do enter into rest, [[Hebrews 4.3|Heb. 4:3]].
- Believe on Jesus Christ, and love one another, [[1 John 3.23|1 Jn. 3:23]].

- The Messiah will destroy none who believe in him, [[2 Nephi 6.14|2 Ne. 6:14]].
- The Jews will be persecuted until they are persuaded to believe in Christ, [[2 Nephi 25.16|2 Ne. 25:16]].
- If ye believe these things, see that ye do them, [[Mosiah 4.10|Mosiah 4:10]].
- The Son will take upon him the transgressions of those who believe on his name, [[Alma 11.40|Alma 11:40]].
- Blessed is he who believes in the word of God without being compelled, [[Alma 32.16|Alma 32:16]].
- If ye can no more than desire to believe, let this desire work in you, [[Alma 32.27|Alma 32:27]].
- If ye believe on Christ’s name, ye will repent, [[Helaman 14.13|Hel. 14:13]].
- Those who believe in Christ believe in the Father also, [[3 Nephi 11.35|3 Ne. 11:35]].
- Never has man believed in the Lord as the brother of Jared did, [[Ether 3.15|Ether 3:15]].
- Every thing that persuades to believe in Christ is sent by the power of Christ, [[Moroni 7.16|Moro. 7:16–17]][[Moroni 7.17|]].

- Those who believe on the Lord’s words will be given a manifestation of the Spirit, [[D&C 5.16|D&C 5:16]].
- Those who believe on the Lord’s name will become sons of God, [[D&C 11.30|D&C 11:30]] ([[John 1.12|John 1:12]]).
- To some it is given to believe on others’ words, [[D&C 46.14|D&C 46:14]].
- Signs follow those who believe, [[D&C 58.64|D&C 58:64]] ([[D&C 63.7|D&C 63:7–12]][[D&C 63.8|]][[D&C 63.9|]][[D&C 63.10|]][[D&C 63.11|]][[D&C 63.12|]]).

- Those who believe, repent, and are baptized shall receive the Holy Ghost, [[Moses 6.52|Moses 6:52]].
