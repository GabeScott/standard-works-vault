# Zarahemla

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/zarahemla?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Ammon, Descendant of Zarahemla|Ammon, Descendant of Zarahemla]]; [[GS Mulek|Mulek]]

In the Book of Mormon, Zarahemla refers to (1) a man who led Mulek’s colony, (2) a city named after him, (3) the land of Zarahemla, or (4) the people who followed him.

- Zarahemla rejoiced that the Lord had sent the Nephites, [[Omni 1.14|Omni 1:14]].
- Zarahemla gave a genealogy of the fathers, [[Omni 1.18|Omni 1:18]].
- Ammon was a descendant of Zarahemla, [[Mosiah 7.3|Mosiah 7:3, 13]][[Mosiah 7.13|]].
- The Church was established in the city of Zarahemla, [[Alma 5.2|Alma 5:2]].
- Because of the righteous the wicked were saved in Zarahemla, [[Helaman 13.12|Hel. 13:12]].
- The city of Zarahemla was burned at Christ’s death, [[3 Nephi 8.8|3 Ne. 8:8, 24]][[3 Nephi 8.24|]].
