# Patience

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/patience?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Endure|Endure]]; [[GS Meek, Meekness|Meek, Meekness]]

Calm endurance; the ability to endure affliction, insult, or injury without complaint or retaliation.

- Rest in the Lord, and wait patiently for him, [[Psalms 37.7|Ps. 37:7–8]][[Psalms 37.8|]].
- He that is slow to wrath is of great understanding, [[Proverbs 14.29|Prov. 14:29]].

- In your patience possess ye your souls, [[Luke 21.19|Luke 21:19]].
- We have hope through patience and comfort of the scriptures, [[Romans 15.4|Rom. 15:4]].
- Be followers of them who through faith and patience inherit the promises, [[Hebrews 6.12|Heb. 6:12–15]][[Hebrews 6.13|]][[Hebrews 6.14|]][[Hebrews 6.15|]].
- Let patience have her perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire, [[James 1.2|James 1:2–4]][[James 1.3|]][[James 1.4|]].
- Ye have heard of the patience of Job, [[James 5.11|James 5:11]].

- They did submit cheerfully and with patience to all the will of the Lord, [[Mosiah 24.15|Mosiah 24:15]].
- Thou didst bear all these things with patience because the Lord was with thee, [[Alma 38.4|Alma 38:4–5]][[Alma 38.5|]].

- Continue in patience until ye are perfected, [[D&C 67.13|D&C 67:13]].
