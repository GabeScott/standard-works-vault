# Immortal, Immortality

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/immortal-immortality?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Mortal, Mortality|Mortal, Mortality]]; [[GS Resurrection|Resurrection]]; [[GS Salvation|Salvation]]

The condition of living forever in a resurrected state, not subject to physical death.

- He is risen, [[Mark 16.6|Mark 16:6]].
- Even so in Christ shall all be made alive, [[1 Corinthians 15.22|1 Cor. 15:22]].
- Death is swallowed up when this mortal shall put on immortality, [[1 Corinthians 15.53|1 Cor. 15:53–54]][[1 Corinthians 15.54|]].
- Christ hath abolished death and brought immortality, [[2 Timothy 1.10|2 Tim. 1:10]].

- Immortality is the restoring of the spirit to the body, [[2 Nephi 9.13|2 Ne. 9:13]].
- Spirits united with their bodies become immortal, never to die again, [[Alma 11.45|Alma 11:45]].

- The faithful will be crowned with immortality and eternal life, [[D&C 75.5|D&C 75:5]].
- The earth will be sanctified and immortal, [[D&C 77.1|D&C 77:1]] ([[D&C 130.9|D&C 130:9]]).

- God’s work and glory is to bring to pass the immortality and eternal life of man, [[Moses 1.39|Moses 1:39]].
