# Coriantumr

- Was discovered by the people of Zarahemla, [[Omni 1.21|Omni 1:21]].
- Was king over all the land, [[Ether 12.1|Ether 12:1–2]][[Ether 12.2|]].
- Was captured by Shared and liberated by his sons, [[Ether 13.23|Ether 13:23–24]][[Ether 13.24|]].

- Repented, [[Ether 15.3|Ether 15:3]].
