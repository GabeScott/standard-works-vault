# Lot

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/lot?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Abraham|Abraham]]

In the Old Testament, the son of Haran and nephew of Abraham ([[Genesis 11.27|Gen. 11:27, 31]][[Genesis 11.31|]]; [[Abraham 2.4|Abr. 2:4]]). Haran died because of the famine in Ur ([[Abraham 2.1|Abr. 2:1]]). Lot left Ur with Abraham and Sarah and traveled with them to Canaan ([[Genesis 12.4|Gen. 12:4–5]][[Genesis 12.5|]]). Lot chose to live in Sodom. The Lord sent messengers to warn Lot to flee from Sodom before the Lord destroyed it because of the wickedness of the people ([[Genesis 13.8|Gen. 13:8–13]][[Genesis 13.9|]][[Genesis 13.10|]][[Genesis 13.11|]][[Genesis 13.12|]][[Genesis 13.13|]]; [[Genesis 19.1|19:1, 13, 15]][[Genesis 19.13|]][[Genesis 19.15|]]); however, Lot’s wife looked backward at the destruction and became a pillar of salt ([[Genesis 19.26|Gen. 19:26]]). The New Testament contains references to Lot ([[Luke 17.29|Luke 17:29]]; [[2 Peter 2.6|2 Pet. 2:6–7]][[2 Peter 2.7|]]).
