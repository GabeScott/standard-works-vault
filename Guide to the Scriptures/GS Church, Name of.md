# Church, Name of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/church-name-of?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Church of Jesus Christ|Church of Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, The|Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, The]]; [[GS Mormon(s)|Mormon(s)]]

In the Book of Mormon, when Jesus Christ visited the righteous Nephites shortly after His Resurrection, He said that His Church should bear His name ([[3 Nephi 27.3|3 Ne. 27:3–8]][[3 Nephi 27.4|]][[3 Nephi 27.5|]][[3 Nephi 27.6|]][[3 Nephi 27.7|]][[3 Nephi 27.8|]]). In modern times the Lord revealed the name of the Church to be “The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints” ([[D&C 115.4|D&C 115:4]]).
