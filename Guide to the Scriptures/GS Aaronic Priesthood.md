# Aaronic Priesthood

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/aaronic-priesthood?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Aaron, Brother of Moses|Aaron, Brother of Moses]]; [[GS Law of Moses|Law of Moses]]; [[GS Priesthood|Priesthood]]

The lesser priesthood ([[Hebrews 7.11|Heb. 7:11–12]][[Hebrews 7.12|]]; [[D&C 107.13|D&C 107:13–14]][[D&C 107.14|]]). Its offices are bishop, priest, teacher, and deacon ([[D&C 84.30|D&C 84:30]]; [[D&C 107.10|107:10, 14–15, 87–88]][[D&C 107.14|]][[D&C 107.15|]][[D&C 107.87|]][[D&C 107.88|]]). Anciently, under the law of Moses, there were high priests, priests, and Levites. Because the ancient Israelites rebelled against God, Moses and the holy priesthood were taken from them and the lesser priesthood continued. They had refused to be sanctified and receive the Melchizedek Priesthood and its ordinances. (See [[D&C 84.23|D&C 84:23–26]][[D&C 84.24|]][[D&C 84.25|]][[D&C 84.26|]].) The Aaronic Priesthood deals with the temporal and outward ordinances of the law and the gospel ([[1 Chronicles 23.27|1 Chr. 23:27–32]][[1 Chronicles 23.28|]][[1 Chronicles 23.29|]][[1 Chronicles 23.30|]][[1 Chronicles 23.31|]][[1 Chronicles 23.32|]]; [[D&C 84.26|D&C 84:26–27]][[D&C 84.27|]]; [[D&C 107.20|107:20]]). It holds the keys of the ministering of angels, of the gospel of repentance, and of baptism ([[D&C 13.1|D&C 13]]). The Aaronic Priesthood was restored to the earth in this dispensation on May 15, 1829. John the Baptist conferred it on Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery near Harmony, Pennsylvania ([[D&C 13.1|D&C 13]]; [[Joseph Smith History 1.68|JS—H 1:68–73]][[Joseph Smith History 1.69|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.70|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.71|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.72|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.73|]]).

- And he shall have the covenant of an everlasting priesthood, [[Numbers 25.13|Num. 25:13]].
- The Lord shall purify the sons of Levi, and purge them, [[Malachi 3.3|Mal. 3:3]] ([[3 Nephi 24.3|3 Ne. 24:3]]).

- No man taketh this honor unto himself, [[Hebrews 5.4|Heb. 5:4]].
- Perfection does not come by the Levitical Priesthood, [[Hebrews 7.11|Heb. 7:11]].

- This priesthood shall never be taken from the earth until the sons of Levi do offer an offering, [[D&C 13.1|D&C 13]].
- Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery were ordained unto the Aaronic Priesthood, [[D&C 27.8|D&C 27:8]].
- The lesser priesthood holds the key of the ministering of angels, [[D&C 84.26|D&C 84:26]] ([[D&C 13.1|D&C 13]]).
- There are two priesthoods, namely, the Melchizedek and Aaronic, [[D&C 107.1|D&C 107:1]].
- The second priesthood is called the Priesthood of Aaron, [[D&C 107.13|D&C 107:13]].
