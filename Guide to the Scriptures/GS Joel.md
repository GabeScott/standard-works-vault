# Joel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/joel?lang=eng)

A prophet in the land of Judah in the Old Testament. The date of his life is uncertain—he may have lived sometime between the reign of Joash, before 850 , and the return of the tribe of Judah from captivity in Babylon.

## The book of Joel

The book centers on a prophecy that Joel made after the land of Judah was smitten with a severe drought and with locusts ([[Joel 1.4|Joel 1:4–20]][[Joel 1.5|]][[Joel 1.6|]][[Joel 1.7|]][[Joel 1.8|]][[Joel 1.9|]][[Joel 1.10|]][[Joel 1.11|]][[Joel 1.12|]][[Joel 1.13|]][[Joel 1.14|]][[Joel 1.15|]][[Joel 1.16|]][[Joel 1.17|]][[Joel 1.18|]][[Joel 1.19|]][[Joel 1.20|]]). Joel assured the people that through repentance they would again receive the blessings of God ([[Joel 2.12|Joel 2:12–14]][[Joel 2.13|]][[Joel 2.14|]]).
