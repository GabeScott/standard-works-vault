# Miracle

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/miracle?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Faith|Faith]]; [[GS Sign|Sign]]

An extraordinary event caused by the power of God. Miracles are an important element in the work of Jesus Christ. They include healings, restoring the dead to life, and resurrection. Miracles are a part of the gospel of Jesus Christ. Faith is necessary in order for miracles to be manifested ([[Mark 6.5|Mark 6:5–6]][[Mark 6.6|]]; [[Mormon 9.10|Morm. 9:10–20]][[Mormon 9.11|]][[Mormon 9.12|]][[Mormon 9.13|]][[Mormon 9.14|]][[Mormon 9.15|]][[Mormon 9.16|]][[Mormon 9.17|]][[Mormon 9.18|]][[Mormon 9.19|]][[Mormon 9.20|]]; [[Ether 12.12|Ether 12:12]]).

- When Pharaoh shall speak, Shew a miracle, [[Exodus 7.9|Ex. 7:9]].

- No man which shall do a miracle in my name can lightly speak evil of me, [[Mark 9.39|Mark 9:39]].
- Cana was the place where Jesus performed his first miracle, [[John 2.11|John 2:11]].

- I am a God of miracles, [[2 Nephi 27.23|2 Ne. 27:23]].
- The power of God works miracles, [[Alma 23.6|Alma 23:6]].
- Christ could show greater miracles to those on the American continent because they had great faith, [[3 Nephi 19.35|3 Ne. 19:35]].
- God has not ceased to be a God of miracles, [[Mormon 9.15|Morm. 9:15]].

- Require not miracles, except the Lord shall command you, [[D&C 24.13|D&C 24:13–14]][[D&C 24.14|]].
- To some is given the gift of working miracles, [[D&C 46.21|D&C 46:21]] ([[Moroni 10.12|Moro. 10:12]]).
