# Truth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/truth?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Intelligence, Intelligences|Intelligence, Intelligences]]; [[GS Knowledge|Knowledge]]; [[GS Light, Light of Christ|Light, Light of Christ]]

Knowledge of things as they are, and as they were, and as they are to come ([[D&C 93.24|D&C 93:24]]). Truth also refers to light and revelation from heaven.

- Truth shall spring out of the earth, [[Psalms 85.11|Ps. 85:11]] ([[Moses 7.62|Moses 7:62]]).

- Ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free, [[John 8.32|John 8:32]].
- I am the way, the truth, and the life, [[John 14.6|John 14:6]].
- If we say that we have no sin, the truth is not in us, [[1 John 1.8|1 Jn. 1:8]].

- The guilty take the truth to be hard, [[1 Nephi 16.2|1 Ne. 16:2]].
- The righteous love the truth, [[2 Nephi 9.40|2 Ne. 9:40]].
- The Spirit speaketh the truth and lieth not, [[Jacob 4.13|Jacob 4:13]].
- Thou art a God of truth and cannot lie, [[Ether 3.12|Ether 3:12]].
- By the power of the Holy Ghost ye may know the truth of all things, [[Moroni 10.5|Moro. 10:5]].

- Truth abideth forever and ever, [[D&C 1.39|D&C 1:39]].
- Thou hast been enlightened by the Spirit of truth, [[D&C 6.15|D&C 6:15]].
- The Book of Mormon contains the truth and the word of God, [[D&C 19.26|D&C 19:26]].
- The Comforter was sent forth to teach the truth, [[D&C 50.14|D&C 50:14]].
- He that receiveth the word by the Spirit of truth receiveth it as it is preached by the Spirit of truth, [[D&C 50.17|D&C 50:17–22]][[D&C 50.18|]][[D&C 50.19|]][[D&C 50.20|]][[D&C 50.21|]][[D&C 50.22|]].
- Proclaim the truth according to the revelations I have given you, [[D&C 75.3|D&C 75:3–4]][[D&C 75.4|]].
- Whatsoever is truth is light, [[D&C 84.45|D&C 84:45]].
- The light of Christ is the light of truth, [[D&C 88.6|D&C 88:6–7, 40]][[D&C 88.7|]][[D&C 88.40|]].
- My Spirit is truth, [[D&C 88.66|D&C 88:66]].
- Intelligence, or the light of truth, was not created, [[D&C 93.29|D&C 93:29]].
- The glory of God is intelligence, or light and truth, [[D&C 93.36|D&C 93:36]].
- I have commanded you to bring up your children in light and truth, [[D&C 93.40|D&C 93:40]].

- Mine Only Begotten is full of grace and truth, [[Moses 1.6|Moses 1:6]].
