# Beatitudes

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/beatitudes?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Sermon on the Mount|Sermon on the Mount]]

A series of teachings that Jesus gave in the Sermon on the Mount that describe a refined and spiritual character ([[Matthew 5.3|Matt. 5:3–12]][[Matthew 5.4|]][[Matthew 5.5|]][[Matthew 5.6|]][[Matthew 5.7|]][[Matthew 5.8|]][[Matthew 5.9|]][[Matthew 5.10|]][[Matthew 5.11|]][[Matthew 5.12|]]; [[Luke 6.20|Luke 6:20–23]][[Luke 6.21|]][[Luke 6.22|]][[Luke 6.23|]]). The Beatitudes are arranged in such a way that each statement builds upon the one that precedes it.
