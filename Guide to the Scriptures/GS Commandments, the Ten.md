# Commandments, the Ten

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/commandments-the-ten?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Commandments of God|Commandments of God]]; [[GS Moses|Moses]]

Ten laws given by God through the prophet Moses to govern moral behavior.

The Hebrew name for these is the “Ten Words.” They are also called the Covenant ([[Deuteronomy 9.9|Deut. 9:9]]) or the Testimony ([[Exodus 25.21|Ex. 25:21]]; [[Exodus 32.15|32:15]]). God’s giving of the Ten Commandments to Moses, and through him to Israel, is described in [[Exodus 19.1|Ex. 19:9–20:23]][[Exodus 19.2|]][[Exodus 19.3|]][[Exodus 19.4|]][[Exodus 19.5|]][[Exodus 19.6|]][[Exodus 19.7|]][[Exodus 19.8|]][[Exodus 19.9|]][[Exodus 19.10|]][[Exodus 19.11|]][[Exodus 19.12|]][[Exodus 19.13|]][[Exodus 19.14|]][[Exodus 19.15|]][[Exodus 19.16|]][[Exodus 19.17|]][[Exodus 19.18|]][[Exodus 19.19|]][[Exodus 19.20|]][[Exodus 19.21|]][[Exodus 19.22|]][[Exodus 19.23|]][[Exodus 19.24|]][[Exodus 19.25|]]; [[Exodus 32.15|32:15–19]][[Exodus 32.16|]][[Exodus 32.17|]][[Exodus 32.18|]][[Exodus 32.19|]]; [[Exodus 34.1|34:1]]. The commandments were engraved on two tables of stone, which were placed in the Ark; hence the Ark was called the Ark of the Covenant ([[Numbers 10.33|Num. 10:33]]). The Lord, quoting from [[Deuteronomy 6.4|Deut. 6:4–5]][[Deuteronomy 6.5|]] and [[Leviticus 19.18|Lev. 19:18]], has summarized the Ten Commandments in “two great commandments” ([[Matthew 22.37|Matt. 22:37–39]][[Matthew 22.38|]][[Matthew 22.39|]]).

The Ten Commandments have been repeated in latter-day revelation ([[JST Exodus 34.1|JST, Ex. 34:1–2, 14 (Bible Appendix)]][[JST Exodus 34.2|]][[JST Exodus 34.14|]]; [[Mosiah 12.32|Mosiah 12:32–37]][[Mosiah 12.33|]][[Mosiah 12.34|]][[Mosiah 12.35|]][[Mosiah 12.36|]][[Mosiah 12.37|]]; [[Mosiah 13.12|13:12–24]][[Mosiah 13.13|]][[Mosiah 13.14|]][[Mosiah 13.15|]][[Mosiah 13.16|]][[Mosiah 13.17|]][[Mosiah 13.18|]][[Mosiah 13.19|]][[Mosiah 13.20|]][[Mosiah 13.21|]][[Mosiah 13.22|]][[Mosiah 13.23|]][[Mosiah 13.24|]]; [[D&C 42.18|D&C 42:18–28]][[D&C 42.19|]][[D&C 42.20|]][[D&C 42.21|]][[D&C 42.22|]][[D&C 42.23|]][[D&C 42.24|]][[D&C 42.25|]][[D&C 42.26|]][[D&C 42.27|]][[D&C 42.28|]]; [[D&C 59.5|59:5–13]][[D&C 59.6|]][[D&C 59.7|]][[D&C 59.8|]][[D&C 59.9|]][[D&C 59.10|]][[D&C 59.11|]][[D&C 59.12|]][[D&C 59.13|]]).
