# Timothy

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/timothy?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Paul|Paul]]

In the New Testament, a young missionary companion of Paul during Paul’s ministry ([[Acts 16.1|Acts 16:1–3]][[Acts 16.2|]][[Acts 16.3|]]; [[2 Timothy 1.1|2 Tim. 1:1–5]][[2 Timothy 1.2|]][[2 Timothy 1.3|]][[2 Timothy 1.4|]][[2 Timothy 1.5|]]); the son of a Greek father and Jewish mother; he and his parents lived in Lystra.

Paul spoke of Timothy as his “own son in the faith” ([[1 Timothy 1.2|1 Tim. 1:2, 18]][[1 Timothy 1.18|]]; [[2 Timothy 1.2|2 Tim. 1:2]]). Timothy was perhaps Paul’s most trusted and capable assistant ([[Philippians 2.19|Philip. 2:19–23]][[Philippians 2.20|]][[Philippians 2.21|]][[Philippians 2.22|]][[Philippians 2.23|]]).
