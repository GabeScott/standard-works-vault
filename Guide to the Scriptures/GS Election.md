# Election

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/election?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Calling and Election|Calling and Election]]

Based on premortal worthiness, God chose those who would be the seed of Abraham and the house of Israel and become the covenant people ([[Deuteronomy 32.7|Deut. 32:7–9]][[Deuteronomy 32.8|]][[Deuteronomy 32.9|]]; [[Abraham 2.9|Abr. 2:9–11]][[Abraham 2.10|]][[Abraham 2.11|]]). These people are given special blessings and duties so that they can bless all the nations of the world ([[Romans 11.5|Rom. 11:5–7]][[Romans 11.6|]][[Romans 11.7|]]; [[1 Peter 1.2|1 Pet. 1:2]]; [[Alma 13.1|Alma 13:1–5]][[Alma 13.2|]][[Alma 13.3|]][[Alma 13.4|]][[Alma 13.5|]]; [[D&C 84.99|D&C 84:99]]). However, even these chosen ones must be called and elected in this life in order to gain salvation.
