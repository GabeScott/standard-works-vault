# Chosen (adjective or noun)

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/chosen-adjective-or-noun?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Call, Called of God, Calling|Call, Called of God, Calling]]

Those selected by God for special responsibilities.

- I have made a covenant with my chosen, [[Psalms 89.3|Ps. 89:3]].

- The Saints are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, [[1 Peter 2.9|1 Pet. 2:9]].

- Christ was God’s Beloved and Chosen from the beginning, [[Moses 4.2|Moses 4:2]].
