# Principle

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/principle?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Gospel|Gospel]]

A basic doctrine, truth, or law. The first principles of the gospel are faith in the Lord Jesus Christ and repentance ([[Articles of Faith 1.4|A of F 1:4]]).

- Not leaving the principles of the doctrine of Christ, let us go on unto perfection, [[JST Hebrews 6.1|JST, Heb. 6:1]].

- The elders, priests, and teachers shall teach the principles of my gospel, which are in the scriptures, [[D&C 42.12|D&C 42:12]].
- Be instructed more perfectly in principle, in doctrine, in all things, [[D&C 88.78|D&C 88:78]] ([[D&C 97.14|D&C 97:14]]).
- Every man may act in doctrine and principle according to the moral agency I have given him, [[D&C 101.78|D&C 101:78]].
- Whatever principle of intelligence we attain in this life will rise with us in the resurrection, [[D&C 130.18|D&C 130:18–19]][[D&C 130.19|]].
