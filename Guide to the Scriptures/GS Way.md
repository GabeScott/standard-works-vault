# Way

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/way?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Walk, Walk with God|Walk, Walk with God]]

The path or course a person follows. Jesus said He was the way ([[John 14.4|John 14:4–6]][[John 14.5|]][[John 14.6|]]).

- Keep the commandments of the Lord to walk in his ways, [[Deuteronomy 8.6|Deut. 8:6]].
- Train up a child in the way he should go, [[Proverbs 22.6|Prov. 22:6]] ([[2 Nephi 4.5|2 Ne. 4:5]]).
- The Lord said his ways are higher than our ways, [[Isaiah 55.8|Isa. 55:8–9]][[Isaiah 55.9|]].

- Strait is the gate and narrow is the way that leads to life, [[Matthew 7.13|Matt. 7:13–14]][[Matthew 7.14|]] ([[3 Nephi 14.13|3 Ne. 14:13–14]][[3 Nephi 14.14|]]; [[3 Nephi 27.33|27:33]]; [[D&C 132.22|D&C 132:22, 25]][[D&C 132.25|]]).
- God will make a way for you to escape temptation, [[1 Corinthians 10.13|1 Cor. 10:13]].

- The Lord gives no commandment that he does not prepare a way for his children to keep, [[1 Nephi 3.7|1 Ne. 3:7]] ([[1 Nephi 9.6|1 Ne. 9:6]]; [[1 Nephi 17.3|17:3, 13]][[1 Nephi 17.13|]]).
- There is none other way except it be by the gate, [[2 Nephi 9.41|2 Ne. 9:41]].
- Ye are free to act for yourselves—to choose the way of everlasting death or the way of eternal life, [[2 Nephi 10.23|2 Ne. 10:23]].
- This is the way and there is no other way nor name, [[2 Nephi 31.21|2 Ne. 31:21]] ([[Mosiah 3.17|Mosiah 3:17]]; [[Alma 38.9|Alma 38:9]]; [[Helaman 5.9|Hel. 5:9]]).
- In the gift of his Son hath God prepared a more excellent way, [[Ether 12.11|Ether 12:11]] ([[1 Corinthians 12.31|1 Cor. 12:31]]).

- Every man walketh in his own way, [[D&C 1.16|D&C 1:16]].
- It must be done in my own way, [[D&C 104.16|D&C 104:16]].
