# First Presidency

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/first-presidency?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Keys of the Priesthood|Keys of the Priesthood]]; [[GS President|President]]; [[GS Revelation|Revelation]]

The President of the Church and his Counselors. They are a quorum of three high priests and preside over the whole Church. The First Presidency holds all the keys of the priesthood.

- The keys of the kingdom always belong to the Presidency of the High Priesthood, [[D&C 81.2|D&C 81:2]].
- The Presidency of the High Priesthood has the right to officiate in all offices, [[D&C 107.9|D&C 107:9, 22]][[D&C 107.22|]].
- Whosoever receiveth me receiveth the First Presidency, [[D&C 112.20|D&C 112:20, 30]][[D&C 112.30|]].
- The First Presidency is to receive oracles \[revelations\] for the whole Church, [[D&C 124.126|D&C 124:126]].
