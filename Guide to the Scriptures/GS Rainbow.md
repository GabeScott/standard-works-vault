# Rainbow

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/rainbow?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Ark|Ark]]; [[GS Flood at Noah’s Time|Flood at Noah’s Time]]; [[GS Noah, Bible Patriarch|Noah, Bible Patriarch]]

The sign or token of God’s covenant with Noah ([[Genesis 9.13|Gen. 9:13–17]][[Genesis 9.14|]][[Genesis 9.15|]][[Genesis 9.16|]][[Genesis 9.17|]]). [[JST Genesis 9.21|JST, Genesis 9:21–25 (Bible Appendix)]][[JST Genesis 9.22|]][[JST Genesis 9.23|]][[JST Genesis 9.24|]][[JST Genesis 9.25|]] explains that the covenant includes the promises that the earth will never again be covered with a flood of water, that the Zion of Enoch will return, and that the Lord will come again to dwell on the earth.
