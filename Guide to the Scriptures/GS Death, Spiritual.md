# Death, Spiritual

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/death-spiritual?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Damnation|Damnation]]; [[GS Devil|Devil]]; [[GS Fall of Adam and Eve|Fall of Adam and Eve]]; [[GS Hell|Hell]]; [[GS Salvation|Salvation]]; [[GS Sons of Perdition|Sons of Perdition]]

Separation from God and His influences; to die as to things pertaining to righteousness. Lucifer and a third part of the hosts of heaven suffered a spiritual death when they were cast out of heaven ([[D&C 29.36|D&C 29:36–37]][[D&C 29.37|]]).

Spiritual death was introduced into the world by the Fall of Adam ([[Moses 6.48|Moses 6:48]]). Mortals with evil thoughts, words, and works are spiritually dead while still alive on earth ([[1 Timothy 5.6|1 Tim. 5:6]]). Through the Atonement of Jesus Christ and by obedience to the principles and ordinances of the gospel, men and women can become clean from sin and overcome spiritual death.

Spiritual death also occurs following the death of the mortal body. Both resurrected beings and the devil and his angels will be judged. Those who have willfully rebelled against the light and truth of the gospel will suffer spiritual death. This death is often called the second death ([[Alma 12.16|Alma 12:16]]; [[Helaman 14.16|Hel. 14:16–19]][[Helaman 14.17|]][[Helaman 14.18|]][[Helaman 14.19|]]; [[D&C 76.36|D&C 76:36–38]][[D&C 76.37|]][[D&C 76.38|]]).

- Evildoers shall be cut off, [[Psalms 37.9|Ps. 37:9]].

- To be carnally minded is death, [[Romans 8.6|Rom. 8:6]] ([[2 Nephi 9.39|2 Ne. 9:39]]).
- Lusts drown men in destruction and perdition, [[1 Timothy 6.9|1 Tim. 6:9]].
- Sin brings forth death, [[James 1.15|James 1:15]].
- He that overcomes shall not be hurt of the second death, [[Revelation 2.11|Rev. 2:11]].
- On such the second death has no power, [[Revelation 20.6|Rev. 20:6, 12–14]][[Revelation 20.12|]][[Revelation 20.13|]][[Revelation 20.14|]].
- The wicked shall have their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone: which is the second death, [[Revelation 21.8|Rev. 21:8]] ([[D&C 63.17|D&C 63:17–18]][[D&C 63.18|]]).

- Men are free to choose liberty and eternal life or to choose captivity and death, [[2 Nephi 2.27|2 Ne. 2:27]] ([[2 Nephi 10.23|2 Ne. 10:23]]; [[Alma 29.5|Alma 29:5]]; [[Helaman 14.30|Hel. 14:30–31]][[Helaman 14.31|]]).
- God has prepared a way for our escape from death and hell, [[2 Nephi 9.10|2 Ne. 9:10]].

- Alma was encircled about by the everlasting chains of death, [[Alma 36.18|Alma 36:18]].
- The wicked die as to things pertaining to righteousness, [[Alma 40.26|Alma 40:26]] ([[Alma 12.16|Alma 12:16]]).
