# Plan of Redemption

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/plan-of-redemption?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Fall of Adam and Eve|Fall of Adam and Eve]]; [[GS Gospel|Gospel]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Salvation|Salvation]]

The fulness of the gospel of Jesus Christ, designed to bring about man’s immortality and eternal life. It includes the Creation, Fall, and Atonement, along with all God-given laws, ordinances, and doctrines.
