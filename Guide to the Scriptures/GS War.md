# War

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/war?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Peace|Peace]]

A battle or armed conflict; fighting with weapons. The Lord approves of war only as a last means for His Saints to defend their families, property, rights, privileges, and freedoms ([[Alma 43.9|Alma 43:9, 45–47]][[Alma 43.45|]][[Alma 43.46|]][[Alma 43.47|]]).

- Moroni sought to defend his people, his rights, his country, and his religion, [[Alma 48.10|Alma 48:10–17]][[Alma 48.11|]][[Alma 48.12|]][[Alma 48.13|]][[Alma 48.14|]][[Alma 48.15|]][[Alma 48.16|]][[Alma 48.17|]].

- Joseph Smith received a revelation and prophecy on war, [[D&C 87.1|D&C 87]][[D&C 87.2|]][[D&C 87.3|]][[D&C 87.4|]][[D&C 87.5|]][[D&C 87.6|]][[D&C 87.7|]][[D&C 87.8|]].
- Renounce war and proclaim peace, [[D&C 98.16|D&C 98:16, 34–46]][[D&C 98.34|]][[D&C 98.35|]][[D&C 98.36|]][[D&C 98.37|]][[D&C 98.38|]][[D&C 98.39|]][[D&C 98.40|]][[D&C 98.41|]][[D&C 98.42|]][[D&C 98.43|]][[D&C 98.44|]][[D&C 98.45|]][[D&C 98.46|]].
- We believe that men are justified in defending themselves, their friends, and property, and the government, [[D&C 134.11|D&C 134:11]].

- We believe in obeying, honoring, and sustaining the law, [[Articles of Faith 1.12|A of F 1:12]].
