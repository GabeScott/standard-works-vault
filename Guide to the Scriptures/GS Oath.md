# Oath

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/oath?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Covenant|Covenant]]; [[GS Oath and Covenant of the Priesthood|Oath and Covenant of the Priesthood]]

As used in the scriptures, usually a sacred covenant or promise. However, wicked persons, including Satan and his angels, also make oaths to accomplish their evil purposes. In Old Testament times, oaths were acceptable; however, Jesus Christ taught that people should not swear in the name of God or His creations ([[Matthew 5.33|Matt. 5:33–37]][[Matthew 5.34|]][[Matthew 5.35|]][[Matthew 5.36|]][[Matthew 5.37|]]).

- I will perform the oath which I swear unto Abraham, [[Genesis 26.3|Gen. 26:3]].
- If a man swears an oath to bind his soul, he shall not break it, [[Numbers 30.2|Num. 30:2]].
- They entered into an oath to walk in God’s law, [[Nehemiah 10.29|Neh. 10:29]].

- Thou shalt perform unto the Lord thine oaths, [[Matthew 5.33|Matt. 5:33]] ([[Ecclesiastes 5.4|Eccl. 5:4–5]][[Ecclesiastes 5.5|]]; [[3 Nephi 12.33|3 Ne. 12:33]]).
- God swears with an oath that the faithful shall be saved, [[Hebrews 6.13|Heb. 6:13–18]][[Hebrews 6.14|]][[Hebrews 6.15|]][[Hebrews 6.16|]][[Hebrews 6.17|]][[Hebrews 6.18|]].

- When Zoram made an oath, our fears did cease, [[1 Nephi 4.37|1 Ne. 4:37]].
- The people of Ammon took an oath that they would never shed blood, [[Alma 53.11|Alma 53:11]].
- The wicked Nephites made secret oaths and covenants with Satan, [[Helaman 6.21|Hel. 6:21–30]][[Helaman 6.22|]][[Helaman 6.23|]][[Helaman 6.24|]][[Helaman 6.25|]][[Helaman 6.26|]][[Helaman 6.27|]][[Helaman 6.28|]][[Helaman 6.29|]][[Helaman 6.30|]].

- Men gain eternal life through the oath and covenant of the priesthood, [[D&C 84.33|D&C 84:33–42]][[D&C 84.34|]][[D&C 84.35|]][[D&C 84.36|]][[D&C 84.37|]][[D&C 84.38|]][[D&C 84.39|]][[D&C 84.40|]][[D&C 84.41|]][[D&C 84.42|]].
- All covenants, contracts, bonds, obligations, and oaths not sealed by the Holy Spirit of Promise end when men die, [[D&C 132.7|D&C 132:7]].
