# Word of God

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/word-of-god?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Commandments of God|Commandments of God]]; [[GS Revelation|Revelation]]; [[GS Scriptures|Scriptures]]

Instructions, commandments, or a message from God. God’s children may receive His word directly by revelation through the Spirit or from His chosen servants ([[D&C 1.38|D&C 1:38]]).

- By every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the Lord doth man live, [[Deuteronomy 8.3|Deut. 8:3]] ([[Matthew 4.4|Matt. 4:4]]; [[D&C 84.43|D&C 84:43–44]][[D&C 84.44|]]).
- Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path, [[Psalms 119.105|Ps. 119:105]].

- They were all filled with the Holy Ghost and spake the word of God with boldness, [[Acts 4.31|Acts 4:31–33]][[Acts 4.32|]][[Acts 4.33|]].

- The iron rod was the word of God that led to the tree of life, [[1 Nephi 11.25|1 Ne. 11:25]] ([[1 Nephi 15.23|1 Ne. 15:23–25]][[1 Nephi 15.24|]][[1 Nephi 15.25|]]).
- Ye were past feeling, that ye could not feel his words, [[1 Nephi 17.45|1 Ne. 17:45–46]][[1 Nephi 17.46|]].
- Wo be unto him that rejecteth the word of God, [[2 Nephi 27.14|2 Ne. 27:14]] ([[2 Nephi 28.29|2 Ne. 28:29]]; [[Ether 4.8|Ether 4:8]]).
- Press forward, feasting upon the word of Christ, [[2 Nephi 31.20|2 Ne. 31:20]] ([[2 Nephi 32.3|2 Ne. 32:3]]).
- Because of their unbelief they could not understand the word of God, [[Mosiah 26.3|Mosiah 26:3]] ([[Alma 12.10|Alma 12:10]]).
- They had searched the scriptures diligently, that they might know the word of God, [[Alma 17.2|Alma 17:2]].
- Try the virtue of the word of God, [[Alma 31.5|Alma 31:5]].
- Alma compared the word of God to a seed, [[Alma 32.28|Alma 32:28–43]][[Alma 32.29|]][[Alma 32.30|]][[Alma 32.31|]][[Alma 32.32|]][[Alma 32.33|]][[Alma 32.34|]][[Alma 32.35|]][[Alma 32.36|]][[Alma 32.37|]][[Alma 32.38|]][[Alma 32.39|]][[Alma 32.40|]][[Alma 32.41|]][[Alma 32.42|]][[Alma 32.43|]].

- Whatsoever they speak when moved upon by the Holy Ghost shall be the word of the Lord, [[D&C 68.4|D&C 68:4]].
- Live by every word that proceedeth from the mouth of God, [[D&C 84.44|D&C 84:44–45]][[D&C 84.45|]].

- Whoso treasureth up my word shall not be deceived, [[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.37|JS—M 1:37]].
