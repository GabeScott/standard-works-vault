# Saint

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/saint?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Christians|Christians]]; [[GS Church of Jesus Christ|Church of Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, The|Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, The]]

A faithful member of the Church of Jesus Christ.

- Gather my Saints together unto me, [[Psalms 50.5|Ps. 50:5]].

- Saul did much evil to the Saints at Jerusalem, [[Acts 9.1|Acts 9:1–21]][[Acts 9.2|]][[Acts 9.3|]][[Acts 9.4|]][[Acts 9.5|]][[Acts 9.6|]][[Acts 9.7|]][[Acts 9.8|]][[Acts 9.9|]][[Acts 9.10|]][[Acts 9.11|]][[Acts 9.12|]][[Acts 9.13|]][[Acts 9.14|]][[Acts 9.15|]][[Acts 9.16|]][[Acts 9.17|]][[Acts 9.18|]][[Acts 9.19|]][[Acts 9.20|]][[Acts 9.21|]].
- Peter came down also to the Saints which dwelt at Lydda, [[Acts 9.32|Acts 9:32]].
- Grace and peace to all that be in Rome, called to be Saints, [[Romans 1.7|Rom. 1:7]].
- Ye are fellowcitizens with the Saints, [[Ephesians 2.19|Eph. 2:19–21]][[Ephesians 2.20|]][[Ephesians 2.21|]].

- I beheld the Church of the Lamb, who were the Saints of God, [[1 Nephi 14.12|1 Ne. 14:12]].
- The natural man is an enemy to God unless he becometh a saint through the atonement of Christ, [[Mosiah 3.19|Mosiah 3:19]].

- I, the Lord, have blessed the land for the use of my Saints, [[D&C 61.17|D&C 61:17]].
- Satan maketh war with the Saints of God, [[D&C 76.28|D&C 76:28–29]][[D&C 76.29|]].
- Labor diligently to prepare the Saints for the judgment which is to come, [[D&C 88.84|D&C 88:84–85]][[D&C 88.85|]].
- Saints should impart of their substance to the poor and afflicted, [[D&C 105.3|D&C 105:3]].
- The above offices I have given for the work of the ministry and the perfecting of my Saints, [[D&C 124.143|D&C 124:143]] ([[Ephesians 4.12|Eph. 4:12]]).
