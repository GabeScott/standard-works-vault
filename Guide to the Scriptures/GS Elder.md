# Elder

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/elder?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Melchizedek Priesthood|Melchizedek Priesthood]]; [[GS Priesthood|Priesthood]]

The term *elder* is used in various ways in the Bible. In the Old Testament it often refers to the older men in a tribe, who were usually entrusted with governmental affairs ([[Genesis 50.7|Gen. 50:7]]; [[Joshua 20.4|Josh. 20:4]]; [[Ruth 4.2|Ruth 4:2]]; [[Matthew 15.2|Matt. 15:2]]). Their age and experience made their counsel valuable. Their position was not necessarily a priesthood calling.

There were also ordained elders in the Melchizedek Priesthood in Old Testament times ([[Exodus 24.9|Ex. 24:9–11]][[Exodus 24.10|]][[Exodus 24.11|]]). In the New Testament, elders are mentioned as a priesthood office in the Church ([[James 5.14|James 5:14–15]][[James 5.15|]]). Among the Nephites there were also ordained elders in the priesthood ([[Alma 4.7|Alma 4:7, 16]][[Alma 4.16|]]; [[Moroni 3.1|Moro. 3:1]]). In this dispensation, Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery were the first elders to be ordained ([[D&C 20.2|D&C 20:2–3]][[D&C 20.3|]]).

Elder is now the title given to all holders of the Melchizedek Priesthood. For example, male missionaries are addressed as elders. Also, an Apostle is an elder, and it is proper to speak of members of the Quorum of the Twelve or Quorums of the Seventy by this title ([[D&C 20.38|D&C 20:38]]; [[1 Peter 5.1|1 Pet. 5:1]]). The duties of ordained elders in the Church today have been given in latter-day revelation ([[D&C 20.38|D&C 20:38–45]][[D&C 20.39|]][[D&C 20.40|]][[D&C 20.41|]][[D&C 20.42|]][[D&C 20.43|]][[D&C 20.44|]][[D&C 20.45|]]; [[D&C 42.44|42:44]]; [[D&C 46.2|46:2]]; [[D&C 107.12|107:12]]).

- Moses wrote unto all the elders of Israel, [[Deuteronomy 31.9|Deut. 31:9]].

- Barnabas sent relief to the elders of the church, [[Acts 11.30|Acts 11:30]].
- Elders were ordained in every church, [[Acts 14.23|Acts 14:23]] ([[Titus 1.5|Titus 1:5]]).
- Call for the elders to pray over the sick, [[James 5.14|James 5:14]].

- Elders were ordained by the laying on of hands, [[Alma 6.1|Alma 6:1]].

- Elders are to bless children, [[D&C 20.70|D&C 20:70]].
- Elders are to conduct meetings by the Holy Spirit, [[D&C 46.2|D&C 46:2]].
- Elders are to give an account of their stewardship, [[D&C 72.5|D&C 72:5]].
- Elders are to preach the gospel to the nations, [[D&C 133.8|D&C 133:8]].
