# Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, The

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/church-of-jesus-christ-of-latter-day-saints-the?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Church, Name of|Church, Name of]]; [[GS Church, Signs of the True|Church, Signs of the True]]; [[GS Church of Jesus Christ|Church of Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Restoration of the Gospel|Restoration of the Gospel]]

The name given to the Church of Christ in the latter days to distinguish it from the Church in other dispensations ([[D&C 115.3|D&C 115:3–4]][[D&C 115.4|]]).

- The Lord will pour down knowledge upon the Latter-day Saints, [[D&C 121.33|D&C 121:33]].
- Joseph Smith is the prophet and seer of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, [[D&C 127.12|D&C 127:12]].
- The great day of the Lord is at hand for the Latter-day Saints, [[D&C 128.21|D&C 128:21, 24]][[D&C 128.24|]].
- Joseph Smith has helped gather the Latter-day Saints, [[D&C 135.3|D&C 135:3]].
- Companies of Latter-day Saints are to be organized for the trip to the western territory of the United States, [[D&C 136.2|D&C 136:2]].
- Laws of marriage clarified for the Latter-day Saints, [[Official Declaration 1.1|OD 1]].
- The priesthood is given to all worthy male members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, [[Official Declaration 2.1|OD 2]].

- The account of the First Vision to all Latter-day Saints, [[Joseph Smith History 1.1|JS—H 1:1]].
