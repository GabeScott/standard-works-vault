# Gadianton Robbers

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/gadianton-robbers?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Secret Combinations|Secret Combinations]]

In the Book of Mormon, a band of robbers founded by a wicked Nephite named Gadianton. Their organization was based on secrecy and satanic oaths.

- Gadianton caused the destruction of the Nephite nation, [[Helaman 2.12|Hel. 2:12–13]][[Helaman 2.13|]].
- The devil gave secret oaths and covenants to Gadianton, [[Helaman 6.16|Hel. 6:16–32]][[Helaman 6.17|]][[Helaman 6.18|]][[Helaman 6.19|]][[Helaman 6.20|]][[Helaman 6.21|]][[Helaman 6.22|]][[Helaman 6.23|]][[Helaman 6.24|]][[Helaman 6.25|]][[Helaman 6.26|]][[Helaman 6.27|]][[Helaman 6.28|]][[Helaman 6.29|]][[Helaman 6.30|]][[Helaman 6.31|]][[Helaman 6.32|]].
- Secret combinations caused the destruction of the Jaredite nation, [[Ether 8.15|Ether 8:15–26]][[Ether 8.16|]][[Ether 8.17|]][[Ether 8.18|]][[Ether 8.19|]][[Ether 8.20|]][[Ether 8.21|]][[Ether 8.22|]][[Ether 8.23|]][[Ether 8.24|]][[Ether 8.25|]][[Ether 8.26|]].
