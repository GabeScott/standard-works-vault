# Archangel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/archangel?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adam|Adam]]; [[GS Michael|Michael]]

Michael, or Adam, is the archangel or chief angel.

- The Lord shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, [[1 Thessalonians 4.16|1 Thes. 4:16]].
- Michael is the archangel, [[Jude 1.9|Jude 1:9]] ([[D&C 29.26|D&C 29:26]]; [[D&C 88.112|88:112]]; [[D&C 128.20|128:20–21]][[D&C 128.21|]]).
