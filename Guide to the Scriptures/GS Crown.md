# Crown

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/crown?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Eternal Life|Eternal Life]]

A circular ornament worn on the head by rulers. It can be a symbol of celestial power, dominion, and godhood. Those who endure to the end by keeping all of God’s commandments will receive a crown of eternal life. (See [[D&C 20.14|D&C 20:14]]; [[Moses 7.56|Moses 7:56]]; [[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.1|JS—M 1:1]].)

- There is laid up for me a crown of righteousness, [[2 Timothy 4.8|2 Tim. 4:8]].
- Ye shall receive a crown of glory that fades not, [[1 Peter 5.4|1 Pet. 5:4]].

- The dead who die in the Lord will receive a crown of righteousness, [[D&C 29.13|D&C 29:13]].
- They shall receive a crown in the mansions of my Father, [[D&C 59.2|D&C 59:2]].
- The Lord prepares Saints to come up unto the crown prepared for them, [[D&C 78.15|D&C 78:15]].
- The Lord has promised his Saints a crown of glory at his right hand, [[D&C 104.7|D&C 104:7]].
