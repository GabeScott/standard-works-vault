# Flesh

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/flesh?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Body|Body]]; [[GS Carnal|Carnal]]; [[GS Mortal, Mortality|Mortal, Mortality]]; [[GS Natural Man|Natural Man]]

*Flesh* has several meanings: (1) the soft tissue that makes up the bodies of mankind, animals, fowls, or fish; (2) mortality; or (3) the physical or carnal nature of man.

## Body tissue

- Animals shall be meat for you, [[Genesis 9.3|Gen. 9:3]].
- Animals should not be killed needlessly, [[JST Genesis 9.10|JST, Gen. 9:10–11]][[JST Genesis 9.11|]] ([[D&C 49.21|D&C 49:21]]).

- Animals and fowl are for man to eat and use as clothing, [[D&C 49.18|D&C 49:18–19]][[D&C 49.19|]] ([[D&C 59.16|D&C 59:16–20]][[D&C 59.17|]][[D&C 59.18|]][[D&C 59.19|]][[D&C 59.20|]]).
- We should eat meat sparingly, [[D&C 89.12|D&C 89:12–15]][[D&C 89.13|]][[D&C 89.14|]][[D&C 89.15|]].

## Mortality

- Jesus is the only one begotten of the Father into mortality, [[John 1.14|John 1:14]] ([[Mosiah 15.1|Mosiah 15:1–3]][[Mosiah 15.2|]][[Mosiah 15.3|]]).

- Adam became the first flesh, [[Moses 3.7|Moses 3:7]].

## Carnal nature of man

- Cursed be the man that makes flesh his arm, [[Jeremiah 17.5|Jer. 17:5]].

- The spirit is willing, but the flesh is weak, [[Mark 14.38|Mark 14:38]].
- The lust of the flesh is not of the Father, [[1 John 2.16|1 Jn. 2:16]].

- Nephi was sorrowful because of his flesh and iniquities, [[2 Nephi 4.17|2 Ne. 4:17–18, 34]][[2 Nephi 4.18|]][[2 Nephi 4.34|]].
- Do not reconcile yourselves to the devil and the flesh, [[2 Nephi 10.24|2 Ne. 10:24]].
