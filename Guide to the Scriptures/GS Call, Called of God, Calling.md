# Call, Called of God, Calling

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/call-called-of-god-calling?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Authority|Authority]]; [[GS Choose, Chose, Chosen (verb)|Choose, Chose, Chosen (verb)]]; [[GS Chosen (adjective or noun)|Chosen (adjective or noun)]]; [[GS Ordain, Ordination|Ordain, Ordination]]; [[GS Steward, Stewardship|Steward, Stewardship]]

To be called of God is to receive an appointment or invitation from Him or His duly authorized Church leaders to serve Him in a particular way.

- He laid his hands upon him and gave him a charge, [[Numbers 27.23|Num. 27:23]].
- I ordained thee a prophet, [[Jeremiah 1.5|Jer. 1:5]].

- I have chosen you and ordained you, [[John 15.16|John 15:16]].
- Paul was called to be an Apostle, [[Romans 1.1|Rom. 1:1]].
- No man takes this honor unto himself, but he that is called of God, [[Hebrews 5.4|Heb. 5:4]].
- Jesus was called of God after the order of Melchizedek, [[Hebrews 5.10|Heb. 5:10]].

- I have been called to preach the word of God according to the spirit of revelation and prophecy, [[Alma 8.24|Alma 8:24]].
- Priests were called and prepared from the foundation of the world, [[Alma 13.3|Alma 13:3]].

- If ye have desires to serve God, ye are called, [[D&C 4.3|D&C 4:3]].
- Stand fast in the work to which I have called you, [[D&C 9.14|D&C 9:14]].
- You need not suppose that you are called to preach until you are called, [[D&C 11.15|D&C 11:15]].
- Elders are called to bring to pass the gathering of the elect, [[D&C 29.7|D&C 29:7]].
- No one shall preach my gospel or build up my Church unless he is ordained, [[D&C 42.11|D&C 42:11]].
- There are many called, but few are chosen, [[D&C 121.34|D&C 121:34]].

- Man must be called of God, [[Articles of Faith 1.5|A of F 1:5]].
