# Sensual, Sensuality

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/sensual-sensuality?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adultery|Adultery]]; [[GS Chastity|Chastity]]; [[GS Fornication|Fornication]]; [[GS Lust|Lust]]; [[GS Sexual Immorality|Sexual Immorality]]

A fondness or desire for unrighteous physical pleasure, especially sexual immorality.

- His master’s wife cast her eyes upon Joseph, [[Genesis 39.7|Gen. 39:7]].

- Whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery, [[Matthew 5.28|Matt. 5:28]] ([[3 Nephi 12.28|3 Ne. 12:28]]).
- Abstain from fleshly lusts, which war against the soul, [[1 Peter 2.11|1 Pet. 2:11]].
- The lust of the flesh and the lust of the eyes are not of the Father, [[1 John 2.16|1 Jn. 2:16]].

- Go no more after the lusts of your eyes, [[Alma 39.9|Alma 39:9]].

- By transgression of holy laws, man became sensual, [[D&C 20.20|D&C 20:20]].
- If any commit adultery in their hearts, they shall not have the Spirit, [[D&C 63.16|D&C 63:16]].
- Cease from all your lustful desires, [[D&C 88.121|D&C 88:121]].

- Men began to be carnal, sensual, devilish, [[Moses 5.13|Moses 5:13]] ([[Mosiah 16.3|Mosiah 16:3]]; [[Moses 6.49|Moses 6:49]]).
