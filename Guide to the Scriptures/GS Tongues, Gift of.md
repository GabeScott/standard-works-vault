# Tongues, Gift of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/tongues-gift-of?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Gifts of the Spirit|Gifts of the Spirit]]; [[GS Tongue|Tongue]]

A gift of the Holy Ghost that allows inspired individuals to speak in, understand, or interpret unfamiliar languages. We believe in the gift of tongues ([[Articles of Faith 1.7|A of F 1:7]]).

- They were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, [[Acts 2.4|Acts 2:4]].
- He that speaks in an unknown tongue speaks not to men, but to God, [[1 Corinthians 14.1|1 Cor. 14:1–5, 27–28]][[1 Corinthians 14.2|]][[1 Corinthians 14.3|]][[1 Corinthians 14.4|]][[1 Corinthians 14.5|]][[1 Corinthians 14.27|]][[1 Corinthians 14.28|]].
- Tongues are for a sign to them that believe not, [[1 Corinthians 14.22|1 Cor. 14:22–28]][[1 Corinthians 14.23|]][[1 Corinthians 14.24|]][[1 Corinthians 14.25|]][[1 Corinthians 14.26|]][[1 Corinthians 14.27|]][[1 Corinthians 14.28|]].

- Then cometh the baptism of fire and of the Holy Ghost; and then can ye speak with the tongue of angels, [[2 Nephi 31.13|2 Ne. 31:13–14]][[2 Nephi 31.14|]].
- Amaleki exhorted all men to believe in the gift of speaking with tongues, [[Omni 1.25|Omni 1:25]].

- It is given to some to speak with tongues; and to another the interpretation of tongues, [[D&C 46.24|D&C 46:24–25]][[D&C 46.25|]] ([[1 Corinthians 12.10|1 Cor. 12:10]]; [[Moroni 10.8|Moro. 10:8, 15–16]][[Moroni 10.15|]][[Moroni 10.16|]]).
- Let the gift of tongues be poured out, [[D&C 109.36|D&C 109:36]].
