# Nicodemus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/nicodemus?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Pharisees|Pharisees]]

In the New Testament, a righteous ruler of the Jews (probably of the Sanhedrin) and a Pharisee ([[John 3.1|John 3:1]]).

- He spoke to Jesus at night, [[John 3.1|John 3:1–21]][[John 3.2|]][[John 3.3|]][[John 3.4|]][[John 3.5|]][[John 3.6|]][[John 3.7|]][[John 3.8|]][[John 3.9|]][[John 3.10|]][[John 3.11|]][[John 3.12|]][[John 3.13|]][[John 3.14|]][[John 3.15|]][[John 3.16|]][[John 3.17|]][[John 3.18|]][[John 3.19|]][[John 3.20|]][[John 3.21|]].
- He defended Christ to the Pharisees, [[John 7.50|John 7:50–53]][[John 7.51|]][[John 7.52|]][[John 7.53|]].
- He brought spices to Jesus’ burial, [[John 19.39|John 19:39–40]][[John 19.40|]].
