# Endure

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/endure?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adversity|Adversity]]; [[GS Patience|Patience]]; [[GS Tempt, Temptation|Tempt, Temptation]]

To remain firm in a commitment to be true to the commandments of God despite temptation, opposition, and adversity.

- He that endureth to the end shall be saved, [[Matthew 10.22|Matt. 10:22]] ([[Mark 13.13|Mark 13:13]]).
- They have no root and so endure but for a time, [[Mark 4.17|Mark 4:17]].
- Charity endureth all things, [[1 Corinthians 13.7|1 Cor. 13:7]].
- After Abraham had patiently endured, he obtained the promise, [[Hebrews 6.15|Heb. 6:15]].

- If they endure unto the end they shall be lifted up at the last day, [[1 Nephi 13.37|1 Ne. 13:37]].
- If ye shall be obedient to the commandments, and endure to the end, ye shall be saved, [[1 Nephi 22.31|1 Ne. 22:31]] ([[Alma 5.13|Alma 5:13]]).
- If ye shall press forward, feasting upon the word of Christ, and endure to the end, ye shall have eternal life, [[2 Nephi 31.20|2 Ne. 31:20]] ([[3 Nephi 15.9|3 Ne. 15:9]]; [[D&C 14.7|D&C 14:7]]).
- Whoso taketh upon him my name, and endureth to the end, shall be saved, [[3 Nephi 27.6|3 Ne. 27:6]].

- Whosoever endureth of my church to the end, him will I establish upon my rock, [[D&C 10.69|D&C 10:69]].
- He that endureth in faith shall overcome the world, [[D&C 63.20|D&C 63:20, 47]][[D&C 63.47|]].
- All thrones and dominions shall be set forth upon all who have endured valiantly for the gospel of Jesus Christ, [[D&C 121.29|D&C 121:29]].
