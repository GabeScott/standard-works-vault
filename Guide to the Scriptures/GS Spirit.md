# Spirit

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/spirit?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Death, Physical|Death, Physical]]; [[GS Man, Men|Man, Men]]; [[GS Resurrection|Resurrection]]; [[GS Soul|Soul]]

That part of a living being that exists before mortal birth, dwells in the physical body during mortality, and exists after death as a separate being until the Resurrection. All living things—mankind, animals, and plants—were spirits before any form of life existed upon the earth ([[Genesis 2.4|Gen. 2:4–5]][[Genesis 2.5|]]; [[Moses 3.4|Moses 3:4–7]][[Moses 3.5|]][[Moses 3.6|]][[Moses 3.7|]]). The spirit body looks like the physical body ([[1 Nephi 11.11|1 Ne. 11:11]]; [[Ether 3.15|Ether 3:15–16]][[Ether 3.16|]]; [[D&C 77.2|D&C 77:2]]; [[D&C 129.1|129]][[D&C 129.2|]][[D&C 129.3|]][[D&C 129.4|]][[D&C 129.5|]][[D&C 129.6|]][[D&C 129.7|]][[D&C 129.8|]][[D&C 129.9|]]). Spirit is matter, but it is more fine or pure than mortal element or matter ([[D&C 131.7|D&C 131:7]]).

Every person is literally a son or a daughter of God, having been born as a spirit to Heavenly Parents before being born to mortal parents on the earth ([[Hebrews 12.9|Heb. 12:9]]). Each person on earth has an immortal spirit body in addition to a body of flesh and bone. As sometimes defined in scripture, the spirit and the physical body together constitute the soul ([[Genesis 2.7|Gen. 2:7]]; [[D&C 88.15|D&C 88:15]]; [[Moses 3.7|Moses 3:7, 9, 19]][[Moses 3.9|]][[Moses 3.19|]]; [[Abraham 5.7|Abr. 5:7]]). A spirit can live without a physical body, but the physical body cannot live without the spirit ([[James 2.26|James 2:26]]). Physical death is the separation of the spirit from the body. In the Resurrection, the spirit is reunited with the same physical body of flesh and bone it possessed as a mortal, with two major differences: they will never be separated again, and the physical body will be immortal and perfected ([[Alma 11.45|Alma 11:45]]; [[D&C 138.16|D&C 138:16–17]][[D&C 138.17|]]).
