# Agency

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/agency?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Account, Accountable, Accountability|Account, Accountable, Accountability]]; [[GS Free, Freedom|Free, Freedom]]

The ability and privilege God gives people to choose and to act for themselves.

- Of every tree thou mayest freely eat, [[Genesis 2.16|Gen. 2:16]].
- Choose you this day whom ye will serve, [[Joshua 24.15|Josh. 24:15]] ([[Alma 30.8|Alma 30:8]]; [[Moses 6.33|Moses 6:33]]).

- Man could not act for himself save it should be that he was enticed, [[2 Nephi 2.15|2 Ne. 2:15–16]][[2 Nephi 2.16|]].
- Men are free to choose liberty and eternal life or captivity and death, [[2 Nephi 2.27|2 Ne. 2:27]].
- Ye are free; ye are permitted to act for yourselves, [[Helaman 14.30|Hel. 14:30]].

- A third part of the hosts of heaven turned he away because of their agency, [[D&C 29.36|D&C 29:36]].
- It must needs be that the devil should tempt men, or they could not be agents, [[D&C 29.39|D&C 29:39]].
- Let every man choose for himself, [[D&C 37.4|D&C 37:4]].
- Every man may act according to the moral agency which I have given unto him, [[D&C 101.78|D&C 101:78]].

- Satan sought to destroy the agency of man, [[Moses 4.3|Moses 4:3]].
- The Lord gave unto man his agency, [[Moses 7.32|Moses 7:32]].
