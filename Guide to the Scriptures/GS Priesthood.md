# Priesthood

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/priesthood?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Aaronic Priesthood|Aaronic Priesthood]]; [[GS Authority|Authority]]; [[GS Keys of the Priesthood|Keys of the Priesthood]]; [[GS Melchizedek Priesthood|Melchizedek Priesthood]]; [[GS Oath and Covenant of the Priesthood|Oath and Covenant of the Priesthood]]; [[GS Ordain, Ordination|Ordain, Ordination]]; [[GS Power|Power]]

The authority and power that God gives to man to act in all things for the salvation of man ([[D&C 50.26|D&C 50:26–27]][[D&C 50.27|]]). Male members of the Church who hold the priesthood are organized into quorums and are authorized to perform ordinances and certain administrative functions in the Church.

- Their anointing shall surely be an everlasting priesthood, [[Exodus 40.15|Ex. 40:15]] ([[Numbers 25.13|Num. 25:13]]).

- I have ordained you, [[John 15.16|John 15:16]].
- Ye are built up a spiritual house, an holy priesthood, [[1 Peter 2.5|1 Pet. 2:5]].
- Ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, [[1 Peter 2.9|1 Pet. 2:9]] ([[Exodus 19.6|Ex. 19:6]]).

- Men are called as high priests because of their exceeding faith and good works, [[Alma 13.1|Alma 13:1–12]][[Alma 13.2|]][[Alma 13.3|]][[Alma 13.4|]][[Alma 13.5|]][[Alma 13.6|]][[Alma 13.7|]][[Alma 13.8|]][[Alma 13.9|]][[Alma 13.10|]][[Alma 13.11|]][[Alma 13.12|]].
- I give unto you power to baptize, [[3 Nephi 11.21|3 Ne. 11:21]].
- Ye shall have power to give the Holy Ghost, [[Moroni 2.2|Moro. 2:2]].

- I will reveal unto you the priesthood, by the hand of Elijah, [[D&C 2.1|D&C 2:1]] ([[Joseph Smith History 1.38|JS—H 1:38]]).
- The Lord confirmed a priesthood also upon Aaron and his seed, [[D&C 84.18|D&C 84:18]].
- This greater priesthood administereth the gospel, [[D&C 84.19|D&C 84:19]].
- He took Moses out of their midst, and the Holy Priesthood, [[D&C 84.25|D&C 84:25]].
- The oath and covenant of the priesthood is described, [[D&C 84.33|D&C 84:33–42]][[D&C 84.34|]][[D&C 84.35|]][[D&C 84.36|]][[D&C 84.37|]][[D&C 84.38|]][[D&C 84.39|]][[D&C 84.40|]][[D&C 84.41|]][[D&C 84.42|]].
- The priesthood hath continued through the lineage of your fathers, [[D&C 86.8|D&C 86:8]].
- There are, in the Church, two priesthoods, [[D&C 107.1|D&C 107:1]].
- The first priesthood is the Holy Priesthood, after the Order of the Son of God, [[D&C 107.2|D&C 107:2–4]][[D&C 107.3|]][[D&C 107.4|]].
- The rights of the priesthood are inseparably connected with the powers of heaven, [[D&C 121.36|D&C 121:36]].
- No power or influence can or ought to be maintained by virtue of the priesthood only by persuasion and love unfeigned, [[D&C 121.41|D&C 121:41]].
- Every faithful, worthy male member of the Church may receive the priesthood, [[Official Declaration 2.1|OD 2]].

- We believe that a man must be called of God, [[Articles of Faith 1.5|A of F 1:5]].
