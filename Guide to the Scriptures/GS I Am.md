# I Am

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/i-am?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jehovah|Jehovah]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

One of the names of the Lord Jesus Christ.

- God said unto Moses, I Am That I Am, [[Exodus 3.14|Ex. 3:14–15]][[Exodus 3.15|]].
- I am the Lord, [[Exodus 6.2|Ex. 6:2–3]][[Exodus 6.3|]].

- Before Abraham was, I am, [[John 8.56|John 8:56–59]][[John 8.57|]][[John 8.58|]][[John 8.59|]].

- Listen to the voice of Jesus Christ, the Great I Am, [[D&C 29.1|D&C 29:1]] ([[D&C 38.1|D&C 38:1]]; [[D&C 39.1|39:1]]).
