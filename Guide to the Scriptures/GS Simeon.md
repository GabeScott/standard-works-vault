# Simeon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/simeon?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Israel|Israel]]; [[GS Jacob, Son of Isaac|Jacob, Son of Isaac]]

In the Old Testament, the second son of Jacob and his wife Leah ([[Genesis 29.33|Gen. 29:33]]; [[Genesis 35.23|35:23]]; [[Exodus 1.2|Ex. 1:2]]). He joined Levi in the massacre of the Shechemites ([[Genesis 34.25|Gen. 34:25–31]][[Genesis 34.26|]][[Genesis 34.27|]][[Genesis 34.28|]][[Genesis 34.29|]][[Genesis 34.30|]][[Genesis 34.31|]]). Jacob’s prophecy concerning Simeon is found in [[Genesis 49.5|Genesis 49:5–7]][[Genesis 49.6|]][[Genesis 49.7|]].

## The tribe of Simeon

The descendants of Simeon often dwelt with the tribe of Judah and within the borders of the kingdom of Judah ([[Joshua 19.1|Josh. 19:1–9]][[Joshua 19.2|]][[Joshua 19.3|]][[Joshua 19.4|]][[Joshua 19.5|]][[Joshua 19.6|]][[Joshua 19.7|]][[Joshua 19.8|]][[Joshua 19.9|]]; [[1 Chronicles 4.24|1 Chr. 4:24–33]][[1 Chronicles 4.25|]][[1 Chronicles 4.26|]][[1 Chronicles 4.27|]][[1 Chronicles 4.28|]][[1 Chronicles 4.29|]][[1 Chronicles 4.30|]][[1 Chronicles 4.31|]][[1 Chronicles 4.32|]][[1 Chronicles 4.33|]]). The tribe of Simeon joined Judah in battle against the Canaanites ([[Judges 1.3|Judg. 1:3, 17]][[Judges 1.17|]]). They also later joined David’s armies ([[1 Chronicles 12.25|1 Chr. 12:25]]).
