# Titus, Epistle to

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/titus-epistle-to?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Paul|Paul]]; [[GS Pauline Epistles|Pauline Epistles]]; [[GS Titus|Titus]]

While Paul was temporarily free from Roman imprisonment, he wrote the epistle to Titus, who was in Crete. The letter mainly addresses questions relating to the internal discipline and organization of the Church.

[[Titus 2.1|Chapters 2–3]][[Titus 2.2|]][[Titus 2.3|]][[Titus 2.4|]][[Titus 2.5|]][[Titus 2.6|]][[Titus 2.7|]][[Titus 2.8|]][[Titus 2.9|]][[Titus 2.10|]][[Titus 2.11|]][[Titus 2.12|]][[Titus 2.13|]][[Titus 2.14|]][[Titus 2.15|]] contain general teachings and personal messages to Titus about the treatment of various groups in the Cretan Church. Paul encouraged the Saints to overcome perversity, be sober and faithful, and maintain good works.
