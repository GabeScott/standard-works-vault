# Testimony

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/testimony?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Holy Ghost|Holy Ghost]]; [[GS Testify|Testify]]; [[GS Witness|Witness]]

Knowledge and a spiritual witness given by the Holy Ghost. A testimony can also be an official or legal declaration of what a person perceives as truth ([[D&C 102.26|D&C 102:26]]).

- I know that my redeemer liveth, [[Job 19.25|Job 19:25–26]][[Job 19.26|]].

- Be not ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, [[2 Timothy 1.8|2 Tim. 1:8]].
- The testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy, [[Revelation 19.10|Rev. 19:10]].

- Stand as witnesses of God at all times, [[Mosiah 18.9|Mosiah 18:9]].
- There was no way to reclaim the people save by bearing down in pure testimony against them, [[Alma 4.19|Alma 4:19–20]][[Alma 4.20|]].
- I have all things as a testimony that these things are true, [[Alma 30.41|Alma 30:41–44]][[Alma 30.42|]][[Alma 30.43|]][[Alma 30.44|]].
- Ye receive no witness until after the trial of your faith, [[Ether 12.6|Ether 12:6]].

- Did I not speak peace to your mind? What greater witness can you have than from God, [[D&C 6.22|D&C 6:22–23]][[D&C 6.23|]].
- And now, after the many testimonies which have been given of him, this is the testimony which we give of him, [[D&C 76.22|D&C 76:22–24]][[D&C 76.23|]][[D&C 76.24|]].
- I sent you out to testify and warn the people, [[D&C 88.81|D&C 88:81–82]][[D&C 88.82|]].
- The testators are now dead, and their testament is in force, [[D&C 135.4|D&C 135:4–5]][[D&C 135.5|]].

- Enoch beheld angels bearing testimony of the Father and Son, [[Moses 7.27|Moses 7:27]].
- Though I was hated and persecuted for saying that I had seen a vision, yet it was true, [[Joseph Smith History 1.24|JS—H 1:24–25]][[Joseph Smith History 1.25|]].
