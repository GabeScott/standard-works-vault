# Liberty

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/liberty?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Agency|Agency]]; [[GS Captivity|Captivity]]; [[GS Free, Freedom|Free, Freedom]]

The state or condition of being able to act and think freely. Obedience to gospel principles frees a person from the spiritual bondage of sin ([[John 8.31|John 8:31–36]][[John 8.32|]][[John 8.33|]][[John 8.34|]][[John 8.35|]][[John 8.36|]]).

- I will walk at liberty: for I seek thy precepts, [[Psalms 119.45|Ps. 119:45]].

- Where the Spirit of the Lord is, there is liberty, [[2 Corinthians 3.17|2 Cor. 3:17]].
- Stand fast in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free, [[Galatians 5.1|Gal. 5:1]] ([[D&C 88.86|D&C 88:86]]).

- Men are free to choose liberty and eternal life, [[2 Nephi 2.27|2 Ne. 2:27]].
- This land shall be a land of liberty, [[2 Nephi 10.11|2 Ne. 10:11]].
- Moroni planted the standard of liberty among the Nephites, [[Alma 46.36|Alma 46:36]].

- The Lord and his servants declare liberty to the captive spirits, [[D&C 138.18|D&C 138:18, 31, 42]][[D&C 138.31|]][[D&C 138.42|]].
