# Gethsemane

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/gethsemane?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Olives, Mount of|Olives, Mount of]]

A garden spoken of in the New Testament as being near the Mount of Olives. In Aramaic, the word *gethsemane* means “olive press.” Jesus went to the garden on the night that Judas betrayed Him. There He prayed and suffered in Gethsemane for the sins of mankind ([[Matthew 26.36|Matt. 26:36, 39]][[Matthew 26.39|]]; [[Mark 14.32|Mark 14:32]]; [[John 18.1|John 18:1]]; [[Alma 21.9|Alma 21:9]]; [[D&C 19.15|D&C 19:15–19]][[D&C 19.16|]][[D&C 19.17|]][[D&C 19.18|]][[D&C 19.19|]]).
