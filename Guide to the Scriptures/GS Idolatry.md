# Idolatry

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/idolatry?lang=eng)

The worship of idols or an excessive attachment or devotion to anything.

- Thou shalt have no other gods before me, [[Exodus 20.3|Ex. 20:3]] ([[Mosiah 12.35|Mosiah 12:35]]; [[Mosiah 13.12|13:12–13]][[Mosiah 13.13|]]).
- If thou walk after other gods, ye shall surely perish, [[Deuteronomy 8.19|Deut. 8:19]].
- Stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry, [[1 Samuel 15.23|1 Sam. 15:23]].
- Thou hast discovered thyself to another than me, [[Isaiah 57.8|Isa. 57:8]].
- Thou hast praised the gods of silver and gold, [[Daniel 5.23|Dan. 5:23]].

- Ye cannot serve God and mammon, [[Matthew 6.24|Matt. 6:24]].
- Covetousness is idolatry, [[Colossians 3.5|Col. 3:5]].
- Children, keep yourselves from idols, [[1 John 5.21|1 Jn. 5:21]].

- Wo unto those that worship idols, [[2 Nephi 9.37|2 Ne. 9:37]].
- The idolatry of the people of Nephi brought upon them their wars and destructions, [[Alma 50.21|Alma 50:21]].

- Every man walketh after the image of his own god, [[D&C 1.16|D&C 1:16]].
- Let them labor with their own hands that there be no idolatry, [[D&C 52.39|D&C 52:39]].

- Abraham’s father was led away by idolatry, [[Abraham 1.27|Abr. 1:27]].
