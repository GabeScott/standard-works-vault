# Yoke

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/yoke?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Disciple|Disciple]]

A device put around the necks of animals or men to harness them together. The yoke of Christ is a symbol for discipleship, whereas the yoke of bondage is a symbol for oppression.

- My yoke is easy, and my burden is light, [[Matthew 11.29|Matt. 11:29–30]][[Matthew 11.30|]].
- Be ye not unequally yoked together with unbelievers, [[2 Corinthians 6.14|2 Cor. 6:14]].
- Be not entangled with the yoke of bondage, [[Galatians 5.1|Gal. 5:1]].

- Neither do we desire to bring any one to the yoke of bondage, [[Alma 44.2|Alma 44:2]].

- The sufferings of the Saints is an iron yoke, a strong band, and the chains of hell, [[D&C 123.1|D&C 123:1–3, 7–8]][[D&C 123.2|]][[D&C 123.3|]][[D&C 123.7|]][[D&C 123.8|]].
