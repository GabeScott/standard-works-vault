# Mulek

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/mulek?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Zedekiah|Zedekiah]]

A son of the Old Testament king Zedekiah (about 589 ). The Bible records that all the sons of Zedekiah were slain ([[2 Kings 25.7|2 Kgs. 25:7]]), but the Book of Mormon clarifies that Mulek survived ([[Helaman 8.21|Hel. 8:21]]).

- Zarahemla was a descendant of Mulek, [[Mosiah 25.2|Mosiah 25:2]].
- The people of Mulek joined the Nephites, [[Mosiah 25.13|Mosiah 25:13]].
- The Lord brought Mulek into the land north, [[Helaman 6.10|Hel. 6:10]].
- All of Zedekiah’s sons were slain except Mulek, [[Helaman 8.21|Hel. 8:21]].
