# Celestial Glory

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/celestial-glory?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Degrees of Glory|Degrees of Glory]]; [[GS Eternal Life|Eternal Life]]; [[GS Exaltation|Exaltation]]

The highest of the three degrees of glory that a person can attain after this life. Here the righteous will dwell in the presence of God the Father and His Son Jesus Christ.

- The glory of the celestial is one, [[1 Corinthians 15.40|1 Cor. 15:40]] ([[D&C 76.96|D&C 76:96]]).
- Paul was caught up to the third heaven, [[2 Corinthians 12.2|2 Cor. 12:2]].

- Celestial glory was shown in vision, [[D&C 76.50|D&C 76:50–70]][[D&C 76.51|]][[D&C 76.52|]][[D&C 76.53|]][[D&C 76.54|]][[D&C 76.55|]][[D&C 76.56|]][[D&C 76.57|]][[D&C 76.58|]][[D&C 76.59|]][[D&C 76.60|]][[D&C 76.61|]][[D&C 76.62|]][[D&C 76.63|]][[D&C 76.64|]][[D&C 76.65|]][[D&C 76.66|]][[D&C 76.67|]][[D&C 76.68|]][[D&C 76.69|]][[D&C 76.70|]].
- If Saints desire a place in the celestial world, they must prepare, [[D&C 78.7|D&C 78:7]].
- He who is not able to abide the law of a celestial kingdom cannot abide a celestial glory, [[D&C 88.15|D&C 88:15–22]][[D&C 88.16|]][[D&C 88.17|]][[D&C 88.18|]][[D&C 88.19|]][[D&C 88.20|]][[D&C 88.21|]][[D&C 88.22|]].
- In the celestial glory there are three heavens; conditions are set forth for attaining the highest, [[D&C 131.1|D&C 131:1–2]][[D&C 131.2|]].
- Children who die before the age of accountability are saved in the celestial kingdom, [[D&C 137.10|D&C 137:10]].
