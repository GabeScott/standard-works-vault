# Parable

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/parable?lang=eng)

A simple story used to illustrate and teach a spiritual truth or principle. A parable is based on comparing an ordinary object or event to a truth, and the underlying meaning or message of a parable is often hidden to listeners who are not spiritually prepared to receive it ([[Matthew 13.10|Matt. 13:10–17]][[Matthew 13.11|]][[Matthew 13.12|]][[Matthew 13.13|]][[Matthew 13.14|]][[Matthew 13.15|]][[Matthew 13.16|]][[Matthew 13.17|]]).

Jesus often taught with parables. For a listing of His key parables, see [Harmony of the Gospels](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/harmony/introduction?lang=eng) in the Bible appendix.
