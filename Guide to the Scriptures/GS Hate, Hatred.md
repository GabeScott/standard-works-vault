# Hate, Hatred

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/hate-hatred?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Enmity|Enmity]]; [[GS Love|Love]]; [[GS Vengeance|Vengeance]]

Hate is a strong dislike for someone or for something.

- I, God, visit the iniquity of the fathers upon the children of them that hate me, [[Exodus 20.5|Ex. 20:5]].
- These six things doth the Lord hate, [[Proverbs 6.16|Prov. 6:16]].
- A foolish man despiseth his mother, [[Proverbs 15.20|Prov. 15:20]].
- He is despised and rejected of men, [[Isaiah 53.3|Isa. 53:3]].

- Do good to them that hate you, [[Matthew 5.44|Matt. 5:44]].
- Either he will hate the one, and love the other; or else he will hold to the one, and despise the other, [[Matthew 6.24|Matt. 6:24]].
- Ye shall be hated of all men for my name’s sake, [[Matthew 10.22|Matt. 10:22]].
- Every one that doeth evil hateth the light, [[John 3.20|John 3:20]].
- Let no man despise thy youth, [[1 Timothy 4.12|1 Tim. 4:12]].

- Because they are rich, they despise the poor, [[2 Nephi 9.30|2 Ne. 9:30]].
- Despise not the revelations of God, [[Jacob 4.8|Jacob 4:8]].
- They had an eternal hatred against us, [[Jacob 7.24|Jacob 7:24]].

- Men set at naught the counsels of God, and despise his words, [[D&C 3.7|D&C 3:7]].

- I was hated and persecuted for saying that I had seen a vision, [[Joseph Smith History 1.25|JS—H 1:25]].
