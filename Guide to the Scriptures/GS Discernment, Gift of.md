# Discernment, Gift of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/discernment-gift-of?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Gifts of the Spirit|Gifts of the Spirit]]

To understand or know something through the power of the Spirit. The gift of discernment is one of the gifts of the Spirit. It includes perceiving the true character of people and the source and meaning of spiritual manifestations.

- Man looks on the outward appearance, but the Lord looks on the heart, [[1 Samuel 16.7|1 Sam. 16:7]].
- Wo unto them that call evil good, [[Isaiah 5.20|Isa. 5:20]] ([[2 Nephi 15.20|2 Ne. 15:20]]).

- The things of God are spiritually discerned, [[1 Corinthians 2.14|1 Cor. 2:14]].
- To some people is given the gift of discerning of spirits, [[1 Corinthians 12.10|1 Cor. 12:10]].

- Ammon could discern his thoughts, [[Alma 18.18|Alma 18:18]].
- The small voice did pierce them that did hear, [[3 Nephi 11.3|3 Ne. 11:3]].

- That ye may not be deceived, seek the best gifts, [[D&C 46.8|D&C 46:8, 23]][[D&C 46.23|]].
- Church leaders are given power to discern the gifts of the Spirit, [[D&C 46.27|D&C 46:27]].
- That body which is filled with light comprehendeth all things, [[D&C 88.67|D&C 88:67]].

- Moses beheld the earth, discerning it by the Spirit of God, [[Moses 1.27|Moses 1:27]].
