# Enmity

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/enmity?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Envy|Envy]]; [[GS Love|Love]]; [[GS Vengeance|Vengeance]]

In the scriptures, antagonism, hostility, and hate.

- I will put enmity between thee and the woman, [[Genesis 3.15|Gen. 3:15]] ([[Moses 4.21|Moses 4:21]]).

- The carnal mind is enmity against God, [[Romans 8.7|Rom. 8:7]].
- The friendship of the world is enmity with God, [[James 4.4|James 4:4]].

- In that day enmity shall cease from before my face, [[D&C 101.26|D&C 101:26]].
