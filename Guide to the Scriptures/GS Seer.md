# Seer

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/seer?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Prophet|Prophet]]; [[GS Urim and Thummim|Urim and Thummim]]

A person authorized of God to see with spiritual eyes things that God has hidden from the world ([[Moses 6.35|Moses 6:35–38]][[Moses 6.36|]][[Moses 6.37|]][[Moses 6.38|]]). He is a revelator and a prophet ([[Mosiah 8.13|Mosiah 8:13–16]][[Mosiah 8.14|]][[Mosiah 8.15|]][[Mosiah 8.16|]]). In the Book of Mormon, Ammon taught that only a seer could use special interpreters, or a Urim and Thummim ([[Mosiah 8.13|Mosiah 8:13]]; [[Mosiah 28.16|28:16]]). A seer knows the past, present, and future. Anciently, a prophet was often called a seer ([[1 Samuel 9.9|1 Sam. 9:9]]; [[2 Samuel 24.11|2 Sam. 24:11]]).

Joseph Smith is the great seer of the latter days ([[D&C 21.1|D&C 21:1]]; [[D&C 135.3|135:3]]). In addition, the First Presidency and the Council of the Twelve are sustained as prophets, seers, and revelators.

- This is a rebellious people that say to the seers, See not; and to the prophets, Prophesy not, [[Isaiah 30.9|Isa. 30:9–10]][[Isaiah 30.10|]].

- A choice seer will I raise up out of the fruit of thy loins, [[2 Nephi 3.6|2 Ne. 3:6–15]][[2 Nephi 3.7|]][[2 Nephi 3.8|]][[2 Nephi 3.9|]][[2 Nephi 3.10|]][[2 Nephi 3.11|]][[2 Nephi 3.12|]][[2 Nephi 3.13|]][[2 Nephi 3.14|]][[2 Nephi 3.15|]].

- Here is wisdom; yea, to be a seer, a revelator, a translator, and a prophet, [[D&C 107.92|D&C 107:92]].
- The Lord appointed Hyrum Smith to be a prophet, seer, and revelator to the Church, [[D&C 124.91|D&C 124:91–94]][[D&C 124.92|]][[D&C 124.93|]][[D&C 124.94|]].
