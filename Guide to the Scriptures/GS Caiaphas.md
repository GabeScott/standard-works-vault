# Caiaphas

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/caiaphas?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Annas|Annas]]; [[GS Sadducees|Sadducees]]

In the New Testament, a high priest and son-in-law of Annas. Caiaphas took an active part in opposing Jesus and His disciples ([[Matthew 26.3|Matt. 26:3–4]][[Matthew 26.4|]]; [[John 11.47|John 11:47–51]][[John 11.48|]][[John 11.49|]][[John 11.50|]][[John 11.51|]]; [[John 18.13|18:13–14]][[John 18.14|]]).
