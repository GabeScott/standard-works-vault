# Japheth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/japheth?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Noah, Bible Patriarch|Noah, Bible Patriarch]]

The eldest son of Noah, an Old Testament prophet ([[Moses 8.12|Moses 8:12]]).

- Noah begat Japheth, [[Genesis 5.32|Gen. 5:32]] ([[Genesis 6.10|Gen. 6:10]]; [[Moses 8.12|Moses 8:12]]).
- Japheth and his wife entered Noah’s ark, [[Genesis 7.13|Gen. 7:13]].
- Japheth came out of the ark, [[Genesis 9.18|Gen. 9:18]].
- God shall enlarge Japheth, [[Genesis 9.27|Gen. 9:27]].
