# Liberty Jail, Missouri (USA)

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/liberty-jail-missouri-usa?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Smith, Joseph, Jr.|Smith, Joseph, Jr.]]

A small jail in which the Prophet Joseph Smith and others were unjustly imprisoned from November 1838 to April 1839.
