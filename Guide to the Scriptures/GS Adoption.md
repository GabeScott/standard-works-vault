# Adoption

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/adoption?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Abraham|Abraham—The seed of Abraham]]; [[GS Children of Christ|Children of Christ]]; [[GS Israel|Israel]]; [[GS Sons and Daughters of God|Sons and Daughters of God]]

The scriptures speak of two types of adoption.

(1) A person who is of non-Israelite lineage becomes a member of the family of Abraham and the house of Israel by having faith in Jesus Christ, repenting, being baptized by immersion, and receiving the Holy Ghost ([[2 Nephi 31.17|2 Ne. 31:17–18]][[2 Nephi 31.18|]]; [[D&C 84.73|D&C 84:73–74]][[D&C 84.74|]]; [[Abraham 2.6|Abr. 2:6, 10–11]][[Abraham 2.10|]][[Abraham 2.11|]]).

(2) All who have received the saving ordinances of the gospel become sons and daughters of Jesus Christ by continued obedience to His commandments ([[Romans 8.15|Rom. 8:15–17]][[Romans 8.16|]][[Romans 8.17|]]; [[Galatians 3.24|Gal. 3:24–29]][[Galatians 3.25|]][[Galatians 3.26|]][[Galatians 3.27|]][[Galatians 3.28|]][[Galatians 3.29|]]; [[Galatians 4.5|4:5–7]][[Galatians 4.6|]][[Galatians 4.7|]]; [[Mosiah 5.7|Mosiah 5:7–8]][[Mosiah 5.8|]]).
