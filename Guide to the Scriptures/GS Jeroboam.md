# Jeroboam

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/jeroboam?lang=eng)

In the Old Testament, Jeroboam was the first king of the northern part of divided Israel. He was a member of the tribe of Ephraim. The wicked Jeroboam led a revolt against the house of Judah and the family of David.

- Jeroboam set up idols for the people in Dan and Bethel to worship, [[1 Kings 12.28|1 Kgs. 12:28–29]][[1 Kings 12.29|]].
- Ahijah rebuked Jeroboam, [[1 Kings 14.6|1 Kgs. 14:6–16]][[1 Kings 14.7|]][[1 Kings 14.8|]][[1 Kings 14.9|]][[1 Kings 14.10|]][[1 Kings 14.11|]][[1 Kings 14.12|]][[1 Kings 14.13|]][[1 Kings 14.14|]][[1 Kings 14.15|]][[1 Kings 14.16|]].
- Jeroboam was remembered for having brought a terrible sin to Israel, [[1 Kings 15.34|1 Kgs. 15:34]] ([[1 Kings 12.30|1 Kgs. 12:30]]).
