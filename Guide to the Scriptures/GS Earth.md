# Earth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/earth?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Create, Creation|Create, Creation]]; [[GS World|World]]

The planet on which we live, created by God through Jesus Christ to be used by man during his mortal probation. Its final destiny is to become glorified and exalted ([[D&C 77.1|D&C 77:1–2]][[D&C 77.2|]]; [[D&C 130.8|130:8–9]][[D&C 130.9|]]). The earth will become an eternal inheritance of those who have lived worthy of a celestial glory ([[D&C 88.14|D&C 88:14–26]][[D&C 88.15|]][[D&C 88.16|]][[D&C 88.17|]][[D&C 88.18|]][[D&C 88.19|]][[D&C 88.20|]][[D&C 88.21|]][[D&C 88.22|]][[D&C 88.23|]][[D&C 88.24|]][[D&C 88.25|]][[D&C 88.26|]]). They will enjoy the presence of the Father and the Son ([[D&C 76.62|D&C 76:62]]).

## Created for man

- God gave man dominion over the earth, [[Genesis 1.28|Gen. 1:28]] ([[Moses 2.28|Moses 2:28]]).
- The earth is the Lord’s, [[Exodus 9.29|Ex. 9:29]] ([[Psalms 24.1|Ps. 24:1]]).
- The Lord has given the earth to the children of men, [[Psalms 115.16|Ps. 115:16]].
- I have made the earth and created man upon it, [[Isaiah 45.12|Isa. 45:12]].

- By the power of his word man came upon the earth, [[Jacob 4.9|Jacob 4:9]].

- The earth shall be given to those who have taken the Holy Spirit for their guide, [[D&C 45.56|D&C 45:56–58]][[D&C 45.57|]][[D&C 45.58|]] ([[D&C 103.7|D&C 103:7]]).
- Those who have obeyed the gospel are rewarded with the good things of the earth, [[D&C 59.3|D&C 59:3]].
- The poor and the meek of the earth shall inherit it, [[D&C 88.17|D&C 88:17]] ([[Matthew 5.5|Matt. 5:5]]; [[3 Nephi 12.5|3 Ne. 12:5]]).

- We will make an earth, and we will prove them, [[Abraham 3.24|Abr. 3:24–25]][[Abraham 3.25|]].

## A living entity

- The earth abideth forever, [[Ecclesiastes 1.4|Eccl. 1:4]].

- The sea of glass is the earth in its sanctified, immortal, and eternal state, [[D&C 77.1|D&C 77:1]].
- The earth must be sanctified and prepared for the celestial glory, [[D&C 88.18|D&C 88:18–19]][[D&C 88.19|]].

- The earth mourned aloud, [[Moses 7.48|Moses 7:48]].

## Division of the earth

- Let the waters be gathered together unto one place, [[Genesis 1.9|Gen. 1:9]].
- In the days of Peleg was the earth divided, [[Genesis 10.25|Gen. 10:25]].

- After the waters had receded, it became a choice land, [[Ether 13.2|Ether 13:2]].

- The earth shall be like it was before it was divided, [[D&C 133.24|D&C 133:24]].

## Cleansing of the earth

- Rain fell upon the earth for forty days, [[Genesis 7.4|Gen. 7:4]].

- The earth is reserved unto fire against the day of judgment, [[2 Peter 3.7|2 Pet. 3:7]].

- After today cometh the burning, [[D&C 64.24|D&C 64:24]].

- The earth desires to be cleansed from filthiness, [[Moses 7.48|Moses 7:48]].

## Final state of the earth

- The earth will be wrapt together as a scroll and pass away, [[3 Nephi 26.3|3 Ne. 26:3]] ([[D&C 29.23|D&C 29:23]]).
- There shall be a new heaven and a new earth, [[Ether 13.9|Ether 13:9]] ([[D&C 29.23|D&C 29:23]]).

- The sea of glass is the earth in its sanctified, immortal, and eternal state, [[D&C 77.1|D&C 77:1]].
- The earth must be sanctified and prepared for the celestial glory, [[D&C 88.18|D&C 88:18–19]][[D&C 88.19|]].
- This earth will be made like unto crystal and will be a Urim and Thummim, [[D&C 130.8|D&C 130:8–9]][[D&C 130.9|]].

- For a thousand years the earth shall rest, [[Moses 7.64|Moses 7:64]].
- The earth will be renewed, [[Articles of Faith 1.10|A of F 1:10]].
