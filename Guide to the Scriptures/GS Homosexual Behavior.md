# Homosexual Behavior

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/homosexual-behavior?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adultery|Adultery]]; [[GS Sensual, Sensuality|Sensual, Sensuality]]

Sexual association of individuals of the same gender. God forbids sexual activity of this kind.

- Bring them out unto us, that we may know them, [[Genesis 19.1|Gen. 19:1–11]][[Genesis 19.2|]][[Genesis 19.3|]][[Genesis 19.4|]][[Genesis 19.5|]][[Genesis 19.6|]][[Genesis 19.7|]][[Genesis 19.8|]][[Genesis 19.9|]][[Genesis 19.10|]][[Genesis 19.11|]] ([[Moses 5.51|Moses 5:51–53]][[Moses 5.52|]][[Moses 5.53|]]).
- Thou shalt not lie with mankind; it is an abomination, [[Leviticus 18.22|Lev. 18:22]] ([[Leviticus 20.13|Lev. 20:13]]).
- There shall be no sodomite of the sons of Israel, [[Deuteronomy 23.17|Deut. 23:17]].
- They declare their sin as Sodom, they hide it not, [[Isaiah 3.9|Isa. 3:9]] ([[2 Nephi 13.9|2 Ne. 13:9]]).

- Men burned in their lust one toward another, [[Romans 1.27|Rom. 1:27]].
- Abusers of themselves with mankind will not inherit the kingdom of God, [[1 Corinthians 6.9|1 Cor. 6:9–10]][[1 Corinthians 6.10|]].
- The law is not made for a righteous man, but for them that defile themselves with mankind, [[1 Timothy 1.9|1 Tim. 1:9–10]][[1 Timothy 1.10|]].
- Those who go after strange flesh are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire, [[Jude 1.7|Jude 1:7]].
