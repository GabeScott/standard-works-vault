# Trust

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/trust?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Confidence|Confidence]]; [[GS Faith|Faith]]

To rely upon or place confidence in someone or something. In spiritual matters, trust includes relying on God and His Spirit.

- Though he slay me, yet will I trust in him, [[Job 13.15|Job 13:15]].
- Trust in the Lord with all thine heart, [[Proverbs 3.5|Prov. 3:5]].
- God delivered his servants that trusted in him, [[Daniel 3.19|Dan. 3:19–28]][[Daniel 3.20|]][[Daniel 3.21|]][[Daniel 3.22|]][[Daniel 3.23|]][[Daniel 3.24|]][[Daniel 3.25|]][[Daniel 3.26|]][[Daniel 3.27|]][[Daniel 3.28|]].

- I will trust in thee forever, [[2 Nephi 4.34|2 Ne. 4:34]].
- Rejoice and put your trust in God, [[Mosiah 7.19|Mosiah 7:19]].
- Whoever puts his trust in God shall be lifted up at the last day, [[Mosiah 23.22|Mosiah 23:22]].
- Whoever shall put their trust in God shall be supported in their trials, [[Alma 36.3|Alma 36:3, 27]][[Alma 36.27|]].

- Trust not in the arm of flesh, [[D&C 1.19|D&C 1:19]].
- Put your trust in that Spirit which leadeth to do good, [[D&C 11.12|D&C 11:12]].
- Let him trust in me and he shall not be confounded, [[D&C 84.116|D&C 84:116]].
