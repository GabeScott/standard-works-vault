# Titus

Titus delivered Paul’s first epistle to the Saints at Corinth ([[2 Corinthians 7.5|2 Cor. 7:5–8, 13–15]][[2 Corinthians 7.6|]][[2 Corinthians 7.7|]][[2 Corinthians 7.8|]][[2 Corinthians 7.13|]][[2 Corinthians 7.14|]][[2 Corinthians 7.15|]]).
