# Zenock

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/zenock?lang=eng)

A prophet of Israel in Old Testament times who is mentioned only in the Book of Mormon.

- Prophesied of Christ’s death, [[1 Nephi 19.10|1 Ne. 19:10]].
- Spoke of the Son of God, [[Alma 33.15|Alma 33:15]] ([[Alma 34.7|Alma 34:7]]).
- Was a martyr for the truth, [[Alma 33.17|Alma 33:17]].
- Prophesied concerning the coming of the Messiah, [[Helaman 8.20|Hel. 8:20]].
