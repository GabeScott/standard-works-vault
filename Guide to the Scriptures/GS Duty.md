# Duty

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/duty?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Obedience, Obedient, Obey|Obedience, Obedient, Obey]]

In the scriptures, a task, assignment, or responsibility, often given by the Lord or His servants.

- Keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man, [[Ecclesiastes 12.13|Eccl. 12:13]].
- What doth the Lord require of thee, but to do justly, [[Micah 6.8|Micah 6:8]].

- We ought to obey God rather than men, [[Acts 5.29|Acts 5:29]].

- They were smitten with afflictions to stir them up in remembrance of their duty, [[Mosiah 1.17|Mosiah 1:17]].

- The duties of the elders, priests, teachers, and deacons are described, [[D&C 20.38|D&C 20:38–67]][[D&C 20.39|]][[D&C 20.40|]][[D&C 20.41|]][[D&C 20.42|]][[D&C 20.43|]][[D&C 20.44|]][[D&C 20.45|]][[D&C 20.46|]][[D&C 20.47|]][[D&C 20.48|]][[D&C 20.49|]][[D&C 20.50|]][[D&C 20.51|]][[D&C 20.52|]][[D&C 20.53|]][[D&C 20.54|]][[D&C 20.55|]][[D&C 20.56|]][[D&C 20.57|]][[D&C 20.58|]][[D&C 20.59|]][[D&C 20.60|]][[D&C 20.61|]][[D&C 20.62|]][[D&C 20.63|]][[D&C 20.64|]][[D&C 20.65|]][[D&C 20.66|]][[D&C 20.67|]].
