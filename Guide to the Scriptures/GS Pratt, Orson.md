# Pratt, Orson

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/pratt-orson?lang=eng)

One of the first Twelve Apostles called after the Restoration of the Church in modern times ([[D&C 124.128|D&C 124:128–129]][[D&C 124.129|]]). He had been a member of the Church only six weeks when the Lord gave a revelation to him through Joseph Smith ([[D&C 34.1|D&C 34]][[D&C 34.2|]][[D&C 34.3|]][[D&C 34.4|]][[D&C 34.5|]][[D&C 34.6|]][[D&C 34.7|]][[D&C 34.8|]][[D&C 34.9|]][[D&C 34.10|]][[D&C 34.11|]][[D&C 34.12|]]). Orson Pratt was also a missionary for the Church ([[D&C 52.26|D&C 52:26]]; [[D&C 75.14|75:14]]) and served as Church historian for several years.
