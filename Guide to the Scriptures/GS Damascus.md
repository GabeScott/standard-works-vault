# Damascus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/damascus?lang=eng)

An ancient city of Syria.

Damascus stands on a rich plain on the edge of the desert and is well watered by the river Barada. It is mentioned frequently in the scriptures (beginning with [[Genesis 14.15|Gen. 14:15]]).
