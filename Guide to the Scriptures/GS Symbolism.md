# Symbolism

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/symbolism?lang=eng)

To use something as a likeness or image of another thing. Symbolism in the scriptures uses a familiar object, event, or circumstance to represent a principle or teaching of the gospel. For example, the Book of Mormon prophet Alma used a seed to represent the word of God ([[Alma 32.1|Alma 32]][[Alma 32.2|]][[Alma 32.3|]][[Alma 32.4|]][[Alma 32.5|]][[Alma 32.6|]][[Alma 32.7|]][[Alma 32.8|]][[Alma 32.9|]][[Alma 32.10|]][[Alma 32.11|]][[Alma 32.12|]][[Alma 32.13|]][[Alma 32.14|]][[Alma 32.15|]][[Alma 32.16|]][[Alma 32.17|]][[Alma 32.18|]][[Alma 32.19|]][[Alma 32.20|]][[Alma 32.21|]][[Alma 32.22|]][[Alma 32.23|]][[Alma 32.24|]][[Alma 32.25|]][[Alma 32.26|]][[Alma 32.27|]][[Alma 32.28|]][[Alma 32.29|]][[Alma 32.30|]][[Alma 32.31|]][[Alma 32.32|]][[Alma 32.33|]][[Alma 32.34|]][[Alma 32.35|]][[Alma 32.36|]][[Alma 32.37|]][[Alma 32.38|]][[Alma 32.39|]][[Alma 32.40|]][[Alma 32.41|]][[Alma 32.42|]][[Alma 32.43|]]).

Prophets throughout the scriptures used symbolism to teach about Jesus Christ. Some of these symbols include ceremonies and ordinances ([[Moses 6.63|Moses 6:63]]), sacrifices ([[Hebrews 9.11|Heb. 9:11–15]][[Hebrews 9.12|]][[Hebrews 9.13|]][[Hebrews 9.14|]][[Hebrews 9.15|]]; [[Moses 5.7|Moses 5:7–8]][[Moses 5.8|]]), the sacrament ([[JST Mark 14.20|JST, Mark 14:20–24 (Bible Appendix)]][[JST Mark 14.21|]][[JST Mark 14.22|]][[JST Mark 14.23|]][[JST Mark 14.24|]]; [[Luke 22.13|Luke 22:13–20]][[Luke 22.14|]][[Luke 22.15|]][[Luke 22.16|]][[Luke 22.17|]][[Luke 22.18|]][[Luke 22.19|]][[Luke 22.20|]]), and baptism ([[Romans 6.1|Rom. 6:1–6]][[Romans 6.2|]][[Romans 6.3|]][[Romans 6.4|]][[Romans 6.5|]][[Romans 6.6|]]; [[D&C 128.12|D&C 128:12–13]][[D&C 128.13|]]). Many biblical names are symbolic. The Old Testament tabernacle ceremony and the law of Moses represented eternal truths ([[Hebrews 8.1|Heb. 8–10]][[Hebrews 8.2|]][[Hebrews 8.3|]][[Hebrews 8.4|]][[Hebrews 8.5|]][[Hebrews 8.6|]][[Hebrews 8.7|]][[Hebrews 8.8|]][[Hebrews 8.9|]][[Hebrews 8.10|]][[Hebrews 8.11|]][[Hebrews 8.12|]][[Hebrews 8.13|]]; [[Mosiah 13.29|Mosiah 13:29–32]][[Mosiah 13.30|]][[Mosiah 13.31|]][[Mosiah 13.32|]]; [[Alma 25.15|Alma 25:15]]; [[Helaman 8.14|Hel. 8:14–15]][[Helaman 8.15|]]). For other examples, see [[Matthew 5.13|Matthew 5:13–16]][[Matthew 5.14|]][[Matthew 5.15|]][[Matthew 5.16|]]; [[John 3.14|John 3:14–15]][[John 3.15|]]; [[Jacob 4.5|Jacob 4:5]]; [[Alma 37.38|Alma 37:38–45]][[Alma 37.39|]][[Alma 37.40|]][[Alma 37.41|]][[Alma 37.42|]][[Alma 37.43|]][[Alma 37.44|]][[Alma 37.45|]].
