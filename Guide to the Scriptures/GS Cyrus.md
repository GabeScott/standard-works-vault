# Cyrus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/cyrus?lang=eng)

In the Old Testament, the king of Persia who fulfilled Isaiah’s prophecy ([[2 Chronicles 36.22|2 Chr. 36:22–23]][[2 Chronicles 36.23|]]; [[Isaiah 44.28|Isa. 44:28]]; [[Isaiah 45.1|45:1]]) by allowing the Jews to return to Jerusalem to rebuild the temple, thus partially ending the Babylonian captivity. Isaiah’s prophecy was made about 180 years before the king’s action.
