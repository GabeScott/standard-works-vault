# Jezebel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/jezebel?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Ahab|Ahab]]

A wicked woman in the Old Testament from the land of Phoenicia. She was the wife of Ahab ([[1 Kings 16.30|1 Kgs. 16:30–31]][[1 Kings 16.31|]]), a king of Israel who reigned while Elijah was prophet.

Jezebel’s marriage to Ahab, more than any other single event, caused the downfall of the northern kingdom of Israel; Jezebel introduced into Israel the worst forms of idol worship from her land in place of the worship of Jehovah ([[1 Kings 18.13|1 Kgs. 18:13, 19]][[1 Kings 18.19|]]).

- Jezebel killed many of God’s prophets, [[1 Kings 18.4|1 Kgs. 18:4]].
- Jezebel tried to kill Elijah, [[1 Kings 19.1|1 Kgs. 19:1–3]][[1 Kings 19.2|]][[1 Kings 19.3|]].
- Jezebel’s wickedness ended with her terrible death, [[2 Kings 9.30|2 Kgs. 9:30–37]][[2 Kings 9.31|]][[2 Kings 9.32|]][[2 Kings 9.33|]][[2 Kings 9.34|]][[2 Kings 9.35|]][[2 Kings 9.36|]][[2 Kings 9.37|]].
