# Fellowship

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/fellowship?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Love|Love]]; [[GS Unity|Unity]]

For Latter-day Saints, fellowship includes offering friendly companionship, serving, uplifting, and strengthening others.

- Love thy neighbor as thyself, [[Leviticus 19.18|Lev. 19:18]] ([[Matthew 19.19|Matt. 19:19]]; [[D&C 59.6|D&C 59:6]]).

- When thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren, [[Luke 22.32|Luke 22:32]].
- Ye are my disciples if ye have love one to another, [[John 13.35|John 13:35]].
- Feed my sheep, [[John 21.15|John 21:15–17]][[John 21.16|]][[John 21.17|]].
- They prayed that we would take upon us the fellowship of the ministering to the Saints, [[2 Corinthians 8.1|2 Cor. 8:1–5]][[2 Corinthians 8.2|]][[2 Corinthians 8.3|]][[2 Corinthians 8.4|]][[2 Corinthians 8.5|]].
- Our fellowship is with the Father, and with his Son, [[1 John 1.3|1 Jn. 1:3]].

- The Nephites and Lamanites did fellowship one with another, [[Helaman 6.3|Hel. 6:3]].

- Let every man esteem his brother as himself, [[D&C 38.24|D&C 38:24–25]][[D&C 38.25|]].
- If ye are not one ye are not mine, [[D&C 38.27|D&C 38:27]].
- I receive you to fellowship to be your friend and brother, [[D&C 88.133|D&C 88:133]].
