# Warn, Warning

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/warn-warning?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Watch, Watchmen|Watch, Watchmen]]

To give notice or caution. Prophets, leaders, and parents warn and teach others to be obedient to the Lord and His teachings.

- Jacob warned the people of Nephi against every kind of sin, [[Jacob 3.12|Jacob 3:12]].

- The voice of warning is to all people, [[D&C 1.4|D&C 1:4]].
- Let your preaching be the warning voice, [[D&C 38.41|D&C 38:41]].
- This is a day of warning, [[D&C 63.58|D&C 63:58]].
- Every man who has been warned should warn his neighbor, [[D&C 88.81|D&C 88:81]].
- I have warned you, and forewarn you, by giving you this word of wisdom, [[D&C 89.4|D&C 89:4]].
