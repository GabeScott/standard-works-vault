# Taylor, John

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/taylor-john?lang=eng)

The third President of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

- Was called to the Council of the Twelve, [[D&C 118.6|D&C 118:6]] ([[D&C 124.128|D&C 124:128–129]][[D&C 124.129|]]).
- Was wounded at the martyrdom, [[D&C 135.2|D&C 135:2]].
- Was among the mighty ones seen by Joseph F. Smith in the spirit world, [[D&C 138.53|D&C 138:53–56]][[D&C 138.54|]][[D&C 138.55|]][[D&C 138.56|]].
