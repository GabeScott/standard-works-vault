# Priest, Aaronic Priesthood

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/priest-aaronic-priesthood?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Aaron, Brother of Moses|Aaron, Brother of Moses]]; [[GS Aaronic Priesthood|Aaronic Priesthood]]; [[GS High Priest|High Priest]]

An office of the Aaronic Priesthood. Anciently, the highest office in the Levitical Priesthood was held only by Aaron and his descendants. When Christ fulfilled the law of Moses, this restriction was removed.

- A priest’s duties in the restored Church are described, [[D&C 20.46|D&C 20:46–52]][[D&C 20.47|]][[D&C 20.48|]][[D&C 20.49|]][[D&C 20.50|]][[D&C 20.51|]][[D&C 20.52|]].
