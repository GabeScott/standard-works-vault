# War in Heaven

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/war-in-heaven?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Council in Heaven|Council in Heaven]]; [[GS Premortal Life|Premortal Life]]

The conflict that took place in the premortal life among the spirit children of God.

- Satan was cast out of heaven and into the earth, [[Revelation 12.4|Rev. 12:4, 7–9]][[Revelation 12.7|]][[Revelation 12.8|]][[Revelation 12.9|]].

- The devil and a third part of heaven were thrust down, [[D&C 29.36|D&C 29:36–37]][[D&C 29.37|]].
- Lucifer rebelled against the Only Begotten Son, [[D&C 76.25|D&C 76:25–26]][[D&C 76.26|]].

- Satan sought the glory of the Father and to destroy the agency of man, [[Moses 4.1|Moses 4:1–4]][[Moses 4.2|]][[Moses 4.3|]][[Moses 4.4|]] ([[Isaiah 14.12|Isa. 14:12–15]][[Isaiah 14.13|]][[Isaiah 14.14|]][[Isaiah 14.15|]]; [[Abraham 3.27|Abr. 3:27–28]][[Abraham 3.28|]]).
- Those who followed God kept their first estate, came to earth, and received bodies, [[Abraham 3.26|Abr. 3:26]].
