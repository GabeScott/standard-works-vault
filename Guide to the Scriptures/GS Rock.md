# Rock

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/rock?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Gospel|Gospel]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Revelation|Revelation]]

Figuratively, Jesus Christ and His gospel, which are a strong foundation and support ([[D&C 11.24|D&C 11:24]]; [[D&C 33.12|33:12–13]][[D&C 33.13|]]). *Rock* can also refer to revelation, by which God makes His gospel known to man ([[Matthew 16.15|Matt. 16:15–18]][[Matthew 16.16|]][[Matthew 16.17|]][[Matthew 16.18|]]).

- He is the rock, his work is perfect, [[Deuteronomy 32.4|Deut. 32:4]].
- The Lord is my rock; in him will I trust, [[2 Samuel 22.2|2 Sam. 22:2–3]][[2 Samuel 22.3|]].
- A stone was cut out without hands, [[Daniel 2.34|Dan. 2:34–35]][[Daniel 2.35|]].

- It was founded upon a rock, [[Matthew 7.25|Matt. 7:25]] ([[3 Nephi 14.25|3 Ne. 14:25]]).
- Jesus Christ is the stone that was set at nought, [[Acts 4.10|Acts 4:10–11]][[Acts 4.11|]].
- That rock was Christ, [[1 Corinthians 10.1|1 Cor. 10:1–4]][[1 Corinthians 10.2|]][[1 Corinthians 10.3|]][[1 Corinthians 10.4|]] ([[Exodus 17.6|Ex. 17:6]]).

- He that is built upon the rock receiveth the truth, [[2 Nephi 28.28|2 Ne. 28:28]].
- The Jews will reject the stone \[Christ\] upon which they might build, [[Jacob 4.15|Jacob 4:15–17]][[Jacob 4.16|]][[Jacob 4.17|]].
- It is upon the rock of our Redeemer that we must build our foundation, [[Helaman 5.12|Hel. 5:12]].
- Whoso builds on Christ’s doctrines builds upon his rock and will not fall when the floods come, [[3 Nephi 11.39|3 Ne. 11:39–40]][[3 Nephi 11.40|]] ([[Matthew 7.24|Matt. 7:24–27]][[Matthew 7.25|]][[Matthew 7.26|]][[Matthew 7.27|]]; [[3 Nephi 18.12|3 Ne. 18:12–13]][[3 Nephi 18.13|]]).
- A wise man built his house upon a rock, [[3 Nephi 14.24|3 Ne. 14:24]].

- If ye are built upon my rock, earth and hell cannot prevail, [[D&C 6.34|D&C 6:34]].
- He that buildeth upon this rock shall never fall, [[D&C 50.44|D&C 50:44]].

- I am Messiah, the King of Zion, the Rock of Heaven, [[Moses 7.53|Moses 7:53]].
