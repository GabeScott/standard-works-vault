# Zebulun

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/zebulun?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Israel|Israel]]; [[GS Jacob, Son of Isaac|Jacob, Son of Isaac]]

In the Old Testament, a son of Jacob and Leah ([[Genesis 30.19|Gen. 30:19–20]][[Genesis 30.20|]]).

## The tribe of Zebulun

Jacob blessed the tribe of Zebulun ([[Genesis 49.13|Gen. 49:13]]). The tribe of Zebulun joined with Deborah and Barak in fighting against the enemies of Israel ([[Judges 4.4|Judg. 4:4–6, 10]][[Judges 4.5|]][[Judges 4.6|]][[Judges 4.10|]]). They also joined with Gideon in fighting the Midianites ([[Judges 6.33|Judg. 6:33–35]][[Judges 6.34|]][[Judges 6.35|]]).
