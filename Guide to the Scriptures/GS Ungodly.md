# Ungodly

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/ungodly?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Filth, Filthiness|Filth, Filthiness]]; [[GS Sin|Sin]]; [[GS Unrighteous, Unrighteousness|Unrighteous, Unrighteousness]]; [[GS Wicked, Wickedness|Wicked, Wickedness]]

Someone or something that is not in accordance with the will or commandments of God; wicked and unholy.

- The way of the ungodly shall perish, [[Psalms 1.6|Ps. 1:6]].

- If the righteous scarcely be saved, where shall the ungodly appear, [[1 Peter 4.18|1 Pet. 4:18]].

- Deny yourselves of all ungodliness, [[Moroni 10.32|Moro. 10:32]].

- Vengeance comes speedily upon the ungodly, [[D&C 97.22|D&C 97:22]].
- Among the ungodly, the Savior’s voice was not raised, [[D&C 138.20|D&C 138:20]].
