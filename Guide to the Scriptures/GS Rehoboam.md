# Rehoboam

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/rehoboam?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Solomon|Solomon]]

In the Old Testament, the son of King Solomon. He succeeded his father and reigned for seventeen years in Jerusalem ([[1 Kings 11.43|1 Kgs. 11:43]]; [[1 Kings 14.21|14:21, 31]][[1 Kings 14.31|]]). During Rehoboam’s reign, the kingdom was divided into the kingdom of Israel in the north and the kingdom of Judah in the south ([[1 Kings 11.31|1 Kgs. 11:31–36]][[1 Kings 11.32|]][[1 Kings 11.33|]][[1 Kings 11.34|]][[1 Kings 11.35|]][[1 Kings 11.36|]]; [[1 Kings 12.19|12:19–20]][[1 Kings 12.20|]]). Rehoboam ruled over the kingdom of Judah.
