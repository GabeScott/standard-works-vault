# Amos

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/amos?lang=eng)

A prophet of the Old Testament who prophesied from approximately 792 to 740  in the days of Uzziah, king of Judah, and Jeroboam, king of Israel.

## The book of Amos

A book in the Old Testament. Many of Amos’s prophecies warn Israel and her neighboring nations to return to righteousness.
