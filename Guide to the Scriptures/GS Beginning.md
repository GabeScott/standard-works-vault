# Beginning

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/beginning?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Create, Creation|Create, Creation]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Premortal Life|Premortal Life]]

Generally refers to the time before this mortal life—that is, the premortal life. Sometimes Jesus Christ is referred to as the Beginning.

- In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth, [[Genesis 1.1|Gen. 1:1]] ([[Moses 2.1|Moses 2:1]]).

- In the beginning was the Word, [[John 1.1|John 1:1]].

- I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end, [[3 Nephi 9.18|3 Ne. 9:18]].

- Christ is the beginning and the end, [[D&C 19.1|D&C 19:1]].
- The new and everlasting covenant was from the beginning, [[D&C 22.1|D&C 22:1]].
- Man was in the beginning with God, [[D&C 93.23|D&C 93:23, 29]][[D&C 93.29|]].
- Noble and great spirits were chosen in the beginning to be rulers, [[D&C 138.55|D&C 138:55]].

- Mine Only Begotten was with me from the beginning, [[Moses 2.26|Moses 2:26]].
