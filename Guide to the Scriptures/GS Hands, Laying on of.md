# Hands, Laying on of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/hands-laying-on-of?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Administration to the Sick|Administration to the Sick]]; [[GS Gift of the Holy Ghost|Gift of the Holy Ghost]]; [[GS Ordain, Ordination|Ordain, Ordination]]; [[GS Setting Apart|Setting Apart]]

The act of placing one’s hands on a person’s head as part of a priesthood ordinance. Many priesthood ordinances are performed by the laying on of hands, such as ordinations, blessings, administering to the sick, confirming Church membership, and conferring the Holy Ghost.

- Moses laid his hands upon the head of Joshua, as the Lord commanded, [[Numbers 27.18|Num. 27:18, 22–23]][[Numbers 27.22|]][[Numbers 27.23|]] ([[Deuteronomy 34.9|Deut. 34:9]]).

- Jesus laid his hands upon a few sick folk, and healed them, [[Mark 6.5|Mark 6:5]] ([[Mormon 9.24|Morm. 9:24]]).
- The Apostles laid their hands on the seven who would assist them, [[Acts 6.5|Acts 6:5–6]][[Acts 6.6|]].
- The Holy Ghost was given by the laying on of hands, [[Acts 8.14|Acts 8:14–17]][[Acts 8.15|]][[Acts 8.16|]][[Acts 8.17|]].
- Ananias laid his hands on Saul and restored his sight, [[Acts 9.12|Acts 9:12, 17–18]][[Acts 9.17|]][[Acts 9.18|]].
- Paul laid his hands on him and healed him, [[Acts 28.8|Acts 28:8]].
- Paul taught the doctrine of baptism and the laying on of hands, [[Hebrews 6.2|Heb. 6:2]].

- Alma ordained priests and elders by the laying on of hands, [[Alma 6.1|Alma 6:1]].
- Jesus gave his disciples the power to confer the Holy Ghost by the laying on of hands, [[3 Nephi 18.36|3 Ne. 18:36–37]][[3 Nephi 18.37|]].
- Upon whom ye shall lay your hands, ye shall give the Holy Ghost, [[Moroni 2.2|Moro. 2:2]].

- Elders are to lay their hands upon children for a blessing, [[D&C 20.70|D&C 20:70]].
- They shall receive the Holy Ghost by the laying on of the hands, [[D&C 35.6|D&C 35:6]] ([[Articles of Faith 1.4|A of F 1:4]]).
- Elders shall lay hands upon the sick, [[D&C 42.44|D&C 42:44]] ([[D&C 66.9|D&C 66:9]]).
- Children are to receive the laying on of hands after baptism, [[D&C 68.27|D&C 68:27]].
- The priesthood is received by the laying on of hands, [[D&C 84.6|D&C 84:6–16]][[D&C 84.7|]][[D&C 84.8|]][[D&C 84.9|]][[D&C 84.10|]][[D&C 84.11|]][[D&C 84.12|]][[D&C 84.13|]][[D&C 84.14|]][[D&C 84.15|]][[D&C 84.16|]].
