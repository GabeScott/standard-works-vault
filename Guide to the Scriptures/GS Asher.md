# Asher

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/asher?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Israel|Israel]]; [[GS Jacob, Son of Isaac|Jacob, Son of Isaac]]

In the Old Testament, a son of Jacob and Zilpah, Leah’s handmaiden ([[Genesis 30.12|Gen. 30:12–13]][[Genesis 30.13|]]).

## The tribe of Asher

Jacob blessed Asher ([[Genesis 49.20|Gen. 49:20]]), and Moses blessed the descendants of Asher ([[Deuteronomy 33.1|Deut. 33:1, 24–29]][[Deuteronomy 33.24|]][[Deuteronomy 33.25|]][[Deuteronomy 33.26|]][[Deuteronomy 33.27|]][[Deuteronomy 33.28|]][[Deuteronomy 33.29|]]). These descendants were called “mighty men of valour” ([[1 Chronicles 7.40|1 Chr. 7:40]]).
