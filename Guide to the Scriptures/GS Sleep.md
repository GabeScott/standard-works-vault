# Sleep

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/sleep?lang=eng)

A state of rest in which a person is inactive and unconscious. The Lord has counseled His Saints to not sleep longer than is needful ([[D&C 88.124|D&C 88:124]]). Sleep can also be a symbol for spiritual death ([[1 Corinthians 11.30|1 Cor. 11:30]]; [[2 Nephi 1.13|2 Ne. 1:13]]) or for physical death ([[Mormon 9.13|Morm. 9:13]]).
