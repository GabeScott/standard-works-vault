# Moroni, Captain

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/moroni-captain?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Title of Liberty|Title of Liberty]]

In the Book of Mormon, a righteous Nephite military commander who lived about 100 

- Moroni was appointed chief captain of all the Nephite armies, [[Alma 43.16|Alma 43:16–17]][[Alma 43.17|]].
- Inspired the Nephite soldiers to fight for their freedom, [[Alma 43.48|Alma 43:48–50]][[Alma 43.49|]][[Alma 43.50|]].
- Made a title of liberty out of a piece of his coat, [[Alma 46.12|Alma 46:12–13]][[Alma 46.13|]].
- Was a man of God, [[Alma 48.11|Alma 48:11–18]][[Alma 48.12|]][[Alma 48.13|]][[Alma 48.14|]][[Alma 48.15|]][[Alma 48.16|]][[Alma 48.17|]][[Alma 48.18|]].
- Was angry with the government over their indifference about the country’s freedom, [[Alma 59.13|Alma 59:13]].
