# Comforter

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/comforter?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Holy Ghost|Holy Ghost]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

The scriptures speak of two Comforters. The first is the Holy Ghost ([[John 14.26|John 14:26–27]][[John 14.27|]]; [[Moroni 8.26|Moro. 8:26]]; [[D&C 21.9|D&C 21:9]]; [[D&C 42.17|42:17]]; [[D&C 90.11|90:11]]). The Second Comforter is the Lord Jesus Christ ([[John 14.18|John 14:18, 21, 23]][[John 14.21|]][[John 14.23|]]). When someone obtains the Second Comforter, Jesus Christ will appear to him from time to time, will reveal the Father, and will teach him face to face ([[D&C 130.3|D&C 130:3]]).
