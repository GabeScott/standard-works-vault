# Worthy, Worthiness

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/worthy-worthiness?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Righteous, Righteousness|Righteous, Righteousness]]

To be personally righteous and to stand approved in the sight of God and His appointed leaders.

- He that takes not his cross is not worthy of me, [[Matthew 10.38|Matt. 10:38]].
- The laborer is worthy of his hire, [[Luke 10.7|Luke 10:7]] ([[D&C 31.5|D&C 31:5]]).

- See that ye do all things in worthiness, [[Mormon 9.29|Morm. 9:29]].
- They were not baptized save they were worthy, [[Moroni 6.1|Moro. 6:1]].

- The slothful shall not be counted worthy to stand, [[D&C 107.100|D&C 107:100]].
- He that will not bear chastisement is not worthy of my kingdom, [[D&C 136.31|D&C 136:31]].
- The priesthood was extended to all worthy male members, [[Official Declaration 2.1|OD 2]].
