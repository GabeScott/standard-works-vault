# Inspiration, Inspire

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/inspiration-inspire?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Holy Ghost|Holy Ghost]]; [[GS Revelation|Revelation]]

Divine guidance given to man by God. Inspiration often comes by the Spirit in a variety of ways to the mind or heart of a person.

- After the fire came a still small voice, [[1 Kings 19.12|1 Kgs. 19:12]].

- The Holy Ghost will teach you all things and bring all things to your remembrance, [[John 14.26|John 14:26]].
- The Spirit of truth will guide you into all truth, [[John 16.13|John 16:13]].

- I was led by the Spirit, not knowing beforehand the things which I should do, [[1 Nephi 4.6|1 Ne. 4:6]].
- The voice of the Lord came into my mind, [[Enos 1.10|Enos 1:10]].
- Everything which inviteth and enticeth to do good is inspired of God, [[Moroni 7.13|Moro. 7:13–16]][[Moroni 7.14|]][[Moroni 7.15|]][[Moroni 7.16|]].

- Did I not speak peace to your mind, [[D&C 6.23|D&C 6:23]].
- I will tell you in your mind and in your heart, [[D&C 8.2|D&C 8:2]].
- My Spirit shall enlighten your mind, which shall fill your soul with joy, [[D&C 11.13|D&C 11:13]].
- It shall be given thee in the very moment what thou shalt speak or write, [[D&C 24.6|D&C 24:6]] ([[D&C 84.85|D&C 84:85]]).
- The still small voice whispers through and pierces all things, [[D&C 85.6|D&C 85:6]].
