# Helaman, Sons of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/helaman-sons-of?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Anti-Nephi-Lehies|Anti-Nephi-Lehies]]; [[GS Helaman, Son of Alma|Helaman, Son of Alma]]

In the Book of Mormon, sons of converted Lamanites known as Ammonites who became warriors under Helaman’s charge ([[Alma 53.16|Alma 53:16–22]][[Alma 53.17|]][[Alma 53.18|]][[Alma 53.19|]][[Alma 53.20|]][[Alma 53.21|]][[Alma 53.22|]]).

- Helaman considered them worthy to be called his sons, [[Alma 56.10|Alma 56:10]].
- Their mothers had taught them not to doubt the Lord’s ability to deliver them, [[Alma 56.47|Alma 56:47]].
- They defeated the Lamanites and were preserved by their faith such that none were slain, [[Alma 56.52|Alma 56:52–54, 56]][[Alma 56.53|]][[Alma 56.54|]][[Alma 56.56|]]; [[Alma 57.26|57:26]].
