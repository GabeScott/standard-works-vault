# Cross

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/cross?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Crucifixion|Crucifixion]]; [[GS Golgotha|Golgotha]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Sacrament|Sacrament]]

The wooden structure upon which Jesus Christ was crucified ([[Mark 15.20|Mark 15:20–26]][[Mark 15.21|]][[Mark 15.22|]][[Mark 15.23|]][[Mark 15.24|]][[Mark 15.25|]][[Mark 15.26|]]). Many in the world now think of it as a symbol of Christ’s Crucifixion and atoning sacrifice; however, the Lord has established His own symbols for His Crucifixion and sacrifice—the bread and the water of the sacrament ([[Matthew 26.26|Matt. 26:26–28]][[Matthew 26.27|]][[Matthew 26.28|]]; [[D&C 20.40|D&C 20:40, 75–79]][[D&C 20.75|]][[D&C 20.76|]][[D&C 20.77|]][[D&C 20.78|]][[D&C 20.79|]]). In the scriptures, those who take up their cross are those who love Jesus Christ so much that they deny themselves ungodliness and every worldly lust and keep His commandments ([[JST Matthew 16.25|JST, Matt. 16:25–26 (Bible Appendix)]][[JST Matthew 16.26|]]).

- Take up the cross, and follow me, [[Mark 10.21|Mark 10:21]] ([[3 Nephi 12.30|3 Ne. 12:30]]; [[D&C 23.6|D&C 23:6]]).
- Jesus endured the cross and is set down at the right hand of the throne of God, [[Hebrews 12.2|Heb. 12:2]].

- He was lifted up upon the cross and slain for the sins of the world, [[1 Nephi 11.33|1 Ne. 11:33]].
- They who have endured the crosses of the world shall inherit the kingdom of God, [[2 Nephi 9.18|2 Ne. 9:18]].
- My Father sent me that I might be lifted up upon the cross, [[3 Nephi 27.14|3 Ne. 27:14–15]][[3 Nephi 27.15|]].

- He that will not take up his cross and follow me and keep my commandments, the same shall not be saved, [[D&C 56.2|D&C 56:2]].

- Behold the Son of Man lifted up on the cross, [[Moses 7.55|Moses 7:55]].
