# Terrestrial Glory

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/terrestrial-glory?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Degrees of Glory|Degrees of Glory]]

The second of the three degrees of glory in which people will dwell after the Final Judgment.

- Paul saw a terrestrial glory, likened to the glory of the moon, [[1 Corinthians 15.40|1 Cor. 15:40–41]][[1 Corinthians 15.41|]].

- Joseph Smith and Sidney Rigdon saw the terrestrial glory, [[D&C 76.71|D&C 76:71–80]][[D&C 76.72|]][[D&C 76.73|]][[D&C 76.74|]][[D&C 76.75|]][[D&C 76.76|]][[D&C 76.77|]][[D&C 76.78|]][[D&C 76.79|]][[D&C 76.80|]].
- The glory of the terrestrial excels that of the telestial, [[D&C 76.91|D&C 76:91]].
- Those who cannot abide the law of a terrestrial kingdom cannot abide a terrestrial glory, [[D&C 88.23|D&C 88:23, 30, 38]][[D&C 88.30|]][[D&C 88.38|]].
