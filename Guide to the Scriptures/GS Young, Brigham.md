# Young, Brigham

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/young-brigham?lang=eng)

An early Apostle in this dispensation and the second President of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. He led the Saints westward from Nauvoo, Illinois, to the Salt Lake Valley and was a great colonizer in the western United States.

- Brigham Young was called to be President over the Twelve Apostles, [[D&C 124.127|D&C 124:127]].
- Brigham Young was commended for his labors and relieved of future travel abroad, [[D&C 126.1|D&C 126]][[D&C 126.2|]][[D&C 126.3|]].
