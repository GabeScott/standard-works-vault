# Restitution, Restoration

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/restitution-restoration?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Restoration of the Gospel|Restoration of the Gospel]]

The return of a thing or condition that has been taken away or lost.

- The spirit and the body shall be reunited in its perfect form, [[Alma 11.43|Alma 11:43–44]][[Alma 11.44|]].
- Restoration is to bring back evil for evil, righteous for righteous, [[Alma 41.10|Alma 41:10–15]][[Alma 41.11|]][[Alma 41.12|]][[Alma 41.13|]][[Alma 41.14|]][[Alma 41.15|]].

- We believe in the restoration of the Ten Tribes and that the earth will be renewed and receive its paradisiacal glory, [[Articles of Faith 1.10|A of F 1:10]] ([[D&C 133.23|D&C 133:23–24]][[D&C 133.24|]]).
