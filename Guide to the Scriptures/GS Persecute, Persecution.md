# Persecute, Persecution

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/persecute-persecution?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adversity|Adversity]]

To cause anguish or pain to others because of their beliefs or social status; to harass or oppress.

- Blessed are they which are persecuted for righteousness’ sake, [[Matthew 5.10|Matt. 5:10]] ([[3 Nephi 12.10|3 Ne. 12:10]]).
- Pray for them which despitefully use you and persecute you, [[Matthew 5.44|Matt. 5:44]] ([[3 Nephi 12.44|3 Ne. 12:44]]).

- Because they are rich, they persecute the meek, [[2 Nephi 9.30|2 Ne. 9:30]] ([[2 Nephi 28.12|2 Ne. 28:12–13]][[2 Nephi 28.13|]]).
- The righteous that look forward to Christ notwithstanding all persecution shall not perish, [[2 Nephi 26.8|2 Ne. 26:8]].

- All these things shall give thee experience, [[D&C 122.7|D&C 122:7]].
