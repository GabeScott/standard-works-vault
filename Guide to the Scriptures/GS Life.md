# Life

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/life?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Eternal Life|Eternal Life]]; [[GS Light, Light of Christ|Light, Light of Christ]]

Temporal and spiritual existence made possible by the power of God.

- I have set before thee this day life and good, [[Deuteronomy 30.15|Deut. 30:15–20]][[Deuteronomy 30.16|]][[Deuteronomy 30.17|]][[Deuteronomy 30.18|]][[Deuteronomy 30.19|]][[Deuteronomy 30.20|]].
- Thou wilt shew me the path of life, [[Psalms 16.11|Ps. 16:11]].
- He that followeth after righteousness findeth life, [[Proverbs 21.21|Prov. 21:21]].

- He that findeth his life shall lose it: and he that loseth his life for my sake shall find it, [[Matthew 10.39|Matt. 10:39]] ([[Matthew 16.25|Matt. 16:25]]; [[Mark 8.35|Mark 8:35]]; [[Luke 9.24|Luke 9:24]]; [[Luke 17.33|17:33]]).
- The Son of Man is not come to destroy men’s lives, but to save them, [[Luke 9.56|Luke 9:56]].
- In him was life, and the life was the light of men, [[John 1.4|John 1:4]].
- He that believeth on him that sent me is passed from death unto life, [[John 5.24|John 5:24]].
- I am the way, the truth, and the life, [[John 14.6|John 14:6]].
- If in this life only we have hope in Christ, we are most miserable, [[1 Corinthians 15.19|1 Cor. 15:19–22]][[1 Corinthians 15.20|]][[1 Corinthians 15.21|]][[1 Corinthians 15.22|]].
- Godliness has promise of the life that now is, and of that which is to come, [[1 Timothy 4.8|1 Tim. 4:8]].

- Our children may look forward to that life which is in Christ, [[2 Nephi 25.23|2 Ne. 25:23–27]][[2 Nephi 25.24|]][[2 Nephi 25.25|]][[2 Nephi 25.26|]][[2 Nephi 25.27|]].
- This life is the time for men to prepare to meet God, [[Alma 34.32|Alma 34:32]] ([[Alma 12.24|Alma 12:24]]).
- I am the light and the life of the world, [[3 Nephi 9.18|3 Ne. 9:18]] ([[Mosiah 16.9|Mosiah 16:9]]; [[3 Nephi 11.11|3 Ne. 11:11]]; [[Ether 4.12|Ether 4:12]]).

- Blessed are they who are faithful, whether in life or in death, [[D&C 50.5|D&C 50:5]].
- This is eternal lives—to know God and Jesus Christ, [[D&C 132.24|D&C 132:24]].

- This is my work and my glory—to bring to pass the immortality and eternal life of man, [[Moses 1.39|Moses 1:39]].
