# Shiblon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/shiblon?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Alma, Son of Alma|Alma, Son of Alma]]

In the Book of Mormon, a son of Alma the Younger. Shiblon taught the gospel to the Zoramites and was persecuted for his righteousness. The Lord delivered him from his persecution because of his faithfulness and patience ([[Alma 38.1|Alma 38]][[Alma 38.2|]][[Alma 38.3|]][[Alma 38.4|]][[Alma 38.5|]][[Alma 38.6|]][[Alma 38.7|]][[Alma 38.8|]][[Alma 38.9|]][[Alma 38.10|]][[Alma 38.11|]][[Alma 38.12|]][[Alma 38.13|]][[Alma 38.14|]][[Alma 38.15|]]). Shiblon also took care of the Nephite records for a time ([[Alma 63.1|Alma 63:1–2, 11–13]][[Alma 63.2|]][[Alma 63.11|]][[Alma 63.12|]][[Alma 63.13|]]).
