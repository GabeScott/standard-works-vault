# Michael

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/michael?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adam|Adam]]; [[GS Archangel|Archangel]]

The name by which Adam was known in the premortal life. He is called the Archangel. In Hebrew the name means “Who is like God.”
