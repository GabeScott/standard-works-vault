# Mustard Seed

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/mustard-seed?lang=eng)

The seeds of a mustard plant. Though a seed is very small, the height of the plant that grows from it is very great. Jesus compared the kingdom of heaven to a mustard seed ([[Matthew 13.31|Matt. 13:31]]).

- If you have faith as a grain of mustard seed, you can move mountains, [[Matthew 17.20|Matt. 17:20]].
