# Zoram, Zoramites

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/zoram-zoramites?lang=eng)

In the Book of Mormon, the servant of Laban who joined with Nephi and Lehi to come to the promised land ([[1 Nephi 4.31|1 Ne. 4:31–38]][[1 Nephi 4.32|]][[1 Nephi 4.33|]][[1 Nephi 4.34|]][[1 Nephi 4.35|]][[1 Nephi 4.36|]][[1 Nephi 4.37|]][[1 Nephi 4.38|]]). Because of Zoram’s faithfulness, Lehi blessed him along with Lehi’s own sons ([[2 Nephi 1.30|2 Ne. 1:30–32]][[2 Nephi 1.31|]][[2 Nephi 1.32|]]). His descendants were known as Zoramites ([[Jacob 1.13|Jacob 1:13]]).
