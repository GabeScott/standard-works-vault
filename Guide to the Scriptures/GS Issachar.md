# Issachar

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/issachar?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Israel|Israel]]; [[GS Jacob, Son of Isaac|Jacob, Son of Isaac]]

A son of Jacob and Leah in the Old Testament ([[Genesis 30.17|Gen. 30:17–18]][[Genesis 30.18|]]; [[Genesis 35.23|35:23]]; [[Genesis 46.13|46:13]]). His descendants became one of the twelve tribes of Israel.

## The tribe of Issachar

Jacob’s blessing to Issachar is found in [[Genesis 49.14|Genesis 49:14–15]][[Genesis 49.15|]]. After settling in Canaan, the tribe received some of the richest land of Palestine, including the plain of Esdraelon. Within Issachar’s borders were several important places in Jewish history, for example, Carmel, Megiddo, Dothan, Gilboa, Jezreel, Tabor, and Nazareth ([[Joshua 19.17|Josh. 19:17–23]][[Joshua 19.18|]][[Joshua 19.19|]][[Joshua 19.20|]][[Joshua 19.21|]][[Joshua 19.22|]][[Joshua 19.23|]]).
