# Weakness

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/weakness?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Humble, Humility|Humble, Humility]]

The condition of being mortal and lacking ability, strength, or skill. Weakness is a state of being. All people are weak, and it is only by God’s grace that they receive power to do righteous acts ([[Jacob 4.6|Jacob 4:6–7]][[Jacob 4.7|]]). This weakness is manifest in part in the individual weaknesses or frailties that each person has.

- Strengthen ye the weak hands, [[Isaiah 35.3|Isa. 35:3–4]][[Isaiah 35.4|]].

- The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak, [[Matthew 26.41|Matt. 26:41]] ([[Mark 14.38|Mark 14:38]]).

- Because of the weakness which is in me, according to the flesh, I would excuse myself, [[1 Nephi 19.6|1 Ne. 19:6]].
- I have been commanded to write these things, notwithstanding my weakness, [[2 Nephi 33.11|2 Ne. 33:11]].
- Do not be angry with thy servant because of his weakness, [[Ether 3.2|Ether 3:2]].
- The Gentiles will mock at these things because of our weakness in writing, [[Ether 12.23|Ether 12:23–25, 40]][[Ether 12.24|]][[Ether 12.25|]][[Ether 12.40|]].
- I will show unto them their weakness, [[Ether 12.27|Ether 12:27–28]][[Ether 12.28|]].
- Because thou hast seen thy weakness thou shalt be made strong, [[Ether 12.37|Ether 12:37]].

- He that is weak among you hereafter shall be made strong, [[D&C 50.16|D&C 50:16]].
- Jesus Christ knows the weakness of man, [[D&C 62.1|D&C 62:1]].
