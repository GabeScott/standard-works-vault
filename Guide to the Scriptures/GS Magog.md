# Magog

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/magog?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Gog|Gog]]; [[GS Second Coming of Jesus Christ|Second Coming of Jesus Christ]]

In the Bible, a land and people near the Black Sea. Their king, Gog, shall lead the armies of Magog in a last great battle before the Second Coming of Christ ([[Ezekiel 38.2|Ezek. 38:2]]; [[Ezekiel 39.6|39:6]]). The scriptures speak of another great battle of Gog and Magog at the end of the Millennium between the forces of God and the forces of evil ([[Revelation 20.7|Rev. 20:7–9]][[Revelation 20.8|]][[Revelation 20.9|]]; [[D&C 88.111|D&C 88:111–116]][[D&C 88.112|]][[D&C 88.113|]][[D&C 88.114|]][[D&C 88.115|]][[D&C 88.116|]]).
