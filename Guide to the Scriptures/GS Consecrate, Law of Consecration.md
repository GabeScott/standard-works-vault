# Consecrate, Law of Consecration

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/consecrate-law-of-consecration?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Kingdom of God or Kingdom of Heaven|Kingdom of God or Kingdom of Heaven]]; [[GS United Order|United Order]]

To dedicate, to make holy, or to become righteous. The law of consecration is a divine principle whereby men and women voluntarily dedicate their time, talents, and material wealth to the establishment and building up of God’s kingdom.

- Consecrate yourselves to day to the Lord, [[Exodus 32.29|Ex. 32:29]].

- All that believed had all things common, [[Acts 2.44|Acts 2:44–45]][[Acts 2.45|]].

- They had all things common among them; therefore there were not rich and poor, [[4 Nephi 1.3|4 Ne. 1:3]].

- The Lord explained the principles of consecration, [[D&C 42.30|D&C 42:30–39]][[D&C 42.31|]][[D&C 42.32|]][[D&C 42.33|]][[D&C 42.34|]][[D&C 42.35|]][[D&C 42.36|]][[D&C 42.37|]][[D&C 42.38|]][[D&C 42.39|]] ([[D&C 51.2|D&C 51:2–19]][[D&C 51.3|]][[D&C 51.4|]][[D&C 51.5|]][[D&C 51.6|]][[D&C 51.7|]][[D&C 51.8|]][[D&C 51.9|]][[D&C 51.10|]][[D&C 51.11|]][[D&C 51.12|]][[D&C 51.13|]][[D&C 51.14|]][[D&C 51.15|]][[D&C 51.16|]][[D&C 51.17|]][[D&C 51.18|]][[D&C 51.19|]]; [[D&C 58.35|58:35–36]][[D&C 58.36|]]).
- One man should not possess more than another, [[D&C 49.20|D&C 49:20]].
- Every man was given an equal portion according to his family, [[D&C 51.3|D&C 51:3]].
- An order was established so that the Saints could be equal in bonds of heavenly and earthly things, [[D&C 78.4|D&C 78:4–5]][[D&C 78.5|]].
- Every man was to have equal claim according to his wants and needs, [[D&C 82.17|D&C 82:17–19]][[D&C 82.18|]][[D&C 82.19|]].
- Zion can only be built up by the principles of celestial law, [[D&C 105.5|D&C 105:5]].

- The people of Enoch were of one heart and one mind and dwelt in righteousness, and there were no poor among them, [[Moses 7.18|Moses 7:18]].
