# Power

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/power?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Authority|Authority]]; [[GS Priesthood|Priesthood]]

The ability to do something. To have power over someone or something is to have the ability to control or command that person or thing. In the scriptures, power is often connected with the power of God or the power of heaven. It is often closely related to priesthood authority, which is the permission or right to act for God.

- For this cause have I raised thee up, to show in thee my power, [[Exodus 9.16|Ex. 9:16]].
- God is my strength and power, [[2 Samuel 22.33|2 Sam. 22:33]].
- Withhold not good when it is in the power of thine hand to do it, [[Proverbs 3.27|Prov. 3:27]].
- Truly I am full of power by the spirit of the Lord, [[Micah 3.8|Micah 3:8]].

- All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth, [[Matthew 28.18|Matt. 28:18]].
- They were astonished at his doctrine: for his word was with power, [[Luke 4.32|Luke 4:32]].
- Tarry ye in the city until ye be endued with power from on high, [[Luke 24.49|Luke 24:49]].
- As many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, [[John 1.12|John 1:12]] ([[D&C 11.30|D&C 11:30]]).
- Ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you, [[Acts 1.8|Acts 1:8]].
- There is no power but of God, [[Romans 13.1|Rom. 13:1]].
- You are kept by the power of God through faith unto salvation, [[1 Peter 1.3|1 Pet. 1:3–5]][[1 Peter 1.4|]][[1 Peter 1.5|]].

- I am filled with the power of God, [[1 Nephi 17.48|1 Ne. 17:48]].
- It has been made manifest unto me by the power of the Holy Ghost, [[Jacob 7.12|Jacob 7:12]].
- A man may have great power given him from God, [[Mosiah 8.16|Mosiah 8:16]].
- They taught with power and authority of God, [[Alma 17.2|Alma 17:2–3]][[Alma 17.3|]].
- Nephi did minister with power and with great authority, [[3 Nephi 7.15|3 Ne. 7:15–20]][[3 Nephi 7.16|]][[3 Nephi 7.17|]][[3 Nephi 7.18|]][[3 Nephi 7.19|]][[3 Nephi 7.20|]] ([[3 Nephi 11.19|3 Ne. 11:19–22]][[3 Nephi 11.20|]][[3 Nephi 11.21|]][[3 Nephi 11.22|]]).

- Although a man may have power to do mighty works, if he boasts in his own strength he must fall, [[D&C 3.4|D&C 3:4]].
- The power to do good is in each person, [[D&C 58.27|D&C 58:27–28]][[D&C 58.28|]].
