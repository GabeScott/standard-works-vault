# Harvest

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/harvest?lang=eng)

The scriptures sometimes use the word *harvest* figuratively to refer to bringing people into the Church, which is the kingdom of God on the earth, or to a time of judgment, such as the Second Coming of Jesus Christ.

- The harvest is past, the summer is ended, and we are not saved, [[Jeremiah 8.20|Jer. 8:20]] ([[D&C 56.16|D&C 56:16]]).

- The harvest is great, but the laborers are few, [[Matthew 9.37|Matt. 9:37]].
- The harvest is the end of the world, [[Matthew 13.39|Matt. 13:39]].
- Whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap, [[Galatians 6.7|Gal. 6:7–9]][[Galatians 6.8|]][[Galatians 6.9|]] ([[D&C 6.33|D&C 6:33]]).

- The field is white already to harvest, [[D&C 4.4|D&C 4:4]].
- The harvest is ended and your souls are not saved, [[D&C 45.2|D&C 45:2]].
- The time of harvest is come, and my word must be fulfilled, [[D&C 101.64|D&C 101:64]].
