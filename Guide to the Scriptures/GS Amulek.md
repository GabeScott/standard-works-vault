# Amulek

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/amulek?lang=eng)

In the Book of Mormon, a missionary companion of Alma, son of Alma.

- Was visited by an angel, [[Alma 8.20|Alma 8:20]]; [[Alma 10.7|10:7]].
- Received Alma into his home, [[Alma 8.21|Alma 8:21–27]][[Alma 8.22|]][[Alma 8.23|]][[Alma 8.24|]][[Alma 8.25|]][[Alma 8.26|]][[Alma 8.27|]].
- Preached with power to the people of Ammonihah, [[Alma 8.29|Alma 8:29–32]][[Alma 8.30|]][[Alma 8.31|]][[Alma 8.32|]]; [[Alma 10.1|10:1–11]][[Alma 10.2|]][[Alma 10.3|]][[Alma 10.4|]][[Alma 10.5|]][[Alma 10.6|]][[Alma 10.7|]][[Alma 10.8|]][[Alma 10.9|]][[Alma 10.10|]][[Alma 10.11|]].
- Was a descendant of Nephi, Lehi, and Manasseh, [[Alma 10.2|Alma 10:2–3]][[Alma 10.3|]].
- Testified of truth, [[Alma 10.4|Alma 10:4–11]][[Alma 10.5|]][[Alma 10.6|]][[Alma 10.7|]][[Alma 10.8|]][[Alma 10.9|]][[Alma 10.10|]][[Alma 10.11|]].
- Called people to repentance and was rejected, [[Alma 10.12|Alma 10:12–32]][[Alma 10.13|]][[Alma 10.14|]][[Alma 10.15|]][[Alma 10.16|]][[Alma 10.17|]][[Alma 10.18|]][[Alma 10.19|]][[Alma 10.20|]][[Alma 10.21|]][[Alma 10.22|]][[Alma 10.23|]][[Alma 10.24|]][[Alma 10.25|]][[Alma 10.26|]][[Alma 10.27|]][[Alma 10.28|]][[Alma 10.29|]][[Alma 10.30|]][[Alma 10.31|]][[Alma 10.32|]].
- Contended with Zeezrom, [[Alma 11.20|Alma 11:20–40]][[Alma 11.21|]][[Alma 11.22|]][[Alma 11.23|]][[Alma 11.24|]][[Alma 11.25|]][[Alma 11.26|]][[Alma 11.27|]][[Alma 11.28|]][[Alma 11.29|]][[Alma 11.30|]][[Alma 11.31|]][[Alma 11.32|]][[Alma 11.33|]][[Alma 11.34|]][[Alma 11.35|]][[Alma 11.36|]][[Alma 11.37|]][[Alma 11.38|]][[Alma 11.39|]][[Alma 11.40|]].

- Wanted to stop martyrdom of believers, [[Alma 14.9|Alma 14:9–10]][[Alma 14.10|]].
- Was imprisoned with Alma, [[Alma 14.14|Alma 14:14–23]][[Alma 14.15|]][[Alma 14.16|]][[Alma 14.17|]][[Alma 14.18|]][[Alma 14.19|]][[Alma 14.20|]][[Alma 14.21|]][[Alma 14.22|]][[Alma 14.23|]].
