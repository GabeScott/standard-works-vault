# Spirit Creation

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/spirit-creation?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Create, Creation|Create, Creation]]; [[GS Man, Men|Man, Men]]

The Lord created all things spiritually before He created them physically ([[Moses 3.5|Moses 3:5]]).

- The Lord made every plant before it was in the earth, [[Genesis 2.4|Gen. 2:4–6]][[Genesis 2.5|]][[Genesis 2.6|]] ([[Abraham 5.5|Abr. 5:5]]).

- By the power of my Spirit created I all things—first spiritual, secondly temporal, [[D&C 29.31|D&C 29:31–32]][[D&C 29.32|]].
- That which is temporal is in the likeness of that which is spiritual, [[D&C 77.2|D&C 77:2]].

- I made the world and men before they were in the flesh, [[Moses 6.51|Moses 6:51]].
