# Gad, Son of Jacob

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/gad-son-of-jacob?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Israel|Israel]]; [[GS Jacob, Son of Isaac|Jacob, Son of Isaac]]

In the Old Testament, a son of Jacob and Zilpah ([[Genesis 30.10|Gen. 30:10–11]][[Genesis 30.11|]]). His descendants became a tribe of Israel.

## The tribe of Gad

For Jacob’s blessing on his son Gad, see [[Genesis 49.19|Genesis 49:19]]. For Moses’ blessing on the tribe of Gad, see [[Deuteronomy 33.20|Deuteronomy 33:20–21]][[Deuteronomy 33.21|]]. According to these blessings, Gad’s descendants were to be a warlike race. The lands allotted to them in the land of Canaan were east of the Jordan River and contained good pasture land with abundant water.
