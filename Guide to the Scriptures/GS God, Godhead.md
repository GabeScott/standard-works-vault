# God, Godhead

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/god-godhead?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Father in Heaven|Father in Heaven]]; [[GS Holy Ghost|Holy Ghost]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Lord|Lord]]

There are three separate persons in the Godhead: God, the Eternal Father; His Son, Jesus Christ; and the Holy Ghost. We believe in each of Them ([[Articles of Faith 1.1|A of F 1:1]]). From latter-day revelation we learn that the Father and the Son have tangible bodies of flesh and bone and that the Holy Ghost is a personage of spirit, without flesh and bone ([[D&C 130.22|D&C 130:22–23]][[D&C 130.23|]]). These three persons are one in perfect unity and harmony of purpose and doctrine ([[John 17.21|John 17:21–23]][[John 17.22|]][[John 17.23|]]; [[2 Nephi 31.21|2 Ne. 31:21]]; [[3 Nephi 11.27|3 Ne. 11:27, 36]][[3 Nephi 11.36|]]).

## God the Father

It is generally the Father, or Elohim, who is referred to by the title God. He is called the Father because He is the father of our spirits ([[Numbers 16.22|Num. 16:22]]; [[Numbers 27.16|27:16]]; [[Malachi 2.10|Mal. 2:10]]; [[Matthew 6.9|Matt. 6:9]]; [[Ephesians 4.6|Eph. 4:6]]; [[Hebrews 12.9|Heb. 12:9]]). God the Father is the supreme ruler of the universe. He is all powerful ([[Genesis 18.14|Gen. 18:14]]; [[Alma 26.35|Alma 26:35]]; [[D&C 19.1|D&C 19:1–3]][[D&C 19.2|]][[D&C 19.3|]]), all knowing ([[Matthew 6.8|Matt. 6:8]]; [[2 Nephi 2.24|2 Ne. 2:24]]), and everywhere present through His Spirit ([[Psalms 139.7|Ps. 139:7–12]][[Psalms 139.8|]][[Psalms 139.9|]][[Psalms 139.10|]][[Psalms 139.11|]][[Psalms 139.12|]]; [[D&C 88.7|D&C 88:7–13, 41]][[D&C 88.8|]][[D&C 88.9|]][[D&C 88.10|]][[D&C 88.11|]][[D&C 88.12|]][[D&C 88.13|]][[D&C 88.41|]]). Mankind has a special relationship to God that sets man apart from all other created things: men and women are God’s spirit children ([[Psalms 82.6|Ps. 82:6]]; [[1 John 3.1|1 Jn. 3:1–3]][[1 John 3.2|]][[1 John 3.3|]]; [[D&C 20.17|D&C 20:17–18]][[D&C 20.18|]]).

There are few recorded instances of God the Father appearing to or speaking to man. The scriptures say that He spoke to Adam and Eve ([[Moses 4.14|Moses 4:14–31]][[Moses 4.15|]][[Moses 4.16|]][[Moses 4.17|]][[Moses 4.18|]][[Moses 4.19|]][[Moses 4.20|]][[Moses 4.21|]][[Moses 4.22|]][[Moses 4.23|]][[Moses 4.24|]][[Moses 4.25|]][[Moses 4.26|]][[Moses 4.27|]][[Moses 4.28|]][[Moses 4.29|]][[Moses 4.30|]][[Moses 4.31|]]) and introduced Jesus Christ on several occasions ([[Matthew 3.17|Matt. 3:17]]; [[Matthew 17.5|17:5]]; [[John 12.28|John 12:28–29]][[John 12.29|]]; [[3 Nephi 11.3|3 Ne. 11:3–7]][[3 Nephi 11.4|]][[3 Nephi 11.5|]][[3 Nephi 11.6|]][[3 Nephi 11.7|]]). He appeared to Stephen ([[Acts 7.55|Acts 7:55–56]][[Acts 7.56|]]) and Joseph Smith ([[Joseph Smith History 1.17|JS—H 1:17]]). Later He appeared to both Joseph Smith and Sidney Rigdon ([[D&C 76.20|D&C 76:20, 23]][[D&C 76.23|]]). To those who love God and purify themselves before Him, God sometimes grants the privilege of seeing and knowing for themselves that He is God ([[Matthew 5.8|Matt. 5:8]]; [[3 Nephi 12.8|3 Ne. 12:8]]; [[D&C 76.116|D&C 76:116–118]][[D&C 76.117|]][[D&C 76.118|]]; [[D&C 93.1|93:1]]).

- My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me, [[Mark 15.34|Mark 15:34]].
- These men are the servants of the most high God, [[Acts 16.17|Acts 16:17]].
- We are the offspring of God, [[Acts 17.28|Acts 17:28–29]][[Acts 17.29|]].

- Thou shalt offer thy sacraments unto the Most High, [[D&C 59.10|D&C 59:10–12]][[D&C 59.11|]][[D&C 59.12|]].

## God the Son

The God known as Jehovah is the Son, Jesus Christ ([[Isaiah 12.2|Isa. 12:2]]; [[Isaiah 43.11|43:11]]; [[Isaiah 49.26|49:26]]; [[1 Corinthians 10.1|1 Cor. 10:1–4]][[1 Corinthians 10.2|]][[1 Corinthians 10.3|]][[1 Corinthians 10.4|]]; [[1 Timothy 1.1|1 Tim. 1:1]]; [[Revelation 1.8|Rev. 1:8]]; [[2 Nephi 22.2|2 Ne. 22:2]]). Jesus works under the direction of the Father and is in complete harmony with Him. All mankind are His brothers and sisters, for He is the eldest of the spirit children of Elohim. Some scripture references refer to Him by the word *God.* For example, the scripture says that “God created the heaven and the earth” ([[Genesis 1.1|Gen. 1:1]]), but it was actually Jesus who was the Creator under the direction of God the Father ([[John 1.1|John 1:1–3, 10, 14]][[John 1.2|]][[John 1.3|]][[John 1.10|]][[John 1.14|]]; [[Hebrews 1.1|Heb. 1:1–2]][[Hebrews 1.2|]]).

## God the Holy Ghost

The Holy Ghost is also a God and is called the Holy Spirit, the Spirit, and the Spirit of God, among other similar names and titles. With the aid of the Holy Ghost, man can know the will of God the Father and know that Jesus is the Christ ([[1 Corinthians 12.3|1 Cor. 12:3]]).

- The Holy Ghost will teach you what you should say, [[Luke 12.12|Luke 12:12]].
- The Holy Ghost is the Comforter, [[John 14.26|John 14:26]] ([[John 16.7|John 16:7–15]][[John 16.8|]][[John 16.9|]][[John 16.10|]][[John 16.11|]][[John 16.12|]][[John 16.13|]][[John 16.14|]][[John 16.15|]]).
- Jesus gave commandments to the Apostles through the Holy Ghost, [[Acts 1.2|Acts 1:2]].
- The Holy Ghost bears witness of God and Christ, [[Acts 5.29|Acts 5:29–32]][[Acts 5.30|]][[Acts 5.31|]][[Acts 5.32|]] ([[1 Corinthians 12.3|1 Cor. 12:3]]).
- The Holy Ghost also is a witness to us, [[Hebrews 10.10|Heb. 10:10–17]][[Hebrews 10.11|]][[Hebrews 10.12|]][[Hebrews 10.13|]][[Hebrews 10.14|]][[Hebrews 10.15|]][[Hebrews 10.16|]][[Hebrews 10.17|]].

- By the power of the Holy Ghost you may know the truth of all things, [[Moroni 10.5|Moro. 10:5]].

- The Holy Ghost is the spirit of revelation, [[D&C 8.2|D&C 8:2–3]][[D&C 8.3|]] ([[D&C 68.4|D&C 68:4]]).
