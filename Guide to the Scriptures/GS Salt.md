# Salt

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/salt?lang=eng)

Used as an important preservative of food in the ancient world; it was considered essential for life.

- Lot’s wife became a pillar of salt, [[Genesis 19.26|Gen. 19:26]].

- Ye are the salt of the earth, [[Matthew 5.13|Matt. 5:13]] ([[Luke 14.34|Luke 14:34]]; [[3 Nephi 12.13|3 Ne. 12:13]]).

- The Lord’s covenant people are accounted as the salt of the earth, [[D&C 101.39|D&C 101:39–40]][[D&C 101.40|]].
- If the Saints are not the saviors of men, they are as salt that has lost its savor, [[D&C 103.9|D&C 103:9–10]][[D&C 103.10|]].
