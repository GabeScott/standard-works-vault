# Adversity

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/adversity?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Chasten, Chastening|Chasten, Chastening]]; [[GS Endure|Endure]]; [[GS Persecute, Persecution|Persecute, Persecution]]; [[GS Tempt, Temptation|Tempt, Temptation]]

Through adversity—trials, troubles, and distress—man can have many experiences that lead to spiritual growth and eternal progress by turning to the Lord.

- God himself saved you out of all your adversities and your tribulations, [[1 Samuel 10.19|1 Sam. 10:19]].
- They cried unto the Lord in their trouble, [[Psalms 107.6|Ps. 107:6, 13, 19, 28]][[Psalms 107.13|]][[Psalms 107.19|]][[Psalms 107.28|]].
- Though the Lord give you the bread of adversity, yet shall not thy teachers be removed, [[Isaiah 30.20|Isa. 30:20–21]][[Isaiah 30.21|]].

- It must needs be, that there is an opposition in all things, [[2 Nephi 2.11|2 Ne. 2:11]].

- If they never should have bitter they could not know the sweet, [[D&C 29.39|D&C 29:39]].
- Thine adversity shall be but a small moment, [[D&C 121.7|D&C 121:7–8]][[D&C 121.8|]].
- All these things shall give thee experience, and shall be for thy good, [[D&C 122.5|D&C 122:5–8]][[D&C 122.6|]][[D&C 122.7|]][[D&C 122.8|]].

- They taste the bitter, that they may know to prize the good, [[Moses 6.55|Moses 6:55]].
