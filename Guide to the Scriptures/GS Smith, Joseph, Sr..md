# Smith, Joseph, Sr.

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/smith-joseph-sr?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Smith, Joseph, Jr.|Smith, Joseph, Jr.]]; [[GS Smith, Lucy Mack|Smith, Lucy Mack]]

Father of the Prophet Joseph Smith. He was born July 12, 1771. He married Lucy Mack, and they had nine children ([[Joseph Smith History 1.4|JS—H 1:4]]). Joseph became a faithful believer in the latter-day Restoration and the first Patriarch to the Church. He died September 14, 1840.

- God revealed instructions to him through his son Joseph, [[D&C 4.1|D&C 4]][[D&C 4.2|]][[D&C 4.3|]][[D&C 4.4|]][[D&C 4.5|]][[D&C 4.6|]][[D&C 4.7|]]; [[D&C 23.5|23:5]].
- Let mine aged servant continue with his family, [[D&C 90.20|D&C 90:20]].
- My aged servant Joseph sitteth with Abraham at his right hand, [[D&C 124.19|D&C 124:19]].
- Joseph Smith Jr. saw his father in a vision of the celestial kingdom, [[D&C 137.5|D&C 137:5]].

- An angel commanded Joseph Smith Jr. to tell his father of the vision he had received, [[Joseph Smith History 1.49|JS—H 1:49–50]][[Joseph Smith History 1.50|]].
