# Unity

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/unity?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS God, Godhead|God, Godhead]]

To become one in thought, desire, and purpose first with our Father in Heaven and Jesus Christ, and then with other Saints.

- It is good for brethren to dwell together in unity, [[Psalms 133.1|Ps. 133:1]].

- I and my Father are one, [[John 10.30|John 10:30]] ([[D&C 50.43|D&C 50:43]]).
- Jesus prayed that all may be one as he and his Father are one, [[John 17.11|John 17:11–23]][[John 17.12|]][[John 17.13|]][[John 17.14|]][[John 17.15|]][[John 17.16|]][[John 17.17|]][[John 17.18|]][[John 17.19|]][[John 17.20|]][[John 17.21|]][[John 17.22|]][[John 17.23|]] ([[3 Nephi 19.23|3 Ne. 19:23]]).
- I beseech you that there be no divisions among you, but that ye be perfectly joined together, [[1 Corinthians 1.10|1 Cor. 1:10]].

- Be determined in one mind and in one heart, united in all things, [[2 Nephi 1.21|2 Ne. 1:21]].
- Saints should have their hearts knit together in unity, [[Mosiah 18.21|Mosiah 18:21]].
- Jesus prayed for unity among his Nephite disciples, [[3 Nephi 19.23|3 Ne. 19:23]].
- The disciples were united in mighty prayer and fasting, [[3 Nephi 27.1|3 Ne. 27:1]].

- The Father, Son, and Holy Ghost are one, [[D&C 20.27|D&C 20:27–28]][[D&C 20.28|]] ([[D&C 35.2|D&C 35:2]]; [[D&C 50.43|50:43]]).
- It is your duty to unite with the true church, [[D&C 23.7|D&C 23:7]].
- Whatsoever ye shall ask in faith, being united in prayer, ye shall receive, [[D&C 29.6|D&C 29:6]].
- If ye are not one ye are not mine, [[D&C 38.27|D&C 38:27]].

- The Lord called his people Zion because they were of one heart and one mind, [[Moses 7.18|Moses 7:18]].
