# Manna

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/manna?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Bread of Life|Bread of Life]]; [[GS Exodus|Exodus]]

A small, round food substance with the taste of honey wafers ([[Exodus 16.14|Ex. 16:14–31]][[Exodus 16.15|]][[Exodus 16.16|]][[Exodus 16.17|]][[Exodus 16.18|]][[Exodus 16.19|]][[Exodus 16.20|]][[Exodus 16.21|]][[Exodus 16.22|]][[Exodus 16.23|]][[Exodus 16.24|]][[Exodus 16.25|]][[Exodus 16.26|]][[Exodus 16.27|]][[Exodus 16.28|]][[Exodus 16.29|]][[Exodus 16.30|]][[Exodus 16.31|]]) or of fresh oil ([[Numbers 11.7|Num. 11:7–8]][[Numbers 11.8|]]). The Lord provided it to feed the children of Israel during their forty years in the wilderness ([[Exodus 16.4|Ex. 16:4–5, 14–30, 35]][[Exodus 16.5|]][[Exodus 16.14|]][[Exodus 16.15|]][[Exodus 16.16|]][[Exodus 16.17|]][[Exodus 16.18|]][[Exodus 16.19|]][[Exodus 16.20|]][[Exodus 16.21|]][[Exodus 16.22|]][[Exodus 16.23|]][[Exodus 16.24|]][[Exodus 16.25|]][[Exodus 16.26|]][[Exodus 16.27|]][[Exodus 16.28|]][[Exodus 16.29|]][[Exodus 16.30|]][[Exodus 16.35|]]; [[Joshua 5.12|Josh. 5:12]]; [[1 Nephi 17.28|1 Ne. 17:28]]).

The children of Israel called it manna (or *man-hu* in Hebrew)—which meant “What is it?”—because they did not know what it was ([[Exodus 16.15|Ex. 16:15]]). It was also called “angels’ food” and “bread from heaven” ([[Psalms 78.24|Ps. 78:24–25]][[Psalms 78.25|]]; [[John 6.31|John 6:31]]). It was a symbol for Christ, who would be the Bread of Life ([[John 6.31|John 6:31–35]][[John 6.32|]][[John 6.33|]][[John 6.34|]][[John 6.35|]]).
