# Good Shepherd

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/good-shepherd?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

Jesus Christ is the Good Shepherd. Symbolically, His followers are like sheep whom Jesus watches over.

- The Lord is my shepherd, [[Psalms 23.1|Ps. 23:1]].
- He shall feed his flock like a shepherd, [[Isaiah 40.11|Isa. 40:11]].
- So will I seek out my sheep, [[Ezekiel 34.12|Ezek. 34:12]].

- I am the good shepherd, [[John 10.14|John 10:14–15]][[John 10.15|]].
- Jesus is that great shepherd of the sheep, [[Hebrews 13.20|Heb. 13:20]].

- He numbereth his sheep and they know him, [[1 Nephi 22.25|1 Ne. 22:25]].
- The good shepherd calls you in his name, which is Christ, [[Alma 5.38|Alma 5:38, 60]][[Alma 5.60|]].
- There shall be one fold, and one shepherd, [[3 Nephi 15.21|3 Ne. 15:21]] ([[John 10.16|John 10:16]]).
