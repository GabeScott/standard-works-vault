# Habakkuk

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/habakkuk?lang=eng)

An Old Testament prophet in Judah who spoke of the sinfulness of the people possibly during the reign of Jehoiachin (about 600 ).
