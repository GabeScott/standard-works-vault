# Create, Creation

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/create-creation?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Beginning|Beginning]]; [[GS Earth|Earth]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Sabbath Day|Sabbath Day]]; [[GS Spirit Creation|Spirit Creation]]

To organize. God, working through His Son, Jesus Christ, organized the elements in nature to form the heavens and the earth. Heavenly Father and Jesus created man in Their image ([[Moses 2.26|Moses 2:26–27]][[Moses 2.27|]]).

- In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth, [[Genesis 1.1|Gen. 1:1]].
- Let us make man in our image, [[Genesis 1.26|Gen. 1:26]] ([[Moses 2.26|Moses 2:26–27]][[Moses 2.27|]]; [[Abraham 4.26|Abr. 4:26]]).

- All things were made by him, [[John 1.3|John 1:3, 10]][[John 1.10|]].
- By him were all things created that are in heaven, [[Colossians 1.16|Col. 1:16]] ([[Mosiah 3.8|Mosiah 3:8]]; [[Helaman 14.12|Hel. 14:12]]).
- God made the worlds by his Son, [[Hebrews 1.2|Heb. 1:2]].

- Man was created in the beginning, [[Mosiah 7.27|Mosiah 7:27]].
- I created the heavens and the earth and all things, [[3 Nephi 9.15|3 Ne. 9:15]] ([[Mormon 9.11|Morm. 9:11, 17]][[Mormon 9.17|]]).
- All men were created in the beginning after mine own image, [[Ether 3.15|Ether 3:15]].

- Jesus Christ created the heavens and the earth, [[D&C 14.9|D&C 14:9]].
- He created man, male and female, after his own image, [[D&C 20.18|D&C 20:18]].

- Worlds without number have I created, [[Moses 1.33|Moses 1:33]].
- By mine Only Begotten I created heaven, [[Moses 2.1|Moses 2:1]].
- I, the Lord God, created all things spiritually before they were naturally upon the face of the earth, [[Moses 3.5|Moses 3:5]].
- Millions of earths like this would not be a beginning to the number of thy creations, [[Moses 7.30|Moses 7:30]].
- The Gods organized and formed the heavens, [[Abraham 4.1|Abr. 4:1]].
