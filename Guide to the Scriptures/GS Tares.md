# Tares

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/tares?lang=eng)

A grass or poisonous weed that is similar in appearance to wheat. It cannot be distinguished from wheat until it is fully grown ([[Matthew 13.24|Matt. 13:24–30]][[Matthew 13.25|]][[Matthew 13.26|]][[Matthew 13.27|]][[Matthew 13.28|]][[Matthew 13.29|]][[Matthew 13.30|]]; [[D&C 86.1|D&C 86:1–7]][[D&C 86.2|]][[D&C 86.3|]][[D&C 86.4|]][[D&C 86.5|]][[D&C 86.6|]][[D&C 86.7|]]).
