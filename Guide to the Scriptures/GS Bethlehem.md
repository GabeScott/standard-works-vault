# Bethlehem

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/bethlehem?lang=eng)

A small city located five miles (eight kilometers) south of Jerusalem. In Hebrew, Bethlehem means “house of bread”; it is also called Ephrath, meaning “fruitful.” Jesus Christ was born in Bethlehem ([[Micah 5.2|Micah 5:2]]; [[Matthew 2.1|Matt. 2:1–8]][[Matthew 2.2|]][[Matthew 2.3|]][[Matthew 2.4|]][[Matthew 2.5|]][[Matthew 2.6|]][[Matthew 2.7|]][[Matthew 2.8|]]). It is the burial place of Rachel ([[Genesis 35.19|Gen. 35:19]]; [[Genesis 48.7|48:7]]).

- Ruth and Boaz lived there, [[Ruth 1.22|Ruth 1:22]].
- Samuel anointed David there, [[1 Samuel 16.1|1 Sam. 16:1–13]][[1 Samuel 16.2|]][[1 Samuel 16.3|]][[1 Samuel 16.4|]][[1 Samuel 16.5|]][[1 Samuel 16.6|]][[1 Samuel 16.7|]][[1 Samuel 16.8|]][[1 Samuel 16.9|]][[1 Samuel 16.10|]][[1 Samuel 16.11|]][[1 Samuel 16.12|]][[1 Samuel 16.13|]]; [[1 Samuel 17.12|17:12, 15]][[1 Samuel 17.15|]]; [[1 Samuel 20.6|20:6, 28]][[1 Samuel 20.28|]].

- Herod had the children slain there, [[Matthew 2.16|Matt. 2:16]].
