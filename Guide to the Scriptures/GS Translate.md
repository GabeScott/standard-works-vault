# Translate

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/translate?lang=eng)

To express the meaning of an idea given in one language in equal terms in another language ([[Mosiah 8.8|Mosiah 8:8–13]][[Mosiah 8.9|]][[Mosiah 8.10|]][[Mosiah 8.11|]][[Mosiah 8.12|]][[Mosiah 8.13|]]; [[Articles of Faith 1.8|A of F 1:8]]). In the scriptures it is often referred to as a gift from God ([[Alma 9.21|Alma 9:21]]; [[D&C 8.1|D&C 8]][[D&C 8.2|]][[D&C 8.3|]][[D&C 8.4|]][[D&C 8.5|]][[D&C 8.6|]][[D&C 8.7|]][[D&C 8.8|]][[D&C 8.9|]][[D&C 8.10|]][[D&C 8.11|]][[D&C 8.12|]]; [[D&C 9.7|9:7–9]][[D&C 9.8|]][[D&C 9.9|]]). It sometimes can mean to improve or correct an existing translation in a language or to restore a lost text ([[D&C 45.60|D&C 45:60–61]][[D&C 45.61|]]). Joseph Smith was commanded to undertake an inspired translation of the King James Bible ([[D&C 42.56|D&C 42:56]]; [[D&C 76.15|76:15]]).

- Joseph Smith had the power to translate through the mercy of God, by the power of God, [[D&C 1.29|D&C 1:29]].
- You have a gift to translate, [[D&C 5.4|D&C 5:4]].
- I grant unto you a gift, if you desire of me, to translate, [[D&C 6.25|D&C 6:25]].
- God gave Joseph Smith power from on high to translate the Book of Mormon, [[D&C 20.8|D&C 20:8]].

- By the means of the Urim and Thummim I translated some of the characters, [[Joseph Smith History 1.62|JS—H 1:62]] ([[Mosiah 8.13|Mosiah 8:13]]; [[Mosiah 28.13|28:13]]).
