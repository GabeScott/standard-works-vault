# Ahab

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/ahab?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jezebel|Jezebel]]

In the Old Testament, one of the most wicked and powerful kings of northern Israel. He married Jezebel, a Sidonian princess, through whose influence the worship of Baal and Ashtoreth was established in Israel ([[1 Kings 16.29|1 Kgs. 16:29–33]][[1 Kings 16.30|]][[1 Kings 16.31|]][[1 Kings 16.32|]][[1 Kings 16.33|]]; [[2 Kings 3.2|2 Kgs. 3:2]]) and an attempt was made to do away with the prophets and the worship of Jehovah ([[1 Kings 18.13|1 Kgs. 18:13]]).

- Reigned over Israel in Samaria twenty and two years, [[1 Kings 16.29|1 Kgs. 16:29]] ([[1 Kings 16.1|1 Kgs. 16–22]][[1 Kings 16.2|]][[1 Kings 16.3|]][[1 Kings 16.4|]][[1 Kings 16.5|]][[1 Kings 16.6|]][[1 Kings 16.7|]][[1 Kings 16.8|]][[1 Kings 16.9|]][[1 Kings 16.10|]][[1 Kings 16.11|]][[1 Kings 16.12|]][[1 Kings 16.13|]][[1 Kings 16.14|]][[1 Kings 16.15|]][[1 Kings 16.16|]][[1 Kings 16.17|]][[1 Kings 16.18|]][[1 Kings 16.19|]][[1 Kings 16.20|]][[1 Kings 16.21|]][[1 Kings 16.22|]][[1 Kings 16.23|]][[1 Kings 16.24|]][[1 Kings 16.25|]][[1 Kings 16.26|]][[1 Kings 16.27|]][[1 Kings 16.28|]][[1 Kings 16.29|]][[1 Kings 16.30|]][[1 Kings 16.31|]][[1 Kings 16.32|]][[1 Kings 16.33|]][[1 Kings 16.34|]]).
- Did evil in the sight of the Lord above all that were before him, [[1 Kings 16.30|1 Kgs. 16:30]].
- Was slain in battle, [[1 Kings 22.29|1 Kgs. 22:29–40]][[1 Kings 22.30|]][[1 Kings 22.31|]][[1 Kings 22.32|]][[1 Kings 22.33|]][[1 Kings 22.34|]][[1 Kings 22.35|]][[1 Kings 22.36|]][[1 Kings 22.37|]][[1 Kings 22.38|]][[1 Kings 22.39|]][[1 Kings 22.40|]].
