# Innocence, Innocent

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/innocence-innocent?lang=eng)

Blameless or sinless.

- Before the fall, Adam and Eve were in a state of innocence, [[2 Nephi 2.23|2 Ne. 2:23]].
- The blood of the innocent shall stand as a witness, [[Alma 14.11|Alma 14:11]].

- Every spirit of man was innocent in the beginning, [[D&C 93.38|D&C 93:38]].
- The innocent are not to be condemned with the unjust, [[D&C 104.7|D&C 104:7]].
- Joseph and Hyrum Smith were innocent of any crime, [[D&C 135.6|D&C 135:6–7]][[D&C 135.7|]].

- Children are whole from the foundation of the earth, [[Moses 6.54|Moses 6:54]].
