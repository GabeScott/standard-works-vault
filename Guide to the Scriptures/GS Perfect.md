# Perfect

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/perfect?lang=eng)

Complete, whole, and fully developed; totally righteous. Perfect can also mean without sin or evil. Only Christ was totally perfect. True followers of Christ may become perfect through His grace and Atonement.

- Let your heart be perfect with the Lord, [[1 Kings 8.61|1 Kgs. 8:61]].

- Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father in heaven, [[Matthew 5.48|Matt. 5:48]] ([[3 Nephi 12.48|3 Ne. 12:48]]).
- If any man offend not in word, the same is a perfect man, [[James 3.2|James 3:2]].

- Faith is not to have a perfect knowledge of things, [[Alma 32.21|Alma 32:21, 26]][[Alma 32.26|]].
- The Atonement was made so that God might be a perfect God, [[Alma 42.15|Alma 42:15]].
- Moroni was a man of perfect understanding, [[Alma 48.11|Alma 48:11–13, 17–18]][[Alma 48.12|]][[Alma 48.13|]][[Alma 48.17|]][[Alma 48.18|]].
- The Spirit of Christ is given to every man to judge and know with a perfect knowledge whether something is of God or the devil, [[Moroni 7.15|Moro. 7:15–17]][[Moroni 7.16|]][[Moroni 7.17|]].
- Come unto Christ, and be perfected in him, [[Moroni 10.32|Moro. 10:32]].

- Continue in patience until ye are perfected, [[D&C 67.13|D&C 67:13]].
- These are they who are just men made perfect through Jesus, [[D&C 76.69|D&C 76:69]].
- The offices in the Church are for the perfecting of the Saints, [[D&C 124.143|D&C 124:143]] ([[Ephesians 4.11|Eph. 4:11–13]][[Ephesians 4.12|]][[Ephesians 4.13|]]).
- The living are not perfect without their dead, [[D&C 128.15|D&C 128:15, 18]][[D&C 128.18|]].

- Noah was a just man, and perfect in his generation, [[Moses 8.27|Moses 8:27]].
