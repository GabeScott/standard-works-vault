# Alma the Elder

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/alma-the-elder?lang=eng)

A Nephite prophet in the Book of Mormon who organized the Church in the days of wicked King Noah.

- Was a priest of wicked King Noah and a descendant of Nephi, [[Mosiah 17.1|Mosiah 17:1–2]][[Mosiah 17.2|]].
- After hearing and believing Abinadi, was cast out by the king. He fled, hid, and wrote Abinadi’s words, [[Mosiah 17.3|Mosiah 17:3–4]][[Mosiah 17.4|]].
- Repented and taught the words of Abinadi, [[Mosiah 18.1|Mosiah 18:1]].
- Baptized at the waters of Mormon, [[Mosiah 18.12|Mosiah 18:12–16]][[Mosiah 18.13|]][[Mosiah 18.14|]][[Mosiah 18.15|]][[Mosiah 18.16|]].
- Organized the Church, [[Mosiah 18.17|Mosiah 18:17–29]][[Mosiah 18.18|]][[Mosiah 18.19|]][[Mosiah 18.20|]][[Mosiah 18.21|]][[Mosiah 18.22|]][[Mosiah 18.23|]][[Mosiah 18.24|]][[Mosiah 18.25|]][[Mosiah 18.26|]][[Mosiah 18.27|]][[Mosiah 18.28|]][[Mosiah 18.29|]].
- Arrived with his people in Zarahemla, [[Mosiah 24.25|Mosiah 24:25]].
- Was given authority over the Church, [[Mosiah 26.8|Mosiah 26:8]].
- Judged and led the Church, [[Mosiah 26.34|Mosiah 26:34–39]][[Mosiah 26.35|]][[Mosiah 26.36|]][[Mosiah 26.37|]][[Mosiah 26.38|]][[Mosiah 26.39|]].
- Conferred the office of high priest on his son, [[Alma 4.4|Alma 4:4]] ([[Mosiah 29.42|Mosiah 29:42]]; [[Alma 5.3|Alma 5:3]]).
