# Passover

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/passover?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Lamb of God|Lamb of God]]; [[GS Last Supper|Last Supper]]

The Feast of the Passover was instituted to help the children of Israel remember when the destroying angel passed over their houses and delivered them from the Egyptians ([[Exodus 12.21|Ex. 12:21–28]][[Exodus 12.22|]][[Exodus 12.23|]][[Exodus 12.24|]][[Exodus 12.25|]][[Exodus 12.26|]][[Exodus 12.27|]][[Exodus 12.28|]]; [[Exodus 13.14|13:14–15]][[Exodus 13.15|]]). The unblemished lambs, whose blood was used as a sign to save Israel anciently, are a symbol of Jesus Christ, the Lamb of God, whose sacrifice redeemed all mankind.

- This is the ordinance of the Passover, [[Exodus 12.43|Ex. 12:43]].

- Jesus and his Apostles kept the Passover at the Last Supper, [[Matthew 26.17|Matt. 26:17–29]][[Matthew 26.18|]][[Matthew 26.19|]][[Matthew 26.20|]][[Matthew 26.21|]][[Matthew 26.22|]][[Matthew 26.23|]][[Matthew 26.24|]][[Matthew 26.25|]][[Matthew 26.26|]][[Matthew 26.27|]][[Matthew 26.28|]][[Matthew 26.29|]] ([[Mark 14.12|Mark 14:12–25]][[Mark 14.13|]][[Mark 14.14|]][[Mark 14.15|]][[Mark 14.16|]][[Mark 14.17|]][[Mark 14.18|]][[Mark 14.19|]][[Mark 14.20|]][[Mark 14.21|]][[Mark 14.22|]][[Mark 14.23|]][[Mark 14.24|]][[Mark 14.25|]]).
- Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world, [[John 1.29|John 1:29, 36]][[John 1.36|]].
- Christ our Passover is sacrificed for us, [[1 Corinthians 5.7|1 Cor. 5:7]].
- We are redeemed by the blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish, [[1 Peter 1.18|1 Pet. 1:18–19]][[1 Peter 1.19|]].

- Have faith on the Lamb of God who taketh away the sins of the world, [[Alma 7.14|Alma 7:14]].

- Saints who live by the Word of Wisdom to be preserved as were the children of Israel, [[D&C 89.21|D&C 89:21]].

- The Lamb is slain from the foundation of the world, [[Moses 7.47|Moses 7:47]].
