# Mind

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/mind?lang=eng)

Mental faculties; conscious powers of thought.

- Serve him with a perfect heart and with a willing mind, [[1 Chronicles 28.9|1 Chr. 28:9]].

- Love the Lord thy God with all thy mind, [[Matthew 22.37|Matt. 22:37]].

- To be carnally minded is death; to be spiritually minded is life eternal, [[2 Nephi 9.39|2 Ne. 9:39]].
- The voice of the Lord came into my mind, [[Enos 1.10|Enos 1:10]].
- The word had a more powerful effect upon the minds of the people than the sword, [[Alma 31.5|Alma 31:5]].

- I will tell you in your mind, [[D&C 8.2|D&C 8:2]].
- Study it out in your mind, [[D&C 9.8|D&C 9:8]].
- Let the solemnities of eternity rest upon your minds, [[D&C 43.34|D&C 43:34]].
- Your minds in times past have been darkened, [[D&C 84.54|D&C 84:54]].
- Retire to thy bed early, arise early that your bodies and minds may be invigorated, [[D&C 88.124|D&C 88:124]].

- Satan knew not the mind of God, [[Moses 4.6|Moses 4:6]].
- The Lord called his people Zion, because they were of one heart and one mind, [[Moses 7.18|Moses 7:18]].
