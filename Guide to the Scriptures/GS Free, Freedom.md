# Free, Freedom

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/free-freedom?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Agency|Agency]]; [[GS Captivity|Captivity]]; [[GS Liberty|Liberty]]

The power or ability to make personal choices without compulsion. In a spiritual sense, a person who repents and obeys God’s will is free from the bondage of sin through the Atonement of Jesus Christ ([[Mosiah 5.8|Mosiah 5:8]]).

- The truth shall make you free, [[John 8.32|John 8:32]].
- Those who become free from sin receive eternal life, [[Romans 6.19|Rom. 6:19–23]][[Romans 6.20|]][[Romans 6.21|]][[Romans 6.22|]][[Romans 6.23|]].

- A righteous branch of the house of Israel shall be brought out of captivity unto freedom, [[2 Nephi 3.5|2 Ne. 3:5]].
- They cried unto the Lord for their freedom, [[Alma 43.48|Alma 43:48–50]][[Alma 43.49|]][[Alma 43.50|]].
- Moroni did joy in the freedom of his country, [[Alma 48.11|Alma 48:11]].
- The Spirit of God is the spirit of freedom, [[Alma 61.15|Alma 61:15]].

- Follow me, and you shall be a free people, [[D&C 38.22|D&C 38:22]].
