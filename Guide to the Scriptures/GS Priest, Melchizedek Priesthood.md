# Priest, Melchizedek Priesthood

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/priest-melchizedek-priesthood?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS High Priest|High Priest]]; [[GS Melchizedek Priesthood|Melchizedek Priesthood]]

A person who performs religious rites for others and directed to God. Frequently in the scriptures, priests are actually high priests after the order of Melchizedek ([[Alma 13.2|Alma 13:2]]). Those who receive a fulness of God’s glory after the Resurrection become priests and kings in the celestial world.

- Melchizedek was a priest of the most high God, [[Genesis 14.18|Gen. 14:18]].
- Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchizedek, [[Psalms 110.4|Ps. 110:4]] ([[Hebrews 5.6|Heb. 5:6]]; [[Hebrews 7.17|7:17, 21]][[Hebrews 7.21|]]).

- Christ hath made us kings and priests unto God and his Father, [[Revelation 1.6|Rev. 1:6]] ([[Revelation 5.10|Rev. 5:10]]; [[Revelation 20.6|20:6]]).

- Remember that the Lord God ordained priests after his holy order, [[Alma 13.1|Alma 13:1–20]][[Alma 13.2|]][[Alma 13.3|]][[Alma 13.4|]][[Alma 13.5|]][[Alma 13.6|]][[Alma 13.7|]][[Alma 13.8|]][[Alma 13.9|]][[Alma 13.10|]][[Alma 13.11|]][[Alma 13.12|]][[Alma 13.13|]][[Alma 13.14|]][[Alma 13.15|]][[Alma 13.16|]][[Alma 13.17|]][[Alma 13.18|]][[Alma 13.19|]][[Alma 13.20|]].

- Those who come forth in the resurrection of the just are priests and kings, [[D&C 76.50|D&C 76:50, 55–60]][[D&C 76.55|]][[D&C 76.56|]][[D&C 76.57|]][[D&C 76.58|]][[D&C 76.59|]][[D&C 76.60|]].
