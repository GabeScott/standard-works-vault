# Pure, Purity

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/pure-purity?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Clean and Unclean|Clean and Unclean]]; [[GS Sanctification|Sanctification]]

Free from sin or guilt; blameless. A person becomes pure when his thoughts and actions are clean in every way. A person who has committed sin can become pure through faith in Jesus Christ, repentance, and receiving the ordinances of the gospel.

- He that hath clean hands and a pure heart shall receive the Lord’s blessings, [[Psalms 24.3|Ps. 24:3–5]][[Psalms 24.4|]][[Psalms 24.5|]].
- Be ye clean, that bear the vessels of the Lord, [[Isaiah 52.11|Isa. 52:11]] ([[D&C 133.4|D&C 133:4–5]][[D&C 133.5|]]).

- Blessed are the pure in heart, [[Matthew 5.8|Matt. 5:8]] ([[3 Nephi 12.8|3 Ne. 12:8]]).
- Whatsoever things are pure, think on these things, [[Philippians 4.8|Philip. 4:8]] ([[Articles of Faith 1.13|A of F 1:13]]).

- All ye that are pure in heart, lift up your heads and receive the pleasing word of God, [[Jacob 3.2|Jacob 3:2–3]][[Jacob 3.3|]].
- Can ye look up to God at that day with a pure heart and clean hands, [[Alma 5.19|Alma 5:19]].
- Being pure and spotless before God, they could not look upon sin save it were with abhorrence, [[Alma 13.12|Alma 13:12]].
- Be purified even as Christ is pure, [[Moroni 7.48|Moro. 7:48]] ([[Mormon 9.6|Morm. 9:6]]).

- Christ will reserve unto himself a pure people, [[D&C 43.14|D&C 43:14]].
- The Lord commanded that a house be built in Zion in which the pure shall see God, [[D&C 97.10|D&C 97:10–17]][[D&C 97.11|]][[D&C 97.12|]][[D&C 97.13|]][[D&C 97.14|]][[D&C 97.15|]][[D&C 97.16|]][[D&C 97.17|]].
- This is Zion—the pure in heart, [[D&C 97.21|D&C 97:21]].
