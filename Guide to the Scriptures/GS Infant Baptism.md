# Infant Baptism

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/infant-baptism?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Account, Accountable, Accountability|Account, Accountable, Accountability]]; [[GS Baptism, Baptize|Baptism, Baptize—Baptism not for infants]]; [[GS Child, Children|Child, Children]]; [[GS Salvation|Salvation—Salvation of children]]

The unnecessary practice of baptizing babies and children who are under the age of accountability, which is eight years old. The Lord condemns infant baptism ([[Moroni 8.10|Moro. 8:10–21]][[Moroni 8.11|]][[Moroni 8.12|]][[Moroni 8.13|]][[Moroni 8.14|]][[Moroni 8.15|]][[Moroni 8.16|]][[Moroni 8.17|]][[Moroni 8.18|]][[Moroni 8.19|]][[Moroni 8.20|]][[Moroni 8.21|]]). Children are born innocent and without sin. Satan has no power to tempt children until they begin to become accountable ([[D&C 29.46|D&C 29:46–47]][[D&C 29.47|]]) so they have no need to repent or be baptized. Children should be baptized at the age of eight ([[D&C 68.25|D&C 68:25–27]][[D&C 68.26|]][[D&C 68.27|]]).
