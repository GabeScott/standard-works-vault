# Captivity

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/captivity?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Free, Freedom|Free, Freedom]]

To be in physical or spiritual bondage.

- The house of Israel went into captivity for their iniquity, [[Ezekiel 39.23|Ezek. 39:23]].

- He that leads into captivity shall go into captivity, [[Revelation 13.10|Rev. 13:10]].

- The wicked will be brought down into the captivity of the devil, [[1 Nephi 14.4|1 Ne. 14:4, 7]][[1 Nephi 14.7|]].
- Men are free to choose liberty and eternal life or captivity and death, [[2 Nephi 2.27|2 Ne. 2:27]].
- The will of the flesh gives the spirit of the devil power to captivate, [[2 Nephi 2.29|2 Ne. 2:29]].
- Have you sufficiently retained in remembrance the captivity of your fathers, [[Alma 5.5|Alma 5:5–6]][[Alma 5.6|]].
- They who harden their hearts are taken captive by the devil, [[Alma 12.11|Alma 12:11]].
- Watch and pray always, lest ye be tempted by the devil, and ye be led away captive by him, [[3 Nephi 18.15|3 Ne. 18:15]].
