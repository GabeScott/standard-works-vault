# Leprosy

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/leprosy?lang=eng)

A terrible form of skin disease spoken of in the Old and New Testaments. Many notable people in the Bible were at some time afflicted with it, including Moses ([[Exodus 4.6|Ex. 4:6–7]][[Exodus 4.7|]]), his sister Miriam ([[Numbers 12.10|Num. 12:10]]), Naaman ([[2 Kings 5.1|2 Kgs. 5]][[2 Kings 5.2|]][[2 Kings 5.3|]][[2 Kings 5.4|]][[2 Kings 5.5|]][[2 Kings 5.6|]][[2 Kings 5.7|]][[2 Kings 5.8|]][[2 Kings 5.9|]][[2 Kings 5.10|]][[2 Kings 5.11|]][[2 Kings 5.12|]][[2 Kings 5.13|]][[2 Kings 5.14|]][[2 Kings 5.15|]][[2 Kings 5.16|]][[2 Kings 5.17|]][[2 Kings 5.18|]][[2 Kings 5.19|]][[2 Kings 5.20|]][[2 Kings 5.21|]][[2 Kings 5.22|]][[2 Kings 5.23|]][[2 Kings 5.24|]][[2 Kings 5.25|]][[2 Kings 5.26|]][[2 Kings 5.27|]]), and King Uzziah ([[2 Chronicles 26.19|2 Chr. 26:19–21]][[2 Chronicles 26.20|]][[2 Chronicles 26.21|]]).

- Jesus cured leprosy in several people, [[Matthew 8.2|Matt. 8:2–4]][[Matthew 8.3|]][[Matthew 8.4|]] ([[Mark 1.40|Mark 1:40–45]][[Mark 1.41|]][[Mark 1.42|]][[Mark 1.43|]][[Mark 1.44|]][[Mark 1.45|]]; [[Luke 5.12|Luke 5:12–15]][[Luke 5.13|]][[Luke 5.14|]][[Luke 5.15|]]; [[3 Nephi 17.7|3 Ne. 17:7–9]][[3 Nephi 17.8|]][[3 Nephi 17.9|]]).
- Jesus cured ten lepers, [[Luke 17.11|Luke 17:11–19]][[Luke 17.12|]][[Luke 17.13|]][[Luke 17.14|]][[Luke 17.15|]][[Luke 17.16|]][[Luke 17.17|]][[Luke 17.18|]][[Luke 17.19|]].
