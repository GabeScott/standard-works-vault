# Zerubbabel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/zerubbabel?lang=eng)

In the Old Testament, when Cyrus gave permission for the Jews to return to Palestine, Zerubbabel was appointed governor or representative of the Jewish royal house. His Persian name was Sheshbazzar ([[Ezra 1.8|Ezra 1:8]]). He was involved in rebuilding the temple in Jerusalem ([[Ezra 3.2|Ezra 3:2, 8]][[Ezra 3.8|]]; [[Ezra 5.2|5:2]]).
