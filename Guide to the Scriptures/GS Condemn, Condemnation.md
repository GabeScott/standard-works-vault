# Condemn, Condemnation

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/condemn-condemnation?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Judge, Judgment|Judge, Judgment]]; [[GS Judgment, the Last|Judgment, the Last]]

To judge or be judged guilty by God.

- God will condemn a man of wicked devices, [[Proverbs 12.2|Prov. 12:2]].

- We are chastened of the Lord, that we should not be condemned with the world, [[1 Corinthians 11.32|1 Cor. 11:32]].

- Our words, works, and thoughts will condemn us, [[Alma 12.14|Alma 12:14]].
- By knowing things and not doing them, men come under condemnation, [[Helaman 14.19|Hel. 14:19]].
- If we should cease to labor, we should be brought under condemnation, [[Moroni 9.6|Moro. 9:6]].

- He that forgives not his brother stands condemned before the Lord, [[D&C 64.9|D&C 64:9]].
- He who sins against the greater light shall receive the greater condemnation, [[D&C 82.3|D&C 82:3]].
- The whole Church is under condemnation until they repent and remember the Book of Mormon, [[D&C 84.54|D&C 84:54–57]][[D&C 84.55|]][[D&C 84.56|]][[D&C 84.57|]].
