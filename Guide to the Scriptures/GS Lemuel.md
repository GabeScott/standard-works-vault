# Lemuel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/lemuel?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Laman|Laman]]; [[GS Lamanites|Lamanites]]; [[GS Lehi, Father of Nephi|Lehi, Father of Nephi]]

In the Book of Mormon, the second son of Lehi and one of Nephi’s older brothers. He joined with Laman to oppose Nephi.

- Lehi admonished Lemuel to be steadfast, like a valley, [[1 Nephi 2.10|1 Ne. 2:10]].
- Became angry with Nephi and hearkened unto Laman, [[1 Nephi 3.28|1 Ne. 3:28]].
- Lemuelites were included among the Lamanites, [[Jacob 1.13|Jacob 1:13–14]][[Jacob 1.14|]] ([[Alma 47.35|Alma 47:35]]).
