# Sinai, Mount

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/sinai-mount?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Law of Moses|Law of Moses]]; [[GS Moses|Moses]]

A mountain on the Sinai Peninsula near which Moses and the Israelites camped three months after their exodus from Egypt; it is also called Mount Horeb ([[Exodus 3.1|Ex. 3:1]]). Here God gave His law to Moses for the house of Israel; here also the tabernacle was built ([[Exodus 19.2|Ex. 19:2]]; [[Exodus 20.18|20:18]]; [[Exodus 24.12|24:12]]; [[Exodus 32.15|32:15]]).
