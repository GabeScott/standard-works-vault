# Joy

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/joy?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Obedience, Obedient, Obey|Obedience, Obedient, Obey]]

A condition of great happiness coming from righteous living. The purpose of mortal life is for all people to have joy ([[2 Nephi 2.22|2 Ne. 2:22–25]][[2 Nephi 2.23|]][[2 Nephi 2.24|]][[2 Nephi 2.25|]]). A full joy will come only through Jesus Christ ([[John 15.11|John 15:11]]; [[D&C 93.33|D&C 93:33–34]][[D&C 93.34|]]; [[D&C 101.36|101:36]]).

- The meek also shall increase their joy in the Lord, [[Isaiah 29.19|Isa. 29:19]] ([[2 Nephi 27.30|2 Ne. 27:30]]).

- I bring you good tidings of great joy, [[Luke 2.10|Luke 2:10]].
- Your joy no man taketh away from you, [[John 16.22|John 16:22]].
- The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, [[Galatians 5.22|Gal. 5:22]].

- The fruit thereof filled my soul with exceedingly great joy, [[1 Nephi 8.12|1 Ne. 8:12]].
- Men are that they might have joy, [[2 Nephi 2.25|2 Ne. 2:25]].
- The joy of the righteous shall be full forever, [[2 Nephi 9.18|2 Ne. 9:18]].
- They may dwell with God in a state of never-ending happiness, [[Mosiah 2.41|Mosiah 2:41]].
- I will give up all that I possess that I may receive this great joy, [[Alma 22.15|Alma 22:15]].
- Perhaps I may be an instrument in the hands of God to bring some soul to repentance, and this is my joy, [[Alma 29.9|Alma 29:9]].
- What joy, and what marvelous light I beheld, [[Alma 36.20|Alma 36:20]].

- My Spirit shall fill your soul with joy, [[D&C 11.13|D&C 11:13]].
- How great shall be your joy with him in the kingdom of my Father, [[D&C 18.15|D&C 18:15–16]][[D&C 18.16|]].
- In this world your joy is not full, but in me your joy is full, [[D&C 101.36|D&C 101:36]].

- In this life I shall have joy, [[Moses 5.10|Moses 5:10–11]][[Moses 5.11|]].
