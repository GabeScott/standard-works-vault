- When you feel these swelling motions, ye will begin to say that this is a good seed, [[Alma 32.28|Alma 32:28]].

- You shall feel that it is right, [[D&C 9.8|D&C 9:8]].
- All people who shall enter the Lord’s house may feel thy power, [[D&C 109.13|D&C 109:13]].
