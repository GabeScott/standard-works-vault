# Counsel (verb)

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/counsel-verb?lang=eng)

As used in the scriptures, counsel means to advise or instruct.

- I counsel thee to buy of me gold tried in the fire, [[Revelation 3.18|Rev. 3:18]].

- Seek not to counsel the Lord, [[Jacob 4.10|Jacob 4:10]].
- He doth counsel in wisdom over all his works, [[Alma 37.12|Alma 37:12]].

- Your sins have come up unto me because you seek to counsel in your own ways, [[D&C 56.14|D&C 56:14]].
