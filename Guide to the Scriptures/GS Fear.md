# Fear

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/fear?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Courage, Courageous|Courage, Courageous]]; [[GS Faith|Faith]]; [[GS Reverence|Reverence]]

*Fear* can have two meanings: (1) to fear God is to feel reverence and awe for Him and to obey His commandments; (2) to fear man, mortal dangers, pain, and evil is to be afraid of such things and to dread them.

## Fear of God

- Surely the fear of God is not in this place, [[Genesis 20.11|Gen. 20:11]].
- Thou shalt fear the Lord thy God, [[Deuteronomy 6.13|Deut. 6:13]] ([[Joshua 24.14|Josh. 24:14]]; [[1 Samuel 12.24|1 Sam. 12:24]]).
- Serve the Lord with fear, [[Psalms 2.11|Ps. 2:11]].
- Fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, [[Psalms 111.10|Ps. 111:10]].
- Fear the Lord and depart from evil, [[Proverbs 3.7|Prov. 3:7]].
- It shall be well with them that fear God, [[Ecclesiastes 8.12|Eccl. 8:12]].

- Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling, [[Philippians 2.12|Philip. 2:12]].
- Fear God, and give glory to him, [[Revelation 14.7|Rev. 14:7]] ([[D&C 88.104|D&C 88:104]]).

- Prophets stirred up the people continually to keep them in the fear of the Lord, [[Enos 1.23|Enos 1:23]].
- Alma and the sons of Mosiah fell to the earth, for the fear of the Lord came upon them, [[Alma 36.7|Alma 36:7]].
- Work out your salvation with fear and trembling, [[Mormon 9.27|Morm. 9:27]].

- They who do not fear me, I will disturb and cause to tremble, [[D&C 10.56|D&C 10:56]].
- He that feareth me shall be looking for the signs of the coming of the Son of Man, [[D&C 45.39|D&C 45:39]].

## Fear of man

- Fear not, for I am with thee, [[Genesis 26.24|Gen. 26:24]] ([[Isaiah 41.10|Isa. 41:10]]).
- The Lord is with us: fear them not, [[Numbers 14.9|Num. 14:9]].
- Fear not: for they that be with us are more, [[2 Kings 6.16|2 Kgs. 6:16]].
- I will not fear what flesh can do, [[Psalms 56.4|Ps. 56:4]].
- Fear not the reproach of men, [[Isaiah 51.7|Isa. 51:7]] ([[2 Nephi 8.7|2 Ne. 8:7]]).

- God hath not given us the spirit of fear, [[2 Timothy 1.7|2 Tim. 1:7]].
- Perfect love casteth out fear, [[1 John 4.18|1 Jn. 4:18]] ([[Moroni 8.16|Moro. 8:16]]).

- The sons of Helaman did not fear death, [[Alma 56.46|Alma 56:46–48]][[Alma 56.47|]][[Alma 56.48|]].
- The fear of death fills the breasts of the wicked, [[Mormon 6.7|Morm. 6:7]].
- Fear not what man can do, [[Moroni 8.16|Moro. 8:16]].

- You should not have feared man more than God, [[D&C 3.7|D&C 3:7]] ([[D&C 30.1|D&C 30:1, 11]][[D&C 30.11|]]; [[D&C 122.9|122:9]]).
- Fear not to do good, [[D&C 6.33|D&C 6:33]].
- Whosoever belongeth to my church need not fear, [[D&C 10.55|D&C 10:55]].
- If ye are prepared ye shall not fear, [[D&C 38.30|D&C 38:30]].
- Strip yourselves from fear, [[D&C 67.10|D&C 67:10]].
- Be of good cheer, and do not fear, for I the Lord am with you, [[D&C 68.6|D&C 68:6]].
- Fear not thine enemies, [[D&C 136.17|D&C 136:17]].
