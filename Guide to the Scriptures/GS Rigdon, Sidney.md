# Rigdon, Sidney

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/rigdon-sidney?lang=eng)

An early convert and leader in the restored Church in the 1830s and early 1840s. Sidney Rigdon served for a time as First Counselor to Joseph Smith in the First Presidency of the Church ([[D&C 35.1|D&C 35]][[D&C 35.2|]][[D&C 35.3|]][[D&C 35.4|]][[D&C 35.5|]][[D&C 35.6|]][[D&C 35.7|]][[D&C 35.8|]][[D&C 35.9|]][[D&C 35.10|]][[D&C 35.11|]][[D&C 35.12|]][[D&C 35.13|]][[D&C 35.14|]][[D&C 35.15|]][[D&C 35.16|]][[D&C 35.17|]][[D&C 35.18|]][[D&C 35.19|]][[D&C 35.20|]][[D&C 35.21|]][[D&C 35.22|]][[D&C 35.23|]][[D&C 35.24|]][[D&C 35.25|]][[D&C 35.26|]][[D&C 35.27|]]; [[D&C 58.50|58:50, 57]][[D&C 58.57|]]; [[D&C 63.55|63:55–56]][[D&C 63.56|]]; [[D&C 76.11|76:11–12, 19–23]][[D&C 76.12|]][[D&C 76.19|]][[D&C 76.20|]][[D&C 76.21|]][[D&C 76.22|]][[D&C 76.23|]]; [[D&C 90.6|90:6]]; [[D&C 93.44|93:44]]; [[D&C 100.9|100:9–11]][[D&C 100.10|]][[D&C 100.11|]]; [[D&C 124.126|124:126]]).
