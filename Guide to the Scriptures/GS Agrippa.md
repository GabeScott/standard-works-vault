# Agrippa

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/agrippa?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Paul|Paul]]

In the New Testament, the son of Herod Agrippa Ⅰ and brother of Bernice and Drusilla. He was king of Chalcis, located in the Lebanons.
