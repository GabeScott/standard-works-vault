# Moronihah, Son of Captain Moroni

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/moronihah-son-of-captain-moroni?lang=eng)

A righteous Nephite commander in the Book of Mormon (about 60 ).

- Moroni turned the armies over to his son Moronihah, [[Alma 62.43|Alma 62:43]].
- Retook the city of Zarahemla, [[Helaman 1.33|Hel. 1:33]].
- Brought the Nephites to repentance and regained control over half of the land, [[Helaman 4.14|Hel. 4:14–20]][[Helaman 4.15|]][[Helaman 4.16|]][[Helaman 4.17|]][[Helaman 4.18|]][[Helaman 4.19|]][[Helaman 4.20|]].
