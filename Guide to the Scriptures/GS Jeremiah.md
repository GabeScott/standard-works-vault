# Jeremiah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/jeremiah?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Lamentations, Book of|Lamentations, Book of]]

An Old Testament prophet who was born to a priestly family and prophesied in Judah from 626 to 586  He lived near the time of other great prophets: Lehi, Ezekiel, Hosea, and Daniel.

Jeremiah was ordained to be a prophet in the premortal life ([[Jeremiah 1.4|Jer. 1:4–5]][[Jeremiah 1.5|]]). During his approximately forty years as prophet he taught against idolatry and immorality among the Jewish people ([[Jeremiah 3.1|Jer. 3:1–5]][[Jeremiah 3.2|]][[Jeremiah 3.3|]][[Jeremiah 3.4|]][[Jeremiah 3.5|]]; [[Jeremiah 7.8|7:8–10]][[Jeremiah 7.9|]][[Jeremiah 7.10|]]). He had to face continuous opposition and insult ([[Jeremiah 20.2|Jer. 20:2]]; [[Jeremiah 36.18|36:18–19]][[Jeremiah 36.19|]]; [[Jeremiah 38.4|38:4]]). After the fall of Jerusalem, the Jews who escaped into Egypt took Jeremiah with them ([[Jeremiah 43.5|Jer. 43:5–6]][[Jeremiah 43.6|]]), where, according to tradition, they stoned him to death.

This event of the latter days will be larger in proportion than even Moses’ bringing of Israel out of Egypt ([[Jeremiah 16.13|Jer. 16:13–15]][[Jeremiah 16.14|]][[Jeremiah 16.15|]]; [[Jeremiah 23.8|23:8]]).
