# Mary, Mother of Jesus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/mary-mother-of-jesus?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Joseph, Husband of Mary|Joseph, Husband of Mary]]

In the New Testament, a virgin chosen by God the Father to be the mother of His Son in the flesh. After Jesus’ birth, Mary had other children ([[Mark 6.3|Mark 6:3]]).

- She was betrothed to Joseph, [[Matthew 1.18|Matt. 1:18]] ([[Luke 1.27|Luke 1:27]]).
- Joseph was told not to divorce Mary or release her from the betrothal, [[Matthew 1.18|Matt. 1:18–25]][[Matthew 1.19|]][[Matthew 1.20|]][[Matthew 1.21|]][[Matthew 1.22|]][[Matthew 1.23|]][[Matthew 1.24|]][[Matthew 1.25|]].
- The wise men visited Mary, [[Matthew 2.11|Matt. 2:11]].
- Mary and Joseph fled with the child Jesus to Egypt, [[Matthew 2.13|Matt. 2:13–14]][[Matthew 2.14|]].
- After Herod’s death, the family returned to Nazareth, [[Matthew 2.19|Matt. 2:19–23]][[Matthew 2.20|]][[Matthew 2.21|]][[Matthew 2.22|]][[Matthew 2.23|]].
- The angel Gabriel visited her, [[Luke 1.26|Luke 1:26–38]][[Luke 1.27|]][[Luke 1.28|]][[Luke 1.29|]][[Luke 1.30|]][[Luke 1.31|]][[Luke 1.32|]][[Luke 1.33|]][[Luke 1.34|]][[Luke 1.35|]][[Luke 1.36|]][[Luke 1.37|]][[Luke 1.38|]].
- She visited Elisabeth, her cousin, [[Luke 1.36|Luke 1:36, 40–45]][[Luke 1.40|]][[Luke 1.41|]][[Luke 1.42|]][[Luke 1.43|]][[Luke 1.44|]][[Luke 1.45|]].
- Mary gave a psalm of praise to the Lord, [[Luke 1.46|Luke 1:46–55]][[Luke 1.47|]][[Luke 1.48|]][[Luke 1.49|]][[Luke 1.50|]][[Luke 1.51|]][[Luke 1.52|]][[Luke 1.53|]][[Luke 1.54|]][[Luke 1.55|]].
- Mary went to Bethlehem with Joseph, [[Luke 2.4|Luke 2:4–5]][[Luke 2.5|]].
- Mary gave birth to Jesus and laid him in a manger, [[Luke 2.7|Luke 2:7]].
- The shepherds went to Bethlehem to visit the Christ child, [[Luke 2.16|Luke 2:16–20]][[Luke 2.17|]][[Luke 2.18|]][[Luke 2.19|]][[Luke 2.20|]].
- Mary and Joseph took Jesus to the temple in Jerusalem, [[Luke 2.21|Luke 2:21–38]][[Luke 2.22|]][[Luke 2.23|]][[Luke 2.24|]][[Luke 2.25|]][[Luke 2.26|]][[Luke 2.27|]][[Luke 2.28|]][[Luke 2.29|]][[Luke 2.30|]][[Luke 2.31|]][[Luke 2.32|]][[Luke 2.33|]][[Luke 2.34|]][[Luke 2.35|]][[Luke 2.36|]][[Luke 2.37|]][[Luke 2.38|]].
- Mary and Joseph took Jesus to the Passover, [[Luke 2.41|Luke 2:41–52]][[Luke 2.42|]][[Luke 2.43|]][[Luke 2.44|]][[Luke 2.45|]][[Luke 2.46|]][[Luke 2.47|]][[Luke 2.48|]][[Luke 2.49|]][[Luke 2.50|]][[Luke 2.51|]][[Luke 2.52|]].
- Mary was at the wedding at Cana, [[John 2.2|John 2:2–5]][[John 2.3|]][[John 2.4|]][[John 2.5|]].
- The Savior, while on the cross, asked John to care for his mother, [[John 19.25|John 19:25–27]][[John 19.26|]][[John 19.27|]].
- Mary was with the Apostles after Christ was taken up into heaven, [[Acts 1.14|Acts 1:14]].

- Mary was a virgin, most beautiful and fair above all other virgins, [[1 Nephi 11.13|1 Ne. 11:13–20]][[1 Nephi 11.14|]][[1 Nephi 11.15|]][[1 Nephi 11.16|]][[1 Nephi 11.17|]][[1 Nephi 11.18|]][[1 Nephi 11.19|]][[1 Nephi 11.20|]].
- Christ’s mother was to be called Mary, [[Mosiah 3.8|Mosiah 3:8]].
- Mary would be a virgin, a precious and chosen vessel, [[Alma 7.10|Alma 7:10]].
