# Hebron

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/hebron?lang=eng)

An ancient city of Judah, twenty miles (thirty-two kilometers) south of Jerusalem. It was the burying place of Abraham and his family ([[Genesis 49.29|Gen. 49:29–32]][[Genesis 49.30|]][[Genesis 49.31|]][[Genesis 49.32|]]). It was David’s capital during the early part of his reign ([[2 Samuel 5.3|2 Sam. 5:3–5]][[2 Samuel 5.4|]][[2 Samuel 5.5|]]).
