# Mary Magdalene

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/mary-magdalene?lang=eng)

A woman in the New Testament who became a devoted disciple of Jesus Christ. Magdalene refers to Magdala, the place from which this Mary came. It is located on the western shore of the Sea of Galilee.

- She was near the cross, [[Matthew 27.56|Matt. 27:56]] ([[Mark 15.40|Mark 15:40]]; [[John 19.25|John 19:25]]).
- She was at Christ’s burial, [[Matthew 27.61|Matt. 27:61]] ([[Mark 15.47|Mark 15:47]]).
- She was at the tomb in the morning of the resurrection, [[Matthew 28.1|Matt. 28:1]] ([[Mark 16.1|Mark 16:1]]; [[Luke 24.10|Luke 24:10]]; [[John 20.1|John 20:1, 11]][[John 20.11|]]).
- Jesus appeared first to her after his resurrection, [[Mark 16.9|Mark 16:9]] ([[John 20.14|John 20:14–18]][[John 20.15|]][[John 20.16|]][[John 20.17|]][[John 20.18|]]).
- Seven devils went out of her, [[Luke 8.2|Luke 8:2]].
