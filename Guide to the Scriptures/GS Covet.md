# Covet

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/covet?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Envy|Envy]]

As used in the scriptures, to covet is to envy someone or to have an excessive desire for something.

- Thou shalt not covet, [[Exodus 20.17|Ex. 20:17]] ([[Deuteronomy 5.21|Deut. 5:21]]; [[Mosiah 13.24|Mosiah 13:24]]; [[D&C 19.25|D&C 19:25]]).
- He that hates covetousness shall prolong his days, [[Proverbs 28.16|Prov. 28:16]].
- They coveted fields and took them, [[Micah 2.2|Micah 2:2]].

- Beware of covetousness, [[Luke 12.15|Luke 12:15]].
- The law said, Thou shalt not covet, [[Romans 7.7|Rom. 7:7]].
- In the last days, men shall be covetous, [[2 Timothy 3.1|2 Tim. 3:1–2]][[2 Timothy 3.2|]].

- When Laban saw our property, he lusted after it, [[1 Nephi 3.25|1 Ne. 3:25]].

- Thou shalt not covet thine own property, [[D&C 19.26|D&C 19:26]].
- Cease to be covetous, [[D&C 88.123|D&C 88:123]].
- Do not covet that which is thy brother’s, [[D&C 136.20|D&C 136:20]].
