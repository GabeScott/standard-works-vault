# Broken Heart

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/broken-heart?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Heart|Heart]]; [[GS Humble, Humility|Humble, Humility]]; [[GS Meek, Meekness|Meek, Meekness]]; [[GS Repent, Repentance|Repent, Repentance]]; [[GS Sacrifice|Sacrifice]]

To have a broken heart is to be humble, contrite, repentant, and meek—that is, receptive to the will of God.

- I dwell with him that is of a contrite and humble spirit, [[Isaiah 57.15|Isa. 57:15]].

- Christ offered himself for those who have a broken heart and a contrite spirit, [[2 Nephi 2.7|2 Ne. 2:7]].
- Offer for sacrifice unto the Lord a broken heart and contrite spirit, [[3 Nephi 9.20|3 Ne. 9:20]] ([[D&C 59.8|D&C 59:8]]).
- Only those with broken hearts and contrite spirits are received unto baptism, [[Moroni 6.2|Moro. 6:2]].

- Jesus was crucified for the remission of sins unto the contrite heart, [[D&C 21.9|D&C 21:9]].
- He whose spirit is contrite is accepted, [[D&C 52.15|D&C 52:15]].
- The Holy Spirit is promised to those who are contrite, [[D&C 55.3|D&C 55:3]].
- My Spirit is sent forth to enlighten the humble and contrite, [[D&C 136.33|D&C 136:33]].
