# Confidence

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/confidence?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Belief, Believe|Belief, Believe]]; [[GS Faith|Faith]]; [[GS Trust|Trust]]

Having an assurance, belief, trust, or faith in something, especially God and Jesus Christ.

- It is better to trust in the Lord than to put confidence in man, [[Psalms 118.8|Ps. 118:8]].
- The Lord shall be thy confidence, [[Proverbs 3.26|Prov. 3:26]].

- When Christ shall appear, we may have confidence, [[1 John 2.28|1 Jn. 2:28]].

- The wicked Nephites lost the confidence of their children, [[Jacob 2.35|Jacob 2:35]].

- Then shall thy confidence wax strong in the presence of God, [[D&C 121.45|D&C 121:45]].
