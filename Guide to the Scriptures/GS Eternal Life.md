# Eternal Life

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/eternal-life?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Celestial Glory|Celestial Glory]]; [[GS Crown|Crown]]; [[GS Exaltation|Exaltation]]; [[GS Life|Life]]

To live forever as families in God’s presence ([[D&C 132.19|D&C 132:19–20, 24, 55]][[D&C 132.20|]][[D&C 132.24|]][[D&C 132.55|]]). Eternal life is God’s greatest gift to man.

- Thou hast the words of eternal life, [[John 6.68|John 6:68]].
- This is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, [[John 17.3|John 17:3]] ([[D&C 132.24|D&C 132:24]]).
- Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life, [[1 Timothy 6.12|1 Tim. 6:12]].

- People are free to choose liberty and eternal life, [[2 Nephi 2.27|2 Ne. 2:27]] ([[Helaman 14.31|Hel. 14:31]]).
- To be spiritually minded is life eternal, [[2 Nephi 9.39|2 Ne. 9:39]].
- Then are ye in the narrow path that leads to eternal life, [[2 Nephi 31.17|2 Ne. 31:17–20]][[2 Nephi 31.18|]][[2 Nephi 31.19|]][[2 Nephi 31.20|]].
- To believe in Christ and endure to the end is life eternal, [[2 Nephi 33.4|2 Ne. 33:4]] ([[3 Nephi 15.9|3 Ne. 15:9]]).

- He that hath eternal life is rich, [[D&C 6.7|D&C 6:7]] ([[D&C 11.7|D&C 11:7]]).
- Eternal life is the greatest of all the gifts of God, [[D&C 14.7|D&C 14:7]] ([[Romans 6.23|Rom. 6:23]]).
- The righteous will receive peace in this world, and eternal life in the world to come, [[D&C 59.23|D&C 59:23]].
- Those who endure to the end shall have a crown of eternal life, [[D&C 66.12|D&C 66:12]] ([[D&C 75.5|D&C 75:5]]).
- All who die without the gospel who would have received it had they lived are heirs of the celestial kingdom, [[D&C 137.7|D&C 137:7–9]][[D&C 137.8|]][[D&C 137.9|]].

- God’s work and glory is to bring to pass the immortality and eternal life of man, [[Moses 1.39|Moses 1:39]].
- God gives eternal life to the obedient, [[Moses 5.11|Moses 5:11]].
