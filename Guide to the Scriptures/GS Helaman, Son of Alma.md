# Helaman, Son of Alma

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/helaman-son-of-alma?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Alma, Son of Alma|Alma, Son of Alma]]; [[GS Anti-Nephi-Lehies|Anti-Nephi-Lehies]]; [[GS Helaman, Sons of|Helaman, Sons of]]

In the Book of Mormon, the oldest son of Alma, son of Alma ([[Alma 31.7|Alma 31:7]]). Helaman was a prophet and a military leader.

- Alma entrusted his son Helaman with the records of his people and with the plates of the Jaredites, [[Alma 37.1|Alma 37:1–2, 21]][[Alma 37.2|]][[Alma 37.21|]].
- Alma commanded Helaman to continue writing the history of his people, [[Alma 45.1|Alma 45–62]][[Alma 45.2|]][[Alma 45.3|]][[Alma 45.4|]][[Alma 45.5|]][[Alma 45.6|]][[Alma 45.7|]][[Alma 45.8|]][[Alma 45.9|]][[Alma 45.10|]][[Alma 45.11|]][[Alma 45.12|]][[Alma 45.13|]][[Alma 45.14|]][[Alma 45.15|]][[Alma 45.16|]][[Alma 45.17|]][[Alma 45.18|]][[Alma 45.19|]][[Alma 45.20|]][[Alma 45.21|]][[Alma 45.22|]][[Alma 45.23|]][[Alma 45.24|]].
- Helaman reestablished the Church, [[Alma 45.22|Alma 45:22–23]][[Alma 45.23|]].
- Two thousand young Ammonite warriors desired Helaman to be their leader, [[Alma 53.19|Alma 53:19, 22]][[Alma 53.22|]].
- Helaman and his stripling Ammonites battled the Lamanites and were preserved by faith, [[Alma 57.19|Alma 57:19–27]][[Alma 57.20|]][[Alma 57.21|]][[Alma 57.22|]][[Alma 57.23|]][[Alma 57.24|]][[Alma 57.25|]][[Alma 57.26|]][[Alma 57.27|]].
