# Abominable, Abomination

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/abominable-abomination?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Sin|Sin]]

In the scriptures, something that causes disgust or hatred to the righteous and pure.

- Lying lips are abomination to the Lord, [[Proverbs 12.22|Prov. 12:22]].

- Pride is abominable in the sight of the Lord, [[Jacob 2.13|Jacob 2:13–22]][[Jacob 2.14|]][[Jacob 2.15|]][[Jacob 2.16|]][[Jacob 2.17|]][[Jacob 2.18|]][[Jacob 2.19|]][[Jacob 2.20|]][[Jacob 2.21|]][[Jacob 2.22|]].
- The wicked are consigned to a view of their own abominations, [[Mosiah 3.25|Mosiah 3:25]].
- Unchastity is most abominable above all sins save murder and denying the Holy Ghost, [[Alma 39.3|Alma 39:3–5]][[Alma 39.4|]][[Alma 39.5|]].

- The indignation of the Lord is kindled against their abominations, [[D&C 97.24|D&C 97:24]].
