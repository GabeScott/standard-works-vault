# Envy

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/envy?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Covet|Covet]]; [[GS Jealous, Jealousy|Jealous, Jealousy]]

According to the scriptures, to desire something that belongs to another is wrong.

- The patriarchs, moved with envy, sold Joseph into Egypt, [[Acts 7.9|Acts 7:9]].
- Charity envieth not, [[1 Corinthians 13.4|1 Cor. 13:4]] ([[Moroni 7.45|Moro. 7:45]]).
- Envy comes of pride, [[1 Timothy 6.4|1 Tim. 6:4]].
- Where envy is, there is confusion and every evil work, [[James 3.16|James 3:16]].

- The Lord commanded men that they should not envy, [[2 Nephi 26.32|2 Ne. 26:32]].
- There was no envy among the people of Nephi, [[4 Nephi 1.15|4 Ne. 1:15–18]][[4 Nephi 1.16|]][[4 Nephi 1.17|]][[4 Nephi 1.18|]].

- The envy and wrath of man have been my common lot all the days of my life, [[D&C 127.2|D&C 127:2]].
