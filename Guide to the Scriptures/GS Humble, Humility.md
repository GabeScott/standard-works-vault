# Humble, Humility

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/humble-humility?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Broken Heart|Broken Heart]]; [[GS Meek, Meekness|Meek, Meekness]]; [[GS Poor|Poor]]; [[GS Pride|Pride]]; [[GS Weakness|Weakness]]

To make meek and teachable, or the condition of being meek and teachable. Humility includes recognizing our dependence upon God and desiring to submit to His will.

- God led thee forty years in the wilderness to humble thee, [[Deuteronomy 8.2|Deut. 8:2]].
- I humbled my soul with fasting, [[Psalms 35.13|Ps. 35:13]].
- Better is a poor and wise child than an old and foolish king, [[Ecclesiastes 4.13|Eccl. 4:13]].
- The Lord dwells with him that is humble, [[Isaiah 57.15|Isa. 57:15]].

- Whosoever shall humble himself as this little child, the same is greatest in the kingdom of heaven, [[Matthew 18.4|Matt. 18:4]].
- He that shall humble himself shall be exalted, [[Matthew 23.12|Matt. 23:12]] ([[Luke 14.11|Luke 14:11]]; [[Luke 18.14|18:14]]).
- Jesus humbled himself and became obedient unto death, [[Philippians 2.8|Philip. 2:8]] ([[Luke 22.42|Luke 22:42]]; [[Luke 23.46|23:46]]).
- God resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble, [[1 Peter 5.5|1 Pet. 5:5–6]][[1 Peter 5.6|]] ([[2 Nephi 9.42|2 Ne. 9:42]]).

- Humble yourselves in the depths of humility, [[Mosiah 4.11|Mosiah 4:11]] ([[2 Nephi 9.42|2 Ne. 9:42]]; [[3 Nephi 12.2|3 Ne. 12:2]]).
- Have ye been sufficiently humble, [[Alma 5.27|Alma 5:27–28]][[Alma 5.28|]].
- The more humble part of the people became stronger in their humility, [[Helaman 3.33|Hel. 3:33–35]][[Helaman 3.34|]][[Helaman 3.35|]].
- I give unto men weakness that they may be humble, [[Ether 12.27|Ether 12:27]].

- Humility is a qualification for baptism, [[D&C 20.37|D&C 20:37]].
- Humble yourselves before me and you shall see me and know that I am, [[D&C 67.10|D&C 67:10]].
- Be thou humble and the Lord shall give thee answer to thy prayers, [[D&C 112.10|D&C 112:10]].
- Let him that is ignorant learn wisdom by humbling himself, [[D&C 136.32|D&C 136:32]].
- The Spirit is sent forth to enlighten the humble, [[D&C 136.33|D&C 136:33]].
