# Three Nephite Disciples

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/three-nephite-disciples?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Disciple|Disciple]]; [[GS Nephites|Nephites]]; [[GS Translated Beings|Translated Beings]]

Three of Christ’s chosen Nephite disciples referred to in the Book of Mormon.

The Lord granted to these disciples the same blessing granted to John the Beloved—that they might stay on the earth to bring souls to Christ until the Lord comes again. They were translated so that they would feel no pain and would not die ([[3 Nephi 28.1|3 Ne. 28]][[3 Nephi 28.2|]][[3 Nephi 28.3|]][[3 Nephi 28.4|]][[3 Nephi 28.5|]][[3 Nephi 28.6|]][[3 Nephi 28.7|]][[3 Nephi 28.8|]][[3 Nephi 28.9|]][[3 Nephi 28.10|]][[3 Nephi 28.11|]][[3 Nephi 28.12|]][[3 Nephi 28.13|]][[3 Nephi 28.14|]][[3 Nephi 28.15|]][[3 Nephi 28.16|]][[3 Nephi 28.17|]][[3 Nephi 28.18|]][[3 Nephi 28.19|]][[3 Nephi 28.20|]][[3 Nephi 28.21|]][[3 Nephi 28.22|]][[3 Nephi 28.23|]][[3 Nephi 28.24|]][[3 Nephi 28.25|]][[3 Nephi 28.26|]][[3 Nephi 28.27|]][[3 Nephi 28.28|]][[3 Nephi 28.29|]][[3 Nephi 28.30|]][[3 Nephi 28.31|]][[3 Nephi 28.32|]][[3 Nephi 28.33|]][[3 Nephi 28.34|]][[3 Nephi 28.35|]][[3 Nephi 28.36|]][[3 Nephi 28.37|]][[3 Nephi 28.38|]][[3 Nephi 28.39|]][[3 Nephi 28.40|]]).

- Christ granted the three disciples’ desire to tarry until his coming, [[3 Nephi 28.1|3 Ne. 28:1–9]][[3 Nephi 28.2|]][[3 Nephi 28.3|]][[3 Nephi 28.4|]][[3 Nephi 28.5|]][[3 Nephi 28.6|]][[3 Nephi 28.7|]][[3 Nephi 28.8|]][[3 Nephi 28.9|]].
- They will never suffer the pains of death or sorrow, [[3 Nephi 28.7|3 Ne. 28:7–9]][[3 Nephi 28.8|]][[3 Nephi 28.9|]].
- They shall have fulness of joy, [[3 Nephi 28.10|3 Ne. 28:10]].
- They were temporarily caught up into heaven, [[3 Nephi 28.13|3 Ne. 28:13–17]][[3 Nephi 28.14|]][[3 Nephi 28.15|]][[3 Nephi 28.16|]][[3 Nephi 28.17|]].
- They ministered unto the people and endured persecution, [[3 Nephi 28.18|3 Ne. 28:18–23]][[3 Nephi 28.19|]][[3 Nephi 28.20|]][[3 Nephi 28.21|]][[3 Nephi 28.22|]][[3 Nephi 28.23|]].
- They ministered unto Mormon, [[3 Nephi 28.24|3 Ne. 28:24–26]][[3 Nephi 28.25|]][[3 Nephi 28.26|]] ([[Mormon 8.10|Morm. 8:10–11]][[Mormon 8.11|]]).
- They will minister to the Gentiles, Jews, scattered tribes, and all nations, [[3 Nephi 28.27|3 Ne. 28:27–29]][[3 Nephi 28.28|]][[3 Nephi 28.29|]].
- Satan has no power over them, [[3 Nephi 28.39|3 Ne. 28:39]].
