# Esteem

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/esteem?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Honor|Honor]]; [[GS Reverence|Reverence]]

To regard someone or something as having worth and value, especially in a gospel setting.

- He was despised, and we esteemed him not, [[Isaiah 53.3|Isa. 53:3–4]][[Isaiah 53.4|]].

- That which is highly esteemed among men is abomination in the sight of God, [[Luke 16.15|Luke 16:15]].
- Let each esteem others better than themselves, [[Philippians 2.3|Philip. 2:3]].

- The Lord esteems all flesh in one, [[1 Nephi 17.35|1 Ne. 17:35]].
- Every man should esteem his neighbor as himself, [[Mosiah 27.4|Mosiah 27:4]] ([[D&C 38.24|D&C 38:24–25]][[D&C 38.25|]]).

- In the day of their peace they esteemed lightly my counsel, [[D&C 101.8|D&C 101:8]].
