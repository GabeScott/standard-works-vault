# Anointed One

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/anointed-one?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Messiah|Messiah]]

Jesus is called the *Christ* (a Greek word) or the *Messiah* (an Aramaic word). Both words mean “the anointed.” He is the one *anointed* of the Father to be the Father’s personal representative in all things pertaining to the salvation of mankind.

- The Lord has anointed me, [[Isaiah 61.1|Isa. 61:1–3]][[Isaiah 61.2|]][[Isaiah 61.3|]].

- He has anointed one to preach the gospel, [[Luke 4.16|Luke 4:16–22]][[Luke 4.17|]][[Luke 4.18|]][[Luke 4.19|]][[Luke 4.20|]][[Luke 4.21|]][[Luke 4.22|]].
- Jesus was anointed by God the Father, [[Acts 4.27|Acts 4:27]].
- God anointed Jesus of Nazareth, [[Acts 10.38|Acts 10:38]].
