# Money

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/money?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Alms, Almsgiving|Alms, Almsgiving]]; [[GS Riches|Riches]]; [[GS Tithes, Tithing|Tithes, Tithing]]; [[GS Worldliness|Worldliness]]

Coins, paper, certificates, or something people use as payment for goods or services. It is sometimes a symbol of materialism.

- Ye shall be redeemed without money, [[Isaiah 52.3|Isa. 52:3]].

- The Twelve were told to take nothing for their journey, no scrip, no bread, no money, [[Mark 6.8|Mark 6:8]].
- Peter told Simon the sorcerer that his money would perish with him, [[Acts 8.20|Acts 8:20]].
- The love of money is the root of all evil, [[1 Timothy 6.10|1 Tim. 6:10]].

- Do not spend money for that which is of no worth, [[2 Nephi 9.50|2 Ne. 9:50–51]][[2 Nephi 9.51|]] ([[Isaiah 55.1|Isa. 55:1–2]][[Isaiah 55.2|]]; [[2 Nephi 26.25|2 Ne. 26:25–27]][[2 Nephi 26.26|]][[2 Nephi 26.27|]]).
- If they labor for money, they shall perish, [[2 Nephi 26.31|2 Ne. 26:31]].
- Before you seek for riches, seek the kingdom of God, [[Jacob 2.18|Jacob 2:18–19]][[Jacob 2.19|]].
- Churches shall say that for your money you shall be forgiven of your sins, [[Mormon 8.32|Morm. 8:32, 37]][[Mormon 8.37|]].

- He who gives his money for the cause of Zion shall in nowise lose his reward, [[D&C 84.89|D&C 84:89–90]][[D&C 84.90|]].
