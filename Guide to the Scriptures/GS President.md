# President

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/president?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS First Presidency|First Presidency]]; [[GS Prophet|Prophet]]

A title for the presiding officer of an organization. The President of the Church is a prophet, seer, and revelator ([[D&C 21.1|D&C 21:1]]; [[D&C 107.91|107:91–92]][[D&C 107.92|]]), and members of the Church are to call the prophet of the Church by the title “President” ([[D&C 107.65|D&C 107:65]]). He is the only person on earth authorized to exercise all priesthood keys.

The heads of priesthood quorums and other Church organizations may also have the title of president.

- The Lord gave the keys of the kingdom to Joseph Smith, [[D&C 81.1|D&C 81:1–2]][[D&C 81.2|]].
- Three presidents form a quorum of the Presidency of the Church, [[D&C 107.21|D&C 107:21–24]][[D&C 107.22|]][[D&C 107.23|]][[D&C 107.24|]].
- Presidents were ordained after the order of Melchizedek, [[D&C 107.29|D&C 107:29]].
- The duties of the presidents over deacons, teachers, priests, and elders are described, [[D&C 107.85|D&C 107:85–89]][[D&C 107.86|]][[D&C 107.87|]][[D&C 107.88|]][[D&C 107.89|]] ([[D&C 124.136|D&C 124:136–138, 142]][[D&C 124.137|]][[D&C 124.138|]][[D&C 124.142|]]).
- There are seven presidents over all other Seventy, [[D&C 107.93|D&C 107:93–95]][[D&C 107.94|]][[D&C 107.95|]].
- Presidents of stakes were appointed, [[D&C 124.133|D&C 124:133–135]][[D&C 124.134|]][[D&C 124.135|]].
