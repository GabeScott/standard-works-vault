# Zion

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/zion?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Enoch|Enoch]]; [[GS New Jerusalem|New Jerusalem]]

The pure in heart ([[D&C 97.21|D&C 97:21]]). Zion also means a place where the pure in heart live. The city built by Enoch and his people that was eventually taken to heaven because of righteousness was named Zion ([[D&C 38.4|D&C 38:4]]; [[Moses 7.18|Moses 7:18–21, 69]][[Moses 7.19|]][[Moses 7.20|]][[Moses 7.21|]][[Moses 7.69|]]). In the latter days a city named Zion will be built near Jackson County, Missouri (United States of America), to which the tribes of Israel will gather ([[D&C 103.11|D&C 103:11–22]][[D&C 103.12|]][[D&C 103.13|]][[D&C 103.14|]][[D&C 103.15|]][[D&C 103.16|]][[D&C 103.17|]][[D&C 103.18|]][[D&C 103.19|]][[D&C 103.20|]][[D&C 103.21|]][[D&C 103.22|]]; [[D&C 133.18|133:18]]). The Saints are counseled to build up Zion wherever they are living in the world.

- Out of Zion shall go forth the law, [[Isaiah 2.2|Isa. 2:2–3]][[Isaiah 2.3|]] ([[Micah 4.2|Micah 4:2]]; [[2 Nephi 12.2|2 Ne. 12:2–3]][[2 Nephi 12.3|]]).

- I will take you one of a city, and two of a family, and I will bring you to Zion, [[Jeremiah 3.14|Jer. 3:14]].
- In mount Zion and in Jerusalem shall be deliverance, [[Joel 2.32|Joel 2:32]] ([[Obadiah 1.17|Obad. 1:17]]).

- Blessed are they who shall seek to bring forth my Zion, [[1 Nephi 13.37|1 Ne. 13:37]].
- The daughters of Zion are haughty, [[2 Nephi 13.16|2 Ne. 13:16]] ([[Isaiah 3.16|Isa. 3:16]]).
