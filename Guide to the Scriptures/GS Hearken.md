# Hearken

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/hearken?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Ear|Ear]]; [[GS Obedience, Obedient, Obey|Obedience, Obedient, Obey]]

To hear and obey the voice or teachings of the Lord.

- The Lord will raise up a prophet like Moses, and the people should hearken to him, [[Deuteronomy 18.15|Deut. 18:15]].
- To hearken is better than the fat of the rams of sacrifice, [[1 Samuel 15.20|1 Sam. 15:20–23]][[1 Samuel 15.21|]][[1 Samuel 15.22|]][[1 Samuel 15.23|]].
- We have not hearkened unto the Lord’s servants, the prophets, [[Daniel 9.6|Dan. 9:6]].

- The righteous who hearken unto the words of the prophets shall not perish, [[2 Nephi 26.8|2 Ne. 26:8]].
- If you will not hearken to the voice of the good shepherd, you are not his sheep, [[Alma 5.38|Alma 5:38]] ([[Helaman 7.18|Hel. 7:18]]).

- Hearken, O ye people of my church, [[D&C 1.1|D&C 1:1]].
- Those who hearken to the voice of the Spirit are enlightened and come to the Father, [[D&C 84.46|D&C 84:46–47]][[D&C 84.47|]].
- They were slow to hearken to the Lord; therefore, he is slow to hearken to their prayers, [[D&C 101.7|D&C 101:7–9]][[D&C 101.8|]][[D&C 101.9|]].
- Those who do not hearken to the commandments are chastened, [[D&C 103.4|D&C 103:4]] ([[Moses 4.4|Moses 4:4]]).
