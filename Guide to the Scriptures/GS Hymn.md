# Hymn

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/hymn?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Music|Music]]; [[GS Sing|Sing]]

A song of praise to God.

- A hymn was sung by the Twelve Apostles before the Lord retired to Gethsemane, [[Matthew 26.30|Matt. 26:30]].

- The Lord called Emma Smith to make a selection of sacred hymns, [[D&C 25.11|D&C 25:11]].
- The song of the righteous is a prayer unto me, and shall be answered with a blessing upon their heads, [[D&C 25.12|D&C 25:12]].
- Praise the Lord with singing and with music, [[D&C 136.28|D&C 136:28]].
