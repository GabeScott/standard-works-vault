# Service

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/service?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Love|Love]]; [[GS Welfare|Welfare]]

Care given or work done for the benefit of God and others. As we serve others, we also serve God.

- Choose you this day whom ye will serve, [[Joshua 24.15|Josh. 24:15]].

- Inasmuch as ye have done it unto the least of these, ye have done it unto me, [[Matthew 25.35|Matt. 25:35–45]][[Matthew 25.36|]][[Matthew 25.37|]][[Matthew 25.38|]][[Matthew 25.39|]][[Matthew 25.40|]][[Matthew 25.41|]][[Matthew 25.42|]][[Matthew 25.43|]][[Matthew 25.44|]][[Matthew 25.45|]].
- Present your bodies a living sacrifice, which is your reasonable service, [[Romans 12.1|Rom. 12:1]].
- By love serve one another, [[Galatians 5.13|Gal. 5:13]].

- Thy days shall be spent in the service of thy God, [[2 Nephi 2.3|2 Ne. 2:3]].
- When ye are in the service of your fellow beings ye are only in the service of your God, [[Mosiah 2.17|Mosiah 2:17]].
- Those who possess the land of promise must serve God or be swept off, [[Ether 2.8|Ether 2:8–12]][[Ether 2.9|]][[Ether 2.10|]][[Ether 2.11|]][[Ether 2.12|]].

- Those who embark in the service of God should serve with their whole hearts, [[D&C 4.2|D&C 4:2]].
- The Lord gave commandments that mankind should love and serve him, [[D&C 20.18|D&C 20:18–19]][[D&C 20.19|]].
- In the name of Jesus Christ thou shalt serve God, [[D&C 59.5|D&C 59:5]].
- I, the Lord, delight to honor those who serve me, [[D&C 76.5|D&C 76:5]].

- Worship God, for him only shalt thou serve, [[Moses 1.15|Moses 1:15]].
