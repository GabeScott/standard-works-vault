# Jared, Brother of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/jared-brother-of?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jared|Jared]]; [[GS Jaredites|Jaredites]]

A Book of Mormon prophet. He and his brother founded the Jaredite nation when they led a colony of people from the Tower of Babel to a promised land in the western hemisphere ([[Ether 1.1|Ether 1–6]][[Ether 1.2|]][[Ether 1.3|]][[Ether 1.4|]][[Ether 1.5|]][[Ether 1.6|]][[Ether 1.7|]][[Ether 1.8|]][[Ether 1.9|]][[Ether 1.10|]][[Ether 1.11|]][[Ether 1.12|]][[Ether 1.13|]][[Ether 1.14|]][[Ether 1.15|]][[Ether 1.16|]][[Ether 1.17|]][[Ether 1.18|]][[Ether 1.19|]][[Ether 1.20|]][[Ether 1.21|]][[Ether 1.22|]][[Ether 1.23|]][[Ether 1.24|]][[Ether 1.25|]][[Ether 1.26|]][[Ether 1.27|]][[Ether 1.28|]][[Ether 1.29|]][[Ether 1.30|]][[Ether 1.31|]][[Ether 1.32|]][[Ether 1.33|]][[Ether 1.34|]][[Ether 1.35|]][[Ether 1.36|]][[Ether 1.37|]][[Ether 1.38|]][[Ether 1.39|]][[Ether 1.40|]][[Ether 1.41|]][[Ether 1.42|]][[Ether 1.43|]]). He was a man of such great faith that he spoke with the Lord face to face ([[D&C 17.1|D&C 17:1]]). His story is recorded in the book of Ether.

- The brother of Jared was a large and mighty man, and highly favored of the Lord, [[Ether 1.34|Ether 1:34]].
- Because of his faith, the brother of Jared saw the finger of the Lord, [[Ether 3.6|Ether 3:6–9]][[Ether 3.7|]][[Ether 3.8|]][[Ether 3.9|]] ([[Ether 12.20|Ether 12:20]]).
- Christ showed his spirit body to the brother of Jared, [[Ether 3.13|Ether 3:13–20]][[Ether 3.14|]][[Ether 3.15|]][[Ether 3.16|]][[Ether 3.17|]][[Ether 3.18|]][[Ether 3.19|]][[Ether 3.20|]].
- There never were greater things made manifest than those which were made manifest to the brother of Jared, [[Ether 4.4|Ether 4:4]].
- The brother of Jared warned his people against a monarchy, [[Ether 6.22|Ether 6:22–23]][[Ether 6.23|]].
- The Lord showed all things to the brother of Jared, [[Ether 12.21|Ether 12:21]].
- The brother of Jared was mighty in writing, [[Ether 12.24|Ether 12:24]].
- Through faith the brother of Jared moved mount Zerin, [[Ether 12.30|Ether 12:30]].
