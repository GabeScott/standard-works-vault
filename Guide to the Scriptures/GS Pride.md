# Pride

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/pride?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Humble, Humility|Humble, Humility]]; [[GS Money|Money]]; [[GS Riches|Riches]]; [[GS Vain, Vanity|Vain, Vanity]]; [[GS Worldliness|Worldliness]]

A lack or absence of humility or teachableness. Pride sets people in opposition to each other and to God. A proud person sets himself above those around him and follows his own will rather than God’s will. Conceit, envy, hardheartedness, and haughtiness are also typical of a proud person.

- Beware that thou forget not the Lord lest thine heart be lifted up, [[Deuteronomy 8.11|Deut. 8:11–14]][[Deuteronomy 8.12|]][[Deuteronomy 8.13|]][[Deuteronomy 8.14|]].
- Pride and arrogancy do I hate, [[Proverbs 8.13|Prov. 8:13]] ([[Proverbs 6.16|Prov. 6:16–17]][[Proverbs 6.17|]]).
- Pride goeth before destruction, [[Proverbs 16.18|Prov. 16:18]].
- The day of the Lord shall be upon the proud, [[Isaiah 2.11|Isa. 2:11–12]][[Isaiah 2.12|]] ([[2 Nephi 12.11|2 Ne. 12:11–12]][[2 Nephi 12.12|]]).
- The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee, [[Obadiah 1.3|Obad. 1:3]].
- All the proud shall be stubble, [[Malachi 4.1|Mal. 4:1]] ([[1 Nephi 22.15|1 Ne. 22:15]]; [[3 Nephi 25.1|3 Ne. 25:1]]; [[D&C 29.9|D&C 29:9]]).

- Whosoever shall exalt himself shall be abased, [[Matthew 23.12|Matt. 23:12]] ([[D&C 101.42|D&C 101:42]]).
