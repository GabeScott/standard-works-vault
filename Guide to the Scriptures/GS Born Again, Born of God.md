# Born Again, Born of God

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/born-again-born-of-god?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Baptism, Baptize|Baptism, Baptize]]; [[GS Begotten|Begotten]]; [[GS Children of Christ|Children of Christ]]; [[GS Conversion, Convert|Conversion, Convert]]; [[GS Natural Man|Natural Man]]; [[GS Sons and Daughters of God|Sons and Daughters of God]]

To have the Spirit of the Lord cause a mighty change in a person’s heart so that he has no more desire to do evil, but rather desires to seek the things of God.

- I will put a new spirit within you, [[Ezekiel 11.19|Ezek. 11:19]] ([[Ezekiel 18.31|Ezek. 18:31]]; [[Ezekiel 36.26|36:26]]).

- Those who believed on Christ’s name were born, not of blood, but of God, [[John 1.12|John 1:12–13]][[John 1.13|]].
- Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God, [[John 3.3|John 3:3–7]][[John 3.4|]][[John 3.5|]][[John 3.6|]][[John 3.7|]].
- We can be born again by the word of God, [[1 Peter 1.3|1 Pet. 1:3–23]][[1 Peter 1.4|]][[1 Peter 1.5|]][[1 Peter 1.6|]][[1 Peter 1.7|]][[1 Peter 1.8|]][[1 Peter 1.9|]][[1 Peter 1.10|]][[1 Peter 1.11|]][[1 Peter 1.12|]][[1 Peter 1.13|]][[1 Peter 1.14|]][[1 Peter 1.15|]][[1 Peter 1.16|]][[1 Peter 1.17|]][[1 Peter 1.18|]][[1 Peter 1.19|]][[1 Peter 1.20|]][[1 Peter 1.21|]][[1 Peter 1.22|]][[1 Peter 1.23|]].
- Whosoever is born of God does not continue in sin, [[JST 1 John 3.9|JST, 1 Jn. 3:9]].
- For whatsoever is born of God overcomes the world, [[1 John 5.4|1 Jn. 5:4]].

- Those who are born of Christ covenant with God, [[Mosiah 3.19|Mosiah 3:19]]; [[Mosiah 5.2|5:2–7]][[Mosiah 5.3|]][[Mosiah 5.4|]][[Mosiah 5.5|]][[Mosiah 5.6|]][[Mosiah 5.7|]].
- All people must be born again; yea, born of God, [[Mosiah 27.25|Mosiah 27:25–26]][[Mosiah 27.26|]] ([[Alma 5.49|Alma 5:49]]).
- Have ye spiritually been born of God, [[Alma 5.12|Alma 5:12–19]][[Alma 5.13|]][[Alma 5.14|]][[Alma 5.15|]][[Alma 5.16|]][[Alma 5.17|]][[Alma 5.18|]][[Alma 5.19|]].
- If ye are not born again, ye cannot inherit the kingdom of heaven, [[Alma 7.14|Alma 7:14]].

- Whosoever believes on my words shall be born of me, even of water and of the Spirit, [[D&C 5.16|D&C 5:16]].

- Ye must be born again into the kingdom of heaven, [[Moses 6.59|Moses 6:59]].
