# Meek, Meekness

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/meek-meekness?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Broken Heart|Broken Heart]]; [[GS Humble, Humility|Humble, Humility]]; [[GS Patience|Patience]]

Godfearing, righteous, humble, teachable, and patient under suffering. The meek are willing to follow gospel teachings.

- Moses was very meek, [[Numbers 12.3|Num. 12:3]].
- The meek shall inherit the earth, [[Psalms 37.11|Ps. 37:11]] ([[Matthew 5.5|Matt. 5:5]]; [[3 Nephi 12.5|3 Ne. 12:5]]; [[D&C 88.17|D&C 88:17]]).
- Seek ye the Lord, all ye meek; seek righteousness, seek meekness, [[Zephaniah 2.3|Zeph. 2:3]] ([[1 Timothy 6.11|1 Tim. 6:11]]).

- Learn of me for I am meek and lowly in heart, [[Matthew 11.29|Matt. 11:29]].
- Meekness is a fruit of the Spirit, [[Galatians 5.22|Gal. 5:22–23]][[Galatians 5.23|]].
- The servant of the Lord must be gentle, apt to teach, patient, in meekness instructing those that oppose, [[2 Timothy 2.24|2 Tim. 2:24–25]][[2 Timothy 2.25|]].
- A meek and quiet spirit is of great price in the sight of God, [[1 Peter 3.4|1 Pet. 3:4]].

- Put off the natural man and become meek, [[Mosiah 3.19|Mosiah 3:19]] ([[Alma 13.27|Alma 13:27–28]][[Alma 13.28|]]).
- God commanded Helaman to teach the people to be meek, [[Alma 37.33|Alma 37:33]].
- The Lord’s grace is sufficient for the meek, [[Ether 12.26|Ether 12:26]].
- You have faith in Christ because of your meekness, [[Moroni 7.39|Moro. 7:39]].
- None is acceptable before God except the meek and lowly of heart, [[Moroni 7.44|Moro. 7:44]].
- The remission of sins bringeth meekness, and because of meekness cometh the visitation of the Holy Ghost, [[Moroni 8.26|Moro. 8:26]].

- Walk in the meekness of my Spirit, [[D&C 19.23|D&C 19:23]].
- Govern your house in meekness, [[D&C 31.9|D&C 31:9]].
- Power and influence of the priesthood can be maintained by gentleness and meekness, [[D&C 121.41|D&C 121:41]].
