# Oath and Covenant of the Priesthood

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/oath-and-covenant-of-the-priesthood?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Covenant|Covenant]]; [[GS Oath|Oath]]; [[GS Priesthood|Priesthood]]

An oath is a sworn affirmation to be true and faithful to one’s promises. A covenant is a solemn promise between two parties. The Aaronic Priesthood is received by covenant alone. Melchizedek Priesthood holders receive the priesthood by unspoken oath as well as by covenant. As priesthood holders are faithful and magnify their callings as God directs, He blesses them. Those who are faithful to the end and do all He asks of them will receive all that the Father has ([[D&C 84.33|D&C 84:33–39]][[D&C 84.34|]][[D&C 84.35|]][[D&C 84.36|]][[D&C 84.37|]][[D&C 84.38|]][[D&C 84.39|]]).

- The Lord covenanted with Abraham, and Abraham obeyed, [[Genesis 15.18|Gen. 15:18]]; [[Genesis 17.1|17:1]]; [[Genesis 22.16|22:16–18]][[Genesis 22.17|]][[Genesis 22.18|]].
- Priests during Ezekiel’s time fed not the flock, [[Ezekiel 34.2|Ezek. 34:2–3]][[Ezekiel 34.3|]].
- Priests during Malachi’s time corrupted the covenant, [[Malachi 1.1|Mal. 1–2]][[Malachi 1.2|]][[Malachi 1.3|]][[Malachi 1.4|]][[Malachi 1.5|]][[Malachi 1.6|]][[Malachi 1.7|]][[Malachi 1.8|]][[Malachi 1.9|]][[Malachi 1.10|]][[Malachi 1.11|]][[Malachi 1.12|]][[Malachi 1.13|]][[Malachi 1.14|]].
