# Gold Plates

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/gold-plates?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Book of Mormon|Book of Mormon]]; [[GS Plates|Plates]]

A record written on plates of gold. It tells the story of two great civilizations on the American continents. Joseph Smith translated and published a portion of these plates. This translation is called the Book of Mormon. (For more information, see “[Introduction](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/introduction?lang=eng)” and “[The Testimony of the Prophet Joseph Smith](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/js?lang=eng)” in the Book of Mormon.)
