# Advocate

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/advocate?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

Jesus Christ is our Advocate with the Father ([[Moroni 7.28|Moro. 7:28]]) and pleads our cause with Him.

- Jesus Christ is our advocate with the Father, [[1 John 2.1|1 Jn. 2:1]] ([[D&C 110.4|D&C 110:4]]).

- Jesus shall make intercession for all, [[2 Nephi 2.9|2 Ne. 2:9]] ([[Hebrews 7.25|Heb. 7:25]]).
- Jesus gained victory over death, giving him power to make intercession for the children of men, [[Mosiah 15.8|Mosiah 15:8]].

- I am your advocate with the Father, [[D&C 29.5|D&C 29:5]].
- Jesus Christ is pleading your cause, [[D&C 45.3|D&C 45:3–5]][[D&C 45.4|]][[D&C 45.5|]].
