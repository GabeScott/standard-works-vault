# Witness

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/witness?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Testimony|Testimony]]

A statement or other evidence that something is true; a testimony. A witness can also be someone who gives such a statement or evidence based on personal knowledge; that is, someone who bears testimony.

- Thou shalt not bear false witness, [[Exodus 20.16|Ex. 20:16]].

- The gospel will be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations, [[Matthew 24.14|Matt. 24:14]] ([[Joseph Smith Matthew 1.31|JS—M 1:31]]).
- Ye shall be witnesses unto me, [[Acts 1.8|Acts 1:8]].
- The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, [[Romans 8.16|Rom. 8:16]] ([[1 John 5.6|1 Jn. 5:6]]).

- Ye are willing to stand as witnesses of God at all times, [[Mosiah 18.8|Mosiah 18:8–9]][[Mosiah 18.9|]].
- We partake of the sacrament to witness unto the Father that we will keep the commandments and always remember Jesus, [[3 Nephi 18.10|3 Ne. 18:10–11]][[3 Nephi 18.11|]] ([[Moroni 4.1|Moro. 4–5]][[Moroni 4.2|]][[Moroni 4.3|]]; [[D&C 20.77|D&C 20:77–79]][[D&C 20.78|]][[D&C 20.79|]]).
- Ye receive no witness until after the trial of your faith, [[Ether 12.6|Ether 12:6]].

- Law of witnesses: in the mouth of two or three witnesses shall every word be established, [[D&C 6.28|D&C 6:28]] ([[Deuteronomy 17.6|Deut. 17:6]]; [[Matthew 18.16|Matt. 18:16]]; [[2 Corinthians 13.1|2 Cor. 13:1]]; [[Ether 5.4|Ether 5:4]]; [[D&C 128.3|D&C 128:3]]).
- I have ordained you to be Apostles and especial witnesses of my name, [[D&C 27.12|D&C 27:12]] ([[D&C 107.23|D&C 107:23]]).
- The Seventy are called to be especial witnesses unto the Gentiles and in all the world, [[D&C 107.25|D&C 107:25]].
- Let there be a recorder, and let him be eye-witness of your baptisms, [[D&C 127.6|D&C 127:6]] ([[D&C 128.2|D&C 128:2–4]][[D&C 128.3|]][[D&C 128.4|]]).
