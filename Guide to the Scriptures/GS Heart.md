# Heart

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/heart?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Born Again, Born of God|Born Again, Born of God]]; [[GS Broken Heart|Broken Heart]]

A symbol of the mind and will of man and the figurative source of all emotions and feelings.

- Love the Lord thy God with all thine heart, [[Deuteronomy 6.5|Deut. 6:5]] ([[Deuteronomy 6.3|Deut. 6:3–7]][[Deuteronomy 6.4|]][[Deuteronomy 6.5|]][[Deuteronomy 6.6|]][[Deuteronomy 6.7|]]; [[Matthew 22.37|Matt. 22:37]]; [[Luke 10.27|Luke 10:27]]; [[D&C 59.5|D&C 59:5]]).
- The Lord sought a man after his own heart, [[1 Samuel 13.14|1 Sam. 13:14]].
- Man looketh on the outward appearance, but the Lord looketh on the heart, [[1 Samuel 16.7|1 Sam. 16:7]].
- He that has clean hands and a pure heart will ascend to the hill of the Lord and be blessed, [[Psalms 24.3|Ps. 24:3–5]][[Psalms 24.4|]][[Psalms 24.5|]] ([[2 Nephi 25.16|2 Ne. 25:16]]).
- As a man thinketh in his heart, so is he, [[Proverbs 23.7|Prov. 23:7]].
- Elijah will turn the hearts of the fathers to the children, and the hearts of the children to their fathers, [[Malachi 4.5|Mal. 4:5–6]][[Malachi 4.6|]] ([[Luke 1.17|Luke 1:17]]; [[D&C 2.2|D&C 2:2]]; [[D&C 110.14|110:14–15]][[D&C 110.15|]]; [[D&C 138.47|138:47]]; [[Joseph Smith History 1.38|JS—H 1:38–39]][[Joseph Smith History 1.39|]]).

- Blessed are the pure in heart, [[Matthew 5.8|Matt. 5:8]] ([[3 Nephi 12.8|3 Ne. 12:8]]).
- A man speaks from the good or evil in his heart, [[Luke 6.45|Luke 6:45]].

- Follow the Son with full purpose of heart, [[2 Nephi 31.13|2 Ne. 31:13]].
- Have you been spiritually born of God and experienced a mighty change in your heart, [[Alma 5.14|Alma 5:14]].
- Offer for a sacrifice unto the Lord a broken heart and a contrite spirit, [[3 Nephi 9.20|3 Ne. 9:20]] ([[3 Nephi 12.19|3 Ne. 12:19]]; [[Ether 4.15|Ether 4:15]]; [[Moroni 6.2|Moro. 6:2]]).

- I will tell you in your mind and in your heart by the Holy Ghost, [[D&C 8.2|D&C 8:2]].
