# Shepherd

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/shepherd?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Good Shepherd|Good Shepherd]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

Symbolically, a person who cares for the Lord’s children.

- The Lord is my shepherd, [[Psalms 23.1|Ps. 23:1]].
- Shepherds should feed the flock, [[Ezekiel 34.2|Ezek. 34:2–3]][[Ezekiel 34.3|]].
