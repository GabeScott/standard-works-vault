# Circumcision

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/circumcision?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Abrahamic Covenant|Abrahamic Covenant]]

The token of the Abrahamic covenant for male Israelites during Old Testament dispensations ([[Genesis 17.10|Gen. 17:10–11, 23–27]][[Genesis 17.11|]][[Genesis 17.23|]][[Genesis 17.24|]][[Genesis 17.25|]][[Genesis 17.26|]][[Genesis 17.27|]]; [[JST Genesis 17.11|JST, Gen. 17:11 (Bible Appendix)]]). Circumcision was performed by cutting off the “flesh of the foreskin” of male infants and adults alike. Those who received it enjoyed the privileges and accepted the responsibilities of the covenant. Circumcision as a token of the covenant was done away with by Christ’s mission ([[Moroni 8.8|Moro. 8:8]]; [[D&C 74.3|D&C 74:3–7]][[D&C 74.4|]][[D&C 74.5|]][[D&C 74.6|]][[D&C 74.7|]]).
