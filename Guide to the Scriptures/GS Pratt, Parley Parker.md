# Pratt, Parley Parker

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/pratt-parley-parker?lang=eng)

Older brother of Orson Pratt and one of the first Twelve Apostles called after the Restoration of the Church in modern times ([[D&C 124.128|D&C 124:128–129]][[D&C 124.129|]]). Parley Pratt was called on the first of several missionary efforts when the Lord gave a revelation to him through Joseph Smith in October 1830 ([[D&C 32.1|D&C 32]][[D&C 32.2|]][[D&C 32.3|]][[D&C 32.4|]][[D&C 32.5|]]; [[D&C 50.37|50:37]]).
