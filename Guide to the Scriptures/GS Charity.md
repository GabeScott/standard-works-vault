# Charity

The pure love of Christ ([[Moroni 7.47|Moro. 7:47]]); the love that Christ has for the children of men and that the children of men should have for one another ([[2 Nephi 26.30|2 Ne. 26:30]]; [[2 Nephi 33.7|33:7–9]][[2 Nephi 33.8|]][[2 Nephi 33.9|]]; [[Ether 12.33|Ether 12:33–34]][[Ether 12.34|]]); the highest, noblest, strongest kind of love, not merely affection.

- Knowledge puffs up, but charity edifies, [[1 Corinthians 8.1|1 Cor. 8:1]].
- Charity, a pure love, excels and exceeds almost all else, [[1 Corinthians 13.1|1 Cor. 13]][[1 Corinthians 13.2|]][[1 Corinthians 13.3|]][[1 Corinthians 13.4|]][[1 Corinthians 13.5|]][[1 Corinthians 13.6|]][[1 Corinthians 13.7|]][[1 Corinthians 13.8|]][[1 Corinthians 13.9|]][[1 Corinthians 13.10|]][[1 Corinthians 13.11|]][[1 Corinthians 13.12|]][[1 Corinthians 13.13|]].
- The end of the commandment is charity out of a pure heart, [[1 Timothy 1.5|1 Tim. 1:5]].
- Add to brotherly kindness charity, [[2 Peter 1.7|2 Pet. 1:7]].

- The Lord has commanded that all men should have charity, [[2 Nephi 26.30|2 Ne. 26:30]] ([[Moroni 7.44|Moro. 7:44–47]][[Moroni 7.45|]][[Moroni 7.46|]][[Moroni 7.47|]]).
- See that ye have faith, hope, and charity, [[Alma 7.24|Alma 7:24]].
- The love that the Lord has for men is charity, [[Ether 12.33|Ether 12:33–34]][[Ether 12.34|]].
- Without charity men cannot inherit that place prepared in the Father’s mansions, [[Ether 12.34|Ether 12:34]] ([[Moroni 10.20|Moro. 10:20–21]][[Moroni 10.21|]]).
- Moroni wrote Mormon’s words on faith, hope, and charity, [[Moroni 7.1|Moro. 7]][[Moroni 7.2|]][[Moroni 7.3|]][[Moroni 7.4|]][[Moroni 7.5|]][[Moroni 7.6|]][[Moroni 7.7|]][[Moroni 7.8|]][[Moroni 7.9|]][[Moroni 7.10|]][[Moroni 7.11|]][[Moroni 7.12|]][[Moroni 7.13|]][[Moroni 7.14|]][[Moroni 7.15|]][[Moroni 7.16|]][[Moroni 7.17|]][[Moroni 7.18|]][[Moroni 7.19|]][[Moroni 7.20|]][[Moroni 7.21|]][[Moroni 7.22|]][[Moroni 7.23|]][[Moroni 7.24|]][[Moroni 7.25|]][[Moroni 7.26|]][[Moroni 7.27|]][[Moroni 7.28|]][[Moroni 7.29|]][[Moroni 7.30|]][[Moroni 7.31|]][[Moroni 7.32|]][[Moroni 7.33|]][[Moroni 7.34|]][[Moroni 7.35|]][[Moroni 7.36|]][[Moroni 7.37|]][[Moroni 7.38|]][[Moroni 7.39|]][[Moroni 7.40|]][[Moroni 7.41|]][[Moroni 7.42|]][[Moroni 7.43|]][[Moroni 7.44|]][[Moroni 7.45|]][[Moroni 7.46|]][[Moroni 7.47|]][[Moroni 7.48|]].

- Charity qualifies men for the Lord’s work, [[D&C 4.5|D&C 4:5–6]][[D&C 4.6|]] ([[D&C 12.8|D&C 12:8]]).
