# Merciful, Mercy

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/merciful-mercy?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Atone, Atonement|Atone, Atonement]]; [[GS Forgive|Forgive]]; [[GS Grace|Grace]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Justice|Justice]]

The spirit of compassion, tenderness, and forgiveness. Mercy is one of the attributes of God. Jesus Christ offers mercy to us through His atoning sacrifice.

- The Lord is merciful and gracious, [[Exodus 34.6|Ex. 34:6]] ([[Deuteronomy 4.31|Deut. 4:31]]).
- His mercy endureth for ever, [[1 Chronicles 16.34|1 Chr. 16:34]].
- Goodness and mercy shall follow me, [[Psalms 23.6|Ps. 23:6]].
- He that has mercy on the poor is happy, [[Proverbs 14.21|Prov. 14:21]].
- I desired mercy and not sacrifice, [[Hosea 6.6|Hosea 6:6]].

- Blessed are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy, [[Matthew 5.7|Matt. 5:7]] ([[3 Nephi 12.7|3 Ne. 12:7]]).
- Woe unto hypocrites who pay tithes and omit the weightier matters of the law, judgment, mercy, and faith, [[Matthew 23.23|Matt. 23:23]].
- Be ye therefore merciful, as your Father also is merciful, [[Luke 6.36|Luke 6:36]].
- Not by our works of righteousness, but according to his mercy he saved us, [[Titus 3.5|Titus 3:5]].

- The tender mercies of the Lord are over all, [[1 Nephi 1.20|1 Ne. 1:20]].
- Mercy hath no claim on the unrepentant, [[Mosiah 2.38|Mosiah 2:38–39]][[Mosiah 2.39|]].
- God is merciful unto all who believe on his name, [[Alma 32.22|Alma 32:22]].
- Mercy can satisfy the demands of justice, [[Alma 34.16|Alma 34:16]].
- Do you suppose that mercy can rob justice, [[Alma 42.25|Alma 42:25]] ([[Alma 42.13|Alma 42:13–25]][[Alma 42.14|]][[Alma 42.15|]][[Alma 42.16|]][[Alma 42.17|]][[Alma 42.18|]][[Alma 42.19|]][[Alma 42.20|]][[Alma 42.21|]][[Alma 42.22|]][[Alma 42.23|]][[Alma 42.24|]][[Alma 42.25|]]).
- Little children are alive in Christ because of his mercy, [[Moroni 8.19|Moro. 8:19–20]][[Moroni 8.20|]] ([[D&C 29.46|D&C 29:46]]).

- Jesus Christ’s arm of mercy hath atoned for your sins, [[D&C 29.1|D&C 29:1]].
- By virtue of the blood I spilt, have I pleaded before the Father for those who believe in my name, [[D&C 38.4|D&C 38:4]].
- They who have kept the covenant shall obtain mercy, [[D&C 54.6|D&C 54:6]].
- I, the Lord, forgive sins, and am merciful unto those who confess their sins with humble hearts, [[D&C 61.2|D&C 61:2]].
- I, the Lord, show mercy unto all the meek, [[D&C 97.2|D&C 97:2]].
- And who receiveth you as a little child, receiveth my kingdom, for they shall obtain mercy, [[D&C 99.3|D&C 99:3]].

- Mercy shall go before thy face, [[Moses 7.31|Moses 7:31]].
