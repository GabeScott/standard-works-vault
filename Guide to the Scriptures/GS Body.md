# Body

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/body?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Death, Physical|Death, Physical]]; [[GS Mortal, Mortality|Mortal, Mortality]]; [[GS Resurrection|Resurrection]]; [[GS Soul|Soul]]

The mortal, physical structure of flesh and bones created in God’s image that is combined with a spirit to make up a living person. The physical bodies of all men and women will be reunited eternally with their spirits in the Resurrection. The scriptures sometimes refer to a body and spirit joined together as a soul ([[Genesis 2.7|Gen. 2:7]]; [[D&C 88.15|D&C 88:15]]; [[Moses 3.7|Moses 3:7, 9, 19]][[Moses 3.9|]][[Moses 3.19|]]; [[Abraham 5.7|Abr. 5:7]]).

- The Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, [[Genesis 2.7|Gen. 2:7]] ([[Moses 3.7|Moses 3:7]]).

- Handle me and see, for a spirit has not flesh and bones, [[Luke 24.39|Luke 24:39]].
- I discipline my body and bring it into subjection, [[1 Corinthians 9.27|1 Cor. 9:27]].
- There is a natural body, and there is a spiritual body, [[1 Corinthians 15.44|1 Cor. 15:44]].
- The body without the spirit is dead, [[James 2.26|James 2:26]].

- The mortal body will be raised as an immortal body, [[Alma 11.43|Alma 11:43–45]][[Alma 11.44|]][[Alma 11.45|]].
- Every part of the body shall be restored, [[Alma 41.2|Alma 41:2]].
- Jesus showed his resurrected body to the Nephites, [[3 Nephi 10.18|3 Ne. 10:18–19]][[3 Nephi 10.19|]]; [[3 Nephi 11.13|11:13–15]][[3 Nephi 11.14|]][[3 Nephi 11.15|]].

- The Father has a body of flesh and bones as tangible as man’s; the Son also, [[D&C 130.22|D&C 130:22]].

- God created male and female in the image of his own body, [[Moses 6.9|Moses 6:9]] ([[Genesis 9.6|Gen. 9:6]]).
