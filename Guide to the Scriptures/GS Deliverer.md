# Deliverer

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/deliverer?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]

Jesus Christ is the Deliverer of mankind because He rescues all from the bondage of death and the repentant from the penalties of sin.

- The Lord is my rock and my deliverer, [[2 Samuel 22.2|2 Sam. 22:2]] ([[Psalms 18.2|Ps. 18:2]]; [[Psalms 144.2|144:2]]).
- Thou art my help and my deliverer, [[Psalms 40.17|Ps. 40:17]] ([[Psalms 70.5|Ps. 70:5]]).

- The Deliverer shall come out of Zion, [[Romans 11.26|Rom. 11:26]].

- The Saints acknowledged the Son of God as their Redeemer and Deliverer, [[D&C 138.23|D&C 138:23]].
