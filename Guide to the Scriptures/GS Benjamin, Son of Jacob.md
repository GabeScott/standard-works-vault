In the Old Testament, the second son of Jacob and Rachel ([[Genesis 35.16|Gen. 35:16–20]][[Genesis 35.17|]][[Genesis 35.18|]][[Genesis 35.19|]][[Genesis 35.20|]]).

## The tribe of Benjamin

Jacob blessed Benjamin ([[Genesis 49.27|Gen. 49:27]]). The descendants of Benjamin were a warlike race. Two important Benjamites were Saul, the first Israelite king ([[1 Samuel 9.1|1 Sam. 9:1–2]][[1 Samuel 9.2|]]), and Paul, the New Testament Apostle ([[Romans 11.1|Rom. 11:1]]).
