# Glory

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/glory?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Degrees of Glory|Degrees of Glory]]; [[GS Light, Light of Christ|Light, Light of Christ]]; [[GS Truth|Truth]]

In the scriptures, glory often refers to God’s light and truth. It may also refer to praise or honor and to a certain condition of eternal life or to the glory of God.

- Holy is the Lord of hosts: the whole earth is full of his glory, [[Isaiah 6.3|Isa. 6:3]] ([[2 Nephi 16.3|2 Ne. 16:3]]).

- We are changed into the same image from glory to glory, [[2 Corinthians 3.18|2 Cor. 3:18]].

- He will raise me up to dwell with him in glory, [[Alma 36.28|Alma 36:28]].
