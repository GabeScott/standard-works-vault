# Deceit, Deceive, Deception

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/deceit-deceive-deception?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Guile|Guile]]; [[GS Lying|Lying]]

In the scriptures, to deceive is to cause someone to believe something that is not true.

- He who has not sworn deceitfully shall ascend into the hill of the Lord, [[Psalms 24.3|Ps. 24:3–4]][[Psalms 24.4|]].
- Deliver me from the deceitful, [[Psalms 43.1|Ps. 43:1]].
- Woe unto them that call evil good, and good evil, [[Isaiah 5.20|Isa. 5:20]] ([[2 Nephi 15.20|2 Ne. 15:20]]).

- Let no man deceive himself, [[1 Corinthians 3.18|1 Cor. 3:18]].
- Let no man deceive you with vain words, [[Ephesians 5.6|Eph. 5:6]].
- Evil men shall deceive and be deceived, [[2 Timothy 3.13|2 Tim. 3:13]].
- Satan, who deceived the whole world, was cast out, [[Revelation 12.9|Rev. 12:9]].
- Satan was bound that he should deceive the nations no more, [[Revelation 20.1|Rev. 20:1–3]][[Revelation 20.2|]][[Revelation 20.3|]].

- The Lord cannot be deceived, [[2 Nephi 9.41|2 Ne. 9:41]].
- If ye follow the Son, acting no deception before God, ye shall receive the Holy Ghost, [[2 Nephi 31.13|2 Ne. 31:13]].
- Sherem confessed that he was deceived by the power of the devil, [[Jacob 7.18|Jacob 7:18]].
- The people of King Noah were deceived by flattering words, [[Mosiah 11.7|Mosiah 11:7]].

- The wise have taken the Holy Spirit for their guide, and have not been deceived, [[D&C 45.57|D&C 45:57]].
- Wo unto them that are deceivers, [[D&C 50.6|D&C 50:6]].

- He became Satan, the father of all lies, to deceive and to blind men, [[Moses 4.4|Moses 4:4]].
