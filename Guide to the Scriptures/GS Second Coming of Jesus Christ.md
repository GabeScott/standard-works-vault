# Second Coming of Jesus Christ

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/second-coming-of-jesus-christ?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Armageddon|Armageddon]]; [[GS Gog|Gog]]; [[GS Jesus Christ|Jesus Christ]]; [[GS Magog|Magog]]; [[GS Signs of the Times|Signs of the Times]]

At the beginning of the Millennial Era, Christ will return to the earth. This event will mark the end of the mortal probation of this earth. The wicked will be removed from the earth and the righteous will be caught up in a cloud while the earth is cleansed.
