# Annas

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/annas?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Caiaphas|Caiaphas]]

In the New Testament, a man of great influence in the Sanhedrin. Jesus, when arrested, was first brought to him ([[John 18.13|John 18:13]]); he also took a leading part in the trial of the Apostles ([[Acts 4.3|Acts 4:3–6]][[Acts 4.4|]][[Acts 4.5|]][[Acts 4.6|]]).
