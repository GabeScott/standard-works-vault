# Manifesto

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/manifesto?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Marriage, Marry|Marriage, Marry—Plural marriage]]; [[GS Woodruff, Wilford|Woodruff, Wilford]]

An official declaration by President Wilford Woodruff in 1890 clearly stating that the Church and its members submitted to the law of the land and no longer entered into plural marriage ([[Official Declaration 1.1|OD 1]]). President Woodruff issued the Manifesto following a vision and revelation from God.
