# Brethren, Brother

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/brethren-brother?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Man, Men|Man, Men]]; [[GS Sister|Sister]]; [[GS Woman, Women|Woman, Women]]

As children of our Heavenly Father, all men and women are spiritually brothers and sisters. In the Church, male members and friends of the Church are often addressed as brothers.

- When thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren, [[Luke 22.32|Luke 22:32]].
- He who loves not his brother abides in death, [[1 John 3.10|1 Jn. 3:10–17]][[1 John 3.11|]][[1 John 3.12|]][[1 John 3.13|]][[1 John 3.14|]][[1 John 3.15|]][[1 John 3.16|]][[1 John 3.17|]].

- Think of your brethren like unto yourselves, [[Jacob 2.17|Jacob 2:17]].

- Let every man esteem his brother as himself, [[D&C 38.24|D&C 38:24–25]][[D&C 38.25|]].
- Strengthen your brethren in all your conversations, [[D&C 108.7|D&C 108:7]].
