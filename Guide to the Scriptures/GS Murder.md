# Murder

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/murder?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Cain|Cain]]; [[GS Capital Punishment|Capital Punishment]]

The deliberate and unjustified taking of human life. Murder is a sin condemned from earliest times ([[Genesis 4.1|Gen. 4:1–12]][[Genesis 4.2|]][[Genesis 4.3|]][[Genesis 4.4|]][[Genesis 4.5|]][[Genesis 4.6|]][[Genesis 4.7|]][[Genesis 4.8|]][[Genesis 4.9|]][[Genesis 4.10|]][[Genesis 4.11|]][[Genesis 4.12|]]; [[Moses 5.18|Moses 5:18–41]][[Moses 5.19|]][[Moses 5.20|]][[Moses 5.21|]][[Moses 5.22|]][[Moses 5.23|]][[Moses 5.24|]][[Moses 5.25|]][[Moses 5.26|]][[Moses 5.27|]][[Moses 5.28|]][[Moses 5.29|]][[Moses 5.30|]][[Moses 5.31|]][[Moses 5.32|]][[Moses 5.33|]][[Moses 5.34|]][[Moses 5.35|]][[Moses 5.36|]][[Moses 5.37|]][[Moses 5.38|]][[Moses 5.39|]][[Moses 5.40|]][[Moses 5.41|]]).

- Whoever sheds a man’s blood shall have his blood shed by man, [[Genesis 9.6|Gen. 9:6]] ([[JST Genesis 9.12|JST, Gen. 9:12–13]][[JST Genesis 9.13|]]; [[Exodus 21.12|Ex. 21:12]]; [[Alma 34.12|Alma 34:12]]).
- Thou shalt not kill, [[Exodus 20.13|Ex. 20:13]] ([[Deuteronomy 5.17|Deut. 5:17]]; [[Matthew 5.21|Matt. 5:21–22]][[Matthew 5.22|]]; [[Mosiah 13.21|Mosiah 13:21]]; [[D&C 59.6|D&C 59:6]]).

- Jesus said, thou shalt do no murder, [[Matthew 19.18|Matt. 19:18]].
- Murderers shall have their part in the second death, [[Revelation 21.8|Rev. 21:8]].

- Ye are murderers in your hearts, [[1 Nephi 17.44|1 Ne. 17:44]].
- Wo to the murderer who kills deliberately, [[2 Nephi 9.35|2 Ne. 9:35]].
- God has commanded that men should not murder, [[2 Nephi 26.32|2 Ne. 26:32]].
- Murder is an abomination to the Lord, [[Alma 39.5|Alma 39:5–6]][[Alma 39.6|]].

- He that kills shall not have forgiveness, [[D&C 42.18|D&C 42:18]].
- Any person who kills shall be delivered up to the laws of the land, [[D&C 42.79|D&C 42:79]].
