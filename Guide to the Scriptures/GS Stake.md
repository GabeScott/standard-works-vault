# Stake

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/stake?lang=eng)

One of the organizational and administrative units of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints. A stake is composed of a number of wards or branches. It generally has a set of geographical boundaries and conforms to the tent image described in [[Isaiah 54.2|Isaiah 54:2]]: “Lengthen thy cords, and strengthen thy stakes.” Each stake of Zion supports and helps to hold up the Church the way a tent or tabernacle is held up by its stakes. A stake is the gathering place for the remnants of scattered Israel ([[D&C 82.13|D&C 82:13–14]][[D&C 82.14|]]; [[D&C 101.17|101:17–21]][[D&C 101.18|]][[D&C 101.19|]][[D&C 101.20|]][[D&C 101.21|]]).

- Strengthen thy stakes and enlarge thy borders, [[Moroni 10.31|Moro. 10:31]] ([[D&C 82.14|D&C 82:14]]).

- Appoint unto Zion other stakes besides this one, [[D&C 109.59|D&C 109:59]].
- The gathering together upon Zion, and upon her stakes, may be for a defense, [[D&C 115.6|D&C 115:6]] ([[D&C 101.21|D&C 101:21]]).
- Other places should be appointed for stakes, [[D&C 115.18|D&C 115:18]].
- High priests quorum president instituted to qualify those who shall be appointed stake presidents, [[D&C 124.133|D&C 124:133–134]][[D&C 124.134|]].
- Go ye forth unto the land of Zion, that her stakes may be strengthened, [[D&C 133.9|D&C 133:9]].
