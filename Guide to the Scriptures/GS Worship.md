# Worship

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/worship?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS God, Godhead|God, Godhead]]

Love, reverence, service, and devotion for God ([[D&C 20.19|D&C 20:19]]). Worship includes prayer, fasting, church service, participating in gospel ordinances, and other practices that show devotion and love for God.

- Thou shalt have no other gods before me, [[Exodus 20.3|Ex. 20:3]] ([[Exodus 32.1|Ex. 32:1–8, 19–35]][[Exodus 32.2|]][[Exodus 32.3|]][[Exodus 32.4|]][[Exodus 32.5|]][[Exodus 32.6|]][[Exodus 32.7|]][[Exodus 32.8|]][[Exodus 32.19|]][[Exodus 32.20|]][[Exodus 32.21|]][[Exodus 32.22|]][[Exodus 32.23|]][[Exodus 32.24|]][[Exodus 32.25|]][[Exodus 32.26|]][[Exodus 32.27|]][[Exodus 32.28|]][[Exodus 32.29|]][[Exodus 32.30|]][[Exodus 32.31|]][[Exodus 32.32|]][[Exodus 32.33|]][[Exodus 32.34|]][[Exodus 32.35|]]; [[Psalms 81.9|Ps. 81:9]]).

- Worship the Father in spirit and in truth, [[John 4.23|John 4:23]].
- Worship him that made heaven and earth, [[Revelation 14.7|Rev. 14:7]] ([[D&C 133.38|D&C 133:38–39]][[D&C 133.39|]]).

- Worship him with all your might, mind, and strength, [[2 Nephi 25.29|2 Ne. 25:29]].
- They believed in Christ and worshiped the Father in his name, [[Jacob 4.5|Jacob 4:5]].
- Zenos taught that men should pray and worship in all places, [[Alma 33.3|Alma 33:3–11]][[Alma 33.4|]][[Alma 33.5|]][[Alma 33.6|]][[Alma 33.7|]][[Alma 33.8|]][[Alma 33.9|]][[Alma 33.10|]][[Alma 33.11|]].

- All men must repent, believe on Jesus Christ, and worship the Father in his name, [[D&C 20.29|D&C 20:29]].
- I give unto you these sayings that you may understand and know how to worship, and know what you worship, [[D&C 93.19|D&C 93:19]].
