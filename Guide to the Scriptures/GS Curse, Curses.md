# Curse, Curses

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/curse-curses?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Damnation|Damnation]]; [[GS Profanity|Profanity]]

In the scriptures, a curse is the application of divine law that allows or brings judgments and their consequences upon a thing, person, or people primarily because of unrighteousness. Curses are a manifestation of God’s divine love and justice. They may be invoked directly by God or pronounced by His authorized servants. Sometimes, the full reasons for curses are known only to God. In addition, a cursed state is experienced by those who willfully disobey God and thereby withdraw themselves from the Spirit of the Lord.

The Lord may remove curses because of the individual’s or people’s faith in Jesus Christ and obedience to the laws and ordinances of the gospel ([[Alma 23.16|Alma 23:16–18]][[Alma 23.17|]][[Alma 23.18|]]; [[3 Nephi 2.14|3 Ne. 2:14–16]][[3 Nephi 2.15|]][[3 Nephi 2.16|]]; [[Articles of Faith 1.3|A of F 1:3]]).

## Profanity

To curse is also to use language that is profane, blasphemous, or contemptuous.

- People are not to curse their fathers or mothers, [[Exodus 21.17|Ex. 21:17]] ([[Matthew 15.4|Matt. 15:4]]).
- Do not curse those who rule over you, [[Exodus 22.28|Ex. 22:28]] ([[Ecclesiastes 10.20|Eccl. 10:20]]).
- Men and women are not to curse God, [[Leviticus 24.13|Lev. 24:13–16]][[Leviticus 24.14|]][[Leviticus 24.15|]][[Leviticus 24.16|]].

- Peter cursed when he denied knowing Jesus, [[Matthew 26.69|Matt. 26:69–74]][[Matthew 26.70|]][[Matthew 26.71|]][[Matthew 26.72|]][[Matthew 26.73|]][[Matthew 26.74|]].

- Wicked Nephites cursed God and wanted to die, [[Mormon 2.14|Morm. 2:14]].
