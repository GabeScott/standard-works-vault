# Secret Combinations

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/secret-combinations?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Cain|Cain]]; [[GS Gadianton Robbers|Gadianton Robbers]]

An organization of people bound together by oaths to carry out the evil purposes of the group.

- The father of lies stirreth up the children of men unto secret combinations, [[2 Nephi 9.9|2 Ne. 9:9]].
- I must needs destroy the secret works of darkness, [[2 Nephi 10.15|2 Ne. 10:15]].
- Judgments of God did come upon these workers of secret combinations, [[Alma 37.30|Alma 37:30]].
- Gadianton did prove almost the entire destruction of the people of Nephi, [[Helaman 2.4|Hel. 2:4–13]][[Helaman 2.5|]][[Helaman 2.6|]][[Helaman 2.7|]][[Helaman 2.8|]][[Helaman 2.9|]][[Helaman 2.10|]][[Helaman 2.11|]][[Helaman 2.12|]][[Helaman 2.13|]].
- Satan put it into the hearts of the people to form secret oaths and covenants, [[Helaman 6.21|Hel. 6:21–31]][[Helaman 6.22|]][[Helaman 6.23|]][[Helaman 6.24|]][[Helaman 6.25|]][[Helaman 6.26|]][[Helaman 6.27|]][[Helaman 6.28|]][[Helaman 6.29|]][[Helaman 6.30|]][[Helaman 6.31|]].
- The Lord worketh not in secret combinations, [[Ether 8.19|Ether 8:19]].
- Nations that uphold secret combinations shall be destroyed, [[Ether 8.22|Ether 8:22–23]][[Ether 8.23|]].
- They did reject all the words of the prophets, because of their secret society, [[Ether 11.22|Ether 11:22]].

- From the days of Cain, there was a secret combination, [[Moses 5.51|Moses 5:51]].
