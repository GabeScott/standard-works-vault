# Peace

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/peace?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Millennium|Millennium]]; [[GS Peacemaker|Peacemaker]]; [[GS Rest|Rest]]

In the scriptures, peace can mean either freedom from conflict and turmoil or the inner calm and comfort born of the Spirit that God gives to His faithful Saints.

## Freedom from conflict and turmoil

- He maketh wars to cease, [[Psalms 46.9|Ps. 46:9]].
- Neither shall they learn war any more, [[Isaiah 2.4|Isa. 2:4]].

- Live peaceably with all men; avenge not yourselves, [[Romans 12.18|Rom. 12:18–21]][[Romans 12.19|]][[Romans 12.20|]][[Romans 12.21|]].

- There was still peace in the land, [[4 Nephi 1.4|4 Ne. 1:4, 15–20]][[4 Nephi 1.15|]][[4 Nephi 1.16|]][[4 Nephi 1.17|]][[4 Nephi 1.18|]][[4 Nephi 1.19|]][[4 Nephi 1.20|]].

- Renounce war and proclaim peace, [[D&C 98.16|D&C 98:16]].
- Lift up an ensign of peace, [[D&C 105.39|D&C 105:39]].

## Peace from God to the obedient

- The Savior will be called the Prince of Peace, [[Isaiah 9.6|Isa. 9:6]].
- There is no peace unto the wicked, [[Isaiah 48.22|Isa. 48:22]].

- There was a heavenly host, praising God and saying, Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, [[Luke 2.13|Luke 2:13–14]][[Luke 2.14|]].
- Peace I leave with you, [[John 14.27|John 14:27]].
- The peace of God passeth all understanding, [[Philippians 4.7|Philip. 4:7]].

- King Benjamin’s people received peace of conscience, [[Mosiah 4.3|Mosiah 4:3]].
- How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of those who publish peace, [[Mosiah 15.14|Mosiah 15:14–18]][[Mosiah 15.15|]][[Mosiah 15.16|]][[Mosiah 15.17|]][[Mosiah 15.18|]] ([[Isaiah 52.7|Isa. 52:7]]).
- Alma cried unto the Lord and found peace, [[Alma 38.8|Alma 38:8]].
- The spirits of the righteous are received into a state of peace, [[Alma 40.12|Alma 40:12]].

- Did I not speak peace to your mind concerning the matter, [[D&C 6.23|D&C 6:23]].
- Walk in the meekness of my Spirit, and you shall have peace in me, [[D&C 19.23|D&C 19:23]].
- He who doeth the works of righteousness shall receive peace, [[D&C 59.23|D&C 59:23]].
- Clothe yourselves with the bond of charity, which is the bond of perfectness and peace, [[D&C 88.125|D&C 88:125]].
- My son, peace be unto thy soul, [[D&C 121.7|D&C 121:7]].

- Finding there was greater peace, I sought for the blessings of the fathers, [[Abraham 1.2|Abr. 1:2]].
