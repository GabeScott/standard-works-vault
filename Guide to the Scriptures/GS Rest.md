# Rest

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/rest?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Peace|Peace]]; [[GS Sabbath Day|Sabbath Day]]

The enjoyment of peace and freedom from worry and turmoil. The Lord has promised such rest to His faithful followers during this life. He has also prepared a place of rest for them in the next life.

- My presence shall go with thee, and I will give thee rest, [[Exodus 33.14|Ex. 33:14]].

- Come unto me, all ye that are heavy laden, and I will give you rest, [[Matthew 11.28|Matt. 11:28–29]][[Matthew 11.29|]].

- We labored that they might enter into his rest, [[Jacob 1.7|Jacob 1:7]] ([[Hebrews 4.1|Heb. 4:1–11]][[Hebrews 4.2|]][[Hebrews 4.3|]][[Hebrews 4.4|]][[Hebrews 4.5|]][[Hebrews 4.6|]][[Hebrews 4.7|]][[Hebrews 4.8|]][[Hebrews 4.9|]][[Hebrews 4.10|]][[Hebrews 4.11|]]).
- Whosoever repenteth shall enter into my rest, [[Alma 12.34|Alma 12:34]].
- There were an exceedingly great many, who were made pure, and entered into the rest of the Lord, [[Alma 13.12|Alma 13:12–16]][[Alma 13.13|]][[Alma 13.14|]][[Alma 13.15|]][[Alma 13.16|]].
- Paradise is a state of rest, [[Alma 40.12|Alma 40:12]] ([[Alma 60.13|Alma 60:13]]).
- Nothing entereth into his rest save it be those who have washed their garments in my blood, [[3 Nephi 27.19|3 Ne. 27:19]].

- Declare repentance unto this people, that you may rest with them in the kingdom of my Father, [[D&C 15.6|D&C 15:6]] ([[D&C 16.6|D&C 16:6]]).
- Those that die shall rest from all their labors, [[D&C 59.2|D&C 59:2]] ([[Revelation 14.13|Rev. 14:13]]).
- The Lord’s rest is the fulness of his glory, [[D&C 84.24|D&C 84:24]].
