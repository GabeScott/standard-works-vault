# Marsh, Thomas B.

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/marsh-thomas-b?lang=eng)

The first President of the Quorum of the Twelve Apostles after the Restoration of the Church in 1830. He held the keys to the kingdom as pertained to the Twelve ([[D&C 112.16|D&C 112:16]]) and, in 1838, was commanded by revelation to publish the Lord’s word ([[D&C 118.2|D&C 118:2]]). [[D&C 31.1|Section 31 of the Doctrine and Covenants]][[D&C 31.2|]][[D&C 31.3|]][[D&C 31.4|]][[D&C 31.5|]][[D&C 31.6|]][[D&C 31.7|]][[D&C 31.8|]][[D&C 31.9|]][[D&C 31.10|]][[D&C 31.11|]][[D&C 31.12|]][[D&C 31.13|]] is directed to him. Marsh was excommunicated from the Church in 1839 but was rebaptized in July 1857.
