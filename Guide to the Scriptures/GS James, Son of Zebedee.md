# James, Son of Zebedee

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/james-son-of-zebedee?lang=eng)

One of the Twelve Apostles chosen by Jesus during His mortal ministry. He was the brother of John. He was one of three Apostles chosen to be with Jesus on certain special occasions: at the raising of the daughter of Jairus ([[Mark 5.37|Mark 5:37]]), at the Transfiguration ([[Matthew 17.1|Matt. 17:1]]; [[Mark 9.2|Mark 9:2]]; [[Luke 9.28|Luke 9:28]]), and at Gethsemane ([[Matthew 26.37|Matt. 26:37]]; [[Mark 14.33|Mark 14:33]]). With Peter and John, he restored the Melchizedek Priesthood to the earth by ordaining Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery ([[D&C 27.12|D&C 27:12]]; [[D&C 128.20|128:20]]; [[Joseph Smith History 1.72|JS—H 1:72]]).
