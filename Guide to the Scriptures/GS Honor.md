# Honor

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/honor?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Esteem|Esteem]]; [[GS Reverence|Reverence]]

As usually used in the scriptures, to show respect and reverence to someone or something.

- Honor thy father and thy mother, [[Exodus 20.12|Ex. 20:12]] ([[1 Nephi 17.55|1 Ne. 17:55]]; [[Mosiah 13.20|Mosiah 13:20]]).
- Honor the Lord with thy substance, [[Proverbs 3.9|Prov. 3:9]].

- If any man serve me, him will my Father honor, [[John 12.26|John 12:26]].
- Husbands should give honor to their wives, [[1 Peter 3.7|1 Pet. 3:7]].

- With their lips only do they honor the Lord, [[2 Nephi 27.25|2 Ne. 27:25]] ([[Isaiah 29.13|Isa. 29:13]]).
- I seek not for the honor of the world, [[Alma 60.36|Alma 60:36]].

- The devil rebelled against me, saying, Give me thine honor, which is my power, [[D&C 29.36|D&C 29:36]].
- The faithful shall be crowned with honor, [[D&C 75.5|D&C 75:5]] ([[D&C 124.55|D&C 124:55]]).
- The Lord delights to honor those who serve him, [[D&C 76.5|D&C 76:5]].
- They are not chosen because they aspire to the honors of men, [[D&C 121.34|D&C 121:34–35]][[D&C 121.35|]].

- We believe in honoring and sustaining the law, [[Articles of Faith 1.12|A of F 1:12]] ([[D&C 134.6|D&C 134:6]]).
