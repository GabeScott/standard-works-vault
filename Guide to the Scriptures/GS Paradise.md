# Paradise

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/paradise?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Heaven|Heaven]]

That part of the spirit world in which the righteous spirits who have departed from this life await the resurrection of the body. It is a condition of happiness and peace.

*Paradise* is also used in the scriptures to mean the world of spirits ([[Luke 23.43|Luke 23:43]]), the celestial kingdom ([[2 Corinthians 12.4|2 Cor. 12:4]]), and the glorified millennial condition of the earth ([[Articles of Faith 1.10|A of F 1:10]]).

- To him that overcometh will I give to eat of the tree of life, which is in the midst of the paradise of God, [[Revelation 2.7|Rev. 2:7]].

- The paradise of God must deliver up the spirits of the righteous, [[2 Nephi 9.13|2 Ne. 9:13]].
- The spirits of the righteous are received into a state of happiness, which is called paradise, [[Alma 40.11|Alma 40:11–12]][[Alma 40.12|]].
- All the disciples of Jesus had gone to the paradise of God, save it were three, [[4 Nephi 1.14|4 Ne. 1:14]].
- I soon go to rest in the paradise of God, [[Moroni 10.34|Moro. 10:34]].
