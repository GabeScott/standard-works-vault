# Capital Punishment

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/capital-punishment?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Murder|Murder]]

Punishment by death for a crime committed, especially associated with punishment for murder.

- Whoso sheds man’s blood, by man shall his blood be shed, [[Genesis 9.6|Gen. 9:6]] ([[JST Genesis 9.12|JST, Gen. 9:12–13]][[JST Genesis 9.13|]]).
- The murderer shall surely be put to death, [[Numbers 35.16|Num. 35:16]].

- Murderers who deliberately kill shall die, [[2 Nephi 9.35|2 Ne. 9:35]].
- Thou art condemned to die according to the law, [[Alma 1.13|Alma 1:13–14]][[Alma 1.14|]].
- He that murdered was punished unto death, [[Alma 1.18|Alma 1:18]].
- The law requires the life of him who has murdered, [[Alma 34.12|Alma 34:12]].

- He that kills shall die, [[D&C 42.19|D&C 42:19]].
