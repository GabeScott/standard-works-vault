# New Jerusalem

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/new-jerusalem?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Zion|Zion]]

The place where the Saints will gather and Christ will personally reign with them during the Millennium. Zion (the New Jerusalem) will be built upon the American continent, and the earth will be renewed and receive its paradisiacal glory ([[Articles of Faith 1.10|A of F 1:10]]). It also refers to a holy city that will come down out of heaven at the beginning of the Millennium.

- The law shall go forth out of Zion, [[Micah 4.2|Micah 4:2]].

- The name of the city of my God is New Jerusalem, [[Revelation 3.12|Rev. 3:12]].
- John saw the holy city, New Jerusalem, [[Revelation 21.1|Rev. 21:1–5]][[Revelation 21.2|]][[Revelation 21.3|]][[Revelation 21.4|]][[Revelation 21.5|]].

- This people will I establish in this land, and it shall be a New Jerusalem, [[3 Nephi 20.22|3 Ne. 20:22]].
- A New Jerusalem will be built in America, [[Ether 13.3|Ether 13:3–6, 10]][[Ether 13.4|]][[Ether 13.5|]][[Ether 13.6|]][[Ether 13.10|]].
