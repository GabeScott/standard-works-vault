# Lots

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/lots?lang=eng)

A way of selecting or eliminating several options of a choice, often done by choosing one slip of paper or piece of wood from among several. This is called casting lots.

- They parted his garments, casting lots, [[Matthew 27.35|Matt. 27:35]] ([[Psalms 22.18|Ps. 22:18]]; [[Mark 15.24|Mark 15:24]]; [[Luke 23.34|Luke 23:34]]; [[John 19.24|John 19:24]]).
- They gave forth their lots, and the lot fell upon Matthias, [[Acts 1.23|Acts 1:23–26]][[Acts 1.24|]][[Acts 1.25|]][[Acts 1.26|]].

- We cast lots—who of us should go in unto the house of Laban, [[1 Nephi 3.11|1 Ne. 3:11]].
