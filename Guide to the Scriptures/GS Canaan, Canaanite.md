# Canaan, Canaanite

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/canaan-canaanite?lang=eng)

In Old Testament times, the fourth son of Ham ([[Genesis 9.22|Gen. 9:22]]; [[Genesis 10.1|10:1, 6]][[Genesis 10.6|]]) and grandson of Noah. *Canaanite* refers to someone from the land where Canaan originally lived and also to his descendants. *Canaanite* was also a name for the people who inhabited the lowland along the Mediterranean coast of Palestine. This name was sometimes used to describe all the non-Israelite inhabitants of the country west of Jordan, whom the Greeks called Phoenicians.
