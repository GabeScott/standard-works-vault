# Countenance

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/countenance?lang=eng)

The general appearance of a person’s face, which often reflects spiritual attitude and state of mind.

- Their countenance witnessed against them, [[Isaiah 3.9|Isa. 3:9]].
- The king’s countenance was changed, and his thoughts troubled him, [[Daniel 5.6|Dan. 5:6]].

- His countenance was like lightning, [[Matthew 28.3|Matt. 28:3]].
- His countenance was as the sun, [[Revelation 1.16|Rev. 1:16]].

- Have ye received God’s image in your countenance, [[Alma 5.14|Alma 5:14, 19]][[Alma 5.19|]].
- Ammon saw that the countenance of the king was changed, [[Alma 18.12|Alma 18:12]].

- Fast and pray with a glad heart and a cheerful countenance, [[D&C 59.14|D&C 59:14–15]][[D&C 59.15|]].
- His countenance shone above the brightness of the sun, [[D&C 110.3|D&C 110:3]].
