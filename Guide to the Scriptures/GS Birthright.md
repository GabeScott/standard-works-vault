# Birthright

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/birthright?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Covenant|Covenant]]; [[GS Firstborn|Firstborn]]

A right of inheritance belonging to the firstborn son. In a broad sense, the birthright includes any or all rights or inheritance transmitted to a person when he is born into a family and culture.

- Sell me this day thy birthright, [[Genesis 25.29|Gen. 25:29–34]][[Genesis 25.30|]][[Genesis 25.31|]][[Genesis 25.32|]][[Genesis 25.33|]][[Genesis 25.34|]] ([[Genesis 27.36|Gen. 27:36]]).
- The firstborn sat according to his birthright, [[Genesis 43.33|Gen. 43:33]].
- He set Ephraim before Manasseh, [[Genesis 48.14|Gen. 48:14–20]][[Genesis 48.15|]][[Genesis 48.16|]][[Genesis 48.17|]][[Genesis 48.18|]][[Genesis 48.19|]][[Genesis 48.20|]] ([[Jeremiah 31.9|Jer. 31:9]]).
- The birthright was Joseph’s, [[1 Chronicles 5.2|1 Chr. 5:2]].

- Esau sold his birthright, [[Hebrews 12.16|Heb. 12:16]].

- Ye are lawful heirs, [[D&C 86.9|D&C 86:9]].
- Zion has a right to the priesthood by lineage, [[D&C 113.8|D&C 113:8]] ([[Abraham 2.9|Abr. 2:9–11]][[Abraham 2.10|]][[Abraham 2.11|]]).
