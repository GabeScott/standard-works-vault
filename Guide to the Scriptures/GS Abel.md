# Abel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/abel?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adam|Adam]]; [[GS Cain|Cain]]

In the Old Testament, a son of Adam and Eve.

- Offered to God a more excellent sacrifice than did his brother Cain, [[Genesis 4.4|Gen. 4:4–5]][[Genesis 4.5|]] ([[Hebrews 11.4|Heb. 11:4]]; [[Moses 5.16|Moses 5:16–21]][[Moses 5.17|]][[Moses 5.18|]][[Moses 5.19|]][[Moses 5.20|]][[Moses 5.21|]]).
- Was murdered by Cain, [[Genesis 4.8|Gen. 4:8]] ([[Moses 5.32|Moses 5:32]]).

- Received the priesthood from Adam, [[D&C 84.16|D&C 84:16]].

- Satan plotted with Cain to murder Abel, [[Moses 5.28|Moses 5:28–31]][[Moses 5.29|]][[Moses 5.30|]][[Moses 5.31|]] ([[Helaman 6.27|Hel. 6:27]]).
