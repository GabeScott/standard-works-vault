# Lying

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/lying?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Deceit, Deceive, Deception|Deceit, Deceive, Deception]]; [[GS Evil Speaking|Evil Speaking]]; [[GS Honest, Honesty|Honest, Honesty]]

Any communication of falsehood or untruth for the purpose of deception.

- Ye shall not steal, neither deal falsely, neither lie, [[Leviticus 19.11|Lev. 19:11]].
- I hate and abhor lying, [[Psalms 119.163|Ps. 119:163]].
- Lying lips are abomination to the Lord, [[Proverbs 12.22|Prov. 12:22]].
- They are my people that will not lie, [[Isaiah 63.8|Isa. 63:8]].

- The devil is a liar and the father of lies, [[John 8.44|John 8:44]] ([[2 Nephi 2.18|2 Ne. 2:18]]; [[Ether 8.25|Ether 8:25]]; [[Moses 4.4|Moses 4:4]]).
- Thou hast not lied unto men, but unto God, [[Acts 5.4|Acts 5:4]] ([[Alma 12.3|Alma 12:3]]).
- If a man say, I love God, and hateth his brother, he is a liar, [[1 John 4.20|1 Jn. 4:20]].
- All liars have part in the second death, [[Revelation 21.8|Rev. 21:8]] ([[D&C 63.17|D&C 63:17]]).

- Wo unto the liar, for he shall be thrust down to hell, [[2 Nephi 9.34|2 Ne. 9:34]].
- Many will teach false doctrine, saying: lie a little and there is no harm in it, [[2 Nephi 28.8|2 Ne. 28:8–9]][[2 Nephi 28.9|]] ([[D&C 10.25|D&C 10:25]]).
- Do ye imagine you can lie unto the Lord, [[Alma 5.17|Alma 5:17]].
- Thou art a God of truth and canst not lie, [[Ether 3.12|Ether 3:12]] ([[Numbers 23.19|Num. 23:19]]; [[1 Samuel 15.29|1 Sam. 15:29]]; [[Titus 1.2|Titus 1:2]]; [[Hebrews 6.18|Heb. 6:18]]; [[Enos 1.6|Enos 1:6]]).

- He that lieth and will not repent shall be cast out, [[D&C 42.21|D&C 42:21]].
- Liars inherit telestial glory, [[D&C 76.81|D&C 76:81, 103–106]][[D&C 76.103|]][[D&C 76.104|]][[D&C 76.105|]][[D&C 76.106|]].

- We believe in being honest, [[Articles of Faith 1.13|A of F 1:13]].
