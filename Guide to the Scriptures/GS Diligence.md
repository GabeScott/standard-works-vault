# Diligence

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/diligence?lang=eng)

Consistent, valiant effort, especially in serving the Lord and obeying His word.

- Hearken diligently unto me, [[Isaiah 55.2|Isa. 55:2]].

- God is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him, [[Hebrews 11.6|Heb. 11:6]].
- Giving all diligence, add to your faith virtue, [[2 Peter 1.5|2 Pet. 1:5]].

- Teach the word of God with all diligence, [[Jacob 1.19|Jacob 1:19]].
- They had searched the scriptures diligently, [[Alma 17.2|Alma 17:2]].
- They were willing with all diligence to keep the commandments, [[3 Nephi 6.14|3 Ne. 6:14]].
- Let us labor diligently, [[Moroni 9.6|Moro. 9:6]].

- Be anxiously engaged in a good cause, [[D&C 58.27|D&C 58:27]].
- Neither be idle but labor with your might, [[D&C 75.3|D&C 75:3]].
- Give diligent heed to the words of eternal life, [[D&C 84.43|D&C 84:43]].
- Let every man learn his duty, and to act in all diligence, [[D&C 107.99|D&C 107:99]].
