# Woodruff, Wilford

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/woodruff-wilford?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Manifesto|Manifesto]]; [[GS Official Declaration 1|Official Declaration 1]]

The fourth President of the Church following the Restoration of the gospel through the Prophet Joseph Smith. He was born in 1807 and died in 1898.

- Was called to fill a position in the Council of the Twelve, [[D&C 118.6|D&C 118:6]].
- Was among the choice spirits reserved to come forth in the fulness of times, [[D&C 138.53|D&C 138:53]].
- Received a revelation to stop plural marriage in the Church, [[Official Declaration 1.1|OD 1]].
