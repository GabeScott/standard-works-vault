# Centurion

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/centurion?lang=eng)

An officer in the Roman army in command of a company of fifty to one hundred men. Such a company formed one-sixtieth part of a Roman legion. (See [[Matthew 8.5|Matt. 8:5]]; [[Luke 23.47|Luke 23:47]]; [[Acts 10.1|Acts 10:1–8]][[Acts 10.2|]][[Acts 10.3|]][[Acts 10.4|]][[Acts 10.5|]][[Acts 10.6|]][[Acts 10.7|]][[Acts 10.8|]].)
