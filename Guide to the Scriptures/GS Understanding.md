# Understanding

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/understanding?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Knowledge|Knowledge]]; [[GS Truth|Truth]]; [[GS Wisdom|Wisdom]]

To gain a knowledge of or to perceive the meaning of some truth, including its application to life.

- Lean not unto thine own understanding, [[Proverbs 3.5|Prov. 3:5]].
- With all thy getting get understanding, [[Proverbs 4.7|Prov. 4:7]].

- Jesus spoke in parables and some did not understand, [[Matthew 13.12|Matt. 13:12–17]][[Matthew 13.13|]][[Matthew 13.14|]][[Matthew 13.15|]][[Matthew 13.16|]][[Matthew 13.17|]].
- The Lord opened their understanding, [[Luke 24.45|Luke 24:45]].

- If ye cannot understand these words, it will be because ye ask not, [[2 Nephi 32.4|2 Ne. 32:4]] ([[3 Nephi 17.3|3 Ne. 17:3]]).
- The records were preserved so that we might read and understand, [[Mosiah 1.2|Mosiah 1:2–5]][[Mosiah 1.3|]][[Mosiah 1.4|]][[Mosiah 1.5|]].
- Because of their unbelief, they could not understand the word of God, [[Mosiah 26.3|Mosiah 26:3]].
- They were men of a sound understanding, [[Alma 17.2|Alma 17:2–3]][[Alma 17.3|]].
- The word beginneth to enlighten my understanding, [[Alma 32.28|Alma 32:28]].

- Let us reason together that ye may understand, [[D&C 50.10|D&C 50:10–12, 19–23]][[D&C 50.11|]][[D&C 50.12|]][[D&C 50.19|]][[D&C 50.20|]][[D&C 50.21|]][[D&C 50.22|]][[D&C 50.23|]].
- Parents must teach their children to understand, [[D&C 68.25|D&C 68:25]].
- The works and mysteries of God can only be understood by the Holy Spirit, [[D&C 76.114|D&C 76:114–116]][[D&C 76.115|]][[D&C 76.116|]].
- Satan seeks to turn men’s hearts from understanding, [[D&C 78.10|D&C 78:10]].
- The Light of Christ quickens our understandings, [[D&C 88.11|D&C 88:11]].
