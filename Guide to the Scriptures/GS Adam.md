# Adam

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/adam?lang=eng)

*See also* [[GS Adam-ondi-Ahman|Adam-ondi-Ahman]]; [[GS Archangel|Archangel]]; [[GS Eden|Eden]]; [[GS Eve|Eve]]; [[GS Fall of Adam and Eve|Fall of Adam and Eve]]; [[GS Michael|Michael]]

The first man created on earth.

Adam is the father and patriarch of the human race on the earth. His transgression in the Garden of Eden ([[Genesis 3.1|Gen. 3]][[Genesis 3.2|]][[Genesis 3.3|]][[Genesis 3.4|]][[Genesis 3.5|]][[Genesis 3.6|]][[Genesis 3.7|]][[Genesis 3.8|]][[Genesis 3.9|]][[Genesis 3.10|]][[Genesis 3.11|]][[Genesis 3.12|]][[Genesis 3.13|]][[Genesis 3.14|]][[Genesis 3.15|]][[Genesis 3.16|]][[Genesis 3.17|]][[Genesis 3.18|]][[Genesis 3.19|]][[Genesis 3.20|]][[Genesis 3.21|]][[Genesis 3.22|]][[Genesis 3.23|]][[Genesis 3.24|]]; [[D&C 29.40|D&C 29:40–42]][[D&C 29.41|]][[D&C 29.42|]]; [[Moses 4.1|Moses 4]][[Moses 4.2|]][[Moses 4.3|]][[Moses 4.4|]][[Moses 4.5|]][[Moses 4.6|]][[Moses 4.7|]][[Moses 4.8|]][[Moses 4.9|]][[Moses 4.10|]][[Moses 4.11|]][[Moses 4.12|]][[Moses 4.13|]][[Moses 4.14|]][[Moses 4.15|]][[Moses 4.16|]][[Moses 4.17|]][[Moses 4.18|]][[Moses 4.19|]][[Moses 4.20|]][[Moses 4.21|]][[Moses 4.22|]][[Moses 4.23|]][[Moses 4.24|]][[Moses 4.25|]][[Moses 4.26|]][[Moses 4.27|]][[Moses 4.28|]][[Moses 4.29|]][[Moses 4.30|]][[Moses 4.31|]][[Moses 4.32|]]) caused him to “fall” and become mortal, a step necessary in order for mankind to progress on this earth ([[2 Nephi 2.14|2 Ne. 2:14–29]][[2 Nephi 2.15|]][[2 Nephi 2.16|]][[2 Nephi 2.17|]][[2 Nephi 2.18|]][[2 Nephi 2.19|]][[2 Nephi 2.20|]][[2 Nephi 2.21|]][[2 Nephi 2.22|]][[2 Nephi 2.23|]][[2 Nephi 2.24|]][[2 Nephi 2.25|]][[2 Nephi 2.26|]][[2 Nephi 2.27|]][[2 Nephi 2.28|]][[2 Nephi 2.29|]]; [[Alma 12.21|Alma 12:21–26]][[Alma 12.22|]][[Alma 12.23|]][[Alma 12.24|]][[Alma 12.25|]][[Alma 12.26|]]). Adam and Eve should therefore be honored for their role in making our eternal growth possible. Adam is the Ancient of Days and is also known as Michael ([[Daniel 7.1|Dan. 7]][[Daniel 7.2|]][[Daniel 7.3|]][[Daniel 7.4|]][[Daniel 7.5|]][[Daniel 7.6|]][[Daniel 7.7|]][[Daniel 7.8|]][[Daniel 7.9|]][[Daniel 7.10|]][[Daniel 7.11|]][[Daniel 7.12|]][[Daniel 7.13|]][[Daniel 7.14|]][[Daniel 7.15|]][[Daniel 7.16|]][[Daniel 7.17|]][[Daniel 7.18|]][[Daniel 7.19|]][[Daniel 7.20|]][[Daniel 7.21|]][[Daniel 7.22|]][[Daniel 7.23|]][[Daniel 7.24|]][[Daniel 7.25|]][[Daniel 7.26|]][[Daniel 7.27|]][[Daniel 7.28|]]; [[D&C 27.11|D&C 27:11]]; [[D&C 107.53|107:53–54]][[D&C 107.54|]]; [[D&C 116.1|116]]; [[D&C 138.38|138:38]]). He is the archangel ([[D&C 107.54|D&C 107:54]]) and will come again to the earth as the patriarch of the human family ([[D&C 116.1|D&C 116]]).
