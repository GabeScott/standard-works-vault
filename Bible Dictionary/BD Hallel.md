# Hallel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hallel?lang=eng)

A name given to the group of Psalms ([[Psalms 113.1|113–18]]) recited by Jews at the great feasts ([[Matthew 26.30|Matt. 26:30]]; [[Mark 14.26|Mark 14:26]]). [[Psalms 136.1|Ps. 136]] was generally known as the “Great Hallel.”
