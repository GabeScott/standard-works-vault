# Uzzah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/uzzah?lang=eng)

*Strength.* Slain for touching the Ark ([[2 Samuel 6.6|2 Sam. 6:6]]); called Uzza ([[1 Chronicles 13.9|1 Chr. 13:9]]). The penalty may appear to us severe, but it must be remembered that the Ark was a sacred repository, and the people had been clearly warned that only designated persons could touch the Ark, and warned also of the consequences of disobedience.
