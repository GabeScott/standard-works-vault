# Accursed

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/accursed?lang=eng)

Old Testament, “devoted,” “banned,” “proscribed.” Things forbidden to the people individually, but to be used for the benefit of the whole congregation or for sacrifice ([[Joshua 6.17|Josh. 6:17]]; [[Joshua 7.1|7:1, 11, 15]][[Joshua 7.11|]][[Joshua 7.15|]]). New Testament, “condemned.” [[Galatians 1.8|Gal. 1:8]].
