# Elihu

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/elihu?lang=eng)

*God (is) He.* (1) Reproves Job’s three friends and reasons with Job ([[Job 32.1|Job 32–37]][[Job 32.2|]][[Job 32.3|]][[Job 32.4|]][[Job 32.5|]][[Job 32.6|]][[Job 32.7|]][[Job 32.8|]][[Job 32.9|]][[Job 32.10|]][[Job 32.11|]][[Job 32.12|]][[Job 32.13|]][[Job 32.14|]][[Job 32.15|]][[Job 32.16|]][[Job 32.17|]][[Job 32.18|]][[Job 32.19|]][[Job 32.20|]][[Job 32.21|]][[Job 32.22|]]). See *[[BD Job, book of|Job, book of]].*

(2) An ancestor of Samuel ([[1 Samuel 1.1|1 Sam. 1:1]]); called Eliab ([[1 Chronicles 6.27|1 Chr. 6:27]]) and Eliel ([[1 Chronicles 6.34|6:34]]).

(3) Others in [[1 Chronicles 12.20|1 Chr. 12:20]] and [[1 Chronicles 26.7|26:7]].

(4) There was also an Elihu who lived sometime between Abraham and Moses and who is mentioned as a bearer of the Melchizedek Priesthood prior to Moses. Nothing more is known of him today ([[D&C 84.8|D&C 84:8–9]][[D&C 84.9|]]).
