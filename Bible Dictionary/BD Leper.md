# Leper

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/leper?lang=eng)

Leprosy is a terrible form of skin disease, still occuring in various parts of the world. Lepers were forbidden by the law to enter any walled city. If a stranger approached, the leper was obliged to cry “unclean.” The disease was regarded as a living death, indicated by bare head, rent clothes, and covered lip.
