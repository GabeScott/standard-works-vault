# Eber

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/eber?lang=eng)

Great-grandson of Shem and the ancestor of the Hebrew people ([[Genesis 10.24|Gen. 10:24]]; [[Genesis 11.14|11:14–17]][[Genesis 11.15|]][[Genesis 11.16|]][[Genesis 11.17|]]; [[Numbers 24.24|Num. 24:24]]). Sometimes called Heber ([[Luke 3.35|Luke 3:35]]). The word *Hebrew* is a patronymic name derived from Eber.
