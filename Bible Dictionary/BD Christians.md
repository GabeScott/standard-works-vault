# Christians

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/christians?lang=eng)

A name first given to believers in Jesus Christ at Antioch in Syria, about  43 ([[Acts 11.26|Acts 11:26]]). It was perhaps given contemptuously but was accepted by followers of Christ as a fit title. See [[1 Peter 4.16|1 Pet. 4:16]]; [[Alma 46.15|Alma 46:15]].
