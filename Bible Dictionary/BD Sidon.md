# Sidon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/sidon?lang=eng)

[[Genesis 10.15|Gen. 10:15]]. Firstborn of Canaan, and name of a city ([[Genesis 10.19|Gen. 10:19]] and elsewhere in the Old Testament). People from the district came to listen to the Lord ([[Mark 3.8|Mark 3:8]]), and on one occasion He visited the neighborhood ([[Mark 7.24|7:24]]; see also [[Matthew 11.21|Matt. 11:21–22]][[Matthew 11.22|]]; [[Matthew 15.21|15:21]]; [[Luke 4.26|Luke 4:26]]; [[Luke 6.17|6:17]]; [[Luke 10.13|10:13–14]][[Luke 10.14|]]; [[Acts 12.20|Acts 12:20]]; [[Acts 27.3|27:3]]). See *[[BD Zidon|Zidon]].*
