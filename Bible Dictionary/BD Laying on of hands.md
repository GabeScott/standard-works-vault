# Laying on of hands

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/laying-on-of-hands?lang=eng)

A procedure used from the earliest times in the manner of blessing, conferring the Holy Ghost, and ordaining to the priesthood. See [[Genesis 48.14|Gen. 48:14, 17]][[Genesis 48.17|]]; [[Numbers 27.18|Num. 27:18, 23]][[Numbers 27.23|]]; [[Deuteronomy 34.9|Deut. 34:9]]; [[Matthew 9.18|Matt. 9:18]]; [[Mark 10.13|Mark 10:13, 16]][[Mark 10.16|]]; [[Luke 4.40|Luke 4:40]]; [[Acts 6.6|Acts 6:6]]; [[Acts 8.14|8:14–17]][[Acts 8.15|]][[Acts 8.16|]][[Acts 8.17|]]; [[Acts 9.12|9:12, 17]][[Acts 9.17|]]; [[Acts 13.3|13:3]]; [[Acts 19.1|19:1–7]][[Acts 19.2|]][[Acts 19.3|]][[Acts 19.4|]][[Acts 19.5|]][[Acts 19.6|]][[Acts 19.7|]]; [[Acts 28.8|28:8]]; [[1 Timothy 4.14|1 Tim. 4:14]]; [[1 Timothy 5.22|5:22]]; [[2 Timothy 1.6|2 Tim. 1:6]]; [[Hebrews 6.2|Heb. 6:2]]; [[James 5.14|James 5:14–16]][[James 5.15|]][[James 5.16|]]; [[Alma 6.1|Alma 6:1]]; [[Moroni 2.1|Moro. 2]]; [[D&C 20.41|D&C 20:41, 58, 68, 70]][[D&C 20.58|]][[D&C 20.68|]][[D&C 20.70|]]; [[D&C 36.1|36:1–2]][[D&C 36.2|]]; [[Articles of Faith 1.5|A of F 1:5]]. Such procedure is in accord with the revealed will of the Lord and is not a mere formality. In latter-day revelation the laying on of hands is discussed by the Lord as follows: “I will lay my hand upon you by the hand of my servant Sidney Rigdon, and you shall receive my Spirit …” ([[D&C 36.2|D&C 36:2]]).

The laying on of hands also formed part of the ritual of sacrifice under the law of Moses ([[Exodus 29.10|Ex. 29:10, 15, 19]][[Exodus 29.15|]][[Exodus 29.19|]]; [[Leviticus 1.4|Lev. 1:4]]; [[Leviticus 3.2|3:2, 8, 13]][[Leviticus 3.8|]][[Leviticus 3.13|]]; [[Leviticus 4.4|4:4]]; [[Leviticus 8.14|8:14]]).
