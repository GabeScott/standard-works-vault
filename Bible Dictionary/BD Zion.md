# Zion

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/zion?lang=eng)

The word *Zion* is used repeatedly in all the standard works of the Church and is defined in latter-day revelation as “the pure in heart” ([[D&C 97.21|D&C 97:21]]). Other usages of Zion have to do with a geographical location. For example, Enoch built a city that was called Zion ([[Moses 7.18|Moses 7:18–19]][[Moses 7.19|]]); Solomon built his temple on Mount Zion ([[1 Kings 8.1|1 Kgs. 8:1]]; see also [[2 Samuel 5.6|2 Sam. 5:6–7]][[2 Samuel 5.7|]]); and Jackson County, Missouri, is called Zion in many of the revelations in the D&C, such as [[D&C 58.49|58:49–50]][[D&C 58.50|]]; [[D&C 62.4|62:4]]; [[D&C 63.48|63:48]]; [[D&C 72.13|72:13]]; [[D&C 84.76|84:76]]; [[D&C 104.47|104:47]]. The city of New Jerusalem, to be built in Jackson County, Missouri, is to be called Zion ([[D&C 45.66|D&C 45:66–67]][[D&C 45.67|]]). The revelations also speak of “the cause of Zion” ([[D&C 6.6|D&C 6:6]]; [[D&C 11.6|11:6]]). In a wider sense all of North and South America are Zion ( 6:318–19).
