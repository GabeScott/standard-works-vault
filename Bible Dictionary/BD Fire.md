# Fire

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/fire?lang=eng)

Frequently the symbol of God’s presence, revealed either in mercy or in judgment (1) of His glory ([[Exodus 13.21|Ex. 13:21–22]][[Exodus 13.22|]]; [[Ezekiel 1.4|Ezek. 1:4, 13]][[Ezekiel 1.13|]]; [[Ezekiel 10.6|10:6–7]][[Ezekiel 10.7|]]; [[Daniel 7.9|Dan. 7:9]]; [[Daniel 10.6|10:6]]); (2) of His holiness ([[Deuteronomy 4.24|Deut. 4:24]]; [[Hebrews 12.29|Heb. 12:29]]); (3) of His presence to protect ([[2 Kings 6.17|2 Kgs. 6:17]]; [[Zechariah 2.5|Zech. 2:5]]); (4) of His judgments ([[Isaiah 66.15|Isa. 66:15–16]][[Isaiah 66.16|]]; [[Zechariah 13.9|Zech. 13:9]]; [[Malachi 3.2|Mal. 3:2]]; [[1 Corinthians 3.13|1 Cor. 3:13]]); (5) the punishment of the wicked ([[Matthew 18.9|Matt. 18:9]]; [[Matthew 25.41|25:41]]; [[Mark 9.43|Mark 9:43–48]][[Mark 9.44|]][[Mark 9.45|]][[Mark 9.46|]][[Mark 9.47|]][[Mark 9.48|]]; [[Revelation 19.20|Rev. 19:20]]; [[Revelation 20.10|20:10, 14–15]][[Revelation 20.14|]][[Revelation 20.15|]]; [[Revelation 21.8|21:8]]); (6) of the Holy Spirit as a purifying agent ([[Matthew 3.11|Matt. 3:11]]; [[Luke 3.16|Luke 3:16]]); as tongues of fire ([[Acts 2.3|Acts 2:3]]). On the altar there was a perpetual fire, replenished with wood every morning ([[Leviticus 6.12|Lev. 6:12]]; [[Leviticus 9.24|9:24]]; [[2 Chronicles 7.1|2 Chr. 7:1, 3]][[2 Chronicles 7.3|]]), which consumed the burnt sacrifice and the incense offering.
