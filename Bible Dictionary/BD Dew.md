# Dew

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/dew?lang=eng)

Of great importance in Palestine owing to the want of rain during a large portion of the year; hence spoken of as a source of fertility ([[Genesis 27.28|Gen. 27:28]]; [[Deuteronomy 33.13|Deut. 33:13]]); and in poetry as typical of any beneficial effect produced by quiet means ([[Deuteronomy 32.2|Deut. 32:2]]; [[Psalms 110.3|Ps. 110:3]]; [[Psalms 133.3|133:3]]; [[Proverbs 19.12|Prov. 19:12]]; [[Song of Solomon 5.2|Song 5:2]]; [[Isaiah 18.4|Isa. 18:4]]; [[Isaiah 26.19|26:19]]; [[Hosea 6.4|Hosea 6:4]]; [[Hosea 13.3|13:3]]; [[Hosea 14.5|14:5]]; [[Micah 5.7|Micah 5:7]]).
