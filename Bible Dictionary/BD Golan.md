# Golan

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/golan?lang=eng)

A city and district of Bashan, east of the Jordan ([[Deuteronomy 4.43|Deut. 4:43]]; [[Joshua 20.8|Josh. 20:8]]; [[Joshua 21.27|21:27]]; [[1 Chronicles 6.71|1 Chr. 6:71]]). In New Testament times the district was called Gaulanitis.
