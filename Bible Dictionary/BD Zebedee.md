# Zebedee

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/zebedee?lang=eng)

Probably Hebrew *Zebediah,* meaning “endowment of the Lord.” The father of James and John, two of the Twelve; a fisherman in fairly good circumstances at or near Capernaum ([[Mark 1.19|Mark 1:19, 20]][[Mark 1.20|]]; [[Luke 5.10|Luke 5:10]]).
