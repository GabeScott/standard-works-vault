# Bottles

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/bottles?lang=eng)

Generally made of skins of animals; the head and legs being cut off, the skin was removed from the body without further cutting and was then tanned by means of oak-bark. If wine fermented in a bottle of this kind, it would stretch the skin to such an extent that it could never be used for the purpose a second time; hence the lesson drawn by our Lord in [[Matthew 9.17|Matt. 9:17]]; [[Mark 2.22|Mark 2:22]]; [[Luke 5.37|Luke 5:37]]; see also [[Job 32.19|Job 32:19]].
