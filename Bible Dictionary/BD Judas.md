# Judas

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/judas?lang=eng)

(1) Maccabaeus (see *[[BD Maccabees|Maccabees]]*).

(2) Iscariot, meaning “belonging to Kerioth” ([[Joshua 15.25|Josh. 15:25]]) in the tribe of Judah, one of “the twelve,” and the only one who was not a Galilean. The passages in which he is mentioned are [[Matthew 10.4|Matt. 10:4]]; [[Matthew 26.14|26:14]]; [[Mark 3.19|Mark 3:19]]; [[Mark 14.10|14:10]]; [[Luke 6.16|Luke 6:16]]; [[Luke 22.3|22:3]]; [[John 6.71|John 6:71]]; [[John 12.4|12:4]]; [[John 13.2|13:2, 26]][[John 13.26|]]; [[Acts 1.16|Acts 1:16, 25]][[Acts 1.25|]].

(3) A brother of our Lord ([[Matthew 13.55|Matt. 13:55]]); writer of the epistle of Jude (see also *[[BD Brethren Of The Lord|Brethren of the Lord]]*).

(4) “Not Iscariot,” one of the Twelve, also called Judas (son or brother) of James ([[Luke 6.16|Luke 6:16]]; [[John 14.22|John 14:22]]; [[Acts 1.13|Acts 1:13]]); probably the same as Lebbaeus or Thaddaeus ([[Matthew 10.3|Matt. 10:3]]; [[Mark 3.18|Mark 3:18]]).

(5) Of Galilee, leader of a revolt against Roman rule “in the days of the taxing” (of the census); regarded by many as the Messiah. He had a great following but was defeated and slain ([[Acts 5.37|Acts 5:37]]).

(6) Surnamed Barsabbas, a leading member of the Church in Jerusalem ([[Acts 15.22|Acts 15:22, 27, 32]][[Acts 15.27|]][[Acts 15.32|]]).

(7) A man of Damascus with whom Paul lodged after his conversion ([[Acts 9.11|Acts 9:11]]).
