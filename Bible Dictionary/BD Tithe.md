# Tithe

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/tithe?lang=eng)

The word denotes a tenth part, given for the service of God. The first recorded instance is the payment made by Abraham to Melchizedek ([[Genesis 14.20|Gen. 14:20]]; [[Hebrews 7.4|Heb. 7:4–10]][[Hebrews 7.5|]][[Hebrews 7.6|]][[Hebrews 7.7|]][[Hebrews 7.8|]][[Hebrews 7.9|]][[Hebrews 7.10|]]). See also Jacob’s vow ([[Genesis 28.22|Gen. 28:22]]). The law enforced the payment and provided rules with regard to the use to which the tithe should be put. In [[Numbers 18.21|Num. 18:21–28]][[Numbers 18.22|]][[Numbers 18.23|]][[Numbers 18.24|]][[Numbers 18.25|]][[Numbers 18.26|]][[Numbers 18.27|]][[Numbers 18.28|]] it is directed that tithe be paid to the Levites, who in their turn give one-tenth of what they receive to the priests.

A clear exposition of the tithe is given in [[Malachi 3.8|Mal. 3:8–18]][[Malachi 3.9|]][[Malachi 3.10|]][[Malachi 3.11|]][[Malachi 3.12|]][[Malachi 3.13|]][[Malachi 3.14|]][[Malachi 3.15|]][[Malachi 3.16|]][[Malachi 3.17|]][[Malachi 3.18|]], in which it is shown that blessings from the payment of tithing are both temporal and spiritual, and failure to pay an honest tithe is a form of robbery. The importance of Malachi’s words is demonstrated by the fact that the Lord repeated them to the Nephites (see [[3 Nephi 24.1|3 Ne. 24]][[3 Nephi 24.2|]][[3 Nephi 24.3|]][[3 Nephi 24.4|]][[3 Nephi 24.5|]][[3 Nephi 24.6|]][[3 Nephi 24.7|]][[3 Nephi 24.8|]][[3 Nephi 24.9|]][[3 Nephi 24.10|]][[3 Nephi 24.11|]][[3 Nephi 24.12|]][[3 Nephi 24.13|]][[3 Nephi 24.14|]][[3 Nephi 24.15|]][[3 Nephi 24.16|]][[3 Nephi 24.17|]][[3 Nephi 24.18|]]).

Latter-day revelation emphasizes the law of the tithe as a duty and a test of faithfulness ([[D&C 64.23|D&C 64:23–25]][[D&C 64.24|]][[D&C 64.25|]]; [[D&C 85.3|85:3]]; [[D&C 97.11|97:11]]; [[D&C 119.1|119]][[D&C 119.2|]][[D&C 119.3|]][[D&C 119.4|]][[D&C 119.5|]][[D&C 119.6|]][[D&C 119.7|]]). The honest payment of tithing sanctifies both the individual and the land on which he lives.
