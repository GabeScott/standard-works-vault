# Megiddo

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/megiddo?lang=eng)

On the southwestern side of the plain of Esdraelon, and on the caravan route from Egypt to Damascus. Frequently mentioned in Israelite history. The scene of Sisera’s defeat ([[Judges 5.19|Judg. 5:19]]). Fortified by Solomon ([[1 Kings 9.15|1 Kgs. 9:15]]). The place where Ahaziah died of his wounds ([[2 Kings 9.27|2 Kgs. 9:27]]). Also the scene of the tragic defeat and death of King Josiah ([[2 Kings 23.29|23:29–30]][[2 Kings 23.30|]]; [[2 Chronicles 35.22|2 Chr. 35:22]]). In [[Zechariah 12.11|Zech. 12:11]] the place is called “the valley of Megiddon.” See also *[[BD Armageddon|Armageddon]]; [[BD Esdraelon|Esdraelon]].*
