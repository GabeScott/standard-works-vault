# Rabbah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/rabbah?lang=eng)

*Chief (city).* (1) In Judah ([[Joshua 15.60|Josh. 15:60]]).

(2) Chief city of the Ammonites, in the deep fertile valley of the Upper Jabbock ([[Deuteronomy 3.11|Deut. 3:11]]; [[Joshua 13.25|Josh. 13:25]]); taken by David ([[2 Samuel 11.1|2 Sam. 11:1]]; [[2 Samuel 12.26|12:26–27, 29]][[2 Samuel 12.27|]][[2 Samuel 12.29|]]; [[2 Samuel 17.27|17:27]]; [[1 Chronicles 20.1|1 Chr. 20:1]]); prophecies against ([[Jeremiah 49.2|Jer. 49:2–3]][[Jeremiah 49.3|]]; [[Ezekiel 25.5|Ezek. 25:5]]; [[Amos 1.14|Amos 1:14]]). Later it received the name of Philadelphia. It is not mentioned in the New Testament, but it was a town of importance during the Roman occupation and was one of the cities of the Decapolis. There are still extensive ruins at the modern Amman.
