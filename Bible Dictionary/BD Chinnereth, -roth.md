# Chinnereth, -roth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/chinnereth?lang=eng)

(1) A fortified city in the tribe of Naphtali ([[Joshua 19.35|Josh. 19:35]]).

(2) The Old Testament name of the Sea of Galilee ([[Numbers 34.11|Num. 34:11]]; [[Deuteronomy 3.17|Deut. 3:17]]; [[Joshua 11.2|Josh. 11:2]]; [[Joshua 12.3|12:3]]; [[Joshua 13.27|13:27]]; [[1 Kings 15.20|1 Kgs. 15:20]]).
