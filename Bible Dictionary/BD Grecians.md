# Grecians

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/grecians?lang=eng)

Jews of the dispersion (Hellenists) who spoke Greek ([[Acts 6.1|Acts 6:1]]; [[Acts 9.29|9:29]]). Grecians in [[Acts 11.20|Acts 11:20]] should be rendered *Greeks* to convey the proper thought, meaning “persons of Greek lineage.”
