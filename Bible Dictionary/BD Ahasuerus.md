# Ahasuerus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/ahasuerus?lang=eng)

Old Testament name of one Median and two Persian kings. (1) [[Daniel 9.1|Dan. 9:1]], father of “Darius the Mede.”

(2) [[Ezra 4.6|Ezra 4:6]]; probably the same as (3).

(3) [[Esther 1.1|Esth. 1:1]], where Ahasuerus is certainly to be identified with Xerxes.
