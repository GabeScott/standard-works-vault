# Mule

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/mule?lang=eng)

Frequently mentioned in the Bible ([[2 Samuel 18.9|2 Sam. 18:9]]; [[Ezekiel 27.14|Ezek. 27:14]]). During the monarchy the mule was the royal animal ([[1 Kings 1.33|1 Kgs. 1:33, 38, 44]][[1 Kings 1.38|]][[1 Kings 1.44|]]). Mules were not bred in Palestine; Solomon imported them ([[1 Kings 10.25|1 Kgs. 10:25]]; [[2 Chronicles 9.24|2 Chr. 9:24]]).
