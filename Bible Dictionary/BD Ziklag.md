# Ziklag

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/ziklag?lang=eng)

A city in the Negev, originally assigned to Judah ([[Joshua 15.31|Josh. 15:31]]) but transferred to Simeon ([[Joshua 19.5|Josh. 19:5]]). It was a Philistine city in the days of Saul and was given by Achish to David ([[1 Samuel 27.6|1 Sam. 27:6]]). The Amalekites made a raid upon it ([[1 Samuel 30.1|1 Sam. 30:1–26]][[1 Samuel 30.2|]][[1 Samuel 30.3|]][[1 Samuel 30.4|]][[1 Samuel 30.5|]][[1 Samuel 30.6|]][[1 Samuel 30.7|]][[1 Samuel 30.8|]][[1 Samuel 30.9|]][[1 Samuel 30.10|]][[1 Samuel 30.11|]][[1 Samuel 30.12|]][[1 Samuel 30.13|]][[1 Samuel 30.14|]][[1 Samuel 30.15|]][[1 Samuel 30.16|]][[1 Samuel 30.17|]][[1 Samuel 30.18|]][[1 Samuel 30.19|]][[1 Samuel 30.20|]][[1 Samuel 30.21|]][[1 Samuel 30.22|]][[1 Samuel 30.23|]][[1 Samuel 30.24|]][[1 Samuel 30.25|]][[1 Samuel 30.26|]]; see also [[2 Samuel 1.1|2 Sam. 1:1]]; [[2 Samuel 4.10|4:10]]; [[1 Chronicles 4.30|1 Chr. 4:30]]; [[1 Chronicles 12.1|12:1, 20]][[1 Chronicles 12.20|]]; [[Nehemiah 11.28|Neh. 11:28]]).
