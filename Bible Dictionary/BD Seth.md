# Seth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/seth?lang=eng)

Son of Adam and Eve, born in the 130th year of Adam’s life in mortality ([[Luke 3.38|Luke 3:38]]; [[Moses 6.1|Moses 6:1–3]][[Moses 6.2|]][[Moses 6.3|]]; [[Moses 6.9|6:9–11]][[Moses 6.10|]][[Moses 6.11|]]). A prophet and patriarch, he was ordained by Adam at the age of 69 years. He is called “a perfect man, and his likeness was the express likeness of his father, insomuch that he seemed to be like unto his father in all things” ([[D&C 107.42|D&C 107:42–43]][[D&C 107.43|]]). The record says he lived 912 years ([[Genesis 5.8|Gen. 5:8]]; [[Moses 6.16|Moses 6:16]]).
