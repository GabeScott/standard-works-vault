# Cyrus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/cyrus?lang=eng)

The first king of the Persians and Medes. He captured Babylon and overthrew the Chaldean dynasty (of which Nabonidus was the last king, with Belshazzar his son as commander-in-chief). He issued a decree allowing the Jews to return to the land of Judah and rebuild the temple, possibly as a token of gratitude for assistance given to him in his conquest ([[2 Chronicles 36.22|2 Chr. 36:22]]; [[Ezra 1.1|Ezra 1]]; [[Ezra 3.7|3:7]]; [[Isaiah 44.28|Isa. 44:28]]; [[Isaiah 45.1|45:1]]; [[Daniel 1.21|Dan. 1:21]]; [[Daniel 6.28|6:28]]; [[Daniel 10.1|10:1]]).
