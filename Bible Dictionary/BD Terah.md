# Terah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/terah?lang=eng)

The father of Abram, Nahor, and Haran. He migrated with Abram, Sarai, and Lot (Nahor following them later) from Ur of the Chaldees to Charran (Haran), where he died ([[Genesis 11.24|Gen. 11:24, 26, 31]][[Genesis 11.26|]][[Genesis 11.31|]]; [[Joshua 24.2|Josh. 24:2]]; [[1 Chronicles 1.26|1 Chr. 1:26]]; [[Abraham 1.30|Abr. 1:30]]; [[Abraham 2.1|2:1–6]][[Abraham 2.2|]][[Abraham 2.3|]][[Abraham 2.4|]][[Abraham 2.5|]][[Abraham 2.6|]]).
