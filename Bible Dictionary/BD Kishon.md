# Kishon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/kishon?lang=eng)

A river that carries water from the hills around Esdraelon to the Mediterranean. It is connected with two great events, the defeat of Sisera ([[Judges 4.7|Judg. 4:7, 13]][[Judges 4.13|]]; [[Judges 5.21|5:21]]; [[Psalms 83.9|Ps. 83:9]]) and the destruction of the prophets of Baal by Elijah ([[1 Kings 18.40|1 Kgs. 18:40]]).
