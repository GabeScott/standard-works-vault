# Turtle

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/turtle?lang=eng)

The turtledove, as in Song of Solomon: “the voice of the turtle is heard in our land,” being a sign of springtime ([[Song of Solomon 2.11|2:11–13]][[Song of Solomon 2.12|]][[Song of Solomon 2.13|]]; see also [[Jeremiah 8.7|Jer. 8:7]]). Turtledoves were used for offerings, as in [[Genesis 15.9|Gen. 15:9]]; [[Leviticus 1.14|Lev. 1:14]]; [[Leviticus 5.7|5:7, 11]][[Leviticus 5.11|]]; [[Leviticus 12.6|12:6–8]][[Leviticus 12.7|]][[Leviticus 12.8|]]; [[Leviticus 14.22|14:22, 30]][[Leviticus 14.30|]]; [[Leviticus 15.29|15:29]]; [[Numbers 6.10|Num. 6:10]]; [[Luke 2.24|Luke 2:24]].
