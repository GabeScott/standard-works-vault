# Lot

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/lot?lang=eng)

*A covering.* Son of Haran, Abraham’s brother ([[Genesis 11.27|Gen. 11:27, 31]][[Genesis 11.31|]]; [[Genesis 12.4|12:4–5]][[Genesis 12.5|]]; [[Genesis 13.1|13:1, 5]][[Genesis 13.5|]]). He went with Abraham to Canaan; his life after his separation from Abraham is described in [[Genesis 13.1|Gen. 13–14]]; [[Genesis 19.1|19]]. He was the ancestor of Moab and Ammon ([[Genesis 19.37|Gen. 19:37–38]][[Genesis 19.38|]]; [[Deuteronomy 2.9|Deut. 2:9, 19]][[Deuteronomy 2.19|]]). There are New Testament references to him in [[Luke 17.29|Luke 17:29]]; [[2 Peter 2.7|2 Pet. 2:7]]. His wife’s fate is also referred to in [[Genesis 19.26|Gen. 19:26]]; [[Luke 17.32|Luke 17:32]].
