# Cassia

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/cassia?lang=eng)

One of the ingredients in the anointing oil ([[Exodus 30.24|Ex. 30:24]]), and an article of merchandise imported into Tyre by Greek merchants ([[Ezekiel 27.19|Ezek. 27:19]]). There is another Hebrew word also rendered *cassia* ([[Psalms 45.8|Ps. 45:8]]). Both Hebrew words denote aromatic plant products; the former is a cinnamon, while the latter may be Indian orris or costus.
