# Ebenezer

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/ebenezer?lang=eng)

*Stone of help.* A stone set up by Samuel as a token of gratitude for deliverance from the Philistines ([[1 Samuel 7.10|1 Sam. 7:10–12]][[1 Samuel 7.11|]][[1 Samuel 7.12|]]). The place had previously been the scene of two defeats ([[1 Samuel 4.1|1 Sam. 4:1]]; [[1 Samuel 5.1|5:1]]); and in the account of these defeats it is called by the name it received later on. Its position may have been at the head of the valley of Ajalon.
