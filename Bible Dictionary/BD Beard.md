# Beard

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/beard?lang=eng)

Regarded by the Jews as of great importance (whereas Egyptians always shaved); the trimming of the corners was forbidden ([[Leviticus 19.26|Lev. 19:26–28]][[Leviticus 19.27|]][[Leviticus 19.28|]]), such a practice being a mark of idolatry ([[Jeremiah 41.5|Jer. 41:5]]). See also [[1 Samuel 21.13|1 Sam. 21:13]]; [[2 Samuel 10.4|2 Sam. 10:4]]. A leper was compelled to shave ([[Leviticus 14.9|Lev. 14:9]]).
