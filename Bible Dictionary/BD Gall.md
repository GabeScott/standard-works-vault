# Gall

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/gall?lang=eng)

Something bitter; hence the word is applied to bile ([[Job 16.13|Job 16:13]]; [[Job 20.25|20:25]]) and to the poison of serpents ([[Job 20.14|20:14]]). More often it denotes the juice of some bitter plant ([[Deuteronomy 29.18|Deut. 29:18]]; [[Deuteronomy 32.32|32:32]]; [[Matthew 27.34|Matt. 27:34]]). See also *[[BD Wormwood|Wormwood]].*
