# Ahaz

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/ahaz?lang=eng)

*Possessor.* King of Judah ([[2 Kings 16.1|2 Kgs. 16]]; [[2 Chronicles 28.1|2 Chr. 28]]); makes an alliance with Tiglath-pileser, king of Assyria, in order to repel an attack from Syria and Israel; copies heathen altar ([[2 Kings 16.10|2 Kgs. 16:10]]); Isaiah’s message to ([[Isaiah 7.1|Isa. 7]]); dial of Ahaz ([[2 Kings 20.11|2 Kgs. 20:11]]; [[2 Kings 23.12|23:12]]).
