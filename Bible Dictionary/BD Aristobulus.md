# Aristobulus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/aristobulus?lang=eng)

In [[Romans 16.10|Rom. 16:10]] Paul sends a greeting to his “household.” He is probably to be identified with the Aristobulus who was the younger brother of Herod Agrippa Ⅰ and who lived in Rome and was a friend of the Emperor Claudius.
