# Jephthah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jephthah?lang=eng)

Judge of Israel; for his history see [[Judges 11.1|Judg. 11–12]]. He is chiefly known for his rash vow, in consequence of which he offered his own daughter in sacrifice. He is commended in [[Hebrews 11.32|Heb. 11:32]], probably because of his delivery of Israel from Ammon.
