# Zerubbabel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/zerubbabel?lang=eng)

*Born in Babylon* (the name is Assyrian). Grandson of Jehoiachin, king of Judah, and son of Pedaiah ([[1 Chronicles 3.16|1 Chr. 3:16–19]][[1 Chronicles 3.17|]][[1 Chronicles 3.18|]][[1 Chronicles 3.19|]]); in [[Ezra 3.2|Ezra 3:2]] and elsewhere he is called “son of Shealtiel,” probably his nephew or legal heir. Zerubbabel was also known by the Persian name Sheshbazzar ([[Ezra 1.8|Ezra 1:8]], etc.). When Cyrus gave permission to the Jews to return to Palestine, Zerubbabel was appointed *pekhah* or governor, as the representative of the Jewish royal house ([[Ezra 1.8|Ezra 1:8]]). He set up an altar in Jerusalem and rebuilt the temple (see *[[BD Temple Of Zerubbabel|Temple of Zerubbabel]]*) ([[Ezra 3.2|Ezra 3:2, 8]][[Ezra 3.8|]]; [[Ezra 4.2|4:2–3]][[Ezra 4.3|]]; [[Ezra 5.2|5:2]]); being helped by Haggai ([[Haggai 1.1|Hag. 1:1–14]][[Haggai 1.2|]][[Haggai 1.3|]][[Haggai 1.4|]][[Haggai 1.5|]][[Haggai 1.6|]][[Haggai 1.7|]][[Haggai 1.8|]][[Haggai 1.9|]][[Haggai 1.10|]][[Haggai 1.11|]][[Haggai 1.12|]][[Haggai 1.13|]][[Haggai 1.14|]]; [[Haggai 2.1|2]]) and Zechariah ([[Zechariah 4.6|Zech. 4:6–10]][[Zechariah 4.7|]][[Zechariah 4.8|]][[Zechariah 4.9|]][[Zechariah 4.10|]]). In the New Testament he is called “Zorobabel.”
