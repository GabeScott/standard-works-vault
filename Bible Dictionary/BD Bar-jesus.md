# Bar-jesus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/bar-jesus?lang=eng)

*Son of Jesus* (also called Elymas), a Jew who had obtained an influence over Sergius Paulus, the Roman proconsul in Cyprus ([[Acts 13.6|Acts 13:6–12]][[Acts 13.7|]][[Acts 13.8|]][[Acts 13.9|]][[Acts 13.10|]][[Acts 13.11|]][[Acts 13.12|]]). He is called a sorcerer or magician and a false prophet. Paul destroyed his influence by calling down upon him a proof of the divine anger ([[Acts 13.11|Acts 13:11]]).
