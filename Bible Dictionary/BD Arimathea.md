# Arimathea

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/arimathea?lang=eng)

Home of Joseph, the wealthy counselor who was a disciple of Jesus ([[Matthew 27.57|Matt. 27:57]]; [[Luke 23.50|Luke 23:50–51]][[Luke 23.51|]]; [[John 19.38|John 19:38]]) and in whose tomb Jesus was buried. May be Ramathaim of the Old Testament, the birthplace of Samuel ([[1 Samuel 1.1|1 Sam. 1:1]]), lying in the hill country of Ephraim northwest of Jerusalem, and sometimes called Ramah. See also *[[BD Joseph|Joseph]]; [[BD Ramah|Ramah]].*
