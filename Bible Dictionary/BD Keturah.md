# Keturah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/keturah?lang=eng)

*Incense.* Wife of Abraham after Sarah’s death ([[Genesis 25.1|Gen. 25:1, 4]][[Genesis 25.4|]]; [[1 Chronicles 1.32|1 Chr. 1:32–33]][[1 Chronicles 1.33|]]); hence sprang various tribes, including the Midianites.
