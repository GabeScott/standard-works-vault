# Tirshatha

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/tirshatha?lang=eng)

The Persian title for a local or provincial governor; used of Zerubbabel ([[Ezra 2.63|Ezra 2:63]]; [[Nehemiah 7.65|Neh. 7:65, 70]][[Nehemiah 7.70|]]); of Nehemiah ([[Nehemiah 8.9|Neh. 8:9]]; [[Nehemiah 10.1|10:1]]). The corresponding Babylonian title was *Pekhah.*
