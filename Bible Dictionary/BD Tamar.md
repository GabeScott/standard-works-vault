# Tamar

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/tamar?lang=eng)

*Palm tree.* (1) Wife of Er and Onan; mother, by Judah, of Pharez and Zarah ([[Genesis 38.6|Gen. 38:6]]; [[Ruth 4.12|Ruth 4:12]]; [[1 Chronicles 2.4|1 Chr. 2:4]]; [[Matthew 1.3|Matt. 1:3]]).

(2) Daughter of David and Maachah ([[2 Samuel 13.1|2 Sam. 13:1]]).

(3) Daughter of Absalom ([[2 Samuel 14.27|2 Sam. 14:27]]).
