# Mamre

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/mamre?lang=eng)

An Amorite in alliance with Abram ([[Genesis 14.13|Gen. 14:13, 24]][[Genesis 14.24|]]); hence the name of the place where he lived near Hebron ([[Genesis 13.18|13:18]]); afterwards a home of the patriarchs ([[Genesis 18.1|18:1]]; [[Genesis 23.17|23:17, 19]][[Genesis 23.19|]]; [[Genesis 25.9|25:9]]; [[Genesis 35.27|35:27]]; [[Genesis 49.30|49:30]]; [[Genesis 50.13|50:13]]).
