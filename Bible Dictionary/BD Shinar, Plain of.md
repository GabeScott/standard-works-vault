# Shinar, Plain of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/shinar-plain-of?lang=eng)

Place where was found Nimrod’s kingdom ([[Genesis 10.10|Gen. 10:10]]) and the tower of Babel ([[Genesis 11.2|11:2]]). Amraphel was king of Shinar ([[Genesis 14.1|14:1]]), and Judah was carried captive there ([[Isaiah 11.11|Isa. 11:11]]; [[Daniel 1.2|Dan. 1:2]]; [[Zechariah 5.11|Zech. 5:11]]). The name denotes the lower part of the district between the Tigris and Euphrates, and is sometimes used as an equivalent for Babylonia.
