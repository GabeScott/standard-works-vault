# Barnabas

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/barnabas?lang=eng)

*Son of consolation.* A name given to Joseph, a Levite of Cyprus, who sold his possessions and gave the proceeds to the Apostles ([[Acts 4.36|Acts 4:36–37]][[Acts 4.37|]]); was of service to Saul after his conversion ([[Acts 9.27|9:27]]); was sent by the Apostles to Antioch, where he worked with Saul ([[Acts 11.22|11:22–30]][[Acts 11.23|]][[Acts 11.24|]][[Acts 11.25|]][[Acts 11.26|]][[Acts 11.27|]][[Acts 11.28|]][[Acts 11.29|]][[Acts 11.30|]]; [[Acts 12.25|12:25]]); with Paul on missionary journey ([[Acts 13.1|Acts 13–14]]); and at Jerusalem ([[Acts 15.1|Acts 15]]); they parted ([[Acts 15.39|15:39]]). See also [[1 Corinthians 9.6|1 Cor. 9:6]]; [[Galatians 2.1|Gal. 2:1, 9, 13]][[Galatians 2.9|]][[Galatians 2.13|]]; [[Colossians 4.10|Col. 4:10]]. Though not one of the Twelve, he was regarded as an Apostle ([[Acts 14.4|Acts 14:4, 14]][[Acts 14.14|]]).
