# Tammuz

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/tammuz?lang=eng)

Greek *Adonis.* The Babylonian god of spring slain by summer heat, or the god of summer slain by winter cold. The “weeping for Tammuz” was a festival in the autumn, a lament over the season’s decline ([[Ezekiel 8.14|Ezek. 8:14]]). Tammuz was the Jewish name for June or July after the Captivity.
