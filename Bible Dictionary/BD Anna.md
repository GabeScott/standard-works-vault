# Anna

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/anna?lang=eng)

(New Testament form of Hannah.) A prophetess of the tribe of Asher who, after a short married life of seven years, had been, at the time of our Lord’s birth, a widow for 84 years. She was one of those who greeted the infant Jesus at His presentation in the temple ([[Luke 2.36|Luke 2:36–38]][[Luke 2.37|]][[Luke 2.38|]]).
