# Isaac

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/isaac?lang=eng)

*He laugheth.* The son born to Abraham and Sarah in their old age, the child of promise, and the heir of the promises ([[Genesis 15.1|Gen. 15:1–6]][[Genesis 15.2|]][[Genesis 15.3|]][[Genesis 15.4|]][[Genesis 15.5|]][[Genesis 15.6|]]; [[Genesis 17.15|17:15–19]][[Genesis 17.16|]][[Genesis 17.17|]][[Genesis 17.18|]][[Genesis 17.19|]]; [[Genesis 18.9|18:9–15]][[Genesis 18.10|]][[Genesis 18.11|]][[Genesis 18.12|]][[Genesis 18.13|]][[Genesis 18.14|]][[Genesis 18.15|]]; [[Genesis 21.1|21:1–8]][[Genesis 21.2|]][[Genesis 21.3|]][[Genesis 21.4|]][[Genesis 21.5|]][[Genesis 21.6|]][[Genesis 21.7|]][[Genesis 21.8|]]). The main interest of his life gathers around his birth, the sacrifice on Mount Moriah ([[Genesis 22.1|Gen. 22]]), his marriage ([[Genesis 24.1|24]]; [[Genesis 25.1|25]]), and his dealings with his sons ([[Genesis 27.1|27]]; [[Genesis 28.1|28]]). There are numerous references to him in various parts of the Bible (see especially [[Galatians 4.28|Gal. 4:28]]; [[Hebrews 11.9|Heb. 11:9, 17, 20]][[Hebrews 11.17|]][[Hebrews 11.20|]]). In character he appears to have been a peace-loving shepherd of great personal piety, full of affection for the members of his own family. In [[D&C 132.37|D&C 132:37]] we are told that Isaac is now exalted and sits upon a throne with Abraham and Jacob (see also [[Matthew 8.11|Matt. 8:11]]).
