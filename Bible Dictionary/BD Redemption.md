# Redemption

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/redemption?lang=eng)

Of the firstborn, see *[[BD Firstborn|Firstborn]];* of land, see *[[BD Jubilee, Year of|Jubilee, Year of]];* of a slave, [[Leviticus 25.48|Lev. 25:48]]. The word is of constant use in the New Testament in speaking of the sacrificial work of Jesus Christ and our deliverance from sin. He redeemed us with His blood ([[Acts 20.28|Acts 20:28]]; [[Ephesians 1.7|Eph. 1:7, 14]][[Ephesians 1.14|]]; [[Colossians 1.14|Col. 1:14]]; [[Hebrews 9.11|Heb. 9:11–15]][[Hebrews 9.12|]][[Hebrews 9.13|]][[Hebrews 9.14|]][[Hebrews 9.15|]]; [[1 Peter 1.18|1 Pet. 1:18–19]][[1 Peter 1.19|]]; [[1 Peter 3.18|3:18]]; [[Revelation 5.9|Rev. 5:9]]).
