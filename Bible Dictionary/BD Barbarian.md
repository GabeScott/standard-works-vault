# Barbarian

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/barbarian?lang=eng)

Literally means a “foreigner.” The word *barbarian* is not used in the Old Testament but is synonymous with such words as *stranger, alien, sojourner,* and *Gentile.* In the New Testament it connotes peoples of the Mediterranean area who are not of the Greco-Roman culture ([[Romans 1.14|Rom. 1:14]]; [[Colossians 3.11|Col. 3:11]]) or those whose language is not familiar to the hearer, as in [[1 Corinthians 14.11|1 Cor. 14:11]]. See also [[Acts 28.2|Acts 28:2]].
