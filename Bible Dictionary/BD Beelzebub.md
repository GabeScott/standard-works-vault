# Beelzebub

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/beelzebub?lang=eng)

Name of a Philistine god. Used as a title for the “chief of the demons,” or Satan. The Pharisees referred to him as the “prince of the devils” ([[Matthew 12.24|Matt. 12:24]]). Jesus denied that He cast out devils by the power of Beelzebub ([[Luke 11.14|Luke 11:14–20]][[Luke 11.15|]][[Luke 11.16|]][[Luke 11.17|]][[Luke 11.18|]][[Luke 11.19|]][[Luke 11.20|]]). See also [[2 Kings 1.1|2 Kgs. 1:1–6]][[2 Kings 1.2|]][[2 Kings 1.3|]][[2 Kings 1.4|]][[2 Kings 1.5|]][[2 Kings 1.6|]], where it is spelled Baalzebub.
