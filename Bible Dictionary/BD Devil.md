# Devil

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/devil?lang=eng)

The English word *devil* in the KJV is used to represent several different words in Greek *(slanderer, demon,* and *adversary)* and Hebrew *(spoiler).* The devil is the enemy of righteousness and of those who seek to do the will of God. Literally a spirit son of God, he was at one time “an angel” in authority in the presence of God; however, he rebelled in the premortal life, at which time he persuaded a third of the spirit children of the Father to rebel with him, in opposition to the plan of salvation championed by Jehovah (Jesus Christ). “Thus came the devil and his angels” ([[D&C 29.37|D&C 29:37]]). They were cast out of heaven and were denied the experience of mortal bodies and earth life ([[Isaiah 14.12|Isa. 14:12–15]][[Isaiah 14.13|]][[Isaiah 14.14|]][[Isaiah 14.15|]]; [[Revelation 12.4|Rev. 12:4–9]][[Revelation 12.5|]][[Revelation 12.6|]][[Revelation 12.7|]][[Revelation 12.8|]][[Revelation 12.9|]]; [[2 Nephi 2.17|2 Ne. 2:17]]; [[2 Nephi 9.8|9:8]]; [[D&C 29.36|D&C 29:36–38]][[D&C 29.37|]][[D&C 29.38|]]; [[D&C 76.25|76:25–26]][[D&C 76.26|]]; [[D&C 93.25|93:25]]; [[Moses 4.1|Moses 4:1–4]][[Moses 4.2|]][[Moses 4.3|]][[Moses 4.4|]]; [[Abraham 3.27|Abr. 3:27–28]][[Abraham 3.28|]]).

Latter-day revelation confirms the biblical teaching that the devil is a reality and that he does strive to lead men and women from the work of God. One of the major techniques of the devil is to cause human beings to think they are following God’s ways, when in reality they are deceived by the devil to follow other paths.

Since the devil and his premortal angels have no physical body of flesh and bones, they often seek to possess the bodies of mortal beings. There are many such instances recorded in scripture ([[Matthew 9.32|Matt. 9:32]]; [[Matthew 12.22|12:22]]; [[Mark 1.24|Mark 1:24]]; [[Mark 5.7|5:7]]; [[Luke 8.30|Luke 8:30]]; [[Acts 19.15|Acts 19:15]]; see also [[Mosiah 3.6|Mosiah 3:6]]). Such can be evicted by the power of faith in Jesus Christ and the exercise of the holy priesthood. Jesus gave this power to His disciples ([[Matthew 10.1|Matt. 10:1]]; [[Mark 16.17|Mark 16:17]]; [[Luke 10.17|Luke 10:17]]; [[Acts 5.16|Acts 5:16]]; [[D&C 84.67|D&C 84:67]]).

The devil is called the prince of this world ([[John 12.31|John 12:31]]; [[John 14.30|14:30]]; [[John 16.11|16:11]]); the adversary ([[1 Peter 5.8|1 Pet. 5:8]]); Beelzebub, meaning the prince of the devils ([[Mark 3.22|Mark 3:22]]); the wicked one ([[Matthew 13.38|Matt. 13:38]]); the enemy ([[Matthew 13.39|Matt. 13:39]]); Lucifer ([[Isaiah 14.12|Isa. 14:12]]; [[D&C 76.26|D&C 76:26]]); Satan ([[Revelation 12.9|Rev. 12:9]]); prince of the power of the air ([[Ephesians 2.2|Eph. 2:2–3]][[Ephesians 2.3|]]); Perdition ([[D&C 76.26|D&C 76:26]]); son of the morning ([[D&C 76.26|D&C 76:26–27]][[D&C 76.27|]]); that old serpent ([[Revelation 12.9|Rev. 12:9]]; [[D&C 76.28|D&C 76:28]]); the great dragon ([[Revelation 12.7|Rev. 12:7–9]][[Revelation 12.8|]][[Revelation 12.9|]]); a murderer from the beginning ([[John 8.44|John 8:44]]); a liar from the beginning ([[D&C 93.25|D&C 93:25]]); and the accuser ([[Revelation 12.10|Rev. 12:10]]).

He is miserable in his situation and “stirreth up the children of men unto secret combinations of murder and all manner of secret works of darkness” ([[2 Nephi 9.9|2 Ne. 9:9]]). He tries to imitate the work of God by transforming himself nigh unto an angel of light ([[2 Corinthians 11.12|2 Cor. 11:12–15]][[2 Corinthians 11.13|]][[2 Corinthians 11.14|]][[2 Corinthians 11.15|]]; [[2 Nephi 9.9|2 Ne. 9:9]]; [[D&C 128.20|D&C 128:20]]). He is also a worker of miracles, by which he deceives many upon the earth ([[Revelation 13.1|Rev. 13:1–15]][[Revelation 13.2|]][[Revelation 13.3|]][[Revelation 13.4|]][[Revelation 13.5|]][[Revelation 13.6|]][[Revelation 13.7|]][[Revelation 13.8|]][[Revelation 13.9|]][[Revelation 13.10|]][[Revelation 13.11|]][[Revelation 13.12|]][[Revelation 13.13|]][[Revelation 13.14|]][[Revelation 13.15|]]). In fact, the scripture says he deceives the whole world ([[Revelation 12.9|Rev. 12:9]]). He can cite scripture to make his point seem plausible ([[Matthew 4.1|Matt. 4:1–11]][[Matthew 4.2|]][[Matthew 4.3|]][[Matthew 4.4|]][[Matthew 4.5|]][[Matthew 4.6|]][[Matthew 4.7|]][[Matthew 4.8|]][[Matthew 4.9|]][[Matthew 4.10|]][[Matthew 4.11|]]). All of this is his scheme to make man miserable like himself. Protection against the influence of the devil is found by obedience to the commandments and laws of the gospel of Jesus Christ. The message of all the prophets and apostles is that truth, righteousness, and peace shall in the end prevail over error, sin, and war; the faithful shall triumph over all their afflictions and enemies and shall triumph over the devil. There shall be a complete and lasting victory of righteousness over wickedness on this earth, which will be done by the power of the Lord Jesus Christ.
