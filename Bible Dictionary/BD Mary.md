The virgin, cousin of Elisabeth ([[Luke 1.36|Luke 1:36]]); betrothed to Joseph ([[Matthew 1.18|Matt. 1:18]]; [[Luke 1.27|Luke 1:27]]); the Annunciation ([[Luke 1.26|26–38]][[Luke 1.27|]][[Luke 1.28|]][[Luke 1.29|]][[Luke 1.30|]][[Luke 1.31|]][[Luke 1.32|]][[Luke 1.33|]][[Luke 1.34|]][[Luke 1.35|]][[Luke 1.36|]][[Luke 1.37|]][[Luke 1.38|]]); visits Elisabeth ([[Luke 1.40|40–45]][[Luke 1.41|]][[Luke 1.42|]][[Luke 1.43|]][[Luke 1.44|]][[Luke 1.45|]]); magnifies the Lord ([[Luke 1.46|46–55]][[Luke 1.47|]][[Luke 1.48|]][[Luke 1.49|]][[Luke 1.50|]][[Luke 1.51|]][[Luke 1.52|]][[Luke 1.53|]][[Luke 1.54|]][[Luke 1.55|]]); returns ([[Luke 1.56|56]]); Joseph warned not to put her away ([[Matthew 1.18|Matt. 1:18–25]][[Matthew 1.19|]][[Matthew 1.20|]][[Matthew 1.21|]][[Matthew 1.22|]][[Matthew 1.23|]][[Matthew 1.24|]][[Matthew 1.25|]]); goes to Bethlehem with Joseph ([[Luke 2.4|Luke 2:4–5]][[Luke 2.5|]]); the Nativity ([[Luke 2.7|7, 16]][[Luke 2.16|]]); visit of the shepherds ([[Luke 2.16|16–20]][[Luke 2.17|]][[Luke 2.18|]][[Luke 2.19|]][[Luke 2.20|]]); the Purification ([[Luke 2.21|2:21–38]][[Luke 2.22|]][[Luke 2.23|]][[Luke 2.24|]][[Luke 2.25|]][[Luke 2.26|]][[Luke 2.27|]][[Luke 2.28|]][[Luke 2.29|]][[Luke 2.30|]][[Luke 2.31|]][[Luke 2.32|]][[Luke 2.33|]][[Luke 2.34|]][[Luke 2.35|]][[Luke 2.36|]][[Luke 2.37|]][[Luke 2.38|]]); the Magi visit ([[Matthew 2.11|Matt. 2:11]]); in Egypt ([[Matthew 2.13|13–14]][[Matthew 2.14|]]); returns to Nazareth ([[Matthew 2.19|19–23]][[Matthew 2.20|]][[Matthew 2.21|]][[Matthew 2.22|]][[Matthew 2.23|]]; [[Luke 2.39|Luke 2:39]]); goes up to the Passover ([[Luke 2.41|41–52]][[Luke 2.42|]][[Luke 2.43|]][[Luke 2.44|]][[Luke 2.45|]][[Luke 2.46|]][[Luke 2.47|]][[Luke 2.48|]][[Luke 2.49|]][[Luke 2.50|]][[Luke 2.51|]][[Luke 2.52|]]); at the wedding at Cana ([[John 2.2|John 2:2–5]][[John 2.3|]][[John 2.4|]][[John 2.5|]]). Other references during our Lord’s ministry include [[Matthew 12.46|Matt. 12:46]]; [[Matthew 13.54|13:54–55]][[Matthew 13.55|]]; [[Mark 3.31|Mark 3:31–32]][[Mark 3.32|]]; [[Mark 6.3|6:3]]; [[Luke 8.19|Luke 8:19]]. She was entrusted to John ([[John 19.25|John 19:25–26]][[John 19.26|]]) and was with the Apostles after the Ascension ([[Acts 1.14|Acts 1:14]]). There is no trustworthy history of her later years. Latter-day revelation confirms the biblical account and affirms that Mary was a pure and a chosen vessel and the mother of the Son of God in the flesh ([[1 Nephi 11.13|1 Ne. 11:13–20]][[1 Nephi 11.14|]][[1 Nephi 11.15|]][[1 Nephi 11.16|]][[1 Nephi 11.17|]][[1 Nephi 11.18|]][[1 Nephi 11.19|]][[1 Nephi 11.20|]]; [[Mosiah 3.8|Mosiah 3:8]]; [[Alma 7.10|Alma 7:10]]).
