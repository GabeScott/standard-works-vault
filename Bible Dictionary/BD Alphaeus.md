# Alphaeus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/alphaeus?lang=eng)

(1) Father of Matthew or Levi ([[Mark 2.14|Mark 2:14]]).

(2) Father of James the Apostle ([[Matthew 10.3|Matt. 10:3]]; [[Mark 3.18|Mark 3:18]]; [[Luke 6.15|Luke 6:15]]; [[Acts 1.13|Acts 1:13]]); by some scholars identified with Cleopas ([[Luke 24.18|Luke 24:18]]) and Cleophas ([[John 19.25|John 19:25]]). See also *[[BD James|James]].*
