# Centurion

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/centurion?lang=eng)

An officer of the Roman army, in command of a century, or company of 100 men, forming one-sixtieth part of a Roman legion. In New Testament times the legions were not up to their full strength, and a century varied from 50 to 100 men.
