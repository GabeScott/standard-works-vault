# Conversation

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/conversation?lang=eng)

In the KJV, *conversation* usually means behavior, way of life, or conduct, rather than the modern sense of talking together. See [[Psalms 37.14|Ps. 37:14]]; [[2 Corinthians 1.12|2 Cor. 1:12]]; [[Galatians 1.13|Gal. 1:13]]; [[Ephesians 2.3|Eph. 2:3]]; [[Ephesians 4.22|4:22]]; [[Philippians 1.27|Philip. 1:27]]; [[1 Timothy 4.12|1 Tim. 4:12]]; [[James 3.13|James 3:13]]; [[1 Peter 1.15|1 Pet. 1:15, 18]][[1 Peter 1.18|]]; [[1 Peter 2.12|2:12]]; [[1 Peter 3.1|3:1–2]][[1 Peter 3.2|]]; [[2 Peter 2.7|2 Pet. 2:7]]; [[2 Peter 3.11|3:11]]. In [[Philippians 3.20|Philip. 3:20]] it probably means citizenship; and in [[Hebrews 13.5|Heb. 13:5]], disposition.
