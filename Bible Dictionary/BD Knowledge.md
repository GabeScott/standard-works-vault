# Knowledge

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/knowledge?lang=eng)

One of the attributes of God ([[Isaiah 46.9|Isa. 46:9–10]][[Isaiah 46.10|]]; [[Acts 15.18|Acts 15:18]]; [[2 Nephi 9.20|2 Ne. 9:20]]). Knowledge of divine and spiritual things is absolutely essential for one’s salvation; hence the gospel is to be taught to every soul. “How shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard?” ([[Romans 10.14|Rom. 10:14]]). Knowledge is not obtained all at once, even by revelation, but line upon line, precept upon precept ([[Isaiah 28.9|Isa. 28:9–10]][[Isaiah 28.10|]]). The scriptures, and also living prophets, are given so that the people might have knowledge of things of God and “know how to worship, and know what you worship” ([[D&C 93.19|D&C 93:19]]). Knowledge is one of the endowments of the Holy Ghost ([[John 14.26|John 14:26]]; [[John 16.13|16:13]]; [[D&C 34.10|D&C 34:10]]; [[D&C 121.26|121:26–33]][[D&C 121.27|]][[D&C 121.28|]][[D&C 121.29|]][[D&C 121.30|]][[D&C 121.31|]][[D&C 121.32|]][[D&C 121.33|]]) and one of the gifts of the Spirit ([[1 Corinthians 12.8|1 Cor. 12:8]]; [[Moroni 10.10|Moro. 10:10]]; [[D&C 46.18|D&C 46:18]]). Peter lists knowledge along with faith, patience, kindness, and virtue as necessary acquisitions for one who would seek for a divine nature ([[2 Peter 1.3|2 Pet. 1:3–9]][[2 Peter 1.4|]][[2 Peter 1.5|]][[2 Peter 1.6|]][[2 Peter 1.7|]][[2 Peter 1.8|]][[2 Peter 1.9|]]). Since no one can be saved in ignorance of the gospel ([[D&C 131.6|D&C 131:6]]), and one progresses only as fast as he gains knowledge ( 4:588), it follows that the person who gains knowledge will have “the advantage in the world to come” ([[D&C 130.18|D&C 130:18–19]][[D&C 130.19|]]).
