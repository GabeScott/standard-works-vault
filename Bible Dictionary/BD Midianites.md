# Midianites

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/midianites?lang=eng)

A powerful confederation of wandering Arab tribes, akin to the Hebrews, but often in conflict with them. They are referred to in [[Genesis 37.28|Gen. 37:28, 36]][[Genesis 37.36|]]; [[Exodus 2.15|Ex. 2:15]]; [[Exodus 3.1|3:1]]; [[Exodus 4.19|4:19]]; [[Exodus 18.1|18:1]]; [[Numbers 22.4|Num. 22:4, 7]][[Numbers 22.7|]]; [[Numbers 25.1|25]]; [[Numbers 31.1|31]]; [[Judges 6.1|Judg. 6–8]]; [[Psalms 83.9|Ps. 83:9, 11]][[Psalms 83.11|]]; [[Isaiah 9.4|Isa. 9:4]]; [[Isaiah 10.26|10:26]]. See also *[[BD Jethro|Jethro]].*
