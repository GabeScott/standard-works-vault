# Ananias

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/ananias?lang=eng)

(1) Punished for lying ([[Acts 5.1|Acts 5:1–6]][[Acts 5.2|]][[Acts 5.3|]][[Acts 5.4|]][[Acts 5.5|]][[Acts 5.6|]]).

(2) A Christian disciple at Damascus who baptized Paul ([[Acts 9.10|Acts 9:10–18]][[Acts 9.11|]][[Acts 9.12|]][[Acts 9.13|]][[Acts 9.14|]][[Acts 9.15|]][[Acts 9.16|]][[Acts 9.17|]][[Acts 9.18|]]; [[Acts 22.12|22:12]]).

(3) The high priest before whom Paul was brought by Caesar Lysias and who was one of Paul’s accusers at the hearing before Felix ([[Acts 23.2|Acts 23:2–5]][[Acts 23.3|]][[Acts 23.4|]][[Acts 23.5|]]; [[Acts 24.1|24:1]]). An evil person, he was finally murdered by the populace during a disturbance in Jerusalem.
