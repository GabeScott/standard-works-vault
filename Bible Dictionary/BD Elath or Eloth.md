# Elath or Eloth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/elath?lang=eng)

A town of Edom, on the Gulf of Aqabah, one of the “stations” of the Israelites in the wilderness ([[Deuteronomy 2.8|Deut. 2:8]]); a naval station during the monarchy ([[1 Kings 9.26|1 Kgs. 9:26]]; [[2 Kings 8.20|2 Kgs. 8:20]]; [[2 Kings 14.22|14:22]]; [[2 Kings 16.6|16:6]]).
