# Sepharvaim

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/sepharvaim?lang=eng)

A place, either in the north of Babylon or in north Syria, whence the king of Assyria brought men to the cities of Samaria ([[2 Kings 17.24|2 Kgs. 17:24, 31]][[2 Kings 17.31|]]; see also [[2 Kings 18.34|18:34]]; [[2 Kings 19.13|19:13]]; [[Isaiah 36.19|Isa. 36:19]]; [[Isaiah 37.13|37:13]]).
