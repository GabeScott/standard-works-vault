# Ishmael

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/ishmael?lang=eng)

*God heareth.* Son of Abraham and Hagar the Egyptian ([[Genesis 16.11|Gen. 16:11–16]][[Genesis 16.12|]][[Genesis 16.13|]][[Genesis 16.14|]][[Genesis 16.15|]][[Genesis 16.16|]]; [[Genesis 17.18|17:18–26]][[Genesis 17.19|]][[Genesis 17.20|]][[Genesis 17.21|]][[Genesis 17.22|]][[Genesis 17.23|]][[Genesis 17.24|]][[Genesis 17.25|]][[Genesis 17.26|]]). He was the child “born after the flesh” ([[Galatians 4.23|Gal. 4:23]]) and not the child “by promise” and was sent from home after mocking Isaac ([[Genesis 21.8|Gen. 21:8–21]][[Genesis 21.9|]][[Genesis 21.10|]][[Genesis 21.11|]][[Genesis 21.12|]][[Genesis 21.13|]][[Genesis 21.14|]][[Genesis 21.15|]][[Genesis 21.16|]][[Genesis 21.17|]][[Genesis 21.18|]][[Genesis 21.19|]][[Genesis 21.20|]][[Genesis 21.21|]]; see also [[Genesis 25.9|25:9–17]][[Genesis 25.10|]][[Genesis 25.11|]][[Genesis 25.12|]][[Genesis 25.13|]][[Genesis 25.14|]][[Genesis 25.15|]][[Genesis 25.16|]][[Genesis 25.17|]]; [[Genesis 28.9|28:9]]; [[Genesis 36.3|36:3]]). His descendants, the Ishmaelites, appear to have been a wandering race ([[Genesis 16.12|16:12]]), living by plunder, having commercial relations and intermarrying with the heathen nations of Canaan ([[Genesis 37.25|Gen. 37:25–28]][[Genesis 37.26|]][[Genesis 37.27|]][[Genesis 37.28|]]; [[Genesis 39.1|39:1]]; [[Judges 8.24|Judg. 8:24]]; [[Psalms 83.6|Ps. 83:6]]; see also [[Galatians 4.1|Gal. 4:21–5:1]]).
