# Rehoboth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/rehoboth?lang=eng)

*Broad places.* (1) A city built by Nimrod, probably the northeastern suburb of Nineveh ([[Genesis 10.11|Gen. 10:11]]).

(2) A well dug by Isaac ([[Genesis 26.22|Gen. 26:22]]).

(3) A town on the Euphrates ([[Genesis 36.37|Gen. 36:37]]; [[1 Chronicles 1.48|1 Chr. 1:48]]).
