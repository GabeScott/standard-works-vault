# Ramoth-gilead

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/ramoth-gilead?lang=eng)

*Heights of Gilead.* A city of refuge, and (under Solomon) the seat of the governor of a province. Probably the most important Israelite city east of Jordan; identified with the modern Es Salt. See [[1 Kings 4.13|1 Kgs. 4:13]]; [[1 Kings 22.1|22]]; [[2 Kings 8.28|2 Kgs. 8:28]]; [[2 Kings 9.1|9:1, 4, 14]][[2 Kings 9.4|]][[2 Kings 9.14|]]; [[2 Chronicles 18.1|2 Chr. 18]]; [[2 Chronicles 22.5|22:5]].
