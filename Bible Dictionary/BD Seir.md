# Seir

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/seir?lang=eng)

*Shaggy.* (1) The Horite ([[Genesis 36.20|Gen. 36:20–21]][[Genesis 36.21|]]; [[1 Chronicles 1.38|1 Chr. 1:38]]).

(2) Mount Seir, or land of Seir; the mountain ridge that ran south from the Dead Sea through the land of Edom; originally inhabited by Horites ([[Genesis 14.6|Gen. 14:6]]); Esau’s possession ([[Genesis 36.8|Gen. 36:8–9, 30]][[Genesis 36.9|]][[Genesis 36.30|]]; [[Deuteronomy 2.5|Deut. 2:5]]; [[Joshua 24.4|Josh. 24:4]]); passed through by Jacob ([[Genesis 32.3|Gen. 32:3]]; [[Genesis 33.14|33:14, 16]][[Genesis 33.16|]]); by the Israelites ([[Deuteronomy 1.44|Deut. 1:44]]; [[Deuteronomy 2.1|2:1]]; [[2 Chronicles 20.10|2 Chr. 20:10]]; see also [[Isaiah 21.11|Isa. 21:11]]; [[Ezekiel 25.8|Ezek. 25:8]]; [[Ezekiel 35.1|35]]).

(3) Another Mount Seir, between Kirjath-jearim and Beth-shemesh ([[Joshua 15.10|Josh. 15:10]]).
