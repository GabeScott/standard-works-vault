# Rimmon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/rimmon?lang=eng)

(1) God of the Syrians of Damascus ([[2 Kings 5.18|2 Kgs. 5:18]]), identified by them with the sun god Hadad ([[Zechariah 12.11|Zech. 12:11]]). Among the Babylonians he was the god of the air, wind, thunder, and rain.

(2) *Pomegranate.* A Beerothite, father of Baanah and Rechab, the murderers of Ishbosheth ([[2 Samuel 4.2|2 Sam. 4:2, 5, 9]][[2 Samuel 4.5|]][[2 Samuel 4.9|]]).

(3) A place in Judah in the Negev ([[Joshua 15.32|Josh. 15:32]]; [[1 Chronicles 4.32|1 Chr. 4:32]]; [[Zechariah 14.10|Zech. 14:10]]).

(4) The rock Rimmon, in the wilderness of Benjamin, about 3½ miles east of Bethel ([[Judges 20.45|Judg. 20:45, 47]][[Judges 20.47|]]; [[Judges 21.13|21:13]]).
