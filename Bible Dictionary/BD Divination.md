# Divination

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/divination?lang=eng)

Any superstitious method of trying to discover the course of future events. Such practices have been found among all nations and in every age; they are frequently condemned in scripture ([[Leviticus 19.31|Lev. 19:31]]; [[Leviticus 20.6|20:6]]; [[Deuteronomy 18.10|Deut. 18:10]]; [[2 Kings 21.6|2 Kgs. 21:6]]; [[Isaiah 8.19|Isa. 8:19]]; [[Isaiah 19.3|19:3]]; [[Acts 8.9|Acts 8:9]]; [[Acts 13.6|13:6, 8]][[Acts 13.8|]]; [[Acts 16.16|16:16]]).
