# Zin

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/zin?lang=eng)

The site is unknown, but the use of the name “wilderness of Zin” shows it must have been near Kadesh-barnea in the extreme south of Palestine ([[Numbers 13.21|Num. 13:21]]; [[Numbers 20.1|20:1]]; [[Numbers 27.14|27:14]]; [[Numbers 33.36|33:36]]; [[Numbers 34.3|34:3–4]][[Numbers 34.4|]]; [[Deuteronomy 32.51|Deut. 32:51]]; [[Joshua 15.1|Josh. 15:1, 3]][[Joshua 15.3|]]).
