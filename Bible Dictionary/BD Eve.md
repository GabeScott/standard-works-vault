# Eve

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/eve?lang=eng)

The name given in [[Genesis 2.21|Gen. 2:21–22]][[Genesis 2.22|]]; [[Genesis 3.20|3:20]] to the first woman; see also [[Genesis 3.1|Gen. 3]]; [[Genesis 4.1|4:1]]; [[2 Corinthians 11.3|2 Cor. 11:3]]; [[1 Timothy 2.13|1 Tim. 2:13]]. The name means “the mother of all living” ([[Moses 4.26|Moses 4:26]]; see also [[1 Nephi 5.11|1 Ne. 5:11]]). She was the wife of Adam and will share eternal glory with him. Eve’s recognition of the necessity of the Fall and the joys of Redemption is recorded in [[Moses 5.11|Moses 5:11]]. Latter-day revelation confirms the biblical account of Eve and gives us an awareness of her nobility.
