# Korah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/korah?lang=eng)

(1) Son of Esau, and one of the dukes of Edom ([[Genesis 36.5|Gen. 36:5]]).

(2) A grandson of Esau ([[Genesis 36.16|36:16]]).

(3) Son of Hebron ([[1 Chronicles 2.43|1 Chr. 2:43]]).

(4) Son of Izhar, grandson of Kohath, and great-grandson of Levi ([[Exodus 6.21|Ex. 6:21, 24]][[Exodus 6.24|]]), the leader of the rebellion against Moses and Aaron ([[Numbers 16.1|Num. 16]]; [[Numbers 26.9|26:9–11]][[Numbers 26.10|]][[Numbers 26.11|]]; [[Jude 1.11|Jude 1:11]]). His descendants, the Korahites, or Korathites, formed a guild of musicians ([[2 Chronicles 20.19|2 Chr. 20:19]]; titles of [[Psalms 42.1|Ps. 42]]; [[Psalms 44.1|44–49]]; [[Psalms 84.1|84–85]]; [[Psalms 87.1|87–88]]), while others held the responsible post of doorkeeper (porter in KJV) of the temple ([[1 Chronicles 9.17|1 Chr. 9:17–19]][[1 Chronicles 9.18|]][[1 Chronicles 9.19|]]).
