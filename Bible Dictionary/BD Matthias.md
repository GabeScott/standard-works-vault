# Matthias

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/matthias?lang=eng)

*Gift of Jehovah.* The person chosen to fill the place of Judas ([[Acts 1.15|Acts 1:15–26]][[Acts 1.16|]][[Acts 1.17|]][[Acts 1.18|]][[Acts 1.19|]][[Acts 1.20|]][[Acts 1.21|]][[Acts 1.22|]][[Acts 1.23|]][[Acts 1.24|]][[Acts 1.25|]][[Acts 1.26|]]). All that is known of him is that he was a disciple throughout the whole of our Lord’s ministry ([[Acts 1.21|1:21–22]][[Acts 1.22|]]); tradition states that he preached the gospel and suffered martyrdom in Ethiopia.
