# Cherubim

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/cherubim?lang=eng)

Figures representing heavenly creatures, the exact form being unknown. They are found in the Holy of Holies, on the Mercy Seat of the Ark ([[Exodus 25.18|Ex. 25:18, 22]][[Exodus 25.22|]]; [[1 Kings 6.23|1 Kgs. 6:23–28]][[1 Kings 6.24|]][[1 Kings 6.25|]][[1 Kings 6.26|]][[1 Kings 6.27|]][[1 Kings 6.28|]]; [[Hebrews 9.5|Heb. 9:5]]), and in the visions of Ezekiel ([[Ezekiel 10.1|Ezek. 10]]; [[Ezekiel 11.22|11:22]]). In the account of the Fall, cherubim are represented as keeping “the way of the tree of life” ([[Genesis 3.24|Gen. 3:24]]).
