# Machpelah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/machpelah?lang=eng)

*Doubling.* The field and cave bought by Abraham ([[Genesis 23.9|Gen. 23:9, 17]][[Genesis 23.17|]]), where were buried Sarah ([[Genesis 23.19|23:19]]), Abraham ([[Genesis 25.9|25:9]]), Isaac, Rebekah, and Leah ([[Genesis 49.30|49:30–31]][[Genesis 49.31|]]), and Jacob ([[Genesis 50.13|50:13]]). It was situated on the top of the hill on which Hebron was built. The traditional site is now occupied by a mosque.
