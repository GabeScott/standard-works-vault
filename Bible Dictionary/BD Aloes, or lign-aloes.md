# Aloes, or lign-aloes

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/aloes?lang=eng)

Some aromatic and highly prized wood is indicated ([[Numbers 24.6|Num. 24:6]]; [[Psalms 45.8|Ps. 45:8]]; [[Proverbs 7.17|Prov. 7:17]]; [[Song of Solomon 4.14|Song 4:14]]). A mixture of myrrh and aloes was used for embalming ([[John 19.39|John 19:39]]).
