# Gad

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/gad?lang=eng)

*(Good) fortune.* (1) Son of Jacob by Zilpah ([[Genesis 30.11|Gen. 30:11]]). For the blessing pronounced on the tribe of Gad, see [[Genesis 49.19|Gen. 49:19]] and [[Deuteronomy 33.20|Deut. 33:20–21]][[Deuteronomy 33.21|]], where the warlike character of the Gadites is emphasized. The children of Gad along with the Reubenites were owners of cattle, and for that reason grazing land was allotted to them in Canaan ([[Numbers 32.1|Num. 32:1–5]][[Numbers 32.2|]][[Numbers 32.3|]][[Numbers 32.4|]][[Numbers 32.5|]]). Their district lay east of the Jordan and contained good arable pasture land, with an abundance of water. It was given them on condition they went armed before their brethren ([[Numbers 32.1|Num. 32]][[Numbers 32.2|]][[Numbers 32.3|]][[Numbers 32.4|]][[Numbers 32.5|]][[Numbers 32.6|]][[Numbers 32.7|]][[Numbers 32.8|]][[Numbers 32.9|]][[Numbers 32.10|]][[Numbers 32.11|]][[Numbers 32.12|]][[Numbers 32.13|]][[Numbers 32.14|]][[Numbers 32.15|]][[Numbers 32.16|]][[Numbers 32.17|]][[Numbers 32.18|]][[Numbers 32.19|]][[Numbers 32.20|]][[Numbers 32.21|]][[Numbers 32.22|]][[Numbers 32.23|]][[Numbers 32.24|]][[Numbers 32.25|]][[Numbers 32.26|]][[Numbers 32.27|]][[Numbers 32.28|]][[Numbers 32.29|]][[Numbers 32.30|]][[Numbers 32.31|]][[Numbers 32.32|]][[Numbers 32.33|]][[Numbers 32.34|]][[Numbers 32.35|]][[Numbers 32.36|]][[Numbers 32.37|]][[Numbers 32.38|]][[Numbers 32.39|]][[Numbers 32.40|]][[Numbers 32.41|]][[Numbers 32.42|]]; [[Numbers 34.14|34:14]]; [[Deuteronomy 3.12|Deut. 3:12–20]][[Deuteronomy 3.13|]][[Deuteronomy 3.14|]][[Deuteronomy 3.15|]][[Deuteronomy 3.16|]][[Deuteronomy 3.17|]][[Deuteronomy 3.18|]][[Deuteronomy 3.19|]][[Deuteronomy 3.20|]]; [[Deuteronomy 29.8|29:8]]; [[Joshua 1.12|Josh. 1:12–18]][[Joshua 1.13|]][[Joshua 1.14|]][[Joshua 1.15|]][[Joshua 1.16|]][[Joshua 1.17|]][[Joshua 1.18|]]; [[Joshua 4.12|4:12]]). Their boundaries are described ([[Joshua 12.1|Josh. 12:1–6]][[Joshua 12.2|]][[Joshua 12.3|]][[Joshua 12.4|]][[Joshua 12.5|]][[Joshua 12.6|]]; [[Joshua 13.8|13:8–12]][[Joshua 13.9|]][[Joshua 13.10|]][[Joshua 13.11|]][[Joshua 13.12|]]). After the wars they were sent home with a blessing and built an altar, which caused offense ([[Joshua 22.1|Josh. 22]][[Joshua 22.2|]][[Joshua 22.3|]][[Joshua 22.4|]][[Joshua 22.5|]][[Joshua 22.6|]][[Joshua 22.7|]][[Joshua 22.8|]][[Joshua 22.9|]][[Joshua 22.10|]][[Joshua 22.11|]][[Joshua 22.12|]][[Joshua 22.13|]][[Joshua 22.14|]][[Joshua 22.15|]][[Joshua 22.16|]][[Joshua 22.17|]][[Joshua 22.18|]][[Joshua 22.19|]][[Joshua 22.20|]][[Joshua 22.21|]][[Joshua 22.22|]][[Joshua 22.23|]][[Joshua 22.24|]][[Joshua 22.25|]][[Joshua 22.26|]][[Joshua 22.27|]][[Joshua 22.28|]][[Joshua 22.29|]][[Joshua 22.30|]][[Joshua 22.31|]][[Joshua 22.32|]][[Joshua 22.33|]][[Joshua 22.34|]]). The Gadites, who were brave and warlike ([[1 Chronicles 5.11|1 Chr. 5:11–22]][[1 Chronicles 5.12|]][[1 Chronicles 5.13|]][[1 Chronicles 5.14|]][[1 Chronicles 5.15|]][[1 Chronicles 5.16|]][[1 Chronicles 5.17|]][[1 Chronicles 5.18|]][[1 Chronicles 5.19|]][[1 Chronicles 5.20|]][[1 Chronicles 5.21|]][[1 Chronicles 5.22|]]; [[1 Chronicles 12.8|12:8, 14]][[1 Chronicles 12.14|]]), were sometimes known as Gileadites ([[Judges 5.17|Judg. 5:17]]; [[Judges 12.4|12:4]]). Their land was invaded, and they were carried captive by Tiglath-pileser ([[2 Kings 10.33|2 Kgs. 10:33]]; [[1 Chronicles 5.26|1 Chr. 5:26]]).

(2) The seer. A prophet and faithful friend and adviser of David ([[1 Samuel 22.5|1 Sam. 22:5]]; [[2 Samuel 24.11|2 Sam. 24:11–19]][[2 Samuel 24.12|]][[2 Samuel 24.13|]][[2 Samuel 24.14|]][[2 Samuel 24.15|]][[2 Samuel 24.16|]][[2 Samuel 24.17|]][[2 Samuel 24.18|]][[2 Samuel 24.19|]]; [[1 Chronicles 21.9|1 Chr. 21:9–19]][[1 Chronicles 21.10|]][[1 Chronicles 21.11|]][[1 Chronicles 21.12|]][[1 Chronicles 21.13|]][[1 Chronicles 21.14|]][[1 Chronicles 21.15|]][[1 Chronicles 21.16|]][[1 Chronicles 21.17|]][[1 Chronicles 21.18|]][[1 Chronicles 21.19|]]). He wrote a book of the acts of David ([[1 Chronicles 29.29|1 Chr. 29:29]]) and assisted in the arrangement of the music of the house of God ([[2 Chronicles 29.25|2 Chr. 29:25]]).
