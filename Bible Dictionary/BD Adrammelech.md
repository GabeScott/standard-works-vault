# Adrammelech

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/adrammelech?lang=eng)

*Splendor of the king,* or *fire-king.* (1) Name of an idol, probably representing the sun ([[2 Kings 17.31|2 Kgs. 17:31]]).

(2) Son of Sennacherib ([[2 Kings 19.37|2 Kgs. 19:37]]; [[Isaiah 37.38|Isa. 37:38]]).
