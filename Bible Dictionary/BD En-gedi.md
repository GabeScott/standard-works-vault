# En-gedi

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/en-gedi?lang=eng)

*Fountain of the kid.* A place on the western shore of the Dead Sea ([[Joshua 15.62|Josh. 15:62]]; [[2 Chronicles 20.2|2 Chr. 20:2]]; [[Song of Solomon 1.14|Song 1:14]]; [[Ezekiel 47.10|Ezek. 47:10]]). David dwelt in the “strong holds at En-gedi” ([[1 Samuel 23.29|1 Sam. 23:29]]; [[1 Samuel 24.1|24:1]]).
