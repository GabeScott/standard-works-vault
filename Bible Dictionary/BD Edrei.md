# Edrei

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/edrei?lang=eng)

(1) A city of Og, king of Bashan, east of the river Jordan, in territory allotted to the half-tribe of Manasseh ([[Numbers 21.33|Num. 21:33]]; [[Deuteronomy 1.4|Deut. 1:4]]; [[Deuteronomy 3.1|3:1, 10]][[Deuteronomy 3.10|]]; [[Joshua 12.4|Josh. 12:4]]; [[Joshua 13.12|13:12, 31]][[Joshua 13.31|]]). Modern explorations have discovered on the ancient site (now called Edraʼah) an elaborately contrived subterranean city, with streets, shops, and marketplace.

(2) A town allotted to Naphtali ([[Joshua 19.37|Josh. 19:37]]).
