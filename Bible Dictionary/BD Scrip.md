# Scrip

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/scrip?lang=eng)

A bag used by shepherds or by travelers ([[1 Samuel 17.40|1 Sam. 17:40]]; [[Matthew 10.10|Matt. 10:10]]; [[Mark 6.8|Mark 6:8]]; [[Luke 9.3|Luke 9:3]]; [[Luke 10.4|10:4]]; [[Luke 22.35|22:35–36]][[Luke 22.36|]]). The bag was usually made of leather and was used for carrying bread and other food. It should not be confused with a money bag, which was called a purse.
