# Mizraim

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/mizraim?lang=eng)

The Hebrew name for Egypt. Mizraim was the son of Ham, son of Noah ([[Genesis 10.6|Gen. 10:6]]). He was the ancestor of the Hamitic people of lower Egypt and Africa and of the Hamitic people of Canaan and several associated peoples ([[Genesis 10.13|Gen. 10:13–20]][[Genesis 10.14|]][[Genesis 10.15|]][[Genesis 10.16|]][[Genesis 10.17|]][[Genesis 10.18|]][[Genesis 10.19|]][[Genesis 10.20|]]).
