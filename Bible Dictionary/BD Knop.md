# Knop

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/knop?lang=eng)

A bud, especially a rosebud; hence a knob or spherical ornament on the stem of the golden lampstand and under the brim of the molten sea in the temple ([[Exodus 25.31|Ex. 25:31]]; [[1 Kings 7.24|1 Kgs. 7:24]]).
