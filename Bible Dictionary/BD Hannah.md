# Hannah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hannah?lang=eng)

*Grace.* Mother of Samuel, who was given to her in answer to prayer ([[1 Samuel 1.1|1 Sam. 1:2–2:21]]). Her song of thanksgiving may be compared with that of Mary ([[Luke 1.46|Luke 1:46–55]][[Luke 1.47|]][[Luke 1.48|]][[Luke 1.49|]][[Luke 1.50|]][[Luke 1.51|]][[Luke 1.52|]][[Luke 1.53|]][[Luke 1.54|]][[Luke 1.55|]]). It contains the first reference to the title Messiah (“his Anointed”) ([[1 Samuel 2.10|1 Sam. 2:10]]).
