# Gold

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/gold?lang=eng)

Found in the land of Havilah ([[Genesis 2.11|Gen. 2:11]]); used in the tabernacle ([[Exodus 24.1|Ex. 24–40]]), in the temple ([[1 Kings 6.1|1 Kgs. 6–9]]), and in Solomon’s house ([[1 Kings 10.16|1 Kgs. 10:16–21]][[1 Kings 10.17|]][[1 Kings 10.18|]][[1 Kings 10.19|]][[1 Kings 10.20|]][[1 Kings 10.21|]]). Many instances are given in the Old Testament of its use for purposes of ornament, Arabia, Sheba, and Ophir being the countries from which it was brought ([[1 Kings 9.28|1 Kgs. 9:28]]; [[1 Kings 10.2|10:2]]). It is figuratively mentioned in [[2 Timothy 2.20|2 Tim. 2:20]]; [[Revelation 3.18|Rev. 3:18]]; [[Revelation 14.14|14:14]]; [[Revelation 21.15|21:15, 18]][[Revelation 21.18|]]. It formed part of the offering made by the “wise men” to the infant Savior ([[Matthew 2.11|Matt. 2:11]]).
