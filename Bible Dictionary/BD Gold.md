# Gold

Many instances are given in the Old Testament of its use for purposes of ornament, Arabia, Sheba, and Ophir being the countries from which it was brought ([[1 Kings 9.28|1 Kgs. 9:28]]; [[1 Kings 10.2|10:2]]). It is figuratively mentioned in [[2 Timothy 2.20|2 Tim. 2:20]]; [[Revelation 3.18|Rev. 3:18]]; [[Revelation 14.14|14:14]]; [[Revelation 21.15|21:15, 18]][[Revelation 21.18|]]. It formed part of the offering made by the “wise men” to the infant Savior ([[Matthew 2.11|Matt. 2:11]]).
