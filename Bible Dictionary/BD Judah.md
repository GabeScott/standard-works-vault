# Judah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/judah?lang=eng)

Fourth son of Jacob and Leah ([[Genesis 29.35|Gen. 29:35]]; [[Genesis 37.26|37:26–27]][[Genesis 37.27|]]; [[Genesis 43.3|43:3, 8]][[Genesis 43.8|]]; [[Genesis 44.16|44:16]]; [[Genesis 49.8|49:8–12]][[Genesis 49.9|]][[Genesis 49.10|]][[Genesis 49.11|]][[Genesis 49.12|]]; [[Deuteronomy 33.7|Deut. 33:7]]). From these passages we see that he was a natural leader among the sons of Jacob, and the tribe descended from him took the lead after the settlement in Canaan, its chief rival being Ephraim.
