# Rainbow

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/rainbow?lang=eng)

An outward token of God’s covenant with Noah ([[Genesis 9.13|Gen. 9:13]]; see also [[Ezekiel 1.28|Ezek. 1:28]]; [[Revelation 4.3|Rev. 4:3]]; [[Revelation 10.1|10:1]]). The rainbow no doubt existed *before* the Flood, but with Noah the rainbow took on a new significance as the token of the covenant.  Gen. 9:17–25 enlarges the terms of the covenant to include not only that the earth will never again be covered with a flood of water, but also that the Zion of Enoch will return and the Lord will come again to dwell on the earth (see [[Genesis 9.11|Gen. 9:11 note c]]*,* [[Genesis 9.15|15 note b]]*;* [[JST Genesis 9.21|JST Gen. 9:21–25 (Appendix)]][[JST Genesis 9.22|]][[JST Genesis 9.23|]][[JST Genesis 9.24|]][[JST Genesis 9.25|]]).
