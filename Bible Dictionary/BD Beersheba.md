# Beersheba

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/beersheba?lang=eng)

*Well of the oath.* The home of Abraham ([[Genesis 21.31|Gen. 21:31]]), Isaac ([[Genesis 26.23|26:23]]), and Jacob ([[Genesis 28.10|28:10]]); so called from the covenant Abraham made here with Abimelech, king of Gerar ([[Genesis 21.31|21:31]]). It was afterwards in the tribe of Simeon ([[Joshua 19.2|Josh. 19:2]]) and was regarded as the southern limit of Israel ([[Judges 20.1|Judg. 20:1]]; [[1 Samuel 3.20|1 Sam. 3:20]]). The district contained much rich pasture land.
