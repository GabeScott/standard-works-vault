# Lamb of God

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/lamb-of-god?lang=eng)

A name for the Savior used by John the Baptist and others ([[John 1.29|John 1:29]]). It has reference to Jesus being a sacrifice for the sins of mankind. See also [[1 Corinthians 5.7|1 Cor. 5:7]]; [[1 Peter 1.19|1 Pet. 1:19]].
