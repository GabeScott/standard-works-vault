# Aceldama

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/aceldama?lang=eng)

*Field of blood.* The popular name for the field bought with the money returned to the chief priests by Judas ([[Matthew 27.3|Matt. 27:3–10]][[Matthew 27.4|]][[Matthew 27.5|]][[Matthew 27.6|]][[Matthew 27.7|]][[Matthew 27.8|]][[Matthew 27.9|]][[Matthew 27.10|]]). From [[Acts 1.18|Acts 1:18–19]][[Acts 1.19|]] it appears to have been so called as being the scene of his death. It was afterwards used as a buryingplace for “strangers” (Jews of the dispersion and proselytes).
