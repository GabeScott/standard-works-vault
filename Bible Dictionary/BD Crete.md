# Crete

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/crete?lang=eng)

An island south of the Greek Archipelago, visited by Paul on his voyage to Rome ([[Acts 27.7|Acts 27:7–21]][[Acts 27.8|]][[Acts 27.9|]][[Acts 27.10|]][[Acts 27.11|]][[Acts 27.12|]][[Acts 27.13|]][[Acts 27.14|]][[Acts 27.15|]][[Acts 27.16|]][[Acts 27.17|]][[Acts 27.18|]][[Acts 27.19|]][[Acts 27.20|]][[Acts 27.21|]]) and possibly again, later on, between his first and second imprisonments ([[Titus 1.5|Titus 1:5]]). The Cretans bore a bad reputation ([[Titus 1.12|Titus 1:12]]).
