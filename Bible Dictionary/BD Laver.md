# Laver

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/laver?lang=eng)

A vessel containing water for the priests to wash their hands and feet before offering sacrifice. In the tabernacle it stood between the altar and the door ([[Exodus 30.17|Ex. 30:17–21]][[Exodus 30.18|]][[Exodus 30.19|]][[Exodus 30.20|]][[Exodus 30.21|]]). In Solomon’s temple, besides the molten sea, there were 10 lavers on wheels, with bases ([[1 Kings 7.27|1 Kgs. 7:27–39]][[1 Kings 7.28|]][[1 Kings 7.29|]][[1 Kings 7.30|]][[1 Kings 7.31|]][[1 Kings 7.32|]][[1 Kings 7.33|]][[1 Kings 7.34|]][[1 Kings 7.35|]][[1 Kings 7.36|]][[1 Kings 7.37|]][[1 Kings 7.38|]][[1 Kings 7.39|]]), used for washing the animals that were to be sacrificed ([[2 Chronicles 4.6|2 Chr. 4:6]]).
