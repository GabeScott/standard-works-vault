# Jerubbesheth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jerubbesheth?lang=eng)

Also the name of Gideon ([[2 Samuel 11.21|2 Sam. 11:21]]), formed from the name Jerubbaal ([[Judges 6.32|Judg. 6:32]]), just as Ishbosheth from Eshbaal, *bosheth* (shame) being substituted for *baal* through unwillingness to pronounce the name of the heathen god.
