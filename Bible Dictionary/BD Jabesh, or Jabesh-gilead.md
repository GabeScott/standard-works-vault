# Jabesh, or Jabesh-gilead

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jabesh?lang=eng)

*Jabesh* means “dry.” A chief city of Gilead, east of the Jordan, connected with several important events of Israelite history ([[Judges 21.8|Judg. 21:8–14]][[Judges 21.9|]][[Judges 21.10|]][[Judges 21.11|]][[Judges 21.12|]][[Judges 21.13|]][[Judges 21.14|]]; [[1 Samuel 11.1|1 Sam. 11]]; [[1 Samuel 31.11|31:11–13]][[1 Samuel 31.12|]][[1 Samuel 31.13|]]; [[2 Samuel 2.4|2 Sam. 2:4–5]][[2 Samuel 2.5|]]; [[2 Samuel 21.12|21:12]]; [[2 Samuel 15.10|15:10–14]][[2 Samuel 15.11|]][[2 Samuel 15.12|]][[2 Samuel 15.13|]][[2 Samuel 15.14|]]). It was the men of Jabesh-gilead who took Saul’s body down from the walls of Beth-shan and buried it under an oak tree in Jabesh-gilead ([[1 Chronicles 10.11|1 Chr. 10:11–12]][[1 Chronicles 10.12|]]).
