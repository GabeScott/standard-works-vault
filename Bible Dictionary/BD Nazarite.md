# Nazarite

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/nazarite?lang=eng)

*A consecrated man.* A man under a vow to abstain from wine, from any cutting of the hair, and any contact with the dead ([[Judges 13.5|Judg. 13:5]]; [[Judges 16.17|16:17]]; [[1 Samuel 1.11|1 Sam. 1:11]]; [[Amos 2.11|Amos 2:11–12]][[Amos 2.12|]]; for full regulations see [[Numbers 6.1|Num. 6]]). The vow might be lifelong, or for a short, definite period.
