# Cana of Galilee

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/cana-of-galilee?lang=eng)

A town within a few miles of Nazareth, exact site of which is uncertain; the scene of Christ’s first miracle ([[John 2.1|John 2:1–11]][[John 2.2|]][[John 2.3|]][[John 2.4|]][[John 2.5|]][[John 2.6|]][[John 2.7|]][[John 2.8|]][[John 2.9|]][[John 2.10|]][[John 2.11|]]), as well as of a subsequent one ([[John 4.46|John 4:46–54]][[John 4.47|]][[John 4.48|]][[John 4.49|]][[John 4.50|]][[John 4.51|]][[John 4.52|]][[John 4.53|]][[John 4.54|]]), and the birthplace of Nathanael ([[John 21.2|John 21:2]]).
