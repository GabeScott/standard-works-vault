# Abaddon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/abaddon?lang=eng)

A Hebrew word found in the Wisdom literature of the Old Testament denoting “the place of the lost” ([[Job 26.6|Job 26:6]]; [[Proverbs 15.11|Prov. 15:11]]; [[Proverbs 27.20|27:20]]), which the King James Version translates as “destruction.” In [[Revelation 9.11|Rev. 9:11]] it is used as a name of the devil. See also *[[BD Apollyon|Apollyon]].*
