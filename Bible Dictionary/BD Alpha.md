# Alpha

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/alpha?lang=eng)

First letter of the Greek alphabet, often used in conjunction with Omega, the last letter, and applied as descriptive names of the all-encompassing nature of Christ, being the first and the last, the beginning and the end ([[Revelation 1.8|Rev. 1:8, 11]][[Revelation 1.11|]]; [[Revelation 21.6|21:6]]; [[Revelation 22.13|22:13]]; [[3 Nephi 9.18|3 Ne. 9:18]]; [[D&C 19.1|D&C 19:1]]; [[D&C 38.1|38:1]]; [[D&C 45.7|45:7]]).
