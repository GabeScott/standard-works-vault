# Sarah or Sarai

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/sarah?lang=eng)

Sarah means “princess.” The wife of Abraham. From [[Genesis 11.1|Gen. 11:29–17:15]] the form of the name used is *Sarai* (which possibly means “contentions,” or more probably is another form of *Sarah*). She was married to Abraham before he left Ur. In her old age she became the mother of Isaac ([[Genesis 21.2|Gen. 21:2]]). She died before her husband and was buried in the cave of Machpelah at Hebron ([[Genesis 23.2|Gen. 23:2]]). See also [[Isaiah 51.2|Isa. 51:2]]; [[Romans 4.19|Rom. 4:19]]; [[Hebrews 11.11|Heb. 11:11]]; [[1 Peter 3.6|1 Pet. 3:6]].
