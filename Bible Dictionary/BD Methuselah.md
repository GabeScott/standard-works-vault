# Methuselah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/methuselah?lang=eng)

Son of Enoch and the longest lived of the patriarchs ([[Genesis 5.21|Gen. 5:21–27]][[Genesis 5.22|]][[Genesis 5.23|]][[Genesis 5.24|]][[Genesis 5.25|]][[Genesis 5.26|]][[Genesis 5.27|]]; [[Luke 3.37|Luke 3:37]]). Some details of his life and ministry are given in latter-day revelation, from which we learn that he was a righteous man, a prophet, and an astronomer and was left on the earth when the city of Enoch was taken to provide a posterity through which Noah would come. See [[D&C 107.50|D&C 107:50, 52–53]][[D&C 107.52|]][[D&C 107.53|]]; [[Moses 8.1|Moses 8:1–3]][[Moses 8.2|]][[Moses 8.3|]].
