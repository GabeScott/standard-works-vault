# Shemaiah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/shemaiah?lang=eng)

*The Lord heareth.* (1) The prophet, forbids Rehoboam to fight against the ten tribes ([[1 Kings 12.22|1 Kgs. 12:22]]; [[2 Chronicles 11.2|2 Chr. 11:2]]); tells how Shishak shall subdue them ([[2 Chronicles 12.5|2 Chr. 12:5, 7]][[2 Chronicles 12.7|]]); writes the Acts of Rehoboam ([[2 Chronicles 12.15|2 Chr. 12:15]]).

(2) Shemaiah who assisted Nehemiah ([[1 Chronicles 3.22|1 Chr. 3:22]]; [[Nehemiah 3.29|Neh. 3:29]]).

(3) [[1 Chronicles 4.37|1 Chr. 4:37]]; perhaps the same as Shimei.
