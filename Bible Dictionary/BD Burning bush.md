# Burning bush

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/burning-bush?lang=eng)

A bush in which the Lord appeared to Moses when He gave him his commission to bring Israel out of Egypt ([[Exodus 3.2|Ex. 3:2–4]][[Exodus 3.3|]][[Exodus 3.4|]]). Although the bush burned, it was not consumed, and this circumstance caught Moses’ attention. The event was referred to by Jesus in speaking to the Sadducees ([[Mark 12.26|Mark 12:26]]; [[Luke 20.37|Luke 20:37]]). Stephen also makes mention of it ([[Acts 7.30|Acts 7:30]]). In latter-day revelation the experience is verified in [[Moses 1.17|Moses 1:17]].
