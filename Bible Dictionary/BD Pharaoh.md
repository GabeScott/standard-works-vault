# Pharaoh

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/pharaoh?lang=eng)

(See *[[BD Egypt|Egypt]].*) The title given to the Egyptian kings; its meaning is “Great House.” Nine or ten different Pharaohs are mentioned in the Old Testament, belonging to several different dynasties. (1) The Pharaoh (probably one of the Hyksos or Shepherd kings) visited by Abram ([[Genesis 12.15|Gen. 12:15–20]][[Genesis 12.16|]][[Genesis 12.17|]][[Genesis 12.18|]][[Genesis 12.19|]][[Genesis 12.20|]]). (2) Pharaoh (also one of the Hyksos) and Joseph ([[Genesis 39.1|Gen. 39–50]][[Genesis 39.2|]][[Genesis 39.3|]][[Genesis 39.4|]][[Genesis 39.5|]][[Genesis 39.6|]][[Genesis 39.7|]][[Genesis 39.8|]][[Genesis 39.9|]][[Genesis 39.10|]][[Genesis 39.11|]][[Genesis 39.12|]][[Genesis 39.13|]][[Genesis 39.14|]][[Genesis 39.15|]][[Genesis 39.16|]][[Genesis 39.17|]][[Genesis 39.18|]][[Genesis 39.19|]][[Genesis 39.20|]][[Genesis 39.21|]][[Genesis 39.22|]][[Genesis 39.23|]]). (3) The Pharaohs of oppression, Seti Ⅰ and Ramses Ⅱ, belonging to the 19th dynasty ([[Exodus 1.1|Ex. 1]][[Exodus 1.2|]][[Exodus 1.3|]][[Exodus 1.4|]][[Exodus 1.5|]][[Exodus 1.6|]][[Exodus 1.7|]][[Exodus 1.8|]][[Exodus 1.9|]][[Exodus 1.10|]][[Exodus 1.11|]][[Exodus 1.12|]][[Exodus 1.13|]][[Exodus 1.14|]][[Exodus 1.15|]][[Exodus 1.16|]][[Exodus 1.17|]][[Exodus 1.18|]][[Exodus 1.19|]][[Exodus 1.20|]][[Exodus 1.21|]][[Exodus 1.22|]]; [[Exodus 2.1|2]][[Exodus 2.2|]][[Exodus 2.3|]][[Exodus 2.4|]][[Exodus 2.5|]][[Exodus 2.6|]][[Exodus 2.7|]][[Exodus 2.8|]][[Exodus 2.9|]][[Exodus 2.10|]][[Exodus 2.11|]][[Exodus 2.12|]][[Exodus 2.13|]][[Exodus 2.14|]][[Exodus 2.15|]][[Exodus 2.16|]][[Exodus 2.17|]][[Exodus 2.18|]][[Exodus 2.19|]][[Exodus 2.20|]][[Exodus 2.21|]][[Exodus 2.22|]][[Exodus 2.23|]][[Exodus 2.24|]][[Exodus 2.25|]]); it is generally believed that the Pharaoh of the Exodus was Mer-ne-ptah, but the discovery of the Tell el-Amarna tablets has thrown doubt on this. (4) The Pharaoh whose daughter Solomon married, perhaps Pi-netchem of the 21st dynasty ([[1 Kings 3.1|1 Kgs. 3:1]]; [[1 Kings 7.8|7:8]]; [[1 Kings 9.16|9:16, 24]][[1 Kings 9.24|]]). (5) Shishak or Sheshonk, of the 22nd dynasty, who befriended Jeroboam. (6) So (Shabakah) with whom Hoshea makes alliance ([[2 Kings 17.4|2 Kgs. 17:4]]). (7) Pharaoh Necho, who defeated Josiah at Megiddo ([[2 Kings 23.29|2 Kgs. 23:29–35]][[2 Kings 23.30|]][[2 Kings 23.31|]][[2 Kings 23.32|]][[2 Kings 23.33|]][[2 Kings 23.34|]][[2 Kings 23.35|]]; [[Jeremiah 25.19|Jer. 25:19]]; [[Jeremiah 46.17|46:17, 25]][[Jeremiah 46.25|]]; [[Jeremiah 47.1|47:1]]). (8) Pharaoh Hophra, who sent an army to the assistance of Zedekiah ([[Jeremiah 37.5|Jer. 37:5–11]][[Jeremiah 37.6|]][[Jeremiah 37.7|]][[Jeremiah 37.8|]][[Jeremiah 37.9|]][[Jeremiah 37.10|]][[Jeremiah 37.11|]]; [[Jeremiah 43.9|43:9]]; [[Ezekiel 17.17|Ezek. 17:17]]; [[Ezekiel 29.2|29:2–3]][[Ezekiel 29.3|]]; [[Ezekiel 30.21|30:21–25]][[Ezekiel 30.22|]][[Ezekiel 30.23|]][[Ezekiel 30.24|]][[Ezekiel 30.25|]]; [[Ezekiel 31.2|31:2, 18]][[Ezekiel 31.18|]]; [[Ezekiel 32.2|32:2, 31–32]][[Ezekiel 32.31|]][[Ezekiel 32.32|]]).
