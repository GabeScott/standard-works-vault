# Glass

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/glass?lang=eng)

Made and used by the Egyptians, Hebrews, and other early races. In the Revelation of John it is an emblem of brightness ([[Revelation 4.6|Rev. 4:6]]; [[Revelation 15.2|15:2]]; [[Revelation 21.18|21:18]]). The “glass” mentioned in the New Testament as an instrument for reflection ([[1 Corinthians 13.12|1 Cor. 13:12]]; [[2 Corinthians 3.18|2 Cor. 3:18]]; [[James 1.23|James 1:23]]) was a mirror made of polished metal.
