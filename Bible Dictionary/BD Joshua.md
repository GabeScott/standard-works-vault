# Joshua

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/joshua?lang=eng)

*God is help.* The name also occurs in the Old Testament under the various forms Jehoshua, Hoshea, Jeshua, and Jesus; son of Nun, and successor of Moses; born in Egypt before the Exodus ([[Numbers 14.26|Num. 14:26–31]][[Numbers 14.27|]][[Numbers 14.28|]][[Numbers 14.29|]][[Numbers 14.30|]][[Numbers 14.31|]]); fought with Amalek ([[Exodus 17.13|Ex. 17:13–14]][[Exodus 17.14|]]); was Moses’ minister ([[Exodus 24.13|24:13]]; [[Exodus 32.17|32:17]]; [[Exodus 33.11|33:11]]); one of the 12 spies ([[Numbers 13.8|Num. 13:8]]); appointed Moses’ successor ([[Numbers 27.18|27:18–19]][[Numbers 27.19|]]; [[Numbers 34.17|34:17]]; see also [[Deuteronomy 1.38|Deut. 1:38]]; [[Deuteronomy 3.28|3:28]]; [[Deuteronomy 31.3|31:3, 23]][[Deuteronomy 31.23|]]; [[Deuteronomy 34.9|34:9]]). The book of Joshua (see below) contains the history of his conquest of Palestine. He died at the age of 110 and was buried in his own city, Timnath-Serah. He is the highest type of the devout warrior.
