# Hem of garment

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hem-of-garment?lang=eng)

An important part of an Israelite’s dress, owing to the regulation in [[Numbers 15.38|Num. 15:38–39]][[Numbers 15.39|]]. It was really a tassel at each “wing” or corner of the *tallith* or mantle ([[Matthew 14.36|Matt. 14:36]]). The law required that it should be bound with a thread of blue, the color of heaven. The strict Jews to this day wear these tassels, though they are usually concealed. The Pharisees made them conspicuously large ([[Matthew 23.5|Matt. 23:5]]). It would be the tassel that hung over the shoulder at the back that the woman with the issue of blood came and touched ([[Luke 8.44|Luke 8:44]]; also [[Exodus 28.33|Ex. 28:33–34]][[Exodus 28.34|]]).
