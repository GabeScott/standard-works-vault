# Sharon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/sharon?lang=eng)

The maritime plain, about 10 miles wide and 50 miles in length, extending from Carmel to Joppa, celebrated for its beauty and its flowers ([[1 Chronicles 5.16|1 Chr. 5:16]]; [[1 Chronicles 27.29|27:29]]; [[Song of Solomon 2.1|Song 2:1]]; [[Isaiah 33.9|Isa. 33:9]]; [[Isaiah 35.2|35:2]]; [[Isaiah 65.10|65:10]]; [[Acts 9.35|Acts 9:35]]).
