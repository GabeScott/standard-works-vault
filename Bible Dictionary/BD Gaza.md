# Gaza

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/gaza?lang=eng)

One of the five cities of the Philistines ([[Genesis 10.19|Gen. 10:19]]; [[Joshua 10.41|Josh. 10:41]]; [[Joshua 11.22|11:22]]; [[Joshua 15.47|15:47]]; [[Judges 1.18|Judg. 1:18]]; [[Judges 6.4|6:4]]). Its gates were carried away by Samson ([[Judges 16.1|Judg. 16:1–3, 21]][[Judges 16.2|]][[Judges 16.3|]][[Judges 16.21|]]; see also [[1 Samuel 6.17|1 Sam. 6:17]]; [[2 Kings 18.8|2 Kgs. 18:8]]). Prophecies concerning Gaza are found in [[Jeremiah 47.1|Jer. 47:1, 5]][[Jeremiah 47.5|]]; [[Amos 1.6|Amos 1:6]]; [[Zephaniah 2.4|Zeph. 2:4]]; [[Zechariah 9.5|Zech. 9:5]]. The name was also spelled *Azzah.*
