# Kohath

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/kohath?lang=eng)

Second of the three sons of Levi, and grandfather of Moses and Aaron ([[Genesis 46.11|Gen. 46:11]]; [[Exodus 6.16|Ex. 6:16, 18]][[Exodus 6.18|]]). From him all priests were descended through Aaron, while those of his descendants who were not priests were known as *Kohathites* and were the Levites of highest rank ([[Numbers 3.27|Num. 3:27, 30]][[Numbers 3.30|]]; [[Numbers 4.18|4:18, 34, 37]][[Numbers 4.34|]][[Numbers 4.37|]]; [[Numbers 10.21|10:21]]; [[1 Chronicles 6.61|1 Chr. 6:61–70]][[1 Chronicles 6.62|]][[1 Chronicles 6.63|]][[1 Chronicles 6.64|]][[1 Chronicles 6.65|]][[1 Chronicles 6.66|]][[1 Chronicles 6.67|]][[1 Chronicles 6.68|]][[1 Chronicles 6.69|]][[1 Chronicles 6.70|]]; [[1 Chronicles 15.5|15:5]]).
