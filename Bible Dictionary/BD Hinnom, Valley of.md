# Hinnom, Valley of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hinnom-valley-of?lang=eng)

South and west of Jerusalem, it was the scene of the idolatrous worship of the fire-god Moloch ([[2 Chronicles 28.3|2 Chr. 28:3]]; [[2 Chronicles 33.6|33:6]]; [[Jeremiah 7.31|Jer. 7:31]]; [[Jeremiah 32.35|32:35]]; see also [[2 Kings 16.3|2 Kgs. 16:3]]; [[2 Kings 21.6|21:6]]), and was afterwards used as a place of refuse ([[2 Kings 23.10|2 Kgs. 23:10–14]][[2 Kings 23.11|]][[2 Kings 23.12|]][[2 Kings 23.13|]][[2 Kings 23.14|]]). In New Testament times it was called Gehenna and was regarded by the Jews as symbolic of the place of torment.
