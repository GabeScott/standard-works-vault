# Deborah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/deborah?lang=eng)

*A bee.* (1) Nurse of Rebekah ([[Genesis 24.59|Gen. 24:59]]; [[Genesis 35.8|35:8]]).

(2) A famous woman who judged Israel and encouraged Barak against Sisera ([[Judges 4.1|Judg. 4]]); the well-known song of Deborah and Barak commemorated the Israelite victory ([[Judges 5.1|Judg. 5]]).
