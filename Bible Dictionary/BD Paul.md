# Paul

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/paul?lang=eng)

The life and work of the great Apostle Paul is recorded at considerable length in the Acts and the epistles. It is only possible to indicate here a few of the chief facts. He was known in early life as Saul; his Latin name Paul is first mentioned at the beginning of his gentile ministry ([[Acts 13.9|Acts 13:9]]). He belonged to Tarsus, in Cilicia ([[Acts 9.11|Acts 9:11]]); was a Pharisee and a pupil of Gamaliel ([[Acts 22.3|Acts 22:3]]); was active in the persecution of Christians ([[Acts 8.3|Acts 8:3]]; [[Acts 26.10|26:10]]; [[Galatians 1.13|Gal. 1:13]]; [[Philippians 3.6|Philip. 3:6]]); and took part in the martyrdom of Stephen ([[Acts 7.58|Acts 7:58]]; [[Acts 8.1|8:1]]). He started for Damascus for the purpose of further persecution ([[Acts 9.1|Acts 9:1]]) and on the road saw a vision of the Lord Jesus, which changed the whole current of his life ([[Acts 9.4|Acts 9:4–19]][[Acts 9.5|]][[Acts 9.6|]][[Acts 9.7|]][[Acts 9.8|]][[Acts 9.9|]][[Acts 9.10|]][[Acts 9.11|]][[Acts 9.12|]][[Acts 9.13|]][[Acts 9.14|]][[Acts 9.15|]][[Acts 9.16|]][[Acts 9.17|]][[Acts 9.18|]][[Acts 9.19|]]; [[Acts 22.7|22:7]]; [[Acts 26.14|26:14]]; [[Galatians 1.15|Gal. 1:15–16]][[Galatians 1.16|]]). After his baptism by Ananias ([[Acts 9.18|Acts 9:18]]), he retired into Arabia ([[Galatians 1.17|Gal. 1:17]]) and then returned to Damascus, where he preached ([[Acts 9.19|Acts 9:19–25]][[Acts 9.20|]][[Acts 9.21|]][[Acts 9.22|]][[Acts 9.23|]][[Acts 9.24|]][[Acts 9.25|]]; [[2 Corinthians 11.32|2 Cor. 11:32]]; [[Galatians 1.17|Gal. 1:17–18]][[Galatians 1.18|]]). Being compelled to flee, about three years after his conversion he went to Jerusalem, where he stayed 15 days, Barnabas introducing him to Peter and James ([[Acts 9.26|Acts 9:26–30]][[Acts 9.27|]][[Acts 9.28|]][[Acts 9.29|]][[Acts 9.30|]]; [[Galatians 1.18|Gal. 1:18–19]][[Galatians 1.19|]]). Being in danger, he retired to Tarsus ([[Acts 9.29|Acts 9:29–30]][[Acts 9.30|]]) and there remained six or seven years, preaching in Syria and Cilicia ([[Galatians 1.21|Gal. 1:21–24]][[Galatians 1.22|]][[Galatians 1.23|]][[Galatians 1.24|]]). He was then brought by Barnabas to Antioch ([[Acts 11.26|Acts 11:26]]) and after one year paid a visit to Jerusalem ([[Acts 11.29|Acts 11:29–30]][[Acts 11.30|]]). After two more years’ work in Antioch, he started with Barnabas and Mark on his first missionary journey ([[Acts 13.1|Acts 13:1–14:26]]). Then came another visit to Jerusalem with Barnabas to attend a conference with the other Apostles ([[Acts 15.1|Acts 15:1–33]][[Acts 15.2|]][[Acts 15.3|]][[Acts 15.4|]][[Acts 15.5|]][[Acts 15.6|]][[Acts 15.7|]][[Acts 15.8|]][[Acts 15.9|]][[Acts 15.10|]][[Acts 15.11|]][[Acts 15.12|]][[Acts 15.13|]][[Acts 15.14|]][[Acts 15.15|]][[Acts 15.16|]][[Acts 15.17|]][[Acts 15.18|]][[Acts 15.19|]][[Acts 15.20|]][[Acts 15.21|]][[Acts 15.22|]][[Acts 15.23|]][[Acts 15.24|]][[Acts 15.25|]][[Acts 15.26|]][[Acts 15.27|]][[Acts 15.28|]][[Acts 15.29|]][[Acts 15.30|]][[Acts 15.31|]][[Acts 15.32|]][[Acts 15.33|]]; [[Galatians 2.1|Gal. 2:1–10]][[Galatians 2.2|]][[Galatians 2.3|]][[Galatians 2.4|]][[Galatians 2.5|]][[Galatians 2.6|]][[Galatians 2.7|]][[Galatians 2.8|]][[Galatians 2.9|]][[Galatians 2.10|]]), after which they returned to Antioch ([[Acts 15.35|Acts 15:35]]). He then started on his second missionary journey ([[Acts 15.1|Acts 15:36–18:22]]), which lasted about three years and ended with a visit to Jerusalem. After a short stay in Antioch, Paul began his third journey, which occupied about 3½ years ([[Acts 18.1|Acts 18:23–21:15]]). On his return to Jerusalem he was arrested and sent to Caesarea ([[Acts 21.1|Acts 21:17–23:35]]), where he remained a prisoner for two years ([[Acts 24.1|Acts 24:1–26:32]]), and was then sent for trial to Rome, suffering shipwreck on the way ([[Acts 27.1|Acts 27:1–28:10]]). He remained in Rome two years ([[Acts 28.30|Acts 28:30]]) and was then released. He then appears to have visited Asia, Macedonia, Crete, and perhaps Spain. At the end of about four years he was again taken a prisoner to Rome and suffered martyrdom, probably in the spring of  65. For an account of his teaching, see *[[BD Pauline Epistles|Pauline Epistles]].*
