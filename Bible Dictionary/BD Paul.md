# Paul

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/paul?lang=eng)

The life and work of the great Apostle Paul is recorded at considerable length in the Acts and the epistles. It is only possible to indicate here a few of the chief facts. He was known in early life as Saul; his Latin name Paul is first mentioned at the beginning of his gentile ministry ([[Acts 13.9|Acts 13:9]]). He belonged to Tarsus, in Cilicia ([[Acts 9.11|Acts 9:11]]); was a Pharisee and a pupil of Gamaliel ([[Acts 22.3|Acts 22:3]]); was active in the persecution of Christians ([[Acts 8.3|Acts 8:3]]; [[Acts 26.10|26:10]]; [[Galatians 1.13|Gal. 1:13]]; [[Philippians 3.6|Philip. 3:6]]); and took part in the martyrdom of Stephen ([[Acts 7.58|Acts 7:58]]; [[Acts 8.1|8:1]]). He started for Damascus for the purpose of further persecution ([[Acts 9.1|Acts 9:1]]) and on the road saw a vision of the Lord Jesus, which changed the whole current of his life ([[Acts 9.4|Acts 9:4–19]][[Acts 9.5|]][[Acts 9.6|]][[Acts 9.7|]][[Acts 9.8|]][[Acts 9.9|]][[Acts 9.10|]][[Acts 9.11|]][[Acts 9.12|]][[Acts 9.13|]][[Acts 9.14|]][[Acts 9.15|]][[Acts 9.16|]][[Acts 9.17|]][[Acts 9.18|]][[Acts 9.19|]]; [[Acts 22.7|22:7]]; [[Acts 26.14|26:14]]; [[Galatians 1.15|Gal. 1:15–16]][[Galatians 1.16|]]). After his baptism by Ananias ([[Acts 9.18|Acts 9:18]]), he retired into Arabia ([[Galatians 1.17|Gal. 1:17]]) and then returned to Damascus, where he preached ([[Acts 9.19|Acts 9:19–25]][[Acts 9.20|]][[Acts 9.21|]][[Acts 9.22|]][[Acts 9.23|]][[Acts 9.24|]][[Acts 9.25|]]; [[2 Corinthians 11.32|2 Cor. 11:32]]; [[Galatians 1.17|Gal. 1:17–18]][[Galatians 1.18|]]). Being compelled to flee, about three years after his conversion he went to Jerusalem, where he stayed 15 days, Barnabas introducing him to Peter and James ([[Acts 9.26|Acts 9:26–30]][[Acts 9.27|]][[Acts 9.28|]][[Acts 9.29|]][[Acts 9.30|]]; [[Galatians 1.18|Gal. 1:18–19]][[Galatians 1.19|]]). Being in danger, he retired to Tarsus ([[Acts 9.29|Acts 9:29–30]][[Acts 9.30|]]) and there remained six or seven years, preaching in Syria and Cilicia ([[Galatians 1.21|Gal. 1:21–24]][[Galatians 1.22|]][[Galatians 1.23|]][[Galatians 1.24|]]). He was then brought by Barnabas to Antioch ([[Acts 11.26|Acts 11:26]]) and after one year paid a visit to Jerusalem ([[Acts 11.29|Acts 11:29–30]][[Acts 11.30|]]). After two more years’ work in Antioch, he started with Barnabas and Mark on his first missionary journey ([[Acts 13.1|Acts 13:1–14:26]][[Acts 13.2|]][[Acts 13.3|]][[Acts 13.4|]][[Acts 13.5|]][[Acts 13.6|]][[Acts 13.7|]][[Acts 13.8|]][[Acts 13.9|]][[Acts 13.10|]][[Acts 13.11|]][[Acts 13.12|]][[Acts 13.13|]][[Acts 13.14|]][[Acts 13.15|]][[Acts 13.16|]][[Acts 13.17|]][[Acts 13.18|]][[Acts 13.19|]][[Acts 13.20|]][[Acts 13.21|]][[Acts 13.22|]][[Acts 13.23|]][[Acts 13.24|]][[Acts 13.25|]][[Acts 13.26|]][[Acts 13.27|]][[Acts 13.28|]][[Acts 13.29|]][[Acts 13.30|]][[Acts 13.31|]][[Acts 13.32|]][[Acts 13.33|]][[Acts 13.34|]][[Acts 13.35|]][[Acts 13.36|]][[Acts 13.37|]][[Acts 13.38|]][[Acts 13.39|]][[Acts 13.40|]][[Acts 13.41|]][[Acts 13.42|]][[Acts 13.43|]][[Acts 13.44|]][[Acts 13.45|]][[Acts 13.46|]][[Acts 13.47|]][[Acts 13.48|]][[Acts 13.49|]][[Acts 13.50|]][[Acts 13.51|]][[Acts 13.52|]]). Then came another visit to Jerusalem with Barnabas to attend a conference with the other Apostles ([[Acts 15.1|Acts 15:1–33]][[Acts 15.2|]][[Acts 15.3|]][[Acts 15.4|]][[Acts 15.5|]][[Acts 15.6|]][[Acts 15.7|]][[Acts 15.8|]][[Acts 15.9|]][[Acts 15.10|]][[Acts 15.11|]][[Acts 15.12|]][[Acts 15.13|]][[Acts 15.14|]][[Acts 15.15|]][[Acts 15.16|]][[Acts 15.17|]][[Acts 15.18|]][[Acts 15.19|]][[Acts 15.20|]][[Acts 15.21|]][[Acts 15.22|]][[Acts 15.23|]][[Acts 15.24|]][[Acts 15.25|]][[Acts 15.26|]][[Acts 15.27|]][[Acts 15.28|]][[Acts 15.29|]][[Acts 15.30|]][[Acts 15.31|]][[Acts 15.32|]][[Acts 15.33|]]; [[Galatians 2.1|Gal. 2:1–10]][[Galatians 2.2|]][[Galatians 2.3|]][[Galatians 2.4|]][[Galatians 2.5|]][[Galatians 2.6|]][[Galatians 2.7|]][[Galatians 2.8|]][[Galatians 2.9|]][[Galatians 2.10|]]), after which they returned to Antioch ([[Acts 15.35|Acts 15:35]]). He then started on his second missionary journey ([[Acts 15.1|Acts 15:36–18:22]][[Acts 15.2|]][[Acts 15.3|]][[Acts 15.4|]][[Acts 15.5|]][[Acts 15.6|]][[Acts 15.7|]][[Acts 15.8|]][[Acts 15.9|]][[Acts 15.10|]][[Acts 15.11|]][[Acts 15.12|]][[Acts 15.13|]][[Acts 15.14|]][[Acts 15.15|]][[Acts 15.16|]][[Acts 15.17|]][[Acts 15.18|]][[Acts 15.19|]][[Acts 15.20|]][[Acts 15.21|]][[Acts 15.22|]][[Acts 15.23|]][[Acts 15.24|]][[Acts 15.25|]][[Acts 15.26|]][[Acts 15.27|]][[Acts 15.28|]][[Acts 15.29|]][[Acts 15.30|]][[Acts 15.31|]][[Acts 15.32|]][[Acts 15.33|]][[Acts 15.34|]][[Acts 15.35|]][[Acts 15.36|]][[Acts 15.37|]][[Acts 15.38|]][[Acts 15.39|]][[Acts 15.40|]][[Acts 15.41|]]), which lasted about three years and ended with a visit to Jerusalem. After a short stay in Antioch, Paul began his third journey, which occupied about 3½ years ([[Acts 18.1|Acts 18:23–21:15]][[Acts 18.2|]][[Acts 18.3|]][[Acts 18.4|]][[Acts 18.5|]][[Acts 18.6|]][[Acts 18.7|]][[Acts 18.8|]][[Acts 18.9|]][[Acts 18.10|]][[Acts 18.11|]][[Acts 18.12|]][[Acts 18.13|]][[Acts 18.14|]][[Acts 18.15|]][[Acts 18.16|]][[Acts 18.17|]][[Acts 18.18|]][[Acts 18.19|]][[Acts 18.20|]][[Acts 18.21|]][[Acts 18.22|]][[Acts 18.23|]][[Acts 18.24|]][[Acts 18.25|]][[Acts 18.26|]][[Acts 18.27|]][[Acts 18.28|]]). On his return to Jerusalem he was arrested and sent to Caesarea ([[Acts 21.1|Acts 21:17–23:35]][[Acts 21.2|]][[Acts 21.3|]][[Acts 21.4|]][[Acts 21.5|]][[Acts 21.6|]][[Acts 21.7|]][[Acts 21.8|]][[Acts 21.9|]][[Acts 21.10|]][[Acts 21.11|]][[Acts 21.12|]][[Acts 21.13|]][[Acts 21.14|]][[Acts 21.15|]][[Acts 21.16|]][[Acts 21.17|]][[Acts 21.18|]][[Acts 21.19|]][[Acts 21.20|]][[Acts 21.21|]][[Acts 21.22|]][[Acts 21.23|]][[Acts 21.24|]][[Acts 21.25|]][[Acts 21.26|]][[Acts 21.27|]][[Acts 21.28|]][[Acts 21.29|]][[Acts 21.30|]][[Acts 21.31|]][[Acts 21.32|]][[Acts 21.33|]][[Acts 21.34|]][[Acts 21.35|]][[Acts 21.36|]][[Acts 21.37|]][[Acts 21.38|]][[Acts 21.39|]][[Acts 21.40|]]), where he remained a prisoner for two years ([[Acts 24.1|Acts 24:1–26:32]][[Acts 24.2|]][[Acts 24.3|]][[Acts 24.4|]][[Acts 24.5|]][[Acts 24.6|]][[Acts 24.7|]][[Acts 24.8|]][[Acts 24.9|]][[Acts 24.10|]][[Acts 24.11|]][[Acts 24.12|]][[Acts 24.13|]][[Acts 24.14|]][[Acts 24.15|]][[Acts 24.16|]][[Acts 24.17|]][[Acts 24.18|]][[Acts 24.19|]][[Acts 24.20|]][[Acts 24.21|]][[Acts 24.22|]][[Acts 24.23|]][[Acts 24.24|]][[Acts 24.25|]][[Acts 24.26|]][[Acts 24.27|]]), and was then sent for trial to Rome, suffering shipwreck on the way ([[Acts 27.1|Acts 27:1–28:10]][[Acts 27.2|]][[Acts 27.3|]][[Acts 27.4|]][[Acts 27.5|]][[Acts 27.6|]][[Acts 27.7|]][[Acts 27.8|]][[Acts 27.9|]][[Acts 27.10|]][[Acts 27.11|]][[Acts 27.12|]][[Acts 27.13|]][[Acts 27.14|]][[Acts 27.15|]][[Acts 27.16|]][[Acts 27.17|]][[Acts 27.18|]][[Acts 27.19|]][[Acts 27.20|]][[Acts 27.21|]][[Acts 27.22|]][[Acts 27.23|]][[Acts 27.24|]][[Acts 27.25|]][[Acts 27.26|]][[Acts 27.27|]][[Acts 27.28|]][[Acts 27.29|]][[Acts 27.30|]][[Acts 27.31|]][[Acts 27.32|]][[Acts 27.33|]][[Acts 27.34|]][[Acts 27.35|]][[Acts 27.36|]][[Acts 27.37|]][[Acts 27.38|]][[Acts 27.39|]][[Acts 27.40|]][[Acts 27.41|]][[Acts 27.42|]][[Acts 27.43|]][[Acts 27.44|]]). He remained in Rome two years ([[Acts 28.30|Acts 28:30]]) and was then released. He then appears to have visited Asia, Macedonia, Crete, and perhaps Spain. At the end of about four years he was again taken a prisoner to Rome and suffered martyrdom, probably in the spring of  65.
