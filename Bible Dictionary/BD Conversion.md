# Conversion

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/conversion?lang=eng)

Denotes changing one’s views, in a conscious acceptance of the will of God ([[Acts 3.19|Acts 3:19]]). If followed by continued faith in the Lord Jesus Christ, repentance, baptism in water for the remission of sins, and the reception of the Holy Ghost by the laying on of hands, conversion will become complete and will change a natural man into a sanctified, born again, purified person—a new creature in Christ Jesus (see [[2 Corinthians 5.17|2 Cor. 5:17]]). Complete conversion comes after many trials and much testing (see [[Luke 22.32|Luke 22:32]]; [[D&C 112.12|D&C 112:12–13]][[D&C 112.13|]]). To labor for the conversion of one’s self and others is a noble task, as in [[Psalms 51.13|Ps. 51:13]]; [[Daniel 12.3|Dan. 12:3]]; [[James 5.19|James 5:19–20]][[James 5.20|]]; [[Alma 26.1|Alma 26]]; [[D&C 18.15|D&C 18:15–16]][[D&C 18.16|]].
