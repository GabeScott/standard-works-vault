# Endor

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/endor?lang=eng)

A place in the territory of Issachar but possessed by Manasseh ([[Joshua 17.11|Josh. 17:11]]); the scene of the victory over Sisera ([[Psalms 83.10|Ps. 83:10]]); and the home of the witch consulted by Saul the night before his fatal battle at Gilboa ([[1 Samuel 28.7|1 Sam. 28:7]]).
