# Japheth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/japheth?lang=eng)

Eldest son of Noah ([[Moses 8.12|Moses 8:12]]; see also [[Genesis 5.32|Gen. 5:32]]; [[Genesis 6.10|6:10]]; [[Genesis 7.13|7:13]]; [[Genesis 9.18|9:18, 23]][[Genesis 9.23|]]); his blessing ([[Genesis 9.27|Gen. 9:27]]). His descendants were dispersed over the European coasts of the Mediterranean and the districts adjoining the Black and Caspian seas ([[Genesis 10.1|Gen. 10:1–5, 21]][[Genesis 10.2|]][[Genesis 10.3|]][[Genesis 10.4|]][[Genesis 10.5|]][[Genesis 10.21|]]; [[1 Chronicles 1.4|1 Chr. 1:4–5]][[1 Chronicles 1.5|]]), forming what is now called the Indo-European family of nations.
