# Vows

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/vows?lang=eng)

These were of two kinds: *(a)* dedication—some person or thing was given to the Lord ([[Leviticus 27.1|Lev. 27:1–24]][[Leviticus 27.2|]][[Leviticus 27.3|]][[Leviticus 27.4|]][[Leviticus 27.5|]][[Leviticus 27.6|]][[Leviticus 27.7|]][[Leviticus 27.8|]][[Leviticus 27.9|]][[Leviticus 27.10|]][[Leviticus 27.11|]][[Leviticus 27.12|]][[Leviticus 27.13|]][[Leviticus 27.14|]][[Leviticus 27.15|]][[Leviticus 27.16|]][[Leviticus 27.17|]][[Leviticus 27.18|]][[Leviticus 27.19|]][[Leviticus 27.20|]][[Leviticus 27.21|]][[Leviticus 27.22|]][[Leviticus 27.23|]][[Leviticus 27.24|]]); *(b)* abstinence—a promise made to abstain from some lawful act or enjoyment ([[Numbers 6.3|Num. 6:3]]). No unnatural mutilation was permitted ([[Leviticus 19.28|Lev. 19:28]]; [[Deuteronomy 14.1|Deut. 14:1]], etc.). Nothing already holy to the Lord, or intrinsically unholy, or blemished in the slightest degree, could be offered as a vow ([[Leviticus 22.23|Lev. 22:23]]; [[Leviticus 27.26|27:26]]; [[Deuteronomy 23.18|Deut. 23:18]]). Provisions were made for ransoming such offerings when they could not be fitly sacrificed ([[Leviticus 27.11|Lev. 27:11]]). The vows of dependent women (wives or daughters) did not stand unless ratified explicitly or implicitly by the husband or father ([[Numbers 30.3|Num. 30:3–16]][[Numbers 30.4|]][[Numbers 30.5|]][[Numbers 30.6|]][[Numbers 30.7|]][[Numbers 30.8|]][[Numbers 30.9|]][[Numbers 30.10|]][[Numbers 30.11|]][[Numbers 30.12|]][[Numbers 30.13|]][[Numbers 30.14|]][[Numbers 30.15|]][[Numbers 30.16|]]; see also [[Jeremiah 44.19|Jer. 44:19]]). Vows often had an entreating character; they were offered in order to obtain some favor from Jehovah ([[Genesis 28.20|Gen. 28:20]]; [[2 Samuel 15.7|2 Sam. 15:7–8]][[2 Samuel 15.8|]]).
