(3) Son of Herod the Great and Mariamne; first husband of Herodias ([[Matthew 14.3|Matt. 14:3]]; [[Mark 6.17|Mark 6:17]]; [[Luke 3.19|Luke 3:19]]). See *[[BD Herod|Herod]].*

(4) Another son of Herod, tetrarch of Trachonitis ([[Luke 3.1|Luke 3:1]]), founder of Caesarea Philippi.
