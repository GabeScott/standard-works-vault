# Rachel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/rachel?lang=eng)

*Ewe.* The younger of the daughters of Laban, the dearly loved wife of Jacob, and mother of Joseph and Benjamin ([[Genesis 29.1|Gen. 29–31]]; [[Genesis 33.1|33:1–2, 7]][[Genesis 33.2|]][[Genesis 33.7|]]; [[Genesis 35.16|35:16, 24–25]][[Genesis 35.24|]][[Genesis 35.25|]]; [[Genesis 46.19|46:19, 22, 25]][[Genesis 46.22|]][[Genesis 46.25|]]); her grave ([[Genesis 35.19|35:19–20]][[Genesis 35.20|]]; [[Genesis 48.7|48:7]]; [[1 Samuel 10.2|1 Sam. 10:2]]). Jeremiah, in a very beautiful passage, pictures Rachel as weeping in Ramah for her children, the descendants of Benjamin, Ramah being the place at which the exiles were assembled before their departure for Babylon ([[Jeremiah 31.15|Jer. 31:15]]). Matthew quotes the passage in his description of the mourning at Bethlehem (where Rachel’s grave was) after the murder of the children ([[Matthew 2.18|Matt. 2:18]]).
