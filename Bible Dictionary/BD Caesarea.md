# Caesarea

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/caesarea?lang=eng)

An important seaport town of Palestine, on the main road from Tyre to Egypt, 33 miles north of Joppa, and about 60 miles from Jerusalem. Built by Herod the Great, it was the official residence of Festus, Felix, and other Roman prefects and procurators of Judea ([[Acts 23.23|Acts 23:23, 33]][[Acts 23.33|]]; [[Acts 25.1|25:1–13]][[Acts 25.2|]][[Acts 25.3|]][[Acts 25.4|]][[Acts 25.5|]][[Acts 25.6|]][[Acts 25.7|]][[Acts 25.8|]][[Acts 25.9|]][[Acts 25.10|]][[Acts 25.11|]][[Acts 25.12|]][[Acts 25.13|]]). Philip worked in Caesarea ([[Acts 8.40|Acts 8:40]]; [[Acts 21.8|21:8, 16]][[Acts 21.16|]]). It was there that Cornelius, the gentile centurion, was baptized by Peter ([[Acts 10.1|Acts 10:1, 24]][[Acts 10.24|]]; [[Acts 11.11|11:11]]), and that Herod Agrippa died ([[Acts 12.23|Acts 12:23]]). Caesarea is also frequently mentioned in connection with Paul’s journeys ([[Acts 9.30|Acts 9:30]]; [[Acts 18.22|18:22]]; [[Acts 21.8|21:8, 16]][[Acts 21.16|]]; [[Acts 23.23|23:23, 33]][[Acts 23.33|]]; [[Acts 25.1|25:1, 4, 6, 13]][[Acts 25.4|]][[Acts 25.6|]][[Acts 25.13|]]).
