# Timothy

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/timothy?lang=eng)

*Honored of God.* The son of a Greek father and Jewish mother (Eunice) living at Lystra ([[Acts 16.1|Acts 16:1–3]][[Acts 16.2|]][[Acts 16.3|]]; [[2 Timothy 1.5|2 Tim. 1:5]]); circumcised by Paul in order that he might be of greater use for evangelistic work among Jews ([[Acts 16.3|Acts 16:3]]); spoken of by Paul as his own “son in the faith” ([[1 Timothy 1.2|1 Tim. 1:2, 18]][[1 Timothy 1.18|]]; [[2 Timothy 1.2|2 Tim. 1:2]]); at Philippi ([[Philippians 2.22|Philip. 2:22]]); at Beroea ([[Acts 17.14|Acts 17:14]]); sent to Thessalonica ([[1 Thessalonians 3.2|1 Thes. 3:2]]); with Paul at Corinth ([[1 Thessalonians 1.1|1 Thes. 1:1]]; [[2 Thessalonians 1.1|2 Thes. 1:1]]); sent to Macedonia ([[Acts 19.22|Acts 19:22]]); to Corinth ([[1 Corinthians 16.10|1 Cor. 16:10]]); with Paul in Macedonia when he wrote [[2 Corinthians 1.1|2 Cor. 1:1, 19]][[2 Corinthians 1.19|]]; and at Corinth ([[Romans 16.21|Rom. 16:21]]); followed him to Troas ([[Acts 20.4|Acts 20:4–5]][[Acts 20.5|]]); at Rome ([[Philippians 1.1|Philip. 1:1]]; [[Philippians 2.19|2:19]]; [[Colossians 1.1|Col. 1:1]]; [[Philemon 1.1|Philem. 1:1]]); at Ephesus ([[1 Timothy 1.3|1 Tim. 1:3]]); Paul wished him to come to Rome ([[2 Timothy 4.9|2 Tim. 4:9, 21]][[2 Timothy 4.21|]]); set at liberty (the details of the imprisonment are unknown) ([[Hebrews 13.23|Heb. 13:23]]). Timothy was perhaps Paul’s most trusted and capable assistant, as in [[Philippians 2.19|Philip. 2:19–23]][[Philippians 2.20|]][[Philippians 2.21|]][[Philippians 2.22|]][[Philippians 2.23|]].
