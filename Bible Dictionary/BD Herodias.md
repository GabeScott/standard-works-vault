# Herodias

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/herodias?lang=eng)

Sister of Herod Agrippa; married to her uncle, Herod Philip, by whom she had a daughter Salome ([[Matthew 14.6|Matt. 14:6]]). She eloped in order to live as the wife of her step-uncle, Herod Antipas. It was for his rebuke of this crime that John the Baptist was put to death ([[Matthew 14.8|Matt. 14:8–11]][[Matthew 14.9|]][[Matthew 14.10|]][[Matthew 14.11|]]; [[Mark 6.24|Mark 6:24–28]][[Mark 6.25|]][[Mark 6.26|]][[Mark 6.27|]][[Mark 6.28|]]; [[Luke 3.19|Luke 3:19]]).
