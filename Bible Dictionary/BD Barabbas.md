# Barabbas

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/barabbas?lang=eng)

*Son of the father.* Name of the man released at the Feast instead of Jesus at the time of the Crucifixion. A “notable prisoner,” he is identified as an insurrectionist and a robber ([[Matthew 27.16|Matt. 27:16]]; [[Mark 15.7|Mark 15:7]]; [[Luke 23.18|Luke 23:18]]; [[John 18.40|John 18:40]]).
