# Manna

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/manna?lang=eng)

It is impossible to find any natural product that will answer to the requirements of the scriptural narrative in regard to this heavensent food. With regard to the name, we are told ([[Exodus 16.15|Ex. 16:15]]) that the people, seeing the small scale-like substance, said one to another, *“Man-hu,”* “For they wist not what it was.” This also translates “What is it?” See [[Exodus 16.31|Ex. 16:31–35]][[Exodus 16.32|]][[Exodus 16.33|]][[Exodus 16.34|]][[Exodus 16.35|]]; [[Numbers 11.7|Num. 11:7–9]][[Numbers 11.8|]][[Numbers 11.9|]]; [[Joshua 5.12|Josh. 5:12]]; [[John 6.31|John 6:31–32]][[John 6.32|]]; [[1 Corinthians 10.1|1 Cor. 10:1–4]][[1 Corinthians 10.2|]][[1 Corinthians 10.3|]][[1 Corinthians 10.4|]]; [[Hebrews 9.4|Heb. 9:4]]; [[Revelation 2.17|Rev. 2:17]].
