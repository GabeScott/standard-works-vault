# Mizpah or Mizpeh

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/mizpah?lang=eng)

*Watchtower.* (1) The heap of stones raised by Jacob and Laban ([[Genesis 31.49|Gen. 31:49]]; [[Judges 10.17|Judg. 10:17]]; [[Judges 11.11|11:11, 29, 34]][[Judges 11.29|]][[Judges 11.34|]]; [[Judges 20.1|20:1, 3]][[Judges 20.3|]]; [[Judges 21.1|21:1, 5, 8]][[Judges 21.5|]][[Judges 21.8|]]); given to Gad ([[Joshua 18.26|Josh. 18:26]]).

(2) Mizpah of Moab, where David placed his parents ([[1 Samuel 22.3|1 Sam. 22:3]]).

(3) The land of Mizpah, where lived some Hivites ([[Joshua 11.3|Josh. 11:3]]).
