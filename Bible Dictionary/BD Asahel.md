# Asahel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/asahel?lang=eng)

*God hath made.* Youngest son of Zeruiah, one of David’s 30 heroes ([[2 Samuel 23.24|2 Sam. 23:24]]) and a commander in his army ([[1 Chronicles 27.7|1 Chr. 27:7]]); celebrated for his swiftness of foot, an accomplishment much valued in ancient times; slain by Abner ([[2 Samuel 2.18|2 Sam. 2:18–32]][[2 Samuel 2.19|]][[2 Samuel 2.20|]][[2 Samuel 2.21|]][[2 Samuel 2.22|]][[2 Samuel 2.23|]][[2 Samuel 2.24|]][[2 Samuel 2.25|]][[2 Samuel 2.26|]][[2 Samuel 2.27|]][[2 Samuel 2.28|]][[2 Samuel 2.29|]][[2 Samuel 2.30|]][[2 Samuel 2.31|]][[2 Samuel 2.32|]]); Joab’s revenge ([[2 Samuel 3.27|3:27–30]][[2 Samuel 3.28|]][[2 Samuel 3.29|]][[2 Samuel 3.30|]]; see also [[1 Chronicles 2.16|1 Chr. 2:16]]; [[1 Chronicles 11.26|11:26]]). Three other persons, [[2 Chronicles 17.8|2 Chr. 17:8]]; [[2 Chronicles 31.13|31:13]]; [[Ezra 10.15|Ezra 10:15]].
