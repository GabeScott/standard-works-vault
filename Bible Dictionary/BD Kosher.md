# Kosher

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/kosher?lang=eng)

From Hebrew *Kasher,* meaning “fit, proper, and ritually clean” according to Jewish law. Especially applied to the preparation and serving of food according to the specifications given in the law of Moses (see [[Leviticus 11.1|Lev. 11]]).
