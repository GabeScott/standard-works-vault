# El Elyon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/el-elyon?lang=eng)

A Hebrew designation meaning “the most high God,” as in [[Genesis 14.18|Gen. 14:18–20, 22]][[Genesis 14.19|]][[Genesis 14.20|]][[Genesis 14.22|]]. It is the name for deity worshipped by Melchizedek, king of Salem, and appears for the first time in the Bible in [[Genesis 14.18|Gen. 14:18]]. It is found also in [[Acts 16.17|Acts 16:17]]; see also [[D&C 59.10|D&C 59:10–12]][[D&C 59.11|]][[D&C 59.12|]]; [[D&C 76.112|76:112]].
