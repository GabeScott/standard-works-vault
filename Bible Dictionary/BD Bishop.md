# Bishop

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/bishop?lang=eng)

Greek *episkopos,* meaning “overseer,” an office or position of responsibility. Hence, Jesus is called the “Bishop of your souls” ([[1 Peter 2.21|1 Pet. 2:21–25]][[1 Peter 2.22|]][[1 Peter 2.23|]][[1 Peter 2.24|]][[1 Peter 2.25|]]). Judas is also spoken of as having a “bishoprick,” reflecting the fact that the Twelve are overseers ([[Acts 1.20|Acts 1:20]]; [[D&C 114.1|D&C 114]]).

Bishop is also an ordained office in the Aaronic Priesthood ([[D&C 20.67|D&C 20:67]]), and a bishop is a common judge in Israel ([[D&C 107.74|D&C 107:74]]). The desirable qualifications of a bishop are listed in [[1 Timothy 3.1|1 Tim. 3:1–7]][[1 Timothy 3.2|]][[1 Timothy 3.3|]][[1 Timothy 3.4|]][[1 Timothy 3.5|]][[1 Timothy 3.6|]][[1 Timothy 3.7|]]; [[Titus 1.7|Titus 1:7–9]][[Titus 1.8|]][[Titus 1.9|]]. See also *[[BD Ministry|Ministry]].*
