# Girgashites

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/girgashites?lang=eng)

One of the seven nations driven out by the Israelites. Little is known beyond their name ([[Genesis 10.16|Gen. 10:16]]; [[Joshua 3.10|Josh. 3:10]]). Nehemiah ([[Nehemiah 9.7|9:7–8]][[Nehemiah 9.8|]]) cites the fact that they were driven out of Canaan as a fulfillment of the Lord’s promise to Abraham.
