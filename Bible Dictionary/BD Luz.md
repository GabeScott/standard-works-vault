# Luz

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/luz?lang=eng)

*Almond tree.* (1) The scene of Jacob’s dream, afterwards called Bethel ([[Genesis 28.19|Gen. 28:19]]; [[Genesis 35.6|35:6]]; [[Genesis 48.3|48:3]]).

(2) A city of the Hittites ([[Judges 1.26|Judg. 1:26]]), its position entirely unknown.
