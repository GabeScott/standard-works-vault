# Kirjath-arba

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/kirjath-arba?lang=eng)

*City of Arba.* The old name of Hebron ([[Genesis 23.2|Gen. 23:2]]; [[Genesis 35.27|35:27]]). In [[Genesis 35.27|Gen. 35:27]]; [[Joshua 15.13|Josh. 15:13]]; [[Joshua 21.11|21:11]], KJV translates “city of Arba.”
