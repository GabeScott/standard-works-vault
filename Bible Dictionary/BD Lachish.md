# Lachish

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/lachish?lang=eng)

A city of the Amorites, between Hebron and the coast ([[Joshua 10.3|Josh. 10:3, 5, 23, 31–35]][[Joshua 10.5|]][[Joshua 10.23|]][[Joshua 10.31|]][[Joshua 10.32|]][[Joshua 10.33|]][[Joshua 10.34|]][[Joshua 10.35|]]; [[Joshua 12.11|12:11]]); afterwards included in Judah ([[Joshua 15.39|15:39]]); fortified by Rehoboam ([[2 Chronicles 11.9|2 Chr. 11:9]]); here Amaziah was killed ([[2 Kings 14.19|2 Kgs. 14:19]]; [[2 Chronicles 25.27|2 Chr. 25:27]]). It was captured by Sennacherib ([[2 Kings 18.17|2 Kgs. 18:17]]; [[2 Chronicles 32.9|2 Chr. 32:9]]), and the siege is depicted on an Assyrian monument still in existence. After the Return Lachish was again occupied by Jews ([[Nehemiah 11.30|Neh. 11:30]]).
