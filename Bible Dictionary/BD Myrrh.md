# Myrrh

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/myrrh?lang=eng)

Used in the preparation of the holy ointment ([[Exodus 30.23|Ex. 30:23]]); in the purification of women ([[Esther 2.12|Esth. 2:12]]); as a perfume ([[Psalms 45.8|Ps. 45:8]]; [[Song of Solomon 1.1|Song 1]]; [[Song of Solomon 3.1|3–5]]); and for embalming ([[John 19.39|John 19:39]]). It was obtained from various thorny shrubs of the dry districts of Arabia and eastern Africa. In [[Genesis 37.25|Gen. 37:25]]; [[Genesis 43.11|43:11]], myrrh is the gum of the rock rose.
