# Myrrh

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/myrrh?lang=eng)

Used in the preparation of the holy ointment ([[Exodus 30.23|Ex. 30:23]]); in the purification of women ([[Esther 2.12|Esth. 2:12]]); as a perfume ([[Psalms 45.8|Ps. 45:8]]; [[Song of Solomon 1.1|Song 1]][[Song of Solomon 1.2|]][[Song of Solomon 1.3|]][[Song of Solomon 1.4|]][[Song of Solomon 1.5|]][[Song of Solomon 1.6|]][[Song of Solomon 1.7|]][[Song of Solomon 1.8|]][[Song of Solomon 1.9|]][[Song of Solomon 1.10|]][[Song of Solomon 1.11|]][[Song of Solomon 1.12|]][[Song of Solomon 1.13|]][[Song of Solomon 1.14|]][[Song of Solomon 1.15|]][[Song of Solomon 1.16|]][[Song of Solomon 1.17|]]; [[Song of Solomon 3.1|3–5]][[Song of Solomon 3.2|]][[Song of Solomon 3.3|]][[Song of Solomon 3.4|]][[Song of Solomon 3.5|]][[Song of Solomon 3.6|]][[Song of Solomon 3.7|]][[Song of Solomon 3.8|]][[Song of Solomon 3.9|]][[Song of Solomon 3.10|]][[Song of Solomon 3.11|]]); and for embalming ([[John 19.39|John 19:39]]). It was obtained from various thorny shrubs of the dry districts of Arabia and eastern Africa. In [[Genesis 37.25|Gen. 37:25]]; [[Genesis 43.11|43:11]], myrrh is the gum of the rock rose.
