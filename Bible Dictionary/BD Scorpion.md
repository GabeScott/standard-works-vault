# Scorpion

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/scorpion?lang=eng)

An invertebrate animal with a very painful sting, mentioned as one of the dangers of the wilderness of Sinai ([[Deuteronomy 8.15|Deut. 8:15]]; see also [[Ezekiel 2.6|Ezek. 2:6]]; [[Luke 11.12|Luke 11:12]]; [[Revelation 9.5|Rev. 9:5]]).
