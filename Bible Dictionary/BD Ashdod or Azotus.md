# Ashdod or Azotus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/ashdod?lang=eng)

The Arabic Esdud, seat of the worship of Dagon ([[1 Samuel 5.1|1 Sam. 5:1–9]][[1 Samuel 5.2|]][[1 Samuel 5.3|]][[1 Samuel 5.4|]][[1 Samuel 5.5|]][[1 Samuel 5.6|]][[1 Samuel 5.7|]][[1 Samuel 5.8|]][[1 Samuel 5.9|]]). Though allotted to Judah ([[Joshua 15.46|Josh. 15:46–47]][[Joshua 15.47|]]) and occasionally held by kings of Judah ([[2 Chronicles 26.6|2 Chr. 26:6]]), it remained a Philistine city ([[Nehemiah 4.7|Neh. 4:7]]; [[Amos 1.8|Amos 1:8]]). It was on the main route from Egypt to Phoenicia, midway between Gaza and Joppa. See *[[BD Philistines|Philistines]].*
