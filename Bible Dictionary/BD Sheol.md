# Sheol

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/sheol?lang=eng)

The Hebrew name for the abode of departed spirits (corresponding to the Greek Hades), translated in the KJV “grave,” “pit,” and “hell,” depending on the context (see [[Genesis 37.35|Gen. 37:35]]; [[Job 17.16|Job 17:16]]; [[Psalms 16.10|Ps. 16:10]]).
