# Fear

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/fear?lang=eng)

Care should be taken to distinguish between two different uses of this word. The “fear of the Lord” is frequently spoken of as part of man’s duty ([[Psalms 111.10|Ps. 111:10]]; [[Ecclesiastes 12.13|Eccl. 12:13]]; [[Isaiah 11.2|Isa. 11:2–3]][[Isaiah 11.3|]]; [[Luke 1.50|Luke 1:50]]); it is also described as “godly fear” ([[Hebrews 12.28|Heb. 12:28]]). In such passages fear is equivalent to reverence, awe, worship, and is therefore an essential part of the attitude of mind in which we ought to stand toward the All-holy God. On the other hand fear is spoken of as something unworthy of a child of God, something that “perfect love casteth out” ([[1 John 4.18|1 Jn. 4:18]]). The first effect of Adam’s sin was that he was afraid ([[Genesis 3.10|Gen. 3:10]]). Sin destroys that feeling of confidence God’s child should feel in a loving Father and produces instead a feeling of shame and guilt. Ever since the Fall God has been teaching men not to fear, but with penitence to ask forgiveness in full confidence of receiving it.
