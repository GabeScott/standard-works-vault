# Lawyer

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/lawyer?lang=eng)

In the New Testament, equivalent to *scribe,* one who was by profession a student and teacher of the law, including the written law of the Pentateuch, and also “the traditions of the elders” ([[Matthew 22.35|Matt. 22:35]]; [[Mark 12.28|Mark 12:28]]; [[Luke 10.25|Luke 10:25]]).
