# Theophilus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/theophilus?lang=eng)

*Friend of God.* The person to whom Luke addressed his Gospel and the Acts. The use of the title “most excellent” ([[Luke 1.3|Luke 1:3]]) seems to show that he was a real personage. See also [[JST Luke 3.19|JST Luke 3:19–20 (Appendix)]][[JST Luke 3.20|]].
