# Joanna

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/joanna?lang=eng)

(1) Son of Rhesa, and ancestor of Christ ([[Luke 3.27|Luke 3:27]]).

(2) Wife of Chusa, steward to Herod Antipas, tetrarch of Galilee; one of the women who followed Jesus ([[Luke 8.2|Luke 8:2–3]][[Luke 8.3|]]; [[Luke 24.10|24:10]]).
