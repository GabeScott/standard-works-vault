# Carriage

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/carriage?lang=eng)

Baggage, or anything carried, as in [[Judges 18.21|Judg. 18:21]]. Possibly a vessel or small instrument ([[1 Samuel 17.22|1 Sam. 17:22]]). Something lifted up or borne ([[Isaiah 10.28|Isa. 10:28]]; [[Isaiah 46.1|46:1]]; [[Acts 21.15|Acts 21:15]]).
