# Gomorrah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/gomorrah?lang=eng)

The early history of Gomorrah is found in [[Genesis 10.19|Gen. 10:19]]; [[Genesis 13.10|13:10]]; [[Genesis 14.2|14:2–11]][[Genesis 14.3|]][[Genesis 14.4|]][[Genesis 14.5|]][[Genesis 14.6|]][[Genesis 14.7|]][[Genesis 14.8|]][[Genesis 14.9|]][[Genesis 14.10|]][[Genesis 14.11|]]; [[Genesis 18.20|18:20]]; its destruction is noted in [[Genesis 19.1|Gen. 19]]. See also [[Deuteronomy 29.23|Deut. 29:23]]; [[Isaiah 13.19|Isa. 13:19]]; [[Jeremiah 50.40|Jer. 50:40]]; [[Amos 4.11|Amos 4:11]]; [[Matthew 10.15|Matt. 10:15]]; [[Romans 9.29|Rom. 9:29]]; [[2 Peter 2.6|2 Pet. 2:6]]; [[Jude 1.7|Jude 1:7]]. The fate of the city was regarded as a warning against sensual wickedness. The five “cities of the plain,” of which Gomorrah was one, probably stood at the southern end of the Dead Sea.
