# Gamaliel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/gamaliel?lang=eng)

A Pharisee, a doctor of the law, held in honor with all the people ([[Acts 5.34|Acts 5:34]]). He was a celebrated Jewish teacher who belonged to the more liberal school. His influence carried great weight in the Sanhedrin ([[Acts 5.35|5:35–40]][[Acts 5.36|]][[Acts 5.37|]][[Acts 5.38|]][[Acts 5.39|]][[Acts 5.40|]]). Paul had been one of his pupils ([[Acts 22.3|22:3]]).
