# Thomas

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/thomas?lang=eng)

One of the Twelve. The name means a “twin,” and so it is translated in [[John 11.16|John 11:16]] by the Greek Didymus. He is mentioned in the lists of Apostles ([[Matthew 10.3|Matt. 10:3]]; [[Mark 3.18|Mark 3:18]]; [[Luke 6.15|Luke 6:15]]; [[Acts 1.13|Acts 1:13]]); and also in [[John 11.16|John 11:16]]; [[John 14.5|14:5]]; [[John 20.24|20:24, 28]][[John 20.28|]]; [[John 21.2|21:2]].
