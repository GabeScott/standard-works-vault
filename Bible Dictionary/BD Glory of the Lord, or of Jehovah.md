# Glory of the Lord, or of Jehovah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/glory-of-the-lord?lang=eng)

An expression that occurs frequently in the Old Testament. It sometimes denotes the fulness of the majesty of God, revealed in the world and made known to men ([[Numbers 14.21|Num. 14:21–22]][[Numbers 14.22|]]; [[Isaiah 6.3|Isa. 6:3]]; [[Isaiah 66.19|66:19]]); in other places it denotes some outward and visible manifestation of God’s presence ([[Exodus 33.17|Ex. 33:17–23]][[Exodus 33.18|]][[Exodus 33.19|]][[Exodus 33.20|]][[Exodus 33.21|]][[Exodus 33.22|]][[Exodus 33.23|]]; [[Ezekiel 1.28|Ezek. 1:28]]; [[Ezekiel 9.3|9:3]]; [[Ezekiel 10.4|10:4, 18–19]][[Ezekiel 10.18|]][[Ezekiel 10.19|]]; [[Ezekiel 11.23|11:23]]; [[Ezekiel 43.2|43:2–5]][[Ezekiel 43.3|]][[Ezekiel 43.4|]][[Ezekiel 43.5|]]; [[Luke 2.9|Luke 2:9]]; [[Luke 9.32|9:32]]; [[John 1.14|John 1:14]]).
