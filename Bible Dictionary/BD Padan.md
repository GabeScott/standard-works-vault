# Padan

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/padan?lang=eng)

([[Genesis 48.7|Gen. 48:7]]); elsewhere Padan-aram. Probably *plain of Aram.* Also called Mesopotamia, the country between the sources of the Tigris and Euphrates; the home of Rebekah ([[Genesis 25.20|Gen. 25:20]]) and Rachel ([[Genesis 28.2|Gen. 28:2, 5–7]][[Genesis 28.5|]][[Genesis 28.6|]][[Genesis 28.7|]]; see also [[Genesis 31.18|31:18]]; [[Genesis 33.18|33:18]]; [[Genesis 35.9|35:9, 26]][[Genesis 35.26|]]; [[Genesis 46.15|46:15]]).
