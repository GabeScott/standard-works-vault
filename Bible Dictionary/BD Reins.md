# Reins

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/reins?lang=eng)

The kidneys, but always used figuratively to signify the center of feeling, awareness, such as joy or pain, much as current English uses *heart* ([[Job 19.27|Job 19:27]]; [[Isaiah 11.5|Isa. 11:5]]; [[Revelation 2.23|Rev. 2:23]]).
