# Lucifer

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/lucifer?lang=eng)

Literally *the Shining One;* also *Lightbringer* or *Son of the Morning.* Lucifer is also known as Satan or the devil. The name Lucifer appears only once in the Bible ([[Isaiah 14.12|Isa. 14:12]]; compare [[Luke 10.18|Luke 10:18]]). Apparently Lucifer is the name of the devil before his rebellion and fall.
