# Elah, Valley of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/elah-valley-of?lang=eng)

*Valley of the terebinths.* Runs west between Zekharia and Azekah, a valley of the Shephelah, between Philistia and the hill country of Judah. Here Goliath was killed by David ([[1 Samuel 17.2|1 Sam. 17:2, 19]][[1 Samuel 17.19|]]; [[1 Samuel 21.9|21:9]]).
