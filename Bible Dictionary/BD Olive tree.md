# Olive tree

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/olive-tree?lang=eng)

The well-known *Olea europea.* It is extensively cultivated in Palestine for the sake of the oil. The olive requires being grafted; hence Paul’s allusion to the Gentiles ([[Romans 11.17|Rom. 11:17–24]][[Romans 11.18|]][[Romans 11.19|]][[Romans 11.20|]][[Romans 11.21|]][[Romans 11.22|]][[Romans 11.23|]][[Romans 11.24|]]); but what he describes is the reverse of the ordinary gardening operation and is therefore spoken of as “contrary to nature” ([[Romans 11.24|verse 24]]). Using the olive tree in an allegorical sense to illustrate Israel and the Gentiles is also done by Zenos, as quoted by [[Jacob 5.1|Jacob 5]]. Isaiah uses the illustration of a grape vine for the same purpose ([[Isaiah 5.1|Isa. 5:1–7]][[Isaiah 5.2|]][[Isaiah 5.3|]][[Isaiah 5.4|]][[Isaiah 5.5|]][[Isaiah 5.6|]][[Isaiah 5.7|]]).
