# Asaph

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/asaph?lang=eng)

A cymbal-playing Levite appointed leader of David’s choir. Founded a family of singers called the “sons of Asaph” or “children of Asaph”; mentioned in the titles of various Psalms.
