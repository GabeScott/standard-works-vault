# Compass

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/compass?lang=eng)

As a verb, to go completely around, or about, as in [[Matthew 23.15|Matt. 23:15]]. As a noun, a circle, as in [[Proverbs 8.27|Prov. 8:27]]; or a circular direction, as in [[2 Kings 3.9|2 Kgs. 3:9]] and [[Acts 28.13|Acts 28:13]].
