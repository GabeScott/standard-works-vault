# Hezekiah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hezekiah?lang=eng)

(1) King of Judah, and a great religious and political reformer ([[2 Kings 18.1|2 Kgs. 18:1–21:3]]; [[2 Chronicles 29.1|2 Chr. 29:1–33:3]]; [[Isaiah 36.1|Isa. 36–39]]). He suppressed idolatry and reconstituted the temple services. In his reforms both in church and state he had the assistance of the great prophet Isaiah. The early part of the reign was prosperous. Hezekiah made a successful expedition against the Philistines ([[2 Kings 18.8|2 Kgs. 18:8]]; [[2 Chronicles 28.18|2 Chr. 28:18]]) and refused to pay the usual tribute to Assyria ([[2 Kings 18.7|2 Kgs. 18:7]]). Soon after came two Assyrian invasions. The first, under Sargon, is referred to in [[Isaiah 10.24|Isa. 10:24–32]][[Isaiah 10.25|]][[Isaiah 10.26|]][[Isaiah 10.27|]][[Isaiah 10.28|]][[Isaiah 10.29|]][[Isaiah 10.30|]][[Isaiah 10.31|]][[Isaiah 10.32|]]. The second, under Sennacherib, is more fully described in [[2 Kings 18.1|2 Kgs. 18:13–19:7]]. After a time of great anxiety the city was at length delivered, probably by a pestilence that broke out in the Assyrian camp. A year later Hezekiah died, after a reign of 29 years.

(2) Three others ([[1 Chronicles 3.23|1 Chr. 3:23]]; [[Nehemiah 7.21|Neh. 7:21]]; [[Nehemiah 10.17|10:17]]).
