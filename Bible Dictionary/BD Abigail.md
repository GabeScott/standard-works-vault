# Abigail

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/abigail?lang=eng)

*Father of rejoicing.* (1) Wife of Nabal ([[1 Samuel 25.3|1 Sam. 25:3]]); became David’s wife ([[1 Samuel 25.42|25:42]]; [[1 Samuel 27.3|27:3]]; [[1 Samuel 30.5|30:5]]; [[2 Samuel 2.2|2 Sam. 2:2]]); mother of Chileab ([[2 Samuel 3.3|2 Sam. 3:3]]), or Daniel ([[1 Chronicles 3.1|1 Chr. 3:1]]).

(2) Sister of David ([[1 Chronicles 2.16|1 Chr. 2:16]]).
