# Gehenna

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/gehenna?lang=eng)

The valley of Hinnom. South of Jerusalem, the scene of Moloch worship ([[2 Chronicles 28.3|2 Chr. 28:3]]; [[2 Chronicles 33.6|33:6]]; [[Jeremiah 7.31|Jer. 7:31]]; [[Jeremiah 19.2|19:2–6]][[Jeremiah 19.3|]][[Jeremiah 19.4|]][[Jeremiah 19.5|]][[Jeremiah 19.6|]]); polluted by Josiah ([[2 Kings 23.10|2 Kgs. 23:10]]); afterwards a place in which the refuse of the city was burned, and for that reason it was symbolic of the place of torment after death (see [[Matthew 5.29|Matt. 5:29–30]][[Matthew 5.30|]]; [[Matthew 10.28|10:28]]; [[Matthew 23.15|23:15, 33]][[Matthew 23.33|]]; [[Mark 9.43|Mark 9:43, 45]][[Mark 9.45|]]; [[Luke 12.5|Luke 12:5]]; [[James 3.6|James 3:6]]).
