# Damascus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/damascus?lang=eng)

An ancient city of Syria, standing on a rich plain on the edge of the desert, well watered by the river Barada, of which the Abana and Pharpar ([[2 Kings 5.12|2 Kgs. 5:12]]) are tributaries. It is first mentioned in the Bible as the birthplace of Eliezer, Abraham’s steward ([[Genesis 15.2|Gen. 15:2]]). It was taken by David ([[2 Samuel 8.6|2 Sam. 8:6]]; [[1 Chronicles 18.5|1 Chr. 18:5–6]][[1 Chronicles 18.6|]]) (for *Syria-damascus* read “Syria of Damascus”) and is frequently mentioned in the subsequent history ([[1 Kings 11.24|1 Kgs. 11:24]]; [[1 Kings 15.18|15:18]]; [[1 Kings 20.34|20:34]]; [[2 Kings 8.7|2 Kgs. 8:7, 9]][[2 Kings 8.9|]]; [[2 Kings 14.28|14:28]]; [[2 Kings 16.9|16:9–16]][[2 Kings 16.10|]][[2 Kings 16.11|]][[2 Kings 16.12|]][[2 Kings 16.13|]][[2 Kings 16.14|]][[2 Kings 16.15|]][[2 Kings 16.16|]]; [[2 Chronicles 16.2|2 Chr. 16:2]]; [[2 Chronicles 28.5|28:5, 23]][[2 Chronicles 28.23|]]; [[Isaiah 7.8|Isa. 7:8]]; [[Ezekiel 27.18|Ezek. 27:18]]; [[Ezekiel 47.16|47:16–18]][[Ezekiel 47.17|]][[Ezekiel 47.18|]]). It was conquered by Tiglath-pileser and its inhabitants carried captive about 733  ([[2 Kings 16.9|2 Kgs. 16:9]]; [[Isaiah 8.4|Isa. 8:4]]; [[Isaiah 17.1|17:1–3]][[Isaiah 17.2|]][[Isaiah 17.3|]]; [[Jeremiah 49.23|Jer. 49:23–27]][[Jeremiah 49.24|]][[Jeremiah 49.25|]][[Jeremiah 49.26|]][[Jeremiah 49.27|]]; [[Amos 1.3|Amos 1:3–5]][[Amos 1.4|]][[Amos 1.5|]]). It was rebuilt and became an important place during the Persian supremacy. Later on it was conquered by the Greeks and afterwards by the Romans. In New Testament times it was connected with the history of Paul ([[Acts 9.1|Acts 9:1–27]][[Acts 9.2|]][[Acts 9.3|]][[Acts 9.4|]][[Acts 9.5|]][[Acts 9.6|]][[Acts 9.7|]][[Acts 9.8|]][[Acts 9.9|]][[Acts 9.10|]][[Acts 9.11|]][[Acts 9.12|]][[Acts 9.13|]][[Acts 9.14|]][[Acts 9.15|]][[Acts 9.16|]][[Acts 9.17|]][[Acts 9.18|]][[Acts 9.19|]][[Acts 9.20|]][[Acts 9.21|]][[Acts 9.22|]][[Acts 9.23|]][[Acts 9.24|]][[Acts 9.25|]][[Acts 9.26|]][[Acts 9.27|]]; [[Acts 22.5|22:5–16]][[Acts 22.6|]][[Acts 22.7|]][[Acts 22.8|]][[Acts 22.9|]][[Acts 22.10|]][[Acts 22.11|]][[Acts 22.12|]][[Acts 22.13|]][[Acts 22.14|]][[Acts 22.15|]][[Acts 22.16|]]; [[Acts 26.12|26:12, 20]][[Acts 26.20|]]; [[2 Corinthians 11.32|2 Cor. 11:32]]; [[Galatians 1.17|Gal. 1:17]]). It was then part of the dominions of Aretas, an Arabian prince subject to the Roman emperor. It eventually passed,  634, into the hands of Moslem (Muslim) Arabs, and is still a city of many thousands of inhabitants.
