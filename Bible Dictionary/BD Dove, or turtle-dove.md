# Dove, or turtle-dove

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/dove?lang=eng)

Very common in Palestine; first mentioned in [[Genesis 8.8|Gen. 8:8–12]][[Genesis 8.9|]][[Genesis 8.10|]][[Genesis 8.11|]][[Genesis 8.12|]]; a pair of doves or two pigeons were allowed as substitute in some of the offerings for a lamb or kid in the case of poor persons ([[Leviticus 1.14|Lev. 1:14]]; [[Leviticus 5.7|5:7]]; [[Luke 2.24|Luke 2:24]]); it was also the Nazarite’s offering ([[Numbers 6.10|Num. 6:10]]). In [[Song of Solomon 2.12|Song 2:12]] and [[Jeremiah 8.7|Jer. 8:7]] its migratory habits are mentioned; in [[Psalms 74.19|Ps. 74:19]] it is an emblem of affection and timidity; and in [[Matthew 10.16|Matt. 10:16]], of harmlessness. See also *[[BD Dove, sign of|Dove, sign of]]; [[BD Turtle|Turtle]].*
