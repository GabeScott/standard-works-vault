# Abib

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/abib?lang=eng)

*A green head of grain.* The name of the harvest month ([[Exodus 13.4|Ex. 13:4]]; [[Exodus 23.15|23:15]]; [[Exodus 34.18|34:18]]; [[Deuteronomy 16.1|Deut. 16:1]]). After the Captivity it was known as Nisan ([[Nehemiah 2.1|Neh. 2:1]]). See also *[[BD Calendar|Calendar]].*
