# Jezebel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jezebel?lang=eng)

A Phoenician princess, daughter of Ethbaal, king of Zidonians, and wife of Ahab, king of Israel ([[1 Kings 16.31|1 Kgs. 16:31]]). This marriage, more than any other single event, caused the downfall of the northern kingdom, as Jezebel introduced into Israel the worst forms of Phoenician worship in place of the worship of Jehovah ([[1 Kings 18.4|1 Kgs. 18:4, 13, 19]][[1 Kings 18.13|]][[1 Kings 18.19|]]; [[1 Kings 19.1|19:1–2]][[1 Kings 19.2|]]; see also [[1 Kings 21.5|21:5–25]][[1 Kings 21.6|]][[1 Kings 21.7|]][[1 Kings 21.8|]][[1 Kings 21.9|]][[1 Kings 21.10|]][[1 Kings 21.11|]][[1 Kings 21.12|]][[1 Kings 21.13|]][[1 Kings 21.14|]][[1 Kings 21.15|]][[1 Kings 21.16|]][[1 Kings 21.17|]][[1 Kings 21.18|]][[1 Kings 21.19|]][[1 Kings 21.20|]][[1 Kings 21.21|]][[1 Kings 21.22|]][[1 Kings 21.23|]][[1 Kings 21.24|]][[1 Kings 21.25|]]; [[2 Kings 9.1|2 Kgs. 9]]). In [[Revelation 2.20|Rev. 2:20]] the name is applied figuratively to a woman (or sect) causing great harm to the Church in Thyatira. See also *[[BD Athaliah|Athaliah]].*
