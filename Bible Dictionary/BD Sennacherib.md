# Sennacherib

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/sennacherib?lang=eng)

King of Assyria, 705–681  Upon his accession, Hezekiah, king of Judah, tried to throw off the Assyrian yoke. Sennacherib accordingly invaded Judah and took some of the fenced cities ([[2 Kings 18.13|2 Kgs. 18:13]]; [[2 Chronicles 32.1|2 Chr. 32:1]]; [[Isaiah 36.1|Isa. 36:1]]); sent messengers from Lachish, demanding Hezekiah’s subjection ([[2 Kings 18.17|2 Kgs. 18:17]]; [[2 Chronicles 32.9|2 Chr. 32:9]]; [[Isaiah 36.2|Isa. 36:2]]); sent another letter ([[2 Kings 19.9|2 Kgs. 19:9]]; [[2 Chronicles 32.17|2 Chr. 32:17]]; [[Isaiah 37.9|Isa. 37:9]]); his army was destroyed ([[2 Kings 19.35|2 Kgs. 19:35]]; [[2 Chronicles 32.21|2 Chr. 32:21]]; [[Isaiah 37.36|Isa. 37:36]]); he was slain by his sons ([[2 Kings 19.37|2 Kgs. 19:37]]; [[2 Chronicles 32.21|2 Chr. 32:21]]; [[Isaiah 37.38|Isa. 37:38]]). See *[[BD Assyria And Babylonia|Assyria and Babylonia]].*
