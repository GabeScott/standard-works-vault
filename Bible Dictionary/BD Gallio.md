# Gallio

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/gallio?lang=eng)

Deputy of Achaia, who refused to be a judge of Jewish religious disputes ([[Acts 18.12|Acts 18:12–17]][[Acts 18.13|]][[Acts 18.14|]][[Acts 18.15|]][[Acts 18.16|]][[Acts 18.17|]]). A significant feature of this event is that it shows that the Roman officials at that time saw no threat from Christianity and regarded it only as a sect of Judaism. A few years later the empire changed its view concerning Christianity.
