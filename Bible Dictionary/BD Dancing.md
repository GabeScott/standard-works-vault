# Dancing

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/dancing?lang=eng)

A natural sign of rejoicing, and as such frequently formed part of religious ceremonies ([[Exodus 15.20|Ex. 15:20]]; [[Exodus 32.19|32:19]]; [[Judges 11.34|Judg. 11:34]]; [[2 Samuel 6.14|2 Sam. 6:14–16]][[2 Samuel 6.15|]][[2 Samuel 6.16|]]; [[1 Chronicles 15.29|1 Chr. 15:29]]). The dancing was often accompanied by a song with chorus and instrumental music ([[Psalms 68.25|Ps. 68:25]]; [[Psalms 149.3|149:3]]; [[Psalms 150.4|150:4]]).
