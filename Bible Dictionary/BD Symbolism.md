# Symbolism

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/symbolism?lang=eng)

The scriptures are rich in symbolism and figurative expression. Ceremonies and ordinances are symbolic in their performance, and all bear record of Jesus Christ ([[Moses 6.63|Moses 6:63]]). Such symbolism is seen in sacrifice ([[Hebrews 9.11|Heb. 9:11–15]][[Hebrews 9.12|]][[Hebrews 9.13|]][[Hebrews 9.14|]][[Hebrews 9.15|]]; [[Moses 5.7|Moses 5:7–8]][[Moses 5.8|]]); in the sacrament ([[JST Mark 14.20|JST Mark 14:20–24 (Appendix)]][[JST Mark 14.21|]][[JST Mark 14.22|]][[JST Mark 14.23|]][[JST Mark 14.24|]]; [[Luke 22.13|Luke 22:13–20]][[Luke 22.14|]][[Luke 22.15|]][[Luke 22.16|]][[Luke 22.17|]][[Luke 22.18|]][[Luke 22.19|]][[Luke 22.20|]]); and in baptism ([[Romans 6.1|Rom. 6:1–6]][[Romans 6.2|]][[Romans 6.3|]][[Romans 6.4|]][[Romans 6.5|]][[Romans 6.6|]]; [[D&C 128.12|D&C 128:12–13]][[D&C 128.13|]]). Biblical names especially are symbolic (see *[[BD Christ Names Of|Christ, names of]]* and *[[BD Names Of Persons|Names of persons]]*). The tabernacle ceremony was symbolic of eternal things ([[Hebrews 8.1|Heb. 8–10]]), as was the entire law of Moses, containing types and shadows of Christ ([[Mosiah 13.29|Mosiah 13:29–32]][[Mosiah 13.30|]][[Mosiah 13.31|]][[Mosiah 13.32|]]; [[Alma 25.15|Alma 25:15]]). For other symbolic items see [[Exodus 20.8|Ex. 20:8–11]][[Exodus 20.9|]][[Exodus 20.10|]][[Exodus 20.11|]]; [[Matthew 5.13|Matt. 5:13–16]][[Matthew 5.14|]][[Matthew 5.15|]][[Matthew 5.16|]]; [[Jacob 4.5|Jacob 4:5]]; [[Alma 37.38|Alma 37:38–45]][[Alma 37.39|]][[Alma 37.40|]][[Alma 37.41|]][[Alma 37.42|]][[Alma 37.43|]][[Alma 37.44|]][[Alma 37.45|]].
