# Earnest

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/earnest?lang=eng)

A pledge or security. The word thus translated is a commercial term denoting the deposit paid by a buyer on entering into an agreement for the purchase of anything. As used by Paul ([[2 Corinthians 1.22|2 Cor. 1:22]]; [[2 Corinthians 5.5|5:5]]; [[Ephesians 1.14|Eph. 1:14]]) it means that the Lord gives us His Holy Spirit in this life as a foretaste of the joy of eternal life. The Spirit is also the Lord’s surety that He will fulfill His promise to give eternal life to the faithful.
