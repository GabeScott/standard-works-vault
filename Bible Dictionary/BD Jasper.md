# Jasper

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jasper?lang=eng)

A precious stone, one of those in the high priest’s breastplate ([[Exodus 28.20|Ex. 28:20]]; [[Exodus 39.13|39:13]]); also in the walls of the “heavenly Jerusalem” ([[Revelation 21.19|Rev. 21:19]]; see also [[Revelation 4.3|Rev. 4:3]]). Jaspers were of various colors, most frequently green (see also [[Revelation 21.11|Rev. 21:11]]).
