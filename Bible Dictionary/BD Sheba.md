# Sheba

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/sheba?lang=eng)

(1) Grandson of Cush ([[Genesis 10.7|Gen. 10:7]]; [[1 Chronicles 1.9|1 Chr. 1:9]]).

(2) Son of Joktan ([[Genesis 10.28|Gen. 10:28]]; [[1 Chronicles 1.22|1 Chr. 1:22]]); his descendants gave their name to a portion of southern Arabia (now called Arabia Felix).
