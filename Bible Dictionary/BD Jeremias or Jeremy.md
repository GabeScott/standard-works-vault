# Jeremias or Jeremy

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jeremias?lang=eng)

In [[Matthew 16.14|Matt. 16:14]]; [[Matthew 27.9|27:9]], same as Jeremiah. There was another Jeremy who lived near the time of Abraham and who held the Melchizedek Priesthood. Nothing more is known of him today. [[D&C 84.9|D&C 84:9–10]][[D&C 84.10|]].
