# Samaria

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/samaria?lang=eng)

So called from Shemer, its first owner ([[1 Kings 16.24|1 Kgs. 16:24]]); a city built by Omri and made the capital of the kingdom of Israel. It occupied a strong position on a hill, and the Assyrians could not capture it until after a siege of three years ([[2 Kings 17.5|2 Kgs. 17:5–6]][[2 Kings 17.6|]]). Herod rebuilt it and called it Sebaste. In New Testament times Samaria was the name of the whole of the central district of Palestine west of the Jordan. See also *[[BD Samaritans|Samaritans]].*
