# Regeneration

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/regeneration?lang=eng)

The act by which a person is born again. Baptism is called the “washing of regeneration” ([[Titus 3.5|Titus 3:5]]), because through it one’s sins are washed away and one becomes renewed through the Holy Spirit. See also [[Matthew 19.28|Matt. 19:28]]; [[John 1.13|John 1:13]]; [[John 3.3|3:3–5]][[John 3.4|]][[John 3.5|]]; [[Ephesians 4.22|Eph. 4:22–24]][[Ephesians 4.23|]][[Ephesians 4.24|]]; [[1 Peter 1.3|1 Pet. 1:3]].
