# Captain of the Temple

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/captain?lang=eng)

([[Luke 22.4|Luke 22:4]]; [[Acts 4.1|Acts 4:1]]; [[Acts 5.24|5:24]]). Not a military officer, but one in charge of the priests and Levites who formed the temple police and kept watch by night ([[2 Kings 12.9|2 Kgs. 12:9]]; [[2 Kings 25.18|25:18]]).
