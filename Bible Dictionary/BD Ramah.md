# Ramah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/ramah?lang=eng)

*Height.* (1) A town, about the site of which there has been much dispute, in Benjamin, on the border of Ephraim ([[Joshua 18.25|Josh. 18:25]]; [[Judges 4.5|Judg. 4:5]]; [[Judges 19.13|19:13]]); the home of Samuel ([[1 Samuel 1.19|1 Sam. 1:19]]; [[1 Samuel 2.11|2:11]]; [[1 Samuel 7.17|7:17]]; [[1 Samuel 8.4|8:4]]; [[1 Samuel 15.34|15:34]]; [[1 Samuel 16.13|16:13]]; [[1 Samuel 19.18|19:18–23]][[1 Samuel 19.19|]][[1 Samuel 19.20|]][[1 Samuel 19.21|]][[1 Samuel 19.22|]][[1 Samuel 19.23|]]; [[1 Samuel 20.1|20:1]]; [[1 Samuel 22.6|22:6]]; [[1 Samuel 25.1|25:1]]; [[1 Samuel 28.3|28:3]]). Being on the frontier and on the road to Jerusalem, it was the scene of several conflicts between Israel and Judah ([[1 Kings 15.17|1 Kgs. 15:17, 21–22]][[1 Kings 15.21|]][[1 Kings 15.22|]]; [[2 Chronicles 16.1|2 Chr. 16:1, 5–6]][[2 Chronicles 16.5|]][[2 Chronicles 16.6|]]). At the time of the captivity the exiles were collected here before removal to Babylon ([[Jeremiah 31.15|Jer. 31:15]]; [[Jeremiah 40.1|40:1]]; [[Matthew 2.18|Matt. 2:18]]; see *[[BD Rachel|Rachel]];* see also [[Ezra 2.26|Ezra 2:26]]; [[Nehemiah 7.30|Neh. 7:30]]; [[Nehemiah 11.33|11:33]]; [[Isaiah 10.29|Isa. 10:29]]; [[Hosea 5.8|Hosea 5:8]]).
