# Carmel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/carmel?lang=eng)

*Garden or park.* (1) A hill, 1742 feet high, forming the one headland of the coast of Palestine. It is chiefly known in connection with the history of Elijah and Elisha ([[1 Kings 18.1|1 Kgs. 18]]; [[2 Kings 2.25|2 Kgs. 2:25]]; [[2 Kings 4.25|4:25]]); also frequently mentioned by prophets ([[Isaiah 33.9|Isa. 33:9]]; [[Isaiah 35.2|35:2]]; [[Jeremiah 46.18|Jer. 46:18]]; [[Jeremiah 50.19|50:19]]; [[Amos 1.2|Amos 1:2]]; [[Amos 9.3|9:3]]; [[Micah 7.14|Micah 7:14]]; [[Nahum 1.4|Nahum 1:4]]).

(2) A town in the hill country of Judah ([[Joshua 15.55|Josh. 15:55]]; [[1 Samuel 15.12|1 Sam. 15:12]]); residence of Nabal ([[1 Samuel 25.1|1 Sam. 25]]); and native place of David’s wife Abigail ([[1 Samuel 27.3|1 Sam. 27:3]]).
