# Leaven

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/leaven?lang=eng)

Anything that in cooking produces fermentation, a lump of old dough being generally used. No leaven was allowed during the Passover Feast ([[Exodus 12.15|Ex. 12:15, 19]][[Exodus 12.19|]]; [[Exodus 13.7|13:7]]; [[Deuteronomy 16.4|Deut. 16:4]]) or in offerings made to God with fire ([[Exodus 29.2|Ex. 29:2]]; [[Leviticus 2.11|Lev. 2:11]]; [[Leviticus 7.12|7:12]]; [[Numbers 6.15|Num. 6:15]]). It was probably forbidden because there was associated with it the idea of corruption. In the New Testament it was as a rule symbolic of sin ([[Matthew 16.6|Matt. 16:6]]; [[Mark 8.15|Mark 8:15]]; [[Luke 12.1|Luke 12:1]]; [[1 Corinthians 5.7|1 Cor. 5:7]]; [[Galatians 5.9|Gal. 5:9]]); but in [[Matthew 13.33|Matt. 13:33]] it is compared to the inward growth and influence of the kingdom of heaven (church) upon the earth.
