# Machaerus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/machaerus?lang=eng)

A fortress in Perea above the northeastern shore of the Dead Sea. It is not mentioned by name in the New Testament, but Josephus (*Antiquities,* 18.5.1–2) mentions it as the place where John the Baptist was imprisoned and beheaded, as in [[Matthew 14.10|Matt. 14:10–11]][[Matthew 14.11|]] and [[Mark 6.17|Mark 6:17–28]][[Mark 6.18|]][[Mark 6.19|]][[Mark 6.20|]][[Mark 6.21|]][[Mark 6.22|]][[Mark 6.23|]][[Mark 6.24|]][[Mark 6.25|]][[Mark 6.26|]][[Mark 6.27|]][[Mark 6.28|]]. Some ruins still remain at the site.
