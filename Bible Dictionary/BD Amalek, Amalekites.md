# Amalek, Amalekites

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/amalek?lang=eng)

An Arab tribe that lived in the desert of Paran between the Arabah and the Mediterranean and at one time seem to have ranged as far north as Mount Ephraim ([[Judges 5.14|Judg. 5:14]]; [[Judges 12.15|12:15]]).
