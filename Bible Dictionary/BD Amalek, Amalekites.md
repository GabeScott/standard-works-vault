# Amalek, Amalekites

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/amalek?lang=eng)

An Arab tribe that lived in the desert of Paran between the Arabah and the Mediterranean and at one time seem to have ranged as far north as Mount Ephraim ([[Judges 5.14|Judg. 5:14]]; [[Judges 12.15|12:15]]). The Amalekites were at constant war with the Hebrews from the time of Moses ([[Exodus 17.8|Ex. 17:8]], etc.) till their power was broken by Saul and David ([[1 Samuel 15.1|1 Sam. 15]]; [[1 Samuel 27.8|27:8]]; [[1 Samuel 30.1|30]]; [[2 Samuel 8.12|2 Sam. 8:12]]), and their last remnant was destroyed by the Simeonites ([[1 Chronicles 4.43|1 Chr. 4:43]]).
