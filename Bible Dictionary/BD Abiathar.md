# Abiathar

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/abiathar?lang=eng)

*Father of excellence,* or *plenty.* A son of the high priest Ahijah or Ahimelech, he escapes Saul ([[1 Samuel 22.20|1 Sam. 22:20]]); abides with David ([[1 Samuel 23.6|23:6, 9]][[1 Samuel 23.9|]]; [[1 Samuel 30.7|30:7]]); brings the ark back to Jerusalem ([[2 Samuel 15.24|2 Sam. 15:24–36]][[2 Samuel 15.25|]][[2 Samuel 15.26|]][[2 Samuel 15.27|]][[2 Samuel 15.28|]][[2 Samuel 15.29|]][[2 Samuel 15.30|]][[2 Samuel 15.31|]][[2 Samuel 15.32|]][[2 Samuel 15.33|]][[2 Samuel 15.34|]][[2 Samuel 15.35|]][[2 Samuel 15.36|]]; [[2 Samuel 17.15|17:15]]; [[2 Samuel 19.11|19:11]]); takes part in Adonijah’s rebellion ([[1 Kings 1.7|1 Kgs. 1:7]]); is thrust out from the priesthood ([[1 Kings 2.27|2:27, 35]][[1 Kings 2.35|]]; [[Mark 2.26|Mark 2:26]]). Ahimelech and Abiathar are probably interchanged in [[2 Samuel 8.17|2 Sam. 8:17]]; [[1 Chronicles 18.16|1 Chr. 18:16]]; [[1 Chronicles 24.3|24:3, 31]][[1 Chronicles 24.31|]]. See also [[2 Samuel 20.25|2 Sam. 20:25]].
