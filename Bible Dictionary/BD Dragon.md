# Dragon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/dragon?lang=eng)

Two Hebrew words are thus rendered though they are sometimes translated whale, serpent, jackal, or sea-monster. Probably a large snake or snakelike animal in [[Deuteronomy 32.33|Deut. 32:33]]; [[Psalms 74.13|Ps. 74:13]]; [[Psalms 91.13|91:13]]. A jackal in [[Job 30.29|Job 30:29]]; [[Isaiah 13.22|Isa. 13:22]]; [[Isaiah 27.1|27:1]]; [[Isaiah 34.13|34:13]]; [[Isaiah 35.7|35:7]]; [[Isaiah 43.20|43:20]]; [[Isaiah 51.9|51:9]]; [[Jeremiah 9.11|Jer. 9:11]]; [[Jeremiah 10.22|10:22]]; [[Jeremiah 51.34|51:34]]; [[Malachi 1.3|Mal. 1:3]]. Used figuratively in [[Ezekiel 29.3|Ezek. 29:3]]. In the New Testament it is always used figuratively, as in [[Revelation 12.3|Rev. 12:3, 7, 9, 13, 16]][[Revelation 12.7|]][[Revelation 12.9|]][[Revelation 12.13|]][[Revelation 12.16|]]; [[Revelation 13.2|13:2, 4, 11]][[Revelation 13.4|]][[Revelation 13.11|]]; [[Revelation 16.13|16:13]]; [[Revelation 20.2|20:2]]; see also [[Mosiah 20.11|Mosiah 20:11]]; [[Alma 43.44|Alma 43:44]].
