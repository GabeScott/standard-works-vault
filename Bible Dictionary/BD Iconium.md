# Iconium

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/iconium?lang=eng)

A town of Asia Minor, on the high tableland north of the Taurus mountains, on the borders between Phrygia and Lycaonia. It was visited by Paul on his first missionary journey ([[Acts 13.51|Acts 13:51–52]][[Acts 13.52|]]; [[Acts 14.1|14:1–6, 19, 21]][[Acts 14.2|]][[Acts 14.3|]][[Acts 14.4|]][[Acts 14.5|]][[Acts 14.6|]][[Acts 14.19|]][[Acts 14.21|]]); also probably on the second and third journeys ([[Acts 16.2|Acts 16:2]]; [[Acts 18.23|18:23]]). His sufferings there were long remembered ([[2 Timothy 3.11|2 Tim. 3:11]]).
