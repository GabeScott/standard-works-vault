# Leah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/leah?lang=eng)

*Weary.* Eldest daughter of Laban. She became Jacob’s wife through a trick of Laban’s, who passed her off as his daughter Rachel; being veiled, the mistake was not discovered till after the marriage had taken place ([[Genesis 29.1|Gen. 29]]). She became the mother of six sons and one daughter ([[Genesis 29.30|Gen. 29:30–32]][[Genesis 29.31|]][[Genesis 29.32|]]; [[Genesis 30.17|30:17–21]][[Genesis 30.18|]][[Genesis 30.19|]][[Genesis 30.20|]][[Genesis 30.21|]]; see also [[Genesis 31.4|31:4, 14, 33]][[Genesis 31.14|]][[Genesis 31.33|]]; [[Genesis 33.1|33:1–2, 7]][[Genesis 33.2|]][[Genesis 33.7|]]; [[Genesis 35.23|35:23, 26]][[Genesis 35.26|]]; [[Genesis 49.31|49:31]]).
