# Apollos

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/apollos?lang=eng)

An Alexandrian Jew, an eloquent man and mighty in the scriptures ([[Acts 18.1|Acts 18:24–19:1]][[Acts 18.2|]][[Acts 18.3|]][[Acts 18.4|]][[Acts 18.5|]][[Acts 18.6|]][[Acts 18.7|]][[Acts 18.8|]][[Acts 18.9|]][[Acts 18.10|]][[Acts 18.11|]][[Acts 18.12|]][[Acts 18.13|]][[Acts 18.14|]][[Acts 18.15|]][[Acts 18.16|]][[Acts 18.17|]][[Acts 18.18|]][[Acts 18.19|]][[Acts 18.20|]][[Acts 18.21|]][[Acts 18.22|]][[Acts 18.23|]][[Acts 18.24|]][[Acts 18.25|]][[Acts 18.26|]][[Acts 18.27|]][[Acts 18.28|]]). He received instruction in the gospel at Ephesus from Aquila and Priscilla and then went to Corinth; his name was afterwards connected with one of the disputes that arose there ([[1 Corinthians 1.12|1 Cor. 1:12]]; [[1 Corinthians 3.4|3:4–6, 22]][[1 Corinthians 3.5|]][[1 Corinthians 3.6|]][[1 Corinthians 3.22|]]; [[1 Corinthians 16.12|16:12]]). We afterwards hear of him in Crete ([[Titus 3.13|Titus 3:13]]).
