# Apollos

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/apollos?lang=eng)

An Alexandrian Jew, an eloquent man and mighty in the scriptures ([[Acts 18.1|Acts 18:24–19:1]]). He received instruction in the gospel at Ephesus from Aquila and Priscilla and then went to Corinth; his name was afterwards connected with one of the disputes that arose there ([[1 Corinthians 1.12|1 Cor. 1:12]]; [[1 Corinthians 3.4|3:4–6, 22]][[1 Corinthians 3.5|]][[1 Corinthians 3.6|]][[1 Corinthians 3.22|]]; [[1 Corinthians 16.12|16:12]]). We afterwards hear of him in Crete ([[Titus 3.13|Titus 3:13]]).
