# Elam

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/elam?lang=eng)

(1) A mountainous country to the east of Babylonia, with the Persian Gulf on the south and southwest ([[Genesis 10.22|Gen. 10:22]]; [[Acts 2.9|Acts 2:9]]). See *[[BD Chedorlaomer|Chedorlaomer]].* Its chief town was Shushan; prophecies concerning Elam are found in [[Isaiah 11.11|Isa. 11:11]]; [[Isaiah 21.2|21:2]]; [[Isaiah 22.6|22:6]]; [[Jeremiah 25.25|Jer. 25:25]]; [[Jeremiah 49.34|49:34–39]][[Jeremiah 49.35|]][[Jeremiah 49.36|]][[Jeremiah 49.37|]][[Jeremiah 49.38|]][[Jeremiah 49.39|]]; [[Ezekiel 32.24|Ezek. 32:24]].

(2) Several men of this name are mentioned, but only in genealogies.
