# Eliakim

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/eliakim?lang=eng)

*God raiseth up.* (1) Son of Hilkiah and master of Hezekiah’s household ([[2 Kings 18.18|2 Kgs. 18:18, 26, 37]][[2 Kings 18.26|]][[2 Kings 18.37|]]; [[2 Kings 19.2|19:2]]; [[Isaiah 22.20|Isa. 22:20–25]][[Isaiah 22.21|]][[Isaiah 22.22|]][[Isaiah 22.23|]][[Isaiah 22.24|]][[Isaiah 22.25|]]; [[Isaiah 36.3|36:3, 11, 22]][[Isaiah 36.11|]][[Isaiah 36.22|]]; [[Isaiah 37.2|37:2]]).

(2) Original name of Jehoiakim, king of Judah ([[2 Kings 23.34|2 Kgs. 23:34]]; [[2 Chronicles 36.4|2 Chr. 36:4]]).

(3) Others of the same name ([[Nehemiah 12.41|Neh. 12:41]]; [[Matthew 1.13|Matt. 1:13]]; [[Luke 3.30|Luke 3:30]]).
