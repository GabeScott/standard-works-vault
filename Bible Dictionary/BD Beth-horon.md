# Beth-horon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/beth-horon?lang=eng)

*House of caves.* The pass of Beth-horon was part of the vital road from Jerusalem to the sea. The lower end widens out into the valley of Ajalon. The area has seen many battles, including the defeat of the Amorites by Joshua assisted by a hailstorm ([[Joshua 10.8|Josh. 10:8–11]][[Joshua 10.9|]][[Joshua 10.10|]][[Joshua 10.11|]]). See also [[1 Samuel 13.18|1 Sam. 13:18]]; [[2 Chronicles 25.13|2 Chr. 25:13]].
