# Pekah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/pekah?lang=eng)

*Open-eyed.* Son of Remaliah; kills his master Pekahiah and becomes king of Israel ([[2 Kings 15.25|2 Kgs. 15:25–32]][[2 Kings 15.26|]][[2 Kings 15.27|]][[2 Kings 15.28|]][[2 Kings 15.29|]][[2 Kings 15.30|]][[2 Kings 15.31|]][[2 Kings 15.32|]]; [[2 Kings 16.1|16:1]]); invades Judah with Rezin ([[2 Kings 15.37|2 Kgs. 15:37]]; [[2 Kings 16.5|16:5]]; [[2 Chronicles 28.6|2 Chr. 28:6]]; [[Isaiah 7.1|Isa. 7:1]]); Israel invaded by Tiglath-pileser; Pekah killed by Hoshea ([[2 Kings 15.29|2 Kgs. 15:29–30]][[2 Kings 15.30|]]). There is much difficulty in deciding the chronology of Pekah’s reign. See [Bible Chronology](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bible-chron/old-testament?lang=eng&id=p192-p193,p206-p207#p192) in the appendix.
