# Teraphim

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/teraphim?lang=eng)

Images of the size and form of a man ([[1 Samuel 19.13|1 Sam. 19:13, 16]][[1 Samuel 19.16|]]) used from patriarchal times ([[Genesis 31.30|Gen. 31:30, 32]][[Genesis 31.32|]]) and onwards ([[Judges 17.5|Judg. 17:5]]; [[Hosea 3.4|Hosea 3:4–5]][[Hosea 3.5|]]; [[Zechariah 10.2|Zech. 10:2]]) in worship and for magical purposes both in Israel and in Babylon ([[Ezekiel 21.21|Ezek. 21:21]]). To use teraphim was probably not to worship strange gods but to worship the true God in a corrupt manner.
