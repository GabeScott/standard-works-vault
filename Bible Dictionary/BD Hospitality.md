# Hospitality

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hospitality?lang=eng)

This has always been regarded by Eastern nations as a duty of the highest importance. [[Genesis 18.1|Gen. 18:1–5]][[Genesis 18.2|]][[Genesis 18.3|]][[Genesis 18.4|]][[Genesis 18.5|]]; [[Genesis 19.1|19:1–3]][[Genesis 19.2|]][[Genesis 19.3|]]; [[Exodus 2.20|Ex. 2:20]]; [[Judges 13.15|Judg. 13:15]]; [[Judges 19.20|19:20–21]][[Judges 19.21|]] tell of instances of the readiness with which hospitality was offered to strangers. Such hospitality was a necessity of desert life. A host was bound at all cost to protect the life and property of his guest, even though previously unknown to him. To taste another man’s salt was to establish a claim on his protection. The New Testament enjoins the duty even more emphatically ([[Matthew 10.42|Matt. 10:42]]; [[Matthew 25.43|25:43]]; [[Romans 12.13|Rom. 12:13]]; [[1 Timothy 3.2|1 Tim. 3:2]]; [[Titus 1.8|Titus 1:8]]; [[Hebrews 13.2|Heb. 13:2]]; [[1 Peter 4.9|1 Pet. 4:9]]).
