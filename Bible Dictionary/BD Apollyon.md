# Apollyon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/apollyon?lang=eng)

*Destroyer,* a Greek translation of the Hebrew word *Abaddon,* or “Destruction”; in [[Revelation 9.11|Rev. 9:11]] it is the name of the Angel of the Abyss (bottomless pit) made familiar to English readers by Bunyan’s *Pilgrim’s Progress.*
