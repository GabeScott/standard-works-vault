# Levites

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/levites?lang=eng)

Descendants of Levi. The work of ministering in the sanctuary was assigned to this tribe. The Levites are sometimes spoken of as distinct from the priests ([[1 Kings 8.4|1 Kgs. 8:4]]; [[Ezra 2.70|Ezra 2:70]]; [[John 1.19|John 1:19]]); sometimes as though all Levites were also priests, “the priests, the Levites” ([[Deuteronomy 18.1|Deut. 18:1]]; [[Joshua 3.3|Josh. 3:3]]). The work of the Levites was to assist the priests ([[Numbers 3.5|Num. 3:5–10]][[Numbers 3.6|]][[Numbers 3.7|]][[Numbers 3.8|]][[Numbers 3.9|]][[Numbers 3.10|]]; [[Numbers 18.1|18:1–7]][[Numbers 18.2|]][[Numbers 18.3|]][[Numbers 18.4|]][[Numbers 18.5|]][[Numbers 18.6|]][[Numbers 18.7|]]). They acted as musicians ([[1 Chronicles 6.16|1 Chr. 6:16, 31]][[1 Chronicles 6.31|]]; [[1 Chronicles 15.16|15:16]]; [[Nehemiah 11.17|Neh. 11:17, 22]][[Nehemiah 11.22|]]); slaughtered the sacrifices ([[2 Chronicles 29.34|2 Chr. 29:34]]; [[2 Chronicles 35.11|35:11]]; [[Ezra 6.20|Ezra 6:20]]); and generally assisted in the temple ([[Nehemiah 11.16|Neh. 11:16, 19]][[Nehemiah 11.19|]]). The Levites were themselves offered as a wave-offering on behalf of the children of Israel ([[Numbers 8.11|Num. 8:11–15]][[Numbers 8.12|]][[Numbers 8.13|]][[Numbers 8.14|]][[Numbers 8.15|]]); they thus became God’s peculiar property, given to Him in place of the firstborn ([[Numbers 8.16|8:16]]). They were cleansed for their office ([[Numbers 8.7|8:7–16]][[Numbers 8.8|]][[Numbers 8.9|]][[Numbers 8.10|]][[Numbers 8.11|]][[Numbers 8.12|]][[Numbers 8.13|]][[Numbers 8.14|]][[Numbers 8.15|]][[Numbers 8.16|]]). They had no inheritance in Canaan ([[Numbers 18.23|18:23–24]][[Numbers 18.24|]]); but they had the tithe ([[Numbers 18.21|18:21]]), 48 cities ([[Numbers 35.6|35:6]]), and a claim on the alms of the people at feast times ([[Deuteronomy 12.18|Deut. 12:18–19]][[Deuteronomy 12.19|]]; [[Deuteronomy 14.27|14:27–29]][[Deuteronomy 14.28|]][[Deuteronomy 14.29|]]).
