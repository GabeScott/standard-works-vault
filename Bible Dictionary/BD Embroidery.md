# Embroidery

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/embroidery?lang=eng)

Frequently referred to in the Old Testament ([[Exodus 28.39|Ex. 28:39]]; [[Judges 5.30|Judg. 5:30]]; [[Psalms 45.14|Ps. 45:14]]; [[Ezekiel 16.10|Ezek. 16:10, 13, 18]][[Ezekiel 16.13|]][[Ezekiel 16.18|]]). The pattern was sometimes woven into the material, sometimes worked by hand with a needle on cloth or linen stretched on a frame. Embroidery was used for hangings in the tabernacle, on the priests’ clothing, and also in everyday dress.
