# Holy One of Israel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/holy-one-of-israel?lang=eng)

A name for the Savior used 34 times in the Old Testament, most frequently by Isaiah, who cites it 28 times (27 times in the book of Isaiah and once in [[2 Kings 19.22|2 Kgs. 19:22]]). It appears three times in the Psalms ([[Psalms 71.22|71:22]]; [[Psalms 78.41|78:41]]; [[Psalms 89.18|89:18]]), twice in Jeremiah ([[Jeremiah 50.29|50:29]]; [[Jeremiah 51.5|51:5]]), and once in Ezekiel ([[Ezekiel 39.7|39:7]]). Some references in Isaiah are [[Isaiah 1.4|1:4]]; [[Isaiah 5.19|5:19]]; [[Isaiah 12.6|12:6]]; [[Isaiah 30.11|30:11–12, 15]][[Isaiah 30.12|]][[Isaiah 30.15|]]; [[Isaiah 48.17|48:17]]; [[Isaiah 60.14|60:14]]. The term is used frequently in the Book of Mormon, especially in 1 and 2 Nephi. The Holy One of Israel is identified as Christ in [[2 Nephi 25.29|2 Ne. 25:29]].
