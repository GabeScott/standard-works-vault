# Juda

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/juda?lang=eng)

(1) Son of Joanna or Joanan ([[Luke 3.27|Luke 3:27]]).

(2) Mentioned in our Lord’s genealogy ([[Luke 3.30|Luke 3:30]]).

(3) Patriarch Judah ([[Matthew 2.6|Matt. 2:6]] and elsewhere in the New Testament).

(4) One of the Lord’s “brethren” ([[Mark 6.3|Mark 6:3]]).
