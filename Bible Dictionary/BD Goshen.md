# Goshen

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/goshen?lang=eng)

(1) The part of Egypt in which the Israelites dwelt. It contained pasture land ([[Genesis 46.33|Gen. 46:33–34]][[Genesis 46.34|]]) and lay somewhere between Palestine and the capital of Egypt ([[Genesis 46.28|46:28–29]][[Genesis 46.29|]]), probably in the eastern part of the delta of the Nile.

(2) A district in southern Palestine, position uncertain ([[Joshua 10.41|Josh. 10:41]]; [[Joshua 11.16|11:16]]).

(3) A town of Judah ([[Joshua 15.51|Josh. 15:51]]).
