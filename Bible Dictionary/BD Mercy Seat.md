# Mercy Seat

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/mercy-seat?lang=eng)

The golden covering of the Ark of the Covenant in the Holy of Holies. It was the place of the manifestation of God’s glory and His meeting place with His people ([[Exodus 25.22|Ex. 25:22]]; [[Leviticus 16.2|Lev. 16:2]]; [[Numbers 7.89|Num. 7:89]]); and was regarded as the Throne of God ([[Numbers 7.89|Num. 7:89]]; see also [[Exodus 30.6|Ex. 30:6]]; [[1 Samuel 4.4|1 Sam. 4:4]]). Here the blood of the sin offering was sprinkled on the Day of Atonement ([[Leviticus 16.14|Lev. 16:14–15]][[Leviticus 16.15|]]). See *[[BD Tabernacle|Tabernacle]].*
