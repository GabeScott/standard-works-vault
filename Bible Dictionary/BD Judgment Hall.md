# Judgment Hall

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/judgment-hall?lang=eng)

Sometimes used in the KJV for the Latin word *Praetorium.* In [[John 18.28|John 18:28, 33]][[John 18.33|]]; [[John 19.9|19:9]], it denotes Pilate’s official residence in Jerusalem; in [[Acts 23.35|Acts 23:35]] it denotes a part of the buildings erected by Herod in Caesarea. In [[Matthew 27.27|Matt. 27:27]] *praetorium* is translated “common hall” or “governor’s house” (Pilate’s house); in [[Philippians 1.13|Philip. 1:13]] it is translated “palace” or “Caesar’s court,” meaning the officers of the court before which Paul was tried.
