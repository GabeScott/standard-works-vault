# Magog

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/magog?lang=eng)

*Covering.* (1) Son of Japheth ([[Genesis 10.2|Gen. 10:2]]; [[1 Chronicles 1.5|1 Chr. 1:5]]).

(2) A country or people near the Black Sea, and equivalent to Scythian ([[Ezekiel 38.2|Ezek. 38:2]]; [[Ezekiel 39.6|39:6]]; [[Revelation 20.8|Rev. 20:8]]). See also *[[BD Gog|Gog]].*
