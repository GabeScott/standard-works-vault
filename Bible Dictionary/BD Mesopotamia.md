# Mesopotamia

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/mesopotamia?lang=eng)

Hebrew *Aram-naharaim* (see [Ps. 60 heading](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ps/60?lang=eng&id=intro1#intro1)), *Aram of the two rivers* (Euphrates and Tigris), the name being specially applied to the fruitful country between the upper parts of these two rivers. In this region Nahor settled after leaving Ur of the Chaldees ([[Genesis 24.10|Gen. 24:10]]); and here other members of the family remained after Abraham had moved on into Canaan. See *[[BD Aram, Arameans|Aram]].*
