# Book of life

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/book-of-life?lang=eng)

Spoken of in [[Philippians 4.3|Philip. 4:3]]; [[Revelation 3.5|Rev. 3:5]]; [[Revelation 13.8|13:8]]; [[Revelation 17.8|17:8]]; [[Revelation 20.12|20:12]]; [[Revelation 21.27|21:27]]; [[Revelation 22.19|22:19]]; see also [[Daniel 12.1|Dan. 12:1–4]][[Daniel 12.2|]][[Daniel 12.3|]][[Daniel 12.4|]]; [[Luke 10.20|Luke 10:20]]. In one sense the book of life is the sum total of one’s thoughts and actions—the record of his life. However, the scriptures indicate that a heavenly record is kept of the faithful, whose names are recorded, as well as an account of their righteous deeds ([[D&C 88.2|D&C 88:2]]; [[D&C 128.7|128:7]]).
