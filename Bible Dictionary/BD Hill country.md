# Hill country

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hill-country?lang=eng)

A geographical term of frequent occurrence ([[Joshua 13.6|Josh. 13:6]]; [[Joshua 21.11|21:11]]; [[Luke 1.39|Luke 1:39, 65]][[Luke 1.65|]]), denoting the ridge of hills extending from Jezreel to Beersheba, attaining a maximum elevation of about 3000 feet near Hebron. The hills are sometimes bare but more often covered with herbage and shrubbery; the lower slopes are adapted for the culture of the vine, and the valleys are often very fertile.
