# Jesse

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jesse?lang=eng)

Father of David, and therefore ancestor of all the kings of Judah and also of Christ ([[Ruth 4.17|Ruth 4:17, 22]][[Ruth 4.22|]]; [[1 Chronicles 2.5|1 Chr. 2:5–12]][[1 Chronicles 2.6|]][[1 Chronicles 2.7|]][[1 Chronicles 2.8|]][[1 Chronicles 2.9|]][[1 Chronicles 2.10|]][[1 Chronicles 2.11|]][[1 Chronicles 2.12|]]; [[Matthew 1.5|Matt. 1:5–6]][[Matthew 1.6|]]). He is repeatedly mentioned in connection with the early history of David ([[1 Samuel 16.18|1 Sam. 16:18]]; see also [[Isaiah 11.1|Isa. 11:1, 10]][[Isaiah 11.10|]]). We are not told the name of Jesse’s wife.
