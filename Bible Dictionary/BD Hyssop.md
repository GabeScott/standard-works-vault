# Hyssop

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hyssop?lang=eng)

A kind of wild marjoram, used at the Passover ([[Exodus 12.22|Ex. 12:22]]); for sprinkling purposes in the purification of lepers ([[Leviticus 14.4|Lev. 14:4, 51]][[Leviticus 14.51|]]) and in the sacrifice of the red heifer ([[Numbers 19.6|Num. 19:6]]; see also [[1 Kings 4.33|1 Kgs. 4:33]]; [[Psalms 51.7|Ps. 51:7]]). According to John ([[John 19.29|19:29]]), the sponge with the vinegar offered to our Lord on the cross was “put upon hyssop.” Matthew and Mark do not mention the hyssop but only the reed by which it was raised to Jesus’ mouth.
