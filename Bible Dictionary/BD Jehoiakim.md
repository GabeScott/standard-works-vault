# Jehoiakim

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jehoiakim?lang=eng)

*The Lord raiseth up.* King of Judah (609–598 ), originally called Eliakim ([[2 Kings 23.34|2 Kgs. 23:34–36]][[2 Kings 23.35|]][[2 Kings 23.36|]]; [[2 Kings 24.1|24:1–6, 19]][[2 Kings 24.2|]][[2 Kings 24.3|]][[2 Kings 24.4|]][[2 Kings 24.5|]][[2 Kings 24.6|]][[2 Kings 24.19|]]; [[1 Chronicles 3.15|1 Chr. 3:15–16]][[1 Chronicles 3.16|]]; [[2 Chronicles 36.4|2 Chr. 36:4–5, 8]][[2 Chronicles 36.5|]][[2 Chronicles 36.8|]]; [[Jeremiah 1.3|Jer. 1:3]]; [[Jeremiah 22.18|22:18, 24]][[Jeremiah 22.24|]]; [[Jeremiah 26.1|26:1, 21–23]][[Jeremiah 26.21|]][[Jeremiah 26.22|]][[Jeremiah 26.23|]]; [[Jeremiah 27.1|27:1, 20]][[Jeremiah 27.20|]]; [[Jeremiah 28.4|28:4]]; [[Jeremiah 35.1|35:1]]; [[Jeremiah 36.1|36:1, 9, 28–32]][[Jeremiah 36.9|]][[Jeremiah 36.28|]][[Jeremiah 36.29|]][[Jeremiah 36.30|]][[Jeremiah 36.31|]][[Jeremiah 36.32|]]). Jehoiakim was son of Joash, king of Judah, and brother of Zedekiah. He was not a faithful ruler; an interesting event is told of him burning the manuscript of one of the prophecies of Jeremiah ([[Jeremiah 36.1|Jer. 36:1–26]][[Jeremiah 36.2|]][[Jeremiah 36.3|]][[Jeremiah 36.4|]][[Jeremiah 36.5|]][[Jeremiah 36.6|]][[Jeremiah 36.7|]][[Jeremiah 36.8|]][[Jeremiah 36.9|]][[Jeremiah 36.10|]][[Jeremiah 36.11|]][[Jeremiah 36.12|]][[Jeremiah 36.13|]][[Jeremiah 36.14|]][[Jeremiah 36.15|]][[Jeremiah 36.16|]][[Jeremiah 36.17|]][[Jeremiah 36.18|]][[Jeremiah 36.19|]][[Jeremiah 36.20|]][[Jeremiah 36.21|]][[Jeremiah 36.22|]][[Jeremiah 36.23|]][[Jeremiah 36.24|]][[Jeremiah 36.25|]][[Jeremiah 36.26|]]), whereupon Jeremiah wrote the prophecy again. Jehoiakim was given a dishonorable burial of an ass ([[Jeremiah 22.18|Jer. 22:18–19]][[Jeremiah 22.19|]]). He was succeeded by his son Jehoiachin, who in turn was succeeded by Zedekiah. See [Bible Chronology](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bible-chron/old-testament?lang=eng&id=p254-p255#p254) in the appendix.
