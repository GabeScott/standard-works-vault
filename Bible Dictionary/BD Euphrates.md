# Euphrates

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/euphrates?lang=eng)

An important river that rises in the mountains of Armenia and falls into the Persian Gulf, frequently called in scripture “the River.” It provided a route of communication between east and west. It was the boundary of the land promised to the seed of Abraham ([[Genesis 15.18|Gen. 15:18]]; [[Deuteronomy 1.7|Deut. 1:7]]; [[Joshua 1.4|Josh. 1:4]]; [[1 Chronicles 5.9|1 Chr. 5:9]]); this boundary was reached in the time of David ([[2 Samuel 8.3|2 Sam. 8:3]]; [[1 Chronicles 18.3|1 Chr. 18:3]]). The stronghold of Carchemish was built at the ford of the river and was a position of great importance in military operations ([[2 Chronicles 35.20|2 Chr. 35:20]]; [[Jeremiah 46.2|Jer. 46:2]]). On the banks of the Euphrates stood the great city of Babylon.
