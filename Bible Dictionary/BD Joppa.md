# Joppa

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/joppa?lang=eng)

(Modern *Jaffa.*) A town on the southwest coast of Palestine, the nearest port to Jerusalem (called “Japho” in [[Joshua 19.46|Josh. 19:46]]). It became a place of importance as a harbor in Solomon’s reign ([[2 Chronicles 2.16|2 Chr. 2:16]]; see also [[Ezra 3.7|Ezra 3:7]]; [[Jonah 1.3|Jonah 1:3]]; [[Acts 9.36|Acts 9:36–43]][[Acts 9.37|]][[Acts 9.38|]][[Acts 9.39|]][[Acts 9.40|]][[Acts 9.41|]][[Acts 9.42|]][[Acts 9.43|]]; [[Acts 10.5|10:5, 8, 23, 32]][[Acts 10.8|]][[Acts 10.23|]][[Acts 10.32|]]; [[Acts 11.5|11:5]]). In New Testament times it was the only town on the coast that was more Jewish than Gentile.
