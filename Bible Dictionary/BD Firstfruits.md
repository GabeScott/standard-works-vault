# Firstfruits

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/firstfruits?lang=eng)

Regarded as belonging to God, and offered by the nation as a whole at the great feasts, and by private individuals at other times ([[Exodus 22.29|Ex. 22:29]]; [[Exodus 23.19|23:19]]; [[Exodus 34.26|34:26]]). The prescribed ritual included (1) the waving before the Lord of a sheaf of barley on the morrow of the Passover Sabbath ([[Leviticus 23.10|Lev. 23:10–14]][[Leviticus 23.11|]][[Leviticus 23.12|]][[Leviticus 23.13|]][[Leviticus 23.14|]]); (2) the offering of two loaves made with new wheat flour at the Feast of Pentecost ([[Exodus 34.22|Ex. 34:22]]; [[Leviticus 23.15|Lev. 23:15, 17]][[Leviticus 23.17|]]); (3) a thanksgiving for the harvest at the Feast of Tabernacles, or of Ingathering ([[Exodus 23.16|Ex. 23:16]]; [[Exodus 34.22|34:22]]; [[Leviticus 23.39|Lev. 23:39]]). See also [[Numbers 15.19|Num. 15:19, 21]][[Numbers 15.21|]]; [[Deuteronomy 26.2|Deut. 26:2–11]][[Deuteronomy 26.3|]][[Deuteronomy 26.4|]][[Deuteronomy 26.5|]][[Deuteronomy 26.6|]][[Deuteronomy 26.7|]][[Deuteronomy 26.8|]][[Deuteronomy 26.9|]][[Deuteronomy 26.10|]][[Deuteronomy 26.11|]]. Rules with regard to private offerings of firstfruits are found in [[Exodus 23.16|Ex. 23:16]]; [[Leviticus 2.12|Lev. 2:12–16]][[Leviticus 2.13|]][[Leviticus 2.14|]][[Leviticus 2.15|]][[Leviticus 2.16|]]; [[Leviticus 19.23|19:23–25]][[Leviticus 19.24|]][[Leviticus 19.25|]]; [[Leviticus 22.14|22:14]]; [[Leviticus 23.15|23:15–17]][[Leviticus 23.16|]][[Leviticus 23.17|]]; [[Numbers 15.19|Num. 15:19–21]][[Numbers 15.20|]][[Numbers 15.21|]]; [[Deuteronomy 18.4|Deut. 18:4]]; [[Deuteronomy 26.2|26:2]]; [[2 Chronicles 31.5|2 Chr. 31:5, 11]][[2 Chronicles 31.11|]]; [[Nehemiah 10.35|Neh. 10:35, 37]][[Nehemiah 10.37|]]; [[Nehemiah 12.44|12:44]].
