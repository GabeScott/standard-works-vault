# Annas

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/annas?lang=eng)

Appointed high priest in  7 by the Roman legate Quirinius and deposed in  15 by Valerius Gratus. From  18–36 his son-in-law ([[John 18.13|John 18:13]]) Joseph Caiaphas was high priest, and during this time Annas was a person of great influence in the Sanhedrin. Jesus, when arrested, was first brought to him ([[John 18.13|John 18:13]]); he also took a leading part in the trial of the Apostles ([[Acts 4.6|Acts 4:6]]). In accordance with Jewish custom he kept the title “high priest” after he was deposed from office.
