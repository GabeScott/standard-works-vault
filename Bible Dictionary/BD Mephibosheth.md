# Mephibosheth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/mephibosheth?lang=eng)

(1) Son of Saul and Rizpah ([[2 Samuel 21.8|2 Sam. 21:8]]).

(2) A lame son of Jonathan ([[2 Samuel 4.4|2 Sam. 4:4]]); with Machir at Lodebar ([[2 Samuel 9.6|9:6]]); David’s kindness to him ([[2 Samuel 9.5|9:5–13]][[2 Samuel 9.6|]][[2 Samuel 9.7|]][[2 Samuel 9.8|]][[2 Samuel 9.9|]][[2 Samuel 9.10|]][[2 Samuel 9.11|]][[2 Samuel 9.12|]][[2 Samuel 9.13|]]); Ziba’s slander ([[2 Samuel 16.1|16:1–4]][[2 Samuel 16.2|]][[2 Samuel 16.3|]][[2 Samuel 16.4|]]); his defense ([[2 Samuel 19.24|19:24–28]][[2 Samuel 19.25|]][[2 Samuel 19.26|]][[2 Samuel 19.27|]][[2 Samuel 19.28|]]); David spares him ([[2 Samuel 21.7|21:7]]). His true name was Merib-baal; but later Israelites were unwilling to pronounce the name Baal ([[Exodus 23.13|Ex. 23:13]]) and substituted for it *bosheth* (“shameful thing”).
