# Silas

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/silas?lang=eng)

Called Silvanus in the epistles, one of the “chief men” among the Christians at Jerusalem, sent to Antioch ([[Acts 15.22|Acts 15:22]]); a “prophet” ([[Acts 15.32|15:32]]); remained at Antioch ([[Acts 15.34|15:34]]); accompanied Paul on his second journey ([[Acts 15.40|15:40]]); at Philippi ([[Acts 16.19|16:19]]); left at Beroea ([[Acts 17.14|17:14]]); with Paul at Corinth ([[Acts 18.5|18:5]]; see also [[1 Thessalonians 1.1|1 Thes. 1:1]]; [[2 Thessalonians 1.1|2 Thes. 1:1]]; [[1 Peter 5.12|1 Pet. 5:12]]).
