# Chebar

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/chebar?lang=eng)

A canal or waterway that left the Euphrates River north of Babylon and flowed 60 miles southeast before reentering the Euphrates. On its banks lived many of the exiles carried from Judah by Nebuchadnezzar; here Ezekiel saw his earlier visions ([[Ezekiel 1.1|Ezek. 1:1, 3]][[Ezekiel 1.3|]]; [[Ezekiel 3.15|3:15, 23]][[Ezekiel 3.23|]]; [[Ezekiel 10.15|10:15, 20, 22]][[Ezekiel 10.20|]][[Ezekiel 10.22|]]; [[Ezekiel 43.3|43:3]]).
