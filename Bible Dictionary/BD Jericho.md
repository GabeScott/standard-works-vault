# Jericho

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jericho?lang=eng)

A walled city in the valley of the Jordan, 800 feet below the Mediterranean, near the place where the Israelites crossed the river on their first entry into the promised land ([[Joshua 2.1|Josh. 2:1–3]][[Joshua 2.2|]][[Joshua 2.3|]]; [[Joshua 3.16|3:16]]; [[Joshua 6.1|6]][[Joshua 6.2|]][[Joshua 6.3|]][[Joshua 6.4|]][[Joshua 6.5|]][[Joshua 6.6|]][[Joshua 6.7|]][[Joshua 6.8|]][[Joshua 6.9|]][[Joshua 6.10|]][[Joshua 6.11|]][[Joshua 6.12|]][[Joshua 6.13|]][[Joshua 6.14|]][[Joshua 6.15|]][[Joshua 6.16|]][[Joshua 6.17|]][[Joshua 6.18|]][[Joshua 6.19|]][[Joshua 6.20|]][[Joshua 6.21|]][[Joshua 6.22|]][[Joshua 6.23|]][[Joshua 6.24|]][[Joshua 6.25|]][[Joshua 6.26|]][[Joshua 6.27|]]). Joshua placed a curse upon it ([[Joshua 6.26|Josh. 6:26]]), which was afterwards fulfilled ([[1 Kings 16.34|1 Kgs. 16:34]]). It was within the territory assigned to Benjamin ([[Joshua 18.12|Josh. 18:12, 21]][[Joshua 18.21|]]) and was visited by our Lord on His last journey to Jerusalem ([[Mark 10.46|Mark 10:46]]; [[Luke 18.35|Luke 18:35]]; [[Luke 19.1|19:1]]).
