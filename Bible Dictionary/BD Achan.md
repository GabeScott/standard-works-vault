# Achan

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/achan?lang=eng)

A man of the tribe of Judah who seized and attempted to hide the spoils from the battle of Jericho, against the counsel of Joshua. His disobedience resulted in Israel’s defeat at Ai. His transgression was detected through revelation to Joshua, and he was stoned to death.
