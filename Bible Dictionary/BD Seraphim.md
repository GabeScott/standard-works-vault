# Seraphim

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/seraphim?lang=eng)

Probably *fiery beings.* Angelic beings mentioned in the account of Isaiah’s vision ([[Isaiah 6.2|Isa. 6:2]]). They are represented as winged and partly human in form. They are attendants in Jehovah’s court, the ministers of the heavenly sanctuary, joining in adoration before the throne.
