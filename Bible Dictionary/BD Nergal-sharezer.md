# Nergal-sharezer

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/nergal-sharezer?lang=eng)

*Nergal protect the king!* Accompanied Nebuchadnezzar to Jerusalem, holding the office of Rab-mag (chief of the magi) ([[Jeremiah 39.3|Jer. 39:3, 13]][[Jeremiah 39.13|]]). He is probably to be identified with the man of the same name who afterwards became king of Babylonia and is known in classical literature as Neriglissar.
