# Frankincense

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/frankincense?lang=eng)

The fragrant resinous exudation of various species of *Boswellia;* it was imported into Judea from Arabia ([[Isaiah 60.6|Isa. 60:6]]; [[Jeremiah 6.20|Jer. 6:20]]). It was an ingredient in the holy incense for sacrificial purposes ([[Exodus 30.34|Ex. 30:34]]) and was highly valued as a perfume ([[Matthew 2.11|Matt. 2:11]]).
