# Horse

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/horse?lang=eng)

Most of the allusions to horses that occur in the Bible refer to their use in war. They were not used at all by the early Hebrews; Solomon introduced them from Egypt. The Canaanites, in the time of Joshua, made use of war-horses and chariots ([[Joshua 11.9|Josh. 11:9]]), but they could only be used in plains and level country. In [[Job 39.19|Job 39:19–25]][[Job 39.20|]][[Job 39.21|]][[Job 39.22|]][[Job 39.23|]][[Job 39.24|]][[Job 39.25|]] there is a description of a war-horse. There is only one allusion to the horse being employed for agricultural purposes among the Jews, namely, for threshing grain ([[Isaiah 28.28|Isa. 28:28]]).
