# Issachar

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/issachar?lang=eng)

Son of Jacob by Leah ([[Genesis 30.18|Gen. 30:18]]; [[Genesis 35.23|35:23]]; [[Genesis 46.13|46:13]]; [[Genesis 49.14|49:14]]). After the settlement of Canaan, the tribe received some of the richest land of Palestine, including the eastern plain of Esdraelon, and had within its borders several places of note in Israelite history, such as Gilboa, Jezreel, and Tabor.
