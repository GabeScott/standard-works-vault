# Beryl

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/beryl?lang=eng)

A precious stone, pale green in color. It was the first stone of the fourth row of the high priest’s breastplate ([[Exodus 28.20|Ex. 28:20]]) and the eighth stone in the foundation of the wall of the heavenly Jerusalem ([[Revelation 21.20|Rev. 21:20]]).
