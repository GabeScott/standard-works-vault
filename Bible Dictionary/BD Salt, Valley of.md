# Salt, Valley of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/salt-valley-of?lang=eng)

South of the Dead Sea, where the Edomites were defeated (1) by David ([[2 Samuel 8.13|2 Sam. 8:13]]; [[1 Chronicles 18.12|1 Chr. 18:12]]); (2) by Amaziah ([[2 Kings 14.7|2 Kgs. 14:7]]; [[2 Chronicles 25.11|2 Chr. 25:11]]; [Ps. 60 heading](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/ps/60?lang=eng&id=intro1#intro1)).
