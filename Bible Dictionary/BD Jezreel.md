# Jezreel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jezreel?lang=eng)

(1) Plain of Esdraelon ([[Joshua 17.16|Josh. 17:16]]; [[Judges 6.33|Judg. 6:33]]; [[2 Samuel 2.9|2 Sam. 2:9]]; [[2 Samuel 4.4|4:4]]; [[Hosea 1.5|Hosea 1:5, 11]][[Hosea 1.11|]]; [[Hosea 2.22|2:22]]).

(2) A city on a hill rising out of the plain, belonging to the tribe of Issachar ([[Joshua 19.18|Josh. 19:18]]). Ahab made it his capital ([[1 Kings 18.45|1 Kgs. 18:45–46]][[1 Kings 18.46|]]; [[1 Kings 21.1|21:1, 23]][[1 Kings 21.23|]]; [[2 Kings 8.29|2 Kgs. 8:29]]); after the fall of his house it ceased to be a place of much importance.

(3) Symbolic name of the eldest son of the prophet Hosea ([[Hosea 1.4|Hosea 1:4–5]][[Hosea 1.5|]]).
