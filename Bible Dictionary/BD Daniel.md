# Daniel

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/daniel?lang=eng)

*A judge (is) God.* (1) The second son of David, by Abigail the Carmelitess ([[1 Chronicles 3.1|1 Chr. 3:1]]); also called Chileab ([[2 Samuel 3.3|2 Sam. 3:3]]).

(2) The hero of the book of Daniel. Nothing is known of his parentage, though he appears to have been of royal descent ([[Daniel 1.3|Dan. 1:3]]); he was taken captive to Babylon and received the name of Belteshazzar ([[Daniel 1.6|1:6–7]][[Daniel 1.7|]]). Along with three others he refused the “king’s meat” from fear of defilement ([[Daniel 1.8|1:8–16]][[Daniel 1.9|]][[Daniel 1.10|]][[Daniel 1.11|]][[Daniel 1.12|]][[Daniel 1.13|]][[Daniel 1.14|]][[Daniel 1.15|]][[Daniel 1.16|]]).
