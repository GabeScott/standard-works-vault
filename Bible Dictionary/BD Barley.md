# Barley

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/barley?lang=eng)

A food grain cultivated from the earliest times; in Palestine it is sown from the beginning of November till the beginning of December; the harvest is about three weeks earlier than wheat harvest. Often it was mixed with other, more palatable grains in making flour. See [[Exodus 9.31|Ex. 9:31]]; [[Deuteronomy 8.8|Deut. 8:8]]; [[Ruth 1.22|Ruth 1:22]]; [[John 6.9|John 6:9–13]][[John 6.10|]][[John 6.11|]][[John 6.12|]][[John 6.13|]].
