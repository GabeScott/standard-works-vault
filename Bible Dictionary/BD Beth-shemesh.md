# Beth-shemesh

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/beth-shemesh?lang=eng)

*House of the sun.* (1) Probably same as Ir-shemesh ([[Joshua 19.41|Josh. 19:41]]); a priestly city on the border of Judah ([[Joshua 15.10|Josh. 15:10]]), about 12 miles southeast of Ekron; ark taken to Beth-shemesh ([[1 Samuel 6.9|1 Sam. 6:9–20]][[1 Samuel 6.10|]][[1 Samuel 6.11|]][[1 Samuel 6.12|]][[1 Samuel 6.13|]][[1 Samuel 6.14|]][[1 Samuel 6.15|]][[1 Samuel 6.16|]][[1 Samuel 6.17|]][[1 Samuel 6.18|]][[1 Samuel 6.19|]][[1 Samuel 6.20|]]; see also [[1 Kings 4.9|1 Kgs. 4:9]]; [[2 Kings 14.11|2 Kgs. 14:11, 13]][[2 Kings 14.13|]]; [[1 Chronicles 6.59|1 Chr. 6:59]]; [[2 Chronicles 25.21|2 Chr. 25:21, 23]][[2 Chronicles 25.23|]]; [[2 Chronicles 28.18|28:18]]).

(2) For On, or Heliopolis, see [[Jeremiah 43.13|Jer. 43:13]]; probably same as the “city of destruction” ([[Isaiah 19.18|Isa. 19:18]]).

(3) Two other cities: [[Joshua 19.22|Josh. 19:22, 38]][[Joshua 19.38|]]; [[Judges 1.33|Judg. 1:33]].
