# Pisgah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/pisgah?lang=eng)

*Division.* A hill in Moab, opposite Jericho ([[Numbers 21.20|Num. 21:20]]), whence Balaam ([[Numbers 23.14|Num. 23:14]]) and Moses saw the land of Israel ([[Deuteronomy 3.27|Deut. 3:27]]; [[Deuteronomy 34.1|34:1]]). Nebo and the top (or head) of Pisgah may be two names of the same peak.
