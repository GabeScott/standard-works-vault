# Nebuchadnezzar, Nebuchadrezzar

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/nebuchadnezzar?lang=eng)

Or Nabu-kudur-usur. *Nebo, protect the crown!* (or, *the landmark*). The great king of Babylon (604–561 ), son of Nabopolassar, the founder of the empire (see *[[BD Assyria And Babylonia|Assyria and Babylonia]]*); defeats Necho, king of Egypt, at Carchemish and drives the Egyptians from Syria ([[Jeremiah 46.2|Jer. 46:2–12]][[Jeremiah 46.3|]][[Jeremiah 46.4|]][[Jeremiah 46.5|]][[Jeremiah 46.6|]][[Jeremiah 46.7|]][[Jeremiah 46.8|]][[Jeremiah 46.9|]][[Jeremiah 46.10|]][[Jeremiah 46.11|]][[Jeremiah 46.12|]]); subdues Judah ([[2 Kings 24.1|2 Kgs. 24:1]]); besieges Jerusalem ([[2 Kings 24.10|24:10–11]][[2 Kings 24.11|]]) and takes it, carrying away the king and people ([[2 Kings 25.1|25:1, 8, 22]][[2 Kings 25.8|]][[2 Kings 25.22|]]; [[1 Chronicles 6.15|1 Chr. 6:15]]; [[2 Chronicles 36.1|2 Chr. 36]]; [[Ezra 1.7|Ezra 1:7]]; [[Ezra 2.1|2:1]]; [[Ezra 5.12|5:12, 14]][[Ezra 5.14|]]; [[Ezra 6.5|6:5]]; [[Nehemiah 7.6|Neh. 7:6]]; [[Esther 2.6|Esth. 2:6]]; [[Jeremiah 27.6|Jer. 27:6, 8, 20]][[Jeremiah 27.8|]][[Jeremiah 27.20|]]; [[Jeremiah 28.3|28:3, 11, 14]][[Jeremiah 28.11|]][[Jeremiah 28.14|]]; [[Jeremiah 29.1|29:1, 3]][[Jeremiah 29.3|]]; [[Jeremiah 34.1|34:1]]; [[Jeremiah 39.5|39:5]]). For his relations with Daniel, by whom his dreams were interpreted, see [[Daniel 1.1|Dan. 1–5]]. He was for a time smitten with madness and on his recovery acknowledged God’s power and goodness.
