# Chittim or Kittim

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/chittim?lang=eng)

Son of Javan ([[Genesis 10.4|Gen. 10:4]]; [[1 Chronicles 1.7|1 Chr. 1:7]]). He gave his name to the island of Chittim, now called Cyprus ([[Numbers 24.24|Num. 24:24]]; [[Isaiah 23.1|Isa. 23:1, 12]][[Isaiah 23.12|]]; [[Jeremiah 2.10|Jer. 2:10]]; [[Ezekiel 27.6|Ezek. 27:6]]; [[Daniel 11.30|Dan. 11:30]]).
