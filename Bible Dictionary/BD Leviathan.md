# Leviathan

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/leviathan?lang=eng)

Any great sea or land monster, such as the crocodile, either as an actual creature ([[Job 41.1|Job 41:1]]) or as symbolic of a nation ([[Psalms 74.14|Ps. 74:14]]); a large serpent ([[Isaiah 27.1|Isa. 27:1]]).
