# Kenites

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/kenites?lang=eng)

Probably a Midianite tribe, descended from Abraham by his wife Keturah. Moses’ marriage with the daughter of Jethro, a prince and priest of Midian, was also a cause of the long-standing friendship between the Kenites and Israelites ([[Numbers 24.21|Num. 24:21–22]][[Numbers 24.22|]]; [[Judges 1.16|Judg. 1:16]]; [[Judges 4.11|4:11, 17]][[Judges 4.17|]]; [[Judges 5.24|5:24]]; [[1 Samuel 15.6|1 Sam. 15:6]]; [[1 Samuel 27.10|27:10]]; [[1 Samuel 30.29|30:29]]). The Rechabites were a sect or family belonging to this tribe ([[1 Chronicles 2.55|1 Chr. 2:55]]).
