# Merodach-baladan

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/merodach-baladan?lang=eng)

King of Babylon in the days of Hezekiah ([[Isaiah 39.1|Isa. 39:1]]; see also [[2 Kings 20.12|2 Kgs. 20:12]], where he is called Berodach-baladan). He made an alliance with Hezekiah against Sargon, king of Assyria, who, after reducing Judah, Edom, and Moab to submission, deposed Merodach-baladan.
