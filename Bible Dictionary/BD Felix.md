# Felix

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/felix?lang=eng)

Procurator of Judea at the time of Paul’s arrest by the Roman military authorities ([[Acts 23.24|Acts 23:24]]). He investigated the case and heard Paul speak in his own defense ([[Acts 24.1|Acts 24]]) but put off giving any decision, his reason being that he had “more exact knowledge concerning the Way”: he knew more about the case and about Christianity than the prosecutors had told him. He hoped Paul would have offered him some bribe; but none being offered on his recall from Judea, he left the Apostle still in prison. See *[[BD Drusilla|Drusilla]];* see also [Bible Chronology](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bible-chron/new-testament?lang=eng&id=p63-p65#p63) in the appendix.
