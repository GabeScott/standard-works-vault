# Felix

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/felix?lang=eng)

Procurator of Judea at the time of Paul’s arrest by the Roman military authorities ([[Acts 23.24|Acts 23:24]]). He investigated the case and heard Paul speak in his own defense ([[Acts 24.1|Acts 24]][[Acts 24.2|]][[Acts 24.3|]][[Acts 24.4|]][[Acts 24.5|]][[Acts 24.6|]][[Acts 24.7|]][[Acts 24.8|]][[Acts 24.9|]][[Acts 24.10|]][[Acts 24.11|]][[Acts 24.12|]][[Acts 24.13|]][[Acts 24.14|]][[Acts 24.15|]][[Acts 24.16|]][[Acts 24.17|]][[Acts 24.18|]][[Acts 24.19|]][[Acts 24.20|]][[Acts 24.21|]][[Acts 24.22|]][[Acts 24.23|]][[Acts 24.24|]][[Acts 24.25|]][[Acts 24.26|]][[Acts 24.27|]]) but put off giving any decision, his reason being that he had “more exact knowledge concerning the Way”: he knew more about the case and about Christianity than the prosecutors had told him. He hoped Paul would have offered him some bribe; but none being offered on his recall from Judea, he left the Apostle still in prison. See *[[BD Drusilla|Drusilla]];* see also [Bible Chronology](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bible-chron/new-testament?lang=eng&id=p63-p65#p63) in the appendix.
