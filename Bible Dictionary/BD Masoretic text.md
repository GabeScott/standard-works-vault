# Masoretic text

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/masoretic-text?lang=eng)

From the Hebrew *masora,* meaning “traditional.” The traditional Hebrew text of the Old Testament was the result of the standardization and stabilization begun by the council of Jamnia, held about  90. See *[[BD Jamnia|Jamnia]].* The Old Testament of the KJV uses the Masoretic text.
