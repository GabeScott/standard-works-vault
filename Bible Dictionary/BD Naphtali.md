# Naphtali

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/naphtali?lang=eng)

*Wrestlings.* Sixth son of Jacob, and second child of Bilhah, Rachel’s slave ([[Genesis 30.8|Gen. 30:8]]); his four sons ([[Genesis 46.24|Gen. 46:24]]; [[Exodus 1.4|Ex. 1:4]]; [[1 Chronicles 7.13|1 Chr. 7:13]]); the blessing on the tribe ([[Genesis 49.21|Gen. 49:21]]; [[Deuteronomy 33.23|Deut. 33:23]]); land of Naphtali ([[Joshua 19.32|Josh. 19:32–39]][[Joshua 19.33|]][[Joshua 19.34|]][[Joshua 19.35|]][[Joshua 19.36|]][[Joshua 19.37|]][[Joshua 19.38|]][[Joshua 19.39|]]). Barak of Kedesh-Naphtali was the one man of note belonging to the tribe ([[Judges 4.6|Judg. 4:6]]; [[Judges 5.15|5:15–18]][[Judges 5.16|]][[Judges 5.17|]][[Judges 5.18|]]). The history of the tribe ends with the captivity under Tiglath-pileser, but see [[Isaiah 9.1|Isa. 9:1]]; [[Ezekiel 48.3|Ezek. 48:3–4, 34]][[Ezekiel 48.4|]][[Ezekiel 48.34|]]; [[Matthew 4.15|Matt. 4:15]]; [[Revelation 7.6|Rev. 7:6]].
