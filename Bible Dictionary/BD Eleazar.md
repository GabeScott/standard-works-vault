# Eleazar

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/eleazar?lang=eng)

*God hath helped.* (1) Third son of Aaron ([[Exodus 6.23|Ex. 6:23]]); admitted to priestly office ([[Exodus 28.1|Ex. 28:1]]). Eleazar and Ithamar were Aaron’s chief assistants after the death of Nadab and Abihu by fire ([[Leviticus 10.12|Lev. 10:12, 16]][[Leviticus 10.16|]]; see also [[Numbers 20.25|Num. 20:25–29]][[Numbers 20.26|]][[Numbers 20.27|]][[Numbers 20.28|]][[Numbers 20.29|]]; [[Deuteronomy 10.6|Deut. 10:6]]; [[Joshua 14.1|Josh. 14:1]]; [[Joshua 24.33|24:33]]). All the high priests until the Maccabean period were descended from Eleazar, with the exception of those from Eli to Abiathar, inclusive, who belonged to the family of Ithamar.

(2) Son of Abinadab, who took charge of the ark at Kirjath-jearim ([[1 Samuel 7.1|1 Sam. 7:1]]).

(3) One of David’s three mighty men ([[2 Samuel 23.9|2 Sam. 23:9]]).

(4) Three others ([[1 Chronicles 23.21|1 Chr. 23:21–22]][[1 Chronicles 23.22|]]; [[Ezra 8.33|Ezra 8:33]] and [[Nehemiah 12.42|Neh. 12:42]]; [[Ezra 10.25|Ezra 10:25]]).
