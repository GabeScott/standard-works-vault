# Chedorlaomer

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/chedorlaomer?lang=eng)

A king of Elam who, with certain princes of Babylonia, defeated and reduced to servitude the kings of Sodom, Gomorrah, and other neighboring cities ([[Genesis 14.1|Gen. 14:1–17]][[Genesis 14.2|]][[Genesis 14.3|]][[Genesis 14.4|]][[Genesis 14.5|]][[Genesis 14.6|]][[Genesis 14.7|]][[Genesis 14.8|]][[Genesis 14.9|]][[Genesis 14.10|]][[Genesis 14.11|]][[Genesis 14.12|]][[Genesis 14.13|]][[Genesis 14.14|]][[Genesis 14.15|]][[Genesis 14.16|]][[Genesis 14.17|]]). Abraham marched against him in order to recover his nephew Lot, whom the king had taken prisoner.
