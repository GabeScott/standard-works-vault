# Boanerges

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/boanerges?lang=eng)

*Sons of thunder.* Surname given by our Lord to James and John, sons of Zebedee, probably on account of the intensity of their character ([[Mark 3.17|Mark 3:17]]; see also [[Mark 9.38|Mark 9:38]]; [[Mark 10.37|10:37]]; [[Luke 9.54|Luke 9:54]]).
