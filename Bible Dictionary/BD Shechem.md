# Shechem

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/shechem?lang=eng)

*Shoulder.* The most ancient of the sacred towns of the children of Israel, situated in a beautiful plain among the mountains of Ephraim, close to the two hills Ebal and Gerizim. It was visited by Abram on his first arrival in the promised land ([[Genesis 12.6|Gen. 12:6]]); Jacob bought land here ([[Genesis 33.18|33:18–19]][[Genesis 33.19|]]; see also [[Genesis 35.4|Gen. 35:4]]; [[Genesis 37.14|37:14]]; [[Acts 7.16|Acts 7:16]]). It was later included in the territory of Ephraim ([[Joshua 17.7|Josh. 17:7]]) and became a Levitical city and a city of refuge ([[Joshua 20.7|20:7]]; [[Joshua 21.21|21:21]]; [[1 Chronicles 6.67|1 Chr. 6:67]]). Here Joshua addressed the people ([[Joshua 24.1|Josh. 24:1]]), and the bones of Joseph were buried ([[Joshua 24.32|24:32]]). Here Rehoboam came to be crowned in the hope of conciliating the northern tribes ([[1 Kings 12.1|1 Kgs. 12:1]]; [[2 Chronicles 10.1|2 Chr. 10:1]]; see also [[Judges 8.31|Judg. 8:31]]; [[Judges 9.6|9:6, 46]][[Judges 9.46|]]; [[Judges 21.19|21:19]]; [[1 Kings 12.25|1 Kgs. 12:25]]; [[1 Chronicles 7.28|1 Chr. 7:28]]; [[Psalms 60.6|Ps. 60:6]]; [[Jeremiah 41.5|Jer. 41:5]]). The modern town of Nablus lies near the ancient site of Shechem.
