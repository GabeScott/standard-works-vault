# Ark

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/ark?lang=eng)

The vessel built by Noah for saving life during the great Flood. Its dimensions were 300 cubits (about 450 feet) long, 50 cubits wide, and 30 cubits high; it was built of gopher or cypress wood, was three stories high, and was divided into “rooms.” See also *[[BD Noah|Noah]].*
