# Brethren of the Lord

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/brethren-of-the-lord?lang=eng)

A title given to James, Joses, Simon, and Judas ([[Matthew 13.55|Matt. 13:55]]; see also [[Matthew 12.46|Matt. 12:46]]; [[Mark 6.3|Mark 6:3]]; [[Luke 8.19|Luke 8:19]]; [[John 2.12|John 2:12]]; [[John 7.3|7:3]]; [[Acts 1.14|Acts 1:14]]; [[1 Corinthians 9.5|1 Cor. 9:5]]; [[Galatians 1.19|Gal. 1:19]]). They were children of Joseph and Mary, born after Jesus’ birth. [[Matthew 13.56|Matt. 13:56]] shows that there were also sisters. The number of girls is not specified, but the Greek text makes it clear that there were more than two. Since the exact number is not known, this is presented in the KJV as “all his sisters.”
