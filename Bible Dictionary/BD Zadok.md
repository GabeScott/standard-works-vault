# Zadok

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/zadok?lang=eng)

*Righteous.* The priest ([[2 Samuel 8.17|2 Sam. 8:17]]; [[2 Samuel 15.24|15:24–36]][[2 Samuel 15.25|]][[2 Samuel 15.26|]][[2 Samuel 15.27|]][[2 Samuel 15.28|]][[2 Samuel 15.29|]][[2 Samuel 15.30|]][[2 Samuel 15.31|]][[2 Samuel 15.32|]][[2 Samuel 15.33|]][[2 Samuel 15.34|]][[2 Samuel 15.35|]][[2 Samuel 15.36|]]; [[2 Samuel 17.15|17:15]]; [[2 Samuel 18.19|18:19, 27]][[2 Samuel 18.27|]]; [[2 Samuel 19.11|19:11]]; [[2 Samuel 20.25|20:25]]). He belonged to the family of Eleazar ([[1 Chronicles 6.4|1 Chr. 6:4–8]][[1 Chronicles 6.5|]][[1 Chronicles 6.6|]][[1 Chronicles 6.7|]][[1 Chronicles 6.8|]]). During the latter years of Saul’s reign there seems to have been a double high priesthood: Abiathar, who belonged to the family of Ithamar, officiating in David’s camp, and Zadok at Gibeon, where the tabernacle was standing. This arrangement continued after David’s accession, but during the rebellion of Adonijah, Abiathar joined the rebels, while Zadok remained faithful to David ([[1 Kings 1.8|1 Kgs. 1:8, 26]][[1 Kings 1.26|]]). He anointed Solomon ([[1 Kings 1.32|1:32–45]][[1 Kings 1.33|]][[1 Kings 1.34|]][[1 Kings 1.35|]][[1 Kings 1.36|]][[1 Kings 1.37|]][[1 Kings 1.38|]][[1 Kings 1.39|]][[1 Kings 1.40|]][[1 Kings 1.41|]][[1 Kings 1.42|]][[1 Kings 1.43|]][[1 Kings 1.44|]][[1 Kings 1.45|]]); and was finally put in the room of Abiathar. See [[1 Kings 2.35|1 Kgs. 2:35]]; [[1 Kings 4.2|4:2, 4]][[1 Kings 4.4|]]; [[1 Chronicles 6.8|1 Chr. 6:8, 53]][[1 Chronicles 6.53|]]; [[1 Chronicles 12.28|12:28]]; [[1 Chronicles 15.11|15:11]]; [[1 Chronicles 16.39|16:39]]; [[1 Chronicles 18.16|18:16]]; [[1 Chronicles 24.3|24:3, 6, 31]][[1 Chronicles 24.6|]][[1 Chronicles 24.31|]]; [[1 Chronicles 27.17|27:17]]; [[1 Chronicles 29.22|29:22]]; [[2 Chronicles 31.10|2 Chr. 31:10]]; [[Ezra 7.2|Ezra 7:2]]; [[Ezekiel 40.46|Ezek. 40:46]]; [[Ezekiel 43.19|43:19]]; [[Ezekiel 44.15|44:15]]; [[Ezekiel 48.11|48:11]]. He is probably the same as in [[1 Chronicles 6.12|1 Chr. 6:12]]; [[1 Chronicles 9.11|9:11]]; [[Nehemiah 11.11|Neh. 11:11]]; others are in [[2 Kings 15.33|2 Kgs. 15:33]]; [[2 Chronicles 27.1|2 Chr. 27:1]]; [[Nehemiah 3.4|Neh. 3:4, 29]][[Nehemiah 3.29|]]; [[Nehemiah 10.21|10:21]]; [[Nehemiah 13.13|13:13]].
