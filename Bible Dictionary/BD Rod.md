# Rod

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/rod?lang=eng)

There are several meanings of rod in the scriptures. A shepherd’s rod was a club approximately three feet long, with a knob on one end. It was used to beat wolves away from the herd. A staff, sometimes called a rod, is longer; it was used for walking, resting, or for guiding the sheep, as in [[Leviticus 27.32|Lev. 27:32]]; [[Psalms 23.4|Ps. 23:4]].

Moses’ and Aaron’s rods represented authority, as in [[Exodus 4.1|Ex. 4:1–5]][[Exodus 4.2|]][[Exodus 4.3|]][[Exodus 4.4|]][[Exodus 4.5|]]; [[Exodus 7.9|7:9–12, 17]][[Exodus 7.10|]][[Exodus 7.11|]][[Exodus 7.12|]][[Exodus 7.17|]]. A rod was also used for punishing a wayward child, as in [[Proverbs 13.24|Prov. 13:24]]; [[Proverbs 22.15|22:15]]; or to whip a supposed offender, as in [[2 Corinthians 11.25|2 Cor. 11:25]] and [[Matthew 27.26|Matt. 27:26]]. A measuring rod was about 10 feet, 10 inches (330 centimeters) long and was used to determine distances (see [[Ezekiel 40.3|Ezek. 40:3]]). [[Isaiah 11.1|Isa. 11:1]] speaks of a rod (Hebrew *Khoter*—a twig or shoot), which has been identified in latter-day revelation as a prophet of the Restoration ([[D&C 113.1|D&C 113]][[D&C 113.2|]][[D&C 113.3|]][[D&C 113.4|]][[D&C 113.5|]][[D&C 113.6|]][[D&C 113.7|]][[D&C 113.8|]][[D&C 113.9|]][[D&C 113.10|]]).
