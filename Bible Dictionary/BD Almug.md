# Almug

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/almug?lang=eng)

*Sandalwood*; imported by Solomon from Ophir for the temple, for his own house, and for musical instruments, [[1 Kings 10.11|1 Kgs. 10:11–12]][[1 Kings 10.12|]]; [[2 Chronicles 2.8|2 Chr. 2:8]]; [[2 Chronicles 9.10|9:10–11]][[2 Chronicles 9.11|]]. The wood used was probably the red sandalwood of India, of which musical instruments are still made.
