# Beth-shan or Beth-shean

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/beth-shan?lang=eng)

*House of safety.* Three miles from the Jordan River on the edge of the plain of Jezreel, on the road from Damascus to Egypt, and to Jerusalem via Shechem ([[Joshua 17.11|Josh. 17:11, 16]][[Joshua 17.16|]]; [[Judges 1.27|Judg. 1:27]]; [[1 Samuel 31.10|1 Sam. 31:10, 12]][[1 Samuel 31.12|]]; [[2 Samuel 21.12|2 Sam. 21:12]]; [[1 Kings 4.12|1 Kgs. 4:12]]; [[1 Chronicles 7.29|1 Chr. 7:29]]). It was at Beth-shan that the dead bodies of Saul and three of his sons were fastened to the city wall ([[1 Samuel 31.10|1 Sam. 31:10–13]][[1 Samuel 31.11|]][[1 Samuel 31.12|]][[1 Samuel 31.13|]]). It was afterwards known as Scythopolis and in our Lord’s time was one of the cities of Decapolis. Beth-shan has been an extremely fruitful archaeological site, containing Egyptian and Canaanite ruins of early Palestinian history.
