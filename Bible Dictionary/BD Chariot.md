# Chariot

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/chariot?lang=eng)

A vehicle, generally used for purposes of war ([[Exodus 14.7|Ex. 14:7]]; [[Joshua 17.18|Josh. 17:18]]; [[Judges 1.19|Judg. 1:19]]; [[Judges 4.3|4:3]]; [[1 Samuel 13.5|1 Sam. 13:5]]). Up to the time of David the Israelites had few chariots ([[1 Samuel 8.11|1 Sam. 8:11–12]][[1 Samuel 8.12|]]); in later times a regular force was maintained ([[2 Samuel 8.4|2 Sam. 8:4]]; [[1 Kings 10.26|1 Kgs. 10:26]]). The armies that fought against Israel also had chariots, such as the Syrians ([[2 Samuel 8.4|2 Sam. 8:4]]; [[2 Kings 8.21|2 Kgs. 8:21]]), the Assyrians ([[2 Kings 19.23|2 Kgs. 19:23]]), and the Persians ([[Isaiah 22.6|Isa. 22:6–7]][[Isaiah 22.7|]]). The Israelite fighting chariot was probably after the Egyptian model, the plan of which is known from Egyptian paintings and reliefs still in existence.
