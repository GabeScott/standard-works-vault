# Abomination

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/abomination?lang=eng)

An object that excites loathing ([[Proverbs 12.22|Prov. 12:22]]); hence an idol ([[2 Kings 23.13|2 Kgs. 23:13]]; [[Isaiah 44.19|Isa. 44:19]]). The word is also used to denote any heathen or immoral practice ([[Deuteronomy 18.9|Deut. 18:9, 12]][[Deuteronomy 18.12|]]; [[Deuteronomy 20.18|20:18]]); also the flesh of prohibited animals ([[Leviticus 11.10|Lev. 11:10–13]][[Leviticus 11.11|]][[Leviticus 11.12|]][[Leviticus 11.13|]]), etc.
