# Jethro

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jethro?lang=eng)

Also called Jether and Reuel, a prince and priest of Midian who gave Moses a home after his flight from Egypt and afterwards became his father-in-law ([[Exodus 3.1|Ex. 3:1]]; [[Exodus 4.18|4:18]]; [[Exodus 18.1|18:1–12]][[Exodus 18.2|]][[Exodus 18.3|]][[Exodus 18.4|]][[Exodus 18.5|]][[Exodus 18.6|]][[Exodus 18.7|]][[Exodus 18.8|]][[Exodus 18.9|]][[Exodus 18.10|]][[Exodus 18.11|]][[Exodus 18.12|]]). It was from Jethro that Moses received the Melchizedek Priesthood ([[D&C 84.6|D&C 84:6–7]][[D&C 84.7|]]). He also gave Moses some practical advice about administrative delegation of responsibility ([[Exodus 18.13|Ex. 18:13–27]][[Exodus 18.14|]][[Exodus 18.15|]][[Exodus 18.16|]][[Exodus 18.17|]][[Exodus 18.18|]][[Exodus 18.19|]][[Exodus 18.20|]][[Exodus 18.21|]][[Exodus 18.22|]][[Exodus 18.23|]][[Exodus 18.24|]][[Exodus 18.25|]][[Exodus 18.26|]][[Exodus 18.27|]]).
