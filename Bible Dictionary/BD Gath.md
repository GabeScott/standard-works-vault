# Gath

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/gath?lang=eng)

*Winepress.* One of the five cities of the Philistines ([[Joshua 11.22|Josh. 11:22]]); the men of the place were punished on account of the Ark ([[1 Samuel 5.8|1 Sam. 5:8]]; [[1 Samuel 6.17|6:17]]; [[1 Samuel 7.14|7:14]]). It was the birthplace of Goliath ([[1 Samuel 17.1|1 Sam. 17]]) and a place of refuge for David ([[1 Samuel 21.10|1 Sam. 21:10–15]][[1 Samuel 21.11|]][[1 Samuel 21.12|]][[1 Samuel 21.13|]][[1 Samuel 21.14|]][[1 Samuel 21.15|]]; [[1 Samuel 27.1|27]]). Afterwards it was captured by David ([[1 Chronicles 18.1|1 Chr. 18:1]]). See also [[2 Samuel 1.20|2 Sam. 1:20]]; [[2 Samuel 15.18|15:18]]; [[2 Kings 12.17|2 Kgs. 12:17]]; [[2 Chronicles 26.6|2 Chr. 26:6]]; [[Amos 6.2|Amos 6:2]]; [[Micah 1.10|Micah 1:10]].
