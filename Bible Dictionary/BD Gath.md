# Gath

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/gath?lang=eng)

*Winepress.* One of the five cities of the Philistines ([[Joshua 11.22|Josh. 11:22]]); the men of the place were punished on account of the Ark ([[1 Samuel 5.8|1 Sam. 5:8]]; [[1 Samuel 6.17|6:17]]; [[1 Samuel 7.14|7:14]]).
