# Red Sea

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/red-sea?lang=eng)

Between Egypt and Arabia. Probably best known because it was miraculously parted so that the Israelites under Moses’ leadership could pass through on dry ground, whereas the armies of Pharaoh were drowned ([[Exodus 13.18|Ex. 13:18]]; [[Exodus 14.15|14:15–31]][[Exodus 14.16|]][[Exodus 14.17|]][[Exodus 14.18|]][[Exodus 14.19|]][[Exodus 14.20|]][[Exodus 14.21|]][[Exodus 14.22|]][[Exodus 14.23|]][[Exodus 14.24|]][[Exodus 14.25|]][[Exodus 14.26|]][[Exodus 14.27|]][[Exodus 14.28|]][[Exodus 14.29|]][[Exodus 14.30|]][[Exodus 14.31|]]; [[Acts 7.36|Acts 7:36]]; [[Hebrews 11.29|Heb. 11:29]]). The parting of the sea by Moses is confirmed in latter-day revelation in [[1 Nephi 4.2|1 Ne. 4:2]]; [[1 Nephi 17.26|17:26–27]][[1 Nephi 17.27|]]; [[Helaman 8.11|Hel. 8:11]]; [[Moses 1.25|Moses 1:25]]. Solomon had a fleet on the Red Sea ([[1 Kings 9.26|1 Kgs. 9:26]]).
