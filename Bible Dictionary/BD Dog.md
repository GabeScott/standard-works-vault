# Dog

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/dog?lang=eng)

Sometimes used for guarding flocks, but usually held in aversion by the Israelites, being regarded as half-wild, greedy creatures, running about at will without a master and acting as public scavengers ([[Deuteronomy 23.18|Deut. 23:18]]; [[1 Samuel 17.43|1 Sam. 17:43]]; [[1 Samuel 24.14|24:14]]; [[2 Samuel 3.8|2 Sam. 3:8]]; [[2 Samuel 9.8|9:8]]; [[2 Samuel 16.9|16:9]]; [[2 Kings 8.13|2 Kgs. 8:13]]; [[Psalms 59.6|Ps. 59:6]]; [[Matthew 7.6|Matt. 7:6]]; [[Matthew 15.26|15:26]]; [[Philippians 3.2|Philip. 3:2]]); the name is applied to false teachers ([[Isaiah 56.10|Isa. 56:10–11]][[Isaiah 56.11|]]; [[Philippians 3.2|Philip. 3:2]]; [[Revelation 22.15|Rev. 22:15]]); and frequently by Jews to Gentiles ([[Mark 7.27|Mark 7:27]]).
