# Moriah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/moriah?lang=eng)

(1) The land of, the district where was found the mountain on which Isaac was offered in sacrifice ([[Genesis 22.2|Gen. 22:2]]); probably the same district as that in which was the “plain (or oak) of Moreh.”

(2) Mount Moriah, the hill on which Solomon built the temple ([[2 Chronicles 3.1|2 Chr. 3:1]]); a tradition first recorded by the Jewish historian Josephus, who regards this as identical with (1).
