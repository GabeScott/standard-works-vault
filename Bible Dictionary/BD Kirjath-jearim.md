# Kirjath-jearim

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/kirjath-jearim?lang=eng)

*City of woods.* A town on the boundary between Judah and Benjamin, 7½ miles northwest of Jerusalem ([[Joshua 9.17|Josh. 9:17]]; [[Joshua 18.14|18:14–15]][[Joshua 18.15|]]); the Ark was here for 20 years ([[1 Samuel 6.21|1 Sam. 6:21]]; [[2 Samuel 6.2|2 Sam. 6:2]]; [[1 Chronicles 13.5|1 Chr. 13:5–6]][[1 Chronicles 13.6|]]; [[2 Chronicles 1.4|2 Chr. 1:4]]). It was also known as Kirjath-Baal or Baale-Judah, and Kirjath-arim.
