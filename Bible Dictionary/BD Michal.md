# Michal

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/michal?lang=eng)

Saul’s second daughter, wife of David ([[1 Samuel 14.49|1 Sam. 14:49]]; [[1 Samuel 18.20|18:20, 27]][[1 Samuel 18.27|]]; [[1 Samuel 19.11|19:11–13, 17]][[1 Samuel 19.12|]][[1 Samuel 19.13|]][[1 Samuel 19.17|]]; [[1 Samuel 25.44|25:44]]; [[2 Samuel 3.13|2 Sam. 3:13–16]][[2 Samuel 3.14|]][[2 Samuel 3.15|]][[2 Samuel 3.16|]]; [[2 Samuel 6.16|6:16, 23]][[2 Samuel 6.23|]]). In [[2 Samuel 21.8|2 Sam. 21:8]] the name Michal is mistakenly used for Merab, who was the wife of Adriel.
