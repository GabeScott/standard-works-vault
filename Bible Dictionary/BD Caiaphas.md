# Caiaphas

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/caiaphas?lang=eng)

High priest from  18 to  36; son-in-law of Annas, high priest  7–14. He belonged to the Sadducee party and took an active part in the attack made upon our Lord and His disciples ([[Matthew 26.3|Matt. 26:3, 57]][[Matthew 26.57|]]; [[Luke 3.2|Luke 3:2]]; [[John 11.49|John 11:49]]; [[John 18.13|18:13–14, 24, 28]][[John 18.14|]][[John 18.24|]][[John 18.28|]]; [[Acts 4.6|Acts 4:6]]).
