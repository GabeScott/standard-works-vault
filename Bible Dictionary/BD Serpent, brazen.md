# Serpent, brazen

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/serpent-brazen?lang=eng)

Made by Moses at God’s command to be a sacramental means of healing for the Israelites in the wilderness ([[Numbers 21.9|Num. 21:9]]). It seems to have been carefully preserved and became an object of superstitious worship ([[2 Kings 18.4|2 Kgs. 18:4]]); it was accordingly destroyed by Hezekiah, who called it “Nehushtan.” The “lifting up” of the serpent in the wilderness is referred to by the Lord as a type of His own “lifting up” upon the cross ([[John 3.14|John 3:14–15]][[John 3.15|]]). Latter-day revelation confirms the episode of the fiery serpents and the healing properties associated with looking upon Moses’ brazen serpent ([[1 Nephi 17.41|1 Ne. 17:41]]; [[2 Nephi 25.20|2 Ne. 25:20]]; [[Alma 33.19|Alma 33:19–22]][[Alma 33.20|]][[Alma 33.21|]][[Alma 33.22|]]; [[Alma 37.46|37:46]]).
