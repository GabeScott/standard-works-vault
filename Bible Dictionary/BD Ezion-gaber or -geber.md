# Ezion-gaber or -geber

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/ezion-gaber?lang=eng)

A place of encampment for the Israelites during the Exodus ([[Numbers 33.35|Num. 33:35]]; [[Deuteronomy 2.8|Deut. 2:8]]); afterwards one of the headquarters of Solomon’s navy ([[1 Kings 9.26|1 Kgs. 9:26]]; [[1 Kings 22.48|22:48]]; [[2 Chronicles 8.17|2 Chr. 8:17]]; [[2 Chronicles 20.36|20:36]]). It was in the extreme south of the country of Edom, at the head of the Gulf of Aqabah.
