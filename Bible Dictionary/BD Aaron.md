# Aaron

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/aaron?lang=eng)

Son of Amram and Jochebed, of the tribe of Levi ([[Exodus 6.16|Ex. 6:16–20]][[Exodus 6.17|]][[Exodus 6.18|]][[Exodus 6.19|]][[Exodus 6.20|]]); elder brother of Moses ([[Exodus 7.7|Ex. 7:7]]). He was appointed by the Lord to assist Moses in bringing the children of Israel out of Egypt and to be his spokesman ([[Exodus 4.10|Ex. 4:10–16, 27–31]][[Exodus 4.11|]][[Exodus 4.12|]][[Exodus 4.13|]][[Exodus 4.14|]][[Exodus 4.15|]][[Exodus 4.16|]][[Exodus 4.27|]][[Exodus 4.28|]][[Exodus 4.29|]][[Exodus 4.30|]][[Exodus 4.31|]]; [[Exodus 5.1|5:1–12:50]]). He was with Moses until the 40th year of the wanderings. At Sinai he was one of those who accompanied Moses up the mountain and saw the Lord ([[Exodus 19.24|Ex. 19:24]]; [[Exodus 24.1|24:1, 9–11]][[Exodus 24.9|]][[Exodus 24.10|]][[Exodus 24.11|]]). When Moses and Joshua were called up into the mount to commune with God, Aaron and Hur were appointed judges during their absence ([[Exodus 24.14|Ex. 24:14, 18]][[Exodus 24.18|]]); it was then that Aaron made the golden calf at the people’s request ([[Exodus 32.1|Ex. 32:1–6, 21, 24, 35]][[Exodus 32.2|]][[Exodus 32.3|]][[Exodus 32.4|]][[Exodus 32.5|]][[Exodus 32.6|]][[Exodus 32.21|]][[Exodus 32.24|]][[Exodus 32.35|]]). On Mount Sinai Moses received directions about the appointment of Aaron and his four sons, Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar, to a priesthood that is known as the Aaronic Priesthood ([[Exodus 28.1|Ex. 28:1–4]][[Exodus 28.2|]][[Exodus 28.3|]][[Exodus 28.4|]]; [[Exodus 29.1|29]]; [[D&C 84.18|D&C 84:18, 26–27]][[D&C 84.26|]][[D&C 84.27|]]). On the completion of the tabernacle Moses consecrated them to their office ([[Leviticus 8.9|Lev. 8:9–13]][[Leviticus 8.10|]][[Leviticus 8.11|]][[Leviticus 8.12|]][[Leviticus 8.13|]]). Aaron died on Mount Hor at the age of 123 ([[Numbers 20.22|Num. 20:22–29]][[Numbers 20.23|]][[Numbers 20.24|]][[Numbers 20.25|]][[Numbers 20.26|]][[Numbers 20.27|]][[Numbers 20.28|]][[Numbers 20.29|]]; [[Numbers 33.38|33:38–39]][[Numbers 33.39|]]). For additional items see [[Exodus 16.2|Ex. 16:2–10, 33–34]][[Exodus 16.3|]][[Exodus 16.4|]][[Exodus 16.5|]][[Exodus 16.6|]][[Exodus 16.7|]][[Exodus 16.8|]][[Exodus 16.9|]][[Exodus 16.10|]][[Exodus 16.33|]][[Exodus 16.34|]]; [[Exodus 18.12|18:12]].
