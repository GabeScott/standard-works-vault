# Firmament

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/firmament?lang=eng)

The expanse of the heavens. It is the KJV English translation of a Hebrew word meaning expanse, and is spoken of in [[Genesis 1.1|Gen. 1]]; [[Psalms 19.1|Ps. 19:1]]; [[Moses 2.1|Moses 2]]; [Abr. 4; Abr., fac. 1, fig. 12](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/abr/fac-1?lang=eng#figure1_p12); see esp. [[Abraham 4.7|Abr. 4:7–8, 20]][[Abraham 4.8|]][[Abraham 4.20|]].
