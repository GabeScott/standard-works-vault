# Cor

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/cor?lang=eng)

A measure amounting to about 10 bushels and 3 gallons, used for both solids and fluids ([[Ezekiel 45.14|Ezek. 45:14]]); generally translated “measure” in [[1 Kings 4.22|1 Kgs. 4:22]]; [[1 Kings 5.11|5:11]]; [[2 Chronicles 2.10|2 Chr. 2:10]]; [[2 Chronicles 27.5|27:5]]; [[Ezra 7.22|Ezra 7:22]].
