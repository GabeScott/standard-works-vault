# Hebron

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hebron?lang=eng)

An ancient city of Judah, 3040 feet above the Mediterranean, 20 miles south of Jerusalem; known also as Mamre and Kirjath-arba ([[Genesis 13.18|Gen. 13:18]]; [[Genesis 35.27|35:27]]). It was the burying place of Abraham and his family ([[Genesis 23.2|23:2, 19]][[Genesis 23.19|]]; [[Genesis 49.29|49:29–32]][[Genesis 49.30|]][[Genesis 49.31|]][[Genesis 49.32|]]), and became the property of Caleb ([[Joshua 14.13|Josh. 14:13–15]][[Joshua 14.14|]][[Joshua 14.15|]]). It was one of the six cities of refuge ([[Joshua 20.7|Josh. 20:7]]), a Levitical city ([[Joshua 21.11|Josh. 21:11, 13]][[Joshua 21.13|]]), and David’s capital during the earlier part of his reign ([[2 Samuel 5.3|2 Sam. 5:3–5]][[2 Samuel 5.4|]][[2 Samuel 5.5|]]). During the later history of the nation it continued to be a place of importance, and it still has a large population.
