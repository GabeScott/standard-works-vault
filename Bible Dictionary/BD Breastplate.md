# Breastplate

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/breastplate?lang=eng)

(1) The front part of a soldier’s dress, worn for protection. In this sense Isaiah and Paul spoke of a “breastplate of righteousness,” which all saints should possess, protecting the vital organs against the evil things of life ([[Isaiah 59.17|Isa. 59:17]]; [[Ephesians 6.14|Eph. 6:14]]).

(2) The high priest in the law of Moses wore a breastplate as part of his sacred attire. This was called the “breastplate of judgment” ([[Exodus 28.13|Ex. 28:13–30]][[Exodus 28.14|]][[Exodus 28.15|]][[Exodus 28.16|]][[Exodus 28.17|]][[Exodus 28.18|]][[Exodus 28.19|]][[Exodus 28.20|]][[Exodus 28.21|]][[Exodus 28.22|]][[Exodus 28.23|]][[Exodus 28.24|]][[Exodus 28.25|]][[Exodus 28.26|]][[Exodus 28.27|]][[Exodus 28.28|]][[Exodus 28.29|]][[Exodus 28.30|]]; [[Exodus 39.8|39:8–21]][[Exodus 39.9|]][[Exodus 39.10|]][[Exodus 39.11|]][[Exodus 39.12|]][[Exodus 39.13|]][[Exodus 39.14|]][[Exodus 39.15|]][[Exodus 39.16|]][[Exodus 39.17|]][[Exodus 39.18|]][[Exodus 39.19|]][[Exodus 39.20|]][[Exodus 39.21|]]). It was made of linen, very colorfully arranged, bearing 12 precious stones and the Urim and Thummim. Other references to a breastplate are found in [[1 Thessalonians 5.8|1 Thes. 5:8]]; [[Revelation 9.9|Rev. 9:9]]; [[D&C 17.1|D&C 17:1]]; [[Joseph Smith History 1.35|JS—H 1:35, 42, 52]][[Joseph Smith History 1.42|]][[Joseph Smith History 1.52|]].
