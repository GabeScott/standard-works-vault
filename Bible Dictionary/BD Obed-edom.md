# Obed-edom

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/obed-edom?lang=eng)

*Servant of Edom.* A Levite belonging to the family of Kohath; called a Gittite because he belonged to the Levitical city of Gath-rimmon. The Ark was kept at his house for six months ([[2 Samuel 6.10|2 Sam. 6:10–12]][[2 Samuel 6.11|]][[2 Samuel 6.12|]]; [[1 Chronicles 13.13|1 Chr. 13:13–14]][[1 Chronicles 13.14|]]). He was made doorkeeper of the Ark in Zion ([[1 Chronicles 15.24|1 Chr. 15:24–25]][[1 Chronicles 15.25|]]; [[1 Chronicles 16.5|16:5, 38]][[1 Chronicles 16.38|]]). His family kept the temple storehouse ([[1 Chronicles 26.4|1 Chr. 26:4, 8, 15]][[1 Chronicles 26.8|]][[1 Chronicles 26.15|]]; [[2 Chronicles 25.24|2 Chr. 25:24]]).
