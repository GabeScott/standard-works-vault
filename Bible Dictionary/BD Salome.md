# Salome

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/salome?lang=eng)

(1) Wife of Zebedee ([[Matthew 20.20|Matt. 20:20]]; [[Matthew 27.56|27:56]]; [[Mark 15.40|Mark 15:40]]; [[Mark 16.1|16:1]]).

(2) The daughter of Herodias who danced before Herod Antipas, culminating in the death of John the Baptist ([[Matthew 14.3|Matt. 14:3–11]][[Matthew 14.4|]][[Matthew 14.5|]][[Matthew 14.6|]][[Matthew 14.7|]][[Matthew 14.8|]][[Matthew 14.9|]][[Matthew 14.10|]][[Matthew 14.11|]]; [[Mark 6.17|Mark 6:17–28]][[Mark 6.18|]][[Mark 6.19|]][[Mark 6.20|]][[Mark 6.21|]][[Mark 6.22|]][[Mark 6.23|]][[Mark 6.24|]][[Mark 6.25|]][[Mark 6.26|]][[Mark 6.27|]][[Mark 6.28|]]). Her name is not mentioned in scripture but is given in Josephus, *Antiquities,* 18.5.4.
