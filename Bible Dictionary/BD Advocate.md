# Advocate

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/advocate?lang=eng)

One who pleads another’s cause. Jesus Christ is the Advocate with the Father, on behalf of the righteous ([[1 John 2.1|1 Jn. 2:1]]; [[Moroni 7.28|Moro. 7:28]]; [[D&C 45.3|D&C 45:3–5]][[D&C 45.4|]][[D&C 45.5|]]; [[D&C 110.4|110:4]]). See also *[[BD Atonement|Atonement]]; [[BD Christ|Christ]].*
