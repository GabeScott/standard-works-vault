# Giants

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/giants?lang=eng)

Unusually large, tall persons, apparently having great physical strength. They are mentioned both before the Flood ([[Genesis 6.4|Gen. 6:4]]; [[Moses 8.18|Moses 8:18]]) and after ([[Numbers 13.33|Num. 13:33]]; [[Deuteronomy 2.10|Deut. 2:10–11, 20]][[Deuteronomy 2.11|]][[Deuteronomy 2.20|]]; [[Deuteronomy 3.11|3:11–13]][[Deuteronomy 3.12|]][[Deuteronomy 3.13|]]; [[Deuteronomy 9.2|9:2]]; [[Joshua 15.8|Josh. 15:8]]; [[Joshua 18.16|18:16]]). Raphah of Gath was said to be the father of several giants of whom Goliath was one ([[1 Samuel 17.4|1 Sam. 17:4–7]][[1 Samuel 17.5|]][[1 Samuel 17.6|]][[1 Samuel 17.7|]]; [[2 Samuel 21.16|2 Sam. 21:16–22]][[2 Samuel 21.17|]][[2 Samuel 21.18|]][[2 Samuel 21.19|]][[2 Samuel 21.20|]][[2 Samuel 21.21|]][[2 Samuel 21.22|]]; [[1 Chronicles 20.6|1 Chr. 20:6]]). A 12-fingered, 12-toed giant is also mentioned as one of the sons. The giants in Palestine were also known as Anakims, Emims, and Zamzummims.
