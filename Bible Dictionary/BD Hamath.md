# Hamath

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hamath?lang=eng)

An important city of Syria, in the valley of the Orontes; the “entering in of Hamath” was the northern limit of the Promised Land ([[Numbers 34.8|Num. 34:8]]; [[Joshua 13.5|Josh. 13:5]]). Toi, king of Hamath, was on friendly terms with David ([[2 Samuel 8.10|2 Sam. 8:10]]). The city seems to have come under the dominion of Solomon ([[1 Kings 4.21|1 Kgs. 4:21–24]][[1 Kings 4.22|]][[1 Kings 4.23|]][[1 Kings 4.24|]]; [[2 Chronicles 8.4|2 Chr. 8:4]]). It was afterwards taken by the Assyrians ([[2 Kings 14.28|2 Kgs. 14:28]]). It is still a town of considerable importance.
