# Melchizedek

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/melchizedek?lang=eng)

*King of Righteousness.* A notable prophet and leader who lived about 2000  He is called the king of Salem (Jerusalem), king of peace, and “priest of the most High God.” Unfortunately, information concerning him in the Bible is relatively scarce, being limited to [[Genesis 14.18|Gen. 14:18–20]][[Genesis 14.19|]][[Genesis 14.20|]]; [[Hebrews 5.6|Heb. 5:6]]; [[Hebrews 7.1|7:1–3]][[Hebrews 7.2|]][[Hebrews 7.3|]]. Mention of the priesthood of Melchizedek is given in several other instances, primarily in Psalms and in Hebrews. However, latter-day revelation gives us much more about him and his priesthood (see [[Genesis 14.18|JST Gen. 14:17 (Gen. 14:18 note d)]]; [[JST Genesis 14.25|JST Gen. 14:25–40]][[JST Genesis 14.26|]][[JST Genesis 14.27|]][[JST Genesis 14.28|]][[JST Genesis 14.29|]][[JST Genesis 14.30|]][[JST Genesis 14.31|]][[JST Genesis 14.32|]][[JST Genesis 14.33|]][[JST Genesis 14.34|]][[JST Genesis 14.35|]][[JST Genesis 14.36|]][[JST Genesis 14.37|]][[JST Genesis 14.38|]][[JST Genesis 14.39|]][[JST Genesis 14.40|]]; [[JST Hebrews 7.3|JST Heb. 7:3 (Appendix)]]; [[Alma 13.14|Alma 13:14–19]][[Alma 13.15|]][[Alma 13.16|]][[Alma 13.17|]][[Alma 13.18|]][[Alma 13.19|]]; [[D&C 84.14|D&C 84:14]]; [[D&C 107.1|107:1–4]][[D&C 107.2|]][[D&C 107.3|]][[D&C 107.4|]]). From these sources we realize something of the greatness of this prophet and the grandeur of his ministry.
