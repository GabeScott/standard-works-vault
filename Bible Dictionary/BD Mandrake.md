# Mandrake

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/mandrake?lang=eng)

The Hebrew name denotes “love fruit.” The fruit had a pleasant taste and odor and was supposed to ensure conception ([[Genesis 30.14|Gen. 30:14–16]][[Genesis 30.15|]][[Genesis 30.16|]]; [[Song of Solomon 7.13|Song 7:13]]).
