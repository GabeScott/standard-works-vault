# Charity

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/charity?lang=eng)

The highest, noblest, strongest kind of love, not merely affection; the pure love of Christ. It is never used to denote alms or deeds of benevolence, although it may be a prompting motive ([[1 Corinthians 8.1|1 Cor. 8:1]]; [[1 Corinthians 13.1|13:1–4, 8, 13]][[1 Corinthians 13.2|]][[1 Corinthians 13.3|]][[1 Corinthians 13.4|]][[1 Corinthians 13.8|]][[1 Corinthians 13.13|]]; [[1 Corinthians 14.1|14:1]]; [[Moroni 7.47|Moro. 7:47]]).
