# Chemosh

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/chemosh?lang=eng)

The god of Moab ([[1 Kings 11.7|1 Kgs. 11:7]]); also of Ammon ([[Judges 11.24|Judg. 11:24]]). Solomon built for him a high place on Mount Olivet ([[1 Kings 11.7|1 Kgs. 11:7]]), which Josiah destroyed ([[2 Kings 23.13|2 Kgs. 23:13]]). Chemosh was worshipped with human sacrifices ([[2 Kings 3.27|2 Kgs. 3:27]]). See also *[[BD Moabite Stone|Moabite Stone]].*
