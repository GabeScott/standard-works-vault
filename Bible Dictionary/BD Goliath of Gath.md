# Goliath of Gath

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/goliath?lang=eng)

The Philistine giant killed by David ([[1 Samuel 17.1|1 Sam. 17]]). In [[2 Samuel 21.19|2 Sam. 21:19]] the reference is probably to a different man of the same name, or some error may have crept into the text. See *[[BD Giants|Giants]].*
