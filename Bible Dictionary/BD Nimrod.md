# Nimrod

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/nimrod?lang=eng)

Son of Cush, grandson of Ham; the Babylonian hunter and traditional builder of Nineveh ([[Genesis 10.8|Gen. 10:8–9]][[Genesis 10.9|]]; [[1 Chronicles 1.10|1 Chr. 1:10]]). See also [[Micah 5.6|Micah 5:6]], where Assyria is called “land of Nimrod.”
