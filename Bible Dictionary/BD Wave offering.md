# Wave offering

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/wave-offering?lang=eng)

Part of the ritual of the Feast of the Passover ([[Leviticus 23.10|Lev. 23:10–14]][[Leviticus 23.11|]][[Leviticus 23.12|]][[Leviticus 23.13|]][[Leviticus 23.14|]]); see *[[BD Feasts|Feasts]].* Waving was a movement backwards and forwards, while heaving was a movement up and down.
