# Suffer, suffered

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/suffer?lang=eng)

To permit, allow ([[Genesis 20.6|Gen. 20:6]]; [[Genesis 31.7|31:7]]; [[Matthew 3.15|Matt. 3:15]]; [[Matthew 17.17|17:17]]; [[1 Timothy 2.12|1 Tim. 2:12]]). Also used in its current meaning of enduring and tolerating pain, affliction, or an uncomfortable situation ([[Psalms 9.13|Ps. 9:13]]; [[Acts 3.18|Acts 3:18]]; [[Hebrews 5.8|Heb. 5:8]]; [[1 Peter 3.18|1 Pet. 3:18]]; [[1 Peter 4.1|4:1]]).
