# Cain

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/cain?lang=eng)

A son of Adam and Eve who slew his younger brother Abel over jealousy and greed ([[Genesis 4.1|Gen. 4:1–16]][[Genesis 4.2|]][[Genesis 4.3|]][[Genesis 4.4|]][[Genesis 4.5|]][[Genesis 4.6|]][[Genesis 4.7|]][[Genesis 4.8|]][[Genesis 4.9|]][[Genesis 4.10|]][[Genesis 4.11|]][[Genesis 4.12|]][[Genesis 4.13|]][[Genesis 4.14|]][[Genesis 4.15|]][[Genesis 4.16|]]). Some of his descendants were skilled in the use of the harp and organ and in working with brass and iron ([[Genesis 4.17|Gen. 4:17–25]][[Genesis 4.18|]][[Genesis 4.19|]][[Genesis 4.20|]][[Genesis 4.21|]][[Genesis 4.22|]][[Genesis 4.23|]][[Genesis 4.24|]][[Genesis 4.25|]]). Due to the incompleteness of the biblical record, many have regarded Cain as the eldest son of Adam. However, latter-day revelation tells us that Adam and Eve had many sons and daughters before Cain was born ([[Moses 5.1|Moses 5:1–3, 16–18]][[Moses 5.2|]][[Moses 5.3|]][[Moses 5.16|]][[Moses 5.17|]][[Moses 5.18|]]). He married one of his brothers’ daughters ([[Moses 5.28|Moses 5:28]]).

Cain’s offering was rejected by the Lord, probably for several reasons, including the fact that it was not done in faith nor in the prescribed and revealed manner (compare [[Moses 5.5|Moses 5:5–8]][[Moses 5.6|]][[Moses 5.7|]][[Moses 5.8|]] with [[Genesis 4.3|Gen. 4:3–5]][[Genesis 4.4|]][[Genesis 4.5|]]). He entered into an unholy covenant with Satan by which he became known as Master Mahan, or “master of this great secret,” and by which he could “murder and get gain” ([[Moses 5.29|Moses 5:29–31]][[Moses 5.30|]][[Moses 5.31|]]). A mark was set upon him by which he could be distinguished from the other children of Adam ([[Genesis 4.15|Gen. 4:15]]; [[Moses 5.38|Moses 5:38–41]][[Moses 5.39|]][[Moses 5.40|]][[Moses 5.41|]]). Cain and his descendants lived separately from the other descendants of Adam ([[Moses 7.22|Moses 7:22]]).

Cain is mentioned frequently in the scriptures, including [[Hebrews 11.4|Heb. 11:4]]; [[1 John 3.12|1 Jn. 3:12]]; [[Jude 1.11|Jude 1:11]]; [[Helaman 6.27|Hel. 6:27]]; [[Ether 8.15|Ether 8:15]]; [[Moses 5.1|Moses 5–7]]; see also [[D&C 84.16|D&C 84:16]].
