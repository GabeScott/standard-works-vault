# Retaliation, law of, orlex talionis

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/retaliation-law-of?lang=eng)

([[Exodus 21.23|Ex. 21:23–25]][[Exodus 21.24|]][[Exodus 21.25|]]; [[Leviticus 24.18|Lev. 24:18–21]][[Leviticus 24.19|]][[Leviticus 24.20|]][[Leviticus 24.21|]]; [[Deuteronomy 19.21|Deut. 19:21]].) The Mosaic law aimed at making the punishment an appropriate equivalent to the offence committed. Wrongdoing must not go unpunished; on the other hand, however, the punishment must not be vindictive or excessive. In the Sermon on the Mount ([[Matthew 5.38|Matt. 5:38–42]][[Matthew 5.39|]][[Matthew 5.40|]][[Matthew 5.41|]][[Matthew 5.42|]]), the Lord goes further, and urges us, so far as private wrongs to ourselves are concerned, not to retaliate at all ([[Romans 12.17|Rom. 12:17, 19]][[Romans 12.19|]]). See also *[[BD Revenge|Revenge]].*
