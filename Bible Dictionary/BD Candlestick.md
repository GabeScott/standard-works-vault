# Candlestick

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/candlestick?lang=eng)

(More properly *menorah,* or lampstand.) Made for the tabernacle ([[Exodus 25.31|Ex. 25:31–37]][[Exodus 25.32|]][[Exodus 25.33|]][[Exodus 25.34|]][[Exodus 25.35|]][[Exodus 25.36|]][[Exodus 25.37|]]; [[Exodus 37.17|37:17–24]][[Exodus 37.18|]][[Exodus 37.19|]][[Exodus 37.20|]][[Exodus 37.21|]][[Exodus 37.22|]][[Exodus 37.23|]][[Exodus 37.24|]]), placed on the south side, opposite the table of shewbread. From the main vertical stem three pairs of branches extended, curving upward so that all seven lamps on the ends were at the same level. In Solomon’s temple there were 10 golden lampstands ([[1 Kings 7.49|1 Kgs. 7:49]]; [[2 Chronicles 4.7|2 Chr. 4:7]]). In the temple of Zerubbabel there was a single lampstand, afterwards carried off by Titus from the Herodian temple, and said to have been finally placed ( 533) in a Christian church in Jerusalem. The metaphor in [[1 Kings 11.36|1 Kgs. 11:36]], [[Psalms 18.28|Ps. 18:28]], and [[Revelation 2.5|Rev. 2:5]] refers to the eastern custom of keeping a lamp burning day and night.
