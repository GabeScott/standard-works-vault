# Parousia

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/parousia?lang=eng)

A Greek word that is the technical term in the New Testament for the Second Coming of the Lord in glory to judge the world (see [[Matthew 24.3|Matt. 24:3, 27, 39]][[Matthew 24.27|]][[Matthew 24.39|]]; [[1 Corinthians 15.23|1 Cor. 15:23]]; [[1 Thessalonians 2.19|1 Thes. 2:19]]; [[1 Thessalonians 3.13|3:13]]; [[1 Thessalonians 4.15|4:15]]; [[1 Thessalonians 5.23|5:23]]; [[2 Thessalonians 2.1|2 Thes. 2:1, 8]][[2 Thessalonians 2.8|]]; [[2 Peter 1.16|2 Pet. 1:16]]; [[2 Peter 3.4|3:4]]).
