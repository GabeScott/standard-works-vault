# Frontlets or phylacteries

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/frontlets?lang=eng)

Strips of parchment on which were written four passages of scriptures ([[Exodus 13.1|Ex. 13:1–10, 11–16]][[Exodus 13.2|]][[Exodus 13.3|]][[Exodus 13.4|]][[Exodus 13.5|]][[Exodus 13.6|]][[Exodus 13.7|]][[Exodus 13.8|]][[Exodus 13.9|]][[Exodus 13.10|]][[Exodus 13.11|]][[Exodus 13.12|]][[Exodus 13.13|]][[Exodus 13.14|]][[Exodus 13.15|]][[Exodus 13.16|]]; [[Deuteronomy 6.5|Deut. 6:5–9]][[Deuteronomy 6.6|]][[Deuteronomy 6.7|]][[Deuteronomy 6.8|]][[Deuteronomy 6.9|]]; [[Deuteronomy 11.13|11:13–21]][[Deuteronomy 11.14|]][[Deuteronomy 11.15|]][[Deuteronomy 11.16|]][[Deuteronomy 11.17|]][[Deuteronomy 11.18|]][[Deuteronomy 11.19|]][[Deuteronomy 11.20|]][[Deuteronomy 11.21|]]) and that were rolled up and attached to bands of leather worn (as an act of obedience to [[Exodus 13.9|Ex. 13:9, 16]][[Exodus 13.16|]]; [[Deuteronomy 6.8|Deut. 6:8]]; [[Deuteronomy 11.18|11:18]]) around the forehead or around the arm. The Jews called them *tephillin.* The Pharisees wore them broader than other people in order to make them as conspicuous as possible ([[Matthew 23.5|Matt. 23:5]]).
