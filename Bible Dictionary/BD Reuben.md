# Reuben

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/reuben?lang=eng)

Eldest son of Jacob ([[Genesis 29.32|Gen. 29:32]]; [[Genesis 30.14|30:14]]; [[Genesis 35.22|35:22–23]][[Genesis 35.23|]]; [[Genesis 37.21|37:21–22, 29]][[Genesis 37.22|]][[Genesis 37.29|]]; [[Genesis 42.22|42:22, 37]][[Genesis 42.37|]]; [[Numbers 26.5|Num. 26:5]]); settled east of Jordan ([[Numbers 32.1|Num. 32]]); the special characteristics of the tribe are referred to in [[Genesis 49.3|Gen. 49:3]]; [[Deuteronomy 33.6|Deut. 33:6]]. The tribe’s numbers gradually diminished, and though it continued to exist as a tribe, it became politically unimportant. In [[Judges 5.15|Judg. 5:15–16]][[Judges 5.16|]], it is reproached by Deborah for its indifference during a time of national peril. For other references see [[Joshua 4.12|Josh. 4:12]]; [[Joshua 13.15|13:15–23]][[Joshua 13.16|]][[Joshua 13.17|]][[Joshua 13.18|]][[Joshua 13.19|]][[Joshua 13.20|]][[Joshua 13.21|]][[Joshua 13.22|]][[Joshua 13.23|]]; [[Joshua 18.7|18:7]]; [[Joshua 22.1|22]]; [[1 Chronicles 5.1|1 Chr. 5:1, 3, 18]][[1 Chronicles 5.3|]][[1 Chronicles 5.18|]]; [[Ezekiel 48.6|Ezek. 48:6–7, 31]][[Ezekiel 48.7|]][[Ezekiel 48.31|]]; [[Revelation 7.5|Rev. 7:5]]. Levite cities in, [[Joshua 20.8|Josh. 20:8]]; [[Joshua 21.7|21:7, 36]][[Joshua 21.36|]]; [[1 Chronicles 6.63|1 Chr. 6:63, 78]][[1 Chronicles 6.78|]]. Dathan and Abiram, men of Reuben, [[Numbers 16.1|Num. 16:1]]; [[Deuteronomy 11.6|Deut. 11:6]]; Bohan, son of Reuben, [[Joshua 15.6|Josh. 15:6]]; [[Joshua 18.17|18:17]].

Although Reuben was the firstborn of Jacob and Leah, he lost his birthright because of transgression ([[Genesis 35.22|Gen. 35:22]]; [[Genesis 49.3|49:3–4]][[Genesis 49.4|]]). The blessing therefore came upon Joseph, the firstborn of Jacob and Rachel ([[1 Chronicles 5.1|1 Chr. 5:1–2]][[1 Chronicles 5.2|]]).
