# Egypt, River of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/egypt-river-of?lang=eng)

Frequently mentioned in the Old Testament ([[Numbers 34.5|Num. 34:5]]; [[Joshua 15.4|Josh. 15:4, 47]][[Joshua 15.47|]]; [[1 Kings 8.65|1 Kgs. 8:65]]; [[2 Kings 24.7|2 Kgs. 24:7]]). The word translated *river* is really *brook;* consequently the name denotes not the Nile, but the Wady el ʼArish, on the border of Egypt, in northern Sinai.
