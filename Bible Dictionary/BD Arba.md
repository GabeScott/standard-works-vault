# Arba

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/arba?lang=eng)

Father of Anak, ancestor of a race of giants ([[Joshua 14.15|Josh. 14:15]]; [[Joshua 15.13|15:13]]; [[Joshua 21.11|21:11]]); founder of city of Arba, called Kirjath-arba, afterwards known as Hebron ([[Genesis 35.27|Gen. 35:27]]).
