# Topheth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/topheth?lang=eng)

A spot in the valley of the son of Hinnom, south of Jerusalem, where human sacrifices were offered to Molech ([[2 Kings 23.10|2 Kgs. 23:10]]; [[Isaiah 30.33|Isa. 30:33]]; [[Jeremiah 7.31|Jer. 7:31–32]][[Jeremiah 7.32|]]; [[Jeremiah 19.6|19:6, 13]][[Jeremiah 19.13|]]).
