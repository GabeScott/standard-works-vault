# Anoint

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/anoint?lang=eng)

To apply oil or ointment to the head or the person. Anciently anointing was done for reasons both secular and sacred. It is a sign of hospitality in [[Luke 7.46|Luke 7:46]] and of routine personal grooming in [[2 Samuel 12.20|2 Sam. 12:20]] and [[Matthew 6.17|Matt. 6:17]]. The maimed or sick were anointed with wine, oil, or ointment as medicine ([[Isaiah 1.6|Isa. 1:6]]; [[Luke 10.34|Luke 10:34]]; [[Revelation 3.18|Rev. 3:18]]). The sick were also anointed with oil as part of the sacred procedure in healing of the sick by faith and the laying on of hands ([[Mark 6.13|Mark 6:13]]; [[James 5.14|James 5:14–15]][[James 5.15|]]).

Kings were anointed to their office by the prophets ([[1 Samuel 10.1|1 Sam. 10:1]]; [[1 Samuel 16.13|16:13]]; [[2 Samuel 5.3|2 Sam. 5:3]]; [[1 Kings 1.39|1 Kgs. 1:39]]; [[1 Kings 19.16|19:16]]; [[2 Kings 9.3|2 Kgs. 9:3, 6]][[2 Kings 9.6|]]; [[2 Kings 11.12|11:12]]; [[1 Chronicles 11.3|1 Chr. 11:3]]; [[1 Chronicles 29.22|29:22]]; [[2 Chronicles 23.11|2 Chr. 23:11]]). The anointing of the priests is outlined in [[Exodus 40.15|Ex. 40:15]]; of the high priest (Aaronic order) in [[Leviticus 21.10|Lev. 21:10]]. Elisha was to be anointed a prophet by Elijah ([[1 Kings 19.16|1 Kgs. 19:16]]).

The holy anointing oil used in the law of Moses was composed of olive oil mixed with spices and was to be restricted in use to certain specified ceremonies ([[Exodus 30.22|Ex. 30:22–33]][[Exodus 30.23|]][[Exodus 30.24|]][[Exodus 30.25|]][[Exodus 30.26|]][[Exodus 30.27|]][[Exodus 30.28|]][[Exodus 30.29|]][[Exodus 30.30|]][[Exodus 30.31|]][[Exodus 30.32|]][[Exodus 30.33|]]; [[Exodus 37.29|37:29]]). Paul and John speak of an anointing of the Spirit ([[2 Corinthians 1.21|2 Cor. 1:21–22]][[2 Corinthians 1.22|]]; [[1 John 2.20|1 Jn. 2:20, 27]][[1 John 2.27|]]), and Peter says that “God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power” ([[Acts 10.38|Acts 10:38]]). See *[[BD Anointed One|Anointed One]].*

In the Church today holy consecrated olive oil is used in anointing persons in various sacred ceremonies, including administration to the sick. Although the scriptures do not specifically so state, we may confidently assume that anointing with oil has been part of true, revealed religion ever since the gospel was first introduced on this earth to Adam.
