# Zaphnath-paaneah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/zaphnath-paaneah?lang=eng)

A name given by Pharaoh to Joseph ([[Genesis 41.45|Gen. 41:45]]). Although its meaning is uncertain, the Rabbinical commentaries, following the Targum, interpret the name as “he who reveals that which is hidden.”
