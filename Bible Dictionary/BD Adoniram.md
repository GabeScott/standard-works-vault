# Adoniram

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/adoniram?lang=eng)

*My Lord is exalted* (sometimes abbreviated into Adoram). The officer “over the levy” who superintended the forced labor employed in public works during the reigns of David, Solomon, and Rehoboam ([[2 Samuel 20.24|2 Sam. 20:24]]; [[1 Kings 4.6|1 Kgs. 4:6]]; [[1 Kings 5.14|5:14]]; [[1 Kings 12.18|12:18]]); death of, by stoning ([[2 Chronicles 10.18|2 Chr. 10:18]], where he is called Hadoram).
