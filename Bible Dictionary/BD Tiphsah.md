# Tiphsah

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/tiphsah?lang=eng)

Thapsacus, on the western bank of the Euphrates, the river being at that point fordable; a boundary of Solomon’s dominions ([[1 Kings 4.24|1 Kgs. 4:24]]); also famous in classic history as the place where Cyrus with his 10,000 troops crossed the river. The place of this name mentioned in [[2 Kings 15.16|2 Kgs. 15:16]] is probably within the kingdom of Israel; its site is unknown.
