# Hivites

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hivites?lang=eng)

A Canaanitish race ([[Genesis 10.17|Gen. 10:17]]; [[Genesis 34.2|34:2]]; [[Exodus 3.8|Ex. 3:8, 17]][[Exodus 3.17|]]; [[Exodus 13.5|13:5]]; [[Joshua 9.7|Josh. 9:7]]; [[Joshua 11.19|11:19]]). A colony of Hivites, settled in Gibeon, made a treaty with Joshua, having deceived him by a stratagem; but the main body appear to have lived in northern Palestine ([[Joshua 11.3|Josh. 11:3]]; [[Judges 3.3|Judg. 3:3]]; [[2 Samuel 24.7|2 Sam. 24:7]]).
