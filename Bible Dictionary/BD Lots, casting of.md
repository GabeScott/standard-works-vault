# Lots, casting of

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/lots-casting-of?lang=eng)

There are many instances in scripture of the use of lots for the purpose of making a choice ([[Leviticus 16.8|Lev. 16:8]]; [[Numbers 26.55|Num. 26:55]]; [[Numbers 33.54|33:54]]; [[Numbers 34.13|34:13]]; [[Joshua 13.6|Josh. 13:6]]; [[Joshua 14.1|14:1–2]][[Joshua 14.2|]]; [[Judges 20.9|Judg. 20:9]]; [[1 Samuel 10.20|1 Sam. 10:20–21]][[1 Samuel 10.21|]]; [[1 Samuel 14.40|14:40–42]][[1 Samuel 14.41|]][[1 Samuel 14.42|]]; [[1 Chronicles 24.5|1 Chr. 24:5, 31]][[1 Chronicles 24.31|]]; [[1 Chronicles 25.8|25:8]]; [[1 Chronicles 26.13|26:13–14]][[1 Chronicles 26.14|]]; [[Acts 1.26|Acts 1:26]]). [[Proverbs 16.33|Prov. 16:33]] expresses a feeling on the subject of the Lord’s hand in the matter.
