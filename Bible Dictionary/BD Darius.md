# Darius

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/darius?lang=eng)

(1) The Mede, king of Babylon after the death of Belshazzar ([[Daniel 5.31|Dan. 5:31]]; [[Daniel 6.9|6:9, 25–28]][[Daniel 6.25|]][[Daniel 6.26|]][[Daniel 6.27|]][[Daniel 6.28|]]; [[Daniel 9.1|9:1]]; [[Daniel 11.1|11:1]]). It is impossible to identify him with any of the kings of Babylon known to secular history.

(3) Darius Codomannus, called Darius the Persian in [[Nehemiah 12.22|Neh. 12:22]]; the last king of Persia, overthrown by Alexander the Great 330
