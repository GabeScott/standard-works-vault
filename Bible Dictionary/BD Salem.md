# Salem

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/salem?lang=eng)

*Peace.* The home of Melchizedek ([[Genesis 14.18|Gen. 14:18]]; [[Hebrews 7.1|Heb. 7:1–2]][[Hebrews 7.2|]]; [[Alma 13.17|Alma 13:17–18]][[Alma 13.18|]]). Salem was called Jebus at the time the Israelites came into Canaan. However, the Tell el-Amarna tablets of about 1400  give the name of the city as Uru-Salem (an older name than Jebus), which is easily identified as Jerusalem, the “City of Peace,” the city of Melchizedek about 2000
