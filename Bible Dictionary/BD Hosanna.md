# Hosanna

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hosanna?lang=eng)

*Save now.* The word is taken from [[Psalms 118.25|Ps. 118:25]], one of the Psalms of the Hallel. The chanting of this psalm was connected at the Feast of Tabernacles with the waving of palm branches; hence the use of the word by the multitudes at our Lord’s triumphal entry into Jerusalem ([[Matthew 21.9|Matt. 21:9, 15]][[Matthew 21.15|]]; [[Mark 11.9|Mark 11:9–10]][[Mark 11.10|]]; [[John 12.13|John 12:13]]).
