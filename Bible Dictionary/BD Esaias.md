# Esaias

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/esaias?lang=eng)

The New Testament (Greek) form of the name Isaiah, as in [[Luke 4.17|Luke 4:17]] and [[Acts 8.30|Acts 8:30]]. Also an ancient prophet who lived in the days of Abraham and who was blessed by him. He was a bearer of the Melchizedek Priesthood ([[D&C 84.11|D&C 84:11–13]][[D&C 84.12|]][[D&C 84.13|]]).
