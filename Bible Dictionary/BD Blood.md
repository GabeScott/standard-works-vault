# Blood

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/blood?lang=eng)

Regarded by the Israelites as the seat of the life or vital energy of all flesh ([[Leviticus 17.10|Lev. 17:10–14]][[Leviticus 17.11|]][[Leviticus 17.12|]][[Leviticus 17.13|]][[Leviticus 17.14|]]). In Old Testament times blood was forbidden as food ([[Genesis 9.4|Gen. 9:4]]; [[Leviticus 3.17|Lev. 3:17]]; [[Leviticus 7.26|7:26–27]][[Leviticus 7.27|]]; [[Leviticus 19.26|19:26]]; [[Deuteronomy 12.16|Deut. 12:16, 23]][[Deuteronomy 12.23|]]; [[Deuteronomy 15.23|15:23]]; [[1 Samuel 14.32|1 Sam. 14:32–34]][[1 Samuel 14.33|]][[1 Samuel 14.34|]]), which restriction was continued in the Church in New Testament times, at the Jerusalem conference ([[Acts 15.20|Acts 15:20–29]][[Acts 15.21|]][[Acts 15.22|]][[Acts 15.23|]][[Acts 15.24|]][[Acts 15.25|]][[Acts 15.26|]][[Acts 15.27|]][[Acts 15.28|]][[Acts 15.29|]]). The atoning power of a sacrifice was in the blood because it was regarded as containing the life of the animal and because the sacrifice was a type of the great sacrifice who is Jesus Christ ([[Leviticus 17.11|Lev. 17:11]]; [[Hebrews 9.22|Heb. 9:22]]). The scripture says that “almost all things are by the law purged with blood; and without shedding of blood is no remission” ([[Hebrews 9.22|Heb. 9:22]]). Jesus worked out a perfect atonement by the shedding of His own blood ([[1 John 1.7|1 Jn. 1:7]]; [[Revelation 5.9|Rev. 5:9–10]][[Revelation 5.10|]]; [[Mosiah 3.16|Mosiah 3:16–19]][[Mosiah 3.17|]][[Mosiah 3.18|]][[Mosiah 3.19|]]; [[3 Nephi 27.19|3 Ne. 27:19–21]][[3 Nephi 27.20|]][[3 Nephi 27.21|]]; [[D&C 45.4|D&C 45:4]]; [[D&C 76.69|76:69]]). Joseph Smith, as have many other prophets, sealed his testimony with his blood that the righteous might be honored and the wicked might be condemned ([[Revelation 6.9|Rev. 6:9–10]][[Revelation 6.10|]]; [[D&C 135.6|D&C 135:6–7]][[D&C 135.7|]]; [[D&C 136.39|136:39]]).

Often a covenant was sealed with blood ([[Genesis 15.9|Gen. 15:9–18]][[Genesis 15.10|]][[Genesis 15.11|]][[Genesis 15.12|]][[Genesis 15.13|]][[Genesis 15.14|]][[Genesis 15.15|]][[Genesis 15.16|]][[Genesis 15.17|]][[Genesis 15.18|]]; [[Exodus 24.8|Ex. 24:8]]; [[Zechariah 9.11|Zech. 9:11]]; [[Matthew 26.28|Matt. 26:28]]; [[Hebrews 10.29|Heb. 10:29]]; [[Hebrews 13.20|13:20]]).
