# Praetorium

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/praetorium?lang=eng)

The headquarters of the Roman military governor, wherever he happened to be ([[Matthew 27.27|Matt. 27:27]]; [[Mark 15.16|Mark 15:16]]; [[John 18.28|John 18:28]]). In [[Philippians 1.13|Philip. 1:13]] it possibly denotes the body of judges forming the supreme court at Rome.
