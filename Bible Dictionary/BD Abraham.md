# Abraham

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/abraham?lang=eng)

*Father of a multitude.* Originally called *Abram,* “exalted father” ([[Genesis 11.26|Gen. 11:26]]; [[Genesis 17.5|17:5]]). Son of Terah, born in Ur of the Chaldees ([[Genesis 11.26|Gen. 11:26–28]][[Genesis 11.27|]][[Genesis 11.28|]]). The migration to Haran, where Terah died, is found in [[Genesis 11.31|Gen. 11:31]]. Abraham’s journey to Canaan, the divine call, and the covenant are recorded in [[Genesis 12.1|Gen. 12:1–5]][[Genesis 12.2|]][[Genesis 12.3|]][[Genesis 12.4|]][[Genesis 12.5|]]. His sojourn in Egypt is given in [[Genesis 11.1|Gen. 11–20]] and [[Abraham 1.1|Abr. 1–3]]. Other important events: settlement in Hebron ([[Genesis 13.18|Gen. 13:18]]); rescue of Lot and the meeting with Melchizedek ([[Genesis 14.1|Gen. 14]]); further blessings and promises ([[Genesis 15.1|Gen. 15:1]]; [[Genesis 17.8|17:8]]); institution of circumcision and change of name to Abraham ([[Genesis 17.9|Gen. 17:9–27]][[Genesis 17.10|]][[Genesis 17.11|]][[Genesis 17.12|]][[Genesis 17.13|]][[Genesis 17.14|]][[Genesis 17.15|]][[Genesis 17.16|]][[Genesis 17.17|]][[Genesis 17.18|]][[Genesis 17.19|]][[Genesis 17.20|]][[Genesis 17.21|]][[Genesis 17.22|]][[Genesis 17.23|]][[Genesis 17.24|]][[Genesis 17.25|]][[Genesis 17.26|]][[Genesis 17.27|]]); intercession for Lot at Sodom ([[Genesis 19.1|Gen. 19]]); offering of Isaac and renewal of the covenant and blessings ([[Genesis 22.1|Gen. 22]]); death of Sarah and purchase of the cave of Machpelah as a family burial place ([[Genesis 23.1|Gen. 23]]); Abraham’s death and burial ([[Genesis 25.8|Gen. 25:8–10]][[Genesis 25.9|]][[Genesis 25.10|]]).

Abraham is always regarded in the Old Testament as founder of the covenant race, which is personified in the house of Israel. He is the “father of the faithful.” John the Baptist and Paul rebuked those holding the erroneous idea that natural descent from Abraham was by itself sufficient to secure God’s favor ([[Matthew 3.9|Matt. 3:9]]; [[Romans 9.7|Rom. 9:7]]). For references to him in Jesus’ teachings see [[Matthew 8.11|Matt. 8:11]]; [[Luke 16.22|Luke 16:22]]; [[John 8.56|John 8:56]].

Latter-day revelation has clarified the significance of the Abrahamic covenant and other aspects of Abraham’s life and ministry. We learn that he was greatly blessed with divine revelation concerning the planetary system, the creation of the earth, and the premortal activities of the spirits of mankind. One of the most valiant spirits in the premortal life, he was chosen to be a leader in the kingdom of God before he was born into this world ([[Abraham 1.1|Abr. 1–5]]). We also learn from latter-day revelation that because of Abraham’s faithfulness he is now exalted and sits upon a throne in eternity ([[D&C 132.29|D&C 132:29, 37]][[D&C 132.37|]]).
