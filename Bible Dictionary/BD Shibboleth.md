# Shibboleth

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/shibboleth?lang=eng)

A Hebrew word (meaning either a flowing stream or a head of grain), which the Gileadites under Jephthah used in order to discover which of their prisoners were Ephraimites. The Ephraimite pronunciation was “Sibboleth” ([[Judges 12.5|Judg. 12:5–6]][[Judges 12.6|]]).
