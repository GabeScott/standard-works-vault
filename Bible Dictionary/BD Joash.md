# Joash

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/joash?lang=eng)

(1) Gideon’s father ([[Judges 6.11|Judg. 6:11, 29–31]][[Judges 6.29|]][[Judges 6.30|]][[Judges 6.31|]]; [[Judges 7.14|7:14]]; [[Judges 8.13|8:13, 29, 32]][[Judges 8.29|]][[Judges 8.32|]]).

(2) A son of Ahab ([[1 Kings 22.26|1 Kgs. 22:26]]; [[2 Chronicles 18.25|2 Chr. 18:25]]; [[2 Chronicles 25.23|25:23]]).

(3) King of Judah, and son of Ahaziah; placed on the throne by the high priest Jehoiada ([[2 Kings 11.1|2 Kgs. 11–12]]; [[2 Kings 14.1|14]]).

(4) King of Israel, son of Jehoahaz ([[2 Kings 13.1|2 Kgs. 13]]; [[2 Kings 14.1|14:1–16]][[2 Kings 14.2|]][[2 Kings 14.3|]][[2 Kings 14.4|]][[2 Kings 14.5|]][[2 Kings 14.6|]][[2 Kings 14.7|]][[2 Kings 14.8|]][[2 Kings 14.9|]][[2 Kings 14.10|]][[2 Kings 14.11|]][[2 Kings 14.12|]][[2 Kings 14.13|]][[2 Kings 14.14|]][[2 Kings 14.15|]][[2 Kings 14.16|]]; [[2 Chronicles 25.1|2 Chr. 25]]); one of the best of the kings of Israel; Elisha, Hosea, and Amos prophesied during his reign.

(5) Four others ([[1 Chronicles 4.22|1 Chr. 4:22]]; [[1 Chronicles 7.8|7:8]]; [[1 Chronicles 12.3|12:3]]; [[1 Chronicles 27.28|27:28]]).
