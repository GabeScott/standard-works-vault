# Hypocrite

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hypocrite?lang=eng)

The word generally denotes one who pretends to be religious when he is not (though it is sometimes used to mean simply a bad man; see [[Job 8.13|Job 8:13]]; [[Job 13.16|13:16]]; [[Isaiah 9.17|Isa. 9:17]]; [[Isaiah 33.14|33:14]]).
