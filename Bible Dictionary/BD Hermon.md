# Hermon

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/hermon?lang=eng)

A mountain, 9400 feet high, in the extreme northeast of the Holy Land, being the southern point of a range of hills called Anti-Libanus. It is visible not only from a great part of Galilee, but also from various places as far south as Jericho. The Hermonites ([[Psalms 42.6|Ps. 42:6]]) are the three peaks of Mount Hermon. Jewish poetry contains several allusions to this mountain ([[Psalms 89.12|Ps. 89:12]]; [[Psalms 133.3|133:3]]; [[Song of Solomon 4.8|Song 4:8]]).
