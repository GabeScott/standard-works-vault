# Mary Magdalene

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/mary-magdalene?lang=eng)

Mary of Magdala, probably the place of that name on the western shore of the Sea of Galilee. Out of her went seven devils ([[Luke 8.2|Luke 8:2]]). She was near the cross ([[Matthew 27.56|Matt. 27:56]]; [[Mark 15.40|Mark 15:40]]; [[John 19.25|John 19:25]]); at the burial ([[Matthew 27.61|Matt. 27:61]]; [[Mark 15.47|Mark 15:47]]); at the tomb in the morning ([[Matthew 28.1|Matt. 28:1]]; [[Mark 16.1|Mark 16:1]]; [[Luke 24.10|Luke 24:10]]; [[John 20.1|John 20:1, 11]][[John 20.11|]]). Jesus appeared to her ([[Mark 16.9|Mark 16:9]]; [[John 20.14|John 20:14–18]][[John 20.15|]][[John 20.16|]][[John 20.17|]][[John 20.18|]]).
