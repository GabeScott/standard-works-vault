# Watches

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/watches?lang=eng)

The Jews, like the Babylonians and Greeks, divided the night into three watches, “the beginning of the watches,” sunset till 10 p.m. ([[Lamentations 2.19|Lam. 2:19]]); the “middle watch,” 10 p.m. till 2 a.m. ([[Judges 7.19|Judg. 7:19]]); and the “morning watch,” 2 a.m. till sunrise ([[Exodus 14.24|Ex. 14:24]]; [[1 Samuel 11.11|1 Sam. 11:11]]; [[Luke 12.38|Luke 12:38]]). The Romans had four watches, and it is their practice that is followed in [[Matthew 14.25|Matt. 14:25]]; [[Mark 6.48|Mark 6:48]]; [[Mark 13.35|13:35]].
