# Jesus

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/jesus?lang=eng)

(1) The Greek form of the name Joshua or Jeshua, “God is help” or “Savior.” Joshua son of Nun is so spoken of in [[Acts 7.45|Acts 7:45]] and [[Hebrews 4.8|Heb. 4:8]].

(2) The name given by Joseph to the Savior of the world at His circumcision ([[Matthew 1.25|Matt. 1:25]]; [[Luke 2.21|Luke 2:21]]), in accordance with the direction of the angel Gabriel ([[Luke 1.31|Luke 1:31]]; see also [[Matthew 1.21|Matt. 1:21]]). The name was not an uncommon one among the Jews. An outline of the chief events of His earthly life can be found in the [Harmony of the Gospels](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/harmony/introduction?lang=eng) in the appendix. A list of references bearing on the doctrine of His person is given in this dictionary under the heading *[[BD Christ|Christ]].*

(3) A Roman Christian mentioned by Paul ([[Colossians 4.11|Col. 4:11]]).
