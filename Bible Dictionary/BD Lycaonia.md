# Lycaonia

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/lycaonia?lang=eng)

A district of Asia Minor, where the cities of Iconium, Lystra, and Derbe are found ([[Acts 14.6|Acts 14:6]]). The “speech of Lycaonia” ([[Acts 14.11|14:11]]) was a dialect the people used, but they were also able to understand the Greek spoken by Paul and Barnabas.
