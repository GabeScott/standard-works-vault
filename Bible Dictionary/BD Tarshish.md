# Tarshish

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/tarshish?lang=eng)

(1) Probably Tartessus in Spain; ships of Tarshish (the name came to be used to denote ships of the largest size, suitable for long voyages) ([[1 Kings 10.22|1 Kgs. 10:22]]; [[Psalms 48.7|Ps. 48:7]]; [[Isaiah 2.16|Isa. 2:16]]; [[Isaiah 23.1|23:1]]; [[Isaiah 60.9|60:9]]; [[Ezekiel 27.25|Ezek. 27:25]]); kings of Tarshish ([[Psalms 72.10|Ps. 72:10]]); merchants of Tarshish ([[Ezekiel 27.12|Ezek. 27:12]]; [[Ezekiel 38.13|38:13]]; also [[Isaiah 66.19|Isa. 66:19]]; [[Jeremiah 10.9|Jer. 10:9]]; [[Jonah 1.3|Jonah 1:3]]; [[Jonah 4.2|4:2]]).

(2) A Tarshish in the Indian Ocean ([[1 Kings 22.48|1 Kgs. 22:48]]; [[2 Chronicles 9.21|2 Chr. 9:21]]; [[2 Chronicles 20.36|20:36]]).
