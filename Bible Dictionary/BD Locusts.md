# Locusts

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/locusts?lang=eng)

Large flying insects, moving in large numbers and destroying all vegetation. There are some ten different kinds, frequently referred to in the Old Testament. They were sometimes used for food ([[Leviticus 11.21|Lev. 11:21–22]][[Leviticus 11.22|]]; [[Matthew 3.4|Matt. 3:4]]), being cooked in various ways and having somewhat the taste of shrimp.
