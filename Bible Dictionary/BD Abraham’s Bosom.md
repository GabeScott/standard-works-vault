# Abraham’s Bosom

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/abrahams-bosom?lang=eng)

A term used to denote the place of the righteous dead, [[Luke 16.22|Luke 16:22–23]][[Luke 16.23|]]. It connotes the harmony that exists among the righteous in paradise as they await the Resurrection.
