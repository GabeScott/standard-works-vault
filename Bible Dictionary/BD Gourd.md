# Gourd

[Link](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/gourd?lang=eng)

In [[Jonah 4.6|Jonah 4:6–10]][[Jonah 4.7|]][[Jonah 4.8|]][[Jonah 4.9|]][[Jonah 4.10|]], *gourd* refers to the castor-oil plant, *Ricinus communis* or *palma-Christi,* a plant of very rapid growth; in [[2 Kings 4.38|2 Kgs. 4:38–40]][[2 Kings 4.39|]][[2 Kings 4.40|]] *gourd* refers to the colocynth, which has vine-shaped leaves, hence is called “wild vine.”
